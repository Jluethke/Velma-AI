# Work Sample Simulation Designer

Turn the candidate tasks into a self-contained 4 to 8 hour simulation. The brief must be completable by someone who does not know your internal systems, must include every piece of context needed to start, and must mirror the real work rather than a puzzle.

## Inputs

- `ranked-task-list`: The impact-ranked task list from the audit
- `work-sample-candidate-tasks`: The tasks selected for simulation

## Outputs

- `work-sample-brief`: The complete task brief given to candidates, with all context, the deliverable, and the time scope
- `time-and-materials-spec`: The expected hours and the reference materials provided, confirmed to expose no private information

## Process

1. Write the brief as if assigning the task to a new hire on day one, including every piece of context they need. If a candidate would have to ask a clarifying question to begin, the brief is incomplete.
2. Set the scope to 4 to 8 hours of work. Shorter samples give too little signal; longer ones impose too much on the candidate.
3. List the materials you provide, such as data files and reference documents, and confirm none expose private information.
4. Define the exact deliverable: what the candidate submits, in what format, and by when.
