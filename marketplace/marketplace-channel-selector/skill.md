# Marketplace Channel Selector

Select the marketplace channels based on product fit and audience.

## Inputs

- `product-catalog`: Products to sell
- `target-audience`: Target customer demographics and behavior

## Outputs

- `channel-selection`: Selected marketplace channels with rationale
- `channel-requirements`: Account and listing requirements per channel

## Process

1. Evaluate channel fit by product category and price point.
2. Compare fee structures (referral fees, fulfillment fees, subscription costs).
3. Assess the audience overlap with your target customer.
4. Select the channels and document the requirements for each.
