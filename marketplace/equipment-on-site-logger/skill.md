# Equipment On Site Logger

Log all equipment present on the job site and its status.

## Inputs

- `project-name`: Name of the construction project
- `report-date`: Date of the field report

## Outputs

- `equipment-log`: List of equipment on site with status
- `equipment-issues`: Any equipment breakdowns or needs

## Process

1. List each piece of equipment on site (cranes, excavators, lifts, generators).
2. Record operating status for each (active, idle, down for repair).
3. Note any equipment arriving or leaving the site.
