# Pollen Counting Identification

Count pollen grains under the microscope and identify each to the lowest possible taxonomic level.

## Inputs

- `prepared-slides`: Slides with concentrated pollen
- `reference-collection`: Pollen morphology keys and reference slides for the study region

## Outputs

- `pollen-counts`: Raw counts of each pollen taxon per sample with a minimum of 300 terrestrial grains
- `identification-notes`: Difficult identifications, damaged grains, and non-pollen palynomorphs noted

## Process

1. Scan traverse lines across the slide at 400x magnification
2. Identify each pollen grain using morphological features: aperture type and number, wall structure, size, shape
3. Count until at least 300 terrestrial pollen grains are reached per sample
4. Record non-pollen palynomorphs (spores, algae, charcoal) encountered during counting
