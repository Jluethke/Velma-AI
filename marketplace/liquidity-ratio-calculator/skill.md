# Liquidity Ratio Calculator

Calculate liquidity ratios: current ratio, quick ratio, and cash ratio.

## Inputs

- `balance-sheet`: Balance sheet data
- `cash-flow-data`: Cash flow statement data

## Outputs

- `liquidity-ratios`: Current, quick, and cash ratios with interpretation
- `working-capital-analysis`: Working capital calculation and trend

## Process

1. Calculate the current ratio (current assets divided by current liabilities).
2. Calculate the quick ratio (excluding inventory and prepaids).
3. Calculate the cash ratio (cash and equivalents only).
4. Analyze working capital adequacy and trend over time.
