# Photo Exporter Deliverer

Export in required formats and deliver to the client.

## Inputs

- `retouched-images`: Final retouched images
- `shoot-brief`: Delivery specifications and format requirements

## Outputs

- `delivered-image-set`: Exported images in all required formats and sizes
- `delivery-confirmation`: Client delivery receipt and download link

## Process

1. Export high-resolution files for print use
2. Export web-resolution files for digital use
3. Upload to the client delivery gallery
4. Send the delivery notification with download instructions
