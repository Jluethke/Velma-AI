# Similarity Bias Guard

Guard against the pull toward similarity. Liking a candidate is a fit signal, not an add signal. A short checklist after each interview forces interviewers to separate their reaction from the evidence.

## Inputs

- `interview-guide`: The per-interviewer assignment
- `contribution-evidence-form`: The evidence form

## Outputs

- `bias-checklist`: A post-interview checklist that separates reaction to similarity from evidence against criteria
- `debrief-protocol`: An evidence-first debrief where criteria are presented before any overall verdict

## Process

1. Build a checklist each interviewer completes after an interview: did I decide in the first five minutes, is my reaction about similarity or difference, can I name the criterion met or missed?
2. Run the debrief on evidence first: each interviewer presents examples against their assigned criteria before anyone gives an overall verdict.
3. Flag any verdict that rests on fit or feel with no criterion behind it, and send it back for evidence.
4. Record the assessment per criterion, then form the overall decision from the criteria, not the reverse.
