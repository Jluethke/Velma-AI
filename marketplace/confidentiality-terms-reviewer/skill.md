# Confidentiality Terms Reviewer

Review confidentiality obligations and their duration.

## Inputs

- `agreement-text`: Employment agreement text
- `ip-assignment-analysis`: IP assignment analysis

## Outputs

- `confidentiality-analysis`: Analysis of confidentiality scope and duration
- `whistleblower-protections`: Presence of statutory whistleblower protections

## Process

1. Review the definition and scope of confidential information
2. Check the duration of confidentiality obligations post-employment
3. Verify inclusion of statutory whistleblower and DTSA protections
