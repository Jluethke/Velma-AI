# Ooda Loop Context Capture

Capture the competitive situation and the decisions being made over and over. The OODA loop applies where the same kind of decision repeats against a reacting opponent, so name that loop clearly.

## Inputs

- `situation-description`: The competitive situation and who you are up against
- `decision-cadence`: How often the key decisions repeat
- `competitor-tempo`: How fast rivals seem to respond

## Outputs

- `loop-context`: The situation framed as a repeating decision loop
- `decision-inventory`: The specific recurring decisions in the loop

## Process

1. Describe the situation as a loop: a decision made, acted on, observed, and made again.
2. List the recurring decisions that make up the loop.
3. Note how fast rivals react, since the advantage is relative loop speed, not absolute speed.
4. Confirm the situation really is a repeating loop against a reacting opponent, not a one-time choice.
