# Dmaic Define Phase

Define the problem, scope, and project charter.

## Inputs

- `problem-statement`: Description of the quality or performance issue
- `business-case`: Why this problem matters to the business

## Outputs

- `project-charter`: DMAIC project charter with scope, goals, timeline, team
- `ctq-tree`: Critical to quality characteristics derived from customer needs

## Process

1. Write the problem statement in measurable terms
2. Define the project scope, goals, and timeline
3. Identify the team and stakeholders
4. Build the CTQ tree from voice of customer to measurable characteristics
