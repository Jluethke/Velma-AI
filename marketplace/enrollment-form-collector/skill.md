# Enrollment Form Collector

Collect and verify all required enrollment forms from the family.

## Inputs

- `program-options`: The program the family selected after the tour
- `tour-scheduled`: Tour completion confirmation

## Outputs

- `enrollment-packet`: Completed enrollment forms (child info, medical history, emergency contacts, pickup authorizations, consent forms)
- `missing-items`: Any forms or documents still outstanding

## Process

1. Send the enrollment packet to the family (child information form, medical history, emergency contacts, authorized pickup list, photo/media consent, handbook acknowledgment).
2. Receive and review each returned form for completeness.
3. Flag any missing signatures, fields, or required documents.
4. Follow up on outstanding items within the deadline.
