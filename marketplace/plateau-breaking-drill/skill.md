# Plateau Breaking Drill

Design a drill that combines the constraint, the avoided sub-skill, and the specific difference into one practice exercise.

## Inputs

- `selected-constraint`: The constraint to add
- `avoided-sub-skill`: The sub-skill to target
- `specific-difference`: The observable difference from the one-level-up study

## Outputs

- `combined-drill`: A practice exercise that addresses all three plateau factors: adds the constraint, isolates the avoided sub-skill, and implements the specific difference
- `drill-schedule`: How often to do the drill (3-5 times per week for 3 weeks) and how to measure whether the plateau is breaking

## Process

1. Design one drill that hits all three: it forces you to practice the avoided sub-skill, under the added constraint, while implementing the specific difference you observed. This concentrated drill attacks the plateau from three angles simultaneously.
2. The drill should be 15-30 minutes. Short enough to maintain focus, long enough to get meaningful reps.
3. Measure your performance at the start of the 3-week drill period using the same metric you used to diagnose the plateau. Re-measure weekly.
4. If performance improves by the end of week 3, the plateau is breaking. Continue the drill until performance reaches a new stable level, then return to normal practice incorporating the changes. If performance has not improved, one of the three diagnoses was wrong. Revisit the analysis.
