# Tribology Specimen Prep

Prepare test specimens to controlled surface finish and geometry for reproducible tribology measurements.

## Inputs

- `materials-to-test`: Materials and their forms (disk, pin, ball, block)
- `test-configuration`: Required specimen dimensions and surface finish

## Outputs

- `prepared-specimens`: Specimens with documented surface roughness and dimensions
- `pre-test-surface-data`: Surface roughness profiles and optical micrographs before testing

## Process

1. Machine or cut specimens to the required dimensions.
2. Polish to the target surface roughness (Ra) and verify with a profilometer.
3. Clean specimens using an ultrasonic bath with solvent to remove contamination.
4. Document pre-test surface roughness and photograph the surface.
