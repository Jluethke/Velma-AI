# Energy Renewal Rituals

Design renewal rituals for the trough: movement, nutrition, social interaction, or nature exposure that restore energy without caffeine.

## Inputs

- `crash-triggers`: What causes energy drops
- `optimized-schedule`: The new schedule

## Outputs

- `renewal-rituals`: Specific rituals for each energy trough: a 10-minute walk after lunch, a mid-afternoon stretching break, a conversation with a colleague
- `energy-sustaining-habits`: Daily habits that maintain energy: hydration schedule, meal timing, movement frequency

## Process

1. A 10-minute walk after lunch reduces the post-lunch energy dip significantly. The combination of movement and light exposure resets alertness.
2. Hydration affects energy more than most people realize. Mild dehydration (1-2% body weight) reduces cognitive performance by 10-20%. Drink water before you feel thirsty.
3. Small frequent meals sustain energy better than large infrequent ones. A heavy lunch causes a large insulin response and a corresponding energy crash. A lighter lunch with protein maintains afternoon energy.
4. Social interaction can be energizing or draining depending on the interaction. A brief positive conversation restores energy. A contentious meeting drains it. Schedule social interactions strategically.
