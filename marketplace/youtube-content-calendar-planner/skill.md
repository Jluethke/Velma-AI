# Youtube Content Calendar Planner

Plan the YouTube content calendar: topics, formats, and upload schedule.

## Inputs

- `channel-niche`: The channel's topic area and target audience
- `upload-frequency`: How often videos will be published (weekly, biweekly, etc.)

## Outputs

- `content-calendar`: Scheduled list of video topics, formats, and publish dates
- `content-pillars`: The recurring content categories the channel will cover

## Process

1. Review the channel niche and audience interests to identify high-demand topics.
2. Define 3 to 5 content pillars that the channel will rotate through.
3. Map topics to the upload schedule for the planning period.
4. Flag seasonal or time-sensitive topics that need specific publish dates.
