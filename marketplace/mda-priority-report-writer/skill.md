# Mda Priority Report Writer

Write the decision record for the reallocation.

## Inputs

- `reallocation-plan`: The sized moves of dollars
- `priority-ranking`: The ranking behind the moves

## Outputs

- `marginal-analysis-report`: One-page record of the ranking and the moves
- `decision-log`: Which lines were funded, held, or trimmed and why

## Process

1. Document the ranking and the reasoning for each move.
2. Record which lines were funded, held, or trimmed.
3. Produce the one-page summary for sign-off.
