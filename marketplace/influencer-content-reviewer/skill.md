# Influencer Content Reviewer

Review and approve influencer content before publication.

## Inputs

- `content-briefs`: The content briefs sent to influencers
- `partnership-agreements`: The agreed terms and deliverables

## Outputs

- `approved-content`: Content approved for publication with any revision notes
- `publication-schedule`: The publication schedule for all influencer posts

## Process

1. Receive draft content from each influencer.
2. Review for brand alignment, messaging accuracy, and disclosure compliance (FTC guidelines).
3. Request revisions if needed and re-review.
4. Approve final content and confirm the publication dates.
