# Pain Discovery Interview

Interview the prospect to discover the specific pain: what are they doing manually, what errors occur, what opportunities are they missing?

## Inputs

- `prospect-context`: What you know about the prospect's business or situation
- `product-relevance`: How your product or service relates to their situation

## Outputs

- `pain-points`: 3-5 specific pain points described in the prospect's words
- `pain-evidence`: For each pain point: the prospect's estimate of frequency, time spent, errors caused, or opportunities lost

## Process

1. Ask open questions about the current process: 'Walk me through how you handle [the area your product addresses] today. Start from the beginning.' Let them describe it in detail.
2. Listen for time sinks ('it takes us about 3 hours every week'), error rates ('we probably get it wrong 10% of the time'), and missed opportunities ('by the time we have the report, the window has closed').
3. Do not lead the prospect to exaggerate. Ask for specifics: 'When you say it takes a long time, roughly how many hours per week? Who does it?' Specifics produce credible numbers.
4. Record the pain in the prospect's own words and numbers. These become the inputs for the dollar calculation.
