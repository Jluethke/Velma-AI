# Food Safety Record Keeper

Document procedures and maintain required records.

## Inputs

- `implemented-practices`: Active procedures
- `applicable-standard`: Record-keeping requirements

## Outputs

- `record-system`: Organized record-keeping system with templates
- `record-review-schedule`: Frequency for reviewing and updating records

## Process

1. Create record templates for each required log (water tests, hygiene, pesticide use).
2. Set up the filing system (digital or paper).
3. Establish the review schedule for record completeness.
4. Assign responsibility for each record type.
