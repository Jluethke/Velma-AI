# Actual Vs Theoretical Comparer

Compare actual food cost against theoretical to identify variance.

## Inputs

- `theoretical-cost-by-item`: Theoretical cost per item
- `actual-food-cost-data`: Actual purchases, inventory changes, and revenue

## Outputs

- `cost-variance`: Variance between actual and theoretical cost
- `variance-by-category`: Variance broken down by food category

## Process

1. Calculate actual food cost from purchases and inventory.
2. Compute the variance from theoretical.
3. Break variance down by category.
4. Rank categories by variance size.
