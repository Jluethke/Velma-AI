# Behavior Target Definition

Define the specific behaviors you want to increase and the specific behaviors you want to decrease, stated as observable actions, not aspirations.

## Inputs

- `desired-outcomes`: What you want to achieve (health, productivity, learning, financial, relationship goals)
- `current-struggles`: What you currently struggle to do or struggle to stop doing

## Outputs

- `increase-behaviors`: 3-5 specific behaviors you want to do more often, stated as observable actions with frequency targets
- `decrease-behaviors`: 3-5 specific behaviors you want to do less often, stated as observable actions with reduction targets

## Process

1. Convert each desired outcome into a specific, observable behavior. 'Be healthier' becomes 'eat a vegetable with every meal' and 'walk 30 minutes daily.' The behavior must be something a camera could record.
2. Convert each struggle into a specific behavior to decrease. 'Stop wasting time' becomes 'open social media fewer than 3 times per day.'
3. Set a frequency target for each behavior. Increase behaviors need a minimum (at least X times per week). Decrease behaviors need a maximum (no more than X times per week).
4. Limit to 3-5 in each category. Environment redesign works best when focused. Trying to change 15 behaviors simultaneously changes none.
