# Ad Creative Builder

Write ad copy and design creative for each platform and format.

## Inputs

- `campaign-objective`: Campaign objective for messaging alignment
- `platform-plan`: Platforms and ad formats

## Outputs

- `ad-creatives`: Copy, images, and video specs per ad
- `creative-variants`: A/B test variants for each ad

## Process

1. Write headline and body copy for each ad format
2. Specify image or video requirements
3. Create at least two variants for A/B testing
