# Anti Persona Builder

Define the anti-persona: a composite profile of the person you are explicitly choosing not to serve.

## Inputs

- `churn-complaint-patterns`: Recurring mismatch patterns from the audit
- `high-cost-segments`: Segments that cost more than they return

## Outputs

- `anti-persona-profile`: Composite profile including demographics, behaviors, expectations, and the specific mismatch that makes them a poor fit
- `exclusion-rationale`: Why serving this person harms both them and you

## Process

1. From the patterns, write one composite profile. Give it a name. Describe: what they expect, what they need, how they evaluate, and what outcome they actually want.
2. For each trait, explain the mismatch: why your offering does not serve this need, and why trying to serve it dilutes what you do for the right people.
3. State the exclusion rationale in one sentence: serving this person means either changing what you are or disappointing them, and neither outcome is acceptable.
