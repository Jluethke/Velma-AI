# Resource Reallocation Plan

Build a plan to shift resources from the trivial many to the vital few. This is where the analysis becomes action. The goal is not to eliminate the trivial many overnight, but to systematically redirect time, money, and attention toward the highest-output inputs.

## Inputs

- `vital-few`: The high-output inputs
- `trivial-many`: The low-output inputs with their resource consumption

## Outputs

- `reallocation-plan`: For each input: keep and invest more, automate, delegate, reduce, or eliminate, with the specific resource shift
- `expected-impact`: The estimated output gain from the reallocation, based on the observed concentration ratio

## Process

1. For each vital few input: identify one specific way to increase investment (more time, more budget, more attention). The investment should be proportional to the output it produces.
2. For each trivial many input: classify as automate (replace human effort with a system), delegate (hand to someone with lower opportunity cost), reduce (do less of it), or eliminate (stop entirely).
3. Estimate the output gain: if you shift 20% of the resources from trivial many to vital few, and the vital few are 4x more productive per unit of resource, the net gain is 20% * 3 = 60% more output from the same total resources. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.
