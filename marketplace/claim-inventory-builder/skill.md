# Claim Inventory Builder

Inventory every claim, feature, and benefit in your current messaging across all channels.

## Inputs

- `current-messaging`: All current marketing materials: website, ads, sales decks, email sequences, social profiles, packaging
- `offering-description`: What you sell, do, or represent

## Outputs

- `claim-inventory`: Every distinct claim, feature, and benefit extracted from current messaging, deduplicated
- `claim-count`: Total number of distinct claims (most businesses have 15-40; if yours has more than 10, there is room to subtract)

## Process

1. Go through every piece of messaging. For each, extract the distinct claims: what it says you do, what benefits it promises, what features it highlights.
2. Deduplicate: many claims will appear in slightly different words across channels. Merge them into one canonical version.
3. Count them. Most businesses carry 15-40 distinct claims across their materials. The number itself is diagnostic: more than 10 usually means the positioning is blurry.
