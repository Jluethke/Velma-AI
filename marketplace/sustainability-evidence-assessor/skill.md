# Sustainability Evidence Assessor

Collect evidence and assess performance against the sustainability criteria.

## Inputs

- `audit-plan`: Audit plan with scope and schedule
- `criteria-checklist`: Checklist of criteria to assess

## Outputs

- `assessment-findings`: Findings for each criterion (conforming, non-conforming, observation)
- `improvement-opportunities`: Identified opportunities for improvement

## Process

1. Collect documentary evidence: policies, reports, records, and data.
2. Conduct interviews with responsible personnel for each area.
3. Assess each criterion against the evidence and classify as conforming, non-conforming, or observation.
4. Identify improvement opportunities beyond the minimum requirements.
