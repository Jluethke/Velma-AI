# Vsm Product Family Selector

Select the product family and define the value stream boundaries.

## Inputs

- `product-list`: List of products and their process routings
- `business-objectives`: Strategic goals for the improvement effort

## Outputs

- `product-family`: Selected product family for mapping
- `stream-boundaries`: Start and end points of the value stream

## Process

1. Group products by shared process steps using a product-process matrix
2. Select the family with the highest volume or strategic importance
3. Define the upstream boundary (raw material receipt) and downstream boundary (shipment)
4. Identify the customer and their demand rate (takt time)
