# Impact Analysis Writer

Analyze results against targets and write the findings narrative.

## Inputs

- `quantitative-results`: Numbers for each indicator
- `qualitative-findings`: Stories and case studies

## Outputs

- `impact-narrative`: Written analysis of results
- `results-summary`: At-a-glance summary of key metrics

## Process

1. Compare each indicator to its target or benchmark
2. Identify trends, successes, and areas for improvement
3. Integrate qualitative examples to illustrate the numbers
4. Write the narrative connecting activities to outcomes
