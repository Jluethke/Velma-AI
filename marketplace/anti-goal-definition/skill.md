# Anti Goal Definition

Define your anti-goals: the outcomes you refuse to accept, stated as hard boundaries.

## Inputs

- `planning-context`: What you are planning for (a project, a quarter, a year, a life phase)
- `past-regrets`: Previous outcomes that you wish you had avoided

## Outputs

- `anti-goals`: 5-10 outcomes you refuse to accept, stated as specific boundaries (e.g., 'I will not work more than 50 hours per week,' 'We will not take on more than 2 projects simultaneously')
- `anti-goal-rationale`: For each anti-goal, why this boundary exists (usually from a past experience where crossing it caused harm)

## Process

1. List every outcome from the past that you wish you had avoided. These are your anti-goals. 'I burned out last year working 70-hour weeks' becomes the anti-goal 'I will not work more than 50 hours per week.'
2. State each anti-goal as a specific, measurable boundary. 'I will not sacrifice my health' is too vague. 'I will not skip exercise more than once per week' is specific.
3. Include both outcome anti-goals (what you refuse to let happen) and process anti-goals (how you refuse to operate). Outcome: 'We will not ship with known critical bugs.' Process: 'We will not make strategic decisions in the last hour of the day.'
4. Limit to 5-10 anti-goals. Too many constraints leave no room to operate. The anti-goals should define the walls of the playing field, not every step on it.
