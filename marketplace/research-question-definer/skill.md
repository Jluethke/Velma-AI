# Research Question Definer

Define the legal research question and the relevant jurisdiction and area of law.

## Inputs

- `research-request`: The legal question or issue to research
- `matter-context`: Context about the matter that prompted the research

## Outputs

- `research-question`: The precisely framed legal question
- `research-scope`: Jurisdiction, area of law, and any constraints on the research

## Process

1. Clarify the question: what specific legal issue needs to be answered.
2. Identify the relevant jurisdiction (federal, state, local, international).
3. Identify the area of law (contract, tort, employment, regulatory, IP, tax).
4. Define any constraints: budget, deadline, depth of analysis.
