# Security Budget Presentation Builder

Prepare the budget justification presentation for leadership with risk framing and investment recommendations.

## Inputs

- `prioritized-investments`: Ranked investment list
- `benchmark-comparison`: Industry peer comparison
- `risk-quantification`: Financial risk context
- `quick-wins`: Immediate high-value actions

## Outputs

- `budget-justification-document`: Written budget justification with executive summary, risk context, recommendations, and appendices
- `leadership-presentation`: Slide deck for leadership review

## Process

1. Write the executive summary: risk posture, key gaps, requested budget, expected outcome
2. Frame investments in business terms (breach prevention, compliance, revenue protection)
3. Include the benchmark comparison and ROSI analysis
4. Present the phased roadmap with quick wins front-loaded
