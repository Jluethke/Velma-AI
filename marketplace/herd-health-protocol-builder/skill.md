# Herd Health Protocol Builder

Establish the herd health protocol covering vaccinations, deworming, and nutrition.

## Inputs

- `herd-profile`: Species, breed, age groups, and herd size
- `regional-disease-risks`: Prevalent diseases and parasites in the area

## Outputs

- `health-protocol`: Vaccination, deworming, and nutrition schedule
- `product-inventory`: Required vaccines, dewormers, and supplements

## Process

1. List required and recommended vaccinations by species and age group.
2. Set the deworming schedule based on regional parasite pressure.
3. Define nutritional requirements by production stage (growth, lactation, maintenance).
4. Create the product inventory and ordering schedule.
