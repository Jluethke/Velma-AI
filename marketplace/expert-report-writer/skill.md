# Expert Report Writer

Write a forensic expert report suitable for court submission with methods, findings, conclusions, and expert qualifications.

## Inputs

- `lab-results`: Final analytical results after QC
- `statistical-report`: Statistical weight of evidence calculations
- `case-reference`: Case number, requesting authority, and evidence item identifiers

## Outputs

- `expert-report`: Complete forensic report meeting court admissibility standards
- `testimony-outline`: Structured outline for expert testimony on the findings
- `disclosure-package`: All materials required for defense disclosure (data, methods, qualifications)

## Process

1. Structure the report following jurisdiction-specific format requirements
2. Describe methods used in language accessible to non-scientists
3. Present findings and conclusions with the calculated evidential weight
4. State the expert's qualifications, training, and experience relevant to the analysis
5. Prepare the disclosure package including raw data, SOPs, and proficiency test records
