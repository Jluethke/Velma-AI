# Ux Research Question Definer

Define the research questions and select the methodology.

## Inputs

- `product-context`: Description of the product and current user issues
- `stakeholder-goals`: What decisions the research should inform

## Outputs

- `research-plan`: Research questions, methodology, and timeline
- `screener-criteria`: Participant screening criteria and quotas

## Process

1. Translate stakeholder goals into specific research questions
2. Select the methodology (usability test, interview, survey, diary study)
3. Define the participant criteria and sample size
4. Write the research plan with timeline and deliverables
