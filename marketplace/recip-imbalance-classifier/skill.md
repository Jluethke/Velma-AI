# Recip Imbalance Classifier

Classify the imbalanced relationships into where you over-give and where you under-give, and diagnose why, because the cause (generosity, people-pleasing, status-seeking, simple neglect) determines whether and how to rebalance.

## Inputs

- `balance-ratios`: Balance reading per relationship
- `flow-direction-map`: Net direction of value flow across the network

## Outputs

- `over-give-list`: Relationships where you give substantially more than you receive, with the likely cause
- `under-give-list`: Relationships where you receive substantially more than you give, with the likely cause

## Process

1. Pull the clearly imbalanced relationships into two lists. Leave the roughly-even ones alone, they are working. The energy goes to the lopsided ones.
2. For each over-give relationship, diagnose why you give more. Sometimes it is chosen generosity toward someone who genuinely cannot reciprocate (a mentee, a relative in need), which is healthy. Sometimes it is people-pleasing or buying approval, which is not. The distinction decides whether to act.
3. For each under-give relationship, diagnose why you receive more. Often it is simple neglect of someone generous, which is a quiet risk because under-given people eventually withdraw without announcing it. Sometimes you are early in a relationship where the balance has not formed yet.
4. Flag the dangerous cases: chronic over-giving that is draining you, and chronic under-receiving where a generous person is being taken for granted. These are the unstable relationships most likely to break if left alone.
