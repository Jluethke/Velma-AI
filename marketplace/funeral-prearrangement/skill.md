# Funeral Prearrangement

Plan funeral or memorial preferences: type of service, burial vs cremation, location, and cost prepayment.

## Inputs

- `service_preferences`: Religious, secular, celebration of life, etc.
- `disposition_choice`: Burial, cremation, donation to science, etc.
- `budget_range`: Budget or prepayment amount

## Outputs

- `service_plan`: Detailed service preferences
- `disposition_plan`: Body disposition details
- `prepayment_options`: Prepayment or insurance options

## Process

1. Choose disposition method
2. Plan service type and location
3. Select readings, music, speakers
4. Research prepayment vs insurance options
5. Document all preferences in writing
