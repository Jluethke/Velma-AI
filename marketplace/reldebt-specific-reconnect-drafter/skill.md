# Reldebt Specific Reconnect Drafter

Draft reconnection messages built around something specific to each person (a shared memory, a reason they came to mind, a genuine update) rather than a generic checking in, because specificity is what signals the relationship mattered and what makes a reply likely.

## Inputs

- `open-loop-list`: The unresolved thread behind each relationship
- `energy-drain-flags`: Relationships carrying real background energy cost

## Outputs

- `reconnect-drafts`: A specific, personal reconnection message for each recovery candidate
- `reconnect-hooks`: The concrete hook each message hangs on: a shared memory, a relevant update, a reason they came to mind, an overdue acknowledgment

## Process

1. For each candidate, find the hook: a specific shared memory, a piece of news relevant to them, an honest reason they came to mind, or the overdue acknowledgment the open loop calls for. The hook is the message.
2. Where there is an open loop, address it directly and simply. A brief honest line (I owed you a reply and I am sorry it is two years late) clears far more than pretending the gap did not happen.
3. Keep the message low-pressure. Reconnection after long silence works when it asks for nothing beyond the exchange itself, because a reconnect that leads with a request reads as reaching out only because you need something.
4. Write each message to one person. A reconnection that could have been sent to anyone signals the opposite of what you intend, and the whole point is to show this specific relationship was worth remembering.
