# Supplier Audit Conductor

Conduct the on-site supplier audit.

## Inputs

- `questionnaire-responses`: Supplier self-assessment
- `qualification-criteria`: Criteria to verify

## Outputs

- `audit-findings`: On-site audit observations, conformances, and nonconformances
- `process-capability-data`: Observed process capability and capacity data

## Process

1. Prepare the audit checklist from the questionnaire responses and criteria
2. Conduct the on-site audit (quality system, production processes, capacity)
3. Verify claimed certifications and calibration records
4. Document findings: conformances, observations, minor and major nonconformances
