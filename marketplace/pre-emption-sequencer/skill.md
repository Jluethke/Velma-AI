# Pre Emption Sequencer

Take the top three concerns by frequency and build them into your initial message so the audience does not have to raise them. Pre-emption is stronger than rebuttal because it shows you thought about their position before they had to defend it.

## Inputs

- `response-map`: Honest responses to each concern
- `frequency-ranking`: Which objections come up most often
- `concern-clusters`: Groups of objections sharing the same root concern

## Outputs

- `pre-emption-statements`: 3 statements to include in the initial message, each addressing a top concern before it is raised
- `remaining-response-playbook`: Responses for the concerns not pre-empted, ready to use when they come up in discussion

## Process

1. Select the top 3 concern clusters by frequency. These are the objections most people will have.
2. For each, write a pre-emption statement that acknowledges the concern and provides the evidence in 1-2 sentences.
3. Place the pre-emption statements after your bridge and before your ask. They should feel like 'I already thought about this' not 'I am defending myself.'
4. For all remaining concerns, organize responses into a playbook. Each entry: trigger phrase (what they will say), real concern, response, evidence.
