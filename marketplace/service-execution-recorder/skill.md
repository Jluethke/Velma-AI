# Service Execution Recorder

Execute the service and record the outcomes for the planning archive.

## Inputs

- `rehearsal-notes`: Adjustments from rehearsal
- `service-ready-checklist`: Pre-service confirmation

## Outputs

- `service-record`: What happened: attendance, offerings collected, technical issues, and notes for improvement
- `follow-up-items`: Action items arising from the service (pastoral follow-ups, visitor contacts, equipment repairs)

## Process

1. Execute the service according to the order, managing transitions and timing.
2. Record attendance (in-person and online if streaming).
3. Count and record the offering.
4. Log any technical issues, visitor information, and follow-up items.
5. Produce the service record for the planning archive.
