# Vendor Privacy Evaluation

Evaluate the vendor's privacy practices based on their responses and documentation.

## Inputs

- `questionnaire-responses`: Vendor's completed questionnaire
- `vendor-documentation`: Supporting documentation

## Outputs

- `privacy-risk-score`: Risk rating for the vendor's privacy practices (low, medium, high)
- `findings`: Specific findings (strengths and gaps in the vendor's privacy practices)

## Process

1. Review the vendor's data protection measures (encryption, access controls, data segregation, backup).
2. Assess the vendor's breach response capability (detection, notification timeline, remediation).
3. Evaluate the vendor's sub-processor management (list of sub-processors, approval process, oversight).
4. Review the vendor's data retention and deletion practices.
5. Score the vendor's overall privacy risk level.
