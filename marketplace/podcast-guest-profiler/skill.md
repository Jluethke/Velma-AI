# Podcast Guest Profiler

Define the ideal guest profile and source potential guests.

## Inputs

- `podcast-topic-focus`: The podcast's subject area and audience
- `episode-goals`: What the next batch of episodes should cover

## Outputs

- `guest-criteria`: The ideal guest profile (expertise, audience, availability)
- `prospect-list`: Ranked list of potential guests with contact information

## Process

1. Define what makes an ideal guest for the podcast (expertise, story, audience overlap).
2. Source prospects from industry events, social media, other podcasts, and referrals.
3. Rank prospects by fit, audience size, and likelihood of accepting.
4. Compile contact details and notes for outreach.
