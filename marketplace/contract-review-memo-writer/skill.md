# Contract Review Memo Writer

Produce the final contract review memo summarizing findings and recommendations.

## Inputs

- `key-terms-summary`: Key terms summary
- `risk-flags`: Risk flags
- `redline-document`: Redline with proposed changes

## Outputs

- `review-memo`: Contract review memo with findings and recommendations
- `approval-recommendation`: Recommendation to approve, negotiate, or reject

## Process

1. Summarize the contract purpose and key terms
2. List all risk findings with severity ratings
3. State the recommendation with supporting rationale
