# Survey Results Analyzer

Analyze the survey results and produce the findings report.

## Inputs

- `response-data`: The collected survey responses
- `research-question`: The question the survey was designed to answer

## Outputs

- `survey-findings`: Key findings with statistical summary and segment breakdowns
- `survey-design-document`: Complete record of the survey design, method, and results

## Process

1. Clean the data (remove incomplete responses, check for straight-lining, remove duplicates).
2. Calculate descriptive statistics for each question (mean, median, distribution).
3. Segment the results by relevant audience attributes and test for significant differences.
4. Summarize the top findings in relation to the original research question.
5. Produce the survey design document with method, instrument, response rates, and findings.
