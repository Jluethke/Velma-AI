# Driver Training Scheduler

Schedule training and safety courses for drivers.

## Inputs

- `compliance-tracker`: Compliance status
- `expiration-alerts`: Upcoming expirations

## Outputs

- `training-schedule`: Training calendar with required and elective courses
- `training-records`: Completed training records by driver

## Process

1. Identify required training (hazmat, defensive driving, hours of service, cargo securement).
2. Schedule refresher courses before certifications expire.
3. Track completion and record training hours.
4. Identify additional training needs based on incident trends.
