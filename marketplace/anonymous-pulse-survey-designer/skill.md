# Anonymous Pulse Survey Designer

Design a short anonymous pulse survey of current employees aimed at the clusters found in the departure data. Current employees say things departing ones soften, but only if anonymity is real.

## Inputs

- `coded-departure-data`: The structured departure records
- `departure-clusters`: The clusters to probe

## Outputs

- `pulse-survey`: Six to ten questions probing the suspected drivers behind each cluster, mixing scales with one open field
- `anonymity-protocol`: The practical anonymity rules, including never reporting any group smaller than five

## Process

1. Write six to ten questions that probe the suspected drivers behind each cluster, mixing rating scales with one open field.
2. Guarantee anonymity in practice: aggregate results so no group smaller than five is ever reported, and collect no identifying fields.
3. Target the survey at the teams and tenure bands where clusters appeared, plus a control group elsewhere for comparison.
4. Pilot the wording with a few trusted employees to confirm the questions read as neutral rather than leading.
