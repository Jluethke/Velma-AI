# Safety Category Definer

Define the safety categories and severity levels to evaluate.

## Inputs

- `system-description`: Description of the AI system under test
- `risk-context`: Deployment context and user population

## Outputs

- `safety-taxonomy`: Categories with definitions and severity levels
- `evaluation-scope`: What is in and out of scope

## Process

1. List the safety categories relevant to the system.
2. Define severity levels for each category.
3. Specify what is in scope and out of scope.
4. Document the regulatory or policy requirements.
