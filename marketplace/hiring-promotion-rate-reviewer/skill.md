# Hiring Promotion Rate Reviewer

Review hiring and promotion rates by demographic group.

## Inputs

- `demographic-dataset`: Demographic data
- `reporting-period`: Time period

## Outputs

- `flow-rates`: Hiring, promotion, and attrition rates by group
- `disparity-flags`: Statistically significant disparities identified

## Process

1. Calculate hiring rates by demographic group at each stage of the pipeline
2. Calculate promotion rates by group and level
3. Calculate voluntary and involuntary attrition by group
4. Test for statistically significant disparities
