# Photo Selection Retouching

Select the best images and retouch them to final quality.

## Inputs

- `raw-captures`: All images from the shoot
- `creative-direction-doc`: The visual direction to match during retouching

## Outputs

- `selected-images`: The curated set of best images with retouching applied
- `shoot-production-record`: Complete record of the shoot (brief, direction, logistics, selects, finals)

## Process

1. Cull the raw images to the best options for each shot on the list.
2. Present selects to the creative lead for approval.
3. Retouch the approved selects (color correction, cropping, cleanup).
4. Export in all required formats and sizes.
5. Produce the selected images and shoot production record.
