# Recip Balance Assessor

Assess the balance of each relationship by comparing the give and receive inventories, mapping the direction and degree of value flow, while accounting for the fact that healthy relationships balance over time rather than in every single exchange.

## Inputs

- `receive-inventory`: What each person gives you
- `receive-frequency-notes`: Frequency and weight of what they give

## Outputs

- `balance-ratios`: For each relationship, a rough reading of balance: roughly even, you give more, or they give more, and by how much
- `flow-direction-map`: The net direction of value flow across the mapped network, showing where you are a net giver and net receiver

## Process

1. For each relationship, compare the two inventories and read the balance qualitatively: roughly even, tilted toward you giving more, or tilted toward them giving more. Avoid false precision, a direction and a rough degree is enough.
2. Judge balance over time, not per exchange. Healthy relationships are not tit-for-tat, they even out across months and years. A relationship that looks lopsided this month may be balanced across the year, so take the longer view.
3. Account for different currencies. One person gives money and the other gives time, and that can be a fair trade. Imbalance is when the total value is lopsided, not when the currencies differ.
4. Map the net direction across the whole set. Seeing where you are a net giver and where a net receiver across the network reveals patterns a single relationship hides, like giving generously to people who cannot reciprocate while under-investing in peers who could.
