# Market Rent Assessor

Assess current market rent for the unit to inform renewal pricing.

## Inputs

- `expiring-leases`: Leases identified for renewal
- `tenant-history`: Payment and compliance history

## Outputs

- `market-rent-estimates`: Market rent for each unit based on comparables
- `renewal-pricing-recommendations`: Recommended rent and terms for each renewal

## Process

1. Collect comparable listings for each unit by size, location, and amenities.
2. Calculate the market rent range from the comparables.
3. Factor in tenant quality and vacancy cost to set the renewal price.
