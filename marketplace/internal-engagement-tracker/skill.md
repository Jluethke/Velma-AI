# Internal Engagement Tracker

Track readership and engagement for internal communications.

## Inputs

- `internal-templates`: The templates being used
- `approval-workflow`: The approval process

## Outputs

- `engagement-metrics`: Open rates, click rates, and read-through rates by communication type and segment
- `internal-communications-plan`: Complete internal communications plan covering audiences, cadence, templates, approvals, and metrics

## Process

1. Track open rates and click rates for each send.
2. Segment the engagement data by audience and communication type.
3. Identify the most and least engaging communication types.
4. Recommend adjustments based on engagement data.
5. Compile the full internal communications plan.
