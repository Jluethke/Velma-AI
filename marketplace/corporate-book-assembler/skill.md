# Corporate Book Assembler

Set up the corporate book with all organizational documents.

## Inputs

- `formation-documents`: Filed formation documents
- `governing-document`: Governing document
- `ein-confirmation`: EIN confirmation

## Outputs

- `corporate-book`: Assembled corporate book
- `initial-resolutions`: Initial board or member resolutions

## Process

1. Compile all formation documents, governing documents, and EIN letter
2. Draft and adopt initial resolutions (officers, fiscal year, bank account)
3. Issue membership certificates or stock certificates
