# Stack Point Selection

Match your target habit to the best stack point: the existing habit that is most natural to pair it with.

## Inputs

- `habit-inventory`: All automatic habits
- `target-habit`: The habit to build

## Outputs

- `stack-formula`: The specific formula: 'After I [existing habit], I will [new habit].' Example: 'After I pour my morning coffee, I will journal for 5 minutes.'
- `stack-compatibility`: Why this stack point works: same location, same energy level, natural transition, no conflicting activity

## Process

1. The stack formula is: 'After I [existing habit], I will [new habit].' The existing habit is the trigger. The new habit is the response.
2. Select the stack point based on compatibility: same physical location (stacking a kitchen habit onto a bedroom habit fails), appropriate energy level (stacking exercise onto the post-lunch slump fails), and no conflicting activity.
3. Make the new habit tiny: the initial version should take 2 minutes or less. 'After I pour coffee, I will meditate' fails if meditation means 20 minutes. 'After I pour coffee, I will take 3 deep breaths' succeeds because it takes 15 seconds.
4. The tiny version is not the final version. It is the entry point. Once the stack is automatic (2-3 weeks), you expand the duration incrementally.
