# Five Change Candidate List

List the five behavior changes you most want to make, stated as specific daily or weekly actions rather than vague goals.

## Inputs

- `current-life-friction`: The parts of daily life that feel hardest or most frustrating right now
- `improvement-wishlist`: Everything you wish you did more of or less of, without filtering

## Outputs

- `five-candidate-changes`: Exactly five behavior changes, each stated as a concrete observable action with a frequency
- `change-effort-notes`: For each change, a note on how hard it has felt to sustain in the past

## Process

1. Write down every behavior you wish you did more of or less of. Do not filter or rank yet. Include changes you have tried and abandoned before.
2. Reduce the list to the five that would matter most to your daily life. State each as an action a camera could record, such as walk thirty minutes after lunch, not get in shape.
3. For each of the five, note how many times you have tried it before and what made it stop. Past friction is data, not failure.
4. Confirm all five are things you could start this week with no new equipment, money, or permission. Changes that require a precondition belong on a later list.
