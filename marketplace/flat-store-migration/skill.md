# Flat Store Migration

Migrate from a deep folder hierarchy to a flat or minimally structured store. Two levels maximum: a top-level category (work, personal, reference, archive) and then items. Everything within each category is flat.

## Inputs

- `organization-time-cost`: Weekly time spent organizing
- `retrieval-success-rate`: Retrieval success rates for common scenarios

## Outputs

- `flat-store-structure`: The new flat structure with at most two levels and clear rules for what goes where
- `migration-plan`: Plan for moving existing files to the flat structure without losing anything

## Process

1. Create the top-level categories. Four is usually enough: active (current projects), reference (things you look up), archive (completed work), and inbox (unsorted new items).
2. Within each category, items are flat. No subfolders. No sub-subfolders. If you need more structure, use naming conventions (project-name_document-type_date) instead of folders.
3. For the migration: do not reorganize everything at once. Move items to the flat store as you touch them. After 90 days, anything you have not touched stays in the old structure (which becomes the archive).
4. The discipline: when you create a new item, put it in the right top-level category and name it well. That is all the organizing you do.
