# Topic Narrower

Narrow a broad research interest into a specific, feasible thesis topic with a clear research question and scope boundary.

## Inputs

- `broad-interest`: The general research area or phenomenon of interest
- `advisor-guidance`: Advisor's suggested directions, constraints, and lab capabilities
- `degree-requirements`: Scope expectations for masters vs doctoral thesis

## Outputs

- `thesis-topic`: Specific thesis topic statement with research question
- `scope-boundary`: Explicit statement of what is included and excluded from the thesis
- `feasibility-assessment`: Assessment of data availability, methods access, timeline fit, and advisor expertise

## Process

1. List subtopics within the broad area and rate each for novelty, feasibility, and interest
2. Read 10-15 recent papers in the top candidates to gauge the state of knowledge
3. Draft candidate research questions and test each for specificity and answerability
4. Assess feasibility against available time, data, methods, and funding
5. Discuss the shortlist with the advisor and converge on the final topic
