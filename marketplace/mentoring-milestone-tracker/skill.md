# Mentoring Milestone Tracker

Track progress on mentoring milestones and provide mid-program check-ins.

## Inputs

- `milestone-schedule`: Timeline of milestones and check-ins
- `signed-agreements`: Mentoring agreements

## Outputs

- `milestone-status`: Status of each pair's progress against milestones
- `mid-program-feedback`: Feedback from mentors and mentees at midpoint

## Process

1. Collect progress updates from each pair at scheduled check-ins
2. Identify pairs falling behind or encountering problems
3. Provide support or mediation for struggling pairs
4. Compile the mid-program status report
