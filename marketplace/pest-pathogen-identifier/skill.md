# Pest Pathogen Identifier

Identify the pest or pathogen from symptoms and field evidence.

## Inputs

- `symptom-report`: Documented symptoms and photos
- `crop-type`: The crop species and variety affected

## Outputs

- `identification-result`: Identified pest or pathogen with confidence level
- `biology-summary`: Life cycle, host range, and conditions favoring the organism

## Process

1. Compare symptoms against known pest and disease references for the crop.
2. Check for diagnostic signs (frass, spores, egg masses, feeding patterns).
3. Submit samples for lab confirmation if field ID is uncertain.
4. Document the identification with supporting evidence.
