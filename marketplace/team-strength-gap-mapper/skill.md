# Team Strength Gap Mapper

Map the team across professional background, problem-solving style, communication style, and experience, then mark where it has depth and where it is uniform. The aim is to see the team as a whole, not to label individuals.

## Inputs

- `team-roster`: The current team members and their backgrounds
- `team-mission`: What the team exists to accomplish and who it serves

## Outputs

- `strength-gap-map`: Where the team is deep and where it is thin or empty across the mapped dimensions
- `uniformity-flags`: Attributes shared by most of the team, marking where it is too uniform

## Process

1. For each team member, note background, problem-solving style, communication style, and relevant experience.
2. Look for clustering: any attribute shared by most of the team is a uniformity flag.
3. Ask, given the team's mission and who it serves, which perspectives are absent.
4. Produce a map showing where the team is deep and where it is thin or empty.
