# Contribution Plan

Set monthly contribution amounts with automatic increases tied to income growth.

## Inputs

- `monthly_income`: Take-home pay
- `account_plan`: Accounts being funded
- `savings_rate_target`: Target savings percentage

## Outputs

- `monthly_contributions`: Dollar amounts per account
- `annual_increase_plan`: Planned contribution increases
- `milestone_targets`: Account balance milestones by year

## Process

1. Calculate starting contribution amounts
2. Prioritize by tax advantage and match
3. Plan annual increases (1-2% of income per year)
4. Set balance milestones for motivation
5. Automate all contributions
