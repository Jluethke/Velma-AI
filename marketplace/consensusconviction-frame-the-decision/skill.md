# Consensusconviction Frame The Decision

Define the decision and its stakes so the test has a clear subject.

## Inputs

- `decision-topic`: The decision to be made.
- `decision-stakes`: What is at stake if it goes wrong.

## Outputs

- `framed-decision`: The decision written as a single clear question.
- `stakes-summary`: What is at stake and who would carry it out.

## Process

1. Write the decision as a single clear question.
2. State what is at stake if the decision goes wrong.
3. Note who would carry out the decision once made.
