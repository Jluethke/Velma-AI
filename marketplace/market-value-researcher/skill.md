# Market Value Researcher

Research your market value before any salary discussion. Your market value is what other employers would pay for your skills, not what you currently earn.

## Inputs

- `role`: The role being negotiated
- `qualifications`: Your skills, experience, and differentiators

## Outputs

- `market-range`: The salary range for this role in this market: 25th percentile, median, 75th percentile, and 90th percentile, with sources
- `positioning-rationale`: Where you fall in the range based on your specific qualifications, with the evidence

## Process

1. Research salary data from multiple sources: industry surveys, job postings with salary ranges, recruiter conversations, peer data, and public salary databases.
2. Identify the range for your specific role, location, industry, and company size. National averages are too broad.
3. Position yourself in the range based on your differentiators: years of experience, specialized skills, certifications, track record, and the specific value you bring.
4. Your current salary is irrelevant to your market value. Do not anchor to what you earn now.
