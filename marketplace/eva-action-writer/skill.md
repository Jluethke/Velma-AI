# Eva Action Writer

Write the action list for leaks and lumps.

## Inputs

- `cancellable-leaks`: The subscriptions to cancel
- `monthly-set-aside`: The sinking-fund amounts to open

## Outputs

- `velocity-report`: The record of leaks, lumps, and the monthly change
- `expense-action-list`: The dated list of cancellations and funds to open

## Process

1. List the subscriptions to cancel with their annual savings.
2. List the sinking funds to open with their monthly amount.
3. Produce the dated action list and the expected monthly change.
