# Root Cause Identification

Identify the specific causes: which aspects of your work or life are driving each dimension.

## Inputs

- `exhaustion-score`: Exhaustion level
- `detachment-score`: Detachment level
- `inefficacy-score`: Inefficacy level

## Outputs

- `exhaustion-causes`: Specific causes: overwork, insufficient recovery, emotional labor, unclear boundaries, chronic stress
- `detachment-causes`: Specific causes: values mismatch, toxic environment, meaningless tasks, lack of autonomy
- `inefficacy-causes`: Specific causes: no feedback, moving goalposts, perfectionism, imposter syndrome, genuinely poor fit

## Process

1. For exhaustion: list everything that drains energy. Not just work tasks, but emotional labor, commute, caregiving, financial stress. Burnout is not just from work; it is from total demand exceeding total capacity.
2. For detachment: identify what you used to care about that you no longer do. The delta reveals the cause. If you used to care about the team's success and now feel indifferent, something changed in the team or in how your contribution is valued.
3. For inefficacy: separate fact from feeling. Are you actually ineffective (performance data is poor), or do you feel ineffective despite evidence to the contrary (imposter syndrome)? The solutions are different.
4. The causes often cluster: overwork (exhaustion) leads to cynicism (detachment) which leads to feeling like nothing matters (inefficacy). Addressing the upstream cause (overwork) often resolves the downstream effects.
