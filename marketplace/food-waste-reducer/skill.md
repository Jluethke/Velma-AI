# Food Waste Reducer

Reduce food waste through inventory management, FIFO rotation, and leftover transformation.

## Inputs

- `waste_estimate`: Current waste level
- `common_waste_items`: What gets thrown away most
- `household_habits`: Cooking and eating patterns

## Outputs

- `waste_reduction_plan`: Specific actions to reduce waste
- `leftover_recipes`: Ways to use common leftovers
- `inventory_system`: Simple system to track what needs using

## Process

1. Identify top wasted items and why
2. Implement first-in-first-out storage
3. Plan leftover transformation recipes
4. Set up weekly fridge audit habit
5. Track waste reduction over time
