# Small Claims Case Evaluation

Evaluate whether a dispute belongs in small claims court and estimate likelihood of success.

## Inputs

- `dispute_description`: What happened and what is owed
- `amount_claimed`: Dollar amount being sought
- `state_of_filing`: State where filing
- `evidence_available`: What proof exists

## Outputs

- `viability_assessment`: Whether to proceed or not
- `jurisdictional_fit`: Correct court and dollar limit check
- `strength_rating`: Case strength rating with reasoning

## Process

1. Verify amount is within state small claims limit
2. Confirm correct jurisdiction
3. Assess available evidence quality
4. Check statute of limitations
5. Rate case strength honestly
