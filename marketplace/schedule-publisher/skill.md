# Schedule Publisher

Publish the schedule and communicate to the floor.

## Inputs

- `optimized-schedule`: Final schedule
- `schedule-kpis`: Schedule performance metrics

## Outputs

- `published-schedule`: Production schedule distributed to all stakeholders
- `production-schedule-report`: Schedule report with KPIs and exception list

## Process

1. Format the schedule for each audience (shop floor, materials, shipping)
2. Distribute work orders to production supervisors
3. Communicate material pull signals to the warehouse
4. Compile the schedule report with KPIs and risk items
