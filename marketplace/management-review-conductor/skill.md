# Management Review Conductor

Conduct the management review and compile the QMS manual.

## Inputs

- `audit-schedule`: Audit program
- `document-control-system`: Document control system

## Outputs

- `management-review-record`: Management review minutes and decisions
- `qms-manual`: Complete quality management system manual

## Process

1. Prepare management review inputs (audit results, customer feedback, process performance, risks)
2. Conduct the review with top management
3. Record decisions and action items (resource needs, improvements, policy changes)
4. Compile the QMS manual referencing all procedures and the process interaction diagram
