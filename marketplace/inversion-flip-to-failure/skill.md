# Inversion Flip To Failure

Restate the goal as its opposite: how would you guarantee this fails?

## Inputs

- `goal-definition`: The goal written as a single clear sentence.
- `success-markers`: The signs that would tell you the outcome was reached.

## Outputs

- `failure-question`: The goal rewritten as a question about causing failure.
- `failure-frame`: The failure stated as something you are trying to produce.

## Process

1. Rewrite the goal as a question about causing the worst result.
2. Confirm the failure question is the true inverse of the stated goal.
3. Frame the failure as something you are actively trying to produce.
