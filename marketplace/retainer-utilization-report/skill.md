# Retainer Utilization Report

Produce the monthly utilization report for the client.

## Inputs

- `utilization-data`: Hours and utilization metrics
- `work-log`: Itemized work log

## Outputs

- `monthly-report`: The utilization report showing hours used, work performed, and hours remaining
- `recommendations`: Suggestions for adjusting the retainer based on utilization patterns

## Process

1. Summarize the hours used by category or project area.
2. Compare utilization to the prior three months to identify trends.
3. If consistently over or under the allocation, recommend an adjustment.
4. Include a brief narrative of the work performed and any upcoming priorities.
