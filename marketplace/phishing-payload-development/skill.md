# Phishing Payload Development

Develop phishing payloads and pretexts for the authorized simulation campaign.

## Inputs

- `employee-profiles`: Target personnel and their roles
- `infrastructure-intel`: Technology context for realistic payloads

## Outputs

- `phishing-campaign-package`: Prepared phishing emails, landing pages, and payloads
- `pretext-scenarios`: Social engineering scenarios tailored to the target

## Process

1. Develop phishing pretexts based on the target's business context
2. Create landing pages that mimic internal portals for credential harvesting
3. Prepare payloads that demonstrate access without causing harm
4. Test the campaign against email security controls in a pre-flight check
