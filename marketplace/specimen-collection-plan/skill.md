# Specimen Collection Plan

Plan specimen collection with preservation methods, labeling standards, chain of custody, and storage requirements.

## Inputs

- `inventory-protocol`: What will be collected and how
- `permits-needed`: Legal constraints on collection

## Outputs

- `collection-protocol`: Collection methods, preservation, labeling, and storage per taxon
- `voucher-standards`: Voucher specimen preparation and museum deposition plan
- `field-kit-list`: Equipment, preservatives, and consumables needed

## Process

1. Match each taxon to an appropriate collection and preservation method
2. Define labeling fields (date, coordinates, collector, habitat notes)
3. Specify storage conditions and chain of custody
4. Plan voucher specimen preparation for museum deposition
