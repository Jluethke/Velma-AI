# Subcontractor Needs Assessment

Identify the skills and capacity needed from a subcontractor.

## Inputs

- `project-requirements`: The project scope and deliverables requiring outside help
- `skill-gaps`: Skills or capacity you lack for this project

## Outputs

- `sub-role-definition`: Description of the role, skills, deliverables, and time commitment needed
- `budget-allocation`: Budget available for the subcontractor (rate range and total hours)

## Process

1. List the specific tasks or deliverables the subcontractor will handle.
2. Define the required skills, experience level, and any tool proficiency.
3. Estimate the hours and timeline for the subcontracted work.
4. Set the budget range based on market rates and your project margin.
