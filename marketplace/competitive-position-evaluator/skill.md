# Competitive Position Evaluator

Evaluate the competitive landscape for this deal.

## Inputs

- `deal-description`: Deal context and requirements
- `bant-assessment`: BANT scores for the deal

## Outputs

- `competitive-assessment`: Position relative to known competitors
- `differentiation-points`: Where the offering is stronger or weaker

## Process

1. List the known competitors in this deal.
2. Assess the prospect's evaluation criteria.
3. Map strengths and weaknesses against each competitor.
4. Identify the primary differentiation points.
