# Stack Selection Decision

Choose your target skill stack by weighing rarity against market value and personal interest.

## Inputs

- `combination-scores`: 3-skill combinations ranked by rarity
- `market-value-notes`: Where each top combination is valuable

## Outputs

- `chosen-stack`: The 3 skills you will build your identity around
- `stack-rationale`: Why this combination is rare, valuable, and worth your time

## Process

1. Eliminate any combination where one of the skills bores you. You will not sustain practice in something you find tedious.
2. Rank the remaining combinations by: rarity times market value times personal interest. All three matter.
3. Select the top combination. If two are close, pick the one where you have the most headroom for improvement (the weakest skill has the most upside).
4. Write one paragraph explaining why this stack is rare, where it is valuable, and what you will be able to do that most people cannot.
