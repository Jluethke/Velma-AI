# Deliberate Constraint Selection

Choose the constraints you are willing to own in public, since a stated constraint is a promise.

## Inputs

- `tradeoff-list`: The tradeoffs the product makes
- `constraint-candidates`: Tradeoffs that could become positioning

## Outputs

- `owned-constraints`: The constraints you will state publicly and stand behind
- `rejected-constraints`: Candidates you chose not to state, with the reason

## Process

1. For each candidate, ask whether you will still honor it in a year, since a stated constraint is a promise.
2. Keep the constraints that reflect a real belief about how the job should be done.
3. Reject candidates that are merely temporary or that you would abandon for a big enough deal.
4. Record why each rejected candidate was dropped, so the decision is not relitigated every quarter.
