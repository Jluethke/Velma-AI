# Positioning Opportunity Mapping

Map the top themes to the things you do differently, and name the proof each claim will need.

## Inputs

- `ranked-themes`: Themes ordered by score
- `severity-scores`: Per-theme severity ratings

## Outputs

- `positioning-openings`: For each top theme, the opening it creates for your positioning
- `proof-requirements`: The evidence each positioning claim must carry to be believed

## Process

1. For each top theme, state plainly whether you actually solve that complaint better, since claiming one you do not solve backfires.
2. Write the opening as a contrast: the market hits this problem, and here is how your approach avoids it.
3. For each opening, name the proof a skeptical buyer would demand, such as a metric, a reference, or a demo moment.
4. Drop any opening where you cannot supply the proof, and keep it on a list for product to close later.
