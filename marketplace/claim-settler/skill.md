# Claim Settler

Settle the claim by negotiating the payout, issuing payment, and closing the file.

## Inputs

- `liability-determination`: Final liability determination and applicable coverage
- `adjusted-reserve`: Updated reserve amount

## Outputs

- `settlement-agreement`: The agreed settlement amount and terms, including any releases signed
- `claims-adjudication-record`: Complete adjudication record for the closed claim

## Process

1. Present the settlement offer to the claimant or their representative.
2. Negotiate the final settlement amount if the initial offer is disputed.
3. Issue the payment and obtain a signed release where applicable.
4. Close the claim file and archive the adjudication record.
