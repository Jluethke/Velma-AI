# Handyman Materials Tracker

Track materials and time per job for accurate costing and future estimating.

## Inputs

- `job-pipeline`: Active and completed jobs
- `service-categories`: Expected materials per job type

## Outputs

- `job-cost-log`: Actual materials, time, and cost per job
- `materials-markup-report`: Materials cost with markup for invoicing

## Process

1. Record materials purchased per job with receipts.
2. Track labor time from arrival to departure.
3. Apply the materials markup percentage for invoicing.
4. Compare actual costs to the estimate for profitability tracking.
