# Revpar Performance Monitor

Monitor RevPAR, occupancy, and ADR against budget and forecast.

## Inputs

- `rate-strategy`: Active rate strategy
- `actual-performance-data`: Daily revenue, occupancy, and ADR actuals

## Outputs

- `performance-dashboard`: RevPAR, occupancy, and ADR vs budget
- `revenue-management-report`: Final revenue management report with recommendations

## Process

1. Calculate daily RevPAR, ADR, and occupancy.
2. Compare actuals against budget and forecast.
3. Identify underperforming segments and dates.
4. Recommend strategy adjustments for the next period.
