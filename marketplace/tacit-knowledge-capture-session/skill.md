# Tacit Knowledge Capture Session

Run structured capture sessions to document the tacit knowledge as it is transferred. The goal is not perfect documentation but enough context that the receiver (or a future receiver) can reconstruct the judgment.

## Inputs

- `tacit-knowledge-list`: The tacit knowledge to capture
- `apprenticeship-schedule`: The transfer stages and timeline

## Outputs

- `captured-knowledge-documents`: For each piece of tacit knowledge: the situation (when this knowledge applies), the judgment (what to do), the reasoning (why this is the right call), and the exceptions (when this judgment does not apply)
- `decision-playbooks`: Playbooks for the most critical judgment calls: 'When you see X, do Y, because Z. Unless W, then do V.'

## Process

1. Run capture sessions during stage 1 (Observe) and stage 2 (Assist). After each real-world situation, pause and document: what happened, what decision was made, why, and what alternatives were considered.
2. Use the format: Situation (when does this knowledge apply), Judgment (what to do), Reasoning (why this is correct), Exceptions (when this does not apply). This mirrors the SBI format but for knowledge instead of feedback.
3. For the most critical judgment calls, build decision playbooks: 'When you see [specific signal], do [specific action], because [specific reasoning]. Unless [specific exception], then do [alternative action].'
4. The documents are reference material, not replacements for the apprenticeship. Reading about judgment is not the same as developing judgment. The documents support the transfer; they do not replace it.
