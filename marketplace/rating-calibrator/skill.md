# Rating Calibrator

Calibrate ratings across teams to ensure consistency.

## Inputs

- `self-assessments`: Self-assessments
- `peer-feedback`: Peer and upward feedback

## Outputs

- `calibrated-ratings`: Final calibrated ratings per employee
- `calibration-notes`: Notes from calibration discussions

## Process

1. Have managers propose initial ratings with evidence
2. Review the distribution across teams for consistency
3. Discuss borderline cases with supporting evidence
4. Finalize the calibrated ratings
