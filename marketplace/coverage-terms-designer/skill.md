# Coverage Terms Designer

Define the coverage terms, conditions, exclusions, and endorsements for the product.

## Inputs

- `market-assessment`: Market demand and competitive analysis
- `product-requirements`: High-level product requirements

## Outputs

- `coverage-specification`: Detailed coverage specification including insuring agreements, conditions, exclusions, and definitions
- `endorsement-schedule`: List of optional endorsements with their coverage modifications

## Process

1. Draft the insuring agreements defining what is covered.
2. Define the conditions, duties after loss, and claim reporting requirements.
3. Specify the exclusions and any buy-back endorsement options.
4. Review the specification against regulatory requirements for the filing states.
