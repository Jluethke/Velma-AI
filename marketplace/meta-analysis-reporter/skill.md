# Meta Analysis Reporter

Write the meta-analysis results section with all required statistics, figures, and sensitivity checks.

## Inputs

- `pooled-result`: pooled estimate and CI
- `heterogeneity-report`: heterogeneity statistics
- `forest-plot`: forest plot figure
- `bias-test-results`: publication bias test outcomes

## Outputs

- `meta-analysis-report`: complete results narrative with embedded figures and tables
- `grade-assessment`: GRADE certainty of evidence rating for the pooled estimate

## Process

1. Narrate the pooled result, heterogeneity, and leave-one-out sensitivity in plain language
2. Present the forest plot, funnel plot, and any subgroup analyses as figures
3. Report publication bias tests and their implications
4. Rate the certainty of evidence using GRADE (risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias)
