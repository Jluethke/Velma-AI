# Shared Decision Facilitator

Discuss treatment options with the patient using shared decision-making.

## Inputs

- `intervention-list`: Selected interventions
- `long-term-goals`: Long-term treatment goals

## Outputs

- `patient-preferences`: Patient's stated preferences and concerns
- `agreed-plan`: Mutually agreed treatment approach

## Process

1. Present the options with risks, benefits, and alternatives.
2. Elicit the patient's values, preferences, and concerns.
3. Reach a shared decision and document consent.
