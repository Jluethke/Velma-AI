# Restoration Crew Dispatcher

Dispatch repair crews and manage the restoration sequence.

## Inputs

- `outage-scope`: Outage scope and affected infrastructure
- `initial-assessment`: Damage assessment and cause

## Outputs

- `dispatch-plan`: Crew assignments, equipment needs, and restoration sequence
- `restoration-timeline`: Estimated restoration timeline by area or feeder

## Process

1. Prioritize restoration: critical facilities (hospitals, water treatment, emergency services) first.
2. Assign repair crews based on proximity, skillset, and equipment availability.
3. Sequence the restoration to maximize customers restored per switching operation.
4. Communicate the restoration timeline to dispatch, customer service, and public information.
