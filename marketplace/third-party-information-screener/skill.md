# Third Party Information Screener

Screen the third party for sanctions, adverse media, and regulatory issues.

## Inputs

- `third-party-name`: Third party name and identifiers
- `risk-criteria`: Risk criteria for screening

## Outputs

- `screening-results`: Sanctions, PEP, adverse media, and litigation screening results
- `red-flag-summary`: Summary of any red flags identified

## Process

1. Screen against sanctions and PEP lists.
2. Search for adverse media, litigation, and regulatory actions.
3. Review corporate registry filings and ownership structure.
4. Summarize any red flags.
