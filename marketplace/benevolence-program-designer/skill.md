# Benevolence Program Designer

Define the benevolence program: eligibility criteria, assistance types, funding source, and limits.

## Inputs

- `program-budget`: Annual or periodic budget allocated to benevolence
- `community-needs`: Common needs in the community (rent, utilities, food, medical, transportation)

## Outputs

- `program-policy`: Eligibility rules, assistance types, per-request and annual limits, and approval authority
- `application-form`: The form applicants complete to request assistance

## Process

1. Define who can apply: members, regular attendees, community residents, or referrals from partner organizations.
2. List the assistance types: rent or mortgage, utilities, food, medical bills, transportation, and emergency shelter.
3. Set the limits: maximum per request, maximum per household per year, and total program budget.
4. Define the approval process: who reviews, what documentation is required, and the approval authority (pastor, committee, deacons).
