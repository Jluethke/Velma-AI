# Stakeholder Roster Builder

List every person who can affect or is affected by the outcome. Include the ones who are not in the room but could block the result from outside it.

## Inputs

- `initiative-description`: The project, proposal, or initiative you are mapping stakeholders for
- `known-stakeholders`: The people you already know are involved

## Outputs

- `full-roster`: Every person who can affect the outcome, including hidden influencers and potential blockers
- `hidden-stakeholder-notes`: People not in the obvious list who could affect the outcome from outside the project

## Process

1. List every person in the formal approval chain: who signs off, who funds, who manages the team doing the work.
2. List every person affected by the outcome: whose workflow changes, whose budget is impacted, whose team is restructured.
3. List hidden stakeholders: the executive's trusted advisor, the long-tenured employee whose opinion carries informal weight, the customer who will be vocal.
4. For each person, write one sentence about their relationship to the initiative. If you cannot, you do not know enough about them yet.
