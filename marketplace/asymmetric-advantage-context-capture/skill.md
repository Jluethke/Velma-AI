# Asymmetric Advantage Context Capture

Capture your business, your resources, and the competitor field so the advantage search rests on facts. Honest inputs matter most here, since the method fails if you overrate yourself or underrate rivals.

## Inputs

- `your-business-description`: What your business does and how
- `available-resources`: The people, money, time, and access you actually have
- `main-competitors`: The rivals you compete with for the same customers

## Outputs

- `business-context`: A factual profile of your business
- `competitor-set`: The named competitors
- `resource-summary`: Your real resources and access

## Process

1. Describe your business plainly: what you do and for whom.
2. List the resources you truly have, not the ones you wish you had.
3. Name the competitors who go after the same customers.
4. Keep it factual. Judgments about strength and weakness come in the next steps.
