# Blue Ocean Factor Mapping

Map the factors the whole industry competes on and where the current offering sits on each. This is the starting value curve, the shape you are trying to change.

## Inputs

- `industry-description`: The industry and how it competes today
- `current-offering`: The product or service as it stands now
- `target-customer`: Who the offering is for, including non-customers worth reaching

## Outputs

- `competition-factors`: The factors the industry competes on, one per line
- `current-factor-levels`: Where today's offering sits on each factor, high, medium, or low

## Process

1. List every factor the industry sells on: price, features, service levels, convenience, and so on.
2. For each factor, mark where the current offering sits: high, medium, or low.
3. Include factors customers quietly dislike but the industry provides anyway.
4. This set of factors and levels is the current value curve. Name it as the baseline.
