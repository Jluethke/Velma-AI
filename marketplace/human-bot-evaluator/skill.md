# Human Bot Evaluator

Conduct human evaluation of response quality and naturalness.

## Inputs

- `automated-test-results`: Automated test results
- `test-scenarios`: Test scenarios

## Outputs

- `human-eval-results`: Human ratings for response relevance, naturalness, helpfulness, and tone
- `bot-testing-report`: Complete testing report with automated metrics, human evaluation, and recommendations

## Process

1. Select a sample of conversations (mix of successful and failed) for human review.
2. Rate each response on relevance, naturalness, helpfulness, and tone consistency.
3. Identify the responses that need rewriting or flow changes.
4. Compile the testing report with automated metrics, human ratings, and prioritized fixes.
