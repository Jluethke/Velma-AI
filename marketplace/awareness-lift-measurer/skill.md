# Awareness Lift Measurer

Measure the awareness lift from the campaign.

## Inputs

- `mid-campaign-metrics`: Campaign delivery data
- `audience-definition`: Baseline audience context

## Outputs

- `awareness-lift-data`: Pre/post awareness metrics (survey, search volume, mentions)
- `awareness-campaign-report`: Complete campaign report with results and learnings

## Process

1. Compare pre-campaign and post-campaign awareness measures
2. Analyze brand search volume and social mention trends
3. Produce the campaign report
