# Interview Panel Scheduler

Design the interview panel schedule assigning competencies to interviewers.

## Inputs

- `competency-list`: Competencies to assess
- `calibration-materials`: Calibration results showing interviewer readiness

## Outputs

- `panel-schedule`: Interview panel assignments with competencies per interviewer
- `time-allocation`: Time allocated per interview round

## Process

1. Assign competencies to interviewers based on expertise
2. Design the interview round structure and timing
3. Ensure each competency is assessed by at least one interviewer
4. Schedule buffer time for debrief between rounds
