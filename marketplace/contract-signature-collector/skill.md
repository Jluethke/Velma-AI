# Contract Signature Collector

Send the contract for counterparty signature and file the executed copy.

## Inputs

- `reviewed-contract`: The reviewed and approved contract
- `review-notes`: Any conditions or notes from legal review

## Outputs

- `executed-contract`: The fully signed contract
- `contract-record`: Filing record with contract number, parties, dates, and storage location

## Process

1. Send the contract to the counterparty for signature via the e-signature platform.
2. Track signature status and send reminders as needed.
3. Collect the executed contract with all signatures.
4. File the executed contract and produce the contract record.
