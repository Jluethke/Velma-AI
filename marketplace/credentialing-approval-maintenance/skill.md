# Credentialing Approval Maintenance

Record approvals and maintain the re-credentialing calendar.

## Inputs

- `enrollment-tracker`: Status of each payer application

## Outputs

- `credentialing-record`: Complete credentialing record with all payer participation dates and contract terms
- `recredentialing-calendar`: Calendar of upcoming re-credentialing dates and document expiration dates

## Process

1. Record the effective date and participation terms for each approved payer.
2. Set up the re-credentialing calendar (typically every 2 to 3 years per payer).
3. Track document expiration dates (licenses, malpractice insurance, DEA) and set renewal reminders.
4. Maintain the master credentialing file for audits and re-credentialing.
