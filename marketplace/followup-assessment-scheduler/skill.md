# Followup Assessment Scheduler

Schedule follow-up assessments to track progress.

## Inputs

- `treatment-plan-document`: Formal treatment plan
- `patient-preferences`: Patient scheduling preferences

## Outputs

- `followup-schedule`: Dates and types of follow-up visits
- `monitoring-checklist`: What to assess at each follow-up

## Process

1. Determine follow-up intervals based on the condition and treatment.
2. Schedule appointments and any required lab work.
3. Provide the patient with the schedule and preparation instructions.
