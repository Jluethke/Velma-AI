# Insurance Market Researcher

Research the market need and competitive landscape for a new insurance product.

## Inputs

- `product-concept`: The initial product idea and target market
- `market-data`: Industry data, competitor filings, and customer feedback supporting the product need

## Outputs

- `market-assessment`: Assessment of market demand, competitive positioning, and target customer profile
- `product-requirements`: High-level requirements for coverage, pricing targets, and distribution channel

## Process

1. Analyze the target market size and growth trends.
2. Review competitor products, pricing, and coverage gaps.
3. Gather input from agents and underwriters on customer needs.
4. Define the product requirements including target segment and distribution.
