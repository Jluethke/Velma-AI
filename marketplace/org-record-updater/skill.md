# Org Record Updater

Update organizational records to reflect the departure.

## Inputs

- `employee-details`: Employee information
- `final-pay-record`: Final pay processed

## Outputs

- `updated-records`: Confirmation of updated org charts, directories, and HRIS
- `team-notification`: Communication sent to the team about the transition

## Process

1. Update the HRIS system with the termination date and reason
2. Remove from org charts and internal directories
3. Notify the team about the transition and interim arrangements
4. Archive the employee's files per retention policy
