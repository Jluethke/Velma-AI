# Job Portfolio Prioritizer

If multiple jobs emerged, prioritize which job to serve first. Not all jobs are equally valuable or equally served by your current offering.

## Inputs

- `job-statements`: All jobs uncovered
- `competitive-advantage-by-job`: Where you win for each job
- `job-based-messaging`: Messaging for each job

## Outputs

- `job-priority-ranking`: Jobs ranked by: market size, competitive advantage, alignment with your strengths, and profitability
- `jtbd-discovery-report`: Complete report: the jobs, the forces, the real competitors, the messaging, and the priority ranking

## Process

1. Score each job on four dimensions: how many people have this job (market size), how well you serve it vs alternatives (competitive advantage), how well it aligns with what you are building (strategic fit), and how profitable the customers hiring for this job are.
2. The primary job is the one you lead with in positioning. Secondary jobs expand the market later.
3. Produce the final report: the jobs, the forces, the real competitors, the messaging per job, and the priority ranking. This report replaces traditional competitive analysis because it is grounded in customer behavior, not in product feature comparisons.
4. Plan to re-run the discovery annually or when a major product change occurs. Jobs shift as markets evolve.
