# Graceful Disqualification Protocol

When a prospect hits a disqualification criterion, exit gracefully so the door stays open for the future.

## Inputs

- `disqualification-questions`: The questions asked
- `disqualification-criteria`: The criteria to apply

## Outputs

- `exit-scripts`: Professional language for disqualifying a prospect that preserves the relationship: 'Based on what you have shared, I do not think we are the right fit right now because [specific reason]'
- `nurture-handoff`: How to hand disqualified prospects to a nurture sequence so they can re-enter when their situation changes

## Process

1. Be honest and specific: 'Based on what you have shared, I think [competitor/alternative] might actually be a better fit for your situation because [specific reason]. I would rather send you in the right direction than try to make something work that will not.' This builds trust.
2. Never disappear or go silent. An unexplained disappearance burns the bridge. A clear, honest disqualification leaves the bridge intact.
3. Add disqualified prospects to a low-touch nurture sequence. Their situation may change (they grow, they get budget, they fire the competitor). When it does, they should hear from you.
4. Track disqualification rates. If you are disqualifying more than 50% of first conversations, your lead generation is targeting too broadly. If less than 10%, your criteria may be too loose.
