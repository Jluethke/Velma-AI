# Audience Behavior Analyzer

Analyze audience demographics and behavior patterns.

## Inputs

- `raw-performance-data`: The performance data including audience metrics
- `content-rankings`: Content performance rankings

## Outputs

- `audience-insights`: Demographics, active times, content preferences, and engagement patterns of the audience
- `audience-growth-analysis`: Analysis of follower growth sources and churn

## Process

1. Review audience demographics (age, gender, location, interests) on each platform.
2. Identify peak activity times and days for the audience.
3. Analyze which content types and topics drive the most audience interaction.
4. Track follower growth and identify where new followers are coming from.
