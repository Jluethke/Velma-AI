# Privacy Training Effectiveness Review

Review the training program effectiveness and plan improvements.

## Inputs

- `completion-tracking`: Completion and assessment data
- `deployment-schedule`: The rollout schedule

## Outputs

- `effectiveness-report`: Report on training completion, assessment scores, and knowledge gaps
- `privacy-training-program-document`: The complete privacy training program document for compliance records

## Process

1. Analyze completion rates by audience and department.
2. Review assessment scores to identify common knowledge gaps.
3. Gather participant feedback on content relevance and format.
4. Recommend improvements for the next training cycle.
5. Produce the privacy training program document for compliance archives.
