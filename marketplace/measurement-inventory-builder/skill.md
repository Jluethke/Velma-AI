# Measurement Inventory Builder

List every metric currently tracked, across all systems. Include formal metrics (dashboards, reports) and informal ones (the number someone checks in a spreadsheet, the thing you mentally track). The goal is a complete picture of what is being measured.

## Inputs

- `scope-description`: The team, project, business, or personal area to audit
- `data-sources`: All systems, tools, dashboards, and spreadsheets that contain metrics

## Outputs

- `measurement-inventory`: Every metric tracked: name, source, update frequency, collection method, and collection cost (time or money to maintain)
- `total-measurement-cost`: The aggregate cost (time and money) of maintaining all current measurements

## Process

1. Walk through every dashboard, report, spreadsheet, and tracking tool. List every metric, KPI, or number that is tracked on a recurring basis.
2. For each metric, estimate the collection cost: how much time or money does it take to keep this metric updated? Include the time to collect, clean, calculate, and review the data.
3. Sum the total measurement cost. This number is usually surprisingly high and motivates the audit.
