# Vendor Exit Planner

Plan and execute vendor exits when needed.

## Inputs

- `monitoring-alerts`: Alerts and findings
- `compliance-status`: Vendor compliance status

## Outputs

- `exit-plan`: Plan for transitioning away from the vendor
- `vendor-risk-report`: Complete vendor risk management report

## Process

1. For vendors requiring exit, identify replacement options.
2. Plan the data migration and service transition.
3. Execute the contractual termination process.
4. Compile the vendor risk management report.
