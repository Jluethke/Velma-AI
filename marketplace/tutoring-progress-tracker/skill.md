# Tutoring Progress Tracker

Track client progress with session notes, assessments, and milestone tracking.

## Inputs

- `initial-plan`: Lesson or coaching plan with milestones
- `session-calendar`: Completed session records

## Outputs

- `progress-record`: Running record of session notes, scores, and milestone completions
- `progress-report`: Client-facing progress report summarizing growth and next steps

## Process

1. Record session notes after each meeting (topics covered, homework assigned, observations).
2. Track assessment scores and milestone completions against the plan.
3. Adjust the lesson plan based on the client's pace and emerging needs.
4. Generate progress reports for the client (and parents, if applicable) at regular intervals.
