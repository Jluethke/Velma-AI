# Reserve And Liability Determiner

Determine liability and set the reserve based on the investigation findings.

## Inputs

- `investigation-findings`: Evidence and findings from the investigation
- `liability-assessment`: Assessment of liability and damages

## Outputs

- `liability-determination`: Final liability determination with rationale and applicable coverage
- `adjusted-reserve`: Updated reserve amount reflecting the investigation findings

## Process

1. Evaluate the evidence to determine fault, causation, and the extent of damages.
2. Apply policy terms (deductibles, limits, exclusions, conditions) to the determination.
3. Update the reserve to reflect the expected payout.
4. Document the liability determination and rationale in the claim file.
