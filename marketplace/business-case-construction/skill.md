# Business Case Construction

Build the business case in the champion's own language and metrics, so they can present it as theirs.

## Inputs

- `champion-motivation`: What the champion gains
- `risk-concerns`: What the champion fears

## Outputs

- `business-case`: The case for change written in the organization's own terms
- `proof-points`: The evidence that backs each claim in the case

## Process

1. Frame the case around the champion's metrics and the organization's stated goals, not your feature list.
2. Quantify the cost of staying put and the gain from changing, in numbers the finance team will accept.
3. Address each risk concern directly, so the champion is not blindsided when a skeptic raises it.
4. Attach a proof point to every claim, since an unproven claim is a liability the champion will not carry.
