# Site Formation Reconstructor

Reconstruct the processes that formed and transformed the archaeological deposits.

## Inputs

- `harris-matrix`: Stratigraphic relationships
- `activity-area-interpretation`: Spatial analysis results

## Outputs

- `formation-narrative`: Narrative of how the site was formed, occupied, abandoned, and transformed
- `taphonomic-assessment`: Assessment of post-depositional disturbance and its effect on the record

## Process

1. Classify each context as primary deposition, secondary fill, or natural accumulation
2. Identify evidence of post-depositional processes (bioturbation, plowing, erosion, looting)
3. Assess the integrity of each phase based on artifact refitting, fragmentation, and weathering
4. Construct the formation narrative from earliest to latest event
