# Statement Of Work Writer

Write the statement of work for the engagement.

## Inputs

- `engagement-model`: The engagement structure and billing model
- `resource-plan`: Team and allocation details

## Outputs

- `statement-of-work`: The SOW covering scope, deliverables, timeline, team, pricing, and terms
- `acceptance-criteria`: Criteria for accepting each deliverable

## Process

1. Write the background and objectives section in the client's language.
2. Define the scope of work with explicit inclusions and exclusions.
3. List each deliverable with its acceptance criteria and due date.
4. Specify the team, their roles, and the governance structure (who approves what).
5. Include the pricing, payment schedule, assumptions, and change management process.
