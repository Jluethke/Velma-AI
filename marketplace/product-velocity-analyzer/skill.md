# Product Velocity Analyzer

Analyze product mix and velocity using ABC classification.

## Inputs

- `product-catalog`: Full list of products with SKU data
- `order-history`: Historical order and movement data

## Outputs

- `abc-classification`: Products classified into A, B, and C velocity tiers
- `movement-patterns`: Frequency and volume patterns for each SKU

## Process

1. Calculate order frequency and volume for each SKU
2. Rank products by movement velocity
3. Classify into A (high), B (medium), and C (low) tiers
4. Document seasonal or cyclical movement patterns
