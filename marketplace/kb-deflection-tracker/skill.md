# Kb Deflection Tracker

Set up tracking for knowledge base deflection rate and article performance.

## Inputs

- `organized-kb`: Published knowledge base
- `taxonomy-map`: Article organization structure

## Outputs

- `kb-plan`: Knowledge base maintenance and review plan
- `deflection-metrics`: Baseline deflection rate and per-article metrics

## Process

1. Define the deflection metric (searches that do not result in a ticket).
2. Set up tracking for article views, helpfulness votes, and follow-on tickets.
3. Establish the review schedule for article freshness.
4. Produce the knowledge base plan.
