# Counter Argument Preemption

Anticipate the reader's objections and address them before the reader raises them.

## Inputs

- `argument-structure`: Your arguments
- `audience`: Who will read this

## Outputs

- `likely-objections`: The 2-3 objections the reader is most likely to raise
- `preemptive-responses`: For each objection: acknowledgment, response, and weakening evidence

## Process

1. Put yourself in the reader's position. Common objections: risk, cost, alternatives considered, evidence quality.
2. Address objections in the document, not in Q&A.
3. Acknowledging objections builds credibility. A document that ignores obvious risks looks naive.
4. If an objection is valid and not fully addressable, say so honestly.
