# Time To Productivity Re Measurer

After implementing the fixes, measure the time-to-productivity for the next 3-5 hires and compare against the baseline. The target is a 25% reduction in the median time-to-productivity. If the reduction is less than 25%, either the biggest bottleneck was not actually the biggest, or the fix was insufficient.

## Inputs

- `fix-implementations`: The bottleneck fixes that were implemented
- `revised-onboarding-plan`: The updated onboarding process

## Outputs

- `post-fix-measurements`: Time-to-productivity for the first 3-5 hires under the new process, compared against the baseline
- `improvement-assessment`: Whether the 25% reduction target was met, and if not, what the next intervention should be

## Process

1. For each new hire under the revised process, track their milestone dates and time-to-full-productivity using the same definition as the baseline.
2. After 3-5 hires, calculate the new median time-to-productivity and compare against the baseline.
3. If the improvement is 25% or more, the intervention worked. Document it and move to the next bottleneck.
4. If the improvement is less than 25%, interview the recent hires: what slowed them down? The answer will reveal the next bottleneck to fix.
