# Marine Ecosystem Reporter

Compile survey results into a structured marine ecosystem report with maps, data tables, statistical summaries, and management recommendations.

## Inputs

- `diversity-indices`: Calculated biodiversity metrics from all sites
- `water-quality-data`: Environmental measurements from sampling stations
- `coral-health-data`: Bleaching, disease, and recruitment assessments

## Outputs

- `ecosystem-report`: Complete survey report with methods, results, and discussion sections
- `data-archive`: Formatted datasets ready for repository submission (GBIF, OBIS)
- `management-brief`: One-page summary of findings and recommended conservation actions

## Process

1. Organize all data tables, photographs, and maps into a consistent format
2. Write methods section describing transect design, sampling protocols, and analytical approaches
3. Present results with statistical summaries, figures, and spatial maps
4. Discuss findings in the context of regional trends and known stressors
5. Draft management recommendations grounded in the survey data
