# Field Measurement Recorder

Record field measurements at each sampling site: parameters that must be measured on-site because they change during transport.

## Inputs

- `sampling-plan`: Which field parameters to measure and how
- `site-conditions`: Weather, flow conditions, and visual observations at the time of sampling

## Outputs

- `field-data`: On-site measurements: temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, conductivity, turbidity
- `field-notes`: Visual observations: color, odor, foam, algae, flow conditions

## Process

1. Calibrate field instruments before and after each sampling event.
2. Measure temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, specific conductance, and turbidity at each site.
3. Record visual observations: water color, odor, floating materials, algal presence.
4. Document flow conditions (estimated flow rate, water level) and weather.
