# Chargeback Evidence Gatherer

Gather evidence to support the representment.

## Inputs

- `chargeback-case`: Case details and reason code
- `response-deadline`: Deadline for submission

## Outputs

- `evidence-package`: Compiled evidence (receipts, shipping proof, communications, AVS/CVV results)
- `representment-viability`: Assessment of whether representment is likely to succeed

## Process

1. Identify the required evidence based on the reason code.
2. Collect transaction receipts, delivery confirmations, and customer communications.
3. Assess the strength of the evidence and likelihood of success.
