# Organization Cost Audit

Audit how much time you spend organizing versus finding information. Most people spend significant time filing and still cannot find things, which means the organization is not paying off.

## Inputs

- `current-information-systems`: Description of current file systems, note systems, email, and document management tools
- `common-retrieval-scenarios`: The 10 most common things you search for (recent email, a document from last month, a specific note, a meeting agenda)

## Outputs

- `organization-time-cost`: Estimated weekly time spent organizing, filing, naming, and categorizing information
- `retrieval-success-rate`: For each of the 10 common retrieval scenarios: how long does it take and do you find the right thing?

## Process

1. Track for one week: every time you file something (move to a folder, rename, categorize, tag), note the time spent. Sum the total.
2. For each of the 10 common retrieval scenarios, try to find the item right now. Time each search. Note whether you found the right version.
3. Calculate the ratio: time spent organizing divided by successful retrievals. If you spend 2 hours per week organizing and fail to find 3 of 10 items, the system is not working.
4. The question is not 'is my system organized?' but 'can I find what I need in under 30 seconds?'
