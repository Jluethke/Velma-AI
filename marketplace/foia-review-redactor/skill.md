# Foia Review Redactor

Review documents for responsiveness and apply exemption-based redactions.

## Inputs

- `collected-documents`: Documents to review
- `agency-foia-rules`: Applicable exemptions and redaction standards

## Outputs

- `reviewed-documents`: Documents marked responsive, non-responsive, or partially responsive with applied redactions
- `exemption-log`: Log of each redaction with the exemption cited and a brief justification

## Process

1. Review each document for responsiveness to the request.
2. Apply redactions under applicable exemptions (b1 through b9) with documented justification.
3. Mark each page as released in full, released in part, or withheld in full.
4. Log every redaction with the exemption number and reason.
