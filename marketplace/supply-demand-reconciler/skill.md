# Supply Demand Reconciler

Reconcile the demand forecast with supply planning.

## Inputs

- `adjusted-forecast`: Forecast adjusted for known events and qualitative inputs
- `event-calendar`: Planned promotions, launches, and market events

## Outputs

- `consensus-forecast`: Consensus demand plan agreed by sales, marketing, and operations
- `demand-plan`: Final demand plan with volume, revenue, and inventory targets

## Process

1. Compare the adjusted forecast against supply capacity
2. Identify gaps where demand exceeds supply or inventory targets
3. Facilitate the consensus meeting across stakeholders
4. Publish the consensus forecast and demand plan
