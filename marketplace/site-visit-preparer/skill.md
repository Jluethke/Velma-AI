# Site Visit Preparer

Prepare the institution for the accreditation site visit.

## Inputs

- `self-study-report`: Complete self-study report document
- `evidence-appendix`: Organized evidence appendix

## Outputs

- `site-visit-plan`: Schedule, logistics, and interview assignments for the visit
- `accreditation-package`: Final accreditation submission package

## Process

1. Organize the evidence room (physical or virtual)
2. Prepare staff for interviews by standard area
3. Create the site visit schedule and logistics plan
4. Compile and submit the final accreditation package
