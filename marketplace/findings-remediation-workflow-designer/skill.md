# Findings Remediation Workflow Designer

Establish the workflow for tracking findings from exercises through remediation and verification.

## Inputs

- `program-charter`: Reporting structure
- `success-metrics`: What to measure

## Outputs

- `remediation-workflow`: Process for triaging, assigning, tracking, and verifying remediation of findings
- `reporting-templates`: Templates for exercise reports, finding tickets, and executive summaries

## Process

1. Define finding severity levels and response SLAs
2. Design the ticketing workflow (red team submits, blue team owner triages, engineering fixes, red team verifies)
3. Create report templates for technical findings and executive summaries
4. Set up a tracking dashboard for open findings and remediation progress
