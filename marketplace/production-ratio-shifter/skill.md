# Production Ratio Shifter

Shift the production ratio toward compounding content without stopping all decaying content (which serves social and community purposes).

## Inputs

- `compound-decay-ratio`: Current ratio
- `content-architecture-template`: How to build compounding pieces

## Outputs

- `production-calendar`: Content calendar with explicit compounding vs decaying slots, targeting a ratio of at least 50% compounding
- `resource-reallocation`: How to shift production time: one compounding piece may take 5-10x the effort of a decaying piece, but delivers 50x the value over 3 years

## Process

1. Set the target ratio: at least 50% of new content production should be compounding pieces. The other 50% can be timely, social, or community content.
2. Reallocate effort: a compounding piece takes 5-10x longer to produce. But it delivers value for years. Budget the time accordingly: one compounding piece per week is more valuable than five decaying pieces per week.
3. Batch production: write the compounding pieces in focused blocks. They require research, depth, and editing. Timely pieces can be produced in the gaps.
4. Track the ratio monthly. If the compounding percentage drops below 40%, something is pulling production back toward the treadmill.
