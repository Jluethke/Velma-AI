# Privacy Risk Evaluator

Evaluate privacy risks and their potential impact on data subjects.

## Inputs

- `processing-record`: Processing activity details
- `data-flow-diagram`: Data flow for identifying risk points

## Outputs

- `privacy-risk-register`: Identified risks with likelihood and severity scores
- `risk-heat-map`: Visual representation of risk distribution

## Process

1. Identify risks at each stage of the data flow
2. Assess likelihood and severity for each risk
3. Consider risks to data subject rights and freedoms
4. Score and rank risks for mitigation prioritization
