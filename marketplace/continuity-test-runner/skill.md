# Continuity Test Runner

Test the continuity plan with a tabletop or simulation exercise.

## Inputs

- `recovery-procedures`: Recovery procedures to test
- `resource-requirements`: Required resources

## Outputs

- `test-results`: Results of the continuity test including gaps found
- `continuity-plan`: Final IT service continuity plan incorporating test findings

## Process

1. Design the test scenario (tabletop walkthrough or live simulation).
2. Execute the test with the recovery team.
3. Measure actual recovery times against RTO and RPO targets.
4. Document gaps and update the continuity plan.
