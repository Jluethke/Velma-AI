# Retrospective And Skill Assessment

After completing the project, assess what you actually learned and identify the next learning project.

## Inputs

- `milestone-plan`: The plan
- `feedback-plan`: The feedback collected

## Outputs

- `skill-assessment`: Honest assessment of your current skill level after the project, compared to before, with specific evidence
- `next-project-direction`: The hard part you still need to work on, which becomes the seed for the next learning project

## Process

1. Compare your first milestone output to your final output. The difference is visible evidence of learning. If there is no visible difference, the project was too easy or you did not engage with the hard parts.
2. List what you learned that you did not expect to learn. The most valuable learning in a project is often not the planned learning but the unexpected discoveries.
3. Identify the hard part you still struggle with. This becomes the focus of your next learning project. Skill acquisition is iterative: each project reduces the gap but does not close it entirely.
4. Publish or share the final artifact. A shared artifact creates accountability and a public portfolio. The artifact is the proof.
