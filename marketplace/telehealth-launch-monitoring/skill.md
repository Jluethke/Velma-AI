# Telehealth Launch Monitoring

Train staff, launch the program, and monitor adoption and outcomes.

## Inputs

- `telehealth-workflow`: Designed virtual visit workflow
- `billing-guidelines`: Billing instructions for telehealth

## Outputs

- `launch-checklist`: Completed launch checklist including training, testing, and go-live confirmation
- `telehealth-program-document`: Complete program documentation including workflow, billing, compliance, and monitoring plan

## Process

1. Train providers and staff on the platform, workflow, documentation, and billing.
2. Conduct test visits to verify the technology, workflow, and documentation.
3. Launch with a defined set of visit types and monitor volume, no-show rates, and patient satisfaction.
4. Review telehealth claim denials monthly and adjust billing practices as needed.
