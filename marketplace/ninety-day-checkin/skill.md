# Ninety Day Checkin

Conduct the 90-day check-in to assess progress and adjust goals.

## Inputs

- `milestone-goals`: The 30/60/90 day goals
- `success-criteria`: Measurable criteria for each milestone

## Outputs

- `checkin-assessment`: Assessment of progress against goals
- `adjusted-goals`: Updated goals for the next quarter

## Process

1. Review progress against each milestone goal
2. Gather feedback from the manager, buddy, and stakeholders
3. Identify areas of strength and development
4. Set goals for the next quarter
