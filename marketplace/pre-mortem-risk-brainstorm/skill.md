# Pre Mortem Risk Brainstorm

Brainstorm risks using the pre-mortem method: imagine the failure has already happened, then list every plausible cause.

## Inputs

- `scope-description`: What you are protecting (a project, a business, a household, a product launch, a career move)
- `time-horizon`: How far forward to project (3 months, 1 year, 5 years)

## Outputs

- `failure-narrative`: A written narrative of the imagined failure, specific enough that each cause is traceable
- `raw-risk-list`: Every plausible cause of the failure, stated as specific events (not categories)

## Process

1. Write a paragraph describing the worst plausible outcome at the end of the time horizon. Be specific: name the state of affairs, who is affected, and what was lost.
2. Working backward from that outcome, list every event or condition that could have caused it. Force specificity: not 'financial risk' but 'largest client leaves within 6 months and cash reserves cover only 8 weeks of payroll.'
3. For each cause, ask 'what had to be true for this to happen?' to surface second-order risks that hide behind the obvious ones.
4. Remove duplicates and merge causes that describe the same event in different words.
