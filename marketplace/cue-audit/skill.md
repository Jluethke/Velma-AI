# Cue Audit

Audit the current environment for cues that trigger each behavior (both desired and undesired), mapping the physical and digital triggers.

## Inputs

- `increase-behaviors`: Behaviors to do more
- `decrease-behaviors`: Behaviors to do less

## Outputs

- `cue-map`: For each behavior, the specific environmental cues that trigger it: what you see, hear, or encounter that leads to the behavior
- `friction-assessment`: For each behavior, how many steps it takes to start (the friction level)

## Process

1. For each undesired behavior, track when and where it happens for 3 days. What preceded it? What did you see, hear, or feel right before? The cue is usually visual (you saw the phone, the snack, the tab).
2. For each desired behavior, identify what currently prevents you from doing it. How many steps does it take to start? Where is the friction? If going to the gym requires packing a bag, driving, parking, and changing, that is 4 friction points.
3. Map the physical environment: where objects are placed, what is visible, what is within arm's reach. Map the digital environment: what apps are on the home screen, what notifications are on, what tabs are open.
4. The cue audit reveals that most behaviors are triggered by the environment, not by conscious choice. This is why willpower fails: you are fighting the environment dozens of times per day.
