# Cohort Visualizer

Visualize the cohort table and identify patterns.

## Inputs

- `cohort-dataset`: Prepared cohort data
- `data-quality-notes`: Known data issues

## Outputs

- `cohort-table`: Formatted cohort table (heatmap or triangle chart)
- `pattern-observations`: Trends and outliers identified in the data

## Process

1. Build the cohort table as a heatmap with color intensity showing the metric.
2. Identify trends: are newer cohorts performing better or worse?
3. Identify outliers: any cohort or time period that deviates from the pattern.
