# Digital Legacy Plan

Plan for digital accounts after death: social media, email, cloud storage, crypto, and subscriptions.

## Inputs

- `digital_accounts`: Major online accounts
- `digital_assets`: Crypto, domains, digital purchases
- `executor_tech_comfort`: Executor's tech ability

## Outputs

- `digital_inventory`: All digital accounts cataloged
- `access_plan`: How executor accesses each account
- `disposition_wishes`: Delete, memorialize, or transfer per account

## Process

1. Inventory all digital accounts
2. Categorize by type (financial, social, storage, subscription)
3. Document access method for each
4. Decide disposition: delete, memorialize, or transfer
5. Store credentials securely with executor instructions
