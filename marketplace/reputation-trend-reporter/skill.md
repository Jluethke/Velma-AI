# Reputation Trend Reporter

Track reputation trends and produce the management report.

## Inputs

- `reputation-snapshot`: The baseline reputation snapshot
- `monitoring-configuration`: The monitoring setup

## Outputs

- `sentiment-trends`: Trends in rating, review volume, and sentiment over time
- `reputation-management-report`: The complete reputation management report with trends, response metrics, and recommendations

## Process

1. Track rating changes and review volume month over month.
2. Measure response rate and average response time against the SLA.
3. Identify recurring themes in negative reviews for product or service improvement.
4. Compile the reputation management report with trends and action items.
