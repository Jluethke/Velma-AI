# Anchor Researcher

Research the range of reasonable outcomes to determine where to anchor. The anchor must be extreme enough to pull the negotiation your way but plausible enough to be taken seriously.

## Inputs

- `negotiation-context`: What is being negotiated
- `own-target`: Your target outcome

## Outputs

- `market-range`: The range of comparable outcomes from research: low end, median, high end, with sources
- `anchor-position`: The recommended first offer: aggressive but within the range of justifiable positions

## Process

1. Research comparable transactions or outcomes: salary data, property comparables, contract terms, industry rates. Collect at least 3-5 data points.
2. Identify the range: what is the lowest and highest comparable outcome?
3. Set the anchor at the aggressive end of the justifiable range. For sellers: near the high end. For buyers: near the low end.
4. The anchor should be beyond your target but within the range of outcomes you can justify with data. An anchor outside the justifiable range will be dismissed and damage credibility.
