# Survey Pilot Tester

Pilot test the survey with a small group to identify problems before full launch.

## Inputs

- `question-list`: The survey questions to test
- `skip-logic-rules`: The conditional routing rules to test

## Outputs

- `pilot-findings`: Issues found during pilot testing (confusing questions, broken logic, timing)
- `revised-survey`: The final survey instrument with pilot feedback incorporated

## Process

1. Select 5 to 10 pilot respondents who match the target audience profile.
2. Have each pilot respondent complete the survey while noting confusion or difficulty.
3. Review completion times, drop-off points, and skip logic paths.
4. Revise questions, instructions, and logic based on pilot feedback.
