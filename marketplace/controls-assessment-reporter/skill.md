# Controls Assessment Reporter

Produce the internal controls assessment report with findings and remediation plan.

## Inputs

- `deficiency-report`: Classified deficiencies
- `remediation-plan`: Remediation recommendations

## Outputs

- `controls-report`: Internal controls assessment report
- `management-action-plan`: Management response and action plan

## Process

1. Summarize the scope and methodology of the assessment.
2. Present findings organized by severity and process area.
3. Include the remediation plan with owners and timelines.
4. Obtain management response and action plan commitments.
