# Cccm Interaction Rehearsal

Rehearse the specific upcoming interaction with the adjustments in place, and prepare check-back questions that catch a misread before it costs anything. Rehearsal turns a list of adjustments into something you can actually do under the pressure of a live conversation. A check-back question verifies understanding without forcing the counterpart to contradict you.

## Inputs

- `adjustment-actions`: The specific changes to how you send your message
- `setting-summary`: The interaction being prepared for

## Outputs

- `interaction-plan`: The upcoming interaction scripted with the adjustments applied at the moments they matter
- `check-back-questions`: Questions that confirm understanding across the gap without forcing an open contradiction

## Process

1. Walk through the interaction applying each adjustment at the moment it matters: the opening, the ask, the feedback, the close.
2. Prepare check-back questions that surface a misread safely. 'What are the next steps as you see them' reveals a different understanding without a confrontation.
3. Plan for the gaps you are least sure of by watching for the behaviors that would confirm or correct your estimate early.
4. Keep the plan flexible. The map prepares you to read the person in front of you, and the live evidence overrides the estimate every time.
