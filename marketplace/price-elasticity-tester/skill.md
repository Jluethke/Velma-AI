# Price Elasticity Tester

Test price elasticity to find the revenue-maximizing price point.

## Inputs

- `margin-analysis`: Current margin data
- `price-position`: Competitive price position

## Outputs

- `elasticity-results`: Price elasticity estimates by product or category
- `optimal-prices`: Revenue-maximizing price recommendations

## Process

1. Select test products across price sensitivity categories.
2. Design the price test (A/B test, time-series comparison, or conjoint analysis).
3. Measure the volume response to price changes.
4. Estimate the elasticity coefficient and compute the optimal price.
