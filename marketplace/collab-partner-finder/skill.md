# Collab Partner Finder

Identify and evaluate potential collaboration partners.

## Inputs

- `creator-niche`: The creator's topic area, audience, and content style
- `collab-goals`: What the collaboration should achieve (audience growth, content variety, cross-promotion)

## Outputs

- `partner-shortlist`: Ranked list of potential collaborators with fit scores
- `overlap-analysis`: Audience overlap and complementarity analysis for each candidate

## Process

1. Source potential partners from the same or adjacent niches.
2. Analyze audience overlap (too much overlap means less growth potential, too little means poor fit).
3. Evaluate content style compatibility and brand alignment.
4. Rank candidates by expected collaboration value.
