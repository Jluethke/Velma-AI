# Employee Preference Surveyor

Survey employees on their work location preferences and concerns.

## Inputs

- `current-policy`: Current work arrangement policy
- `business-requirements`: Business needs for in-person collaboration

## Outputs

- `preference-data`: Employee preferences by role, team, and location
- `concern-themes`: Top concerns raised by employees

## Process

1. Design the survey covering preferences, commute impact, and concerns
2. Distribute to all employees with a two-week response window
3. Analyze results by role type, team, and seniority
4. Identify the top themes in open-ended responses
