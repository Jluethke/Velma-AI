# Culture Add Profile Definer

Define what is missing as a profile, framed as perspectives and thinking styles rather than technical gaps. A team that only sees upside misses risk; a team of all specialists misses the generalist view.

## Inputs

- `strength-gap-map`: Where the team is deep and thin
- `uniformity-flags`: Where the team is too uniform

## Outputs

- `culture-add-profile`: The specific perspectives and thinking styles the team lacks, each with why it matters
- `blind-spot-examples`: Concrete cases where the team's uniformity caused a miss or a missed opportunity

## Process

1. For each uniformity flag, ask what it has caused the team to miss, and gather concrete examples.
2. Write each missing perspective with a reason it matters for this team's mission.
3. Record blind-spot examples as short stories: we shipped X without considering Y because no one had experience Z.
4. Rank the missing perspectives by how much filling each would improve the team's decisions.
