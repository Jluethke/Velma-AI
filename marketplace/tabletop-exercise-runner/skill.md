# Tabletop Exercise Runner

Conduct a tabletop exercise to test the emergency management plan.

## Inputs

- `emergency-sops`: The written SOPs
- `notification-procedures`: Notification and evacuation procedures

## Outputs

- `exercise-findings`: Findings from the tabletop exercise including gaps and improvement areas
- `emergency-management-plan`: The finalized emergency management plan incorporating exercise lessons

## Process

1. Design the exercise scenario based on a priority hazard.
2. Facilitate the tabletop exercise with EOC personnel and partner agencies.
3. Document decisions, gaps, and improvement opportunities during the exercise.
4. Update the emergency management plan based on exercise findings.
