# Workshop Series Designer

Design the workshop and seminar series based on identified faculty needs.

## Inputs

- `priority-topics`: Ranked list of development topics
- `faculty-roster`: List of faculty with roles and disciplines

## Outputs

- `workshop-calendar`: Schedule of workshops and seminars for the academic year
- `session-outlines`: Outline for each workshop session

## Process

1. Select 8-12 workshop topics from the priority list
2. Design each session with objectives, activities, and takeaways
3. Schedule across the academic year avoiding peak grading periods
4. Identify facilitators for each session
