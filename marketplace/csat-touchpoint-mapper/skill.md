# Csat Touchpoint Mapper

Define the touchpoints where CSAT will be measured across the customer journey.

## Inputs

- `customer-journey-stages`: The stages and touchpoints in the customer lifecycle
- `measurement-goals`: What each touchpoint measurement should reveal

## Outputs

- `csat-touchpoint-map`: Each touchpoint with its survey trigger, question, and target score
- `touchpoint-ownership`: The team responsible for each touchpoint's score

## Process

1. List the customer journey touchpoints (onboarding, first value, support close, renewal, feature launch).
2. Select which touchpoints to measure based on business impact and feasibility.
3. Define the CSAT question and scale for each touchpoint (1-5, 1-7, or thumbs up/down).
4. Assign ownership of each touchpoint score to the responsible team.
