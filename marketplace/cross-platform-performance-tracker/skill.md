# Cross Platform Performance Tracker

Track performance across all platforms and produce the distribution report.

## Inputs

- `publish-schedule`: What was posted where and when
- `cross-promo-plan`: The cross-promotion activities

## Outputs

- `platform-performance`: Metrics by platform (reach, engagement, clicks, followers gained)
- `distribution-report`: Aggregate report comparing platform performance and cross-promo effectiveness

## Process

1. Pull analytics from each platform after the reporting window.
2. Compare reach, engagement rate, and traffic driven across platforms.
3. Evaluate which cross-promotion tactics generated the most referral traffic.
4. Document findings and recommendations for the next distribution cycle.
