# Department Budget Collector

Gather budget requests from each department with justifications.

## Inputs

- `trend-analysis`: Historical trends to inform department budgets
- `budget-template`: Standard budget request template

## Outputs

- `department-requests`: Compiled budget requests from all departments
- `headcount-plan`: Planned headcount changes and associated costs

## Process

1. Distribute budget templates with historical baselines to department heads.
2. Collect completed budget requests with line-item justifications.
3. Review headcount plans (new hires, attrition, reorganizations).
4. Consolidate all department submissions into a single workbook.
