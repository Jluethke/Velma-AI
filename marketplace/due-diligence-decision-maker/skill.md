# Due Diligence Decision Maker

Make the risk-based approval decision and produce the due diligence report.

## Inputs

- `financial-assessment`: Financial assessment
- `red-flag-summary`: Red flags identified

## Outputs

- `approval-decision`: Approve, approve with conditions, or decline
- `due-diligence-report`: Complete due diligence report

## Process

1. Weigh all findings against the risk criteria.
2. Make the approval decision (approve, approve with conditions, decline).
3. Document any conditions or mitigating controls required.
4. Compile the due diligence report for the compliance file.
