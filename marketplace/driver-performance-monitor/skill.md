# Driver Performance Monitor

Monitor driver performance and safety metrics.

## Inputs

- `fleet-inventory`: Current inventory with status, mileage, and condition
- `fuel-report`: Fuel consumption and cost per vehicle and route

## Outputs

- `driver-scorecard`: Performance scores per driver covering safety, efficiency, and compliance
- `safety-incidents`: Log of safety incidents and violations

## Process

1. Collect driving behavior data (speed, braking, idle time)
2. Score each driver on safety, fuel efficiency, and route compliance
3. Log any accidents, citations, or policy violations
4. Produce the driver scorecard and incident log
