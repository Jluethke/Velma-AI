# Solicitation Publisher

Publish the solicitation and manage the question and answer period.

## Inputs

- `solicitation-package`: Complete solicitation to publish
- `requirements-document`: Requirements for answering vendor questions

## Outputs

- `published-solicitation`: Solicitation posted to required venues with proof of publication
- `qa-amendments`: Questions received, answers published, and any amendments to the solicitation

## Process

1. Post the solicitation to required venues (SAM.gov, agency website, trade publications).
2. Collect vendor questions during the Q&A period.
3. Publish answers and any resulting amendments to all prospective vendors.
4. Close the solicitation at the stated deadline.
