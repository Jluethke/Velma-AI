# Qualitative Data Gatherer

Gather qualitative data including stories, testimonials, and case studies.

## Inputs

- `outcome-framework`: Outcomes to illustrate
- `program-description`: Program context

## Outputs

- `qualitative-findings`: Stories, quotes, and case studies
- `consent-records`: Permissions for use of personal stories

## Process

1. Identify participants willing to share their stories
2. Conduct interviews or collect written testimonials
3. Select the most illustrative examples for each outcome
4. Obtain written consent for publication
