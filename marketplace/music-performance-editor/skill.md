# Music Performance Editor

Edit and quantize the recorded performances.

## Inputs

- `raw-tracks`: Individual instrument audio tracks
- `session-notes`: Notes on takes and comping

## Outputs

- `edited-tracks`: Comped, tuned, and timed instrument tracks
- `edit-log`: Record of all edits applied

## Process

1. Comp the best sections from multiple takes
2. Correct timing with quantization or manual alignment
3. Tune vocals and pitched instruments as needed
4. Clean up noise and unwanted sounds between phrases
