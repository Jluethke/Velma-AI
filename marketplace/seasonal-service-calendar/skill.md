# Seasonal Service Calendar

Build the annual service calendar mapping services to months based on climate and growing season.

## Inputs

- `service-catalog`: Full service catalog
- `service-region`: Climate zone and frost dates

## Outputs

- `seasonal-calendar`: Month-by-month calendar of active services and crew focus areas
- `revenue-forecast`: Projected revenue by month based on service mix and client base

## Process

1. Map each service to its active months based on climate zone.
2. Identify the peak, shoulder, and off-season periods.
3. Plan crew size and overtime needs for peak months.
4. Forecast monthly revenue based on recurring contracts and seasonal work.
