# Minimum Effective Dose Design

Design the minimum effective dose: the least amount of movement that produces measurable health benefits and is easy enough to never skip.

## Inputs

- `movement-baseline`: Current activity level
- `failure-patterns`: Why past attempts failed

## Outputs

- `movement-prescription`: The minimum plan: activity type, duration (start at 20 minutes), frequency (3x per week), and intensity (conversational pace)
- `non-negotiable-minimum`: The absolute floor: even on the worst day, you do this (e.g., a 10-minute walk). Never zero.

## Process

1. Start with 20 minutes of walking, 3 times per week. This is enough to produce measurable health benefits (cardiovascular, mood, metabolic) and is easy enough that almost no one will skip it.
2. Choose an activity you already know you enjoy. If you hate running, do not prescribe running. If you like walking, walk. If you like swimming, swim. Enjoyment is the strongest predictor of long-term adherence.
3. Set a non-negotiable minimum: even on the worst day, you do something. A 10-minute walk counts. Zero does not. The habit of movement matters more than the intensity of any single session.
4. No gym membership required. The research shows that walking produces 80% of the health benefits of intense exercise. The barrier to entry must be as low as possible.
