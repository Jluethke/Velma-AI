# Stretch Zone Scoper

Scope the project to the stretch zone: slightly beyond your current skill level but not in the panic zone.

## Inputs

- `project-candidates`: Possible projects
- `current-level`: Your current skill level

## Outputs

- `scoped-project`: The chosen project scoped to 2-4 weeks, with clear boundaries on what is in and out of scope
- `stretch-elements`: The specific aspects of the project that are beyond your current ability (these are where the learning happens)

## Process

1. The stretch zone is where you can succeed with effort but not with your current skills alone. If you could complete the project today with your current skills, it is too easy. If you have no idea where to start, it is too hard.
2. Scope to 2-4 weeks of part-time work (10-20 hours per week). Longer projects lose momentum. Shorter projects do not cover enough hard parts.
3. Define what is in scope (the specific deliverable) and out of scope (things that would be nice but are not necessary for the learning objective). Scope creep turns learning projects into never-finished projects.
4. Explicitly identify the 2-3 elements that are beyond your current ability. These are the learning targets. Everything else in the project is scaffolding.
