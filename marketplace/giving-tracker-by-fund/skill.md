# Giving Tracker By Fund

Track giving by donor and by fund to monitor progress against goals and budgets.

## Inputs

- `acknowledgment-templates`: Templates for donor communication
- `acknowledgment-schedule`: Timing of donor acknowledgments

## Outputs

- `giving-dashboard`: Current giving totals by fund, compared against budget and prior year
- `donor-giving-history`: Per-donor giving record showing amounts, dates, funds, and year-to-date total

## Process

1. Aggregate giving by fund and compare against the annual budget and prior year.
2. Track each donor's year-to-date giving total and gift frequency.
3. Flag significant changes: first-time donors, lapsed donors, and large increases or decreases.
4. Produce the giving dashboard for leadership review.
