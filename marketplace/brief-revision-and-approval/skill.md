# Brief Revision And Approval

Revise the brief based on feedback and get final sign-off.

## Inputs

- `draft-brief`: The original draft brief
- `consolidated-feedback`: The stakeholder feedback to incorporate

## Outputs

- `approved-brief`: The final approved creative brief
- `approval-record`: Who approved, when, and any conditions

## Process

1. Incorporate the feedback into the brief, updating each section.
2. Circulate the revised version for final approval.
3. Collect sign-offs and document any conditions or caveats.
4. Produce the approved brief and approval record.
