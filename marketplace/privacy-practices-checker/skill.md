# Privacy Practices Checker

Check for a current Notice of Privacy Practices and patient rights procedures.

## Inputs

- `organization-scope`: Departments and locations
- `current-controls`: Existing privacy controls

## Outputs

- `npp-status`: Current, outdated, or missing Notice of Privacy Practices
- `patient-rights-status`: Compliance with patient rights requirements

## Process

1. Review the Notice of Privacy Practices for current regulatory alignment.
2. Check that patient rights procedures are implemented (access, amendment, accounting).
3. Verify that the NPP is distributed to patients at first encounter.
