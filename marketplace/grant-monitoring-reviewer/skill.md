# Grant Monitoring Reviewer

Monitor the grantee's progress and compliance throughout the grant period.

## Inputs

- `grant-agreement`: The executed grant agreement
- `monitoring-plan`: The monitoring plan

## Outputs

- `monitoring-findings`: Findings from progress and compliance reviews
- `drawdown-approvals`: Approved and processed drawdown requests

## Process

1. Review the grantee's progress reports against the milestones in the agreement.
2. Process drawdown or reimbursement requests with supporting documentation.
3. Conduct site visits or desk reviews per the monitoring plan.
4. Document any compliance issues and required corrective actions.
