# Moat Intangible Asset Assessment

Assess intangible assets: brand, patents, licenses, regulatory approvals, and culture that rivals cannot simply buy or copy. These assets protect pricing and keep customers choosing the business by default.

## Inputs

- `business-profile`: A tight profile of the business model and customers
- `competitor-set`: The named set of real competitors

## Outputs

- `intangible-asset-score`: A 0 to 5 score for intangible asset strength
- `intangible-asset-evidence`: The specific protected assets and what they shield

## Process

1. List intangible assets: brand trust, patents, exclusive licenses, regulatory approvals, and hard-to-copy culture.
2. For each, ask what it protects: price, default choice, or access to a restricted market.
3. Test whether a rival could buy or rebuild the asset, and how long that would take.
4. Score 0 to 5 and name the assets behind the score.
