# Advocacy Tracking

Track whether the champion is actually advancing the deal and where they stall, so you can help at the right moment.

## Inputs

- `champion-kit`: The internal selling kit
- `primary-champion`: The chosen champion

## Outputs

- `advocacy-signals`: Signs the champion is moving the deal or has gone quiet
- `support-actions`: What you will do to unblock the champion at each stall

## Process

1. Agree on the internal milestones the champion is driving toward and a rough timeline.
2. Watch for signals of real movement, such as new people pulled in, versus silence.
3. When the champion stalls, diagnose whether it is a missing proof, a blocker, or lost priority.
4. Match each stall to a specific support action rather than simply asking for an update.
