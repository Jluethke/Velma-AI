# Mixed Methods Analyzer

Integrate quantitative survey results and qualitative interview findings using a convergent, explanatory, or exploratory design.

## Inputs

- `survey-results`: Statistical analysis output from the quantitative component
- `coded-data`: Themed qualitative findings from the coding step
- `integration-design`: Convergent, sequential explanatory, or sequential exploratory approach

## Outputs

- `joint-display`: Side-by-side matrix comparing quantitative and qualitative findings by construct
- `integration-narrative`: Written analysis of convergence, divergence, and complementarity between data sources
- `meta-inferences`: Overarching conclusions that neither data source could support alone

## Process

1. Organize quantitative results and qualitative themes by shared construct or research question
2. Build a joint display table showing statistical findings alongside illustrative quotes
3. Identify areas of convergence where both sources support the same conclusion
4. Flag divergences where quantitative and qualitative findings conflict and explore explanations
5. Draw meta-inferences that integrate both data streams into unified findings
