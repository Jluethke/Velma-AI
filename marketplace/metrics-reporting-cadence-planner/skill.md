# Metrics Reporting Cadence Planner

Establish the reporting cadence and distribution for security metrics.

## Inputs

- `metric-baselines`: Established baselines and normal ranges
- `metric-targets`: Target values and improvement timelines

## Outputs

- `reporting-schedule`: Weekly, monthly, and quarterly reporting schedule with stakeholder distribution
- `metrics-program-document`: Complete documentation of the metrics program

## Process

1. Define the weekly operational review cadence for the SOC team
2. Set the monthly management report schedule with trend analysis
3. Plan quarterly executive reviews with strategic metrics and improvement progress
4. Document the full metrics program: definitions, baselines, targets, and reporting schedule
