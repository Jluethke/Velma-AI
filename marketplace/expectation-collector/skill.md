# Expectation Collector

Collect each key stakeholder's expectations individually: what they think the project will deliver, when, and how success will be measured.

## Inputs

- `initiative-description`: The project or initiative
- `key-stakeholders`: The stakeholders whose alignment matters (decision makers, funders, users, team leads)

## Outputs

- `expectation-records`: For each stakeholder: their stated expectations for scope (what will be delivered), timeline (when), and success criteria (how they will judge whether it worked)
- `alignment-matrix`: A side-by-side comparison of all stakeholders' expectations, highlighting where they agree and where they diverge

## Process

1. Interview each key stakeholder individually (not in a group, where social pressure creates false agreement). Ask: 'In your own words, what will this project deliver? By when? How will you know it was successful?'
2. Record their answers verbatim. Do not paraphrase or interpret. The exact words reveal what they actually expect, not what the project charter says they should expect.
3. Build the alignment matrix: one column per stakeholder, rows for scope items, timeline, and success criteria. Mark agreements (all stakeholders said the same thing) and divergences (different stakeholders said different things).
4. Divergences are not failures. They are discoveries. A divergence found now is a conversation. A divergence found at delivery is a conflict.
