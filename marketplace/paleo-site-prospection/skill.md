# Paleo Site Prospection

Survey a region for fossil-bearing exposures, rank candidate sites by formation age, accessibility, and preservation potential.

## Inputs

- `target-region`: Geographic area and geologic formation to survey
- `target-taxa`: Taxa or time period of interest
- `access-constraints`: Land ownership, permits, seasonal access windows

## Outputs

- `candidate-sites`: Ranked list of prospection sites with GPS coordinates and formation data
- `permit-requirements`: Permits and landowner agreements needed per site
- `field-logistics`: Access routes, camp locations, water sources, and weather windows

## Process

1. Review published geologic maps and formation descriptions for the target region
2. Cross-reference known fossil localities in the Paleobiology Database with unexplored adjacent exposures
3. Rank each candidate by stratigraphic relevance, surface weathering rate, and logistical feasibility
4. Compile permit requirements and landowner contact information for top-ranked sites
