# Executive Summary Writer

Write the executive summary section translating technical findings into business risk language.

## Inputs

- `deduplicated-findings`: Final finding set for summary
- `severity-distribution`: Finding severity statistics

## Outputs

- `executive-summary`: Non-technical summary of test results and business risk
- `risk-highlights`: Top risks requiring immediate executive attention

## Process

1. Summarize the engagement scope, timeline, and methodology
2. Translate the highest severity findings into business impact statements
3. Present the overall security posture assessment
4. Highlight findings requiring immediate action versus planned remediation
