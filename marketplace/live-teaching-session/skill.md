# Live Teaching Session

Teach the topic to a real audience and capture the questions they ask.

## Inputs

- `gap-fill-notes`: Notes that repair each breakdown
- `repaired-explanation`: The explanation with the gaps filled

## Outputs

- `teaching-record`: A record of how the teaching went
- `audience-questions`: The questions the audience asked

## Process

1. Teach the topic to a person or a small group.
2. Invite questions and write down the ones you could not answer well.
3. Note where the audience looked confused even without asking.
4. Record which parts landed clearly.
