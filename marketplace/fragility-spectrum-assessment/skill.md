# Fragility Spectrum Assessment

Assess where your system falls on the fragility spectrum: fragile (breaks under stress), resilient (survives stress), or antifragile (improves from stress).

## Inputs

- `system-description`: The system to assess (a business, a career, a health regimen, a portfolio, a team)
- `recent-stressors`: Stressors the system has faced in the past 12 months and how it responded

## Outputs

- `fragility-assessment`: For each component of the system: its position on the fragility spectrum (fragile, resilient, antifragile) with the evidence
- `fragile-components`: The components that are fragile (broke or degraded under recent stress), ranked by importance

## Process

1. List the components of the system. For a business: revenue model, team, technology, customer base, supply chain, financial structure. For a career: skill set, network, income sources, reputation, knowledge base.
2. For each component, review how it responded to recent stress. Did it break (fragile), survive unchanged (resilient), or come out stronger (antifragile)?
3. Evidence for antifragility: the team that lost a key member and cross-trained so well that it became less dependent on any one person. The business that survived a downturn and emerged with a leaner cost structure and better processes.
4. Rank the fragile components by their importance to the system's survival.
