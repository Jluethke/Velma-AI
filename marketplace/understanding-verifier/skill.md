# Understanding Verifier

Test the final explanation against a real listener and capture anything still unclear.

## Inputs

- `simplified-explanation`: A clearer explanation with the gaps filled
- `refined-analogies`: Analogies improved by the new understanding

## Outputs

- `verified-explanation`: The explanation after testing it on a listener
- `remaining-gaps`: Gaps to feed the next round of study

## Process

1. Explain the concept to another person or record yourself.
2. Note any question you could not answer cleanly.
3. List remaining gaps to feed the next round of study.
4. Decide whether the concept is understood well enough to stop.
