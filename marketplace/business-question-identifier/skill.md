# Business Question Identifier

Identify the key business questions that metrics must answer.

## Inputs

- `business-objectives`: The organization's goals and strategy
- `stakeholder-interviews`: Input from leaders about what they need to measure

## Outputs

- `business-questions`: Prioritized list of questions the business needs answered
- `measurement-gaps`: Questions that currently have no metric

## Process

1. Review the business objectives and strategy.
2. Interview stakeholders about the questions they ask most often.
3. Prioritize questions by impact and frequency.
4. Identify which questions have no current metric.
