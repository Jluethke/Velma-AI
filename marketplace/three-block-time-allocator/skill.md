# Three Block Time Allocator

Structure the meeting into three rough blocks: priorities, blockers, and growth. The blocks keep the conversation from being consumed entirely by this week's fires and never reaching development.

## Inputs

- `agenda-template`: The shared agenda
- `prep-prompts`: The preparation prompts

## Outputs

- `time-block-structure`: The division of the meeting into priorities, blockers, and growth blocks
- `block-guidance`: What belongs in each block and the rule to protect the growth block

## Process

1. Allocate roughly a third of the time to priorities: what matters most for the report now and whether it is on track.
2. Allocate roughly a third to blockers: what is in the way and what the manager can remove.
3. Allocate roughly a third to growth: skills, career, and development, which is the block fires usually crowd out.
4. Keep the blocks flexible but protect the growth block, since it is the one with no deadline forcing it.
