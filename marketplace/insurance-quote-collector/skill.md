# Insurance Quote Collector

Collect and normalize insurance quotes from multiple carriers for apples-to-apples comparison.

## Inputs

- `coverage_needs`: Desired coverage levels
- `current_premiums`: What being paid now
- `personal_details`: Details needed for quotes

## Outputs

- `quote_matrix`: Normalized quotes from multiple carriers
- `hidden_costs`: Fees, deductibles, and exclusions per quote
- `savings_potential`: Potential savings vs current premiums

## Process

1. Identify 4-5 carriers to quote
2. Ensure identical coverage levels across quotes
3. Document deductibles and exclusions
4. Calculate true annual cost including fees
5. Note claim filing reputation
