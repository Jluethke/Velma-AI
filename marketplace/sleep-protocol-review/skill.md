# Sleep Protocol Review

Review the quality data against the design and adjust the element that is holding sleep back.

## Inputs

- `quality-tracker`: The daily quality log
- `quality-vs-duration-metrics`: Rested feeling relative to time in bed

## Outputs

- `protocol-review-summary`: A summary of which sleep markers improved and which did not
- `sleep-protocol-adjustment`: The single change to make next, aimed at the weakest marker

## Process

1. Read the markers after two to three weeks. Judge each one separately: onset time, awakenings, and morning alertness can move independently.
2. Trace a weak marker to its likely cause. Slow onset points to light or stimulants, frequent awakenings point to environment or alcohol, and unrested mornings with full hours point to quality disruption.
3. Change one element at a time. Adjusting several at once makes it impossible to tell which change helped.
4. Protect the fixed wake time through every adjustment. It is the anchor, and loosening it to chase a quick fix undoes the whole design.
