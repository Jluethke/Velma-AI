# Proposal Section Drafter

Assign section owners and draft each section of the proposal.

## Inputs

- `requirements-matrix`: Requirements and capability mapping
- `bid-decision`: Confirmed bid decision

## Outputs

- `draft-sections`: Drafted sections of the proposal
- `section-assignments`: Who owns each section and the deadline

## Process

1. Break the proposal into sections matching the RFP structure.
2. Assign each section to a subject matter expert.
3. Draft each section addressing the specific requirements.
4. Include evidence, case studies, and data where requested.
