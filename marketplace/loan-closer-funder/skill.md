# Loan Closer Funder

Close the loan, execute documents, and fund the proceeds.

## Inputs

- `underwriting-decision`: Approved decision with any conditions
- `loan-terms`: Finalized rate, term, and conditions

## Outputs

- `closing-package`: Executed loan documents and disclosures
- `loan-origination-record`: Complete origination record with funding confirmation

## Process

1. Verify all conditions of approval are satisfied.
2. Prepare the closing documents (note, deed of trust, disclosures).
3. Execute the documents and fund the loan proceeds.
4. Record the loan in the servicing system.
