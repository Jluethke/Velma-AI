# Individual Development Planner

Create individual development plans for succession candidates.

## Inputs

- `high-potential-list`: High-potential employees
- `development-needs`: Their development gaps

## Outputs

- `development-plans`: Individual development plans for each candidate
- `resource-requirements`: Resources needed (training, mentoring, stretch assignments)

## Process

1. Design a development plan addressing each candidate's gaps
2. Include a mix of experiences (stretch assignments, cross-functional projects)
3. Assign a mentor or sponsor for each candidate
4. Set milestones and review checkpoints
