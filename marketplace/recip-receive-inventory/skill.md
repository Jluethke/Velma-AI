# Recip Receive Inventory

Inventory what each person gives you across the same value lens, which is harder to see clearly because received value (especially emotional support, information, and access) is easy to take for granted and to undercount.

## Inputs

- `give-inventory`: What you give to each relationship
- `give-frequency-notes`: Frequency and weight of your giving

## Outputs

- `receive-inventory`: For each relationship, the concrete things they give you across the value lens
- `receive-frequency-notes`: How often and how substantially they give, measured the same way as the give side

## Process

1. For each relationship, list what they give you across the same categories. Use the give-inventory as a mirror, filling in the other direction for the same person so the two sides are directly comparable.
2. Pay special attention to the forms of value that are easy to take for granted: being reliably available, consistent emotional support, information and context you would not otherwise have, and access to their network.
3. Measure frequency and weight the same way you did for giving, so the comparison is fair. A friend who gives steady low-key support may out-give a contact who did one flashy favor.
4. Correct the opposite bias here. People who undercount their own giving often also undercount what they receive, and people who feel over-given-to often fail to see the quiet value flowing back to them.
