# Facility Energy Surveyor

Survey the facility to inventory energy-consuming systems and identify waste.

## Inputs

- `energy-baseline`: Baseline energy consumption data
- `billing-anomalies`: Anomalies to investigate during the survey

## Outputs

- `equipment-inventory`: Inventory of major energy-consuming equipment (HVAC, lighting, motors, envelope)
- `waste-observations`: Observed energy waste (running during unoccupied hours, poor insulation, leaks)

## Process

1. Walk the facility and inventory major equipment: HVAC units, lighting, motors, and plug loads.
2. Record nameplate data, operating schedules, and observed conditions.
3. Check the building envelope (insulation, windows, air sealing) for deficiencies.
4. Note operational waste: lights on in unoccupied areas, equipment running unnecessarily, temperature setpoints.
