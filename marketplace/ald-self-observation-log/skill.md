# Ald Self Observation Log

Catch yourself in the act for one week. Self-report after the fact is unreliable, so log the moment as close to real time as you can. The goal is frequency data, not self-judgment.

## Inputs

- `failure-definitions`: The four failures in your own words
- `self-suspected-failures`: Your pre-feedback guess at your dominant failures

## Outputs

- `observation-log`: Dated entries noting which failure occurred, in what setting, and what triggered it
- `failure-frequency`: A count of each failure over the week, with the settings where each spiked

## Process

1. For one week, after each meaningful conversation, note any failure you caught yourself committing, the setting, and what set it off.
2. Record the trigger, not just the failure. Interrupting under time pressure and interrupting when excited call for different countermeasures.
3. Tally the counts at week's end. Look for the setting where your failures cluster, because that is where practice will pay off most.
4. Do not try to fix anything during this week. Observation changes behavior enough that early fixing contaminates the data.
