# Weather Conditions Recorder

Record the weather conditions at the job site for the day.

## Inputs

- `project-name`: Name of the construction project
- `report-date`: Date of the field report

## Outputs

- `weather-record`: Temperature, precipitation, wind, and conditions log
- `weather-impact-note`: Any impact of weather on work activities

## Process

1. Record temperature, precipitation, wind speed, and sky conditions at morning, midday, and end of shift.
2. Note any weather-related work stoppages or delays.
3. Flag conditions that affect concrete pours, roofing, or other weather-sensitive activities.
