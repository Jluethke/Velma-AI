# Composite Score Calculator

Calculate the composite score for every contact and use it to generate prioritized action lists.

## Inputs

- `weight-profiles`: Weight sets for different contexts
- `calibration-results`: Calibration test results

## Outputs

- `scored-contact-list`: Every contact with their composite score, dimension breakdown, and rank
- `score-driven-actions`: Actions generated by score patterns: high-score contacts to invest in, declining-score contacts to re-engage, mismatched-dimension contacts to investigate

## Process

1. Calculate the weighted composite: sum of (dimension_score * weight) across all 5 dimensions for each contact. The result is a 0-100 score. Sort contacts by score descending.
2. Generate three action lists from the scores. (1) Invest list: top 15% by score, especially those with high momentum (these relationships are growing and worth accelerating). (2) Re-engage list: contacts whose score dropped more than 20 points in the last quarter (relationships in freefall). (3) Investigate list: contacts with extreme dimension imbalances (high strategic importance but low frequency means you know they matter but are not acting on it).
3. Build the dimension breakdown view for each contact. A single composite score hides the story. A contact with score 65 from balanced dimensions is different from a contact with score 65 from max strategic importance and zero momentum. The breakdown tells you what to do: the balanced 65 needs maintenance, the imbalanced 65 needs urgent re-engagement.
4. Set the review cadence: recalculate scores monthly. Compare month-over-month changes to spot trends. A contact whose score rises 10 points per month is becoming important. A contact dropping 5 points per month is fading. The trend matters more than the absolute number.
