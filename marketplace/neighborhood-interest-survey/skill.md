# Neighborhood Interest Survey

Survey neighbors to gauge interest in community improvement and identify shared concerns.

## Inputs

- `neighborhood_size`: Approximate number of households
- `known_issues`: Issues already identified
- `distribution_method`: How to reach neighbors (door, email, app)

## Outputs

- `survey_results`: Aggregated survey responses
- `top_concerns`: Highest-priority shared concerns
- `interested_neighbors`: People willing to get involved

## Process

1. Design short survey covering key topics
2. Distribute through available channels
3. Collect and aggregate responses
4. Identify top shared priorities
5. Build contact list of interested neighbors
