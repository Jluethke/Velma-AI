# Credit Dispute Strategy

Build and execute a dispute strategy for errors and questionable negative items.

## Inputs

- `dispute_candidates`: Items to dispute
- `supporting_evidence`: Any documentation available

## Outputs

- `dispute_letters`: Drafted dispute letters per bureau
- `dispute_timeline`: Expected response timeline
- `follow_up_plan`: What to do after responses

## Process

1. Prioritize disputes by score impact
2. Draft dispute letters with specific language
3. Send to appropriate bureau with documentation
4. Track response deadlines (30-45 days)
5. Plan follow-up for denied disputes
