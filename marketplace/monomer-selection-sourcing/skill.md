# Monomer Selection Sourcing

Select monomers based on target polymer properties, assess sourcing options, and confirm purity specifications.

## Inputs

- `target-properties`: Desired mechanical, thermal, and chemical properties of the final polymer
- `application-requirements`: End-use environment: temperature range, chemical exposure, regulatory constraints

## Outputs

- `monomer-candidates`: Ranked monomer list with structure, supplier, purity, and cost
- `copolymer-ratios`: Proposed comonomer ratios if a copolymer strategy is needed

## Process

1. Map target properties to polymer backbone and side-chain requirements
2. Identify candidate monomers and rank by property match, availability, and cost
3. Evaluate copolymerization options if a single monomer cannot meet all property targets
4. Confirm supplier purity specs and order sufficient material for screening and scale-up
