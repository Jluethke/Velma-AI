# Career Change Job Search

Execute the job search in a new field: resume positioning, interview prep, and offer evaluation.

## Inputs

- `transferable_skills`: Skills that carry over
- `certification_plan`: Education completed or in progress
- `portfolio_plan`: Portfolio work completed

## Outputs

- `resume_strategy`: How to position the career change on resume
- `interview_prep`: Common questions and how to address the switch
- `application_targets`: Companies and roles to target

## Process

1. Rewrite resume emphasizing transferable skills
2. Prepare career change narrative for interviews
3. Identify companies that hire career changers
4. Practice technical skills for the new field
5. Set application volume and tracking system
