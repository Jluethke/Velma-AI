# Baby Gear Checklist

Build a prioritized list of baby gear with budget tiers and registry recommendations.

## Inputs

- `due_date`: Expected due date
- `budget_range`: Total gear budget range
- `living_situation`: Apartment, house, etc.

## Outputs

- `gear_list`: Prioritized gear list with prices
- `registry_plan`: Registry recommendations by store

## Process

1. Categorize gear by necessity tier (must-have, nice-to-have, skip)
2. Price compare across retailers
3. Build registry with gift-friendly items
4. Flag items to buy used vs new
