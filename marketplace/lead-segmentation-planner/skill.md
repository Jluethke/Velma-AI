# Lead Segmentation Planner

Segment leads by funnel stage, behavior, and demographic attributes.

## Inputs

- `lead-data`: Available lead records and attributes
- `funnel-definition`: Funnel stages used by the sales team

## Outputs

- `segment-definitions`: Named segments with inclusion criteria
- `segment-counts`: Estimated lead count per segment

## Process

1. Review the available lead attributes
2. Define segments by funnel stage and engagement level
3. Estimate the size of each segment
