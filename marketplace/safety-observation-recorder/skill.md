# Safety Observation Recorder

Capture safety observations and incidents from the day.

## Inputs

- `project-name`: Name of the construction project
- `report-date`: Date of the field report

## Outputs

- `safety-observations`: Safety observations and any incidents
- `safety-actions`: Corrective actions taken for safety issues

## Process

1. Record any safety incidents, near misses, or first aid cases.
2. Document safety observations (positive and negative).
3. Note any safety meetings, toolbox talks, or training conducted.
4. List corrective actions taken for any violations.
