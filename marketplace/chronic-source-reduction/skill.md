# Chronic Source Reduction

Plan structural changes for chronic stress, because a background load does not respond to in-the-moment tools and must be reduced at the source.

## Inputs

- `classified-stressors`: The tagged stressors
- `type-distribution`: The share of each stress type

## Outputs

- `chronic-source-plan`: A plan to reduce or remove the ongoing sources of chronic stress
- `structural-changes`: The specific changes to schedule, environment, commitments, or relationships that lower the load

## Process

1. List the ongoing sources behind the chronic stress. Chronic stress comes from a situation that keeps generating it, not from a single event.
2. For each source, ask what structural change would reduce it: removing a commitment, renegotiating a demand, changing an environment, or setting a boundary.
3. Accept that reset tools only mask chronic stress. Breathing exercises do not fix an overloaded schedule, and relying on them lets the source grind on.
4. Pick the one or two changes with the largest effect and plan them concretely, since chronic load usually traces back to a small number of big sources.
