# Source Tracking

Track every referral source and its outcome, so you know which customers and moments produce real deals.

## Inputs

- `routing-process`: The referral path
- `referral-ask`: The specific ask

## Outputs

- `source-ledger`: A record of who referred whom and from which moment
- `close-rate-by-source`: Close rate and value by referral source and by moment

## Process

1. Record each referral with its source customer, the moment it came from, and the ask that prompted it.
2. Follow each referral to its outcome, closed or not, with the deal value.
3. Compute close rate and value by source and by moment, so you can see what produces results.
4. Compare referral close rates against cold leads to size the program's real contribution.
