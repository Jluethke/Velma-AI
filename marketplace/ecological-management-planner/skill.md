# Ecological Management Planner

Write management recommendations based on population status, threats, and habitat condition, with actions prioritized by feasibility and expected benefit.

## Inputs

- `conservation-assessment`: Population status and criteria triggered
- `threat-register`: Threats to address
- `interaction-network`: Key species relationships to preserve

## Outputs

- `management-plan`: Prioritized actions with expected outcomes, timelines, and costs
- `monitoring-protocol`: Indicators to track and evaluation schedule
- `stakeholder-brief`: Non-technical summary for land managers and funders

## Process

1. Match each threat to a management action (habitat restoration, harvest regulation, invasive control)
2. Prioritize actions by benefit-to-cost ratio and feasibility
3. Define success indicators and a monitoring schedule
4. Write a stakeholder brief summarizing the case and recommended actions
