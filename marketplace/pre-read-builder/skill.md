# Pre Read Builder

Build the pre-read: the document attendees must read before the meeting. The pre-read contains the decision, the options, the data, and the recommendation. The meeting is for disagreements, not summaries.

## Inputs

- `decision-statement`: The decision to be made
- `options-list`: The options and trade-offs

## Outputs

- `pre-read-document`: The document containing decision, options, data, and recommendation, under 2 pages
- `distribution-plan`: When to send the pre-read (at least 24 hours before the meeting) and to whom

## Process

1. Write the pre-read: page 1 is the decision and options. Page 2 is the supporting data and your recommendation.
2. The recommendation section says what you would do and why. Attendees who agree do not need to speak in the meeting.
3. Send the pre-read at least 24 hours before the meeting. Tag it clearly: 'Pre-read required for [meeting name].'
4. If the pre-read is longer than 2 pages, it contains material that belongs in an appendix, not the pre-read.
