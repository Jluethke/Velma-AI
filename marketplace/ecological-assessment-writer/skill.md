# Ecological Assessment Writer

Produce the ecological assessment report: survey design, sampling adequacy, community composition, diversity analysis, and conservation recommendations.

## Inputs

- `diversity-table`: Diversity metrics
- `accumulation-curve`: Sampling adequacy assessment
- `species-abundance-matrix`: Community composition data

## Outputs

- `ecological-report`: Structured assessment report
- `species-list`: Annotated species list with abundances, habitats, and conservation status

## Process

1. Summarize the survey design and effort.
2. Assess sampling adequacy from the accumulation curve.
3. Present the community composition and diversity results with figures.
4. Highlight species of conservation concern and recommend management actions.
