# Price Recommendation Builder

Translate the PSM results into a pricing recommendation with the range, the optimal point, and the strategic considerations.

## Inputs

- `intersection-points`: The four key price points from the PSM analysis
- `product-description`: The product or service being priced

## Outputs

- `price-recommendation`: The recommended price with rationale: where within the acceptable range to price, based on the positioning strategy (value, premium, or penetration)
- `price-sensitivity-report`: The complete PSM report: methodology, response summary, the four curves, the four intersection points, the acceptable range, and the pricing recommendation

## Process

1. State the acceptable price range: PMC to PME. Any price within this range will not trigger strong negative reactions from the majority of the market.
2. Choose where within the range to price based on strategy: at the OPP for minimum resistance (good for market entry), at the IDP for meeting expectations (good for competitive markets), or above the IDP toward the PME for premium positioning (good when the product has clear differentiation).
3. Compare the recommended price against current price (if one exists) and competitor prices. If the PSM suggests a higher price than currently charged, there may be room to increase. If lower, the current price may be suppressing demand.
4. Produce the final report: the survey methodology, the number of responses, the four cumulative curves (described or tabulated), the four intersection points with dollar values, the acceptable range, the recommended price, and the rationale for the recommendation.
