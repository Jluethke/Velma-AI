# Consensusconviction Test Info Distribution

Test whether the information needed sits with many people or concentrates in a few experts.

## Inputs

- `framed-decision`: The decision written as a single clear question.
- `stakes-summary`: What is at stake and who would carry it out.

## Outputs

- `info-distribution-score`: How distributed the needed information is.
- `info-holders`: Who holds the information the decision depends on.

## Process

1. Identify who holds the information the decision depends on.
2. Judge whether that information is spread across many people or held by a few.
3. Score how distributed the information is on a shared scale.
