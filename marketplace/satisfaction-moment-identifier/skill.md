# Satisfaction Moment Identifier

Find the moments in the customer journey when satisfaction is highest. The referral ask should land at a peak, not at signup.

## Inputs

- `motivation-map`: Why your customers refer
- `referral-value-design`: What value the referrer gets from the act

## Outputs

- `peak-satisfaction-moments`: The 3-5 moments in the customer journey when satisfaction is highest (first success, milestone reached, problem solved, compliment received)
- `ask-placement-plan`: Where to place the referral ask relative to each peak moment

## Process

1. Map the customer journey from first contact to longtime user. Identify the emotional peaks: first 'aha' moment, first measurable result, first time they told someone about it unprompted.
2. The referral ask goes immediately after the peak, not before. Asking before the peak feels premature. Asking at the peak converts because the person is already feeling generous.
3. Do not ask at signup. The person has not experienced value yet. Asking them to refer before they have received value is asking them to spend social capital on credit.
4. Test multiple placements. The optimal moment varies by product type: for consumer products, it is after first use. For services, it is after first result. For communities, it is after first meaningful interaction.
