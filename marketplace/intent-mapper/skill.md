# Intent Mapper

Map the top customer intents from ticket and chat data.

## Inputs

- `ticket-history`: Historical tickets and chat transcripts
- `channel-data`: Volume and type of inquiries by channel

## Outputs

- `intent-catalog`: Catalog of customer intents ranked by frequency
- `intent-examples`: Example utterances for each intent

## Process

1. Analyze ticket subjects and chat transcripts for recurring patterns.
2. Group into distinct intents.
3. Rank intents by frequency and resolution complexity.
4. Collect example utterances for each intent.
