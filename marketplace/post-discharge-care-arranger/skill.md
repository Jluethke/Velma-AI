# Post Discharge Care Arranger

Arrange post-discharge care including follow-ups and services.

## Inputs

- `readiness-status`: Discharge readiness assessment
- `patient-record`: Clinical record with diagnoses and treatment

## Outputs

- `followup-appointments`: Scheduled follow-up visits with providers
- `service-referrals`: Home health, rehab, or other service arrangements

## Process

1. Schedule follow-up appointments with appropriate providers.
2. Arrange home health, physical therapy, or skilled nursing if needed.
3. Coordinate durable medical equipment delivery.
