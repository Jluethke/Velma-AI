# Observation Statement Writer

Write the observation: the specific, factual, non-interpretive description of what happened. No adjectives, no intent attribution, no generalizations. 'You were late to three meetings this week' not 'You do not respect people's time.'

## Inputs

- `situation-description`: What happened
- `conversation-goal`: What you need from this conversation

## Outputs

- `observation-statement`: The factual, non-interpretive description of what happened
- `removed-interpretations`: Interpretations and judgments you removed from the observation

## Process

1. Write what happened using only observable facts: dates, times, specific actions, specific words said. No 'always' or 'never.'
2. Remove every adjective that is an interpretation. 'Late' is observable. 'Disrespectful' is an interpretation.
3. Remove every attribution of intent. You do not know why they did it. You know what they did.
4. Test: could a security camera or a transcript confirm every word in your observation? If not, it contains interpretation.
