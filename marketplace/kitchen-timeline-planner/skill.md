# Kitchen Timeline Planner

Plan the kitchen production timeline for the event.

## Inputs

- `proposed-menu`: Menu items and courses
- `ingredient-list`: Ingredients and quantities

## Outputs

- `production-schedule`: Time-sequenced kitchen prep and cooking plan
- `equipment-needs`: Equipment and space requirements

## Process

1. Map each menu item to its prep and cook time.
2. Sequence tasks to optimize kitchen workflow.
3. Identify equipment and staging needs.
4. Build the hour-by-hour production schedule.
