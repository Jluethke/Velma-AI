# Blue Ocean Reduce Pass

Decide which factors can be reduced well below the industry standard. Industries often overserve on factors in a race to match rivals, loading cost onto things customers do not need that much of.

## Inputs

- `competition-factors`: The factors the industry competes on
- `eliminate-list`: Factors already marked for removal

## Outputs

- `reduce-list`: Factors to dial well below industry norm
- `reduce-rationale`: Why over-serving on each one wastes cost

## Process

1. Look at the factors that survived elimination and ask which are overserved.
2. Find factors the industry escalates to beat rivals past the point customers care about.
3. Mark those to reduce well below the standard, and say how far.
4. Record the cost that the reduction frees for use elsewhere.
