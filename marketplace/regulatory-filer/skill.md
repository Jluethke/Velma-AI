# Regulatory Filer

Prepare and submit the regulatory filing for the new product.

## Inputs

- `coverage-specification`: Coverage terms and policy forms
- `rate-filing-support`: Actuarial justification for the rates

## Outputs

- `filing-package`: Complete regulatory filing package (forms, rates, rules, actuarial memorandum)
- `filing-status`: Status of the filing in each state (submitted, approved, objected)

## Process

1. Compile the filing package with forms, rates, rules, and the actuarial memorandum.
2. Submit the filing through SERFF or the state-specific filing system.
3. Track the review status and respond to any regulatory objections.
4. Obtain approval and document the effective dates by state.
