# Conflict Pattern Mapper

Identify recurring conflict patterns, triggers, and escalation cycles with de-escalation strategies.

## Inputs

- `recent_conflicts`: Recent disagreements or tension points
- `pain_points`: Known sore spots

## Outputs

- `conflict_patterns`: Named recurring patterns
- `trigger_map`: Triggers and early warning signs
- `de_escalation_tools`: Specific de-escalation techniques

## Process

1. List recent conflicts and categorize themes
2. Identify who initiates and how
3. Map escalation sequences
4. Design de-escalation interventions
5. Agree on timeout signals
