# Feedback Timing Planner

Plan when and where to deliver the feedback. Reinforcing feedback can be fairly public; redirecting feedback is always private. The setting and timing shape whether the message lands or triggers defense.

## Inputs

- `sbi-statement`: The built statement
- `evidence-notes`: The observations behind it

## Outputs

- `delivery-plan`: The chosen time, medium, and opening line that leads with the situation
- `setting-choice`: The setting: private for redirecting feedback, either for reinforcing

## Process

1. Choose the setting: private for redirecting feedback, and either private or public for reinforcing feedback depending on the person.
2. Pick a time inside the window when the person can absorb it, not right before a high-pressure moment.
3. Plan your opening line so it leads with the situation, not with a verdict.
4. Decide the medium: in person or a live call for anything sensitive, never a one-way written message for redirecting feedback.
