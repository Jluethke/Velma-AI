# Friction Engineering

Add friction to undesired behaviors and remove friction from desired behaviors by changing the environment.

## Inputs

- `cue-map`: Environmental cues for each behavior
- `friction-assessment`: Current friction levels for each behavior

## Outputs

- `friction-changes`: Specific changes to make: items to move, apps to rearrange, defaults to change, obstacles to add or remove
- `implementation-checklist`: A checklist of physical and digital changes to make in one session

## Process

1. For each desired behavior, reduce friction to one step or zero steps. Lay out workout clothes the night before (zero-step start). Put the book on the pillow (you see it when you get into bed). Put the healthy food at eye level in the fridge.
2. For each undesired behavior, add friction. Delete social media apps from the phone (you can still use them in a browser, but the extra steps reduce impulse use). Move snacks to a high shelf in a closed cabinet. Log out of streaming services after each use.
3. Change defaults. The default should be the desired behavior. If the TV is off by default and requires 3 steps to start, you will watch less. If the meditation app opens when you unlock your phone in the morning, you will meditate more.
4. Make the changes all at once. A gradual rollout lets the old cues persist. Clear the environment in one session.
