# Opportunity Scoring

Score your opportunity to perform the behavior, covering both the physical environment and the social environment around you.

## Inputs

- `framed-behavior`: The behavior stated as an observable action
- `past-attempt-notes`: The record of past attempts

## Outputs

- `opportunity-score`: A score for whether your time, environment, and social setting allow the behavior
- `opportunity-evidence`: The specific environmental or social barriers that lower the score

## Process

1. Ask whether your environment and schedule physically allow the behavior. Cooking on weeknights is blocked if you get home at nine with no groceries.
2. Separate physical opportunity from social opportunity. Physical is time, space, money, and tools. Social is whether the people around you make the behavior easy or hard.
3. Score opportunity from one to ten, where a low score means that even a capable and motivated person would struggle in your current setting.
4. Record the specific barriers. No time slot, no equipment, a household that eats differently, or a workplace norm are concrete and often changeable.
