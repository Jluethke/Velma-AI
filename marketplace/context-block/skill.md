# Context Block

Provide context in 2-3 sentences between the ask and the details.

## Inputs

- `opening-line`: The ask
- `email-purpose`: The background

## Outputs

- `context-block`: 2-3 sentences of context: why this matters, what led here, deadline pressures
- `context-sufficiency`: Whether the reader has enough context to act

## Process

1. Write 2-3 sentences of context, not a history.
2. Link to a document for complex context.
3. Include the consequence of not acting.
4. The context block should be skippable by readers who have context.
