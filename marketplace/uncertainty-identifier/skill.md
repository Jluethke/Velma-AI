# Uncertainty Identifier

Identify the uncertainties that will most impact your future. An uncertainty is something you cannot control and cannot predict with confidence.

## Inputs

- `strategic-context`: What you are planning for (a business, a career, a project, a market entry)
- `time-horizon`: How far forward to plan (1 year, 3 years, 5 years)

## Outputs

- `uncertainty-long-list`: Every uncertainty that could materially change the outcome, stated as a spectrum (e.g., 'interest rates: stay low vs. rise sharply')
- `top-two-uncertainties`: The two uncertainties with the highest combined impact and unpredictability, each stated as a spectrum

## Process

1. List every external factor you cannot control that could change the outcome: market demand, competitor actions, technology shifts, regulatory changes, economic conditions, talent availability, input costs, geopolitical events, health events.
2. For each, rate two dimensions: impact on your outcome (1-5) and unpredictability (1-5). High impact plus high unpredictability equals the most important uncertainties to plan for.
3. Select the top two. They become the axes of the scenario matrix. State each as a spectrum with two poles (e.g., 'AI adoption: slow and regulated vs. fast and unregulated').
4. Verify the two are independent: knowing the outcome of one should not tell you the outcome of the other. If they are correlated, replace one.
