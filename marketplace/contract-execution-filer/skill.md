# Contract Execution Filer

Execute the contract and file the fully signed version.

## Inputs

- `approved-contract`: The approved contract ready for signatures
- `approval-record`: Approval history

## Outputs

- `executed-contract`: The fully executed contract with all signatures
- `obligation-schedule`: Schedule of key obligations, milestones, and dates from the contract

## Process

1. Send the contract for signature (wet ink or e-signature).
2. Collect all signatures and verify execution.
3. Extract the key obligations and dates into the obligation schedule.
4. File the executed contract in the contract management system.
