# Champion Health Monitoring

Monitor the champion's engagement throughout the deal to detect early if they disengage or lose influence.

## Inputs

- `champion-candidate`: The champion
- `champion-toolkit`: The materials provided

## Outputs

- `engagement-signals`: Positive and negative signals about the champion's engagement and internal influence
- `risk-assessment`: Whether the champion is still active and influential, or whether the deal needs a new champion or a different approach

## Process

1. Track positive signals: the champion responds within 24 hours, provides internal updates unprompted, introduces you to new stakeholders, asks for additional materials.
2. Track negative signals: response times increase, meetings are rescheduled or cancelled, the champion stops providing internal context, they stop forwarding materials.
3. If negative signals appear, have a direct conversation: 'I notice things have slowed down. Is there something happening internally that I should know about?' A good champion will tell you.
4. If the champion has lost influence (new boss, organization change, role shift), you need a new champion. The deal is not dead, but it needs a new internal advocate.
