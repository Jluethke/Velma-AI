# Ad Campaign Objective Setter

Define the campaign objective, budget, and success metrics.

## Inputs

- `business-goal`: What the business wants to achieve
- `budget-range`: Available budget for the campaign

## Outputs

- `campaign-objective`: Objective, budget allocation, and KPIs
- `success-criteria`: Measurable thresholds for success

## Process

1. Select the campaign objective (awareness, consideration, conversion)
2. Allocate the budget across platforms and time
3. Define the KPIs and success thresholds
