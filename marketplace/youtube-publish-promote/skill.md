# Youtube Publish Promote

Publish the video and run the initial promotion.

## Inputs

- `video-file`: The finished video
- `optimized-metadata`: The title, description, tags, and configuration
- `thumbnail-file`: The thumbnail image

## Outputs

- `publish-record`: The published video URL and publish timestamp
- `promotion-actions`: List of promotion activities completed (social posts, community tab, email)

## Process

1. Upload the video with the optimized metadata and thumbnail.
2. Schedule or publish at the optimal time based on audience activity.
3. Post promotion to social media, community tab, and email list.
4. Respond to early comments to boost engagement signals.
