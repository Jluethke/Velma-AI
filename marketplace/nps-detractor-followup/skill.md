# Nps Detractor Followup

Follow up with detractors within the SLA to understand their concern and attempt recovery.

## Inputs

- `segmented-responses`: Responses with promoter/passive/detractor labels
- `nps-score`: The current NPS score for context

## Outputs

- `followup-log`: Record of each detractor follow-up (date, channel, outcome)
- `recovery-rate`: Percentage of detractors who were contacted and resolved

## Process

1. Route detractor responses to the follow-up team within the SLA (24 to 48 hours).
2. Contact the detractor to acknowledge their feedback and understand the root cause.
3. Log the follow-up outcome (resolved, escalated, no response, unresolvable).
4. Track the recovery rate and feed recurring themes to the product or operations team.
