# Specific Aims Drafter

Draft the specific aims page with a clear opening, knowledge gap statement, central hypothesis, and numbered aims with expected outcomes.

## Inputs

- `research-question`: The overarching question the proposed research addresses
- `preliminary-data`: Key preliminary results that support feasibility
- `funding-mechanism`: Grant type (R01, R21, NSF CAREER, etc.) with page and format requirements

## Outputs

- `specific-aims-draft`: One-page specific aims with opening paragraph, gap, hypothesis, and 2-3 aims
- `aim-milestones`: Measurable milestones and expected outcomes for each aim
- `significance-hook`: The core significance statement connecting the work to a broader impact

## Process

1. Write an opening paragraph establishing the importance of the research area
2. State the knowledge gap and what is unknown or unresolved
3. Present the central hypothesis and the rationale supporting it
4. Draft 2-3 specific aims with approach summaries and expected outcomes
5. End with a payoff statement describing the impact if all aims succeed
