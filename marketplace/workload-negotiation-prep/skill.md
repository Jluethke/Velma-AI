# Workload Negotiation Prep

Prepare to negotiate workload with manager: priorities, capacity, delegation, and timeline adjustments.

## Inputs

- `current_workload`: All current work responsibilities
- `boundary_list`: Work boundaries being set
- `manager_relationship`: Current dynamic with manager

## Outputs

- `priority_proposal`: Proposed priority ranking for manager
- `delegation_candidates`: Tasks to delegate with suggested assignees
- `conversation_plan`: Talking points and framing for the conversation

## Process

1. List all tasks and estimate hours per week
2. Propose priority ranking based on business impact
3. Identify tasks to delegate or defer
4. Frame the conversation around output quality not hours
5. Prepare for pushback scenarios
