# Policy Review Router

Route the policy for review and approval by stakeholders.

## Inputs

- `policy-draft`: Draft policy document
- `policy-metadata`: Policy metadata

## Outputs

- `approved-policy`: Approved policy with sign-off records
- `review-comments`: Comments and changes from reviewers

## Process

1. Identify the required reviewers (legal, compliance, business owners).
2. Distribute for review with a deadline.
3. Collect and incorporate feedback.
4. Obtain final approval from the policy owner.
