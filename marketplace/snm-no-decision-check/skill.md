# Snm No Decision Check

Decide whether this is a real no by weighing the cost of yes against the social cost of the no. The point is not to decline everything, it is to decline what you should. Compare honestly, because the hidden cost of a dishonest yes is almost always higher than it first appears.

## Inputs

- `cost-of-yes`: The real cost of agreeing
- `request-summary`: The request and what it commits you to

## Outputs

- `no-decision`: Whether this is a no, a yes, or a conditional yes, with the reasoning
- `decision-rationale`: The real reason behind the decision, which becomes the honest basis for the message

## Process

1. Weigh the cost of yes against the social cost of a clear no. The social cost of a no is usually smaller and shorter than people fear.
2. Recall what a dishonest yes costs: the missed deadline, the half-effort, the trust lost when you cannot deliver. That is the true comparison.
3. Decide: no, yes, or a conditional yes that trims the request to something you can give fully.
4. Write the real reason for the decision. This is for you now, and it becomes the honest basis for the brief reason you will give.
