# Data Sharing Discloser

Describe all data sharing with third parties and cross-border transfers.

## Inputs

- `processing-map`: Data processing map
- `data-systems`: Systems and third parties

## Outputs

- `third-party-disclosures`: List of all third-party data sharing
- `transfer-mechanisms`: Cross-border transfer mechanisms in use

## Process

1. List all third parties who receive personal data and why
2. Identify cross-border transfers and the destination countries
3. Document the transfer mechanism for each (SCCs, adequacy, BCRs)
