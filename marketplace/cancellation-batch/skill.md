# Cancellation Batch

Cancel the meetings tied to no decision and replace the information they shared with a written channel. Reclaimed time is the first and largest return, and a written update serves the same purpose without the synchronous cost.

## Inputs

- `no-decision-meetings`: Meetings enabling no decision

## Outputs

- `cancelled-meetings`: The meetings removed from the calendar
- `reclaimed-hours`: Annual attendee hours returned by cancelling

## Process

1. Cancel each no-decision meeting rather than shortening it.
2. Replace the information it shared with a written update people can read on their own time.
3. Confirm no real recurring decision was quietly riding on the cancelled meeting.
4. Total the attendee hours returned so the reclaimed capacity is visible.
