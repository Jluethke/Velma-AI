# Hipaa Training Assessor

Assess staff HIPAA training records and produce the compliance report.

## Inputs

- `breach-procedure-status`: Breach readiness assessment
- `baa-gaps`: BAA compliance gaps

## Outputs

- `training-compliance`: Staff training completion rates and gaps
- `hipaa-compliance-report`: Complete HIPAA compliance assessment report

## Process

1. Review training records for completion rates by department.
2. Identify staff who have not completed required HIPAA training.
3. Compile all findings into the HIPAA compliance assessment report.
