# Footprint Baseline Comparer

Compare the calculated footprint against baseline year and reduction targets.

## Inputs

- `emissions-by-scope`: Calculated emissions by scope and category
- `methodology-notes`: Methodology documentation

## Outputs

- `trend-analysis`: Year-over-year comparison of emissions against baseline and targets
- `carbon-footprint-report`: The complete carbon footprint report with findings and recommendations

## Process

1. Compare current period emissions to the baseline year by scope and category.
2. Assess progress against any reduction targets (science-based or internal).
3. Identify the largest emission sources and the categories with the most change.
4. Compile the full report with methodology, results, trends, and recommended actions.
