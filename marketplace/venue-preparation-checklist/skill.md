# Venue Preparation Checklist

Prepare the venue for game day: field setup, equipment check, scoreboard, and safety inspection.

## Inputs

- `game-details`: Teams, time, venue, officials, and any special requirements
- `venue-requirements`: Setup specifications for the sport and level of play

## Outputs

- `venue-readiness-report`: Confirmation that the venue is ready with any issues noted
- `equipment-check-record`: Record of equipment inspected and in place

## Process

1. Set up the playing surface (lines, goals, nets, bases, court markings).
2. Inspect safety equipment and first aid supplies.
3. Test the scoreboard, clock, and public address system.
4. Complete the venue readiness checklist.
