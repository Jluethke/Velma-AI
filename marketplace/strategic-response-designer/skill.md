# Strategic Response Designer

For each scenario, design the strategic response: what you would do if this future materializes.

## Inputs

- `scenario-matrix`: The four named scenarios
- `strategic-context`: The planning context

## Outputs

- `scenario-responses`: For each scenario: the strategic posture (grow, defend, pivot, exit), the key actions, and the resources required
- `common-actions`: Actions that are beneficial across three or four scenarios, which should be taken regardless of which scenario materializes

## Process

1. For each scenario, define the strategic posture: grow (invest aggressively), defend (protect current position), pivot (redirect to a new market or model), or exit (wind down and preserve capital).
2. For each posture, define the 3-5 key actions and the resources required. Be specific enough that you could start executing within 30 days of deciding.
3. Identify the common actions: things that help in three or four scenarios. These are your 'no regret' moves. Execute them now.
4. Identify the 'option' actions: things that are cheap to prepare now and valuable if a specific scenario materializes. These are your hedges.
