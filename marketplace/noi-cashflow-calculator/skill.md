# Noi Cashflow Calculator

Calculate NOI and cash flow and compare against budget.

## Inputs

- `reconciled-balance`: Reconciled operating account
- `reconciliation-adjustments`: Adjustments from reconciliation

## Outputs

- `financial-statements`: P&L, NOI, and cash flow statement
- `budget-variance-analysis`: Actuals vs budget by line item

## Process

1. Calculate gross income, operating expenses, and NOI.
2. Calculate cash flow after debt service.
3. Compare each line item against the budget and note variances.
