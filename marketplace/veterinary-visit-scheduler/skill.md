# Veterinary Visit Scheduler

Schedule and manage routine and emergency veterinary visits.

## Inputs

- `health-protocol`: Scheduled health interventions
- `herd-profile`: Herd size and location

## Outputs

- `vet-schedule`: Calendar of scheduled and recommended vet visits
- `emergency-contacts`: Vet and after-hours emergency contact list

## Process

1. Schedule routine herd checks and vaccination visits.
2. Set up pregnancy checks and reproductive management visits.
3. Establish the emergency call protocol and contact list.
4. Budget for veterinary services.
