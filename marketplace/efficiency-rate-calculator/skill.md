# Efficiency Rate Calculator

Calculate the efficiency rate and proficiency rate for each technician.

## Inputs

- `time-records`: Clock hours and flag hours for each technician
- `job-breakdown`: Flag hours by job type

## Outputs

- `efficiency-rates`: Efficiency (flag / clock) and proficiency (flag / available) for each technician
- `benchmark-comparison`: Each technician compared against shop average and industry benchmarks

## Process

1. Calculate efficiency rate as flag hours divided by clock hours for each technician.
2. Calculate proficiency rate as flag hours divided by available hours.
3. Compare each rate against the shop average and industry benchmarks (typically 100% to 125% efficiency).
4. Rank technicians by efficiency to identify top performers and those needing support.
