# Privacy Training Needs Analysis

Identify the training audiences and their privacy knowledge requirements.

## Inputs

- `organization-context`: Description of the organization and its data processing activities
- `regulatory-requirements`: Applicable privacy regulations and their training mandates

## Outputs

- `training-audiences`: Defined training audiences (all staff, data handlers, IT, developers, leadership) with their specific needs
- `knowledge-requirements`: The privacy topics each audience must understand

## Process

1. Identify the training audiences based on their role in data processing (general awareness, data handlers, privacy team, developers, leadership).
2. Define the knowledge requirements for each audience (general: what is personal data, rights, reporting; handlers: classification, retention, access; developers: privacy by design, minimization, security).
3. Review regulatory training requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA mandates) to ensure coverage.
4. Set the training frequency (onboarding, annual refresh, role change).
