# Growth Forecaster

Forecast infrastructure growth based on business projections, traffic trends, and planned feature launches.

## Inputs

- `utilization-data`: Current utilization metrics
- `business-projections`: Expected user growth, traffic, and feature plans

## Outputs

- `growth-forecast`: Projected resource demand over the planning horizon
- `forecast-assumptions`: Documented assumptions behind the forecast

## Process

1. Analyze historical growth trends from utilization data
2. Incorporate business projections for user growth and new features
3. Model resource demand at 3, 6, and 12 month horizons
4. Document the assumptions underlying each projection
