# Sla Review Renegotiator

Review SLA performance annually and renegotiate terms.

## Inputs

- `agreed-slas`: Current SLAs
- `performance-reports`: Historical performance data

## Outputs

- `updated-slas`: Updated SLAs for the next period
- `sla-management-document`: Complete SLA management document with history and current terms

## Process

1. Review the past year's SLA performance.
2. Identify SLAs that were consistently met, missed, or irrelevant.
3. Propose adjustments based on changing business needs.
4. Agree on updated terms and publish the SLA management document.
