# List Growth Planner

Plan and measure list growth tactics.

## Inputs

- `collection-points`: Current collection points
- `segment-map`: How segments map to content
- `hygiene-schedule`: The cleaning schedule

## Outputs

- `growth-tactics`: Ranked list of growth tactics with expected yield (lead magnets, referrals, partnerships, content upgrades)
- `list-management-plan`: The complete list management plan covering collection, segmentation, hygiene, and growth

## Process

1. Evaluate current growth rate and identify the gap to goal.
2. Design lead magnets for each major segment (ebook, checklist, template, free tool, webinar).
3. Plan referral and partnership tactics to reach new audiences.
4. Compile the full list management plan with metrics and review cadence.
