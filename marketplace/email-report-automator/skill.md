# Email Report Automator

Set up automated reporting from the dashboard.

## Inputs

- `dashboard-layout`: The built dashboard
- `kpi-definitions`: The KPIs and targets

## Outputs

- `automated-reports`: Configuration for automated report delivery (weekly digest, monthly review, alert-triggered)
- `email-analytics-plan`: The complete email analytics plan covering KPIs, pipeline, dashboard, and reporting

## Process

1. Configure the weekly digest email with headline metrics and top/bottom campaigns.
2. Configure the monthly review report with trend analysis and segment insights.
3. Configure alert-triggered reports for anomalies (bounce spike, complaint rate over 0.1%, significant drop in open rate).
4. Compile the full email analytics plan.
