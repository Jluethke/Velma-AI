# Warm Intro Forwardable Blurb Writer

Write the forwardable blurb, the single most important artifact in the chain: a short self-contained paragraph the connector can forward to the target with one line of their own, so making the introduction costs them 30 seconds instead of real effort.

## Inputs

- `selected-connectors`: The chosen connector per target
- `connector-fit-rationale`: Why each connector fits and the cost to them

## Outputs

- `forwardable-blurb`: A short paragraph the connector can forward as-is: who you are, why you are reaching out to this target, and what you are asking for
- `blurb-variants`: Versions tuned to the ask (advice, sale, hire) and to the target's likely priorities

## Process

1. Write the blurb so the connector does zero work. It should say who you are in one line, why this specific target (not a generic pitch), and the single clear ask, short enough to forward from a phone.
2. Make the ask small and concrete. A 20 minute call about a specific topic converts far better than an open-ended request to connect, because the target can gauge the cost immediately.
3. Lead with something the target will recognize as relevant to them, not with your credentials. The target reads the forward asking why should I care, and the first sentence has to answer that.
4. Write variants for the different ask types and target priorities, so the connector can pick the one that fits, and so you are not sending a sales-shaped blurb when the ask is really for advice.
