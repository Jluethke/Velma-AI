# Scope Review Checkpoint

At each major milestone, review the scope: is the remaining scope achievable within the remaining constraints?

## Inputs

- `scope-drift-report`: The current scope status
- `constraints`: The remaining time, budget, and quality targets

## Outputs

- `scope-vs-constraints-assessment`: An honest assessment: can the remaining scope be delivered within the remaining constraints? If not, what must be cut or what constraints must be relaxed?
- `scope-adjustment-recommendation`: The recommended scope adjustments: items to defer to a future phase, items to simplify, or constraints to relax

## Process

1. At each milestone, measure: how much scope has been delivered (percentage of the in-scope list completed), how much time has been consumed (percentage of the deadline used), and how much budget has been spent.
2. If scope delivered is behind the constraint consumption (e.g., 40% of scope done but 60% of time used), the project will miss the deadline unless scope is cut or the deadline is extended.
3. Recommend specific cuts: which in-scope items can be deferred to a future phase with the least impact on the project's value? Cut from the bottom of the priority list, not the middle.
4. Present the assessment to stakeholders: 'At the current pace, we can deliver [list] by the deadline. [These items] would need to move to the next phase. Alternatively, we can extend the deadline by [X weeks] to include everything.'
