# Constraint Difficulty Increase

Increase the difficulty of practice by adding constraints that force you out of your comfort zone.

## Inputs

- `plateau-confirmation`: Confirmation that this is a real plateau
- `skill-description`: The skill being practiced

## Outputs

- `constraint-options`: 3-5 constraints you can add to your practice to increase difficulty (time pressure, resource limits, additional requirements)
- `selected-constraint`: The constraint that is most likely to push you past the plateau, with the rationale

## Process

1. List 3-5 constraints you can add. For a musician: play the piece 20% faster. For a writer: write the same quality in half the word count. For a coder: solve the problem without using a specific library. For a speaker: present with no notes.
2. The right constraint makes your current approach fail, forcing you to develop a new, better approach. If the constraint does not make you uncomfortable, it is not a real constraint.
3. Select the constraint that targets the specific part of your practice that has become automatic. The automatic part is where you stopped improving because you stopped thinking.
4. Apply the constraint for 2 weeks. If your performance drops initially, that is expected. The constraint is exposing a weakness that was hidden by your comfortable routine.
