# Proposal Compiler

Compile, review, and submit the final proposal.

## Inputs

- `draft-sections`: All drafted sections
- `rfp-document`: Original RFP for compliance check

## Outputs

- `final-proposal`: Compiled and reviewed proposal document
- `submission-record`: Proof of submission with timestamp

## Process

1. Compile all sections into the required format.
2. Verify compliance with every stated requirement.
3. Conduct a final review for consistency and accuracy.
4. Submit by the deadline and record the submission.
