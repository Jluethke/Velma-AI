# Survey Response Analyzer

Analyze survey scores and verbatim comments.

## Inputs

- `response-data`: Raw survey responses
- `customer-segments`: Segments for breakout analysis

## Outputs

- `score-analysis`: Aggregate and segmented scores with trends
- `theme-analysis`: Themes from verbatim comments ranked by frequency

## Process

1. Calculate aggregate scores and breakdowns by segment.
2. Analyze verbatim comments for recurring themes.
3. Identify drivers of high and low scores.
4. Compare against prior periods for trends.
