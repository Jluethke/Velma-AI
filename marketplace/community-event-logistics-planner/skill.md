# Community Event Logistics Planner

Plan the logistics: venue, food, activities, speakers, and supplies.

## Inputs

- `event-brief`: Event purpose and specifications
- `budget-allocation`: Budget by category

## Outputs

- `logistics-plan`: Venue, layout, food plan, activity schedule, speaker lineup, and supply list
- `vendor-list`: External vendors or suppliers with contact info, orders, and delivery schedule

## Process

1. Select and reserve the venue (own facility or external), confirm capacity and layout.
2. Plan the food: catering, potluck, food trucks, or concessions with quantities and dietary accommodations.
3. Schedule the activities, speakers, or entertainment with time slots.
4. Create the supply and equipment list: tables, chairs, decorations, signage, AV, and safety items.
