# Timeline Reconstruction

Walk the customer through the decision timeline: from the first thought of switching to the moment of purchase.

## Inputs

- `interview-list`: Customers to interview
- `interview-guide`: The JTBD interview guide

## Outputs

- `timeline-narratives`: For each customer: the full story from trigger event to purchase, with specific dates, actions, and emotions at each point
- `trigger-events`: The specific events that caused each customer to start looking for a solution

## Process

1. Start with: 'Take me back to when you first thought about [solving this problem / buying this product / switching from what you were using]. What was happening?' Get the specific moment, not a general timeframe.
2. Probe the trigger: 'What happened that made you start looking? Was there a specific event, a frustration, or a conversation?' The trigger reveals the 'job' the customer was trying to get done.
3. Walk forward through the timeline: 'After that moment, what did you do first? What did you try? What did you search for? Who did you talk to?' Map every step from trigger to purchase.
4. At each step, ask about emotions: 'How did you feel at that point? Were you frustrated? Excited? Anxious?' Emotions reveal the real motivation, which is often different from the stated reason.
