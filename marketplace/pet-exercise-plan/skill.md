# Pet Exercise Plan

Build an exercise and enrichment plan appropriate for species, breed, age, and energy level.

## Inputs

- `pet_type`: Species and breed
- `energy_level`: Activity level
- `living_space`: Yard, apartment, etc.
- `owner_schedule`: Available time for exercise

## Outputs

- `exercise_routine`: Daily exercise plan
- `enrichment_activities`: Mental stimulation activities
- `socialization_plan`: Interaction with other animals and people

## Process

1. Determine daily exercise needs by breed and age
2. Build walk or play schedule
3. Plan mental enrichment activities
4. Include socialization opportunities
5. Adjust for weather and seasonal changes
