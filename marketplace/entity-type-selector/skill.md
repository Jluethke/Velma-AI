# Entity Type Selector

Select the appropriate entity type based on business goals and tax considerations.

## Inputs

- `business-plan`: Business plan and ownership structure
- `tax-goals`: Tax optimization goals and investor requirements

## Outputs

- `entity-type`: Recommended entity type with rationale
- `formation-state`: Recommended state of formation

## Process

1. Evaluate LLC, C-Corp, S-Corp, and partnership structures
2. Consider liability protection, tax treatment, and investor compatibility
3. Select the state of formation based on business needs
