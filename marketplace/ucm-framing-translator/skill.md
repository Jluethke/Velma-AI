# Ucm Framing Translator

Translate your topic into your manager's terms. The same fact can be framed as a technical detail or as a risk to a goal they own. Framed in their terms, your message connects to something they already care about, and it earns attention instead of competing for it.

## Inputs

- `manager-priorities`: What the manager cares about most
- `topic`: The thing you need to communicate

## Outputs

- `reframed-topic`: Your topic restated in terms of the manager's priorities and goals
- `their-language-map`: The words and metrics your manager uses, to replace your internal terms

## Process

1. Connect your topic to the top priority it touches. A migration delay becomes a risk to the launch date the manager owns.
2. Translate internal terms into the manager's language. Replace the system name with the outcome it affects.
3. Lead with the effect on their goal, not the mechanism. They need the mechanism only if they ask.
4. Check the frame against their pressures. A message framed around last quarter's priority lands on a manager who has moved on.
