# Grocery Spending Audit

Audit current grocery spending patterns to identify waste, impulse buys, and savings opportunities.

## Inputs

- `monthly_grocery_spend`: Current monthly grocery spending
- `household_size`: Number of people
- `shopping_frequency`: How often shopping occurs

## Outputs

- `spending_breakdown`: Where money goes by category
- `waste_estimate`: Estimated food waste percentage
- `savings_opportunities`: Identified areas to save

## Process

1. Categorize recent grocery purchases
2. Identify repeat impulse purchases
3. Estimate food thrown away monthly
4. Compare spending to household benchmarks
5. Flag highest-impact savings areas
