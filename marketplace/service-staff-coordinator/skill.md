# Service Staff Coordinator

Coordinate service staff for the event.

## Inputs

- `production-schedule`: Kitchen timeline
- `event-requirements`: Event details and service style

## Outputs

- `staffing-plan`: Staff assignments with roles and timing
- `catering-plan`: Final catering plan combining menu, timeline, and staff

## Process

1. Calculate the server-to-guest ratio by service style.
2. Assign staff to stations and roles.
3. Schedule arrival, briefing, and breakdown times.
4. Produce the final catering plan.
