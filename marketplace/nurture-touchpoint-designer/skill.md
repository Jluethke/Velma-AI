# Nurture Touchpoint Designer

Create the nurture touchpoints: email, retargeting, and content offers.

## Inputs

- `content-map`: Content assigned to funnel stages
- `segment-definitions`: Lead segments

## Outputs

- `touchpoint-plan`: Sequence of touchpoints with channel and timing
- `offer-list`: Content offers and gated assets for each touchpoint

## Process

1. Design the touchpoint sequence for each segment
2. Select the channel for each touchpoint (email, ad, in-app)
3. Define the content offer or CTA for each touchpoint
