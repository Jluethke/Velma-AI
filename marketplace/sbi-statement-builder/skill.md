# Sbi Statement Builder

Build the feedback as a Situation-Behavior-Impact statement. Name the specific situation, describe the observable behavior without interpretation, and state the concrete impact, which keeps the feedback factual rather than a character judgment.

## Inputs

- `feedback-worthiness`: Whether to reinforce or redirect
- `timing-window`: When the feedback must land

## Outputs

- `sbi-statement`: The feedback written as situation, then observable behavior, then concrete impact
- `evidence-notes`: The specific observations behind the statement, stripped of motive-reading

## Process

1. Write the situation: the specific time and place, so the person can picture the moment.
2. Write the behavior as what you observed, not what you assume they intended, such as that they interrupted twice rather than that they were dismissive.
3. Write the impact: what resulted, for the work, the team, or you.
4. Strip out labels and motive-reading; the statement should be something the person could have seen on a recording.
