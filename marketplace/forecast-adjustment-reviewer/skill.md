# Forecast Adjustment Reviewer

Adjust the statistical forecast with qualitative inputs.

## Inputs

- `statistical-forecast`: Baseline statistical forecast by product and period
- `market-intelligence`: Qualitative inputs from sales, marketing, and market research

## Outputs

- `adjusted-forecast`: Forecast adjusted for known events and qualitative inputs
- `adjustment-log`: Log of each adjustment with rationale

## Process

1. Layer promotion and event impacts onto the baseline
2. Incorporate sales intelligence and customer commitments
3. Document each adjustment with the source and rationale
4. Produce the adjusted forecast
