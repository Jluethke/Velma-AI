# Food Cost Analyzer

Analyze food cost percentage and trends.

## Inputs

- `inventory-variance`: Variance from physical counts
- `waste-log`: Waste records

## Outputs

- `food-cost-report`: Food cost percentage by category and trend
- `inventory-management-report`: Final inventory management report with action items

## Process

1. Calculate food cost percentage (cost of goods / revenue).
2. Break down cost by menu category.
3. Identify the top contributors to variance.
4. Recommend actions to reduce waste and cost.
