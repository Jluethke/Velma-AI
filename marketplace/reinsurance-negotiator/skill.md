# Reinsurance Negotiator

Negotiate terms and pricing with reinsurers and bind the treaties.

## Inputs

- `submission-package`: The distributed submission
- `target-reinsurer-list`: Target reinsurers and their indications

## Outputs

- `bound-treaties`: Bound reinsurance treaties with final terms, pricing, and reinsurer participation
- `reinsurance-placement-record`: Complete placement record documenting the marketing process and final program

## Process

1. Collect and compare indications from the responding reinsurers.
2. Negotiate terms, pricing, and conditions with the lead reinsurer.
3. Finalize the panel with participating reinsurers and their shares.
4. Bind the treaties and document the placement record.
