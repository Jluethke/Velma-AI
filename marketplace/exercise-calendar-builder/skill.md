# Exercise Calendar Builder

Build the annual exercise calendar with scenario types, frequency, and escalating complexity.

## Inputs

- `program-charter`: Program scope and objectives
- `staffing-plan`: Team availability and capabilities

## Outputs

- `exercise-calendar`: 12-month schedule of exercises with scenario types and target systems
- `complexity-progression`: How exercise difficulty increases over the year

## Process

1. Schedule monthly or quarterly exercises depending on team capacity
2. Rotate scenario types (network, application, social engineering, physical)
3. Increase complexity over the year (automated scans to full adversary simulation)
4. Avoid scheduling during change freezes or critical business periods
