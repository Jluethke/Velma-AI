# Audience Baseline Analyzer

Analyze the current audience: demographics, engagement, and growth rate.

## Inputs

- `platform-accounts`: The creator's accounts across platforms
- `analysis-period`: The time range to analyze (e.g., last 90 days)

## Outputs

- `audience-profile`: Demographics, interests, and behavior patterns of the current audience
- `growth-baseline`: Current growth rate, engagement rate, and retention metrics

## Process

1. Pull audience demographics from each platform's analytics.
2. Calculate the growth rate (new followers per period) and churn rate.
3. Identify the top sources of new followers (search, recommendations, referrals, paid).
4. Establish the baseline metrics to measure growth experiments against.
