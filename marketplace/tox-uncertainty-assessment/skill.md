# Tox Uncertainty Assessment

Assess the uncertainties in the risk assessment including data gaps, model assumptions, and variability in the exposed population.

## Inputs

- `risk-characterization`: The computed risk metrics
- `uncertainty-factor-table`: Applied uncertainty factors and their justification

## Outputs

- `uncertainty-narrative`: Qualitative and quantitative description of key uncertainties
- `confidence-rating`: Overall confidence in the risk estimate (high, medium, low) with justification

## Process

1. Catalog the data gaps in hazard identification, dose-response, and exposure
2. Evaluate how each uncertainty affects the direction and magnitude of the risk estimate
3. Perform sensitivity analysis on the most influential parameters
4. Assign an overall confidence rating
