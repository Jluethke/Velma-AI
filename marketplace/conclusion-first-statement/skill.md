# Conclusion First Statement

Write the conclusion first. The reader knows your answer in the first sentence.

## Inputs

- `topic`: What you are writing about
- `audience`: Who will read this and what decision they need to make

## Outputs

- `conclusion-statement`: The one-sentence conclusion that answers the reader's question
- `reader-question`: The question the reader has that this document answers

## Process

1. Ask: what question does the reader have? 'Should we launch in Q1?' 'Is this project worth funding?' The conclusion is the answer to that question.
2. Write the conclusion as one sentence: 'We should launch in Q1 because the market window closes in March and the product is ready.' This is the first sentence of the document.
3. Do not build up to the conclusion. Academic writing builds argument, then concludes. Business writing concludes, then supports.
4. If you have multiple conclusions, you have multiple documents. Each document answers one question with one conclusion.
