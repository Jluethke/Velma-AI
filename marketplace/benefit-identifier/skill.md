# Benefit Identifier

Identify and estimate all benefits: revenue, savings, and intangible value.

## Inputs

- `decision-framework`: Alternatives and evaluation criteria
- `evaluation-scope`: Time horizon and boundary conditions

## Outputs

- `benefit-estimates`: Itemized benefit estimates for each alternative by period
- `benefit-assumptions`: Assumptions behind each benefit estimate

## Process

1. Identify revenue benefits (new sales, price increases, market expansion).
2. Identify cost-saving benefits (efficiency gains, headcount reduction, waste reduction).
3. Estimate intangible benefits and assign proxy values where possible.
4. Assign benefits to time periods across the evaluation horizon.
