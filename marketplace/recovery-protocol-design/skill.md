# Recovery Protocol Design

Design a recovery protocol targeted to your dominant dimension and root causes.

## Inputs

- `exhaustion-causes`: What is draining you
- `detachment-causes`: What is disconnecting you
- `inefficacy-causes`: What is making you feel ineffective

## Outputs

- `recovery-plan`: Specific daily and weekly actions for recovery: what to add (rest, boundaries, meaning, wins) and what to remove (overcommitments, energy drains, toxic interactions)
- `boundary-changes`: Boundaries to set: work hours, email access, meeting limits, project load

## Process

1. For exhaustion: the primary intervention is reducing demand and increasing recovery. Set hard work hours and enforce them. Take one full day off per week with zero work. Sleep 8 hours. Cancel non-essential commitments for 30 days.
2. For detachment: the primary intervention is reconnecting with purpose. Schedule 1:1 conversations with people your work helps. Revisit why you chose this work. If the values mismatch is real (not just burnout-colored), begin exploring alternatives.
3. For inefficacy: the primary intervention is stacking small wins. Choose 3 tasks you can complete this week. Complete them. Celebrate them. Ask a trusted colleague for honest feedback on your recent work. Often, the feeling of inefficacy is disconnected from actual performance.
4. The 30-day recovery protocol: Week 1 = emergency stabilization (sleep, boundaries, cancel commitments). Week 2-3 = targeted intervention (based on dominant dimension). Week 4 = reassessment.
