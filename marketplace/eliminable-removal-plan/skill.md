# Eliminable Removal Plan

Remove eliminable decisions at their source rather than deciding them faster. A decision that should never reach you is fixed by changing the setup that routes it to you, not by handling it more efficiently.

## Inputs

- `categorized-decisions`: Decisions tagged by category
- `decision-rules`: Rules already drafted for automatable decisions

## Outputs

- `elimination-actions`: Changes that stop eliminable decisions from reaching you
- `removed-count`: How many daily decisions the changes remove

## Process

1. For each eliminable decision, find the setup that routes it to you in the first place.
2. Design the change that stops it at the source, such as a default, a standing choice, or a handoff.
3. Confirm the change does not simply recreate the decision somewhere else in your day.
4. Total the decisions removed so the reduction in load is measurable.
