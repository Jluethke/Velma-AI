# Adjuvant Screening Protocol

Screen adjuvant candidates for compatibility with the selected antigen, measuring innate immune activation and formulation stability.

## Inputs

- `antigen-candidates`: Lead antigen candidates from selection step
- `target-immune-response`: Desired immune profile: Th1, Th2, mucosal, or balanced

## Outputs

- `adjuvant-shortlist`: Ranked adjuvants with compatibility and activation data
- `adjuvant-stability-data`: Accelerated stability results for antigen-adjuvant combinations

## Process

1. Compile candidate adjuvants matched to the desired immune polarization
2. Run in vitro innate activation assays (dendritic cell maturation, cytokine panel) for each combination
3. Measure antigen integrity after formulation with each adjuvant under accelerated stability conditions
4. Rank combinations by activation strength, antigen stability, and regulatory precedent
