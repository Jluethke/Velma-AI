# Social Ad Creative Designer

Design the ad creative: images, video, and copy for each platform.

## Inputs

- `social-ad-brief`: Campaign brief
- `platform-shortlist`: Platforms and their ad specs

## Outputs

- `social-ad-creatives`: Creative assets per platform and format
- `copy-variants`: Copy variations for testing

## Process

1. Design visual assets to platform specs
2. Write ad copy with headline, body, and CTA
3. Create at least two copy variants for A/B testing
