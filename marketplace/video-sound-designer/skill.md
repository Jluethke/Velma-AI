# Video Sound Designer

Add graphics, music, and sound design to the edited video.

## Inputs

- `fine-cut-timeline`: Picture-locked edit timeline
- `edit-notes`: Notes on graphics and audio needs

## Outputs

- `mixed-timeline`: Timeline with graphics, music, and mixed audio
- `audio-stems`: Separate audio tracks (dialogue, music, effects)

## Process

1. Design and place motion graphics and titles
2. Select and place the music track
3. Create and layer sound effects and ambient audio
4. Mix all audio elements to broadcast levels
