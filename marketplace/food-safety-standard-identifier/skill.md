# Food Safety Standard Identifier

Identify the applicable food safety standard for the operation.

## Inputs

- `operation-profile`: Crop types, markets served, and buyer requirements
- `regulatory-context`: Federal and state food safety regulations that apply

## Outputs

- `applicable-standard`: The standard to comply with (GAP, FSMA, organic, buyer-specific)
- `requirements-summary`: Key requirements of the applicable standard

## Process

1. Determine which regulations apply based on crop type and sales volume.
2. Identify buyer-specific requirements (third-party audits, certifications).
3. Select the standard that satisfies regulatory and market requirements.
4. Summarize the key requirements to address.
