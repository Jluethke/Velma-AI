# Commitment Journey Mapper

Map the full journey from first contact to desired outcome. Identify the current big asks and the gaps where people drop off.

## Inputs

- `desired-outcome`: What you want the person to do (buy, join, commit, adopt, partner, hire you)
- `current-journey`: The current steps a person goes through from first awareness to the desired outcome

## Outputs

- `journey-map`: Each step in the current journey with the ask size (what the person must commit at that step) and the drop-off rate
- `big-ask-locations`: Steps where the ask jumps from small to large, creating a commitment gap that causes drop-off

## Process

1. Map every step from first contact to desired outcome. For each step, write what the person must do (click, sign up, provide info, spend time, spend money, tell someone).
2. Rate each ask on a scale of 1-10 where 1 is effortless (click a link) and 10 is a major commitment (sign a contract, pay thousands, change a habit permanently).
3. Find the gaps: where does the ask jump from a 2 to a 7 in a single step? These gaps are where people drop off. The jump is too big.
4. Note the current drop-off rate at each step if data is available. The highest drop-off rates will correlate with the biggest ask jumps.
