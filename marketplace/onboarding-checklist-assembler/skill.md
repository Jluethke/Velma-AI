# Onboarding Checklist Assembler

Assemble the complete onboarding record with all completed items.

## Inputs

- `prep-checklist`: Pre-arrival checklist
- `checkin-assessment`: 90-day assessment
- `policy-acknowledgments`: Signed policies

## Outputs

- `onboarding-record`: Complete onboarding record for HR files
- `onboarding-feedback`: New hire feedback on the onboarding experience

## Process

1. Compile all checklist items and completion dates
2. Record the 90-day assessment outcome
3. Collect feedback from the new hire on the onboarding experience
4. File the complete record
