# Retainer Renewal Review

Review and renew or adjust the retainer at the end of the period.

## Inputs

- `overage-resolution`: Any overage resolutions from the period
- `updated-terms`: Current terms including any adjustments

## Outputs

- `renewal-decision`: Whether the retainer is renewed, adjusted, or ended
- `retainer-record`: Complete record of the retainer period including utilization, payments, and outcomes

## Process

1. Review the full period's utilization, overages, and client satisfaction.
2. Propose renewal terms based on actual usage patterns.
3. Negotiate any rate or scope changes for the next period.
4. Execute the renewal agreement or close out the retainer.
5. Archive the period record.
