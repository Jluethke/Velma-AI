# Vital Few Deep Dive

Deep-dive into the vital few (the top 20%): understand what makes them different and how to get more of them.

## Inputs

- `pareto-curve`: The cumulative distribution
- `pareto-ratio`: The actual concentration ratio

## Outputs

- `vital-few-profile`: The common characteristics of the top 20% of inputs (what do the best customers, highest-value activities, or most-used features have in common?)
- `replication-strategy`: How to find or create more inputs that match the vital-few profile

## Process

1. List the vital few (top 20% of inputs by output). What do they have in common? For customers: industry, size, use case, acquisition channel. For activities: type of work, time of day, who is involved.
2. The common characteristics of the vital few are your targeting criteria. If your best customers are all mid-market SaaS companies acquired through referrals, your growth strategy is: more mid-market SaaS referrals.
3. Identify what you are doing for the vital few that you are not doing for the rest. Sometimes the vital few produce more because they receive better service, not because they are inherently better.
4. Design a replication strategy: how to find or create more inputs that match the vital-few profile. This is your growth playbook.
