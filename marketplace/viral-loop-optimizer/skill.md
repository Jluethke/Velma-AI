# Viral Loop Optimizer

Optimize the K-factor by increasing invitation rate or invitation conversion rate. Small improvements in either component multiply through the loop.

## Inputs

- `k-factor-components`: The invitation rate and conversion rate
- `loop-friction-points`: The friction in the referral loop

## Outputs

- `optimization-plan`: Specific changes to improve each component: make inviting easier (rate), make the invitation more persuasive (conversion), reduce friction in the invited user's conversion path
- `projected-k-factor`: The expected K-factor after optimizations, with the assumptions behind the projection

## Process

1. To increase invitation rate: make the invite action visible and easy. Trigger it at moments of delight (after the user experiences value). Reward the referrer (discount, credit, recognition).
2. To increase conversion rate: make the invitation personal (from a named person, not the company). Show the invitee what they will gain. Reduce the steps to convert (pre-filled forms, single-click sign-up).
3. Test each optimization independently. Measure the impact on the specific component and on the overall K-factor.
4. Small improvements compound: increasing invitation rate from 0.5 to 0.7 and conversion from 0.3 to 0.4 moves K from 0.15 to 0.28, nearly doubling organic growth contribution.
