# Vaccine Regulatory Pre Submission

Assemble the regulatory pre-submission dossier (pre-IND or scientific advice) with CMC, nonclinical, and proposed clinical sections.

## Inputs

- `optimized-formulation`: CMC data for the vaccine formulation
- `correlate-analysis`: Preclinical efficacy and correlate data
- `target-product-profile`: Proposed TPP with immune response thresholds

## Outputs

- `pre-submission-dossier`: Complete pre-IND or scientific advice briefing document
- `regulatory-questions`: Specific questions for the agency meeting agenda

## Process

1. Compile CMC section: manufacturing process, analytical methods, stability, and specifications
2. Compile nonclinical section: toxicology, immunogenicity, challenge study results, and correlate analysis
3. Draft proposed Phase 1 clinical design: population, dose escalation, endpoints, and stopping rules
4. Format the briefing document per agency template and draft the meeting request with key questions
