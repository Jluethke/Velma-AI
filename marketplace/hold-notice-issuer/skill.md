# Hold Notice Issuer

Issue the litigation hold notice with preservation instructions.

## Inputs

- `custodian-list`: Custodian list
- `hold-scope`: Hold scope

## Outputs

- `hold-notice`: Litigation hold notice document
- `distribution-record`: Record of notice distribution to all custodians

## Process

1. Draft the hold notice with clear preservation instructions
2. Distribute to all custodians and their managers
3. Record the distribution date and method for each recipient
