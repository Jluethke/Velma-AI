# Environmental Control Implementer

Implement environmental controls for infection prevention.

## Inputs

- `precaution-type`: Type of precautions required
- `affected-scope`: Areas requiring controls

## Outputs

- `environmental-plan`: Cleaning, ventilation, and signage requirements
- `implementation-checklist`: Tasks to complete and verify

## Process

1. Set up enhanced cleaning schedules for affected areas.
2. Adjust ventilation and air handling as needed.
3. Post isolation signage and supply PPE at room entry.
