# Resolution Drafter

Draft the board resolution with the required recitals and operative clauses.

## Inputs

- `resolution-type`: Type of resolution
- `approval-requirements`: Quorum and voting requirements

## Outputs

- `draft-resolution`: The draft resolution ready for circulation
- `supporting-materials`: Any supporting documents needed for the board's review

## Process

1. Draft the resolution with recitals (background facts) and operative clauses (the action authorized).
2. Include the effective date and any conditions.
3. Attach or reference any supporting materials the board needs to review.
4. Circulate the draft for legal review before sending to the board.
