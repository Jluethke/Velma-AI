# Dashboard Audience Analyzer

Identify the dashboard audience and the decisions they make.

## Inputs

- `stakeholder-list`: List of people who will use the dashboard
- `business-context`: Description of the business area the dashboard covers

## Outputs

- `audience-profile`: Who the users are and what decisions they need to make
- `information-needs`: What questions the dashboard must answer for each audience

## Process

1. List each stakeholder group and their role.
2. For each group, identify the decisions they make weekly or monthly.
3. Map each decision to the information needed to make it.
