# Youtube Performance Analyzer

Analyze video and channel performance metrics.

## Inputs

- `publish-record`: The published video details
- `promotion-actions`: What promotion was done

## Outputs

- `performance-report`: Metrics report (views, CTR, retention, subscribers gained, revenue)
- `channel-ops-plan`: Updated operations plan with lessons applied

## Process

1. Pull analytics after the initial performance window (48 hours, 7 days, 28 days).
2. Evaluate click-through rate, average view duration, and retention curve.
3. Compare against channel benchmarks and prior videos.
4. Document what worked and what to adjust for the next video.
