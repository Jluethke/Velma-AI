# Optionality Maximizer

Maximize optionality: maintain multiple paths forward so that disorder creates options, not dead ends.

## Inputs

- `system-description`: The system context
- `fragility-assessment`: Where the system is fragile

## Outputs

- `current-options`: The options the system currently holds and their value in different scenarios
- `option-acquisition-plan`: New options to acquire, with the acquisition cost and the scenarios where each pays off

## Process

1. Inventory current options: skills, relationships, contracts with exit clauses, liquid assets, transferable capabilities, multiple revenue streams, diversified customer base.
2. For each option, score its value across multiple scenarios: economic downturn, technology disruption, competitive entry, regulatory change, personal health event.
3. Identify the options that are cheap to hold and valuable in multiple scenarios. These are asymmetric bets: low cost, high potential payoff.
4. Acquire the highest-value options first. An option that costs 1% of revenue to maintain and protects against 20% of scenarios is a strong investment.
