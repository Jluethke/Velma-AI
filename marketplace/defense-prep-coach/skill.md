# Defense Prep Coach

Prepare for the thesis defense with presentation structure, anticipated questions, practice sessions, and procedural logistics.

## Inputs

- `complete-thesis`: Final thesis document submitted to the committee
- `committee-members`: Names and expertise areas of each committee member
- `defense-format`: Presentation length, Q&A format, and institutional procedures

## Outputs

- `defense-slides`: Structured presentation covering introduction through conclusions
- `anticipated-questions`: List of likely questions mapped to committee member expertise
- `logistics-checklist`: Room booking, equipment, recording, and post-defense paperwork

## Process

1. Structure the presentation: motivation, gap, methods, key results, implications
2. Create clear slides with one message per slide and minimal text
3. Anticipate questions from each committee member based on their expertise
4. Practice the presentation three times, adjusting for time and clarity
5. Confirm all logistics (room, projector, remote access, forms, signatures)
