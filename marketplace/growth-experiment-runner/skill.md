# Growth Experiment Runner

Run the growth experiments and collect results.

## Inputs

- `growth-levers`: The prioritized growth tactics
- `experiment-plan`: The experiment design for each tactic

## Outputs

- `experiment-results`: Results of each growth experiment (metrics versus baseline)
- `winning-tactics`: Which tactics produced meaningful growth above baseline

## Process

1. Execute each experiment for the planned duration.
2. Track the growth metrics daily or weekly during the experiment.
3. Compare results against the baseline to determine statistical significance.
4. Identify the winning tactics and the magnitude of their impact.
