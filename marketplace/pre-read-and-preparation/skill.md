# Pre Read And Preparation

Send a pre-read 24 hours before the meeting with the decision, options, and relevant data.

## Inputs

- `decision-statement`: The decision
- `decision-options`: Options with pros/cons
- `required-attendees`: Who is coming

## Outputs

- `pre-read-document`: A 1-2 page document sent 24 hours before: the decision to make, the options with pros/cons, the relevant data, and the recommended option
- `preparation-request`: What each attendee should come prepared to discuss

## Process

1. Write the pre-read: one page maximum. Decision statement at the top, 2-4 options with pros/cons, relevant data, and your recommendation if you have one.
2. Send it 24 hours before the meeting with the expectation that attendees read it.
3. Include a recommendation if you have one. This is not pre-deciding; it gives the discussion a starting point.
4. Ask each attendee to come with their perspective on the options, not with new options. New options discovered in the meeting derail the decision.
