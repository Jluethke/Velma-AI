# Esg Report Reviewer

Route the ESG report for internal review and approval before publication.

## Inputs

- `esg-report-draft`: The draft ESG report
- `review-checklist`: Checklist of framework requirements

## Outputs

- `esg-report-final`: The approved ESG report ready for publication
- `assurance-plan`: Plan for external assurance if applicable

## Process

1. Route the draft to subject matter owners (environment, HR, legal, finance) for fact-checking.
2. Incorporate corrections and resolve any open data quality issues.
3. Obtain sign-off from the executive sponsor and the board (if required).
4. Determine whether external assurance is needed and outline the assurance scope.
