# Awareness Effectiveness Measurer

Measure training effectiveness from assessment results and delivery data, and identify where knowledge gaps remain.

## Inputs

- `assessment-items`: The quiz and exercise items tied to each objective.
- `delivery-schedule`: The planned delivery with dates and channels.

## Outputs

- `effectiveness-metrics`: Completion rates, pass rates, and score trends by segment and by objective.
- `knowledge-gap-report`: Objectives and segments where results fall below the passing standard.

## Process

1. Collect completion and pass rates by segment and by objective.
2. Compare scores against the passing standard set for each objective.
3. Track score trends across delivery waves to see whether they improve.
4. List the objectives and segments that still fall short and the likely cause.
