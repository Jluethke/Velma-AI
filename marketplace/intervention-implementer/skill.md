# Intervention Implementer

Implement the matched interventions: tutoring, advising, financial aid referrals, or counseling.

## Inputs

- `support-needs`: Matched support services needed per student
- `at-risk-list`: List of flagged students

## Outputs

- `intervention-log`: Record of interventions deployed per student
- `referral-confirmations`: Confirmations that referrals were received

## Process

1. Contact each student and explain available support
2. Make referrals to tutoring, advising, financial aid, or counseling
3. Confirm referrals are received and appointments scheduled
4. Log all interventions in the tracking system
