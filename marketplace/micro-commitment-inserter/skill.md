# Micro Commitment Inserter

Insert small, easy commitments between the current steps to bridge the gaps. Each micro-commitment should be a natural, valuable step that happens to also increase commitment.

## Inputs

- `journey-map`: Current journey with ask sizes
- `big-ask-locations`: Where the commitment gaps are

## Outputs

- `revised-journey`: The journey with micro-commitments inserted, where no single step increases the ask by more than 2 points
- `micro-commitment-designs`: For each inserted step: what the person does, what value they receive, and why it naturally leads to the next step

## Process

1. For each gap, design 1-3 intermediate steps. Each step should: (1) be genuinely valuable on its own (not busywork), (2) require slightly more commitment than the previous step, (3) provide information or experience that makes the next step feel natural.
2. Examples of micro-commitments: follow (low), subscribe to email (slightly higher), download a resource (higher, they gave email), attend a webinar (higher, they gave time), start a trial (higher, they changed behavior), make a small purchase (highest before the big ask).
3. The key: each micro-commitment must provide value independent of the next step. If a step only exists to advance the funnel, it feels manipulative and people sense it.
4. Redesign the journey so no single step increases the ask by more than 2 points on the 1-10 scale.
