# Mentor Target Finder

Find people two to five years ahead of you on the path you want.

## Inputs

- `growth-goal`: Where you want to grow toward
- `current-position`: Where you are now on that path

## Outputs

- `candidate-mentors`: People a few years ahead of you
- `selection-notes`: Why each candidate fits the path

## Process

1. Describe the specific path you want to travel.
2. Find people who are two to five years further along it.
3. Prefer people close enough to remember your stage.
4. Record how to reach or follow each candidate.
