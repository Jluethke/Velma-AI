# Argument Organizer

Organize the claims into a logical argument structure: which claim comes first, what transitions connect them, and what the overall narrative arc is.

## Inputs

- `claim-list`: Extracted claims with evidence
- `research-question`: The question driving the narrative

## Outputs

- `argument-outline`: Ordered list of claims with transition logic and section assignments
- `narrative-arc`: One-sentence summary of the story: question, approach, answer

## Process

1. Group claims by the sub-question they address.
2. Order groups to follow the logic: from setup to primary finding to secondary findings to implications.
3. Identify the transitions between groups: causal, contrastive, or additive.
4. Assign each claim group to the appropriate section (results vs. discussion).
