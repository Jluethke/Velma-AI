# Service Order Planner

Plan the order of service including music, readings, message, and announcements.

## Inputs

- `service-date`: The date and time of the service
- `theme-or-series`: The sermon series, liturgical season, or theme for this service

## Outputs

- `order-of-service`: The sequence of elements: prelude, welcome, songs, readings, message, offering, closing
- `content-assignments`: Who is responsible for each element (speaker, musicians, readers)

## Process

1. Select the scripture readings and message topic aligned with the theme or series.
2. Choose the songs or hymns that fit the theme and flow of the service.
3. Sequence the elements: prelude, welcome, opening song, prayer, readings, message, response, offering, announcements, closing song, benediction.
4. Assign each element to a person: speaker, worship leader, readers, prayer leader, announcer.
