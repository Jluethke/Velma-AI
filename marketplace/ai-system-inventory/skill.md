# Ai System Inventory

Inventory all AI systems in use across the organization.

## Inputs

- `organization-scope`: Departments and systems to inventory
- `inventory-criteria`: What qualifies as an AI system

## Outputs

- `ai-inventory`: Catalog of all AI systems with purpose and owner
- `inventory-gaps`: Systems that could not be fully documented

## Process

1. Survey each department for AI system usage.
2. Document purpose, data inputs, outputs, and owner for each.
3. Identify systems that lack documentation.
4. Catalog third-party AI services and APIs.
