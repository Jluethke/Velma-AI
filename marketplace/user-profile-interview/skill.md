# User Profile Interview

Learn who the user is through conversation: their role, industry, experience level, and current situation.

## Inputs

- `library-overview`: Context about what the library offers
- `how-it-works`: Explanation given to the user

## Outputs

- `user-role`: What the user does (job title, function, or life role)
- `user-industry`: The domain or industry the user operates in
- `experience-level`: How familiar the user is with structured workflows and their domain (beginner, intermediate, experienced)
- `current-situation`: What the user is dealing with right now, what prompted them to seek help

## Process

1. Ask the user what they do and what brought them here today. Let them describe it in their own words.
2. From their response, identify their role (e.g., small business owner, developer, teacher, farmer, project manager, or someone figuring things out).
3. Identify their industry or domain. If they do not fit a single domain, note the overlap.
4. Gauge their experience level from how they describe their work. Do not ask them to rate themselves.
5. Summarize back what you heard and ask if you got it right before moving on.
