# Litigation Trigger Assessor

Assess the triggering event and determine the scope of the hold.

## Inputs

- `trigger-event`: Description of the lawsuit, investigation, or anticipated claim
- `matter-details`: Parties, claims, and relevant time period

## Outputs

- `hold-scope`: Defined scope of the litigation hold
- `relevant-topics`: Key topics, custodians, and data types to preserve

## Process

1. Analyze the triggering event and legal claims
2. Define the subject matter scope and relevant time period
3. Identify key topics and data types that must be preserved
