# Exclusive Content Planner

Plan the exclusive content calendar for members.

## Inputs

- `tier-structure`: What each tier receives
- `onboarding-sequence`: What new members see first

## Outputs

- `member-content-calendar`: Scheduled exclusive content by tier and date
- `engagement-activities`: Planned community activities (Q&A, live sessions, challenges)

## Process

1. Define the exclusive content types for each tier (early access, behind-the-scenes, bonus content, live sessions).
2. Schedule the content on a regular cadence that members can expect.
3. Plan community engagement activities (AMAs, challenges, feedback sessions).
4. Balance the effort required against the free content production schedule.
