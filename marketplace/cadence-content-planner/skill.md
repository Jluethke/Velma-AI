# Cadence Content Planner

Plan what to say at each follow-up touchpoint so that every interaction adds value instead of just marking time.

## Inputs

- `re-engagement-sequences`: The dormant contact sequences
- `exit-criteria`: When to stop sequences

## Outputs

- `content-categories`: Types of follow-up content matched to relationship stage and velocity (share insight, ask question, make introduction, offer help, give update)
- `content-rotation-rules`: Rules for rotating content types so you do not send the same kind of message twice in a row

## Process

1. Define 5 content categories: (1) Share insight (an article, observation, or piece of information relevant to their work or interests), (2) Ask a question (seek their expertise or opinion on something specific), (3) Make an introduction (connect them to someone they should know), (4) Offer help (identify a problem they have and offer a specific solution), (5) Give an update (share your own news that they would find relevant).
2. Map content categories to relationship stages. Acquaintances get categories 1 and 5 (low commitment). Active contacts get all five. Trusted contacts get categories 2, 3, and 4 more heavily (reciprocal exchange). Never send a category 5 update to someone who has not shown interest in your work.
3. Set the rotation rule: never use the same content category twice in a row with the same contact. If your last message was a shared article (category 1), the next should be a question, introduction, offer, or update. This prevents the pattern where every follow-up is 'saw this article and thought of you' until the person stops reading.
4. Pre-populate a content bank: for each contact in the active and trusted stages, note 2-3 topics they care about, questions you could ask them, and people you could introduce them to. The bank makes follow-ups fast to execute because the thinking is already done.
