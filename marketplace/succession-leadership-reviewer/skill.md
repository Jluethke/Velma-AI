# Succession Leadership Reviewer

Review the succession plan with leadership annually.

## Inputs

- `bench-strength-map`: Current bench strength
- `assignment-log`: Development progress

## Outputs

- `leadership-review-notes`: Notes from the leadership review
- `plan-updates`: Updates to the succession plan based on review

## Process

1. Present the current bench strength and changes since last review
2. Discuss each critical role and its candidates
3. Update candidate readiness assessments
4. Adjust development plans based on progress and changing business needs
