# Breach Regulatory Notification

Prepare and submit regulatory notifications within required timelines.

## Inputs

- `notification-requirements`: Regulatory obligations and deadlines
- `scope-findings`: Full scope of the breach for notification content

## Outputs

- `regulatory-submissions`: Filed regulatory notifications with confirmation receipts
- `notification-log`: Log of all notifications sent with dates and recipients

## Process

1. Draft regulatory notifications following each jurisdiction's required format
2. Include all required elements (data types, affected count, remediation steps, contact info)
3. Submit notifications within the required timelines
4. Document all submissions with confirmation receipts
