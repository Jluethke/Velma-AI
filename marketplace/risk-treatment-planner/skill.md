# Risk Treatment Planner

Define treatment plans for each risk: accept, mitigate, transfer, or avoid.

## Inputs

- `owned-risks`: Risks with assigned owners
- `risk-matrix`: Risk distribution for prioritization

## Outputs

- `treatment-plans`: Treatment strategy and action items per risk
- `residual-risk-estimates`: Estimated residual risk after treatment

## Process

1. Select the treatment strategy for each risk based on score and cost-benefit
2. Define specific actions, timelines, and resources for mitigation
3. Estimate residual risk after planned treatment
4. Document acceptance rationale for risks retained
