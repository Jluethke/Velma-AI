# Portal Design Finalizer

Finalize the portal design plan with metrics and launch checklist.

## Inputs

- `portal-content`: All portal content
- `forum-structure`: Forum setup

## Outputs

- `portal-design-plan`: Complete portal design plan with content and architecture
- `launch-checklist`: Pre-launch checklist and success metrics

## Process

1. Define the success metrics (deflection rate, search success rate, time on page).
2. Build the pre-launch checklist.
3. Plan the announcement and adoption campaign.
4. Produce the portal design plan.
