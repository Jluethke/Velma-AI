# Ald External Feedback Collector

Ask two or three people who talk with you often which failure they see you commit. Others see your listening from the outside, where it actually lands. Ask a specific question, because a vague one gets a polite non-answer.

## Inputs

- `failure-definitions`: The four failures, to share so feedback is specific
- `self-suspected-failures`: Your guess, held back until after they answer so you do not lead them

## Outputs

- `external-feedback`: What each person reported, with any examples they offered
- `feedback-themes`: The failures that more than one person named, which carry more weight than a single report

## Process

1. Pick two or three people across different settings: a colleague, a family member, someone you manage or are managed by.
2. Ask the specific question: 'Of these four, which do you see me do most, and can you recall a time?' Share the four definitions so they answer in the same terms.
3. Do not reveal your own guess first, and do not defend yourself when they answer. A defense teaches them to stop being honest.
4. Note which failure more than one person named independently. Agreement across people is your strongest signal.
