# Seasonal Irrigation Planner

Set the irrigation plan for the growing season.

## Inputs

- `crop-selections`: Selected crops and their water needs
- `field-assessment`: Field drainage and water source data

## Outputs

- `irrigation-plan`: Irrigation schedule by crop and growth stage
- `water-budget`: Total water volume and cost estimate

## Process

1. Estimate water requirements by crop and growth stage.
2. Assess the water source capacity and delivery system.
3. Set the irrigation schedule with frequency and duration.
4. Calculate the seasonal water budget.
