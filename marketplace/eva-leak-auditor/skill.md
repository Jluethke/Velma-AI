# Eva Leak Auditor

Audit the slow leaks for waste.

## Inputs

- `slow-leak-set`: The steady recurring charges

## Outputs

- `leak-findings`: Each recurring charge with its monthly and annual cost
- `cancellable-leaks`: Duplicates, unused, and forgotten subscriptions

## Process

1. List each recurring charge with its monthly and annual cost.
2. Mark duplicates, unused, and forgotten subscriptions.
3. Total the recoverable amount from cancellations.
