# Control Owner Approver

Obtain approval from control owners for responses in their domain.

## Inputs

- `reviewed-responses`: Reviewed responses requiring owner sign-off
- `review-notes`: Notes from the review process

## Outputs

- `approved-responses`: Responses with owner approval
- `approval-log`: Record of who approved what

## Process

1. Route responses to the appropriate control owner for review
2. Collect approval or correction from each owner
3. Incorporate owner corrections into final responses
4. Record each approval with timestamp
