# Severity Spread Assessor

Assess the severity and rate of spread.

## Inputs

- `identification-result`: Identified pest or pathogen
- `affected-area-map`: Map of affected zones

## Outputs

- `severity-rating`: Severity score and economic threshold assessment
- `spread-forecast`: Predicted spread based on conditions and biology

## Process

1. Rate severity using a standardized scale for the pest or disease.
2. Compare the infestation level to the economic action threshold.
3. Forecast spread based on weather, biology, and field layout.
4. Determine whether treatment is economically justified.
