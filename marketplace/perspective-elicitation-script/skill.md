# Perspective Elicitation Script

After delivering the statement, ask for the person's perspective before moving to solutions. They may have context you lack, and asking turns the feedback into a conversation rather than a verdict.

## Inputs

- `delivery-plan`: The delivery plan
- `setting-choice`: The chosen setting

## Outputs

- `elicitation-questions`: An open question to hand the conversation over and surface the person's view
- `listening-plan`: The plan to listen fully and to adjust if their context changes the picture

## Process

1. Prepare an open question to hand the conversation over, such as how they saw the situation.
2. Plan to listen fully before responding, and to take their context seriously if it changes the picture.
3. Prepare for common reactions such as surprise, disagreement, or over-apology, and how you will keep the conversation on the behavior.
4. Decide what you will do if their perspective shows your read was wrong: adjust, rather than defend the original statement.
