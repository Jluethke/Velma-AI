# Information Architecture Designer

Create the new site's information architecture: sitemap, navigation, and content hierarchy.

## Inputs

- `redesign-goals`: What the redesign must achieve
- `current-state-audit`: Current site structure and content

## Outputs

- `site-architecture`: New sitemap, navigation structure, and page hierarchy
- `content-plan`: What content to keep, rewrite, merge, or remove

## Process

1. Map the new sitemap based on user needs and business goals.
2. Design the navigation (primary, secondary, footer) for clarity.
3. Decide what content to keep, rewrite, consolidate, or remove.
4. Validate the architecture against the top user tasks.
