# Nas Range Researcher

Research the realistic range and the other side.

## Inputs

- `negotiation-context`: What is being negotiated
- `target-outcome`: The target to place within the range

## Outputs

- `market-range`: The range from comparable deals
- `counterpart-profile`: The other side's likely target, limits, and interests

## Process

1. Gather the market range from comparable deals.
2. Estimate the other side's likely target and limits.
3. Note their interests beyond price.
