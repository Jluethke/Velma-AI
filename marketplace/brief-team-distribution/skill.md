# Brief Team Distribution

Distribute the approved brief to the creative team and kick off the project.

## Inputs

- `approved-brief`: The approved creative brief
- `approval-record`: The approval details and any conditions

## Outputs

- `team-assignments`: Who is responsible for each deliverable
- `project-kickoff-record`: Record of the kickoff including questions and clarifications from the team

## Process

1. Identify the team members needed for each deliverable.
2. Distribute the brief with role-specific context.
3. Hold the kickoff meeting to walk through the brief, answer questions, and confirm timelines.
4. Produce the team assignments and kickoff record.
