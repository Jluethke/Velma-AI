# Campaign Audience Segmenter

Segment the donor audience for targeted campaign messaging.

## Inputs

- `campaign-goal`: Dollar target and timeline
- `donor-database`: Current donor records

## Outputs

- `audience-segments`: Segments with estimated capacity
- `prospect-lists`: Contact lists per segment

## Process

1. Identify segments relevant to this campaign (lapsed, current, prospects, major)
2. Estimate giving capacity per segment
3. Build contact lists with preferred communication channels
4. Prioritize segments by expected return on effort
