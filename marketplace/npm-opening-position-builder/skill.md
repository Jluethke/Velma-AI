# Npm Opening Position Builder

Build an opening position more ambitious than your target but still defensible. The opening anchors the negotiation, and a credible, ambitious anchor pulls the final outcome toward you. Ambitious without justification reads as a bluff, so pair the number with a reason.

## Inputs

- `target-price`: The outcome you are aiming for, which the opening sits above
- `their-batna-estimate`: Their alternative, so the opening is ambitious without pushing them to walk

## Outputs

- `opening-position`: The first offer you will make, set above your target and anchored with a rationale
- `opening-justification`: The reason that makes the opening credible rather than a bluff

## Process

1. Set the opening above your target so there is room to concede toward the target and still land well.
2. Keep the opening defensible. An anchor the other side can laugh off does not anchor anything and costs you credibility.
3. Write the justification that comes with the opening: the comparable, the cost basis, the value delivered. The reason is what makes the number stick.
4. Check the opening against their BATNA. If it is so aggressive that walking to their alternative looks better, soften it until it does not.
