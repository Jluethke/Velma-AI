# Not For You Statement Writer

Write a 'not for you if' statement that can appear in marketing, sales conversations, and onboarding materials.

## Inputs

- `anti-persona-profile`: Composite profile of who you are not for
- `exclusion-rationale`: Why serving them harms both sides

## Outputs

- `not-for-you-statement`: Plain language statement of who this is not for, written to repel the wrong buyer without being hostile
- `placement-recommendations`: Where to put the statement (landing page, sales script, onboarding email, FAQ)

## Process

1. Write the statement in second person: 'This is not for you if...' followed by 3-5 concrete conditions drawn from the anti-persona profile.
2. Test each condition: would the right customer read it and think 'that is not me'? If a condition could scare the right customer, rewrite it.
3. Recommend placement: the statement works hardest when it appears before the person commits time (landing page, pricing page, first sales call).
4. Note: this statement often increases conversion rate because it signals confidence and specificity, which the right buyer finds reassuring.
