# Adjacent Area Mapper

Map five to seven adjacent areas that support or surround the core.

## Inputs

- `core-area`: The one area worth most of your learning time
- `depth-target`: What depth looks like for that area

## Outputs

- `adjacent-areas`: Five to seven areas that surround the core
- `adjacency-rationale`: Why each adjacent area matters

## Process

1. List areas that your core area regularly touches.
2. Keep five to seven that most strengthen the core.
3. Record why each adjacent area matters to the core.
4. Drop areas that add little to the core.
