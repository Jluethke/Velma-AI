# Showing Conductor

Conduct the showing and collect prospect feedback.

## Inputs

- `showing-schedule`: Scheduled appointments
- `prospect-profiles`: Prospect information

## Outputs

- `showing-notes`: Notes from each showing including prospect interest level
- `showing-activity-log`: Complete log of all showings conducted

## Process

1. Prepare the unit for showing: clean, lights on, temperature set.
2. Conduct the showing and answer prospect questions.
3. Rate each prospect's interest level and collect their feedback.
4. Follow up with interested prospects within 24 hours.
