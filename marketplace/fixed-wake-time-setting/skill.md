# Fixed Wake Time Setting

Set a single fixed wake time to be held every day including weekends, because the wake time anchors the entire sleep rhythm.

## Inputs

- `current-sleep-pattern`: Your current bedtimes, wake times, and how much they vary day to day
- `daily-obligations`: The fixed demands on your mornings, such as work or family start times

## Outputs

- `fixed-wake-time`: A single wake time to hold every day of the week
- `wake-time-rationale`: Why that time fits your obligations and can be held on weekends

## Process

1. Choose the wake time from your least flexible morning. The wake time must work on the day with the earliest fixed demand, then apply to every other day too.
2. Commit to holding it on weekends within about an hour. Sleeping in shifts the body clock and recreates the Monday morning difficulty every week.
3. Confirm the wake time leaves room for seven to eight hours of sleep at a bedtime you could realistically keep.
4. Write why this time is sustainable. A wake time chosen from ambition rather than obligation will not survive, and an unstable wake time undoes everything downstream.
