# Risk Clause Flagger

Flag high-risk clauses that require negotiation or legal review.

## Inputs

- `contract-text`: Full text of the contract
- `key-terms-summary`: Extracted key terms

## Outputs

- `risk-flags`: List of flagged clauses with risk level and rationale
- `risk-score`: Overall risk assessment score

## Process

1. Check indemnification scope and carve-outs
2. Review limitation of liability caps and exclusions
3. Assess IP assignment and work-for-hire provisions
4. Flag any unusual or non-standard clauses
