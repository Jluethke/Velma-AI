# Licensing Requirements Mapper

Identify and document all applicable state childcare licensing requirements.

## Inputs

- `facility-type`: Type of childcare facility (center, family home, group home)
- `state-jurisdiction`: State and local jurisdiction for licensing rules

## Outputs

- `requirements-checklist`: Complete list of licensing requirements (ratios, qualifications, facility standards, training, health and safety)
- `renewal-timeline`: License renewal dates and required submission deadlines

## Process

1. Look up the state licensing regulations for the facility type.
2. Document the staff-to-child ratio requirements by age group.
3. List the staff qualification requirements (education, certifications, background checks).
4. List the facility requirements (square footage, outdoor space, safety equipment, fire inspection).
5. Record the ongoing training hour requirements for all staff.
