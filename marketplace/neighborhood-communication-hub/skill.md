# Neighborhood Communication Hub

Set up ongoing neighborhood communication: platform choice, update cadence, and engagement strategy.

## Inputs

- `neighborhood_size`: Number of households
- `tech_comfort`: General tech comfort level
- `communication_goals`: What needs communicating

## Outputs

- `platform_choice`: Recommended communication platform
- `update_cadence`: How often to communicate
- `content_plan`: What to share and when

## Process

1. Evaluate platform options (app, email, board)
2. Choose based on neighborhood tech comfort
3. Set regular update cadence
4. Assign communication responsibilities
5. Plan content types and schedule
