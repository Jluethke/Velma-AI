# Shared Goals Alignment

Align on shared life goals across finances, career, family, living situation, and personal growth.

## Inputs

- `individual_goals`: Each partner's personal goals
- `timeline`: Planning horizon (1 year, 5 years, etc.)

## Outputs

- `shared_goals`: Agreed shared goals with timelines
- `individual_goals_supported`: Individual goals the partner will support
- `goal_conflicts`: Goals that need negotiation

## Process

1. Each partner lists their top goals
2. Identify natural overlaps
3. Flag conflicts or competing priorities
4. Negotiate compromises
5. Set shared milestones
