# Candidate Payment Structurer

Pay candidates for the work sample at the role's hourly rate. Unpaid samples select for people who can afford to work for free, which is a bias rather than a filter. Fix the amount, the payment timing, and the ownership of the output up front.

## Inputs

- `work-sample-brief`: The task brief candidates will receive
- `time-and-materials-spec`: The expected hours and provided materials

## Outputs

- `compensation-terms`: The fixed payment amount, when it is paid, and the statement that output stays the candidate's property
- `candidate-instructions`: The candidate-facing package: the brief, the pay, the deadline, and a contact for questions

## Process

1. Set pay at the role's loaded hourly rate times the expected hours, and state the fixed amount up front.
2. Pay on submission, not on advancement. The payment is for the candidate's time, whether or not they are hired.
3. State that any work product remains the candidate's property and will not be used by the company, which keeps the sample fair and avoids free labor.
4. Write the candidate-facing instructions: the brief, the pay, the deadline, and who to contact with questions.
