# College Essay Workshop

Develop college application essays: brainstorm topics, draft, revise, and tailor supplementals.

## Inputs

- `personal_experiences`: Key life experiences and interests
- `school_list`: Schools being applied to
- `essay_prompts`: Common App and supplemental prompts

## Outputs

- `personal_statement`: Polished personal statement draft
- `supplemental_essays`: Tailored supplementals per school
- `revision_notes`: Areas for further polish

## Process

1. Brainstorm personal narrative themes
2. Map themes to prompts
3. Draft personal statement
4. Write school-specific supplementals
5. Self-edit for voice and specificity
