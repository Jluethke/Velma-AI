# Legal Custody Documenter

Prepare chain of custody documentation suitable for legal proceedings.

## Inputs

- `collection-records`: All collection documentation
- `transfer-log`: Complete transfer history
- `verification-results`: All integrity verification results

## Outputs

- `custody-documentation`: Court-ready chain of custody documentation
- `witness-list`: List of custodians who may need to testify

## Process

1. Compile the complete custody chain for each evidence item
2. Format documentation to meet jurisdictional requirements
3. Verify there are no gaps in the custody timeline
4. Identify all custodians who may need to provide testimony
