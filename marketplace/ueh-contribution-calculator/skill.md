# Ueh Contribution Calculator

Compute the contribution margin per unit.

## Inputs

- `unit-model`: The defined unit
- `variable-cost-list`: The variable costs per unit

## Outputs

- `unit-revenue`: The revenue from one unit
- `contribution-margin`: Unit revenue less variable costs, in dollars and percent

## Process

1. Record the revenue from one unit.
2. Subtract the variable costs from the revenue.
3. Report the contribution margin per unit in dollars and percent.
