# Video Footage Editor

Edit the footage through assembly, rough cut, and fine cut stages.

## Inputs

- `raw-footage`: All captured video files
- `script-document`: Script for reference during editing

## Outputs

- `fine-cut-timeline`: Edited video timeline at picture lock
- `edit-notes`: Notes on editorial decisions and remaining items

## Process

1. Sync and organize footage in the editing project
2. Build the assembly cut following the script order
3. Refine pacing and selects into a rough cut
4. Polish transitions and timing into the fine cut
