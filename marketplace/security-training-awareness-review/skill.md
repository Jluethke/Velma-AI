# Security Training Awareness Review

Review security training programs for developers, coverage rates, and knowledge retention.

## Inputs

- `team-structure`: Development team structure and headcount
- `integration-gaps`: Known gaps to assess training impact

## Outputs

- `training-findings`: Gaps in security training coverage and effectiveness
- `training-metrics`: Completion rates, scores, and frequency data

## Process

1. Review the training catalog and which topics are covered (OWASP Top 10, secure coding)
2. Check completion rates by team and role
3. Assess whether training is role-specific (frontend, backend, infrastructure)
4. Look for knowledge retention signals (phishing test results, repeat findings)
