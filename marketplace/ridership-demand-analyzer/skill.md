# Ridership Demand Analyzer

Analyze ridership data and demand patterns.

## Inputs

- `ridership-data`: Historical ridership counts by route, stop, and time period
- `service-area-demographics`: Population density, employment centers, and transit-dependent populations

## Outputs

- `demand-patterns`: Ridership demand by time of day, day of week, and geography
- `underserved-areas`: Areas with unmet demand or poor service coverage

## Process

1. Analyze ridership by route and time period to identify peak and off-peak patterns.
2. Map demand hotspots and corridors with the highest passenger volumes.
3. Identify areas where demand exceeds capacity or service is sparse.
4. Compare demand against current service levels to find gaps.
