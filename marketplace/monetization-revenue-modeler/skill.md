# Monetization Revenue Modeler

Model the revenue potential of the top monetization opportunities.

## Inputs

- `opportunity-list`: Ranked monetization opportunities
- `creator-profile`: The creator's platforms and audience data

## Outputs

- `revenue-model`: Projected revenue by stream for the next 12 months
- `implementation-priority`: Ordered list of which streams to launch first

## Process

1. For each opportunity, model the revenue based on conversion rates and pricing.
2. Factor in the time and cost to set up each stream.
3. Project the total revenue mix over 12 months with the new streams added.
4. Prioritize by the ratio of projected revenue to setup effort.
