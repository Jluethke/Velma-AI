# Transition Communicator

Communicate the hybrid transition plan to all employees.

## Inputs

- `hybrid-policy`: The policy
- `meeting-norms`: Meeting norms

## Outputs

- `transition-communications`: All communications sent about the transition
- `manager-toolkit`: Toolkit for managers to support their teams through the transition

## Process

1. Draft the all-hands announcement with the policy and rationale
2. Create the FAQ addressing common questions
3. Build the manager toolkit with conversation guides
4. Plan the communication cadence (announcement, reminders, launch)
