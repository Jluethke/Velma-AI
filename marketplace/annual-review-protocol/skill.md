# Annual Review Protocol

Define an annual review that reads the trends, confirms the screenings happened, and resets the system for the coming year.

## Inputs

- `trend-tracker`: The year over year view of measures
- `screening-schedule`: The screening and checkup schedule

## Outputs

- `annual-review-checklist`: A checklist for the yearly review of trends, screenings, and routines
- `review-date`: A fixed date each year to run the review

## Process

1. Set a fixed annual date for the review, such as around a birthday, so it recurs without needing to be remembered.
2. On the checklist, read the trend tracker for any measure moving the wrong way and confirm every scheduled screening actually happened.
3. Add the new year of numbers to the tracking sheet during the review, so the record stays current and the trend extends by one year.
4. Confirm the routines still fit the coming year. Age, conditions, and circumstances change, and the system should be reset to match rather than run unchanged.
