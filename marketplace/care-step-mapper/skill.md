# Care Step Mapper

Map the care steps by day or phase.

## Inputs

- `best-practice-list`: Evidence-based care practices
- `inclusion-criteria`: Patient population definition

## Outputs

- `pathway-map`: Care activities organized by timeline
- `responsible-roles`: Which role performs each step

## Process

1. Organize care activities into phases (pre, during, post).
2. Assign each activity to a timeline.
3. Identify the responsible discipline for each step.
