# Final Rule Publisher

Finalize the rule and publish in the Federal Register.

## Inputs

- `comment-responses`: Responses to incorporate in the preamble
- `rule-changes`: Revised rule text

## Outputs

- `final-rule`: Published final rule with preamble, comment responses, regulatory text, and effective date
- `rulemaking-record`: Complete record of the rulemaking process for the administrative record

## Process

1. Assemble the final rule document with updated preamble and comment responses.
2. Set the effective date (minimum 30 days after publication for major rules).
3. Submit to the Federal Register for publication.
4. Compile the administrative record (proposed rule, comments, analyses, final rule).
