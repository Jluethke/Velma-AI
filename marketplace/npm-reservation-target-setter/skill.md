# Npm Reservation Target Setter

Set two numbers: your reservation price and your target. The reservation price is the worst deal you will accept, anchored to your BATNA. The target is the outcome you are aiming for, set at the ambitious edge of what is realistic. Both are decided in advance, in the calm before the room.

## Inputs

- `your-batna`: Your alternative, which anchors the reservation price
- `batna-strength-rating`: How much leverage you hold, which shapes how ambitious the target can be

## Outputs

- `reservation-price`: The least favorable terms you will accept before walking to your BATNA
- `target-price`: The outcome you are aiming for, set at the ambitious end of realistic

## Process

1. Set the reservation price just better than your BATNA. Accepting worse than your BATNA means you should have walked.
2. Set the target at the ambitious edge of what the facts support. Negotiators who set modest targets reach modest outcomes.
3. Write both numbers down before the negotiation. Deciding them in the room, under pressure, is how people talk themselves into bad deals.
4. Let the strength rating calibrate the target: strong leverage supports a more ambitious target, weak leverage pulls it toward realism.
