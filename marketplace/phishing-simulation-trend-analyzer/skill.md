# Phishing Simulation Trend Analyzer

Analyze phishing simulation results and click rate trends over time.

## Inputs

- `training-materials`: Phishing simulation platform data and past campaigns
- `completion-metrics`: Training completion metrics for correlation

## Outputs

- `phishing-trend-analysis`: Click rate trends over time by campaign type and department
- `high-risk-groups`: Groups with persistently high click rates

## Process

1. Compile click rates from all phishing simulation campaigns over the past 12 months
2. Calculate the trend: are click rates decreasing, stable, or increasing
3. Break down by campaign difficulty, department, and employee tenure
4. Identify groups that consistently click at high rates despite repeated training
