# Volunteer Needs Assessment

Determine volunteer needs: roles, skills required, time commitments, and number of people per role.

## Inputs

- `event_or_org`: What volunteers are needed for
- `tasks_list`: All tasks needing volunteers
- `timeline`: When volunteers are needed

## Outputs

- `role_descriptions`: Defined volunteer roles
- `headcount_needed`: Volunteers needed per role
- `skill_requirements`: Skills or certifications needed per role

## Process

1. List all tasks requiring volunteer help
2. Group tasks into defined roles
3. Estimate time commitment per role
4. Identify skill or certification requirements
5. Calculate total volunteers needed
