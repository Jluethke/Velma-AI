# Party Mapper

Map every party: their interests, their power, their alternatives, and their relationships to other parties.

## Inputs

- `parties`: All parties in the negotiation
- `negotiation-issues`: The issues to be resolved

## Outputs

- `party-profiles`: For each party: their key interests, their power (veto, resources, information, alternatives), and their likely priorities
- `relationship-map`: How each party relates to the others: allies, opponents, neutral, dependent, or competitive

## Process

1. For each party, assess: what do they want? Why? What is their BATNA? What power do they hold (can they veto? control resources? hold information others need?)?
2. Map relationships: which parties are natural allies (shared interests)? Which are natural opponents (conflicting interests)? Which are swing parties (could go either way)?
3. Identify information asymmetries: who knows what? Information advantages create negotiating power.
4. Assess decision rules: does the agreement require unanimity, majority, or the consent of specific parties (veto holders)?
