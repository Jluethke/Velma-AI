# Meeting Decision Framer

Frame the meeting around a decision, not a topic. 'Should we hire a contractor or build in-house?' not 'Let's discuss the project staffing.' A meeting without a decision to make is an email.

## Inputs

- `meeting-purpose`: Why you think a meeting is needed
- `attendee-list`: Who needs to be in the room and why

## Outputs

- `decision-statement`: The specific decision to be made, phrased as a question with a finite set of answers
- `options-list`: The options on the table, each with a one-line description of trade-offs
- `meeting-necessity-check`: Whether this meeting is actually needed or could be replaced by async communication

## Process

1. State the decision as a question: 'Should we [option A] or [option B]?' with at most 3-4 options.
2. For each option, write the trade-off in one sentence: what you gain and what you lose.
3. Check necessity: if one person can make this decision with async input, cancel the meeting and send the decision in writing.
4. A meeting is necessary only when the decision requires real-time discussion because the trade-offs are ambiguous or politically sensitive.
