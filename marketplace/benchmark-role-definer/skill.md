# Benchmark Role Definer

Define the roles and levels to benchmark.

## Inputs

- `role-catalog`: List of roles in the organization
- `benchmark-scope`: Which roles or levels to include

## Outputs

- `roles-to-benchmark`: Selected roles with job family and level
- `role-descriptions-summary`: Brief descriptions for matching to survey data

## Process

1. Select the roles to benchmark based on priority (attrition risk, hard to fill, large population)
2. Document each role's key responsibilities and level
3. Group into job families for consistent matching
