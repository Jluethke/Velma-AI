# Compliance Auditor

Monitor compliance with infection control measures through audits.

## Inputs

- `implementation-checklist`: Expected controls in place
- `training-completion-log`: Trained staff list

## Outputs

- `audit-results`: Compliance rates by measure and unit
- `corrective-actions`: Non-compliance issues and corrective steps

## Process

1. Conduct direct observation audits of hand hygiene and PPE use.
2. Verify environmental cleaning compliance.
3. Report results and issue corrective actions for deficiencies.
