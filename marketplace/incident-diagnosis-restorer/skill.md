# Incident Diagnosis Restorer

Diagnose the issue and restore the affected service.

## Inputs

- `major-incident-record`: The major incident record
- `response-activation`: Response team and bridge details

## Outputs

- `diagnosis`: What went wrong and what was done to restore
- `restoration-confirmation`: Confirmation that the service is restored

## Process

1. Gather symptoms, logs, and metrics to identify the failure.
2. Identify and implement the fastest path to restore the service.
3. Verify the service is functioning for all affected users.
4. Document the timeline of actions taken.
