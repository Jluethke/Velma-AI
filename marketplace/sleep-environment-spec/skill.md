# Sleep Environment Spec

Specify the bedroom conditions that support sleep, centered on a cool, dark, quiet room.

## Inputs

- `winddown-sequence`: The wind-down sequence
- `current-sleep-pattern`: Your current sleep conditions and disruptions

## Outputs

- `environment-settings`: The target bedroom settings for temperature, light, and noise
- `environment-change-list`: The specific changes needed to reach those settings

## Process

1. Set the room temperature between about sixty-five and sixty-eight degrees. A cool room supports the drop in body temperature that sleep onset depends on.
2. Make the room as dark as practical with blackout cover or a mask, and remove or cover small light sources that interrupt sleep.
3. Address noise with steady background sound or ear protection if the environment is unpredictable, since sudden noises fragment sleep even when they do not wake you fully.
4. List the concrete changes needed, such as a thermostat schedule, a blackout cover, or moving a device out of the room, and treat them as one-time fixes.
