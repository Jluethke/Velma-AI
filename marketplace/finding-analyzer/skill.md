# Finding Analyzer

Analyze all evidence and determine findings.

## Inputs

- `interview-records`: Interview notes
- `document-findings`: Document review findings

## Outputs

- `factual-findings`: Findings of fact based on the evidence
- `credibility-assessments`: Credibility assessment for conflicting accounts

## Process

1. Weigh the evidence for and against each allegation
2. Assess credibility where accounts conflict (corroboration, consistency, motive)
3. Apply the preponderance of evidence standard
4. Determine findings of fact for each allegation
