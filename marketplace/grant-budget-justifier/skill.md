# Grant Budget Justifier

Build and justify the project budget with personnel effort, equipment, supplies, travel, and indirect costs aligned to the aims.

## Inputs

- `research-strategy`: Approved research plan with methods and timeline
- `institutional-rates`: Salary scales, fringe rates, and indirect cost rate agreement
- `funding-limits`: Maximum budget, cost categories, and funder restrictions

## Outputs

- `budget-table`: Year-by-year budget with personnel, equipment, supplies, travel, and indirect costs
- `budget-justification`: Narrative justification explaining why each cost is necessary for the project
- `subcontract-budgets`: Subaward budgets for collaborating institutions, if applicable

## Process

1. List all personnel with percent effort, role, and salary basis per year
2. Itemize equipment needs (over $5K threshold) with vendor quotes
3. Estimate supplies by aim and year based on experimental plans
4. Justify travel for conferences, collaborator visits, and field work
5. Calculate indirect costs using the institutional rate and identify excluded categories
