# Voice Persona Creator

Define the voice persona including tone, pace, vocabulary, and personality traits.

## Inputs

- `brand-identity`: Brand values, target audience, and personality guidelines
- `use-case-context`: Primary tasks the voice will handle and the environment (phone, smart speaker, in-car)

## Outputs

- `voice-persona`: Defined persona with name, tone, pace, vocabulary level, and personality traits
- `voice-style-guide`: Guidelines for prompt writing, error handling tone, and escalation language

## Process

1. Define the persona attributes (warm vs. professional, formal vs. casual, fast vs. measured).
2. Match the persona to the brand identity and target audience expectations.
3. Write the voice style guide with example prompts, error messages, and escalation scripts.
