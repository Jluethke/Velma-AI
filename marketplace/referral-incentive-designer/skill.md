# Referral Incentive Designer

Define the incentive structure for both the referrer and the referred.

## Inputs

- `product-economics`: Unit economics and customer lifetime value
- `growth-goals`: Referral volume and conversion targets

## Outputs

- `incentive-structure`: Rewards for referrer and referred with terms
- `cost-model`: Projected cost per referral acquisition

## Process

1. Calculate the maximum incentive from unit economics
2. Design the reward for both sides (credit, discount, cash, feature)
3. Model the cost per acquisition at target volume
