# Visit Summary Provider

Provide the patient with a visit summary.

## Inputs

- `encounter-note`: Encounter documentation
- `prescription-orders`: Prescribed medications

## Outputs

- `visit-summary`: Patient-friendly summary of the visit
- `telemedicine-encounter-record`: Complete record for the chart

## Process

1. Generate a patient-friendly summary of the diagnosis and plan.
2. Include medication instructions and follow-up information.
3. Send the summary through the patient portal.
