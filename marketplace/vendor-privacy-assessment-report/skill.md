# Vendor Privacy Assessment Report

Produce the vendor privacy assessment report and file the record.

## Inputs

- `privacy-risk-score`: The vendor's risk rating
- `dpa-terms`: The agreed DPA terms

## Outputs

- `assessment-report`: The vendor privacy assessment report with risk rating, findings, and conditions
- `vendor-privacy-record`: The filed assessment record for ongoing monitoring

## Process

1. Compile the assessment report (vendor profile, scope, risk score, findings, DPA terms, conditions).
2. Record the approval or rejection decision with rationale.
3. Schedule the reassessment date based on risk level (annually for high risk, biennially for low).
4. File the record in the vendor management system.
