# Npm Concession Plan

Plan the concession sequence from your opening down toward your reservation price, trading the low-cost items along the way. Concessions given without a trade train the other side to keep pushing. A planned ladder keeps you from conceding too fast or past your floor.

## Inputs

- `opening-position`: Where you start
- `reservation-price`: The floor you will not cross
- `low-cost-high-value-offers`: The items you trade on the way down

## Outputs

- `concession-ladder`: The planned sequence of concessions from opening toward reservation, each tied to something you get back
- `walk-away-triggers`: The conditions under which you stop and go to your BATNA instead of conceding further

## Process

1. Plan concessions as decreasing steps. Large early concessions signal there is far more room, so shrink each step as you approach your floor.
2. Attach a trade to each concession. 'I can move on the date if you move on volume.' A concession for nothing invites the next demand.
3. Mark the reservation price as a hard stop. Below it, your BATNA is the better deal, so you walk.
4. Write the walk-away triggers plainly, so that under pressure you recognize the moment to stop rather than rationalize one more concession.
