# Completion Tracker

Track training completion and participant feedback.

## Inputs

- `training-calendar`: Scheduled training
- `enrollment-plan`: Enrollment targets

## Outputs

- `completion-data`: Completion rates by program, team, and individual
- `participant-feedback`: Post-training feedback scores and comments

## Process

1. Track attendance and completion in the LMS or tracking system
2. Collect post-training feedback surveys
3. Send reminders for incomplete required training
4. Report completion rates to managers
