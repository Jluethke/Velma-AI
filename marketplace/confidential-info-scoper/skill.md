# Confidential Info Scoper

Define the scope of confidential information covered by the NDA.

## Inputs

- `nda-type`: Type of NDA
- `business-context`: Business relationship context

## Outputs

- `ci-definition`: Definition of confidential information
- `exclusions-list`: Standard exclusions from confidentiality

## Process

1. Draft the definition of confidential information
2. List the standard exclusions (public knowledge, independent development, prior possession)
3. Specify any carve-outs for specific data types
