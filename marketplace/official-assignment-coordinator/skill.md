# Official Assignment Coordinator

Assign referees, umpires, and other officials to scheduled games.

## Inputs

- `season-schedule`: The game schedule with dates and venues
- `venue-assignments`: Where each game takes place

## Outputs

- `official-assignments`: Officials assigned to each game
- `official-schedule`: Per-official schedule showing their assigned games

## Process

1. Inventory the available officials and their certifications.
2. Assign officials to games based on availability, location, and conflict-of-interest rules.
3. Distribute the official schedule and confirm availability.
4. Produce the assignment record.
