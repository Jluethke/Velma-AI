# Sensor Network Designer

Design a monitoring sensor network with station placement, parameter selection, sampling frequency, and telemetry architecture.

## Inputs

- `monitoring-objectives`: What pollutants, parameters, or conditions to track and why
- `site-map`: Geographic extent, land use, and potential source locations
- `budget-envelope`: Available funding for equipment, installation, and ongoing maintenance

## Outputs

- `station-layout`: Map of monitoring stations with coordinates, parameters measured, and sampling intervals
- `equipment-spec`: Sensor models, power requirements, data logger specifications, and telemetry method
- `maintenance-schedule`: Calibration, cleaning, and replacement intervals for each station

## Process

1. Identify monitoring parameters based on regulatory requirements and research objectives
2. Select station locations to capture spatial gradients, point sources, and background levels
3. Choose sensor models that meet detection limits and accuracy requirements within budget
4. Design the data telemetry chain from sensor to central database
5. Create a maintenance and calibration schedule to ensure continuous data quality
