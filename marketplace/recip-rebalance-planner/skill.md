# Recip Rebalance Planner

Build a rebalancing plan for the imbalanced relationships: give more where you have been coasting on others' generosity, and pull back or reset expectations where you have been over-giving, with care, since abruptly changing the terms of a relationship can itself cause harm.

## Inputs

- `over-give-list`: Relationships where you give more, with cause
- `under-give-list`: Relationships where you receive more, with cause

## Outputs

- `rebalance-actions`: Specific actions per relationship: concrete ways to give more to under-given people and to adjust over-giving
- `rebalance-cautions`: Where to move gently, since changing established terms can be read as withdrawal or rejection if done abruptly

## Process

1. For under-give relationships, plan specific ways to give back drawn from the value lens: make the introduction you have been meaning to make, offer the help they would value, reciprocate the support they have given. Vague intent to be better does not rebalance anything.
2. For unhealthy over-giving, plan to reduce gradually and redirect the freed energy. The goal is not to start keeping score with a friend, it is to stop over-investing where it drains you and reinvest where the balance is real.
3. Leave chosen generosity alone. Where you give more on purpose to someone who cannot reciprocate and you are glad to, that is not an imbalance to fix, and the plan should explicitly protect it.
4. Move gently where you are changing established terms. A person used to your over-giving may read a sudden pullback as rejection, so reset expectations slowly and, where appropriate, honestly, rather than withdrawing abruptly and leaving them confused.
