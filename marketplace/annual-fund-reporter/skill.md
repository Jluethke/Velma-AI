# Annual Fund Reporter

Track results and produce the annual fund report.

## Inputs

- `response-tracker`: Gifts and pledges
- `fund-target`: Dollar goal by segment
- `retention-upgrade-goals`: Rate targets

## Outputs

- `annual-fund-report`: Final results with analysis
- `donor-acknowledgment-list`: Donors to thank with gift details

## Process

1. Calculate total raised vs. goal by segment
2. Compute retention, upgrade, and acquisition rates
3. Identify what worked and what to adjust next year
4. Produce the report and the acknowledgment list
