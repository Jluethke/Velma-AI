# Roster Assignment Builder

Assign jersey numbers, positions, and roles to build the official roster.

## Inputs

- `verified-players`: Eligible players
- `ineligible-players`: Players excluded and reasons

## Outputs

- `official-roster`: Finalized roster with jersey numbers, positions, and roles
- `roster-submission-record`: Record submitted to the league for certification

## Process

1. Assign jersey numbers avoiding duplicates and honoring requests where possible.
2. Record each player's primary and secondary positions.
3. Designate team captain and any other roles.
4. Submit the roster to the league for certification.
