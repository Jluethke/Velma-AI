# Results Narrative Organizer

Organize raw results into a logical narrative: choose the order of findings, pair each with the right figure or table, and write the results text.

## Inputs

- `raw-results`: All analysis outputs, figures, and tables from the study
- `study-questions`: The research questions or hypotheses being addressed

## Outputs

- `results-draft`: Structured results section with figure and table references
- `figure-table-list`: Ordered list of figures and tables with captions

## Process

1. Group results by the research question they address.
2. Order findings from most central to supporting or exploratory.
3. Write the results text, stating what each figure or table shows without interpreting.
4. Check that every figure and table is referenced in the text.
