# Asymmetric Advantage Overlap Analysis

Overlap your strengths with competitor weaknesses. Where a real strength of yours lines up with a structural weakness of theirs is an asymmetric advantage, a place you can win that they cannot easily contest.

## Inputs

- `strength-list`: Your durable, real strengths
- `competitor-weakness-map`: Each competitor's structural weaknesses

## Outputs

- `asymmetric-advantages`: The overlaps where you are strong and rivals are structurally weak
- `advantage-ranking`: The overlaps ranked by size and durability

## Process

1. Lay your strength list against the competitor weakness map.
2. Mark every point where a real strength meets a structural weakness of a rival.
3. Keep only overlaps where the rival cannot copy you without damaging their own model.
4. Rank the overlaps by how large and how durable the advantage is.
