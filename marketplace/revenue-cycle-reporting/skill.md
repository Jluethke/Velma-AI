# Revenue Cycle Reporting

Produce the recurring revenue cycle report for practice leadership.

## Inputs

- `improvement-actions`: Actions in progress and their status
- `baseline-metrics`: Baseline for comparison

## Outputs

- `revenue-cycle-report`: Monthly report with current metrics, trend lines, improvement action status, and financial impact
- `executive-summary`: One-page summary of revenue cycle health for leadership

## Process

1. Pull the current month's metrics and compare to baseline and prior month.
2. Report the status of each improvement action (on track, delayed, completed).
3. Calculate the financial impact of completed improvements.
4. Produce the executive summary highlighting wins, risks, and next steps.
