# Tournament Finals Recorder

Record the final match results and produce the final standings and awards.

## Inputs

- `updated-bracket`: Bracket through to the final round
- `match-results-log`: All match results

## Outputs

- `final-standings`: Final placement of all participants
- `tournament-record`: Complete tournament record (bracket, results, awards, statistics)

## Process

1. Run the final and consolation matches.
2. Determine final standings for all participants.
3. Record awards (champion, runner-up, MVP, sportsmanship).
4. Produce the complete tournament record.
