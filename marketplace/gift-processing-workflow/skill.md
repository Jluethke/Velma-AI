# Gift Processing Workflow

Process each received gift from receipt through deposit and recording.

## Inputs

- `channel-configuration`: How each donation channel works
- `fund-structure`: Available funds and their codes

## Outputs

- `processing-procedure`: Step-by-step procedure for processing gifts from each channel
- `internal-controls`: Dual-control and segregation of duties requirements for handling funds

## Process

1. For each channel, define the receipt-to-deposit workflow: who handles, who counts, who records, who deposits.
2. Require dual control: at least two people handle physical gifts from collection through deposit.
3. Record each gift in the donor management system: donor, amount, date, fund, method, and batch number.
4. Reconcile the batch total against the deposit slip before closing the batch.
