# Volunteer Recognition Plan

Plan volunteer recognition to retain and motivate: thank-yous, certificates, events, and references.

## Inputs

- `volunteer_list`: All volunteers who participated
- `contribution_hours`: Hours contributed per person
- `recognition_budget`: Budget for recognition if any

## Outputs

- `recognition_plan`: Recognition activities and timeline
- `thank_you_materials`: Thank-you messages or certificates
- `retention_strategy`: How to keep volunteers coming back

## Process

1. Send immediate thank-you within 48 hours
2. Create certificates for significant contributions
3. Plan recognition event if warranted
4. Offer to serve as reference
5. Invite to future volunteer opportunities
