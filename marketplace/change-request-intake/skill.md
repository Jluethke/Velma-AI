# Change Request Intake

Receive and document a scope change request from the client.

## Inputs

- `change-description`: What the client is requesting to add, remove, or modify
- `current-project-plan`: The current project scope, timeline, and budget

## Outputs

- `change-request-record`: Documented change request with description, requester, date, and priority
- `initial-assessment`: Quick assessment of whether the change is minor, moderate, or major

## Process

1. Document the change request in writing, even if it came verbally.
2. Clarify the request with the client to ensure you understand what they want.
3. Classify the change as minor (within existing scope flexibility), moderate (requires timeline or budget adjustment), or major (requires contract amendment).
4. Acknowledge receipt and set expectations for when the impact assessment will be ready.
