# Three Dimension Assessment

Assess your current state on the three burnout dimensions: are you emotionally exhausted, cynical/detached, or feeling ineffective?

## Inputs

- `current-state`: How you are feeling: energy level, attitude toward work, sense of accomplishment
- `recent-changes`: What changed recently: workload, relationships, role, expectations

## Outputs

- `exhaustion-score`: Emotional exhaustion assessment (1-10): drained, unable to face the day, feeling like every task takes disproportionate effort
- `detachment-score`: Depersonalization assessment (1-10): cynicism, treating people as objects, not caring about outcomes
- `inefficacy-score`: Reduced accomplishment assessment (1-10): feeling that nothing you do matters, that your work has no impact, that you are not good at your job

## Process

1. Score emotional exhaustion (1-10): Do you dread going to work? Does every task feel harder than it should? Do you feel drained by the end of the day even if the day was objectively easy? High scores indicate the energy well is empty.
2. Score depersonalization (1-10): Do you feel cynical about your work or the people you work with? Do you catch yourself not caring about outcomes that used to matter? Are you going through the motions? High scores indicate emotional disconnection.
3. Score reduced accomplishment (1-10): Do you feel like your work does not matter? That you are not making a difference? That you are failing even when the evidence says otherwise? High scores indicate a meaning deficit.
4. The dominant dimension determines the recovery approach: exhaustion needs rest and boundary-setting. Detachment needs reconnection and purpose. Inefficacy needs wins and recognition.
