# Top Performer Selection

Identify the top three to five performers using output signals rather than manager ratings, which measure who the manager likes. If output data is thin, use peer nomination.

## Inputs

- `role-or-team`: The role or team you are building the hiring profile for
- `performance-signal`: Available output data or the basis for peer nomination

## Outputs

- `top-performer-set`: The three to five top performers chosen, with the data behind each choice
- `selection-rationale`: Which method was used, output signal or peer nomination, and why

## Process

1. Collect output signals for everyone in the role: results shipped, revenue produced, cases resolved, whatever the role is measured by.
2. Rank by the output signal. The top three to five are your interview subjects.
3. If output data is unreliable, ask each team member privately to name two people they would want on a critical project, and take the most-named.
4. Record which method you used, so you can revisit it if the resulting profile does not predict well.
