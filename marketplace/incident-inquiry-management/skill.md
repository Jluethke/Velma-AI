# Incident Inquiry Management

Handle incoming inquiries from stakeholders and media during the incident.

## Inputs

- `media-statement`: Prepared holding statement
- `distribution-log`: Record of what has been communicated

## Outputs

- `inquiry-log`: Record of all inquiries received and responses given
- `incident-communication-report`: Complete incident communication report

## Process

1. Log all incoming inquiries with source, question, and response
2. Route media inquiries to the designated spokesperson
3. Provide consistent responses aligned with approved communications
4. Compile the final incident communication report
