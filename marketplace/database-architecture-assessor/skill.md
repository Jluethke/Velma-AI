# Database Architecture Assessor

Assess the current database architecture for reliability risks and improvement opportunities.

## Inputs

- `database-inventory`: List of databases with their roles and configurations
- `reliability-requirements`: RTO, RPO, and availability requirements

## Outputs

- `architecture-assessment`: Assessment of current database reliability posture
- `risk-findings`: Identified reliability risks and gaps

## Process

1. Catalog all databases, their roles, and current configurations
2. Assess single points of failure in the current architecture
3. Compare current capabilities against RTO and RPO requirements
4. Document risks and prioritize by business impact
