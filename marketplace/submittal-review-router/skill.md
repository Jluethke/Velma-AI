# Submittal Review Router

Route submittals to the architect for review and track turnaround.

## Inputs

- `submittal-schedule`: Schedule of required submittals
- `submittal-responsibility-matrix`: Matrix of responsible subcontractors

## Outputs

- `review-status-log`: Status of each submittal in the review process
- `review-results`: Architect review results (approved, approved as noted, revise and resubmit, rejected)

## Process

1. Receive submittals from subcontractors and check for completeness.
2. Forward to the architect with the required review period noted.
3. Track the review status and follow up on overdue reviews.
4. Log the review result for each submittal.
