# Psm Storyline Sequencer

Sequence the slides into an answer-first storyline. The order mirrors the pyramid: governing answer, then the three arguments with their evidence, in the order that best carries the audience. Answer first means a listener who leaves early still got the point.

## Inputs

- `governing-answer`: The answer that opens the presentation
- `slide-outline`: The slides to sequence into a storyline

## Outputs

- `storyline-sequence`: The full slide order, opening on the answer and building through the three arguments
- `opening-slide`: The first content slide, carrying the governing answer so the point lands in the first minute

## Process

1. Open on the governing answer. Do not build up to it over ten slides. State it, then support it.
2. Order the three arguments for the audience, usually strongest first for a skeptical room and most familiar first for a cautious one.
3. Place each argument's evidence directly after it, so the support arrives while the claim is fresh.
4. End by returning to the answer and the action it implies. The close restates the top of the pyramid, now earned.
