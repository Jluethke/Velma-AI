# Demand History Collector

Collect and clean historical demand data.

## Inputs

- `sales-data`: Historical sales data by product and period
- `product-hierarchy`: Product hierarchy and grouping definitions

## Outputs

- `cleaned-demand-history`: Cleaned and structured historical demand data
- `outlier-report`: Outliers and one-time events identified in the history

## Process

1. Extract demand data from the sales system
2. Clean data for returns, cancellations, and stock-outs
3. Identify and document outliers and one-time events
4. Structure the data by product and time period
