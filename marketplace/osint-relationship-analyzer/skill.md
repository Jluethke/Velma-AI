# Osint Relationship Analyzer

Analyze relationships and patterns across verified findings to map connections between entities.

## Inputs

- `verified-findings`: Cross-referenced and confirmed findings
- `unverified-leads`: Single-source findings for context

## Outputs

- `relationship-map`: Graph of connections between entities with supporting evidence
- `pattern-analysis`: Behavioral and temporal patterns identified in the data

## Process

1. Build a graph of entities and their connections (shared infrastructure, communication, financial, organizational)
2. Identify clusters, central nodes, and bridge entities
3. Detect temporal patterns (activity windows, behavioral changes)
4. Highlight anomalies or gaps in the relationship graph
