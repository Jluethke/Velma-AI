# Loss Pattern Extraction

Extract patterns across all interviews: the recurring themes that explain most of your losses.

## Inputs

- `interview-notes`: Notes from all loss interviews
- `real-reasons`: Actual reasons for each loss

## Outputs

- `loss-patterns`: The 3-5 recurring themes across the interviews, ranked by frequency
- `fixable-vs-structural`: Each pattern classified as fixable (can change within 90 days) or structural (requires product/market change)

## Process

1. Lay out all interview notes side by side. Look for themes that appear in 3 or more interviews. A theme in 1 interview is an opinion; a theme in 5 is a pattern.
2. Common patterns: 'Your product does X but does not do Y' (feature gap), 'We could not justify the price to our CFO' (value communication failure), 'Your competitor had a stronger relationship with our team' (relationship gap), 'The switching cost was too high' (migration problem).
3. Classify each pattern as fixable (you could address it within 90 days) or structural (it requires a product redesign, a market repositioning, or accepting the loss). Focus effort on the fixable patterns.
4. Rank by frequency. The pattern that appears most often is the single change that would win back the most deals.
