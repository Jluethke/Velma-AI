# Spc Baseline Collector

Collect baseline data to establish initial control limits.

## Inputs

- `chart-assignments`: Chart types for each characteristic
- `sampling-plan`: Sample size and frequency

## Outputs

- `baseline-data`: Collected baseline measurements
- `data-quality-report`: Assessment of data completeness and measurement system adequacy

## Process

1. Collect at least 25 subgroups under stable conditions
2. Verify the measurement system is adequate (Gage R&R)
3. Check for special causes during baseline collection
4. Document the process conditions during data collection
