# Post Incident Reviewer

Conduct the post-incident review and produce the report.

## Inputs

- `major-incident-record`: The major incident record
- `diagnosis`: Root cause and restoration details
- `communication-log`: Communications during the incident

## Outputs

- `post-incident-report`: Blameless post-incident report with timeline and root cause
- `improvement-actions`: Action items to prevent recurrence

## Process

1. Assemble the timeline from detection to resolution.
2. Identify the root cause and contributing factors.
3. Define action items to prevent recurrence.
4. Publish the blameless post-incident report.
