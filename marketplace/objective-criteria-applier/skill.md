# Objective Criteria Applier

Resolve remaining disagreements using objective criteria: market rates, precedent, expert opinion, or fair standards. Objective criteria prevent negotiation from becoming a contest of will.

## Inputs

- `value-creating-proposals`: The proposals with remaining disagreements
- `negotiation-context`: The context for finding relevant criteria

## Outputs

- `applicable-criteria`: For each unresolved issue: the objective criteria that apply (market rate, comparable transaction, industry standard, legal precedent, expert valuation)
- `criteria-based-resolution`: The resolution suggested by each criterion, with the recommended final terms

## Process

1. For each unresolved issue, research objective criteria: what do others pay or receive in comparable situations? What does industry data show? What do experts recommend?
2. Present criteria, not positions: 'The market rate for this service is X' is stronger than 'I want X.' Criteria depersonalize the disagreement.
3. If multiple criteria conflict, discuss which is most relevant. Do not cherry-pick the criterion that favors you.
4. If no objective criteria exist, propose a fair procedure for resolving the issue: split the difference, flip a coin, use an arbitrator, or take turns choosing.
