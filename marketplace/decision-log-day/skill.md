# Decision Log Day

Log every decision you make for one full day, no matter how small.

## Inputs

- `typical-day-description`: What a typical workday looks like for you
- `known-decision-heavy-periods`: Times of day or week where you feel the most drained from decisions

## Outputs

- `decision-log`: Every decision made in one day, with timestamp and a brief description (expected: 30-70 decisions)
- `decision-count`: Total number of decisions logged

## Process

1. Carry a notebook or keep a note open on your phone. Every time you make a decision, no matter how small, record it with the time. Include: what to eat, what to wear, which email to answer first, how to respond to a message, which task to work on next, whether to attend a meeting.
2. Do not filter. The point is to capture everything. Small decisions are the ones that create fatigue because they are invisible but frequent.
3. At the end of the day, count the decisions. Most people are surprised: they make 50-100 decisions per day, and the cumulative cognitive cost is significant.
4. Note which decisions felt effortful (required thinking, comparison, or deliberation) versus automatic (you chose without thinking). The effortful ones are the fatigue drivers.
