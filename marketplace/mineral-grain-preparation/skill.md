# Mineral Grain Preparation

Extract and prepare quartz or feldspar mineral grains from the sediment under safe-light conditions.

## Inputs

- `collected-samples`: Sediment samples in opaque tubes
- `target-mineral`: Quartz or feldspar, and target grain-size fraction

## Outputs

- `prepared-aliquots`: Mineral grains mounted on discs ready for measurement
- `preparation-log`: Grain size, etching time, density separation parameters, and yield

## Process

1. Open tubes under dim red or orange safe-light and discard the light-exposed ends
2. Wet sieve to isolate the target grain-size fraction (typically 90-250 microns)
3. Treat with HCl and H2O2 to remove carbonates and organics
4. Separate quartz from feldspar using heavy liquid density separation and etch quartz with HF
