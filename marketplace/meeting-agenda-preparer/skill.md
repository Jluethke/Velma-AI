# Meeting Agenda Preparer

Prepare the meeting agenda and required public notices.

## Inputs

- `meeting-items`: List of items to be discussed or acted upon
- `notice-requirements`: Legal notice requirements (timeframe, publication method)

## Outputs

- `meeting-agenda`: The formatted meeting agenda
- `public-notice`: The public notice meeting legal posting requirements

## Process

1. Compile the agenda items with descriptions and any required attachments.
2. Format the agenda per the governing body's standard template.
3. Prepare the public notice with date, time, location, and agenda summary.
4. Publish the notice within the required legal timeframe.
