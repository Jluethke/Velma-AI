# Audience Desired Outcome Clarifier

Define what the audience wants to be true after receiving your message. Their desired outcome is not your desired outcome. Your message succeeds when it connects the two.

## Inputs

- `belief-map`: What the audience believes now
- `vocabulary-list`: How the audience talks about this topic

## Outputs

- `audience-desired-outcome`: What the audience wants to happen, in their own framing
- `your-desired-outcome`: What you want to happen
- `overlap-zone`: Where the two outcomes align, which becomes the foundation of your message

## Process

1. State what the audience wants from this interaction. Not what you think they should want. What they actually want.
2. State what you want from this interaction. Be honest. If you want approval, say approval. If you want behavior change, say behavior change.
3. Find the overlap: the place where giving them what they want also moves you toward what you want. This is your message's center of gravity.
4. If there is no overlap, your message cannot work as designed. Either change what you are asking for, or change who you are asking.
