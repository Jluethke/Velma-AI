# Social Scheduler Publisher

Schedule and publish the content across platforms.

## Inputs

- `ready-to-publish-posts`: Completed posts with copy and visuals
- `posting-schedule`: Optimal times by platform

## Outputs

- `published-posts`: Confirmation of all published content
- `publishing-log`: Record of publish dates and times

## Process

1. Upload content to the scheduling tool
2. Set publish times aligned with the optimal schedule
3. Add hashtags, tags, and alt text for each post
4. Verify all posts are queued correctly
