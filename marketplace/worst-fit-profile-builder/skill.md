# Worst Fit Profile Builder

Compose the worst-fit buyer profile from the signals, named and concrete enough to recognize on a first call.

## Inputs

- `bad-fit-signals`: Signals that precede bad-fit accounts
- `cost-to-serve-notes`: Where bad-fit accounts cost more than they return

## Outputs

- `worst-fit-profile`: A named composite of the buyer you should not sell to
- `disqualifier-list`: The specific, observable disqualifiers drawn from the profile

## Process

1. Write one composite profile from the signals. Give it a name and describe its use case, budget range, timeline, and expectations.
2. For each trait, state why it leads to a bad outcome for both sides, not just for you.
3. Turn the traits into a short list of disqualifiers a rep can observe or ask about directly.
4. Keep disqualifiers observable, since a disqualifier a rep cannot check is useless on a live call.
