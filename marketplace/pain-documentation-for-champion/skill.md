# Pain Documentation For Champion

Create a document the champion can use to justify the purchase internally, with the quantified pain and ROI calculation.

## Inputs

- `pain-presentation`: The pain summary
- `pricing-frame`: Your price vs the pain

## Outputs

- `internal-justification-doc`: A one-page document the champion can present to their CFO or decision maker, showing the pain, the cost, and the ROI
- `cfm-objection-prep`: Answers to the three most likely CFO objections: 'Can we do this ourselves?', 'What if the numbers are wrong?', 'Is there a cheaper option?'

## Process

1. Create the justification doc: Problem (in their words), Cost (using their numbers), Solution (what your product does), Investment (your price), Return (their numbers minus your price).
2. Make the document forward-ready: the champion should be able to email it to the CFO without editing. No jargon, no selling language, just the math.
3. Prepare the champion for three standard CFO objections: 'Can we build this ourselves?' (calculate the internal build cost and compare), 'What if these numbers are too generous?' (show the break-even: even at 50% of the estimated pain, the ROI is still positive), 'Is there a cheaper option?' (cheaper options exist, here is what they do not include).
4. The goal is to make the internal sale as easy as the external one. The champion's conversation with their CFO is the real closing conversation.
