# Campaign Channel Selector

Select and plan the communication channels for the campaign.

## Inputs

- `audience-segments`: Segments with preferred channels
- `campaign-goal`: Dollar target and timeline

## Outputs

- `channel-plan`: Channels selected with rationale
- `channel-budget`: Budget allocation per channel

## Process

1. Evaluate channels (direct mail, email, social media, phone, events, peer-to-peer)
2. Match channels to audience segments
3. Allocate budget across channels based on expected ROI
4. Plan the message sequence per channel
