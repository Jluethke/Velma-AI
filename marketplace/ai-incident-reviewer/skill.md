# Ai Incident Reviewer

Conduct the post-incident review and produce the report.

## Inputs

- `investigation-log`: Investigation timeline
- `fix-deployment`: Fix details

## Outputs

- `post-incident-report`: Full incident report with timeline, root cause, and actions
- `prevention-actions`: Systemic improvements to prevent recurrence

## Process

1. Write the incident timeline from detection to resolution.
2. Document the root cause and contributing factors.
3. List the action items for systemic improvement.
4. Schedule the follow-up review to verify actions completed.
