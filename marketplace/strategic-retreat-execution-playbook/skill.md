# Strategic Retreat Execution Playbook

Write the playbook for executing the retreat once a trigger fires: how to pull back in an orderly way and move the freed resources to their better use. A clean retreat keeps capital; a messy one burns it on the way out.

## Inputs

- `monitoring-plan`: How and how often each trigger is checked
- `reallocation-targets`: The specific better uses the resources would move to

## Outputs

- `retreat-playbook`: The ordered steps to pull back cleanly when a trigger fires
- `reallocation-schedule`: The plan to move freed resources to their targets

## Process

1. Write the ordered steps to wind down the commitment without wasting what can be recovered.
2. Cover obligations, communication, and what to preserve for reuse.
3. Schedule the move of freed resources to the reallocation targets so they do not sit idle.
4. Frame the retreat as reallocation of capital to a better use, and brief it that way.
