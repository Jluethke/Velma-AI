# Habitat Quality Assessor

Assess habitat quality at the study site using vegetation structure, abiotic measurements, disturbance indicators, and land-use history.

## Inputs

- `study-area`: Site location and boundaries
- `species-checklist-template`: Species observed to contextualize habitat use

## Outputs

- `habitat-profile`: Vegetation structure, canopy cover, substrate, water features, and disturbance level
- `abiotic-measurements`: Temperature, humidity, pH, soil type, and water chemistry
- `disturbance-index`: Quantified disturbance level from land-use and physical indicators

## Process

1. Measure vegetation structure (canopy cover, understory density, tree DBH)
2. Record abiotic parameters at standardized points
3. Score disturbance from indicators (roads, clearings, invasive species, erosion)
4. Compile land-use history from maps and local records
