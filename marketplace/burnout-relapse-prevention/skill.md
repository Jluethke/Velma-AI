# Burnout Relapse Prevention

Build an early warning system and maintenance plan to prevent burnout recurrence.

## Inputs

- `energy_map`: Original energy audit results
- `recovery_phases`: Recovery plan details
- `progress_indicators`: Signs of progress

## Outputs

- `warning_signs`: Personal early warning indicators
- `maintenance_practices`: Ongoing prevention practices
- `emergency_plan`: What to do if warning signs appear

## Process

1. Document personal early warning signs from this episode
2. Build monthly check-in ritual
3. Define maintenance boundaries that stay permanent
4. Create emergency response plan for warning signs
5. Schedule quarterly energy audits
