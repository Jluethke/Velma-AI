# Reldebt Dormant List Builder

Rebuild the list of people who genuinely mattered roughly two years ago, using memory prompts to surface the ones you have forgotten, because the relationships quietly draining you are usually the ones you cannot casually recall.

## Inputs

- `memory-prompts`: Sources that jog memory of past-important people: old calendars, photos, past projects, previous jobs, old message threads
- `lookback-window`: How far back to look (two years is the default, long enough that neglect shows but short enough that recovery is realistic)

## Outputs

- `past-important-list`: People who clearly mattered within the lookback window: close colleagues, friends, mentors, collaborators
- `recall-confidence-notes`: How vividly you remember each relationship, since faint recall is itself a signal of how far the relationship has decayed

## Process

1. Work through memory prompts rather than freelancing from memory. Open old calendars, past project rosters, photos, and message threads from around the lookback window. The point is to surface people who have fallen off your mental map.
2. Aim for around 30 people. Include anyone who was a real part of your life or work then: close colleagues, friends, mentors, people you collaborated with closely.
3. For each, note how vividly you recall them. A person you have to reconstruct from a calendar entry is further gone than one whose face comes immediately, and that gap predicts how hard recovery will be.
4. Do not pre-judge who is worth keeping yet. This step is pure recall. Filtering now would drop exactly the neglected relationships the audit exists to catch.
