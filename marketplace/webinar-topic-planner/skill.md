# Webinar Topic Planner

Select the webinar topic, speakers, and format.

## Inputs

- `audience-interests`: Topics the target audience cares about
- `business-goals`: What the webinar should accomplish for the business

## Outputs

- `webinar-plan`: Topic, speakers, format, and date
- `speaker-briefs`: Talking points and logistics for each speaker

## Process

1. Select the topic based on audience interest and business relevance
2. Confirm speakers and assign talking points
3. Set the date, time, and format (live, panel, Q&A)
