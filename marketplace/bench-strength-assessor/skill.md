# Bench Strength Assessor

Assess the current bench strength for each critical role.

## Inputs

- `critical-roles`: Critical roles
- `org-structure`: Organization structure

## Outputs

- `bench-strength-map`: Current bench strength for each critical role (ready now, ready in 1-2 years, developing)
- `coverage-gaps`: Roles with insufficient bench strength

## Process

1. Identify potential successors for each critical role
2. Assess each candidate's readiness (ready now, 1-2 years, developing)
3. Identify roles with no viable internal candidates
4. Map the overall bench strength across the organization
