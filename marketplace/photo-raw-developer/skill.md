# Photo Raw Developer

Develop the raw files with exposure, white balance, and color adjustments.

## Inputs

- `selected-images`: Images flagged for development
- `shoot-brief`: Style direction and color preferences

## Outputs

- `developed-images`: Images with raw development applied
- `style-preset`: The editing preset used for consistency

## Process

1. Correct exposure, highlights, shadows, and white balance
2. Apply the color grade or style preset
3. Adjust individual images for consistency across the set
4. Crop and straighten as needed
