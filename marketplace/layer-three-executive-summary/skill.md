# Layer Three Executive Summary

Layer 3: write a one-sentence executive summary that captures the core idea. If you can say it in one sentence, you understand it. If you cannot, go back to Layer 2.

## Inputs

- `layer-two-result`: Source with highlighted passages
- `highlight-passage-count`: Count of highlighted passages

## Outputs

- `executive-summary`: One sentence capturing the core idea of the entire source material
- `layer-three-result`: Complete progressive summary: one sentence, highlighted passages, bolded passages, full original

## Process

1. Read the highlighted passages. Write one sentence that captures the core idea. The sentence must be specific enough that reading it tells you whether you need to dig deeper.
2. Bad example: 'This article is about pricing strategies.' Good example: 'Customers anchor on the first price they see, so showing a higher-priced option first increases willingness to pay for the mid-tier option.'
3. If you cannot write the sentence, the highlighted passages are not capturing the core idea. Go back to Layer 2 and re-highlight.
4. Place the executive summary at the top of the document. The four layers are now accessible in order: summary, highlights, bold, full original.
