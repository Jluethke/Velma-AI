# Youth Registration Fee Manager

Manage youth registrations, fees, scholarships, and family discounts.

## Inputs

- `youth-divisions`: Divisions to register into
- `safety-guidelines`: Requirements communicated during registration

## Outputs

- `registered-youth`: Registered participants assigned to divisions
- `fee-ledger`: Fees collected, scholarships applied, and outstanding balances

## Process

1. Open registration with the fee schedule and scholarship application.
2. Collect registrations, medical forms, and waivers signed by a parent or guardian.
3. Process fees, apply family discounts and scholarships.
4. Assign participants to divisions and produce the registration record.
