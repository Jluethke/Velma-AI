# Delay Impact Assessor

Assess the impact of delays on the project completion date.

## Inputs

- `updated-schedule`: Schedule with current progress
- `critical-path-changes`: Changes to the critical path

## Outputs

- `impact-assessment`: Assessment of delay impacts on completion date
- `recovery-options`: Options to recover lost time

## Process

1. Compare the current completion date against the baseline.
2. Identify the activities causing the delay.
3. Evaluate recovery options (overtime, additional crews, resequencing, acceleration).
4. Estimate the cost and feasibility of each recovery option.
