# Donor Crm Selection

Select the CRM or database platform for donor management based on requirements and budget.

## Inputs

- `donor-data-model`: Field definitions for the donor record
- `budget-constraints`: Available budget for the system

## Outputs

- `crm-recommendation`: Recommended platform with rationale
- `feature-comparison`: Side-by-side comparison of evaluated options

## Process

1. List must-have features (gift tracking, reporting, email integration, receipting)
2. Evaluate three or more platforms against the requirements
3. Compare pricing including implementation and training costs
4. Recommend the best fit with justification
