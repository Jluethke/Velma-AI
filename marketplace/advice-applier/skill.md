# Advice Applier

Apply whatever answer you receive and record exactly what you did.

## Inputs

- `outreach-message`: A short, easy-to-answer first message
- `contact-plan`: Where and when to send it

## Outputs

- `action-record`: A record of what you did with the answer
- `result-notes`: What resulted from acting on it

## Process

1. Act on the advice or answer you received.
2. Record what you did and what resulted from it.
3. Prepare a short, concrete update to report back.
4. Note any new question that the result raised.
