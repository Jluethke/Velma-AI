# Pricing Impact Monitor

Monitor the margin impact of pricing changes and produce the strategy document.

## Inputs

- `promo-pricing-rules`: Active pricing rules
- `elasticity-results`: Price elasticity data

## Outputs

- `pricing-impact-report`: Revenue and margin impact of pricing changes
- `pricing-strategy-document`: Complete pricing strategy document

## Process

1. Track revenue, margin, and unit volume after each price change.
2. Compare actual results against the elasticity model predictions.
3. Identify products where pricing changes underperformed.
4. Produce the pricing strategy document with recommendations.
