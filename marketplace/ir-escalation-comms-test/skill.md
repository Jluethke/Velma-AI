# Ir Escalation Comms Test

Test escalation procedures and communication plans including notification chains and external reporting.

## Inputs

- `scenario-document`: The incident scenario with injects
- `detection-assessment`: Detection and triage assessment

## Outputs

- `escalation-findings`: Gaps and delays found in escalation procedures
- `communication-findings`: Issues with internal and external communication plans

## Process

1. Walk through the escalation chain: who is notified, in what order, by what channel
2. Test internal communication plans (technical team, management, legal, PR)
3. Check external notification requirements (regulators, customers, law enforcement)
4. Identify single points of failure in the notification chain
