# Post Test Learning Capture

Capture what you learned from the test, regardless of the outcome, and apply it to the next idea.

## Inputs

- `test-results`: What happened
- `decision`: The decision made

## Outputs

- `test-learnings`: What was confirmed, what was surprised, what was learned about the audience, and what would be done differently next time
- `next-test-direction`: If pivoting: the specific change to test next. If building: the features to prioritize based on what drove signups.

## Process

1. Record the key learnings: what the audience responded to, what they ignored, whether they converted, and any qualitative feedback received.
2. If the idea was killed, the learning is still valuable: you now know what this audience does NOT want, which narrows the search for what they do want.
3. If the idea was confirmed, look at the data for feature priorities: what did people mention in the signup form or in follow-up messages? These are the features to build first.
4. File the test results for future reference. Over time, a library of test results builds intuition about what works and what does not.
