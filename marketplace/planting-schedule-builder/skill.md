# Planting Schedule Builder

Build the planting schedule with dates, varieties, and field assignments.

## Inputs

- `crop-selections`: Selected crops and acreage
- `field-assessment`: Field readiness and constraints

## Outputs

- `planting-schedule`: Calendar of planting dates by crop and field
- `variety-selections`: Specific varieties chosen for each crop

## Process

1. Set target planting dates based on frost dates and crop maturity requirements.
2. Select varieties for each crop based on field conditions and market needs.
3. Assign fields to crops in the planting rotation.
4. Build the calendar with sequential planting windows.
