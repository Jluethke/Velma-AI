# Documentary Shoot Planner

Plan the interviews, field shoots, and b-roll list.

## Inputs

- `research-dossier`: Research and key characters identified
- `thesis-statement`: Central argument guiding the film

## Outputs

- `interview-schedule`: Confirmed interviews with questions and logistics
- `field-shoot-plan`: Locations and b-roll shot list

## Process

1. Select and schedule interviewees
2. Prepare interview question sets for each subject
3. Scout field locations and plan the b-roll shot list
4. Arrange travel, equipment, and crew
