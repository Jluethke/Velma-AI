# Gap Surfacer

Draft the full explanation and mark every point where it breaks down.

## Inputs

- `lesson-outline`: The ideas arranged in teaching order
- `key-points`: The few points that matter most

## Outputs

- `explanation-draft`: A full draft of the explanation
- `breakdown-points`: Specific points where the explanation broke

## Process

1. Write or speak the explanation end to end.
2. Mark each point where you hand-wave or lose the thread.
3. Turn each breakdown into a specific study question.
4. Rank the breakdowns by how central they are.
