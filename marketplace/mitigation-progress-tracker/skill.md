# Mitigation Progress Tracker

Track the progress of risk mitigation actions against their timelines.

## Inputs

- `treatment-plans`: Treatment plans with action items and deadlines
- `owner-notifications`: Owner records for follow-up

## Outputs

- `progress-report`: Status of each mitigation action
- `overdue-actions`: List of actions past their deadline

## Process

1. Collect status updates from each risk owner
2. Compare progress against planned timelines
3. Flag overdue actions and escalate as needed
4. Update the risk register with current status
