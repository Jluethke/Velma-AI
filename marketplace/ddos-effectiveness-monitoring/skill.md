# Ddos Effectiveness Monitoring

Monitor the effectiveness of mitigation measures and adjust as needed.

## Inputs

- `mitigation-status`: Active mitigation measures
- `filtering-rules`: Current filtering and rate limiting rules

## Outputs

- `mitigation-effectiveness`: Assessment of how well mitigations are working
- `adjusted-rules`: Updated rules based on monitoring results

## Process

1. Monitor service availability and response times during mitigation
2. Track the ratio of legitimate to attack traffic being passed
3. Adjust filtering rules if the attacker changes tactics
4. Document mitigation effectiveness metrics over time
