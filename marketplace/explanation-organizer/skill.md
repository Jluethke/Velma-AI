# Explanation Organizer

Organize the topic into a teachable structure with a clear order.

## Inputs

- `teach-topic`: The topic framed for teaching
- `audience-profile`: The audience and their starting knowledge

## Outputs

- `lesson-outline`: The ideas arranged in teaching order
- `key-points`: The few points that matter most

## Process

1. Arrange the ideas in the order a learner needs them.
2. Reduce the topic to the few key points that matter most.
3. Choose an example or two that carry each key point.
4. Mark where a learner is most likely to get stuck.
