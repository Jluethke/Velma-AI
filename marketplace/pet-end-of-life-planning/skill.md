# Pet End Of Life Planning

Plan for a pet's end of life: quality of life assessment, euthanasia decisions, grief resources, and memorialization.

## Inputs

- `pet_age`: Current age vs life expectancy
- `health_status`: Current health conditions
- `family_situation`: Children or others who will be affected

## Outputs

- `quality_of_life_scale`: Assessment tool for ongoing monitoring
- `decision_framework`: How to know when it is time
- `grief_resources`: Support resources for family members

## Process

1. Learn quality of life assessment scales
2. Discuss options with veterinarian
3. Plan for how to include children in the process
4. Research cremation, burial, or other options
5. Identify grief support resources
