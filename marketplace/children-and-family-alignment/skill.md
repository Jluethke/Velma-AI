# Children And Family Alignment

Discuss children and family planning: whether to have kids, timing, parenting philosophy, and extended family boundaries.

## Inputs

- `individual_views`: Each person's views on having children
- `family_relationships`: Dynamics with extended families
- `career_considerations`: How careers factor into family timing

## Outputs

- `family_plan`: Agreement on children and timing
- `parenting_principles`: Shared parenting philosophy basics
- `family_boundaries`: Boundaries with extended family

## Process

1. Discuss if, when, and how many children
2. Explore parenting philosophies and values
3. Address differing family cultural expectations
4. Set boundaries with in-laws and extended family
5. Plan for if views change over time
