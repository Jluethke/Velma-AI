# Planned Giving Vehicle Selector

Identify the planned giving vehicles to offer based on organizational capacity.

## Inputs

- `organization-profile`: Size, mission, and endowment goals
- `legal-capacity`: Legal and administrative resources available

## Outputs

- `vehicle-menu`: Planned giving options to market (bequests, trusts, annuities, beneficiary designations)
- `administration-plan`: How each vehicle will be administered

## Process

1. Review planned giving vehicles (bequests, charitable remainder trusts, gift annuities, beneficiary designations, retained life estates)
2. Assess organizational capacity to administer each
3. Select the vehicles to offer based on donor base and complexity
4. Define the administration process for each selected vehicle
