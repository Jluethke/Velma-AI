# Marketing Copy Extractor

Turn the gap descriptions into marketing copy. The customers already wrote the copy for you in their complaints.

## Inputs

- `gap-pattern-report`: The common gaps with representative quotes
- `raw-language-bank`: Exact customer phrases organized by theme

## Outputs

- `derived-marketing-copy`: Headlines, subheads, and ad copy derived directly from customer complaint language, pre-answering the objections competitors created
- `seo-keyword-list`: The exact phrases customers use to describe the problem, which are also the phrases they will search for

## Process

1. For each top gap, find the 3 most vivid customer quotes. These quotes describe the pain in language that other customers will immediately recognize.
2. Write headlines that pre-answer the complaint: if the complaint is 'took 3 days to get a response', the headline is 'Same-day response, every time' (or whatever your actual capability is).
3. Extract the search-worthy phrases. Customers searching for alternatives will type the same words they use in reviews: 'alternative to X that actually Y', 'X but with Z'.
4. Do not fabricate claims you cannot deliver. Only write copy for gaps you actually fill. Unfilled gaps go to the product roadmap, not the marketing copy.
