# Conversation Rehearsal

Rehearse the full conversation: observation, impact, request, silence. Say it out loud. Hearing your own words reveals where you slip from observation into interpretation, from impact into accusation, and from request into venting.

## Inputs

- `observation-statement`: The factual description
- `impact-statement`: The consequences
- `request-statement`: What you need
- `silence-plan`: How you handle the silence

## Outputs

- `rehearsal-notes`: Where you stumbled, softened, or slipped into interpretation during rehearsal
- `final-conversation-script`: The revised, rehearsed conversation script ready for delivery

## Process

1. Say the observation, impact, and request out loud, in order. Record yourself if possible.
2. Listen for interpretation creeping in: 'you always,' 'you never,' 'I think you meant to,' 'it seems like you.'
3. Listen for softening: 'I might be wrong, but,' 'this is just my perception,' 'I do not want to make a big deal of this.' These phrases give the other person permission to dismiss the message.
4. Revise and repeat until you can deliver all three parts in under 60 seconds without interpretation, softening, or judgment.
