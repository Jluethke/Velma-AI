# Internal Audit Program Builder

Implement the internal audit program.

## Inputs

- `process-map`: Processes to audit
- `quality-policy`: Quality objectives to verify

## Outputs

- `audit-schedule`: Annual internal audit schedule covering all processes
- `auditor-qualifications`: Trained internal auditor roster

## Process

1. Build the annual audit schedule covering all processes and clauses
2. Train internal auditors on ISO 19011 audit principles
3. Develop audit checklists for each process
4. Define the nonconformity and corrective action workflow for audit findings
