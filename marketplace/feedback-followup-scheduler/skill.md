# Feedback Followup Scheduler

Schedule the follow-up for about a week out and hold it. Feedback without follow-up signals that the change did not really matter, and the follow-up is where reinforcement and accountability happen.

## Inputs

- `agreed-change`: The single change agreed
- `change-owner`: Who owns it and the support promised

## Outputs

- `followup-schedule`: The follow-up set about a week out, checking the observable change
- `outcome-note`: What happened at the follow-up and the next step if the change did not stick

## Process

1. Set the follow-up about a week out, while the agreed change is still fresh and testable.
2. At the follow-up, check against the observable change: did it happen in the situations where it should have?
3. If it happened, reinforce it specifically so the person knows it was noticed.
4. If it did not, revisit whether the change was clear, achievable, and supported, and adjust rather than simply repeating the feedback.
