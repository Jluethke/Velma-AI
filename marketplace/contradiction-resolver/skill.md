# Contradiction Resolver

Investigate and resolve contradictions between evidence sources by tracing them to methodological, definitional, or contextual differences.

## Inputs

- `divergence-catalog`: contradicting source pairs with hypothesized reasons

## Outputs

- `resolution-table`: each contradiction with its identified root cause and resolution status (resolved, partially resolved, unresolved)
- `residual-contradictions`: contradictions that cannot be resolved with available information

## Process

1. For each contradicting pair, compare study designs, populations, definitions, and measurement instruments
2. Determine whether the contradiction is real (different causal mechanisms) or apparent (different operationalizations of the same construct)
3. Classify the resolution: resolved by moderator analysis, partially resolved by contextual difference, or genuinely unresolved
4. Document residual contradictions as open questions for future research
