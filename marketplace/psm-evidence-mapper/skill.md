# Psm Evidence Mapper

Map the evidence under each argument. Evidence is the bottom layer of the pyramid: the data, examples, and facts that make each argument believable. An argument without evidence is an assertion, and a presentation of assertions convinces no one who did not already agree.

## Inputs

- `supporting-arguments`: The three arguments that need evidence

## Outputs

- `evidence-map`: For each argument, the specific evidence that supports it
- `evidence-gaps`: Arguments that lack solid evidence, flagged for strengthening or softening before the dry run

## Process

1. For each argument, list the evidence you have: a number, a trend, a customer example, a precedent.
2. Rank the evidence by strength and keep the strongest one or two per argument. A slide of ten weak points is weaker than one strong one.
3. Flag arguments with thin or missing evidence. An argument you cannot support should be strengthened with new evidence or dropped.
4. Note where evidence is strong enough to lead the slide and where it only supports the spoken point.
