# Mda Spend Inventory Builder

Build a complete list of current spending lines for the period.

## Inputs

- `spending-context`: The budget or allocation being examined and its purpose
- `time-period`: The period the spending covers, such as a month, quarter, or year

## Outputs

- `spend-inventory`: Every recurring and one-time outflow grouped by category
- `baseline-totals`: Current total by category and overall

## Process

1. List every recurring and one-time outflow for the period.
2. Group the outflows by category.
3. Record the current amount for each line and total it.
