# Update Feedback Loop

After the first month, check whether the updates are being read and whether they are changing decisions. If not, the system needs revision.

## Inputs

- `update-template`: The format being used
- `update-schedule`: The current cadence

## Outputs

- `readership-data`: Who opens the updates, who acts on them, who ignores them
- `revision-plan`: Specific changes to cadence, content, or audience based on the feedback

## Process

1. After 4 weeks, check open rates (if email) or ask directly: are you reading these? What do you use from them?
2. If a stakeholder is not reading, either they do not need the update (remove them) or the update does not contain what they need (fix the content).
3. If the update is read but not producing decisions, the content is informational, not actionable. Add a decision prompt: 'Action needed: approve X by Friday.'
4. Revise and re-test. The system is working when every reader can name a decision the update influenced.
