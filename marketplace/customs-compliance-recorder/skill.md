# Customs Compliance Recorder

Produce the customs compliance record for audit purposes.

## Inputs

- `entry-confirmation`: Customs entry filing confirmation
- `screening-record`: Audit trail of all screening checks performed

## Outputs

- `compliance-record`: Complete customs compliance record
- `audit-trail`: Chronological audit trail of all actions taken

## Process

1. Compile all documents, classifications, and screening results
2. Record the timeline from filing to clearance
3. Archive the compliance record for the required retention period
4. Produce the audit trail for regulatory review
