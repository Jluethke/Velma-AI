# Freelance Contract Negotiation

Negotiate and finalize the contract with the client.

## Inputs

- `project-proposal`: The proposal sent to the client
- `pricing-breakdown`: Itemized pricing

## Outputs

- `signed-contract`: The executed contract or agreement
- `payment-schedule`: Agreed payment milestones and amounts

## Process

1. Send the proposal and allow time for client review.
2. Address client questions and requested changes, tracking each revision.
3. Negotiate any changes to scope, pricing, or terms, ensuring each change is reflected in the contract.
4. Finalize and execute the contract (e-signature or countersigned PDF).
5. Confirm the payment schedule and method.
