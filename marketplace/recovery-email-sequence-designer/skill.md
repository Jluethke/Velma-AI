# Recovery Email Sequence Designer

Design the cart recovery email sequence with timing, content, and incentives.

## Inputs

- `abandonment-segments`: Cart abandoner segments
- `trigger-analysis`: Abandonment trigger analysis

## Outputs

- `email-sequence`: Recovery email sequence (timing, subject, content, offers)
- `incentive-rules`: Discount and incentive rules by segment

## Process

1. Design the email cadence (1 hour, 24 hours, 72 hours after abandonment).
2. Write subject lines and body copy for each email.
3. Define incentive tiers (no discount first, then percentage or free shipping).
4. Set rules for which segments receive which incentive level.
