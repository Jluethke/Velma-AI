# Humane Separation Planner

Plan the separation to be direct, specific, immediate, and humane. Dragging it out, softening it into ambiguity, or ambushing the person all make it worse, and the person should already know the gap and the consequences from prior feedback.

## Inputs

- `decision-determination`: The determination to separate
- `decision-record`: The record behind it

## Outputs

- `separation-plan`: The timing, privacy, support offered, and who is present, planned with dignity
- `communication-script`: A short script that states the decision first and does not relitigate the whole history

## Process

1. Confirm the person has already heard the gap and the consequences in prior feedback, so the decision is not a surprise.
2. Plan to deliver it directly and specifically: the decision, the reason in one or two clear sentences, and that it is final.
3. Plan the logistics with dignity: timing, privacy, what support such as notice, references, or transition help is offered, and who is present.
4. Write a short script that states the decision first and does not relitigate the whole history.
