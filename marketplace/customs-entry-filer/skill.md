# Customs Entry Filer

File the customs entry and track clearance status.

## Inputs

- `customs-documents`: Commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin
- `screening-results`: Pass/fail results for each party screened

## Outputs

- `entry-confirmation`: Customs entry filing confirmation
- `clearance-status`: Current clearance status and any holds

## Process

1. Submit the entry through the customs broker or direct filing system
2. Monitor for examination or hold notices
3. Respond to any customs queries with supporting documents
4. Record the clearance confirmation and release date
