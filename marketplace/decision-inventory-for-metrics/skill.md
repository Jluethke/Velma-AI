# Decision Inventory For Metrics

Inventory the decisions that metrics must inform. A metric without a decision is decoration.

## Inputs

- `context`: What the metrics serve (a business, a product, a team, a personal goal)
- `time-horizon`: The planning horizon for the decisions

## Outputs

- `decision-list`: Every recurring decision with its frequency, current information used, and the uncertainty that makes it difficult
- `highest-uncertainty-decisions`: The 3-5 decisions where better information would produce the biggest improvement in decision quality

## Process

1. List every recurring decision: resource allocation, pricing, hiring, product direction, marketing channel selection, personal spending, health choices.
2. For each decision, identify the uncertainty: what do you not know that, if you knew it, would make the decision obvious?
3. Rank by impact: which decisions have the highest stakes multiplied by the highest uncertainty?
4. Select the top 3-5. These deserve dedicated metrics. Everything else uses existing data or periodic assessment.
