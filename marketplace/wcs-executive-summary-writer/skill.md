# Wcs Executive Summary Writer

For a long document, write the executive summary as if it were the whole document, because for most readers it is. Around 80 percent will read only the summary and act on it. If the summary cannot stand alone, the document will be misread by the majority who never go past it.

## Inputs

- `scannable-layout`: The structured body the summary sits above
- `numbered-actions`: The actions the summary must carry so readers who stop here still act

## Outputs

- `executive-summary`: A summary that states the conclusion, the key facts, and the actions, readable on its own
- `summary-sufficiency-test`: The result of checking whether a reader who reads only the summary can act correctly

## Process

1. Write the summary to stand alone: the conclusion, the two or three facts behind it, and the actions required.
2. Put the actions in the summary, not only in the body. A reader who stops at the summary still needs to know what to do.
3. Run the sufficiency test: hand the summary alone to someone and ask what they would do. If they act correctly, the summary works.
4. Keep the body as the backup for the 20 percent who need the detail, and do not repeat the summary inside it.
