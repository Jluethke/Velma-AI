# Career Change Financial Bridge

Build a financial plan to bridge the transition: savings target, income gap, and expense reduction.

## Inputs

- `current_income`: Current salary
- `expected_starting_salary`: Entry salary in new field
- `transition_timeline`: Expected months to complete switch
- `monthly_expenses`: Current monthly expenses

## Outputs

- `savings_target`: How much to save before making the switch
- `expense_cuts`: Where to reduce spending during transition
- `bridge_plan`: Month-by-month financial plan

## Process

1. Calculate income gap during transition
2. Identify expenses to cut or reduce
3. Set savings target with 3-month buffer
4. Plan for education costs
5. Create month-by-month cash flow projection
