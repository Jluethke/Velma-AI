# Buyer Feedback Collector

Collect feedback from buyers on why they chose or rejected the offering.

## Inputs

- `deal-sample`: Deals selected for analysis
- `contact-list`: Buyer contacts for each deal

## Outputs

- `feedback-responses`: Collected buyer feedback by deal
- `response-rate`: Percentage of buyers who participated

## Process

1. Draft the feedback questionnaire (decision criteria, experience, competitors considered).
2. Contact each buyer by email or call.
3. Record responses in a structured format.
4. Note the response rate and any non-response bias.
