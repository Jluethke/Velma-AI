# Study Area Definer

Define the study area: boundaries, habitat types, accessibility, and known ecological features.

## Inputs

- `area-description`: Geographic location, maps, and prior survey data
- `survey-objectives`: Target taxa, ecological questions, and required precision

## Outputs

- `area-map`: Delineated study area with habitat polygons and access points
- `stratification-plan`: How the area is divided into strata (habitat types) for sampling

## Process

1. Delineate the study area boundary using geographic coordinates.
2. Map distinct habitat types within the area using aerial imagery or prior surveys.
3. Identify access constraints (roads, terrain, landowner permissions).
4. Stratify the area by habitat type for proportional or optimal allocation of sampling effort.
