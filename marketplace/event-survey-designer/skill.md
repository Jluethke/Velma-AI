# Event Survey Designer

Design the post-event feedback survey covering content, logistics, speakers, and overall satisfaction.

## Inputs

- `event-details`: Event name, type, agenda, speakers, and attendee count
- `evaluation-priorities`: What the organizer most wants to learn from the feedback

## Outputs

- `event-survey`: The event feedback survey instrument with questions and response types
- `distribution-timing`: When and how the survey will be sent to attendees

## Process

1. Write questions covering content quality, logistics, speaker ratings, networking value, and overall NPS.
2. Select the response format (1-5 scale for ratings, open text for comments, NPS for likelihood to recommend).
3. Keep the survey short (under 10 questions) to maximize completion.
4. Set the distribution timing (within 24 hours of event close) and channel (email, app notification).
