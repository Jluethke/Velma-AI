# Training Content Relevance Reviewer

Review security awareness training content for relevance, accuracy, and completeness.

## Inputs

- `training-materials`: Current security awareness training content and curriculum
- `threat-landscape`: Current threat landscape relevant to the organization

## Outputs

- `content-review-findings`: Findings on training content quality, accuracy, and coverage gaps
- `topic-coverage-matrix`: Matrix showing which security topics are covered and which are missing

## Process

1. Review each training module for technical accuracy and up-to-date threat examples
2. Map training topics against the current threat landscape for the organization
3. Identify missing topics (MFA fatigue, QR phishing, deepfake voice calls, supply chain attacks)
4. Assess whether content is engaging or likely to be skipped through
