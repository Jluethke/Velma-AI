# Npm Batna Strengthener

If your BATNA is weak, improve it before you negotiate rather than during. The strength of your BATNA, not your arguments in the room, is the real source of your leverage. A better alternative, even a modest one, changes the whole negotiation more than any clever tactic.

## Inputs

- `your-batna`: Your current best alternative
- `their-batna-estimate`: The other side's estimated alternative, for comparison

## Outputs

- `batna-strength-rating`: A rating of how strong your BATNA is relative to theirs: strong, even, or weak
- `batna-improvement-actions`: Concrete actions to strengthen your alternative before entering the negotiation

## Process

1. Rate your BATNA against theirs: strong if you can walk away comfortably, weak if walking away hurts you more than them.
2. If it is weak, list ways to improve it: line up a second option, extend your timeline, reduce your dependence on this deal.
3. Take the improvement actions before the negotiation where you can. A real alternative secured in advance beats a bluff in the room.
4. If the BATNA cannot be improved in time, plan to protect it: keep it private, and do not signal how much you need the deal.
