# Audit Findings Evaluator

Evaluate audit findings, assess misstatements, and determine their effect on the opinion.

## Inputs

- `substantive-results`: Results of substantive procedures
- `misstatements-log`: Accumulated misstatements found

## Outputs

- `misstatement-assessment`: Assessment of misstatements against materiality
- `management-letter-items`: Control deficiencies and recommendations

## Process

1. Aggregate all identified misstatements (factual, judgmental, projected).
2. Compare total misstatement to materiality thresholds.
3. Classify control deficiencies as significant deficiencies or material weaknesses.
4. Discuss findings with management and obtain representations.
