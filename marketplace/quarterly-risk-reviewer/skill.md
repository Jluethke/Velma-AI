# Quarterly Risk Reviewer

Conduct a quarterly review to update risk scores, add new risks, and close resolved ones.

## Inputs

- `progress-report`: Current mitigation status
- `residual-risk-estimates`: Expected residual risk levels

## Outputs

- `updated-risk-register`: Risk register with current scores and statuses
- `risk-register-report`: Quarterly risk report for leadership

## Process

1. Re-score existing risks based on current conditions and mitigation progress
2. Add newly identified risks from the quarter
3. Close risks that have been fully mitigated or are no longer relevant
4. Produce the quarterly report for leadership review
