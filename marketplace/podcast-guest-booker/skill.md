# Podcast Guest Booker

Pitch, schedule, and confirm the guest for recording.

## Inputs

- `guest-criteria`: What the ideal guest looks like
- `prospect-list`: The ranked list of potential guests

## Outputs

- `confirmed-guest`: The booked guest with confirmed date and time
- `booking-details`: Recording platform, time zone, and technical requirements

## Process

1. Send personalized outreach to the top prospects explaining the podcast and why they fit.
2. Negotiate a recording date and time that works for both parties.
3. Send the calendar invite with the recording link and technical instructions.
4. Confirm 48 hours before the recording.
