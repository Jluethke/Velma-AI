# Supplier Quote Solicitor

Solicit quotes from suppliers and issue purchase orders.

## Inputs

- `material-list`: Materials needed with quantities
- `delivery-schedule`: Required delivery dates

## Outputs

- `purchase-orders`: Issued purchase orders
- `supplier-commitments`: Confirmed delivery dates from suppliers

## Process

1. Send quote requests to at least three suppliers per material.
2. Compare quotes on price, lead time, and terms.
3. Issue purchase orders for the selected suppliers.
4. Confirm delivery dates and log the commitments.
