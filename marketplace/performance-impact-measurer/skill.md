# Performance Impact Measurer

Measure the impact of training on performance.

## Inputs

- `completion-data`: Completion data
- `participant-feedback`: Feedback

## Outputs

- `impact-assessment`: Assessment of training impact on skill levels and performance
- `roi-estimate`: Estimated return on training investment

## Process

1. Compare pre- and post-training skill assessments
2. Correlate training completion with performance review outcomes
3. Calculate the cost per learner and estimated ROI
4. Identify which programs had the strongest impact
