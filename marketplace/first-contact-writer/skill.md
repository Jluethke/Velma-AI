# First Contact Writer

Write a short first message that leads with respect for their work and the one question.

## Inputs

- `single-question`: The one question worth asking
- `question-context`: The work you already did on it

## Outputs

- `outreach-message`: A short, easy-to-answer first message
- `contact-plan`: Where and when to send it

## Process

1. Open by referencing something specific in their work.
2. Ask the single question without asking them to be your mentor.
3. Keep the message short and easy to reply to.
4. Choose the channel where they are most likely to respond.
