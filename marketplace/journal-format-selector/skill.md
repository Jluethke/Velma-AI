# Journal Format Selector

Select the journaling format that fits your goals and style.

## Inputs

- `journaling-goals`: What you want from journaling (clarity, gratitude, reflection, creativity)
- `time-available`: Minutes per day available for journaling

## Outputs

- `journal-format`: Selected format (freewrite, gratitude, reflection prompts, bullet journal)
- `format-guidelines`: Rules and structure for the chosen format

## Process

1. Match goals to format: clarity maps to freewrite, positivity to gratitude, growth to reflection prompts.
2. Confirm the format fits the available time.
3. Write the format guidelines (word count, structure, constraints).
