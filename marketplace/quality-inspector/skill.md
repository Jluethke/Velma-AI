# Quality Inspector

Inspect the work against the standards and document findings.

## Inputs

- `hold-point-schedule`: Upcoming inspection hold points
- `inspection-criteria`: Acceptance criteria

## Outputs

- `inspection-results`: Results of each inspection (pass, fail, conditional)
- `finding-photos`: Photos documenting the inspection findings

## Process

1. Inspect the work against the acceptance criteria.
2. Document each finding with photos and measurements.
3. Record the inspection result (pass, fail, conditional pass).
4. Note any deviations that require engineering review.
