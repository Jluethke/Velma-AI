# Medicare Plan Comparison

Compare Original Medicare vs Medicare Advantage and evaluate Part D prescription drug plans.

## Inputs

- `medications`: Current prescription medications
- `doctors`: Current doctors and specialists
- `health_needs`: Expected healthcare needs
- `travel_frequency`: How often traveling out of area

## Outputs

- `plan_comparison`: Side-by-side comparison of options
- `drug_plan_ranking`: Part D plans ranked by total cost
- `recommendation`: Recommended plan with rationale

## Process

1. Compare Original Medicare to Advantage plans
2. Check doctor network participation
3. Run prescription drug plan comparison
4. Estimate total annual costs per option
5. Factor in travel and flexibility needs
