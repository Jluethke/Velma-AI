# Discovery Call Runner

Conduct a discovery call to understand the client's needs and assess project fit.

## Inputs

- `inquiry-summary`: Structured summary of the inquiry
- `qualification-score`: Initial qualification assessment

## Outputs

- `discovery-notes`: Structured notes from the discovery call covering goals, constraints, and success criteria
- `project-scope-draft`: Draft scope including deliverables, timeline estimate, and budget range

## Process

1. Prepare the call agenda: client goals, current situation, success criteria, budget, timeline, decision process.
2. During the call, document answers to each agenda item and note any red flags (scope creep risk, unclear decision maker, unrealistic timeline).
3. After the call, summarize the project scope, deliverables, timeline, and estimated budget.
4. Decide whether to proceed with a proposal or decline the engagement.
