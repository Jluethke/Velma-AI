# Cold Packaging Selector

Select packaging and refrigerant for temperature-controlled shipments.

## Inputs

- `temperature-profiles`: Required temperature range and tolerance per product
- `product-list`: Products requiring temperature-controlled shipping

## Outputs

- `packaging-specifications`: Selected packaging and refrigerant per product
- `thermal-validation`: Thermal performance test results for the packaging

## Process

1. Select insulated packaging appropriate for the temperature range
2. Choose the refrigerant type and quantity (gel packs, dry ice, phase-change)
3. Validate thermal performance against the required transit time
4. Document the packaging specifications
