# Referral Performance Measurer

Measure referral program performance and produce the program plan.

## Inputs

- `incentive-structure`: Incentive design for cost tracking
- `fraud-rules`: Fraud prevention rules

## Outputs

- `referral-metrics`: Referral volume, conversion rate, and cost per acquisition
- `referral-program-plan`: Complete referral program plan document

## Process

1. Track referral volume, conversion rate, and reward redemption
2. Calculate cost per acquisition and compare to other channels
3. Produce the referral program plan
