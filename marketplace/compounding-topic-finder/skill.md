# Compounding Topic Finder

Find the topics that compound: questions people will keep asking next year and the year after. These are the only topics worth investing in.

## Inputs

- `content-classification`: Which existing pieces compound
- `compound-decay-ratio`: Current ratio of compounding to decaying content

## Outputs

- `compounding-topic-list`: Topics that have persistent search demand (verified by search volume stability over 2+ years) and align with your expertise
- `decay-topic-avoidance-list`: Topic patterns to avoid or limit: news reactions, trend commentary, hot takes, and event coverage

## Process

1. Start with what already compounds in your library. What topics do those pieces cover? What format are they in? What search intent do they serve (how-to, what-is, comparison, list)?
2. For candidate topics, check the search volume trend over 2+ years. If the volume is stable or growing, the topic compounds. If the volume spikes and falls, it decays.
3. The highest-value compounding topics answer questions at the moment of decision or action: 'how to X', 'X vs Y', 'best X for Y', and 'what is X'. These serve people who are about to do something.
4. Create the avoidance list: news reactions, trend commentary, predictions, event recaps, and 'just announced' posts. These get a spike of traffic and then nothing. Limit them to 20% or less of output.
