# Scale Up Decision

Decide whether to scale the habit beyond its tiny version, which is allowed only once the trigger fires reliably.

## Inputs

- `reliability-tracker`: The daily record of anchor and habit
- `trigger-firing-rate`: The share of days the habit followed the anchor

## Outputs

- `scale-up-plan`: A plan to grow the habit gradually, triggered only by a reliable firing rate
- `next-stack-candidate`: The next tiny habit to stack once this one is stable, if desired

## Process

1. Scale only when the firing rate holds above eighty percent for two weeks. Scaling an unreliable trigger breaks the habit you were building.
2. Grow the habit in small increments, adding a little effort at a time, so the trigger keeps firing as the size increases.
3. If scaling causes the firing rate to drop, step back to the smaller size. The reliable small habit is worth more than a large one you skip.
4. Once the first stack is stable, pick the next tiny habit and attach it to the end of this one, building a chain one reliable link at a time.
