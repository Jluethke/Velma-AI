# Information Source Audit

Audit every information source you consume: news, email newsletters, social media feeds, Slack channels, podcasts, blogs, RSS feeds, industry reports. Score each by signal-to-noise ratio and time spent.

## Inputs

- `current-information-sources`: List of all information sources you currently consume (subscriptions, feeds, channels, newsletters, podcasts)
- `information-goals`: What you need information for: specific projects, ongoing education, industry awareness, personal interest

## Outputs

- `source-signal-scores`: Each source scored: signal-to-noise ratio (percentage of content that is actionable or educational), weekly time spent, and alignment with information goals
- `noise-sources-identified`: Sources below 30 percent signal ratio flagged for elimination

## Process

1. List every information source: email newsletters, Slack channels, social media accounts, podcasts, blogs, RSS feeds, news sites, industry reports, YouTube channels, subreddits.
2. For each source, review the last 10 items. Count how many were actionable (you did something because of them) or educational (you learned something you have used). Divide by 10. That is the signal ratio.
3. Track the time spent on each source for one week. Include both active consumption (reading, watching) and passive exposure (scrolling, notifications).
4. Score each source: signal ratio times relevance to your current goals. Sources that are interesting but irrelevant to current work score lower than sources that are boring but directly useful.
