# Corrosion Testing Report

Compile the corrosion study into a report with methods, electrochemical data, mass loss, morphology, and material ranking.

## Inputs

- `corrosion-rates`: Calculated rates
- `material-ranking`: Performance comparison
- `corrosion-morphology`: Visual evidence of corrosion form

## Outputs

- `corrosion-report`: Complete corrosion testing report
- `data-archive`: Electrochemical files, mass data, and images

## Process

1. Write methods: test standards, solution chemistry, parameters, specimen preparation, and instrumentation.
2. Include polarization curves, EIS Nyquist plots, and corroded surface images.
3. Present corrosion rates and material ranking in a comparison table.
4. Recommend the material best suited for the service environment.
