# Playoff Championship Manager

Manage the playoff bracket and championship based on regular season standings.

## Inputs

- `league-standings`: Final regular season standings
- `season-statistics`: Statistics used for tiebreakers and seeding

## Outputs

- `playoff-bracket`: Seeded playoff bracket with matchups and schedule
- `championship-record`: Final results, champion, and season summary

## Process

1. Determine playoff qualifiers from final standings using tiebreaker rules.
2. Seed the playoff bracket and schedule the games.
3. Track results through each round and advance winners.
4. Record the championship results and produce the season summary.
