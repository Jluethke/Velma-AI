# Demand Growth Forecaster

Forecast demand growth based on historical trends and business plans.

## Inputs

- `utilization-report`: Current utilization metrics
- `peak-usage-patterns`: Peak usage data

## Outputs

- `demand-forecast`: Projected demand by segment for the planning horizon (6, 12, 24 months)
- `growth-assumptions`: Documented assumptions behind the forecast

## Process

1. Analyze historical utilization trends over the past 12 to 24 months.
2. Factor in planned business changes (new services, customer growth, migrations).
3. Project demand at 6, 12, and 24 month intervals.
4. Document assumptions and confidence levels for each projection.
