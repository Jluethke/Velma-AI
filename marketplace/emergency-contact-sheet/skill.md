# Emergency Contact Sheet

Build the master emergency contact sheet that covers all 6 categories.

## Inputs

- `household-members`: Who lives in the household, their ages, and any special needs
- `location-details`: Home address, nearest hospital, fire station, police station

## Outputs

- `emergency-contact-sheet`: The master contact sheet: medical contacts, financial contacts, legal contacts, insurance contacts, utility contacts, and trusted persons
- `trusted-persons-list`: People outside the household who have been briefed and can act on your behalf in each category

## Process

1. List the contacts for each category: doctors, insurance agents, attorneys, financial advisors, accountants, utility companies, landlord or property manager, trusted neighbor or friend.
2. For each contact, record: name, phone number, relationship, and what they can help with.
3. Identify 2-3 trusted persons outside the household who can act on your behalf. For each, define what they are authorized to do (access accounts, make medical decisions, care for children or pets).
4. Verify all phone numbers are current. Set a reminder to re-verify quarterly.
