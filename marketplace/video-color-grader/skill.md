# Video Color Grader

Color grade and master the video for final delivery.

## Inputs

- `mixed-timeline`: Timeline with all audio and graphics
- `audio-stems`: Final mixed audio stems

## Outputs

- `final-video`: Color graded and mastered video file
- `delivery-package`: All deliverables in required formats and resolutions

## Process

1. Apply primary color correction for exposure and white balance
2. Apply secondary grades and the creative look
3. Export the master file and all required delivery formats
4. Verify technical specifications against platform requirements
