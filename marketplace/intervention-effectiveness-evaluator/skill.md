# Intervention Effectiveness Evaluator

Evaluate the overall effectiveness of the intervention program.

## Inputs

- `progress-report`: Progress metrics per student post-intervention
- `intervention-log`: Record of interventions deployed

## Outputs

- `effectiveness-report`: Analysis of which interventions produced the best outcomes
- `program-recommendations`: Recommendations for improving the intervention program

## Process

1. Calculate retention and grade improvement rates by intervention type
2. Compare outcomes for intervened vs. non-intervened students
3. Identify the most and least effective interventions
4. Produce recommendations for program improvement
