# Historical Actuals Analyzer

Collect and analyze historical financial actuals and trends.

## Inputs

- `financial-history`: Prior period financial data (2-3 years)
- `forecast-period`: Budget period being planned

## Outputs

- `trend-analysis`: Revenue and expense trend analysis with growth rates
- `seasonality-patterns`: Identified seasonal patterns in the data

## Process

1. Import historical revenue and expense data by category and period.
2. Calculate growth rates, averages, and trend lines for each category.
3. Identify seasonal patterns and cyclical trends.
4. Flag anomalies and one-time items that should not be extrapolated.
