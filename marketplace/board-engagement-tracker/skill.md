# Board Engagement Tracker

Track board member engagement and produce the governance handbook.

## Inputs

- `meeting-calendar`: Board and committee meeting schedule
- `expectations-document`: Board member commitments

## Outputs

- `engagement-dashboard`: Attendance, giving, and participation metrics
- `governance-handbook`: Complete board governance reference document

## Process

1. Track attendance at board and committee meetings
2. Monitor giving and fundraising participation
3. Conduct annual board self-assessment
4. Compile the governance handbook with all policies and procedures
