# Insurance Switch Checklist

Execute an insurance switch without coverage gaps: timing, cancellation, and confirmation.

## Inputs

- `current_policies`: Policies being replaced
- `new_policies`: New policies to activate
- `switch_date`: Target switch date

## Outputs

- `switch_timeline`: Step-by-step switch plan
- `overlap_plan`: Ensuring no gap in coverage
- `cancellation_checklist`: How to cancel old policies properly

## Process

1. Activate new policy before canceling old
2. Verify new coverage is active and confirmed
3. Cancel old policy with effective date matching new start
4. Request refund for any prepaid premium
5. Update insurance cards and proof of coverage
