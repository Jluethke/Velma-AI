# Landscaping Job Estimator

Estimate landscaping jobs with accurate labor, material, and equipment costs.

## Inputs

- `service-catalog`: Services and pricing guidelines
- `equipment-requirements`: Equipment needed per service

## Outputs

- `estimate-worksheet`: Job estimate with labor hours, material quantities, equipment costs, and markup
- `proposal-template`: Client-facing proposal with scope, price, timeline, and terms

## Process

1. Measure or assess the property (square footage, slope, obstacles, access).
2. Calculate labor hours based on crew size and production rates.
3. List materials with quantities and current supplier pricing.
4. Apply the overhead and profit margin to produce the final price.
