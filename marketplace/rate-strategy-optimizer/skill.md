# Rate Strategy Optimizer

Optimize the overall rate strategy including BAR, packages, and promotions.

## Inputs

- `channel-allocation`: Current channel allocations
- `demand-forecast`: Predicted demand

## Outputs

- `rate-strategy`: Finalized rate strategy with BAR and packages
- `promotion-calendar`: Scheduled promotions and their terms

## Process

1. Set the best available rate (BAR) ladder by room type.
2. Design value-add packages for low-demand periods.
3. Schedule promotions aligned to need periods.
4. Document the strategy with rationale for each decision.
