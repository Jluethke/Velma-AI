# Jtbd Interview Conductor

Conduct the interviews. The interviewer's job is to reconstruct the timeline of the decision, not to validate hypotheses.

## Inputs

- `interview-guide`: The interview script and structure
- `interview-logistics`: Scheduling and practical details

## Outputs

- `interview-transcripts`: Full transcripts or detailed notes from each interview, organized by the timeline and forces framework
- `raw-forces-data`: For each interview: the push (what was wrong), the pull (what attracted them), the anxiety (what almost stopped them), and the habit (what they were doing before)

## Process

1. Start each interview by asking about the context, not the product: 'Take me back to when you first started thinking about this. What was going on?'
2. Follow the timeline forward. At each stage, ask 'what happened next?' and 'what were you thinking at that point?' The goal is to reconstruct the decision as a story, not as a list of criteria.
3. When the person mentions a feeling ('I was frustrated with...'), go deeper: 'What specifically about it frustrated you? Can you give me an example?'
4. Do not lead. Do not suggest. Do not validate. Your hypotheses about the job are irrelevant during the interview. The person's story is the data.
