# Weekly Theme Planner

Plan weekly themes and activities aligned with developmental goals.

## Inputs

- `developmental-goals`: Goals by developmental domain
- `focus-areas`: Priority areas for the group

## Outputs

- `weekly-plan`: Theme, daily activities, and materials for the week organized by day and time block
- `materials-list`: Supplies and materials needed for the planned activities

## Process

1. Select the weekly theme based on the developmental goals, children's interests, and seasonal relevance.
2. Plan daily activities that address multiple developmental domains through the theme.
3. Include a mix of structured and free-play activities, indoor and outdoor time.
4. List the materials and supplies needed for each activity.
5. Build in flexibility for children who need modified activities.
