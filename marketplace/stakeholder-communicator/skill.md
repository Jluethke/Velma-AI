# Stakeholder Communicator

Communicate status to stakeholders throughout the incident.

## Inputs

- `major-incident-record`: The major incident record
- `diagnosis`: Current diagnosis and restoration status

## Outputs

- `communication-log`: All communications sent during the incident
- `stakeholder-updates`: Status updates sent to business stakeholders

## Process

1. Send the initial notification to all stakeholders.
2. Provide regular updates at the defined cadence (every 30 minutes).
3. Send the resolution notification when the service is restored.
4. Record all communications in the incident log.
