# Onboarding Process Mapper

Map the actual onboarding process as experienced by the new hire, not as documented in the handbook. Walk through the first 4 weeks day by day with a recent hire. For each day, record: what they were supposed to do, what they actually did, what they were waiting for, and what they wished they had known.

## Inputs

- `baseline-measurements`: Current time-to-productivity data
- `variance-analysis`: What explains the difference between fast and slow ramps

## Outputs

- `process-map`: Day-by-day map of the actual onboarding experience, with waiting times, blockers, and gaps annotated
- `new-hire-feedback`: Direct quotes from recent hires about what slowed them down, what was missing, and what helped most

## Process

1. Interview 2-3 recent hires (preferably one fast ramper and one slow ramper). Walk through their first 4 weeks day by day.
2. For each day, ask: What were you supposed to do? What did you actually do? Were you waiting for anything? What did you wish someone had told you?
3. Map the process as a timeline. Mark every waiting period in a different color. Waiting periods are the low-hanging fruit.
4. Note the gaps: moments where the new hire had no task, no direction, and no one to ask. These gaps are where onboarding fails.
