# Geological History Reconstructor

Reconstruct the geological history of the study area from the map, stratigraphy, geochemistry, and structural data.

## Inputs

- `geological-map`: Mapped formations and structures
- `geochemical-protocol`: Analytical results and interpreted processes
- `thin-section-descriptions`: Petrographic evidence for formation conditions

## Outputs

- `geological-history`: Time-ordered sequence of events (deposition, intrusion, deformation, erosion)
- `tectonic-interpretation`: Regional tectonic setting and its influence on local geology
- `open-questions`: Remaining uncertainties and further work needed

## Process

1. Apply superposition, cross-cutting, and inclusion principles to order events
2. Interpret depositional environments from sedimentary structures and fossils
3. Infer metamorphic and igneous conditions from mineral assemblages and geochemistry
4. Place the sequence in a regional tectonic framework
5. Identify remaining gaps and propose follow-up investigations
