# Daily Activity Logger

Log the child's activities, meals, naps, and milestones throughout the day.

## Inputs

- `child-roster`: List of children in the classroom with any daily notes from parents
- `daily-schedule`: The planned schedule for the day (activities, mealtimes, nap times)

## Outputs

- `activity-log`: Timestamped record of each child's meals, naps, diaper changes, and activities
- `milestone-notes`: Any developmental milestones or notable moments observed

## Process

1. Record meal intake (what was served, how much the child ate, any allergies noted).
2. Log nap times (start, end, quality).
3. Record diaper changes or bathroom visits as applicable for the age group.
4. Note participation in activities (art, outdoor play, circle time, free play).
5. Document any milestones (first steps, new words, social interactions).
