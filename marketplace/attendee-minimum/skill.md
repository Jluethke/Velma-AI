# Attendee Minimum

Invite only the people who MUST be in the room for the decision. Everyone else gets the outcome in writing.

## Inputs

- `decision-statement`: The decision to make
- `decision-options`: The options

## Outputs

- `required-attendees`: The minimum set: the decision maker, the people with critical input, and no one else
- `informed-list`: People who need to know the outcome but do not need to be in the room

## Process

1. The decision maker must be in the room. If the decision maker is not available, cancel the meeting. A meeting without the decision maker produces discussion, not decisions.
2. Add people with critical input: those who have information the decision maker needs and cannot get from a document. Typically 2-4 people.
3. Everyone else goes on the informed list. They receive the decision and rationale after the meeting. This is not exclusion; it is respecting their time.
4. The maximum attendee count for a decision meeting is 7. Above 7, group dynamics prevent efficient discussion.
