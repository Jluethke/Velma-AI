# Consensusconviction Test Downside

Test whether the downside of the decision is limited or could be severe.

## Inputs

- `buyin-score`: How much buy-in matters for this decision.
- `buyin-reasons`: Why buy-in does or does not matter here.

## Outputs

- `downside-score`: How limited the downside is.
- `downside-notes`: The worst realistic outcome of the decision.

## Process

1. Describe the worst realistic outcome of the decision.
2. Judge whether that downside is limited and recoverable.
3. Score how limited the downside is on a shared scale.
