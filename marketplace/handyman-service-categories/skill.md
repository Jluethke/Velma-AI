# Handyman Service Categories

Define the service categories and pricing model for a handyman business.

## Inputs

- `skill-set`: Trade skills available (plumbing, electrical, carpentry, painting, general)
- `service-territory`: Geographic coverage area and travel charge policy

## Outputs

- `service-categories`: Categorized services with pricing model (hourly, flat rate, T&M) per category
- `minimum-charge-policy`: Minimum charge, trip charge, and estimate fee policy

## Process

1. Group services by trade category (plumbing, electrical, carpentry, drywall, painting, assembly, general).
2. Set the pricing model per category (flat rate for common tasks, T&M for unknowns).
3. Define the minimum service charge and trip fee.
4. List services that require permits or licensed subcontractors.
