# Event Followup Reporter

Send follow-up communications and produce the event report.

## Inputs

- `donation-collections`: On-site donations
- `ticket-sales-report`: Ticket and sponsorship revenue
- `event-budget`: Original budget

## Outputs

- `event-fundraiser-report`: Final revenue, expenses, and net proceeds
- `thank-you-list`: Donors and sponsors to acknowledge

## Process

1. Send thank-you emails within 48 hours
2. Mail tax receipts for donations and auction purchases
3. Calculate net proceeds (revenue minus expenses)
4. Produce the event report with lessons for next year
