# Decisionjournal Separate Quality From Luck

Grade the quality of the decision apart from the outcome, since a good decision can have a bad result.

## Inputs

- `actual-outcome`: What actually happened by the review date.
- `outcome-gap`: How the actual outcome differs from the expected one.

## Outputs

- `quality-grade`: A grade for the decision given what you knew at the time.
- `process-lessons`: The lessons that should change how you decide next time.

## Process

1. Judge whether the decision was sound given what you knew at the time.
2. Separate a bad result caused by luck from one caused by a weak process.
3. Write the lessons that should change how you decide next time.
