# Failure Guarantee Brainstorm

Brainstorm every way you could guarantee failure. Be specific and creative.

## Inputs

- `inverted-statement`: The inverted question: how to guarantee failure
- `context`: The situation and constraints

## Outputs

- `failure-methods`: A complete list of specific actions that would guarantee failure (15-25 items)
- `categorized-failures`: The failure methods grouped by category (process failures, people failures, resource failures, timing failures, communication failures)

## Process

1. Generate at least 15 specific ways to guarantee failure. Examples for 'How to guarantee customers leave': ignore their support tickets, change the price without warning, break features they depend on, make the product harder to use with each update, never respond to feedback.
2. Be specific, not vague. 'Provide bad service' is too vague. 'Take 5 days to respond to support tickets and then send a generic non-answer' is specific.
3. Include the subtle failures, not just the obvious ones. Obvious: 'delete their data.' Subtle: 'make the cancellation process so easy that any frustrated moment leads to churn.' The subtle ones are more useful because they might already be happening.
4. Group the failures by category to see patterns. If most failure methods are communication failures, that tells you where the real risk is.
