# Tabletop Risk Scenario Selector

Select exercise scenarios based on the organization's risk profile, industry threats, and past incidents.

## Inputs

- `risk-register`: Organization's risk register with likelihood and impact ratings
- `threat-intelligence`: Current threat landscape for the industry
- `past-incident-history`: Previous incidents and near-misses

## Outputs

- `scenario-shortlist`: Ranked list of scenarios by relevance and training value
- `scenario-rationale`: Why each scenario was selected and what it tests

## Process

1. Review the risk register for top risks that have not been exercised
2. Check threat intelligence for emerging threats relevant to the industry
3. Identify gaps in the team's experience based on past incidents
4. Select 4 to 6 scenarios for the annual series
