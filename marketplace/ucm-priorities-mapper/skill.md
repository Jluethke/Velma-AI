# Ucm Priorities Mapper

Map what your manager actually cares about and the pressures they are under. Upward communication fails most often when a message is framed around what you care about instead of what they do. Their priorities, their metrics, and the people they answer to are the frame your message has to fit.

## Inputs

- `manager-context`: Your manager's goals, the metrics they are measured on, and who they report to
- `topic`: The thing you need to communicate upward

## Outputs

- `manager-priorities`: What your manager cares about most right now, ranked
- `decision-pressures`: The pressures and constraints shaping how they will receive your message

## Process

1. List what your manager is measured on and what they talk about most. Those are their priorities, whatever the org chart says.
2. Identify who they answer to and what those people expect. Your manager's boss is often the real audience behind your message.
3. Note current pressures: a deadline, a budget review, a problem they are already fighting. Your message lands into that context.
4. Rank the priorities so you know which one to connect your topic to. The top priority is the frame.
