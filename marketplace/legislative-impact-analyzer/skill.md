# Legislative Impact Analyzer

Analyze the potential impact of advancing legislation on the organization.

## Inputs

- `status-updates`: Current bill status and text
- `amendment-tracker`: Adopted amendments affecting the organization

## Outputs

- `impact-assessments`: Analysis of how each advancing bill would affect the organization if enacted
- `legislative-tracking-report`: Weekly or monthly report of all tracked legislation with status, impact, and recommended actions

## Process

1. Analyze the current text of each advancing bill for operational and financial impact.
2. Identify compliance requirements, timelines, and costs if enacted.
3. Recommend organizational actions (comment, testimony, operational preparation).
4. Compile the tracking report with status, analysis, and recommendations.
