# Feedback To Planning Bridge

Feed the top-ranked feedback themes into product planning.

## Inputs

- `theme-ranking`: Themes ranked by frequency and impact

## Outputs

- `planning-inputs`: Feedback themes formatted for the product planning process with evidence
- `theme-to-roadmap-map`: Mapping of feedback themes to roadmap items or backlog entries

## Process

1. Select the top themes that meet the planning threshold (minimum frequency or impact score).
2. Package each theme with representative quotes, customer count, and revenue context.
3. Submit the themes to the product planning intake process.
4. Map each theme to a roadmap item or backlog entry for tracking.
