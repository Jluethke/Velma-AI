# Video Shoot Planner

Plan the shoot including locations, talent, equipment, and schedule.

## Inputs

- `script-document`: Completed script with scene descriptions
- `shot-list`: List of required shots

## Outputs

- `production-schedule`: Day-by-day shoot schedule with call times
- `equipment-list`: Camera, lighting, audio, and grip gear needed

## Process

1. Scout and confirm locations for each scene
2. Cast talent and confirm availability
3. Build the equipment list based on shot requirements
4. Create the production schedule with call times and scene order
