# Shipment Data Collector

Collect shipment data including origin, destination, weight, and dimensions.

## Inputs

- `shipment-list`: List of shipments with origin, destination, weight, and dimensions
- `delivery-deadlines`: Required delivery dates for each shipment

## Outputs

- `shipment-profiles`: Structured profiles for each shipment
- `constraint-summary`: Summary of time, weight, and routing constraints

## Process

1. Collect origin and destination for each shipment
2. Record weight, dimensions, and handling requirements
3. Document delivery deadlines and special conditions
4. Produce the structured shipment profiles
