# Task Energy Matching

Match your tasks to your energy level: deep work during peaks, routine work during troughs, creative work during the secondary peak.

## Inputs

- `energy-map`: Your energy cycle
- `typical-day`: Your current schedule

## Outputs

- `optimized-schedule`: Your schedule reorganized by energy: deep work at peak, meetings and admin at trough, creative work at secondary peak
- `protected-blocks`: Time blocks that are protected from meetings and interruptions because they are your peak energy hours

## Process

1. Deep work (analysis, writing, coding, strategy) goes in your peak energy window. For most people, this is 9-11am. Never schedule a meeting during your peak.
2. Routine work (email, admin, scheduling, reporting) goes in your energy trough. For most people, this is 1-3pm. These tasks require less cognitive energy and are resilient to low focus.
3. Creative work (brainstorming, problem-solving, ideation) goes in the secondary peak. Some research suggests that slightly lower inhibition (moderate fatigue) actually helps creativity.
4. Protect the peak: block it on your calendar. No meetings, no email, no Slack. If you give away your peak hours to meetings, you are doing your hardest work during your worst hours.
