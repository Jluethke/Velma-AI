# Legal Source Searcher

Search primary and secondary legal sources for authorities relevant to the question.

## Inputs

- `research-question`: The framed legal question
- `research-scope`: Jurisdiction and area of law

## Outputs

- `source-collection`: Collection of relevant statutes, cases, regulations, and secondary sources
- `search-log`: Log of databases searched, search terms used, and results

## Process

1. Search for relevant statutes and regulations in the identified jurisdiction.
2. Search for case law interpreting those statutes and addressing the legal question.
3. Search secondary sources (treatises, law reviews, practice guides) for analysis.
4. Log all searches and organize the results by relevance.
