# Infection Risk Identifier

Identify the infection risk including pathogen and transmission route.

## Inputs

- `pathogen-info`: Known or suspected organism
- `clinical-setting`: Unit type and patient population

## Outputs

- `risk-profile`: Pathogen characteristics and transmission routes
- `affected-scope`: Areas and personnel at risk

## Process

1. Identify the pathogen and its known transmission routes.
2. Determine the clinical areas and populations at risk.
3. Assess the severity and outbreak potential.
