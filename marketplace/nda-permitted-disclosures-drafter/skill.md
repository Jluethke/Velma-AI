# Nda Permitted Disclosures Drafter

Draft the permitted disclosure and compelled disclosure provisions.

## Inputs

- `ci-definition`: Confidential information definition
- `party-roles`: Party role designations

## Outputs

- `permitted-disclosures`: Permitted disclosure provisions
- `compelled-disclosure-process`: Process for legally compelled disclosures

## Process

1. Define who may receive confidential information (employees, advisors, affiliates)
2. Draft the compelled disclosure safe harbor (subpoena, court order)
3. Specify notice requirements before compelled disclosure
