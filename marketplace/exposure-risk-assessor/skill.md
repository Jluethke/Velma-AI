# Exposure Risk Assessor

Assess flood, catastrophe, and liability exposure.

## Inputs

- `coverage-inventory`: Current coverage by property
- `coverage-gaps`: Identified gaps

## Outputs

- `risk-assessment`: Risk exposure by property and peril type
- `coverage-recommendations`: Recommended changes to coverage

## Process

1. Assess each property for flood zone, earthquake, and windstorm exposure.
2. Review liability limits against the number of units and tenant activity.
3. Recommend coverage changes to close identified gaps.
