# Add Impact Tracker

After hiring for culture add, track whether the new hire's different perspective actually produced better outcomes. The proof is in the decisions: did the team make a decision differently because of the new person's input? Did the team catch a blind spot it would have missed? Track these instances for the first 12 months.

## Inputs

- `interviewer-guide`: The culture add framework used in hiring
- `bias-checklist`: The bias check used by interviewers

## Outputs

- `add-impact-log`: Specific instances where the culture-add hire's different perspective changed a team decision or caught a blind spot in the first 12 months
- `method-assessment`: Whether the culture add criteria predicted the right kind of impact, and adjustments for the next hire

## Process

1. At monthly team retrospectives, ask: was there a decision this month where someone's different perspective changed the outcome?
2. Track each instance: what was the decision, what would the team have done without the input, and what did the different perspective add?
3. At 12 months, review the log. If the culture-add hire contributed multiple instances of changed decisions, the criteria were good. If not, either the criteria missed the mark or the team environment did not support the different perspective.
4. Adjust the criteria for the next hire based on what you learned.
