# Jtbd Messaging Translator

Translate the job insights into messaging. Job-based messaging speaks to the situation and the desired outcome, not to features.

## Inputs

- `job-statements`: The jobs your product is hired for
- `real-competitor-map`: Who you actually compete with for each job
- `competitive-advantage-by-job`: Where you win and lose

## Outputs

- `job-based-messaging`: Messaging for each job: headline (the situation), body (how you serve the job better than the alternatives), and proof (evidence from the interviews)
- `anxiety-reducers`: Specific messaging elements designed to reduce the anxieties uncovered in the interviews

## Process

1. For each job, write a headline that describes the situation, not the product: 'When [situation happens], [product] gets you to [desired outcome].'
2. Write the body from the forces: address the push (validate the frustration), name the pull (state the benefit in the customer's own words from the interviews), and reduce the anxiety (address the specific worries they expressed).
3. Use the interview language directly. The words customers use to describe the job are the words other potential customers will recognize and respond to.
4. Do not mention features unless they directly serve the job. A feature that does not connect to the job is irrelevant in this context.
