# Milestone Execution Tracker

Execute the project by tracking progress against milestones and managing deliverables.

## Inputs

- `onboarding-checklist`: Completed onboarding steps
- `project-workspace`: Project workspace details

## Outputs

- `milestone-status`: Status of each milestone (not started, in progress, complete, blocked)
- `deliverables-log`: Record of deliverables submitted and client feedback received

## Process

1. Break the project into milestones with clear deliverables and due dates.
2. Track progress against each milestone, updating the client at the agreed cadence.
3. Submit deliverables for client review at each milestone and document their feedback.
4. Handle revision requests within the scope of the contract.
5. Flag any risks to the timeline or budget early and propose mitigation.
