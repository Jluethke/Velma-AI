# Relationship Satisfaction Audit

Assess satisfaction across key relationship dimensions: communication, intimacy, finances, household, and future plans.

## Inputs

- `relationship_duration`: How long together
- `living_situation`: Living together, apart, etc.
- `recent_changes`: Any recent life changes

## Outputs

- `satisfaction_scores`: Scores per dimension
- `bright_spots`: Areas of strength
- `pain_points`: Areas needing attention

## Process

1. Rate satisfaction in each dimension 1-10
2. Identify patterns in high and low scores
3. Note recent changes that affected scores
4. List bright spots to reinforce
