# Insider Log Preservation

Preserve all relevant logs and access records for the investigation subject.

## Inputs

- `investigation-scope`: Scope and subject of the investigation
- `subject-role-info`: Systems and data the subject has access to

## Outputs

- `preserved-logs`: Preserved access logs, email logs, and system activity for the subject
- `preservation-receipt`: Chain-of-custody documentation for preserved data

## Process

1. Place a legal hold on all relevant logs and communications for the subject
2. Collect authentication logs, VPN logs, and badge access records
3. Preserve email and messaging archives for the investigation period
4. Document all preservation actions with timestamps and chain of custody
