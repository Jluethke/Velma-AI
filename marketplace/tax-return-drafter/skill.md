# Tax Return Drafter

Prepare the tax return using the appropriate forms and schedules.

## Inputs

- `categorized-items`: Organized tax items by category
- `deduction-summary`: Deduction method selection

## Outputs

- `draft-return`: Completed draft tax return with all schedules
- `tax-liability-summary`: Summary of tax owed or refund due

## Process

1. Enter income items on the appropriate forms and schedules.
2. Apply the selected deduction method and enter deductions.
3. Calculate credits and apply against tax liability.
4. Compute the final tax liability, payments made, and refund or balance due.
