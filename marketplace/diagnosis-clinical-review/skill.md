# Diagnosis Clinical Review

Review the diagnosis and clinical findings.

## Inputs

- `patient-record`: Current clinical record including labs and imaging
- `diagnosis-codes`: ICD-10 codes for the current diagnoses

## Outputs

- `clinical-summary`: Summary of findings supporting the diagnosis
- `severity-assessment`: Severity classification of the condition

## Process

1. Review lab results, imaging, and exam findings.
2. Confirm the diagnosis against clinical criteria.
3. Classify the severity and stage of the condition.
