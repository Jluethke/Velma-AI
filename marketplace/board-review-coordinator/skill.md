# Board Review Coordinator

Circulate the resolution to the board and coordinate the review and comment period.

## Inputs

- `draft-resolution`: The draft resolution
- `supporting-materials`: Supporting documents

## Outputs

- `board-feedback`: Comments and questions received from board members
- `revised-resolution`: The resolution revised to incorporate board feedback

## Process

1. Distribute the draft resolution and supporting materials to all board members.
2. Allow the review period specified in the bylaws.
3. Collect and consolidate comments and questions.
4. Revise the resolution to address the feedback.
