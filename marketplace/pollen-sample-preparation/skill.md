# Pollen Sample Preparation

Process sediment samples to extract and concentrate pollen grains for microscopy.

## Inputs

- `core-samples`: Sediment subsamples at defined depth intervals from the core
- `preparation-protocol`: Chemical treatment sequence (HCl, HF, acetolysis, heavy liquid) based on sediment type

## Outputs

- `prepared-slides`: Mounted slides with concentrated pollen residue and exotic marker grains
- `preparation-log`: Sample weights, volumes of exotic marker added, and processing notes

## Process

1. Add a known number of exotic marker grains (Lycopodium or Eucalyptus) to each sample for concentration calculations
2. Dissolve carbonates with HCl and silicates with HF as needed
3. Run acetolysis to remove cellulose and darken pollen walls for easier identification
4. Mount the residue on slides in glycerol jelly or silicone oil
