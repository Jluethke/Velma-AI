# Intervention Selector

Select interventions based on clinical guidelines and evidence.

## Inputs

- `clinical-summary`: Summary of clinical findings
- `short-term-goals`: Near-term treatment goals

## Outputs

- `intervention-list`: Selected medications, procedures, therapies, and referrals
- `evidence-citations`: Guideline references supporting each intervention

## Process

1. Review clinical practice guidelines for the condition.
2. Select first-line and alternative interventions.
3. Document the evidence supporting each selection.
