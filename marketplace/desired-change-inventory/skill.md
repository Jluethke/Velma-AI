# Desired Change Inventory

List every behavior change you want to make, without filtering or prioritizing yet.

## Inputs

- `life-areas`: The areas of life you want to improve (health, work, relationships, finance, learning, creativity)
- `frustrations`: Specific things you are frustrated about or wish were different

## Outputs

- `change-list`: Complete list of 10-20 behavior changes you want to make, stated as specific actions
- `importance-ratings`: Each change rated by how much it would improve your life if adopted (1-10)

## Process

1. List every behavior you wish you did more of or less of. Do not filter. Include small changes (drink more water) and large ones (exercise daily). Include habits you have tried and failed to adopt.
2. State each change as a specific action, not an aspiration. 'Exercise 4 times per week' not 'be healthier.'
3. Rate each change by importance: how much would your life improve if this change stuck? Use a 1-10 scale where 10 means it would change everything.
4. Do not prioritize yet. The goal is a complete inventory, not a plan.
