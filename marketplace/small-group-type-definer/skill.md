# Small Group Type Definer

Define the types of small groups, their purposes, and meeting structures.

## Inputs

- `organization-goals`: What the organization wants small groups to accomplish (fellowship, education, support, service)
- `member-demographics`: Age groups, interests, and geographic distribution of the membership

## Outputs

- `group-types`: Defined group categories with purpose, format, duration, and target audience
- `meeting-guidelines`: Recommended meeting frequency, duration, location, and curriculum or agenda

## Process

1. Define the group categories: study groups, support groups, interest or hobby groups, service teams, age-specific groups.
2. For each type, set the purpose, typical size (6 to 12), meeting frequency, and duration (semester, year-round, or open-ended).
3. Create meeting guidelines: recommended agenda structure, facilitation tips, and reporting requirements.
4. Define how groups relate to the larger organization: reporting line, resource access, and event integration.
