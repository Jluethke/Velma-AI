# Policy Publisher

Publish the policy, communicate to employees, and track acknowledgment.

## Inputs

- `approved-policy`: Approved policy
- `policy-metadata`: Policy metadata including audience

## Outputs

- `publication-record`: Record of publication and distribution
- `acknowledgment-tracker`: Tracker of employee acknowledgments

## Process

1. Publish the policy to the central policy repository.
2. Notify affected employees with a summary and link.
3. Track acknowledgments and follow up on non-responses.
4. Archive the prior version.
