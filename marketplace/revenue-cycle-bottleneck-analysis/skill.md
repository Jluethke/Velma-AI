# Revenue Cycle Bottleneck Analysis

Identify the bottlenecks in the revenue cycle that are reducing cash flow or increasing costs.

## Inputs

- `baseline-metrics`: Current revenue cycle metrics
- `benchmark-comparison`: Gap between current performance and benchmarks

## Outputs

- `bottleneck-list`: Ranked list of revenue cycle bottlenecks with estimated financial impact
- `root-causes`: Root causes identified for each bottleneck

## Process

1. Analyze AR aging to identify where claims are stalling (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days).
2. Review denial reasons by category to find the highest-volume and highest-dollar denial types.
3. Examine the front-end processes (registration errors, insurance verification gaps, authorization delays).
4. Rank the bottlenecks by financial impact and fixability.
