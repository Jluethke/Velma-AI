# Schedule Publication

Finalize and publish the shift schedule to all employees.

## Inputs

- `swap-log`: Swap request history
- `schedule-change-record`: Post-publication changes

## Outputs

- `published-schedule`: The finalized, published schedule accessible to all employees
- `shift-scheduling-record`: Complete shift scheduling record for the period

## Process

1. Review the final schedule for completeness (all shifts covered, no rule violations).
2. Publish to the scheduling system and notify all employees of their assigned shifts.
3. Set the deadline for the next round of swap requests.
4. Compile the shift scheduling record: coverage achieved, preference fulfillment rate, overtime hours, and fairness metrics.
