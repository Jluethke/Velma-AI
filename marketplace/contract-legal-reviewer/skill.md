# Contract Legal Reviewer

Route the draft contract for internal legal review and incorporate feedback.

## Inputs

- `draft-contract`: The populated contract draft

## Outputs

- `reviewed-contract`: The contract with legal review comments incorporated
- `review-notes`: Legal reviewer's notes and any risk flags

## Process

1. Send the draft to the assigned legal reviewer.
2. Track the review against the turnaround SLA.
3. Incorporate the reviewer's redlines and comments.
4. Produce the reviewed contract ready for signature.
