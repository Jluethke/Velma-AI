# Hold Acknowledgment Tracker

Track acknowledgments from all custodians and follow up on non-responses.

## Inputs

- `custodian-list`: Custodian list
- `distribution-record`: Distribution record

## Outputs

- `acknowledgment-log`: Log of all acknowledgments received
- `escalation-list`: Custodians requiring follow-up or escalation

## Process

1. Track acknowledgment receipts from each custodian
2. Send reminders to non-responsive custodians
3. Escalate to management for custodians who do not respond
