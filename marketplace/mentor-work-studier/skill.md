# Mentor Work Studier

Study a candidate's public work before making any contact.

## Inputs

- `candidate-mentors`: People a few years ahead of you
- `selection-notes`: Why each candidate fits the path

## Outputs

- `work-study-notes`: Notes on what the candidate did and why
- `open-questions`: Genuine questions their work leaves open

## Process

1. Read, watch, or use the candidate's public work in depth.
2. Note what they did and the choices behind it.
3. Write the genuine questions their work leaves open for you.
4. Try to answer each question yourself first.
