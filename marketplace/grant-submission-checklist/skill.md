# Grant Submission Checklist

Verify all grant components are complete, formatted correctly, and ready for electronic submission.

## Inputs

- `all-grant-sections`: Assembled specific aims, significance, strategy, budget, and biosketches
- `submission-portal`: eRA Commons, Research.gov, or other electronic submission system
- `institutional-requirements`: Internal deadline, routing forms, and institutional signatures needed

## Outputs

- `submission-checklist`: Item-by-item verification of all required components with status
- `formatting-audit`: Font, margin, page limit, and file format verification per funder rules
- `submission-confirmation`: Confirmation number and timestamp from the submission portal

## Process

1. Verify all required sections are present against the funding opportunity announcement checklist
2. Check formatting (font, margins, page limits) for every attachment
3. Confirm all personnel have active accounts in the submission portal
4. Route through institutional sponsored programs office by the internal deadline
5. Submit and save the confirmation receipt with the application tracking number
