# Followup Roi Review

Review the return the follow-up system is producing, because seeing that consistent follow-up actually strengthens relationships and opens opportunities is what sustains the habit, and the review also reveals where follow-up is being spent on low-value interactions and where it is missing high-value ones.

## Inputs

- `completion-tracker`: The record of follow-ups due, done, and slipped
- `overdue-escalation-rules`: How overdue follow-ups are handled

## Outputs

- `roi-review-method`: A periodic review of what the follow-up system is producing: relationships strengthened, opportunities opened, reliability demonstrated
- `follow-through-metrics`: The few numbers worth watching: follow-up completion rate, average time from capture to completion, and share of meaningful interactions that generated a follow-up

## Process

1. Review periodically what the follow-ups produced. Note the relationships that deepened, the opportunities that came from a reliable follow-up, and the trust built by consistently doing what you said you would. Seeing the return sustains the habit.
2. Track completion rate as the core health metric. A high rate means the system is real, a falling rate means capture or scheduling is breaking down and the habit is slipping.
3. Watch the time from capture to completion. Follow-ups that routinely complete long after their date point to dates set unrealistically or a tracker that is not surfacing them, both fixable.
4. Check where follow-up is being allocated. If you are diligently following up on low-value interactions while high-value ones generate no follow-up, the fix is in what you choose to capture, not in working the system harder.
