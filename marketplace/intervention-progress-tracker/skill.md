# Intervention Progress Tracker

Track student progress after intervention to measure improvement.

## Inputs

- `intervention-log`: Record of interventions deployed
- `student-data`: Enrollment, attendance, grade, and engagement data

## Outputs

- `progress-report`: Progress metrics per student post-intervention
- `escalation-list`: Students not improving who need additional support

## Process

1. Monitor grades, attendance, and engagement at 2-week and 4-week marks
2. Compare post-intervention metrics to pre-intervention baseline
3. Flag students showing no improvement for escalation
4. Produce the progress report
