# Work Completed Describer

Describe the work completed on the job site for the day by area.

## Inputs

- `crew-log`: Crew counts and hours by trade
- `equipment-log`: Equipment on site with status

## Outputs

- `work-description`: Narrative of work completed by area
- `progress-notes`: Percent complete updates for active activities

## Process

1. Describe the work completed in each area of the site.
2. Reference the trades and equipment used for each activity.
3. Note any milestones reached or inspections passed.
4. Update the percent complete for each active activity.
