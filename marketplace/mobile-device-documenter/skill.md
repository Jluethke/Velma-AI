# Mobile Device Documenter

Document the device state, chain of custody, and initial observations.

## Inputs

- `device-info`: Device make, model, OS, and physical condition
- `case-context`: The investigation context and what evidence is sought

## Outputs

- `device-documentation`: Photographs, serial numbers, and state observations
- `custody-record`: Chain of custody form with initial custodian and timestamp

## Process

1. Photograph the device from all angles showing condition and screen state
2. Record make, model, IMEI/MEID, serial number, and OS version
3. Note the power state, network connections, and any displayed notifications
4. Complete the chain of custody form
