# Compensating Control Identifier

Identify compensating controls to reduce the risk of the exception.

## Inputs

- `risk-assessment`: Risks to compensate for
- `documented-request`: Exception details for context

## Outputs

- `compensating-controls`: Controls to implement during the exception
- `residual-risk-estimate`: Expected risk after compensating controls

## Process

1. Identify controls that partially address the same risk
2. Assess the risk reduction from each compensating control
3. Select the most effective and feasible controls
4. Estimate the residual risk with compensating controls in place
