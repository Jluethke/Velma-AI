# Retainer Terms Definition

Define the retainer terms including hours, scope, rollover rules, and reporting.

## Inputs

- `client-needs`: What the client needs on an ongoing basis
- `proposed-terms`: Proposed hours per month, rate, and scope

## Outputs

- `retainer-agreement`: The retainer terms document covering hours, rate, scope, rollover, and reporting
- `tracking-setup`: How hours will be tracked and reported

## Process

1. Define the monthly hour allocation and billing rate.
2. Specify what is in scope and what requires a separate engagement.
3. Set the rollover rules (do unused hours carry forward, and for how long).
4. Define the reporting cadence and format (weekly summary, monthly utilization report).
5. Specify the renewal terms (auto-renew, notice period, rate adjustment window).
