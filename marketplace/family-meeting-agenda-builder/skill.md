# Family Meeting Agenda Builder

Build a family meeting agenda with input from all members, prioritized topics, and time limits.

## Inputs

- `family_members`: Who will attend
- `topics_submitted`: Topics each member wants to discuss
- `meeting_duration`: Available time for the meeting

## Outputs

- `agenda`: Ordered agenda with time allocations
- `ground_rules`: Meeting ground rules
- `facilitator_notes`: Notes for whoever runs the meeting

## Process

1. Collect topic submissions from all members
2. Categorize by urgency and importance
3. Allocate time per topic
4. Set ground rules for respectful discussion
5. Designate facilitator and note-taker roles
