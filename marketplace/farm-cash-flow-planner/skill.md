# Farm Cash Flow Planner

Plan monthly cash flow for the farm operation.

## Inputs

- `revenue-forecast`: Projected income and timing
- `cost-breakdown`: Expense amounts and timing

## Outputs

- `cash-flow-schedule`: Monthly inflows and outflows
- `financing-needs`: Months where operating loans are needed

## Process

1. Map revenue receipts to the months they arrive.
2. Map expenses to the months they are due.
3. Calculate the cumulative cash position by month.
4. Identify months requiring operating loan draws.
