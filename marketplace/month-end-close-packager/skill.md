# Month End Close Packager

Compile the month-end close package with all supporting schedules and sign-offs.

## Inputs

- `income-statement`: Monthly income statement
- `balance-sheet`: Monthly balance sheet
- `reconciliation-report`: All reconciliation reports

## Outputs

- `close-package`: Complete month-end close package
- `close-checklist`: Completed close checklist with sign-offs

## Process

1. Assemble the financial statements, reconciliations, and supporting schedules.
2. Complete the close checklist confirming all steps were performed.
3. Obtain review sign-off from the controller or accounting manager.
4. Archive the close package and lock the period in the accounting system.
