# Outcome Goal Definition

Define the outcome you want to move. State it as a specific, measurable number with a time horizon. 'Increase revenue' is not a goal. 'Revenue of $500K by Q4' is. The specificity of the goal determines the quality of every indicator you derive from it.

## Inputs

- `goal-description`: The outcome you want to achieve, in your own words
- `current-baseline`: Where the metric stands today, with the measurement method

## Outputs

- `defined-outcome`: The goal restated as a specific number with a deadline
- `lagging-metric`: The metric that directly measures the outcome (this is, by definition, a lagging indicator)

## Process

1. Take the goal description and extract the noun (what changes), the direction (up or down), the magnitude (how much), and the deadline (by when).
2. If any of those four components is missing, derive it from the baseline and the context. A goal without a magnitude is a wish.
3. Name the lagging metric: the number you will check at the deadline to know whether you succeeded. This metric moves last, not first.
