# Packaging Requirements Gathering

Gather the product specifications and regulatory requirements for the packaging.

## Inputs

- `product-info`: Product name, dimensions, weight, and materials
- `regulatory-context`: Industry regulations and labeling requirements (FDA, FTC, local laws)

## Outputs

- `packaging-specs`: Complete specifications (dimensions, materials, print area, die line)
- `mandatory-elements`: Required text, symbols, and disclosures for the packaging

## Process

1. Document the product dimensions, weight, and packaging material.
2. Identify the print area, die line, and fold/glue zones.
3. List all mandatory labeling elements (ingredients, warnings, barcodes, recycling symbols).
4. Produce the packaging specs and mandatory elements list.
