# Apparatus Design Spec

Design the measurement apparatus: select sensors, define geometry, specify materials, and draw the schematic with critical dimensions.

## Inputs

- `measurable-quantities`: What needs to be measured and precision required
- `phenomenon-scope`: Expected magnitudes and environmental conditions

## Outputs

- `apparatus-schematic`: Labeled diagram with sensor placement, geometry, and materials
- `sensor-specifications`: Each sensor's range, resolution, response time, and accuracy
- `critical-dimensions`: Tolerances on distances, alignments, and angles that affect the measurement

## Process

1. Select sensors that cover the required measurement range and precision
2. Define the physical layout and mounting geometry
3. Specify materials considering interference, thermal expansion, and vibration
4. Draw the schematic with critical dimensions and tolerances
