# Design Feedback Resolution

Resolve conflicting feedback and prioritize the changes.

## Inputs

- `raw-feedback`: All collected feedback
- `conflict-flags`: Items where reviewers disagree

## Outputs

- `resolved-feedback`: The final list of changes to make, with priority and rationale
- `deferred-items`: Feedback items deferred to a future iteration with reasons

## Process

1. Walk through each conflict and make a decision (with the design lead or product owner).
2. Prioritize the remaining feedback by impact.
3. Identify items that can be deferred without blocking approval.
4. Produce the resolved feedback list and deferred items.
