# Career And Lifestyle Alignment

Align on career ambitions, work-life balance, location flexibility, and lifestyle expectations.

## Inputs

- `career_goals`: Each person's career aspirations
- `location_preferences`: Where each wants to live
- `lifestyle_expectations`: Social life, travel, daily routine preferences

## Outputs

- `career_support_plan`: How to support each other's careers
- `location_decision`: Agreed approach to where to live
- `lifestyle_compromises`: Agreed compromises on lifestyle differences

## Process

1. Share career ambitions and timelines
2. Discuss willingness to relocate
3. Align on work-life balance expectations
4. Address lifestyle differences honestly
5. Plan for supporting career changes
