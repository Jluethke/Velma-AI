# Filing Status Tracker

Track the filing status and respond to any follow-up requests.

## Inputs

- `submission-confirmation`: Submission confirmation
- `deadline-schedule`: Deadline schedule

## Outputs

- `status-log`: Running log of filing status and communications
- `filing-record`: Complete filing record for archives

## Process

1. Monitor the filing status through the regulatory portal
2. Respond to any deficiency notices or information requests
3. Compile the complete filing record once the process concludes
