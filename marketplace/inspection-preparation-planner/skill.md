# Inspection Preparation Planner

Prepare for a licensing inspection by verifying all records and facility standards.

## Inputs

- `facility-audit-results`: Results of the most recent internal facility audit
- `corrective-actions`: Outstanding corrective actions

## Outputs

- `inspection-readiness`: Confirmation that all records, postings, and facility items are ready for inspection
- `compliance-record`: Complete compliance file with all documentation organized for the inspector

## Process

1. Verify all corrective actions from the internal audit are completed.
2. Organize the compliance binder (staff files, child files, fire drill logs, injury reports, training records).
3. Confirm all required postings are current and visible.
4. Brief staff on the inspection process and their responsibilities.
5. Conduct a final walkthrough the day before the expected inspection window.
