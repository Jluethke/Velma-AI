# First Draft Dump

Write the first draft without editing. Get everything out. The first draft is for the writer. Every subsequent draft is for the reader.

## Inputs

- `reader-action`: What the reader should do
- `reader-questions`: The questions the reader needs answered

## Outputs

- `raw-draft`: The complete first draft, unedited
- `word-count`: The word count of the first draft, which will be the high-water mark

## Process

1. Write everything you know about the topic. Do not stop to edit, restructure, or polish.
2. Include context, background, reasoning, evidence, caveats, and the ask. It will be too long. That is correct for a first draft.
3. Record the word count. Your final draft should be 30-50% of this number.
