# Podcast Distributor

Distribute the episode to podcast platforms and promote.

## Inputs

- `mixed-episode`: Final mixed episode audio file
- `show-notes`: Episode description and metadata

## Outputs

- `distribution-confirmation`: Upload confirmations from each platform
- `promotion-posts`: Social media posts promoting the episode

## Process

1. Upload the episode to the hosting platform with metadata
2. Verify the episode appears on all distribution channels
3. Create social media posts with audiograms or quotes
4. Schedule the promotion posts
