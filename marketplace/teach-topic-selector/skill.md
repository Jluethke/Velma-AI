# Teach Topic Selector

Choose what to teach and who the audience is so the teaching has a real target.

## Inputs

- `learning-topic`: The topic you are currently learning
- `audience-context`: Who you could teach and what they know

## Outputs

- `teach-topic`: The topic framed for teaching
- `audience-profile`: The audience and their starting knowledge

## Process

1. Pick a topic you are currently learning, not one you have mastered.
2. Define a specific audience and what they already know.
3. State what the audience should be able to do after the lesson.
4. Set the depth the audience actually needs.
