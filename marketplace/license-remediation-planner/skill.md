# License Remediation Planner

Plan remediation for all non-compliant usage.

## Inputs

- `noncompliance-list`: Non-compliance findings
- `exposure-estimate`: Financial exposure

## Outputs

- `remediation-plan`: Remediation actions with timelines and owners
- `budget-estimate`: Estimated cost of remediation

## Process

1. Prioritize remediations by exposure and audit risk
2. Define actions for each finding (purchase, uninstall, replace, negotiate)
3. Assign owners and deadlines for each remediation action
