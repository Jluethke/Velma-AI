# Qi Scale Adjuster

Scale the change or adjust based on results.

## Inputs

- `impact-analysis`: Results of the change
- `lessons-learned`: What worked and what did not

## Outputs

- `scale-decision`: Adopt, adapt, or abandon the change
- `qi-project-report`: Complete QI project report with recommendations

## Process

1. Decide: adopt (scale up), adapt (modify and retest), or abandon.
2. If scaling, plan the broader rollout.
3. Write the final QI report with findings and sustainability plan.
