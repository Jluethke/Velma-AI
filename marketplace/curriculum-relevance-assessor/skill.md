# Curriculum Relevance Assessor

Assess curriculum relevance and rigor against industry needs and disciplinary standards.

## Inputs

- `feedback-summary`: Aggregated stakeholder feedback
- `peer-comparison`: Comparison against peer programs

## Outputs

- `relevance-assessment`: Assessment of curriculum currency and rigor
- `update-recommendations`: Specific curriculum updates recommended

## Process

1. Map current curriculum to industry competency requirements
2. Identify outdated or missing topics
3. Assess rigor and depth against disciplinary expectations
4. Produce specific update recommendations
