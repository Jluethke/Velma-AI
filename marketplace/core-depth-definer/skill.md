# Core Depth Definer

Define the vertical of the T: the one area you will take to real depth.

## Inputs

- `career-context`: Your field and where you want to stand in it
- `strength-signals`: Signals of where you already perform well

## Outputs

- `core-area`: The one area worth most of your learning time
- `depth-target`: What depth looks like for that area

## Process

1. Choose the one area worth most of your learning time.
2. Define what depth looks like, such as top-decile capability.
3. Write the signals that would show you have reached it.
4. Confirm the area is durable enough to invest years in.
