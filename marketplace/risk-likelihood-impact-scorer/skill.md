# Risk Likelihood Impact Scorer

Assess likelihood and impact for each identified risk and calculate risk scores.

## Inputs

- `risk-register-draft`: List of identified risks
- `risk-sources`: Sources and evidence for each risk

## Outputs

- `scored-risk-register`: Risk register with likelihood, impact, and risk scores
- `risk-heat-map`: Visual heat map plotting risks by likelihood and impact

## Process

1. Define the likelihood and impact scales (1-5 or qualitative).
2. Score each risk for likelihood and impact using available data and expert judgment.
3. Calculate the composite risk score for each entry.
4. Plot risks on the heat map.
