# Standard Terms Comparator

Compare the contract terms against your organization's standard positions.

## Inputs

- `key-terms-summary`: Extracted key terms
- `risk-flags`: Flagged risk clauses
- `missing-protections`: Missing standard clauses

## Outputs

- `deviation-list`: List of deviations from standard terms
- `negotiation-priorities`: Ranked list of items to negotiate

## Process

1. Compare each key term against your standard position
2. Classify deviations as acceptable, negotiable, or non-starter
3. Rank negotiation priorities by business impact
