# Phi System Inventory

Inventory all systems that handle protected health information.

## Inputs

- `organization-scope`: Departments and locations to assess
- `system-list`: Known IT systems and applications

## Outputs

- `phi-systems`: Systems that create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI
- `data-flow-map`: How PHI moves between systems and locations

## Process

1. Catalog all systems that store or process PHI.
2. Map the flow of PHI between systems, locations, and third parties.
3. Identify the types of PHI in each system.
