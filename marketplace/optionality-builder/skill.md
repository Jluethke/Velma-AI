# Optionality Builder

Increase optionality so that disorder creates opportunities, not just damage. Options are cheap when you don't need them and valuable when you do.

## Inputs

- `current-situation`: Your personal or business situation
- `fragility-fixes`: The redundancies you are building

## Outputs

- `optionality-inventory`: A list of options you currently hold (skills, relationships, assets, agreements) and their value in each shock scenario
- `optionality-build-plan`: New options to acquire, with the cost to acquire and the scenarios where each pays off

## Process

1. Inventory your current options: skills that transfer to other industries, relationships that could become income sources, assets that can be liquidated or repurposed, contractual rights (termination clauses, insurance riders, flexible leases).
2. For each option, score its value across the four shock categories. An option that pays off in multiple shock categories is more valuable than one that covers only one.
3. Identify new options to acquire: a certification in a second field (income diversification), a relationship with a potential acquirer (market shock exit), a portable skill (infrastructure shock adaptation).
4. The cost of an option is its acquisition cost. The value is the damage it prevents or the opportunity it creates if a shock hits. Acquire options where the ratio is favorable.
