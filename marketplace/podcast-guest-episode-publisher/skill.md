# Podcast Guest Episode Publisher

Edit, publish, and promote the guest episode with co-promotion assets.

## Inputs

- `raw-recording`: The raw recording file
- `interview-notes`: Timestamps and highlights from the interview

## Outputs

- `published-episode`: The published episode URL and show notes
- `guest-pipeline-record`: Complete record of the guest booking, interview, and episode

## Process

1. Edit the recording (trim, level audio, add intro/outro, remove filler).
2. Write show notes with timestamps, guest bio, and links.
3. Create promotional assets (audiogram clips, quote graphics) for the guest to share.
4. Publish the episode and notify the guest with the promotion assets.
