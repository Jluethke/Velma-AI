# Volunteer Recruiter

Recruit volunteers through targeted outreach channels.

## Inputs

- `volunteer-needs`: Roles, skills, and time commitments needed
- `organization-profile`: Description of the organization and its mission

## Outputs

- `recruitment-plan`: Channels and messaging for volunteer recruitment
- `applicant-pool`: List of volunteer applicants

## Process

1. Define each volunteer role with required skills and time commitment
2. Select recruitment channels (website, social media, volunteer match sites, community partners)
3. Create recruitment messaging that communicates the impact of volunteering
4. Track applications and inquiries
