# Service Categorizer

Categorize services by business function.

## Inputs

- `service-inventory`: List of all IT services

## Outputs

- `service-categories`: Services grouped by business function
- `category-hierarchy`: The category tree structure

## Process

1. Group services by the business function they support (communication, productivity, security, infrastructure).
2. Define the hierarchy of categories and subcategories.
3. Assign each service to exactly one primary category.
4. Review with business stakeholders for alignment.
