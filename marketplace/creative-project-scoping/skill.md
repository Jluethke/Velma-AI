# Creative Project Scoping

Scope the creative project from the approved brief, estimate the effort, and assign the team.

## Inputs

- `approved-brief`: The approved creative brief
- `team-availability`: Available team members and their current workload

## Outputs

- `project-plan`: Timeline, milestones, and deliverable schedule
- `team-assignments`: Who is responsible for each deliverable and phase

## Process

1. Break the brief into discrete deliverables and estimate the effort for each.
2. Assign team members based on skills and availability.
3. Build the project plan with milestones and review gates.
4. Produce the project plan and team assignments.
