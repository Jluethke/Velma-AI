# Move Planner

Plan and coordinate a household move with timeline, checklists, and vendor coordination.

## Inputs

- `current_address`: Current residence address
- `destination`: Target move destination
- `move_date`: Target move date
- `household_size`: Number of people and approximate volume

## Outputs

- `move_timeline`: Week-by-week countdown with milestones
- `vendor_list`: Movers, cleaners, utilities to contact
- `packing_checklist`: Room-by-room packing plan
- `budget_estimate`: Estimated costs broken down by category

## Process

1. Inventory the household and estimate volume
2. Research and compare moving vendors for the route
3. Build a countdown timeline from 8 weeks out
4. Generate room-by-room packing checklists
5. Compile utility transfer and address change lists
6. Estimate total budget with contingency
