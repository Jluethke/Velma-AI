# Drop Off Investigator

Investigate why people drop off at the biggest-drop stage. Do not guess. Ask, observe, or test.

## Inputs

- `biggest-drop`: The stage transition with the worst conversion
- `funnel-stages`: The stage definitions for context

## Outputs

- `drop-off-reasons`: The specific reasons people leave at this stage, ranked by frequency
- `fix-hypotheses`: Testable hypotheses for improving conversion at this stage

## Process

1. Ask the dropouts: survey people who left at this stage. What stopped them? What were they expecting? What was confusing or frustrating?
2. Observe the behavior: watch (or track) what people do at this stage. Where do they click? Where do they hesitate? Where do they leave?
3. Analyze the segments: do all segments drop equally, or is the drop concentrated in one segment (mobile users, a specific source, a specific persona)?
4. Generate fix hypotheses: 'If we [change X], then [Y% more people] will advance because [reason Z].' Each hypothesis should be testable with an A/B test or a before/after comparison.
