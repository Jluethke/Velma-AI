# Insight Extractor

Conduct the analysis and identify the key insight.

## Inputs

- `core-question`: The question to answer
- `audience-context`: What the audience needs

## Outputs

- `key-insight`: The single most important finding
- `supporting-evidence`: Data points that support the key insight

## Process

1. Conduct the analysis to answer the core question.
2. Identify the single most important finding.
3. Gather the supporting data points that make the case.
4. Test the insight: would the audience be surprised or act differently because of it?
