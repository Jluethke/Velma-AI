# Step By Step Capturer

Capture each step by observing someone who knows the procedure perform it. Do not write from memory. Memory skips the steps that have become automatic, which are the steps a new person will fail on.

## Inputs

- `scope-statement`: What the procedure covers
- `prerequisites`: What must be in place before starting

## Outputs

- `raw-steps`: Ordered list of steps as observed, each with the action, the completion indicator, and any decisions made
- `decision-points`: Places where the performer made a choice, with the criteria they used

## Process

1. Watch the performer execute the procedure from start to finish. Record every action, not just the ones they describe.
2. At each step, ask: how do you know this step is done? Record the completion indicator (a confirmation message, a visual check, a value within range).
3. At each decision point, ask: what are you checking, and what do you do if the answer is different? Record both paths.
4. Note any 'tricks' or shortcuts the performer uses. These are undocumented optimizations that a new person would not know.
