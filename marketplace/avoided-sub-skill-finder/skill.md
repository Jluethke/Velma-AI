# Avoided Sub Skill Finder

Decompose the skill and find the sub-skill you have been unconsciously avoiding because it is difficult or uncomfortable.

## Inputs

- `skill-description`: The skill being practiced
- `plateau-onset`: When the plateau started

## Outputs

- `sub-skill-decomposition`: The skill broken into 5-8 sub-skills with honest assessment of how much time you spend practicing each
- `avoided-sub-skill`: The sub-skill you spend the least time on relative to its importance, which is likely the one holding back your overall performance

## Process

1. Break the skill into 5-8 sub-skills. For each, estimate what percentage of your practice time goes to it.
2. Identify the sub-skill that is both important and under-practiced. This is usually the one you avoid because it is frustrating, boring, or embarrassing. You gravitate toward the parts you are already good at because they feel productive.
3. Verify by asking: if someone watched your practice sessions for a week, what would they say you avoid? The answer is your avoided sub-skill.
4. The avoided sub-skill is almost always the bottleneck. Improving it by 20% often produces more overall improvement than improving your best sub-skill by 50%.
