# Do Nothing Risk Section

Add a section on the risk of doing nothing: what happens if the status quo continues for another 12 months.

## Inputs

- `status-quo-cost`: Current annual cost
- `cost-breakdown`: Cost categories

## Outputs

- `do-nothing-risk`: The projected cost of the status quo over the next 12 months, including any trends that suggest costs are growing
- `urgency-frame`: A statement that frames delay as a cost: 'Every month of delay costs approximately $X'

## Process

1. Project the status quo cost forward 12 months. If any cost component is growing (more transactions, more employees, more complexity), the future cost is higher than the current cost.
2. Calculate the monthly cost of delay: total annual status quo cost divided by 12. 'Every month you wait costs approximately $7,250 in continued waste.'
3. Do not manufacture false urgency. If the cost is genuinely flat, say so. But most status quo costs grow because the underlying business grows.
4. Position this section as information, not pressure. 'We want you to have the full picture to make the right decision on your timeline.'
