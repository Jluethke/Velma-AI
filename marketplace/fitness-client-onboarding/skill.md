# Fitness Client Onboarding

Onboard new clients with health screening, fitness assessment, and goal setting.

## Inputs

- `membership-tiers`: Membership options selected by the client
- `package-options`: Package selected if not a member

## Outputs

- `client-fitness-profile`: Client profile with health history, assessment results, and goals
- `liability-waiver`: Signed liability waiver and health screening clearance

## Process

1. Collect the health history questionnaire and PAR-Q screening.
2. Conduct the initial fitness assessment (body composition, movement screen, baseline lifts or cardio).
3. Set goals collaboratively (weight loss, strength, sport performance, rehab).
4. Have the client sign the liability waiver and acknowledge the studio policies.
