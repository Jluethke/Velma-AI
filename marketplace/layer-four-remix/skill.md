# Layer Four Remix

Layer 4 (optional): remix the material into your own words for a specific use. This is expression, not summarization. You are combining this source with your own knowledge and other sources to produce something new.

## Inputs

- `executive-summary`: One-sentence core idea
- `layer-three-result`: Complete four-layer progressive summary

## Outputs

- `remixed-output`: The material rewritten in your own words, integrated with your existing knowledge, for a specific purpose
- `progressive-summary-complete`: Final artifact with all four layers accessible: remix, summary, highlights, bold, full original

## Process

1. Layer 4 is triggered by a specific need: you are writing something, making a decision, or teaching someone. You pull this material into a working set alongside other sources.
2. Rewrite the key ideas in your own words. Connect them to what you already know. Note where this source agrees with, contradicts, or extends other sources.
3. The remix is not a paraphrase. It is a synthesis: your understanding of the material applied to your specific situation.
4. File the remix alongside the original. You now have five levels of access: your remix, the one-sentence summary, the highlights, the bold passages, and the full original.
