# Security Team Staffing Planner

Plan the staffing model for red and blue teams including skills, headcount, and contractor augmentation.

## Inputs

- `program-charter`: Scope and objectives to staff against
- `organizational-context`: Budget constraints and existing team capabilities

## Outputs

- `staffing-plan`: Roles, skill requirements, and sourcing strategy (FTE vs contractor)
- `training-plan`: Certifications and training needed for the team

## Process

1. Define red team roles (operator, developer, infrastructure, social engineering)
2. Define blue team roles (detection engineer, incident responder, threat hunter)
3. Determine FTE versus contractor split based on budget and exercise frequency
4. Identify training and certification requirements (OSCP, GPEN, GCIH)
