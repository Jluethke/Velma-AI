# Gap Study Planner

Study the source material to fill the specific gaps that the explanation exposed.

## Inputs

- `jargon-flags`: Places where a technical term stood in for understanding
- `knowledge-gaps`: Specific questions to research

## Outputs

- `gap-research-notes`: Plain-language answers to each gap question
- `resolved-questions`: The questions you can now answer cleanly

## Process

1. Go back to the source material for each flagged question.
2. Study until you can answer the question in your own words.
3. Record the answer as a short, plain note.
4. Mark any question that is still unresolved for another pass.
