# Tradeoff Inventory

List the tradeoffs your product makes, whether chosen on purpose or forced by the design.

## Inputs

- `product-description`: What the product does and how it is built
- `roadmap-notes`: What you have chosen not to build and why

## Outputs

- `tradeoff-list`: The tradeoffs the product makes, named plainly
- `constraint-candidates`: The tradeoffs that could become deliberate positioning

## Process

1. List everything the product does not do, from missing features to use cases you turn away.
2. For each, note whether it is a deliberate choice or a current limit you intend to close.
3. Pull the deliberate choices into a candidate list, since those are the ones you can stand behind.
4. Keep the forced limits separate, since positioning on a limit you plan to remove is a trap.
