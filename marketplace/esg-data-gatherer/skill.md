# Esg Data Gatherer

Collect data across environmental, social, and governance pillars for the ESG report.

## Inputs

- `selected-frameworks`: Frameworks and their requirements
- `disclosure-requirements`: Specific metrics and disclosures needed

## Outputs

- `esg-data-package`: Collected data organized by E, S, and G pillars with sources
- `data-quality-notes`: Notes on data quality, gaps, and estimation methods

## Process

1. Collect environmental data: emissions, energy, water, waste, biodiversity impacts.
2. Collect social data: workforce demographics, health and safety, community impact, supply chain labor.
3. Collect governance data: board composition, executive compensation, ethics, risk management.
4. Document data sources, quality issues, and any estimates or proxies used.
