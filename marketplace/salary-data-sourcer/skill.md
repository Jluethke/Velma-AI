# Salary Data Sourcer

Select and acquire salary survey data sources.

## Inputs

- `roles-to-benchmark`: Roles to benchmark
- `benchmark-scope`: Geographic and industry scope

## Outputs

- `data-sources`: Selected salary surveys and market data sources
- `market-data`: Raw market data by role and geography

## Process

1. Identify relevant salary surveys by industry and geography
2. Acquire the survey data or reports
3. Verify the data is current (within the last 12 months)
4. Organize by job family and level
