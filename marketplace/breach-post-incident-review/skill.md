# Breach Post Incident Review

Conduct the post-incident review and produce the breach record.

## Inputs

- `submission-record`: Regulatory submission record
- `delivery-record`: Individual notification delivery record

## Outputs

- `post-incident-report`: Post-incident review with root cause, timeline, and lessons learned
- `breach-notification-record`: The complete breach notification record for compliance archives

## Process

1. Conduct a post-incident review to identify the root cause and contributing factors.
2. Document the complete timeline from detection to notification.
3. Identify remediation actions to prevent recurrence.
4. Compile the complete breach notification record (assessment, notifications, responses, remediation) for the compliance archive.
