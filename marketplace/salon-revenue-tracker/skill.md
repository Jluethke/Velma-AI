# Salon Revenue Tracker

Track daily revenue by service category, provider, and product sales.

## Inputs

- `service-menu`: Service menu with pricing
- `inventory-list`: Inventory list for retail product tracking

## Outputs

- `revenue-report`: Daily and weekly revenue breakdown by category, provider, and product
- `salon-operations-plan`: Complete salon operations plan combining all setup elements

## Process

1. Configure the point-of-sale system to track revenue by service, provider, and payment method.
2. Set up tip tracking and commission calculations per provider.
3. Track retail product sales separately from service revenue.
4. Generate the weekly revenue summary and compare against targets.
