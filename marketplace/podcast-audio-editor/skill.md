# Podcast Audio Editor

Edit the audio for noise reduction, level balancing, and content cuts.

## Inputs

- `raw-audio-tracks`: Separate audio tracks from recording
- `recording-notes`: Notes on edits needed

## Outputs

- `edited-audio`: Cleaned and edited audio file
- `edit-decision-list`: Log of all cuts and edits made

## Process

1. Remove background noise and mouth sounds
2. Balance levels across all speakers
3. Cut dead air, false starts, and off-topic tangents
4. Smooth transitions between segments
