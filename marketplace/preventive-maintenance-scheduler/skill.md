# Preventive Maintenance Scheduler

Schedule preventive maintenance for each vehicle in the fleet.

## Inputs

- `fleet-inventory`: Current inventory with status, mileage, and condition
- `maintenance-history`: Historical maintenance records for each vehicle

## Outputs

- `pm-schedule`: Preventive maintenance schedule by vehicle and date
- `parts-forecast`: Forecasted parts and supplies needed

## Process

1. Define maintenance intervals based on manufacturer recommendations and usage
2. Schedule each task to minimize vehicle downtime
3. Forecast parts and supplies needed for the schedule
4. Produce the preventive maintenance calendar
