# Ucm Headline Writer

Write the headline and lead with it. Managers process many inputs a day and read the first line to decide how much attention the rest deserves. A headline that states the point up front gets the message read. A message that builds to its point gets skimmed and misfiled.

## Inputs

- `reframed-topic`: The topic framed in the manager's terms

## Outputs

- `headline`: The one-line headline that states the point before any detail
- `one-line-why-it-matters`: A single line connecting the headline to the priority it affects

## Process

1. Write the headline as the point itself: 'The vendor will miss the date, and here is the plan.' Not 'An update on the vendor.'
2. Keep it to one line. A headline that runs to three sentences is a paragraph, and the reader has already started skimming.
3. Add one line on why it matters, tied to the priority it affects, so the manager knows why to keep reading.
4. Put the headline first, before any background. Background before the headline is the most common way an upward message loses its reader.
