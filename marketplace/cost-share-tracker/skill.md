# Cost Share Tracker

Track cost sharing and matching contributions against grant requirements.

## Inputs

- `grant-agreement`: Cost sharing and matching requirements
- `transaction-data`: Cost sharing transactions and in-kind contributions

## Outputs

- `cost-share-report`: Cost sharing contributions tracked against the requirement
- `shortfall-alert`: Alert if cost sharing is behind schedule

## Process

1. Identify and document cash and in-kind matching contributions.
2. Value in-kind contributions per the applicable cost principles.
3. Compare cumulative cost sharing to the required amount by period.
4. Alert if the cost share is not on pace to meet the requirement.
