# Grant Audit Preparer

Prepare for the grant audit by organizing documentation and testing compliance.

## Inputs

- `budget-vs-actual`: Final budget to actual comparison
- `cost-share-report`: Cost sharing documentation

## Outputs

- `audit-ready-package`: Organized documentation for the grant audit
- `self-assessment-results`: Results of internal compliance self-assessment

## Process

1. Organize all financial records by cost category with supporting documentation.
2. Perform a self-assessment of compliance with grant terms and cost principles.
3. Prepare the schedule of expenditures of federal awards (if applicable).
4. Assemble the audit-ready package for the external auditor.
