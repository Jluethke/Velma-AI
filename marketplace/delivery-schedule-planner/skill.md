# Delivery Schedule Planner

Plan the training delivery schedule and communication strategy.

## Inputs

- `role-curriculum-map`: Who needs what training
- `training-modules`: Modules to schedule

## Outputs

- `delivery-schedule`: Calendar of training sessions and deadlines
- `communication-plan`: Announcements and reminders for participants

## Process

1. Set the annual training calendar with quarterly refreshers
2. Assign deadlines by role group
3. Plan the communication cadence (announcements, reminders, escalations)
4. Schedule specialized sessions for new hires and role changes
