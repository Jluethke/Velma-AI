# Ooda Orient Stage Mapping

Map the Orient stage: how raw information becomes understanding you can act on. Boyd called Orient the most important stage, and it is usually where the loop slows down.

## Inputs

- `loop-context`: The situation framed as a repeating decision loop
- `observe-map`: How information is gathered and from where

## Outputs

- `orient-map`: How information is turned into meaning and options
- `orient-latency`: The time from seeing information to understanding what it means

## Process

1. Trace how observations are interpreted: analysis, mental models, past experience, team discussion.
2. Measure the delay from seeing information to understanding what it means for the decision.
3. Find where orientation stalls: too much data, wrong mental models, or waiting on one expert.
4. Record the orient latency as a number.
