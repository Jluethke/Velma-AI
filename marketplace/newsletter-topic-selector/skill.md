# Newsletter Topic Selector

Select and plan the newsletter topic and content structure.

## Inputs

- `audience-profile`: Who reads this newsletter and what they care about
- `content-calendar`: The editorial calendar for timing context
- `past-performance`: Performance data from recent issues (opens, clicks, replies)

## Outputs

- `issue-plan`: The plan for this issue: topic, sections, key links, and call to action
- `subject-line-candidates`: 3 to 5 subject line options to test

## Process

1. Review what performed well in recent issues (highest opens, highest clicks, most replies).
2. Identify the topic for this issue based on audience interest, timeliness, and editorial calendar.
3. Outline the sections (lead story, supporting content, links, CTA).
4. Write 3 to 5 subject line candidates for testing.
