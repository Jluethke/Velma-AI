# Incident Narrative Documentation

Document a clear narrative of what happened during the incident.

## Inputs

- `raw-timeline`: Collected timeline entries from all sources
- `evidence-references`: Supporting evidence from the incident

## Outputs

- `incident-narrative`: Clear narrative of what happened and when
- `key-decisions-log`: Record of key decisions made during the response and their context

## Process

1. Write a clear chronological narrative from the merged timeline
2. Identify and document key decision points and what information was available at each
3. Note where the response diverged from established procedures
4. Validate the narrative with the responders for accuracy
