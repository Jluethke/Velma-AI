# Pss Decision Recorder

Record the price recommendation and the evidence behind it.

## Inputs

- `context-adjusted-prices`: The adjusted prices and scenarios
- `method-comparison`: The agreement between the two methods

## Outputs

- `price-recommendation`: The recommended price with its scenario
- `price-sensitivity-report`: The full record of both methods and the decision

## Process

1. State the recommended price and the scenario it fits.
2. Summarize the evidence from both methods.
3. Produce the report that records the decision and its basis.
