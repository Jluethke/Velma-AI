# Small Claims Hearing Prep

Prepare for the hearing: opening statement, evidence presentation order, and judge interaction.

## Inputs

- `evidence_binder`: Organized evidence
- `timeline_of_events`: Case narrative
- `opposing_arguments`: What the other side might say

## Outputs

- `opening_statement`: 2-minute opening statement
- `presentation_order`: Order to present evidence
- `rebuttal_prep`: Responses to likely opposing arguments

## Process

1. Draft clear, brief opening statement
2. Organize evidence presentation order
3. Anticipate opposing arguments
4. Prepare rebuttals with evidence
5. Practice presenting within time limits
