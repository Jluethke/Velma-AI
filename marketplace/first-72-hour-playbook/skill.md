# First 72 Hour Playbook

Write the first 72 hours of action for each scenario and severity level.

## Inputs

- `command-structure`: Who is in charge and their authority
- `trigger-scenarios`: The scenario definitions

## Outputs

- `first-72-hour-actions`: For each scenario and severity: the immediate actions (first hour), the stabilization actions (hours 1-24), and the sustained actions (hours 24-72)
- `communication-templates`: Pre-written communication templates for each scenario: internal notification, customer notification, vendor notification, public statement

## Process

1. For each scenario, write the first hour: activate the plan (who calls whom), assess the situation (what happened, what is affected, what is the severity), and take the immediate protective actions (secure people, secure data, secure assets).
2. Write the stabilization phase (hours 1-24): activate the workarounds for the highest-priority activities, notify the stakeholders using the templates, and begin the damage assessment.
3. Write the sustained phase (hours 24-72): execute the recovery sequence in priority order, provide status updates at defined intervals, and adjust the plan as the situation evolves.
4. Pre-write the communication templates. In a crisis, writing from scratch is slow and error-prone. The templates include: what happened (factual, no speculation), what we are doing, what you should do, and when the next update will come.
