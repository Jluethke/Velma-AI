# Drip Email Copywriter

Write the subject line, body copy, and CTA for each email in the sequence.

## Inputs

- `sequence-map`: Email sequence with timing and triggers
- `content-themes`: Content theme per email

## Outputs

- `email-drafts`: Subject line, body, and CTA for each email
- `personalization-tokens`: Merge fields used in the copy

## Process

1. Write the subject line for each email
2. Draft the body copy aligned to the content theme
3. Define the CTA and any personalization tokens
