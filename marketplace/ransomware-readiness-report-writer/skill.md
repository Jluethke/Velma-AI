# Ransomware Readiness Report Writer

Produce the ransomware readiness assessment report.

## Inputs

- `backup-test-results`: Backup integrity results
- `initial-access-results`: Initial access test results
- `tabletop-results`: Incident response exercise findings
- `plan-readiness-score`: Overall readiness score

## Outputs

- `ransomware-readiness-report`: Complete ransomware readiness assessment report
- `improvement-roadmap`: Prioritized roadmap of improvements with timeline and cost

## Process

1. Score readiness across all categories (prevention, detection, response, recovery)
2. Summarize the critical gaps that would determine the outcome of a real attack
3. Produce the improvement roadmap with quick wins, 30-day, 90-day, and 180-day milestones
4. Include a cost-benefit comparison of improvements against estimated ransomware impact
