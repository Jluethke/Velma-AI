# Referral Acknowledgment System

Acknowledge every referral visibly. Unacknowledged referrals kill the loop. Acknowledged referrals reinforce the behavior.

## Inputs

- `tracking-schema`: How referrals are tracked
- `referral-value-design`: What value the referrer gets

## Outputs

- `acknowledgment-workflow`: The automated and manual acknowledgment touchpoints: instant notification, reward delivery, and periodic gratitude
- `referral-loop-report`: Complete referral loop design document covering motivation, mechanism, tracking, and acknowledgment

## Process

1. Instant acknowledgment: the moment someone uses a referral link, notify the referrer. 'Your friend just signed up' or 'Someone used your recommendation.' This closes the feedback loop immediately.
2. Reward delivery: deliver the referrer's value within 24 hours. Delayed rewards feel like broken promises. If the reward is credit or status, apply it instantly.
3. Periodic gratitude: monthly or quarterly, send super-connectors a personal note (not automated template) thanking them. Name the impact: 'You brought in 12 people this quarter. That matters.'
4. Measure the loop: referral rate (what percentage of customers refer), conversion rate (what percentage of referrals convert), and loop ratio (referrals per referrer per month). A healthy loop has a referral rate above 10% and a conversion rate above 20%.
