# Policy Bias Reviewer

Review policies and practices for potential bias.

## Inputs

- `disparity-flags`: Identified disparities
- `flow-rates`: Hiring, promotion, attrition rates

## Outputs

- `policy-review-findings`: Findings on policies that may contribute to disparities
- `practice-recommendations`: Recommendations for process changes

## Process

1. Review hiring practices (job descriptions, sourcing channels, interview processes)
2. Review promotion criteria and nomination processes
3. Review flexible work and leave policies for equitable access
4. Identify practices that may disproportionately affect specific groups
