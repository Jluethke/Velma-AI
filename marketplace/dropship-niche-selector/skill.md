# Dropship Niche Selector

Select the product niche and evaluate potential suppliers.

## Inputs

- `market-interests`: Product categories of interest
- `budget-constraints`: Startup budget and margin requirements

## Outputs

- `selected-niche`: Chosen niche with market analysis
- `supplier-shortlist`: Shortlisted suppliers with evaluation scores

## Process

1. Research niches by demand volume, competition, and margin potential.
2. Evaluate supplier options (reliability, shipping speed, product quality, MOQ).
3. Order samples from top suppliers to verify quality.
4. Select the niche and shortlist the suppliers.
