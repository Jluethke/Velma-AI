# Privacy Training Deployment

Deploy the training to all target audiences and track completion.

## Inputs

- `training-modules`: The developed training modules
- `training-audiences`: The target audiences

## Outputs

- `deployment-schedule`: The rollout schedule by audience and deadline
- `completion-tracking`: Tracking of completion rates and assessment scores by audience

## Process

1. Load the training modules into the learning management system or distribution platform.
2. Assign the modules to each audience with a completion deadline.
3. Send reminders to employees who have not completed by the midpoint.
4. Track completion rates and assessment pass rates.
5. Escalate non-completion to managers as the deadline approaches.
