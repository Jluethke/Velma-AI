# Content Distribution Planner

Set distribution channels and the promotion plan for each content piece.

## Inputs

- `editorial-calendar`: Content calendar
- `persona-profiles`: Where each persona spends time

## Outputs

- `distribution-plan`: Channels and promotion tactics per content piece
- `content-strategy-document`: Complete content marketing strategy document

## Process

1. Select distribution channels for each content piece
2. Define the promotion tactics (social, email, paid, syndication)
3. Produce the content strategy document
