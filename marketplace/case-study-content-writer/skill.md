# Case Study Content Writer

Write the case study content following the challenge, approach, solution, results structure.

## Inputs

- `selected-project`: Project details and rationale
- `client-permission`: Permission scope (named, anonymized, partial)

## Outputs

- `case-study-draft`: Written case study with challenge, approach, solution, and results sections
- `metrics-summary`: Quantified results and before/after comparisons

## Process

1. Write the challenge section: what the client was dealing with before the engagement.
2. Write the approach section: what methodology you used and why.
3. Write the solution section: what you built or delivered.
4. Write the results section: measurable outcomes, before and after metrics, and client feedback.
5. Keep the language factual and let the numbers do the talking.
