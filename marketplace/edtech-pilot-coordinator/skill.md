# Edtech Pilot Coordinator

Pilot the top candidate with a small group of faculty and students.

## Inputs

- `tool-shortlist`: Top tool candidates
- `success-criteria`: Evaluation criteria

## Outputs

- `pilot-results`: Usage data and feedback from the pilot
- `selected-tool`: Final tool selection based on pilot results

## Process

1. Recruit 3-5 faculty volunteers for a one-semester pilot
2. Provide initial training and support during the pilot
3. Collect usage data, student feedback, and faculty observations
4. Evaluate against success criteria and select the tool
