# Social Ad Objective Definer

Define the social ad campaign objective and target audience.

## Inputs

- `business-goal`: Business outcome the campaign should support
- `audience-profile`: Target audience demographics and behaviors

## Outputs

- `social-ad-brief`: Objective, audience, and budget
- `platform-shortlist`: Recommended platforms for the audience

## Process

1. Define the campaign objective (reach, engagement, traffic, conversions)
2. Profile the target audience
3. Shortlist platforms where the audience is active
