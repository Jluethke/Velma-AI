# Prevention System

Build a prevention system: monthly self-check, energy boundaries, and early warning signals.

## Inputs

- `recovery-plan`: What worked in recovery
- `recent-changes`: What triggered the burnout

## Outputs

- `monthly-check-protocol`: A monthly 5-minute self-assessment: rate exhaustion, detachment, and inefficacy. If any score rises above 6, trigger the recovery protocol immediately.
- `early-warning-signals`: Your personal early warning signs: specific behaviors or feelings that indicate burnout is approaching (e.g., dreading Sunday evenings, snapping at colleagues, abandoning hobbies)

## Process

1. Set a monthly calendar reminder for the 3-dimension self-check. Score exhaustion, detachment, and inefficacy 1-10. If any score rises above 6, activate recovery interventions immediately. Do not wait until it reaches 9.
2. Identify your personal early warning signs. Everyone has them: for some it is dreading Sunday evenings, for others it is snapping at loved ones, for others it is abandoning hobbies. Know your signals.
3. Build structural prevention: do not overcommit in the first place. Before saying yes to a new commitment, ask: 'What will I stop doing to make room for this?' If the answer is nothing, you are adding demand without reducing load.
4. Recovery is not a one-time event. It is a recurring need. Schedule recovery proactively (quarterly vacations, weekly off-days, daily downtime) instead of reactively (only resting when you collapse).
