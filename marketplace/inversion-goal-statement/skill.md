# Inversion Goal Statement

State the goal clearly, then invert it. If the goal is 'build a successful product', the inversion is 'how would I guarantee this product fails?' The inversion must be specific enough to generate concrete failure paths, not abstract platitudes.

## Inputs

- `goal-description`: The goal, problem, or decision to solve
- `context`: Relevant constraints, resources, timeline, and stakeholders

## Outputs

- `stated-goal`: The goal in one sentence
- `inverted-question`: The goal reframed as: how would I guarantee failure at this?

## Process

1. Write the goal in one sentence. If you cannot state it in one sentence, you do not understand it yet.
2. Invert it: change 'how do I succeed at X' to 'how would I guarantee failure at X'.
3. Check the inversion is specific. 'How would I guarantee this product launch fails' is better than 'how would things go wrong'.
4. State the constraints that apply to both the forward and inverted versions.
