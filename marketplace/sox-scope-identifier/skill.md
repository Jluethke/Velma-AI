# Sox Scope Identifier

Identify in-scope processes and controls for SOX compliance.

## Inputs

- `financial-processes`: List of financial reporting processes
- `materiality-threshold`: Materiality threshold for scoping

## Outputs

- `in-scope-processes`: Processes and accounts in scope for SOX
- `control-inventory`: Inventory of controls mapped to each process

## Process

1. Identify significant accounts and disclosures.
2. Map the processes that feed into those accounts.
3. Identify the key controls within each process.
4. Document the scope and control inventory.
