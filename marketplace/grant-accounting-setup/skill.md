# Grant Accounting Setup

Set up the grant accounting structure with fund codes and cost categories.

## Inputs

- `grant-agreement`: Signed grant agreement with budget and terms
- `chart-of-accounts`: Existing chart of accounts

## Outputs

- `grant-fund-structure`: Fund codes and cost categories mapped to the grant budget
- `compliance-requirements`: Financial reporting and compliance requirements

## Process

1. Create the fund code and cost center for the grant in the accounting system.
2. Map the grant budget categories to GL accounts.
3. Set up spending limits and authorization rules per the grant terms.
4. Document the reporting schedule and compliance requirements.
