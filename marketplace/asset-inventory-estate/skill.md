# Asset Inventory Estate

Inventory all assets, accounts, debts, and insurance for estate planning purposes.

## Inputs

- `financial_accounts`: Bank, investment, retirement accounts
- `real_property`: Real estate owned
- `insurance_policies`: Life, disability, etc.
- `debts`: Mortgages, loans, credit cards

## Outputs

- `asset_inventory`: Complete asset and debt listing
- `net_worth_estimate`: Estimated net worth
- `beneficiary_audit`: Current beneficiaries on each account

## Process

1. List all financial accounts with approximate values
2. Document real property and ownership type
3. List insurance policies and beneficiaries
4. Tally outstanding debts
5. Calculate net worth
