# Health Target Selection

Select the specific health outcomes you are aiming for and name the measure that tells you whether each is met.

## Inputs

- `health-goals`: What you want from your health, such as endurance, strength, mobility, or weight stability
- `current-activity-baseline`: What you currently do in a typical week

## Outputs

- `selected-targets`: The two or three outcomes you will actually pursue, kept few enough to sustain
- `target-metrics`: The measure for each target that defines whether it is met

## Process

1. Turn each goal into an outcome with a measure. Get fitter becomes resting heart rate or a distance you can cover without stopping.
2. Keep the list to two or three outcomes. Pursuing every dimension of health at once guarantees none of them gets the minimum dose.
3. For each target, write the measure you will check and how often, such as a weekly walk time or a monthly strength test.
4. Confirm each target matters enough to you to sustain for months. A target chosen for appearance alone rarely survives the first dull week.
