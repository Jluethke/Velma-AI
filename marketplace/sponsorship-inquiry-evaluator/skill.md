# Sponsorship Inquiry Evaluator

Evaluate a sponsorship inquiry for brand fit and deal viability.

## Inputs

- `sponsorship-inquiry`: The brand's outreach or proposal
- `creator-brand-guidelines`: The creator's brand values and audience demographics

## Outputs

- `fit-assessment`: Whether the brand aligns with the creator's audience and values
- `deal-parameters`: The proposed deliverables, timeline, and budget range

## Process

1. Review the brand's product, reputation, and target demographic.
2. Compare the brand's audience with the creator's audience for overlap.
3. Assess whether the product is something the creator would genuinely use or recommend.
4. Outline the proposed deliverables and timeline from the inquiry.
