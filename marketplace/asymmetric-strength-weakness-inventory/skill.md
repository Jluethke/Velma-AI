# Asymmetric Strength Weakness Inventory

Inventory your real strengths and your real weaknesses. Both matter: strengths feed the advantage search and weaknesses mark the zones you must avoid fighting in.

## Inputs

- `business-context`: A factual profile of your business
- `resource-summary`: Your real resources and access

## Outputs

- `strength-list`: Your durable, real strengths
- `self-weakness-list`: The weaknesses you cannot quickly fix

## Process

1. List strengths that are durable and specific, not generic claims like good service.
2. For each strength, note the evidence that it is real and hard to copy.
3. List your weaknesses honestly, especially the structural ones you cannot fix soon.
4. Keep the two lists separate. They are used in different later steps.
