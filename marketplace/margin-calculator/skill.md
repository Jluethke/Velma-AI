# Margin Calculator

Calculate cost-plus margins and identify margin improvement opportunities.

## Inputs

- `product-catalog`: Products with cost data
- `competitor-pricing`: Competitor price analysis

## Outputs

- `margin-analysis`: Margin analysis by SKU and category
- `pricing-constraints`: Minimum price floors and margin targets

## Process

1. Calculate landed cost per SKU (product cost, shipping, duties, fees).
2. Compute current margin and target margin by category.
3. Identify negative-margin and below-target SKUs.
4. Set price floors based on minimum margin requirements.
