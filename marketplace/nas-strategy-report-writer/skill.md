# Nas Strategy Report Writer

Assemble the negotiation plan.

## Inputs

- `opening-script`: The opening line and reasoning
- `trade-list`: The non-price trades available

## Outputs

- `negotiation-plan`: The anchor, ladder, and scripts in one plan
- `anchor-strategy-report`: The one-page strategy to rehearse

## Process

1. Assemble the anchor, the ladder, and the scripts into one plan.
2. State the target, the walk-away, and the trades.
3. Produce the one-page strategy to rehearse before the talk.
