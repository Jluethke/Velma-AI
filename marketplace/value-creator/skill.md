# Value Creator

Create value by trading across interests. Give on issues that matter less to you; get on issues that matter more.

## Inputs

- `own-interests`: Your ranked interests
- `counterpart-interests`: Their estimated ranked interests

## Outputs

- `trade-opportunities`: Pairs of issues where you and they have different priorities, creating room for mutually beneficial trades
- `value-creating-proposals`: Specific deal proposals that give each party more of what they value most

## Process

1. Identify asymmetric valuations: issues where you care a lot and they care little, and vice versa. These are the trade opportunities.
2. Construct package proposals: 'I will give you X (which you value highly but costs me little) in exchange for Y (which I value highly but costs you little).'
3. Never negotiate issue by issue. Issue-by-issue negotiation is zero-sum. Package deals allow value creation.
4. Test proposals with 'What if...' questions: 'What if we extended the timeline but reduced the price?' 'What if we included support but changed the payment terms?' These reveal interest priorities without committing.
