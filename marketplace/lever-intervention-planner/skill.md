# Lever Intervention Planner

Turn each selected lever into a concrete intervention with an owner, a timeline, and a metric. A lever without a measurable target becomes a vague initiative that no one can judge.

## Inputs

- `selected-levers`: The levers chosen
- `feasibility-notes`: Their cost, timeline, and probability

## Outputs

- `intervention-plan`: For each lever, the specific change, its owner, the deadline, and the dependencies it needs
- `success-metrics`: The departure rate from each targeted cluster at six and twelve months, against a baseline

## Process

1. For each lever, state exactly what will change, who owns the change, and by when.
2. Define the success metric as the departure rate from the targeted cluster at six and twelve months.
3. Set a baseline from the departure data so you can measure movement.
4. Identify dependencies: approvals, budget, or other teams whose cooperation the lever needs.
