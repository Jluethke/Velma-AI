# Policy Pricer

Price the policy by calculating the premium, selecting deductibles, and applying credits or surcharges.

## Inputs

- `risk-assessment`: Risk grade and assessment details
- `risk-profile`: Exposures and loss data for rating

## Outputs

- `pricing-worksheet`: Detailed premium calculation with rate, exposure base, and adjustments
- `quote-terms`: Proposed premium, deductible options, coverage terms, and conditions

## Process

1. Apply the base rate to the exposure measure (revenue, payroll, units, area).
2. Apply experience modifications, schedule credits, or surcharges.
3. Calculate the final premium for each deductible option.
4. Document any coverage restrictions or endorsement requirements.
