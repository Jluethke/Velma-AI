# Ueh Cac Calculator

Compute the customer acquisition cost.

## Inputs

- `business-context`: The sales and marketing spend and the period
- `unit-model`: The unit the cost is measured against

## Outputs

- `cac-value`: The cost to acquire one unit
- `acquisition-cost-breakdown`: The acquisition cost broken down by channel

## Process

1. Total the sales and marketing spend for the period.
2. Divide the spend by the new units acquired in the same period.
3. Break the cost down by channel.
