# Complaint Assessor

Receive and assess the complaint to determine investigation scope.

## Inputs

- `complaint-details`: The complaint as reported
- `company-policies`: Relevant company policies

## Outputs

- `assessment-decision`: Decision on whether a formal investigation is warranted
- `preliminary-scope`: Initial scope of the investigation if warranted

## Process

1. Review the complaint for specificity and policy relevance
2. Determine if the allegations, if true, would violate policy or law
3. Assess urgency and any immediate protective measures needed
4. Decide whether to proceed with formal investigation
