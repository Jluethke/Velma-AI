# Exception Writing Guide

Train the reporters to write exceptions that are actionable: what happened, what is the impact, what is needed.

## Inputs

- `report-structure`: The report template
- `audience`: Who reads and what they need to do

## Outputs

- `exception-writing-guide`: How to write an exception: the fact (what changed), the impact (what is affected), the ask (what is needed from the reader), and the deadline
- `exception-examples`: Examples of good exceptions (specific, actionable) and bad exceptions (vague, no ask)

## Process

1. An exception has four parts: what changed (the fact), what is affected (the impact), what is needed (the ask), and by when (the deadline).
2. Good exception: 'The vendor delayed the API delivery by 2 weeks (fact). This pushes the integration milestone past the launch date (impact). We need approval to either delay launch by 2 weeks or use the backup API at additional cost (ask) by Friday (deadline).'
3. Bad exception: 'There are some delays with the vendor.' This tells the reader nothing actionable: no impact assessment, no options, no deadline.
4. The test: after reading the exception, can the reader take action without asking follow-up questions? If not, the exception is incomplete.
