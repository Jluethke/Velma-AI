# Research Memo Writer

Write the research memo in the standard format.

## Inputs

- `legal-analysis`: The completed legal analysis
- `conclusion`: The conclusion and confidence level

## Outputs

- `research-memo`: The completed legal research memo (question, brief answer, discussion, conclusion)
- `memo-record`: Record of the memo in the matter file

## Process

1. Write the question presented section.
2. Write the brief answer summarizing the conclusion in one or two sentences.
3. Write the discussion section with the detailed legal analysis and citations.
4. Write the conclusion with any recommendations or caveats.
5. File the memo in the matter repository.
