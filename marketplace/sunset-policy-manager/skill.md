# Sunset Policy Manager

Manage the sunset policy for permanently removing inactive subscribers.

## Inputs

- `re-engagement-results`: Results showing subscribers who did not re-engage

## Outputs

- `sunset-policy`: The documented sunset policy with timeline and criteria
- `lifecycle-management-plan`: Complete subscriber lifecycle management plan covering welcome, engagement, re-engagement, and sunset

## Process

1. Define the sunset timeline (remove subscribers who do not respond to the re-engagement sequence).
2. Set the grace period before final removal.
3. Document the removal process and record retention.
4. Compile the full lifecycle management plan.
