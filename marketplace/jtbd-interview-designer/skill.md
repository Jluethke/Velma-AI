# Jtbd Interview Designer

Design the Jobs-to-be-Done interview. The interview follows a specific structure that uncovers the forces of progress: push, pull, anxiety, and habit.

## Inputs

- `product-or-service`: What you offer (the thing being hired for a job)
- `recent-customers`: A list of recent customers or users to interview (target 8-12 interviews for pattern saturation)

## Outputs

- `interview-guide`: The interview script organized by the four forces: push (situation and struggle), pull (attraction to the new solution), anxiety (what almost stopped them), and habit (what they were doing before)
- `interview-logistics`: Scheduling, recording consent, interview length (45-60 minutes), and compensation plan

## Process

1. Structure the interview around the timeline of the switch: first thought, passive looking, active looking, deciding, buying, first use, and ongoing use.
2. Key questions: What was happening in your life or work when you first thought about this? What were you using before? What was the moment you decided to make a change? What alternatives did you consider? What almost stopped you?
3. Do not ask about features. Ask about situations. The job lives in the situation, not in the product category.
4. Plan for 8-12 interviews. Patterns typically saturate (stop yielding new jobs) by interview 8. If you are still finding new jobs at interview 12, conduct 4 more.
