# Stakeholder Feedback Collector

Gather feedback from students, alumni, employers, and faculty on the program.

## Inputs

- `program-name`: Name of the program
- `review-period`: Time range for the review

## Outputs

- `feedback-summary`: Aggregated feedback by stakeholder group
- `common-themes`: Recurring themes across stakeholder groups

## Process

1. Survey current students on curriculum, teaching, and resources
2. Survey alumni on career preparation and program relevance
3. Interview employers on graduate readiness
4. Collect faculty reflections on curriculum and resources
