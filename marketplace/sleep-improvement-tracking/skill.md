# Sleep Improvement Tracking

Track improvements over 30 days and adjust: compare sleep latency, quality, and energy levels to your baseline.

## Inputs

- `wind-down-routine`: The new routine
- `sleep-baseline`: The original baseline

## Outputs

- `improvement-data`: 30-day comparison: sleep latency (should decrease), quality rating (should increase), and daytime energy (should improve)
- `adjustments`: What to tweak: if latency is still high, the timing or routine needs adjustment. If quality is still low, the environment needs work.

## Process

1. Continue the sleep log for 30 days after implementing changes. Compare week 4 to the baseline.
2. Expected improvements: sleep latency drops below 15 minutes, quality rating increases by 2+ points, weekend-weekday gap shrinks.
3. If latency is still high after 2 weeks, adjust timing: try going to bed 30 minutes later. A shorter window of time in bed with better sleep is more restorative than a longer window with poor sleep.
4. If wake-ups persist, check the environment: room temperature, noise, light leaks. If the environment is clean, consider whether alcohol or late meals are contributing.
