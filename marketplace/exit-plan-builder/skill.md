# Exit Plan Builder

For each commitment on the stop-now list, build a specific exit plan. An exit plan has a date, a sequence of actions, and a statement of what you will do with the freed resources. Without a plan, the decision to stop becomes another deferred decision.

## Inputs

- `stop-now-list`: Commitments to exit
- `reframed-narratives`: Reframed stories for emotional attachments

## Outputs

- `exit-plans`: For each commitment to exit: the date, the actions to take, and what you will do with the freed resources
- `freed-resources`: Total time, money, and energy recovered by exiting all stop-now commitments

## Process

1. For each commitment, set the exit date. Within 2 weeks for simple exits; within 30 days for complex ones.
2. List the specific actions: cancel the subscription, send the email, have the conversation, transfer the responsibility.
3. State what you will do with the freed resources. If you do not redirect them, they will drift to the next sunk-cost trap.
4. Sum the total freed resources across all exits. This is the real payoff of the sunk-cost audit.
