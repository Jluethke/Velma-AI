# Product Page Reviewer

Review product page layouts, CTAs, and information hierarchy.

## Inputs

- `funnel-analysis`: Funnel analysis with product page drop-off data
- `top-issues`: Prioritized conversion blockers

## Outputs

- `page-review-findings`: Product page review findings
- `layout-recommendations`: Recommended layout and CTA changes

## Process

1. Review image quality, quantity, and zoom functionality.
2. Assess the CTA placement, color, and copy.
3. Check the information hierarchy (price, reviews, shipping, returns).
4. Identify missing trust signals (reviews, guarantees, security badges).
