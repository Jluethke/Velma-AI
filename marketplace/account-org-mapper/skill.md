# Account Org Mapper

Map the account's organization chart and decision makers.

## Inputs

- `account-name`: Name of the strategic account
- `known-contacts`: Existing contacts and their roles

## Outputs

- `org-map`: Organizational chart with decision makers identified
- `relationship-gaps`: Key stakeholders not yet engaged

## Process

1. List all known contacts and their roles.
2. Map the reporting structure and decision-making process.
3. Identify the economic buyer, technical buyer, and champion.
4. Flag stakeholders with no existing relationship.
