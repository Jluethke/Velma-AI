# Sponsored Content Producer

Produce the sponsored content and submit for brand approval.

## Inputs

- `signed-agreement`: The sponsorship agreement with deliverables
- `deliverable-schedule`: The production and approval timeline

## Outputs

- `approved-content`: The brand-approved sponsored content ready for publication
- `revision-log`: Record of any revision requests and how they were resolved

## Process

1. Create the sponsored content according to the agreement (video segment, post, story, etc.).
2. Submit for brand review by the agreed deadline.
3. Incorporate feedback within the scope of the agreement.
4. Get final approval and prepare for publication.
