# Farm Input Cost Estimator

Estimate all input costs for the season.

## Inputs

- `crop-plan`: Crops and acreage
- `input-prices`: Current prices for seed, fertilizer, fuel, and labor

## Outputs

- `cost-breakdown`: Total costs by category and crop
- `break-even-prices`: Commodity price needed to cover costs per crop

## Process

1. List all variable costs by crop (seed, fertilizer, chemicals, custom hire).
2. Add fixed costs (land rent, equipment payments, insurance, taxes).
3. Calculate break-even price per bushel or unit for each crop.
4. Identify opportunities to reduce costs without affecting yield.
