# Skill Vs Will Diagnoser

Diagnose whether the problem is skill, meaning the person cannot do the work, or will, meaning they choose not to. The two look similar from the outside but need opposite responses, and mistaking one for the other wastes months.

## Inputs

- `performance-concern`: The specific performance concern prompting the review
- `performance-record`: The history of expectations, feedback, and results for this person

## Outputs

- `problem-diagnosis`: Whether the gap is skill, will, or a mix
- `supporting-evidence`: The cases of trying and falling short, or being able and not doing, behind the diagnosis

## Process

1. State the specific performance gap in observable terms, not as a judgment of the person.
2. Ask the skill question: when the person genuinely tries, can they produce the result? Look for cases where they tried and still fell short.
3. Ask the will question: when the person is capable, do they consistently choose to do it? Look for cases where they could and did not.
4. Diagnose skill, will, or a mix, and record the evidence behind the call.
