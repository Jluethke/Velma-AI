# Aar Follow Through Check

Follow through on the actions. The value of an AAR is zero if the actions are not completed. Schedule a follow-up check 30 days after the review to verify completion.

## Inputs

- `action-register`: Actions with owners, deadlines, and done definitions
- `lesson-archive-entries`: Lessons for the archive

## Outputs

- `follow-through-report`: Status of each action at the 30-day check: completed, in progress, or abandoned (with reason)
- `aar-complete-record`: Complete AAR record: scope, timeline, discussion, actions, and follow-through results

## Process

1. Schedule the follow-up check 30 days after the AAR. Put it on the calendar at the end of the AAR meeting. If it is not scheduled, it will not happen.
2. At the 30-day check, review each action: completed (the thing is done and verified), in progress (underway but not done, with a new deadline), or abandoned (not going to happen, with the reason why).
3. For process changes, verify they are actually happening, not just set up. A weekly status update that was sent once and then forgotten is not a completed action.
4. Archive the complete AAR record (scope, timeline, discussion, actions, outcomes) in the institutional memory system so future teams can learn from it without running into the same problems.
