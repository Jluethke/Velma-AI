# Account Objective Setter

Set measurable account objectives and engagement activities.

## Inputs

- `wallet-share-analysis`: Current state and growth potential
- `expansion-opportunities`: Identified growth areas
- `relationship-gaps`: Stakeholders to engage

## Outputs

- `account-objectives`: Measurable goals for the account
- `engagement-plan`: Activities and timeline to achieve objectives

## Process

1. Set revenue and relationship objectives for the next 12 months.
2. Prioritize expansion opportunities by value and feasibility.
3. Plan engagement activities for each relationship gap.
4. Set milestones and review dates.
