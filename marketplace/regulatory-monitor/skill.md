# Regulatory Monitor

Monitor regulatory sources for new or amended rules.

## Inputs

- `regulatory-scope`: Industries, jurisdictions, and regulatory bodies to monitor
- `current-obligations`: Existing regulatory obligations and compliance status

## Outputs

- `change-alerts`: New regulatory changes identified
- `change-summaries`: Summary of each change with effective dates

## Process

1. Subscribe to regulatory feeds and publications for each jurisdiction.
2. Review new rules, amendments, and guidance documents.
3. Summarize each change with its scope, effective date, and affected areas.
4. Distribute change alerts to the compliance team.
