# Interview Protocol Builder

Write a semi-structured interview protocol with opening, main, and closing sections, probes, and procedural safeguards.

## Inputs

- `research-question`: The qualitative research questions the interviews address
- `construct-definitions`: Constructs and themes to explore in depth
- `participant-profile`: Description of who will be interviewed and in what setting

## Outputs

- `interview-guide`: Structured guide with core questions, follow-up probes, and time estimates
- `consent-script`: Informed consent procedure including recording permission and withdrawal rights
- `interviewer-notes`: Training notes on rapport, neutrality, and handling sensitive topics

## Process

1. Draft opening questions that build rapport and establish the participant's context
2. Write core questions for each construct, moving from general to specific
3. Develop follow-up probes for anticipated responses and unexpected directions
4. Add closing questions that invite participant reflections and final thoughts
5. Write the consent script and interviewer procedural notes
