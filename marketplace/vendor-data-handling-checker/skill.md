# Vendor Data Handling Checker

Check how vendors handle, store, and protect your data.

## Inputs

- `data-sharing-matrix`: Data types shared with each vendor
- `certification-status`: Vendor certifications and audit reports

## Outputs

- `data-handling-findings`: Issues with vendor data storage, encryption, retention, and deletion
- `data-residency-status`: Where each vendor stores your data geographically

## Process

1. Verify that vendors encrypt your data at rest and document the encryption method
2. Check data retention policies and confirm vendors delete data when required
3. Verify data residency complies with your regulatory requirements
4. Check whether vendors use your data for purposes beyond the contract (training ML models, analytics)
