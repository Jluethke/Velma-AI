# Digital Usage Baseline

Measure your actual digital usage for 7 days: screen time by app, pickup frequency, and notification count.

## Inputs

- `current-habits`: Your sense of how much you use your phone and computer
- `concerns`: What prompted this audit: difficulty focusing, poor sleep, anxiety, time lost

## Outputs

- `usage-data`: 7-day averages: total screen time, top 5 apps by time, phone pickups per day, notification count per day
- `reality-gap`: The gap between how much you thought you used your devices and how much you actually do (most people underestimate by 50-100%)

## Process

1. Use your phone's built-in screen time tracker (iOS Screen Time or Android Digital Wellbeing) for 7 days. Do not change your behavior; observe it.
2. Record: total screen time per day, top 5 apps by time, number of phone pickups per day, and total notifications per day.
3. Most people are surprised: average screen time is 3-4 hours per day, average pickups are 80-100 per day, and average notifications are 60-80 per day. Your numbers reveal the magnitude of the problem.
4. Calculate the cost: if you spend 3 hours per day on your phone, that is 21 hours per week, or 1,095 hours per year. What would you do with 1,000 hours?
