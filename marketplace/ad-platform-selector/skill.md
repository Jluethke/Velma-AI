# Ad Platform Selector

Select the advertising platforms and ad formats for the campaign.

## Inputs

- `campaign-objective`: Objective and budget
- `business-goal`: Business context

## Outputs

- `platform-plan`: Selected platforms with budget split and ad formats
- `audience-requirements`: Targeting requirements per platform

## Process

1. Evaluate platforms against the objective and audience
2. Select the ad formats for each platform
3. Split the budget across platforms
