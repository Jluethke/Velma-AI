# Communication Plan Writer

Write the communication plan: for each stakeholder or group, define what they receive, how often, through what channel, and who owns the communication.

## Inputs

- `power-interest-grid`: The stakeholder grid with quadrant strategies
- `quadrant-strategies`: The communication approach for each quadrant

## Outputs

- `communication-plan`: For each stakeholder or group: the message type, frequency, channel, owner, and the escalation trigger
- `communication-calendar`: A calendar of all planned communications with dates, audiences, and owners

## Process

1. For manage-closely stakeholders: weekly or bi-weekly face-to-face (or video) updates. Involve them in decisions before they are made. Share problems early, not after they are resolved.
2. For keep-satisfied stakeholders: monthly written summaries. Highlight outcomes, not process. Flag anything that might concern them before it becomes a surprise.
3. For keep-informed stakeholders: bi-weekly email or newsletter. Share progress, answer questions, and acknowledge their input. They are your advocates if treated well.
4. For monitor stakeholders: quarterly summary or inclusion in broader organizational updates. No special communication unless something changes their power or interest.
