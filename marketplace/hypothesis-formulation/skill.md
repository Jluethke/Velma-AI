# Hypothesis Formulation

Convert knowledge gaps into one or more testable hypotheses with clearly defined independent and dependent variables.

## Inputs

- `knowledge-gap-summary`: Identified gaps from the literature search
- `key-findings-digest`: Established results that inform predictions

## Outputs

- `hypothesis-statement`: Formal testable hypothesis with null and alternative forms
- `variable-definitions`: Independent, dependent, and controlled variables with measurement criteria
- `prediction-set`: Specific measurable predictions that follow from the hypothesis

## Process

1. Identify the most promising knowledge gap as the focal question
2. State the null and alternative hypotheses in falsifiable terms
3. Define each variable and its operational measurement
4. List predictions that distinguish the hypothesis from alternatives
