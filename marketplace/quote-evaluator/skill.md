# Quote Evaluator

Evaluate supplier quotes against the procurement criteria.

## Inputs

- `supplier-shortlist`: Shortlisted suppliers with capability summaries
- `evaluation-criteria`: Criteria for evaluating supplier quotes

## Outputs

- `quote-comparison`: Side-by-side comparison of supplier quotes
- `negotiation-targets`: Target prices and terms for negotiation

## Process

1. Score each quote against the evaluation criteria
2. Normalize pricing to a total cost of ownership basis
3. Identify the top candidates and negotiation targets
4. Produce the quote comparison matrix
