# Ethics Investigation Conductor

Interview witnesses, collect evidence, and analyze findings.

## Inputs

- `case-record`: Logged case record
- `severity-rating`: Severity and scope

## Outputs

- `interview-summaries`: Summaries of all interviews conducted
- `evidence-collection`: Documentary and digital evidence collected

## Process

1. Interview the reporter (if available), the respondent, and witnesses.
2. Collect relevant documents, emails, and system logs.
3. Maintain confidentiality and document the chain of custody.
4. Identify patterns and corroborating evidence.
