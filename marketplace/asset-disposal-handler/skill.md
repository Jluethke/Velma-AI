# Asset Disposal Handler

Securely dispose of or recycle a retired asset.

## Inputs

- `asset-record`: CMDB record for the asset
- `refresh-recommendation`: Retirement or refresh decision

## Outputs

- `disposal-certificate`: Certificate of secure data destruction
- `lifecycle-report`: Complete lifecycle report from procurement to disposal

## Process

1. Remove all data using certified wiping or physical destruction.
2. Obtain the certificate of destruction from the disposal vendor.
3. Update the CMDB to mark the asset as disposed.
4. Archive the complete lifecycle record.
