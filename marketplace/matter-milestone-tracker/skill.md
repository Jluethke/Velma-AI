# Matter Milestone Tracker

Track milestones and deadlines throughout the life of the matter.

## Inputs

- `matter-record`: The opened matter file
- `key-dates`: Critical dates and deadlines

## Outputs

- `milestone-status`: Current status of all milestones and upcoming deadlines
- `overdue-items`: List of any overdue or at-risk milestones

## Process

1. Create the milestone schedule from the key dates and add any standard milestones for the matter type.
2. Track completion of each milestone and flag items approaching their deadline.
3. Escalate overdue items to the responsible attorney and supervising partner.
4. Update the matter status as milestones are completed.
