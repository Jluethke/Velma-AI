# Sla Negotiator

Negotiate SLA terms with business stakeholders.

## Inputs

- `sla-drafts`: Draft SLAs
- `measurement-criteria`: Measurement definitions

## Outputs

- `agreed-slas`: SLAs agreed upon by IT and business stakeholders
- `escalation-procedures`: What happens when an SLA is breached

## Process

1. Present the draft SLAs to business stakeholders.
2. Negotiate targets that are achievable and meet business needs.
3. Define the escalation procedures for SLA breaches.
4. Sign off on the agreed SLAs.
