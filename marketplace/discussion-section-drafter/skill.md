# Discussion Section Drafter

Draft the discussion section: interpret the findings, compare with existing literature, acknowledge limitations, and state implications.

## Inputs

- `claim-list`: The findings to interpret
- `literature-context`: Relevant prior work for comparison

## Outputs

- `discussion-draft`: Complete discussion section text
- `limitation-list`: Each limitation with its potential impact on the conclusions

## Process

1. Open with the main finding restated in the context of the original question.
2. Compare each finding with existing literature: consistent, extends, or contradicts.
3. Present limitations honestly, noting which conclusions they do and do not affect.
4. Close with implications: what should change in the field or in practice.
