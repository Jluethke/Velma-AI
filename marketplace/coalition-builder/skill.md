# Coalition Builder

Build a winning coalition: a group of parties whose combined support is sufficient for agreement. Approach allies first, then swing parties.

## Inputs

- `party-profiles`: The party assessments
- `relationship-map`: The relationship structure

## Outputs

- `coalition-strategy`: The target coalition: which parties to approach, in what order, with what proposals
- `sequencing-plan`: The order of bilateral conversations before the full group convenes: build agreements with allies first, then approach swing parties with momentum

## Process

1. Identify the minimum winning coalition: the fewest parties whose agreement is sufficient for the deal to proceed.
2. Start with natural allies: approach parties with shared interests first. Build preliminary agreement on the issues where you align.
3. Approach swing parties with a coalition: 'Parties A, B, and I have agreed on this framework. We would like your input on...' Momentum creates pressure to join rather than block.
4. Isolate opponents: if a party's interests are fundamentally opposed, do not try to convert them first. Build a coalition around them and offer them a face-saving concession last.
