# Constraint Resource Identification

Identify the one resource you cannot add more of that limits your total output.

## Inputs

- `business-description`: What your business or practice does and how it generates revenue
- `resource-inventory`: All resources used in delivering your product or service (people, equipment, time, capital)

## Outputs

- `constraint-resource`: The single resource that limits total output (the one you would add more of first if you could)
- `constraint-capacity`: How many hours of the constraint resource are available per week

## Process

1. List all resources used in delivery. For a solopreneur, the constraint is almost always your own time. For a service firm, it might be senior partner hours. For a manufacturer, it might be a specific machine.
2. The constraint resource is the one where: if you had more of it, you would produce more revenue, and you cannot easily add more. It is the bottleneck.
3. Measure the available capacity: how many hours per week does this resource operate? For a person, it is working hours minus meetings, admin, and other non-revenue time.
4. This number is your denominator. Every product, service, and client will be measured against it.
