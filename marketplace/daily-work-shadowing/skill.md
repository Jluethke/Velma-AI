# Daily Work Shadowing

Shadow or closely interview each top performer about a normal working day. The goal is to see what they actually do hour to hour, not what their job description claims.

## Inputs

- `top-performer-set`: The performers to study
- `selection-rationale`: How they were chosen

## Outputs

- `daily-activity-log`: For each performer, what they actually do across a representative day, grouped into activities
- `time-allocation-map`: Where the real working hours go for the best people, including activities the posting never mentions

## Process

1. For each top performer, log how they spend a representative day or two, grouped into activities.
2. Note where they spend time that the job posting does not mention at all.
3. Record the order in which they do things and what they deliberately choose not to do.
4. Build a map that shows where the real hours go for the best people.
