# Move In Condition Reporter

Complete the move-in condition report with photos.

## Inputs

- `readiness-checklist`: Verified prerequisites
- `outstanding-items`: Any remaining items

## Outputs

- `condition-report`: Documented condition of every room with photos
- `key-handoff-record`: Keys and access credentials provided to the tenant

## Process

1. Walk through every room and document the condition with photos.
2. Note any existing damage or wear.
3. Have the tenant sign the condition report.
4. Hand over keys, fobs, and access credentials.
