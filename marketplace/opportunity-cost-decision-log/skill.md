# Opportunity Cost Decision Log

Log the decision with the full cost analysis. Review past decisions to calibrate your opportunity cost estimates over time.

## Inputs

- `forward-looking-analysis`: The complete forward-looking analysis
- `alternative-uses`: The alternatives that were considered

## Outputs

- `decision-record`: The complete decision record: proposed use, best alternative, direct cost, opportunity cost, true cost, expected value, and the decision
- `review-date`: The date to review the decision and compare the actual outcome to the estimate

## Process

1. Record the decision: what was allocated, to what use, what was the best alternative foregone, and what was the estimated true cost.
2. Record the expected value of the chosen use. This creates a benchmark for the review.
3. Set the review date: at the point when the value of the chosen use should be measurable (end of the project, end of the quarter, end of the year).
4. At review: compare actual value to expected value and actual opportunity cost to estimated opportunity cost. Use the data to improve future estimates.
