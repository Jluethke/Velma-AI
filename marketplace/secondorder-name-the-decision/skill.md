# Secondorder Name The Decision

Define the decision and name the obvious first thing that happens if you take it.

## Inputs

- `decision-statement`: The decision you are weighing.
- `decision-context`: The situation the decision sits in.

## Outputs

- `decision-defined`: The decision stated as a clear choice.
- `obvious-effect`: The immediate result most people would expect.

## Process

1. State the decision as a clear choice between taking an action and not taking it.
2. Write the immediate result that most people would expect.
3. Note why that immediate result looks attractive on its own.
