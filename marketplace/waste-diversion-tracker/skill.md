# Waste Diversion Tracker

Track waste diversion rates monthly and produce the waste management report.

## Inputs

- `implementation-record`: Record of changes implemented
- `training-materials`: Training materials for reference

## Outputs

- `diversion-metrics`: Monthly diversion rate, tonnage by stream, and cost data
- `waste-management-report`: Quarterly waste management report with trends and recommendations

## Process

1. Collect monthly tonnage data from waste hauler reports for each stream.
2. Calculate the diversion rate and compare against the target.
3. Identify any backsliding or contamination trends and address them.
4. Compile the quarterly report with metrics, trends, and next improvement actions.
