# Office Layout Redesigner

Redesign the office layout for hybrid work patterns.

## Inputs

- `hybrid-policy`: The hybrid policy
- `preference-data`: Expected in-office attendance patterns

## Outputs

- `layout-plan`: Updated office layout plan
- `space-booking-system`: Desk and room booking system requirements

## Process

1. Estimate peak and average in-office attendance
2. Design the mix of assigned desks, hot desks, and collaboration spaces
3. Plan the technology for room and desk booking
4. Ensure the layout supports both focused work and collaboration
