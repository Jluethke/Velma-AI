# Post Separation Team Repair

Repair the team after a separation. A departure the team saw coming can restore trust if handled openly within privacy limits; one handled clumsily makes everyone wonder if they are next. Cover the work and address the team without disparaging the person.

## Inputs

- `separation-plan`: The separation plan
- `communication-script`: The communication script

## Outputs

- `team-communication`: What the team is told, promptly and plainly, without private details or disparagement
- `workload-plan`: Who absorbs the departed person's work temporarily and the timeline to backfill or redistribute

## Process

1. Tell the team promptly and plainly that the person has left, without sharing private details or disparaging them.
2. Reassure the team about what happens to the departed person's work and that the process was fair.
3. Plan the coverage: who absorbs what temporarily, and the timeline to backfill or redistribute.
4. Watch for the team's response: strong performers who were carrying the gap should feel relief, not new fear.
