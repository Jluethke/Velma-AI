# Channel Contribution Reporter

Report on each channel's contribution to conversions and revenue.

## Inputs

- `attribution-results`: Validated attribution output
- `validation-results`: Model accuracy context

## Outputs

- `attribution-report`: Channel contribution with spend, conversions, and ROI
- `budget-recommendations`: Recommended budget reallocation based on attribution

## Process

1. Calculate each channel's attributed conversions and revenue
2. Compute ROI per channel
3. Recommend budget shifts based on the results
