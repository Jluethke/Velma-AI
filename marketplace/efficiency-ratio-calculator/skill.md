# Efficiency Ratio Calculator

Calculate efficiency ratios: inventory turnover, receivables turnover, and asset turnover.

## Inputs

- `income-statement`: Revenue and cost of goods sold
- `balance-sheet`: Asset balances for turnover calculations

## Outputs

- `efficiency-ratios`: Turnover ratios with days outstanding calculations
- `cash-conversion-cycle`: Cash conversion cycle in days

## Process

1. Calculate inventory turnover and days inventory outstanding.
2. Calculate receivables turnover and days sales outstanding.
3. Calculate payables turnover and days payable outstanding.
4. Compute the cash conversion cycle (DIO + DSO minus DPO).
