# Campaign Performance Tracker

Track open rates, click rates, and conversions for the campaign.

## Inputs

- `campaign-brief`: KPIs and success criteria
- `send-rules`: Send configuration for reference

## Outputs

- `performance-report`: Open, click, and conversion rates per email
- `campaign-recommendations`: Optimization recommendations based on results

## Process

1. Collect delivery, open, click, and conversion data per email
2. Compare results against the KPIs
3. Identify the top and bottom performing emails and recommend changes
