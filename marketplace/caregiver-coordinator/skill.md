# Caregiver Coordinator

Coordinate with family or caregivers for post-discharge support.

## Inputs

- `service-referrals`: Post-discharge care arrangements
- `barriers-list`: Social or logistical barriers

## Outputs

- `caregiver-plan`: Caregiver responsibilities and schedule
- `support-resources`: Community resources and contact information

## Process

1. Meet with the caregiver to review the discharge plan.
2. Assign responsibilities for medication management and appointments.
3. Provide community resource contacts for additional support.
