# Target Behavior Framing

Frame the specific behavior you are trying to change and gather the history of past attempts, because the history reveals which component keeps failing.

## Inputs

- `behavior-to-change`: The behavior you keep trying and failing to establish or drop
- `change-history`: What you have already tried and what happened each time

## Outputs

- `framed-behavior`: The behavior stated as a single observable action with a context
- `past-attempt-notes`: A short record of each past attempt and the point at which it broke down

## Process

1. State the behavior as one observable action in a context, such as cook dinner at home on weeknights, not eat healthier.
2. List each past attempt and what specifically ended it. The ending is the clue. Did you not know how, lack the time or tools, or simply not want to in the moment.
3. Separate the behavior from the goal behind it. The goal may be served by several behaviors, and you are diagnosing one.
4. Confirm the behavior is within your control. A behavior that depends on another person is diagnosed differently from one that is yours alone.
