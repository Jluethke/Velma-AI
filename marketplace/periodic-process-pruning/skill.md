# Periodic Process Pruning

Review the process every 6 months and remove any step that has not prevented a problem since the last review.

## Inputs

- `final-process`: The current process
- `process-rationale`: Why each step exists

## Outputs

- `pruning-results`: Steps retained (with evidence of value in the past 6 months) and steps pruned (no evidence of value)
- `updated-process`: The process after pruning

## Process

1. For each step, ask: in the past 6 months, has this step prevented a problem? If no one can cite a specific instance, the step may no longer be necessary.
2. Remove steps that have not proven their value in 6 months. If the removal causes pain, add the step back through the pain-driven protocol.
3. Processes accumulate steps over time like barnacles. Without periodic pruning, the minimal viable process gradually becomes the bloated process you started with.
4. After pruning, re-document and re-communicate. The process should always be shorter after a review, never longer, unless new pain has appeared.
