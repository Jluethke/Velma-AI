# Moat Verdict

Reach a verdict: does this business have a real moat, or is it competing on execution alone? Name what to build or deepen so the business is not left defending a position any better-resourced rival can take.

## Inputs

- `moat-scorecard`: The four scores side by side with their evidence
- `dominant-moat`: The strongest moat, or a note that none is strong

## Outputs

- `moat-verdict`: A clear statement of whether a durable moat exists
- `moat-building-priorities`: The ranked moves to build or widen a moat

## Process

1. State the verdict in one line: durable moat, partial moat, or execution-only.
2. If execution-only, say so plainly. Competing on execution means a better-funded rival can eventually win.
3. Pick the one or two moat types with the best chance of becoming durable for this business.
4. List the specific moves to build or widen those moats, ranked by payoff.
