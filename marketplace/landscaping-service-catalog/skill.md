# Landscaping Service Catalog

Define the landscaping service offerings across maintenance, installation, and seasonal work.

## Inputs

- `business-focus`: Primary focus areas (maintenance, design/build, both)
- `service-region`: Geographic region and climate zone for seasonal planning

## Outputs

- `service-catalog`: Complete service catalog with descriptions, pricing guidelines, and seasonal availability
- `equipment-requirements`: Equipment needed per service type

## Process

1. List the maintenance services (mowing, edging, trimming, fertilization, weed control, leaf removal).
2. List the installation services (planting, mulching, sod, hardscaping, irrigation, drainage).
3. List the seasonal services (spring cleanup, fall cleanup, snow removal, holiday lighting).
4. Set pricing guidelines for each service by property size or job scope.
