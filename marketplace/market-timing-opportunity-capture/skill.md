# Market Timing Opportunity Capture

Capture the opportunity and the product idea so the timing signals have something concrete to test. Timing is judged against a specific offering for a specific market, not a vague theme.

## Inputs

- `opportunity-description`: The problem and the market opportunity
- `product-idea`: The product or service you would build
- `target-market`: Who would buy and roughly how many

## Outputs

- `opportunity-profile`: A tight profile of the opportunity and offering
- `timing-question-set`: The three timing questions framed for this opportunity

## Process

1. State the opportunity: the problem, who has it, and why it matters now.
2. Describe the product idea you would bring to it.
3. Frame the three timing questions for this specific case: workarounds, competitor entries, enabling technology.
4. Keep the profile concrete so each signal can be answered with evidence.
