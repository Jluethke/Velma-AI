# Translator Positioner

Use the combined depth and breadth to position yourself as a translator between specialties.

## Inputs

- `depth-plan`: A sequenced plan for the core area
- `breadth-plan`: A plan for reaching literacy in each adjacent area

## Outputs

- `translator-role`: The bridging role your depth and breadth create
- `t-shape-roadmap`: The full T-shaped learning roadmap

## Process

1. Identify where your depth and breadth let you bridge two groups.
2. Describe the translator role this creates and its value.
3. Compile the full plan into a T-shaped learning roadmap.
4. Set the first milestone for both depth and breadth.
