# Equipment Procurement Planner

Build a prioritized equipment procurement list with specifications, vendor comparison, installation requirements, and phased purchasing plan.

## Inputs

- `research-program`: Planned research directions and methods requiring equipment
- `startup-budget`: Available startup funds and any matching or shared equipment programs
- `lab-space`: Physical lab space dimensions, utilities, and infrastructure

## Outputs

- `equipment-list`: Prioritized list with specifications, estimated costs, and vendors
- `procurement-timeline`: Phased purchasing plan aligned with budget availability and lead times
- `installation-requirements`: Power, ventilation, water, and space requirements for each major item

## Process

1. List all equipment needed for planned research, categorized by priority (essential, important, nice-to-have)
2. Research specifications and get quotes from at least two vendors per major item
3. Identify shared or core facility equipment that does not need purchasing
4. Create a phased purchasing plan matching the budget release schedule
5. Document installation requirements for facilities planning
