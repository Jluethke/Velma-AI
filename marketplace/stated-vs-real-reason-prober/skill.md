# Stated Vs Real Reason Prober

For each major cluster, probe whether the stated reason is the real reason. People leaving because of a bad manager often cite 'other opportunity' because it is safer. The real reason is usually one layer deeper. Cross-reference the stated reasons within each cluster against the manager, team, and tenure data.

## Inputs

- `cluster-report`: The identified clusters
- `base-rate-comparison`: Statistical significance of each cluster

## Outputs

- `real-reason-hypotheses`: For each major cluster, the hypothesized real reason for departure, with supporting evidence from the cross-referencing
- `validation-suggestions`: How to validate each hypothesis (e.g., anonymous survey of current team, manager 360 review)

## Process

1. For each major cluster, look at the stated reasons. If 4 people left the same manager but cited 4 different reasons (compensation, growth, opportunity, work-life), the real reason is probably the manager, not 4 different things.
2. Cross-reference: did the departures coincide with any changes (new manager, reorg, policy change, lost project)?
3. Look at the performance ratings of the departed. If high performers are leaving and low performers are staying, the environment is pushing out the wrong people.
4. Write a hypothesis for each cluster: 'The real reason for this cluster of departures is [X], because [evidence].'
