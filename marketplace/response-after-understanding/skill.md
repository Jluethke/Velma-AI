# Response After Understanding

Only after confirming full understanding do you respond with your own thoughts.

## Inputs

- `deeper-understanding`: Their full position
- `your-goal`: What you needed from the conversation

## Outputs

- `informed-response`: Your response informed by what you heard, acknowledging their points first
- `conversation-outcome`: What was agreed, decided, or learned

## Process

1. Start by referencing what they said: 'Based on what you described, here is how I see it.'
2. Address their points before making yours.
3. If your position changed based on what you heard, say so.
4. End with a summary of what was agreed or the next step.
