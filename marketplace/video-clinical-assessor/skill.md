# Video Clinical Assessor

Conduct the clinical assessment via video.

## Inputs

- `consent-status`: Consent confirmation
- `patient-record`: Current clinical record

## Outputs

- `assessment-findings`: Clinical findings from the video exam
- `patient-reported-symptoms`: Symptoms described by the patient

## Process

1. Review the chief complaint and history of present illness.
2. Conduct the examination within the limits of video.
3. Document observable findings and patient-reported information.
