# Email Render Tester

Test the newsletter rendering across email clients and devices.

## Inputs

- `newsletter-draft`: The assembled newsletter

## Outputs

- `render-test-results`: Screenshots and issues found across email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, mobile)
- `approved-newsletter`: The final approved newsletter ready to send

## Process

1. Send test emails to preview accounts across major clients.
2. Check rendering on desktop and mobile for each client.
3. Verify all links work and tracking is in place.
4. Fix any rendering issues and get final approval.
