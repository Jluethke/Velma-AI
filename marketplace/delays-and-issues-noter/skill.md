# Delays And Issues Noter

Document any delays, issues, or disruptions encountered during the day.

## Inputs

- `weather-impact-note`: Weather impact on work
- `equipment-issues`: Equipment breakdowns or needs

## Outputs

- `delay-log`: List of delays with cause, duration, and impact
- `issue-actions`: Actions taken or needed to resolve issues

## Process

1. List each delay or disruption with its cause (weather, material, labor, equipment, design).
2. Record the duration and the activities affected.
3. Document the actions taken to mitigate or resolve each issue.
4. Flag any issues requiring owner or architect attention.
