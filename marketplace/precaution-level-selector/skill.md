# Precaution Level Selector

Select the appropriate infection control precautions.

## Inputs

- `risk-profile`: Pathogen and transmission characteristics
- `affected-scope`: Areas at risk

## Outputs

- `precaution-type`: Standard, contact, droplet, airborne, or combination
- `ppe-requirements`: Required personal protective equipment

## Process

1. Select precaution level based on the transmission route.
2. Define the required PPE for each staff interaction.
3. Determine room placement requirements (private, negative pressure).
