# Blue Ocean Create Pass

Decide which brand-new factors to create that the industry has never offered. Created factors are the main source of new demand, because they answer needs the industry taught customers to stop expecting.

## Inputs

- `competition-factors`: The factors the industry competes on
- `raise-list`: Factors already marked to raise

## Outputs

- `create-list`: New factors the industry never offered
- `create-rationale`: The new demand each one opens

## Process

1. Ask what customers work around, tolerate, or go elsewhere for because the industry never addressed it.
2. Look at non-customers: what keeps them out of the market entirely?
3. Define new factors that answer those needs and have no equivalent in the current value curve.
4. For each new factor, name the new demand or new buyer it could bring in.
