# Coach Recruitment Trainer

Recruit, screen, and train volunteer coaches for the youth program.

## Inputs

- `registered-youth`: Number of teams needed based on registrations
- `fee-ledger`: Budget available for coach training materials

## Outputs

- `approved-coaches`: Coaches who passed screening and completed training
- `coaching-assignments`: Coach-to-team assignments

## Process

1. Recruit volunteer coaches from the parent community.
2. Run background checks on all coaching applicants.
3. Provide coaching training (sport fundamentals, age-appropriate instruction, safety, positive coaching).
4. Assign coaches to teams.
