# Stack Placement Test

Test the stack in real days to check that the anchor actually leads into the new habit at the chosen moment.

## Inputs

- `stack-formula`: The after-anchor-then-habit sentence
- `anchor-reliability-ratings`: The reliability of each anchor

## Outputs

- `placement-test-result`: Whether the new habit actually followed the anchor during a short trial
- `timing-fit-notes`: Notes on whether the moment was too rushed, too tired, or well placed

## Process

1. Run the stack for several days exactly as written and observe whether the anchor pulled the new habit along without a reminder.
2. Watch for the common failure: the anchor fires but the moment after it is rushed or crowded, so the new habit gets skipped.
3. If the habit keeps getting skipped, the placement is wrong even if the anchor is reliable. Move the habit to a calmer anchor rather than trying harder.
4. Note whether the anchor itself is as reliable in practice as you rated it. An anchor that slips on some days needs a backup anchor.
