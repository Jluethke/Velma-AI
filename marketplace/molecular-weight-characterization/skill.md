# Molecular Weight Characterization

Measure molecular weight distribution by GPC/SEC and confirm structure by NMR and FTIR.

## Inputs

- `reaction-protocol`: Synthesis conditions for the batch being characterized
- `conversion-targets`: Expected Mn, Mw, and dispersity

## Outputs

- `mw-distribution`: Mn, Mw, dispersity, and full MWD curve from GPC
- `structural-confirmation`: NMR and FTIR spectra confirming monomer incorporation and end groups

## Process

1. Dissolve polymer in GPC-grade solvent and run against narrow-standard calibration
2. Record Mn, Mw, Mz, and dispersity from the chromatogram
3. Acquire 1H-NMR to confirm repeat unit structure and estimate end-group composition
4. Collect FTIR spectrum and assign key functional group absorptions
