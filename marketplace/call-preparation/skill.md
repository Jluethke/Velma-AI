# Call Preparation

Prepare the account context and the questions before the call, so the time on the call goes to listening.

## Inputs

- `account-context`: What you know about the account and the person before the call
- `call-objective`: The one outcome this call should produce

## Outputs

- `prep-brief`: A short brief on the account, the person, and the likely situation
- `question-bank`: The questions to ask, grouped by situation, problem, and cost

## Process

1. Gather what you know about the account and the person, and write a short brief you can scan before the call.
2. Set one clear objective for the call, since a call with three objectives achieves none.
3. Draft the questions in advance, grouped into situation, problem, and cost, so you are not improvising.
4. Plan to talk for less than a third of the call, and hold the pitch until you have earned it.
