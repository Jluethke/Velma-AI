# Compliance Report Producer

Produce the periodic compliance report for leadership.

## Inputs

- `compliance-dashboard`: Current compliance status
- `escalation-report`: Overdue and at-risk items

## Outputs

- `compliance-report`: Periodic compliance report showing overall health, completed items, risks, and trends
- `compliance-tracker-record`: Complete record of the compliance tracking period

## Process

1. Summarize the compliance status: percentage complete, on-time rate, overdue count.
2. Highlight any material compliance risks or failures.
3. Note regulatory changes that may affect future obligations.
4. Produce the report for leadership review.
