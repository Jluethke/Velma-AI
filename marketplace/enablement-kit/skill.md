# Enablement Kit

Give the champion a kit that lets them sell internally when you are not there.

## Inputs

- `business-case`: The case for change
- `proof-points`: Evidence behind the case

## Outputs

- `champion-kit`: A short, forwardable set of materials the champion can use internally
- `objection-answers`: Prepared answers to the objections the champion will meet inside the room

## Process

1. Assemble a short deck or one-pager the champion can forward without editing.
2. Keep it skimmable, since internal readers give it less attention than the champion does.
3. Write answers to the objections the champion will face from peers, finance, and security.
4. Rehearse the pitch with the champion so they can deliver it in their own voice.
