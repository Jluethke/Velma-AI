# Decisionjournal Record The Decision

Capture the decision and the context around it at the moment you make it.

## Inputs

- `decision-summary`: The decision you are making.
- `decision-date`: When the decision is being made.

## Outputs

- `logged-decision`: The decision written in one or two plain sentences.
- `decision-context`: The situation that prompted the decision and who it affects.

## Process

1. Write the decision in one or two plain sentences.
2. Record the date and the situation that prompted the decision.
3. Note who made the decision and who it affects.
