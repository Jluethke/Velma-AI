# Self Assessment Collector

Collect self-assessments from all employees in the review cycle.

## Inputs

- `review-criteria`: The review criteria
- `review-timeline`: Deadlines for submission

## Outputs

- `self-assessments`: Completed self-assessments
- `completion-rate`: Percentage of employees who submitted on time

## Process

1. Distribute the self-assessment form with the criteria
2. Send reminders as the deadline approaches
3. Track completion rates by team
4. Compile the submissions for manager review
