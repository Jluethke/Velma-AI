# Osint Requirements Definer

Define the intelligence requirements, key questions, target entities, and collection boundaries for the OSINT investigation.

## Inputs

- `investigation-objective`: The goal of the OSINT investigation
- `target-entities`: People, organizations, domains, or assets to investigate
- `legal-boundaries`: Jurisdictional and legal constraints on collection

## Outputs

- `intelligence-requirements`: Prioritized list of intelligence questions to answer
- `collection-plan`: Sources to query and collection schedule

## Process

1. Translate the investigation objective into specific intelligence questions
2. Identify the target entities and their known identifiers (names, domains, emails, handles)
3. Define legal and ethical boundaries for collection
4. Prioritize the questions by value and feasibility
