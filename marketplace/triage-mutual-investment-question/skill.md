# Triage Mutual Investment Question

Ask the second triage question: is the investment mutual, or are you the only one maintaining the relationship? Mutual investment is what distinguishes a relationship worth sustaining through a hard stretch from one that only exists because you keep it alive single-handedly.

## Inputs

- `energy-assessments`: Energy reading per relationship
- `energy-flagged-relationships`: The clearly draining relationships flagged

## Outputs

- `investment-assessments`: Each relationship marked by whether investment is mutual, mostly one-way toward you, or mostly one-way from you
- `one-sided-flags`: Relationships you maintain entirely alone, where the other person never initiates or reciprocates

## Process

1. For each relationship, ask who initiates and who reciprocates. Do they reach out, remember what matters to you, and show up, or does the relationship only happen when you drive it?
2. Be fair about circumstance. Someone going through their own hard period may invest less temporarily while still being genuinely invested. One-sidedness is a concern when it is the enduring pattern, not a season.
3. Distinguish the two directions of one-sidedness. A relationship where you give far more than you get is a different problem from one where someone depends heavily on you, and both differ from mutual investment.
4. Flag the relationships you maintain entirely alone. A relationship that would simply end the moment you stopped initiating is telling you something, and the life change is the moment to decide whether to keep carrying it or let it rest.
