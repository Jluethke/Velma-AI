# Sales Role Profiler

Define the sales roles and their selling motions.

## Inputs

- `sales-org-structure`: Sales team structure and roles
- `revenue-model`: How revenue is generated (new, expansion, renewal)

## Outputs

- `role-profiles`: Each role's selling motion, cycle, and deal size
- `pay-philosophy`: Compensation philosophy and market positioning

## Process

1. List each distinct sales role.
2. Define the selling motion for each (hunting, farming, hybrid).
3. Describe the typical deal size and cycle length.
4. State the target market percentile for total compensation.
