# Counter Anchor Handler

Handle the other party's counter-anchor. Do not let their anchor re-set your expectations. Re-anchor with your own justification.

## Inputs

- `anchor-position`: Your anchor
- `anchor-justification`: Your justification

## Outputs

- `counter-anchor-response-plan`: How to respond if they anchor first: acknowledge, re-frame, and re-anchor with your own justification
- `concession-plan`: How to make concessions from the anchor: each concession is smaller than the last and comes with a reason

## Process

1. If they anchor first: do not counter immediately. Ask how they arrived at their number. This buys time and may reveal weaknesses in their justification.
2. Re-anchor: present your own anchor with justification. Do not treat their anchor as a starting point. Treat it as their opening position and present yours.
3. Make concessions strategically: each concession should be smaller than the previous one (signaling you are approaching your limit) and should come with a reason ('I can move to X because of Y').
4. Never concede without getting something in return. Unreciprocated concessions signal weakness and invite further demands.
