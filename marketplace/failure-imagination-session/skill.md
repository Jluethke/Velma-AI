# Failure Imagination Session

Gather the team and announce: 'It is 6 months from now. The project has failed completely. Write down why it failed.'

## Inputs

- `plan-snapshot`: The frozen project plan
- `assumption-list`: All explicit assumptions

## Outputs

- `failure-stories`: Each participant's independent story of how and why the project failed
- `raw-failure-causes`: All failure causes mentioned across all stories, with duplicates preserved to show frequency

## Process

1. Announce the frame: 'Imagine we are 6 months in the future. This project has failed badly. It is not a small miss; it is a complete failure. I want each of you to write down, independently, the story of how it failed. What went wrong? Why?'
2. Give each person 10 minutes to write in silence. No discussion. Independent writing prevents groupthink and ensures quiet team members contribute their concerns.
3. Go around the room and have each person read one failure cause. Continue rounds until all causes are stated. Do not allow debate or pushback during this phase. The goal is collection, not evaluation.
4. Record every cause mentioned. Preserve duplicates: if three people independently say 'the vendor missed the deadline,' that convergence is a stronger signal than a unique cause mentioned by one person.
