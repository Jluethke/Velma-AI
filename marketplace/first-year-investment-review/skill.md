# First Year Investment Review

Review first year of investing: performance vs expectations, behavioral lessons, and plan adjustments.

## Inputs

- `portfolio`: Current portfolio holdings
- `contribution_plan`: Original contribution plan
- `actual_contributions`: What was actually contributed

## Outputs

- `performance_review`: Returns vs benchmark and expectations
- `behavioral_audit`: Emotional decisions made and lessons
- `plan_adjustments`: Changes for year two

## Process

1. Compare portfolio performance to benchmark
2. Review if contributions stayed on track
3. Identify any panic sells or FOMO buys
4. Check if allocation drifted from target
5. Adjust plan for year two
