# Venue Vendor Coordinator

Secure the venue and vendors for the event.

## Inputs

- `event-concept`: Event format and date
- `event-budget`: Budget allocation

## Outputs

- `venue-contract`: Confirmed venue with terms
- `vendor-contracts`: Confirmed vendors (catering, AV, entertainment, decor)

## Process

1. Research and visit venue options
2. Request proposals from vendors
3. Negotiate contracts within budget
4. Confirm all bookings with signed agreements
