# Loss Determination Reviewer

Review the loss determination and indemnity calculation.

## Inputs

- `inspection-record`: Adjuster findings
- `policy-details`: Coverage levels and price elections

## Outputs

- `indemnity-calculation`: Expected payment amount based on the determination
- `discrepancy-list`: Any disagreements with the adjuster findings

## Process

1. Review the adjuster's yield appraisal and damage assessment.
2. Verify the indemnity calculation against policy terms.
3. Compare the determination to your own loss estimate.
4. List any discrepancies for potential discussion or appeal.
