# Model Combination Practice

Practice combining two or more models on a single decision to produce deeper insight than any one model alone.

## Inputs

- `top-5-models`: Your most useful models
- `model-rankings`: Full rankings with evidence

## Outputs

- `combination-patterns`: 2-3 model combinations that work well together in your domain (e.g., inversion + second-order thinking for risk analysis)
- `combination-practice-plan`: A plan for applying model combinations to upcoming decisions

## Process

1. Take a complex upcoming decision and apply your top 5 models to it sequentially. Note where one model's output becomes another model's input.
2. Identify natural pairings: inversion + second-order thinking (what would fail, and then what?), Pareto + opportunity cost (which 20% matters most, and what am I giving up?), first principles + margin of safety (what is true, and how much buffer do I need?).
3. Record 2-3 combinations that produced richer insight together than either model alone. These become your multi-model workflows for complex decisions.
4. Practice applying these combinations to one complex decision per month. The goal is for model selection and combination to become automatic, not effortful.
