# Patient Education Provider

Provide discharge education to the patient and caregivers.

## Inputs

- `discharge-med-list`: Discharge medications
- `followup-appointments`: Scheduled follow-ups

## Outputs

- `education-materials`: Printed or digital discharge instructions
- `understanding-confirmation`: Documentation of patient comprehension

## Process

1. Explain the diagnosis, treatment, and expected recovery.
2. Review medication changes and administration instructions.
3. Describe warning signs that require immediate medical attention.
