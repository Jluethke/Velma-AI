# Escalation Review Analyst

Review completed escalations to identify patterns and improvements.

## Inputs

- `resolution-log`: Record of escalation resolutions
- `customer-updates`: Communication history during escalation

## Outputs

- `escalation-report`: Analysis of escalation patterns and root causes
- `improvement-recommendations`: Suggested changes to reduce future escalations

## Process

1. Categorize escalations by root cause.
2. Calculate average resolution time by escalation type.
3. Identify recurring patterns that could be prevented.
4. Produce the escalation review report with recommendations.
