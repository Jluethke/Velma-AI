# Sponsorship Publish Reporter

Publish the sponsored content and report performance to the brand.

## Inputs

- `approved-content`: The approved sponsored content
- `deliverable-schedule`: The publication timeline

## Outputs

- `performance-report`: Metrics report (views, clicks, conversions, engagement) for the brand
- `sponsorship-deal-record`: Complete record of the deal from inquiry to performance

## Process

1. Publish the content at the scheduled time with proper disclosure (ad, sponsored, partnership).
2. Track performance metrics over the reporting period.
3. Compile the performance report for the brand.
4. Send the report and invoice, and document the deal for future reference.
