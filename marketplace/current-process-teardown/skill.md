# Current Process Teardown

Document the current process exactly as it runs today (not how it is supposed to run) and measure how much of it is actually followed.

## Inputs

- `process-to-redesign`: The recurring task or workflow you want to streamline
- `stated-process`: The official process documentation (if any)

## Outputs

- `actual-process`: The process as people actually execute it (observe, do not ask; people describe the ideal, not the real)
- `compliance-rate`: What percentage of the official process is actually followed (typically 40-60%)

## Process

1. Observe the process being executed 3-5 times. Watch what people actually do, not what they say they do. The gap between stated and actual process is where waste hides.
2. Document each step in the actual process, including workarounds, shortcuts, and skipped steps. These are not violations; they are signals that the skipped steps were unnecessary.
3. Calculate the compliance rate: what percentage of the official process steps are actually performed? Steps below 50% compliance are candidates for removal (if people skip them and outcomes are fine, the steps are waste).
4. Identify which steps, when skipped, cause problems. These are the essential steps. All other steps are candidates for removal.
