# Daily Concern Flagger

Note any health, behavior, or safety concerns observed during the day.

## Inputs

- `activity-log`: The day's activity log for all children

## Outputs

- `concern-flags`: Any health symptoms, behavioral concerns, or incidents requiring parent notification
- `positive-highlights`: Positive moments to share with parents (achievements, kindness, creativity)

## Process

1. Review the activity log for any health signs (fever, rash, lethargy, reduced appetite).
2. Note any behavioral concerns (biting, persistent crying, withdrawal).
3. Identify positive highlights worth sharing with the family.
4. Flag any items requiring immediate parent contact versus end-of-day reporting.
