# Policy Binder

Bind the policy on acceptance and issue the binder or policy documents.

## Inputs

- `formal-quote`: The accepted quote
- `underwriting-decision`: The approved underwriting decision

## Outputs

- `bound-policy`: The bound policy with effective date, policy number, and coverage confirmation
- `underwriting-record`: Complete underwriting file for the policy

## Process

1. Confirm acceptance from the broker or applicant.
2. Issue the binder with the effective date and policy number.
3. Generate the policy documents and deliver to the insured.
4. Close the underwriting file and archive the record.
