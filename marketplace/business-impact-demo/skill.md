# Business Impact Demo

Demonstrate the business impact achieved through each vulnerability chain.

## Inputs

- `chain-pocs`: Successful proof-of-concepts
- `success-criteria`: What was achieved

## Outputs

- `impact-demonstrations`: Business impact evidence for each chain
- `data-accessed`: What data or systems the chain provided access to

## Process

1. Document what business data or systems each chain reaches
2. Quantify the impact: records accessible, privilege level achieved, systems compromised
3. Compare the chained impact against the individual finding severities
