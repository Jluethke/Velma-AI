# Form Migration Executor

Migrate from paper to digital with user training and transition support.

## Inputs

- `built-form`: The tested digital form
- `test-results`: Test results confirming the form works

## Outputs

- `migration-record`: Record of the migration: old form retired, new form live, users trained
- `digitization-report`: Before and after comparison of processing time, error rate, and cost

## Process

1. Train users on the new digital form.
2. Run both paper and digital in parallel during the transition period.
3. Retire the paper form once the digital version is validated in production.
4. Measure the improvement and produce the digitization report.
