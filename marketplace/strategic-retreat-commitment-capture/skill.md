# Strategic Retreat Commitment Capture

Capture the current commitment, its goal, and the resources tied up in it. You can only define retreat triggers against a clearly stated commitment and a clear measure of the resources at stake.

## Inputs

- `commitment-description`: The project, market, or bet you are committed to
- `stated-goal`: What the commitment is meant to achieve
- `committed-resources`: The money, people, and time currently tied up

## Outputs

- `commitment-profile`: The commitment and its goal stated clearly
- `resource-ledger`: The resources tied up and their rough value

## Process

1. State the commitment and the goal it is meant to reach, in plain terms.
2. List the resources tied up: money, people, time, and attention.
3. Estimate the rough value of those resources if they were free to use elsewhere.
4. Keep this factual. Triggers and judgments come in the next steps.
