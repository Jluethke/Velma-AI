# Stakeholder Map Maintenance

Stakeholder influence and interest shift over time. A quarterly review catches shifts before they cause a communication failure.

## Inputs

- `scored-roster`: The current stakeholder map
- `communication-plan-per-quadrant`: The current communication strategy

## Outputs

- `review-schedule`: When and how to review the map (quarterly or at major milestones)
- `shift-indicators`: Signals that a stakeholder's influence or interest has changed (new questions, missed meetings, new budget authority, org changes)

## Process

1. Set a quarterly review cadence tied to project milestones or organizational changes.
2. At each review, re-score each stakeholder. Has their authority changed? Have they started or stopped paying attention?
3. Define shift indicators: a stakeholder who starts asking detailed questions has moved from low-interest to high-interest. A stakeholder who stops attending meetings has moved from high-interest to low-interest.
4. When a stakeholder changes quadrants, update their communication plan immediately. The lag between quadrant shift and communication update is where failures happen.
