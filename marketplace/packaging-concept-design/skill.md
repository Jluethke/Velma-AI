# Packaging Concept Design

Develop packaging design concepts based on the specs and brand guidelines.

## Inputs

- `packaging-specs`: The physical specifications and constraints
- `brand-guidelines`: Brand visual identity and design guidelines

## Outputs

- `concept-mockups`: Two to three packaging concept mockups with rationale
- `selected-concept`: The chosen concept with stakeholder approval

## Process

1. Research competitor packaging and category conventions.
2. Design two to three concepts that meet the specs and align with the brand.
3. Create 3D mockups for each concept to show how they look on shelf.
4. Present to stakeholders and select the final concept.
5. Produce the concept mockups and selected concept.
