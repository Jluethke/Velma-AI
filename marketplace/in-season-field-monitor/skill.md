# In Season Field Monitor

Monitor field conditions in-season using remote sensing or scouting.

## Inputs

- `field-boundary-map`: GPS field boundaries
- `management-zones`: Zone delineations for comparison

## Outputs

- `vegetation-index-maps`: NDVI or other index maps from drone or satellite imagery
- `in-season-issues`: Areas of concern identified from imagery or scouting

## Process

1. Acquire drone or satellite imagery at key growth stages.
2. Generate vegetation index maps (NDVI, NDRE).
3. Compare index values to management zone expectations.
4. Flag areas for ground-truthing and targeted scouting.
