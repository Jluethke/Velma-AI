# Remediation Roadmap Builder

Create the remediation roadmap prioritizing fixes by risk, effort, and dependency relationships.

## Inputs

- `deduplicated-findings`: Findings to prioritize for remediation
- `root-cause-map`: Root causes to group remediation efforts
- `executive-summary`: Context for aligning remediation with business priorities

## Outputs

- `remediation-roadmap`: Prioritized remediation plan with timelines
- `pentest-report`: Final assembled penetration test report

## Process

1. Prioritize remediation by severity, exploitability, and business impact
2. Group fixes by root cause to maximize remediation efficiency
3. Estimate effort for each remediation action (quick fix, sprint, project)
4. Assemble the complete report with executive summary, findings, and roadmap
