# Batna Strengthener

Strengthen your BATNA before negotiating. A stronger BATNA gives you more power at the table.

## Inputs

- `batna`: Your current best alternative
- `negotiation-context`: The negotiation context

## Outputs

- `strengthening-actions`: Specific actions to improve your BATNA before the negotiation: create new alternatives, improve existing ones, or reduce the cost of exercising them
- `updated-batna`: The BATNA after strengthening, with the new reservation price

## Process

1. Create new alternatives: can you bring additional options into existence before the negotiation? Get another job offer. Find another supplier. Develop an internal capability.
2. Improve existing alternatives: can you make your best alternative more attractive? Negotiate better terms with the backup option. Reduce the switching cost.
3. Reduce the cost of exercising the BATNA: can you make walking away cheaper or easier?
4. Every improvement to the BATNA raises your reservation price and gives you more negotiating power.
