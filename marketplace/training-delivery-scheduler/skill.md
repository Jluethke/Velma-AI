# Training Delivery Scheduler

Plan delivery and enrollment so each audience segment receives its modules through a suitable channel on a workable schedule.

## Inputs

- `training-modules`: The lessons ready for delivery.
- `audience-segments`: The staff groups and their constraints.

## Outputs

- `delivery-schedule`: Dates, channels, and durations for delivering each module to each segment.
- `enrollment-plan`: Assignment of staff to modules with reminders and completion deadlines.

## Process

1. Pick a delivery channel for each segment that fits its schedule and setting.
2. Lay out dates and durations that avoid known blackout periods.
3. Assign each staff group to its modules with a completion deadline.
4. Set reminder points and an escalation path for overdue completions.
