# Bess Investment Scoper

Define the investment and total its cost.

## Inputs

- `investment-context`: The investment under consideration and what it should produce
- `planning-horizon`: The period the decision must fit inside

## Outputs

- `investment-definition`: The investment and the outcome it targets
- `total-cost`: The upfront cost including setup, fees, and one-time labor

## Process

1. Describe the investment and what it is meant to produce.
2. Total the upfront cost including setup, fees, and one-time labor.
3. State the planning horizon the decision must fit inside.
