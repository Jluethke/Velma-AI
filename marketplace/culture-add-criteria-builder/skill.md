# Culture Add Criteria Builder

Convert the missing perspectives into hiring criteria. Each missing perspective becomes a screening question. Instead of 'is this person a culture fit?' (which means 'is this person like us?'), the questions become 'does this person bring [specific missing perspective]?' This makes the hiring decision evidence-based rather than vibes-based.

## Inputs

- `missing-perspectives`: What the team lacks
- `blind-spot-examples`: Concrete consequences of the gaps

## Outputs

- `culture-add-criteria`: Specific, assessable criteria for each missing perspective, written as things you can observe in a candidate's background, portfolio, or interview responses
- `screening-questions`: Interview questions designed to surface each missing perspective

## Process

1. For each missing perspective, write a criterion that is observable: 'Has worked directly with [customer type the team currently does not understand]' or 'Has experience in [industry the team has not touched]' or 'Approaches problems by [method nobody on the team uses].'
2. For each criterion, write an interview question that surfaces it. Not 'are you analytical?' but 'Tell me about a time you solved a problem that your team thought was unsolvable. What did you see that they did not?'
3. Test the questions: would a candidate who matches the criterion give a different answer than a candidate who does not? If the question does not discriminate, rewrite it.
