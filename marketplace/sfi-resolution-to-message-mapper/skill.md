# Sfi Resolution To Message Mapper

Write the resolution and map it back to the core message so the point lands without a lecture. The resolution shows how the struggle ends, and a well-built story lets the audience draw the message themselves. Stating the moral too bluntly after a strong story insults the audience and weakens the effect.

## Inputs

- `struggle-arc`: The attempts and setbacks leading to the resolution
- `turning-point`: The shift that made resolution possible
- `core-message`: The idea the resolution must deliver

## Outputs

- `resolution`: How the protagonist's problem is resolved, shown rather than summarized
- `message-landing`: How the resolution connects to the core message, with the right amount said aloud

## Process

1. Write the resolution as an outcome shown through what the protagonist does and gains, not as a summary.
2. Connect the resolution to the core message. The way the problem was solved should be the proof of your idea.
3. Decide how much to state outright. If the story carries the message well, one short line at the end is enough. If the link is subtle, say slightly more.
4. Check that the resolution delivers the change you sought. If the audience would finish the story and still not shift, the resolution is not aimed at the message.
