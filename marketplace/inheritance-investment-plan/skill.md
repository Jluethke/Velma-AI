# Inheritance Investment Plan

Build an investment plan for inherited funds aligned to goals, timeline, and risk tolerance.

## Inputs

- `remaining_funds`: Amount available to invest
- `financial_goals`: What the money should accomplish
- `risk_tolerance`: Comfort with market risk
- `existing_portfolio`: Current investments if any

## Outputs

- `investment_plan`: How to invest the inheritance
- `allocation_strategy`: Asset allocation for inherited funds
- `implementation_timeline`: Dollar-cost averaging or lump sum plan

## Process

1. Define goals for the money (retirement, house, education)
2. Assess risk tolerance in context of existing portfolio
3. Design allocation matching goals and timeline
4. Decide lump sum vs dollar-cost averaging
5. Plan tax-efficient account placement
