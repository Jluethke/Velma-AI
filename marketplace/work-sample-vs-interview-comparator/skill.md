# Work Sample Vs Interview Comparator

Collect interviewer predictions separately and compare them against the work sample scores. Place each candidate on a grid of high or low work sample against high or low interview impression. The divergent cases teach you the most.

## Inputs

- `blinding-steps`: The blinding process used
- `inter-rater-resolution`: How scoring disagreements were resolved

## Outputs

- `signal-comparison-grid`: Each candidate placed on a grid of work sample score against interview impression
- `divergence-cases`: The candidates where the two signals disagree, each with a recommendation

## Process

1. After any interview, have each interviewer record a prediction (succeed, unsure, or not) with a one-line reason, before seeing the work sample scores.
2. Plot each candidate with the work sample score on one axis and the interview impression on the other.
3. A high sample with a low interview often marks a strong hire that a conventional process would reject on surface factors. Investigate what drove the low impression.
4. A low sample with a high interview marks someone who interviews well but may underperform. Investigate before advancing them.
