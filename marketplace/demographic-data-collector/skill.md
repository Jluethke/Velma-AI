# Demographic Data Collector

Collect workforce demographic data for DEI analysis.

## Inputs

- `data-scope`: Which demographic dimensions to analyze
- `reporting-period`: Time period for the analysis

## Outputs

- `demographic-dataset`: Workforce demographics by level, function, and tenure
- `data-completeness`: Assessment of data quality and gaps

## Process

1. Extract demographic data from HRIS (self-reported categories)
2. Assess data completeness and identify gaps
3. Ensure privacy protections (minimum group sizes for reporting)
4. Organize by level, function, location, and tenure
