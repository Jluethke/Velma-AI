# Campaign Performance Reporter

Produce the campaign performance report with trends, benchmarks, and recommendations.

## Inputs

- `raw-metrics`: Engagement metrics
- `conversion-data`: Conversion and revenue data

## Outputs

- `performance-report`: Report: per-campaign results, trends over time, benchmarks, top and bottom performers
- `optimization-recommendations`: What to change: subject lines, send times, segments, content, frequency

## Process

1. Compare each campaign to historical averages and industry benchmarks.
2. Identify trends: improving, declining, or flat metrics over the reporting period.
3. Highlight the top-performing and underperforming campaigns with reasons.
4. Recommend specific optimizations for the next send cycle.
