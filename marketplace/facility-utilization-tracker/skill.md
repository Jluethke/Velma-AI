# Facility Utilization Tracker

Track facility utilization rates and produce reports for planning and budgeting.

## Inputs

- `conflict-resolution-protocol`: Operating procedures for the system
- `waitlist-procedures`: Demand indicators from waitlist data

## Outputs

- `utilization-report`: Utilization rates by space, time period, and user group
- `demand-analysis`: Analysis of peak demand, waitlist frequency, and unmet demand

## Process

1. Collect booking data (reservations, cancellations, no-shows) for the reporting period.
2. Calculate utilization rate for each space and time slot.
3. Analyze waitlist data to identify unmet demand.
4. Produce the utilization and demand report.
