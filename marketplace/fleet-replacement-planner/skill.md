# Fleet Replacement Planner

Plan fleet replacement based on age, cost, and reliability data.

## Inputs

- `aging-report`: Vehicle age and remaining useful life estimates
- `fuel-report`: Fuel consumption and cost per vehicle and route

## Outputs

- `replacement-plan`: Vehicles scheduled for replacement with timing and budget
- `fleet-management-report`: Fleet management report with cost, utilization, and replacement plan

## Process

1. Identify vehicles past their economic useful life
2. Calculate total cost of ownership versus replacement cost
3. Prioritize replacements by cost impact and reliability risk
4. Produce the replacement plan and fleet management report
