# Needs Statement Drafter

Draft the needs statement grounded in data and community voice.

## Inputs

- `project-summary`: Description of the project
- `funder-profile`: Funder priorities and focus areas

## Outputs

- `needs-statement`: Data-backed statement of the problem
- `supporting-data`: Statistics and citations used

## Process

1. Define the problem with quantitative data (prevalence, trends, disparities)
2. Include the community perspective and lived experience
3. Frame the need in terms that match the funder's priorities
4. Keep the statement focused and free of jargon
