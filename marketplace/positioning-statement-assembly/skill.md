# Positioning Statement Assembly

Assemble the subtractive positioning statement and the pillars that carry it through all messaging.

## Inputs

- `refusal-statements`: The statements of what you will not do
- `attracted-segments`: The buyers each refusal draws in

## Outputs

- `positioning-statement`: A positioning statement built on what you refuse to do
- `messaging-pillars`: The few pillars that every message must support

## Process

1. Combine the strongest refusals into a single positioning statement aimed at the attracted segments.
2. Lead with the constraint, then with what the constraint lets you do better than anyone trying to do everything.
3. Reduce the statement to a few pillars that any piece of messaging can be checked against.
4. Test the statement on a good-fit buyer and confirm they find it clarifying rather than limiting.
