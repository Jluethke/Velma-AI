# Custody Legal Prep

Prepare for custody discussions or mediation: documentation, evidence, and legal framework understanding.

## Inputs

- `state_of_residence`: State for custody law reference
- `relationship_status`: Married, separated, never married
- `primary_concerns`: Safety concerns, relocation, etc.

## Outputs

- `legal_framework`: State custody law basics
- `documentation_checklist`: Evidence and records to gather
- `mediation_prep`: How to prepare for mediation

## Process

1. Research state custody standards
2. Document parenting involvement history
3. Gather school, medical, and activity records
4. Understand mediation process
5. Identify if attorney consultation needed
