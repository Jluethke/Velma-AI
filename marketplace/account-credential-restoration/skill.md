# Account Credential Restoration

Reset credentials and restore the account with strengthened security.

## Inputs

- `cleanup-log`: Confirmation all persistence is removed
- `account-details`: Account information for restoration

## Outputs

- `restoration-confirmation`: Account restored with new credentials and security controls
- `user-notification`: Notification sent to the account owner

## Process

1. Reset the account password to a new strong credential
2. Enable or verify multi-factor authentication
3. Re-enable the account and verify the owner can access it
4. Notify the account owner with details of the incident and protective steps taken
