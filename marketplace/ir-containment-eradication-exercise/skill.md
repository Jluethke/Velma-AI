# Ir Containment Eradication Exercise

Exercise containment and eradication procedures to stop the attack and remove the attacker's access.

## Inputs

- `triage-walkthrough`: Triage decisions and scope assessment
- `escalation-findings`: Escalation findings

## Outputs

- `containment-assessment`: Assessment of containment effectiveness and speed
- `eradication-gaps`: Gaps in the ability to fully remove the attacker's presence

## Process

1. Walk through containment options (isolate hosts, block IPs, disable accounts, segment network)
2. Assess the blast radius of each containment action (business impact, collateral disruption)
3. Exercise eradication steps: remove malware, patch the entry point, reset compromised credentials
4. Check for persistence mechanisms that would survive initial remediation
