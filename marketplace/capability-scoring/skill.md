# Capability Scoring

Score your capability to perform the behavior, covering both the physical skill and the psychological knowledge it requires.

## Inputs

- `framed-behavior`: The behavior stated as an observable action
- `past-attempt-notes`: The record of past attempts

## Outputs

- `capability-score`: A score for whether you have the skill, knowledge, and physical ability to do the behavior
- `capability-evidence`: The specific gaps in skill or knowledge that lower the score

## Process

1. Ask whether you actually know how to perform the behavior well. Many diet changes fail because the person cannot cook, not because they lack will.
2. Separate physical capability from psychological capability. Physical is the body and skill. Psychological is the knowledge, attention, and self-regulation the behavior needs.
3. Score capability from one to ten, where a low score means you would not know how to do it well even if perfectly motivated and free to act.
4. Record the specific gaps. Not knowing portion sizes, not knowing proper lifting form, or not having the skill to plan a week are concrete and fixable.
