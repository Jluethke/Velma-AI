# Move In Orientation Conductor

Orient the new tenant to the property and produce the move-in checklist.

## Inputs

- `condition-report`: Signed condition report
- `key-handoff-record`: Keys and access provided

## Outputs

- `orientation-confirmation`: Confirmation that the tenant received the orientation
- `move-in-checklist`: Completed move-in checklist with all items signed off

## Process

1. Walk the tenant through property rules, trash, parking, and emergency contacts.
2. Set up utility transfers and confirm the forwarding address.
3. Confirm the tenant has all necessary information and produce the move-in checklist.
