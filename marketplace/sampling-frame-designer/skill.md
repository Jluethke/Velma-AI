# Sampling Frame Designer

Design the sampling frame: who or what is eligible, how they will be selected (simple random, stratified, cluster, multi-stage), and from what population.

## Inputs

- `minimum-sample-size`: How many subjects or units are needed
- `population-description`: The target population and its known structure

## Outputs

- `sampling-design`: The sampling method, strata or clusters, and selection procedure
- `eligibility-criteria`: Inclusion and exclusion criteria for units in the sample

## Process

1. Define the target population and its accessible subpopulation.
2. Choose between simple random, stratified, cluster, or multi-stage sampling based on population structure.
3. If stratified, define the strata and the allocation method (proportional or optimal).
4. Write the eligibility criteria and the procedure for selecting units.
