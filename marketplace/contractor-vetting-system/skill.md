# Contractor Vetting System

Vet and select contractors for home repairs: licensing, insurance, references, and bid comparison.

## Inputs

- `repair_needs`: Work that needs a professional
- `location`: Home location

## Outputs

- `contractor_checklist`: Vetting criteria per trade
- `bid_comparison_template`: How to compare bids apples-to-apples
- `red_flags`: Warning signs to avoid

## Process

1. Identify trades needed per repair
2. Check licensing requirements by state
3. Verify insurance and bonding
4. Get 3 bids minimum per job
5. Check references and online reviews
