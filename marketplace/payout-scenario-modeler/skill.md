# Payout Scenario Modeler

Model payout scenarios and produce the compensation plan document.

## Inputs

- `incentive-plan`: Incentive mechanics
- `pay-mix-structure`: OTE and pay mix

## Outputs

- `payout-scenarios`: Modeled payouts at 80%, 100%, 120%, 150% attainment
- `compensation-plan-document`: Final compensation plan ready for distribution

## Process

1. Model total compensation at four attainment levels.
2. Calculate the cost to the company at each level.
3. Verify that top performers earn meaningfully more than quota.
4. Produce the plan document with examples.
