# Anchor Habit Inventory

Inventory the existing habits that already happen reliably every day, since only a reliable habit can serve as a trigger.

## Inputs

- `new-habit-goal`: The new habit you want to establish
- `daily-routine-description`: A walk through a normal day from waking to sleeping

## Outputs

- `reliable-anchor-habits`: Existing habits that happen at the same time and place almost every day
- `anchor-reliability-ratings`: A reliability rating for each candidate anchor, from rock solid to occasional

## Process

1. List the things you already do every day without deciding to, such as making coffee, brushing teeth, sitting down at your desk, or locking the door.
2. Keep only the ones that happen at a consistent time and place. A habit that floats around the day cannot anchor a new one.
3. Rate each anchor on reliability. An anchor you complete every single day is strong. One you skip on weekends is weak and will leave gaps.
4. Note the exact moment each anchor ends, because that ending is where the new habit will attach.
