# Commitment Escalation Calibrator

Calibrate the escalation rate. Too fast and people feel pushed. Too slow and they lose interest. The right pace matches the value delivery pace.

## Inputs

- `measurement-framework`: Metrics for each step
- `optimization-priorities`: Steps needing improvement

## Outputs

- `calibrated-funnel`: The final funnel with timing, pacing, and escalation calibrated based on actual data
- `funnel-documentation`: Complete documentation of the micro-commitment funnel: every step, its purpose, the value delivered, the commitment asked, and the measurement plan

## Process

1. Match the commitment pace to the value delivery pace. If you deliver value weekly, ask for a commitment weekly. If you deliver value daily, you can ask daily.
2. Test the timing between steps. Some funnels move too fast (3 emails in 3 days asking for more commitment) and some too slow (monthly touchpoints that lose momentum).
3. The calibration is done: when the completion rate at each step is above 50% and the time between steps matches the natural decision rhythm of your audience.
4. Document the final funnel. For each step: what the person does, what they receive, what accumulates, and the metrics that prove it is working.
