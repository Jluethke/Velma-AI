# Delegation Review Loop

Review how the delegation went and feed the result into the next round. Delegation set once and never reviewed either drifts or snaps back to you, and the review also tells you what to delegate next.

## Inputs

- `checkin-schedule`: The check-in schedule
- `cadence-rationale`: The plan to loosen it

## Outputs

- `delegation-review`: For each delegated task, whether the outcome was met and whether the authority and cadence fit
- `next-delegation-set`: The freed time and the next tasks to delegate, starting again from the task grid

## Process

1. At the first natural endpoint, review each delegated task: was the outcome met, and did the authority and cadence fit?
2. Where a task snapped back to you, find why: an unclear outcome, missing authority, or a readiness mismatch.
3. Loosen the cadence on tasks the person now owns reliably, and move them toward the next task.
4. Add newly freed time to the next delegation round, starting again from the task grid.
