# Doer Narration Capture

Record the person who does the task narrating each step as they do it for real. Live narration captures the small moves and decisions that an expert leaves out when describing the task from memory.

## Inputs

- `task-to-document`: The task being captured
- `doer-identity`: The person who normally performs the task

## Outputs

- `narration-recording`: The doer's spoken walkthrough while performing the task
- `task-scope`: Where the task starts and ends

## Process

1. Have the doer perform the real task, not a demonstration, while speaking each action aloud.
2. Ask them to voice the small judgments they make, not only the visible steps.
3. Mark the clear start and end of the task so the scope is bounded.
4. Capture the recording in full before any editing or cleanup.
