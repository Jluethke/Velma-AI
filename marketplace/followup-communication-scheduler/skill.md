# Followup Communication Scheduler

Schedule post-discharge follow-up communication.

## Inputs

- `caregiver-plan`: Caregiver responsibilities
- `understanding-confirmation`: Patient comprehension status

## Outputs

- `communication-schedule`: Timing and method of post-discharge check-ins
- `discharge-plan-document`: Complete discharge plan for the medical record

## Process

1. Schedule a follow-up phone call within 48 hours of discharge.
2. Set up appointment reminders.
3. Finalize and file the discharge plan in the medical record.
