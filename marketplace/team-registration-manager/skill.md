# Team Registration Manager

Manage team registrations, fees, and roster submissions for the league.

## Inputs

- `league-structure`: Divisions and eligibility criteria from league setup
- `eligibility-criteria`: Requirements for participation

## Outputs

- `registered-teams`: List of registered teams with rosters and division assignments
- `registration-ledger`: Record of fees collected and registration status per team

## Process

1. Open registration and collect team applications with rosters.
2. Verify each team and player against the eligibility criteria.
3. Collect registration fees and assign teams to divisions.
4. Close registration and produce the registration ledger.
