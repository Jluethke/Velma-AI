# Nps Trend Reporter

Track NPS trends over time and produce the program report.

## Inputs

- `nps-score`: The current NPS score
- `followup-log`: Record of detractor follow-ups
- `recovery-rate`: Detractor recovery rate

## Outputs

- `nps-trend-report`: NPS score over time with segment breakdowns and commentary themes
- `nps-program-report`: Full program report with response rates, trends, follow-up outcomes, and recommendations

## Process

1. Plot the NPS score over time (monthly or quarterly) with segment breakdowns.
2. Identify statistically significant changes and correlate with business events.
3. Summarize the top themes from open-text comments by promoter and detractor groups.
4. Produce the program report with response rates, NPS trends, follow-up outcomes, and recommendations.
