# Alternative Inventory

List every alternative to reaching a deal with this counterparty. What happens if you walk away?

## Inputs

- `negotiation-context`: What you are negotiating: the deal, the counterparty, what you want
- `current-situation`: Your current position if no deal is reached

## Outputs

- `alternatives-list`: Every alternative to this deal: other suppliers, other buyers, doing nothing, building in-house, waiting, or accepting the current situation
- `alternative-ranking`: Alternatives ranked by attractiveness: which is the best realistic option if this deal falls through

## Process

1. Sign up as a real customer, not a demo or trial (if possible). Use a real email, provide real information, and go through the same process any customer would.
2. Evaluate each alternative honestly. The best alternative is the one you would actually take, not the one that sounds best in a negotiation. If your real alternative is 'do nothing and suffer,' that is your BATNA.
3. Rank the alternatives by attractiveness. Your BATNA is the single best alternative. Everything below it is irrelevant; the BATNA is the floor below which you do not accept a deal.
4. Strengthen weak BATNAs before negotiating. If your best alternative is terrible, spend time improving it: get another offer, build a prototype, or develop a relationship with a competitor.
