# Multi Channel Executor

Execute the multi-channel outreach campaign.

## Inputs

- `appeal-packages`: Personalized content per segment
- `channel-assignments`: Delivery channel per donor

## Outputs

- `outreach-log`: Record of all contacts made
- `response-tracker`: Gifts received and pledges made

## Process

1. Send direct mail appeals on the scheduled date
2. Deploy email campaigns with follow-up sequences
3. Execute phone-a-thon for targeted segments
4. Track responses and gifts as they arrive
