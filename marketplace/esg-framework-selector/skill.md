# Esg Framework Selector

Identify the applicable ESG reporting frameworks and their requirements.

## Inputs

- `company-profile`: Description of the company including industry, size, and stakeholders
- `reporting-requirements`: Any regulatory or investor requirements for ESG disclosure

## Outputs

- `selected-frameworks`: The frameworks to report against (GRI, SASB, TCFD, CDP, or others) with rationale
- `disclosure-requirements`: List of specific metrics and disclosures required by each framework

## Process

1. Assess which frameworks are required by regulation, stock exchange listing, or investor request.
2. Evaluate voluntary frameworks that align with the company's industry and materiality.
3. Map the overlapping requirements across frameworks to reduce duplication.
4. Produce the list of specific metrics, indicators, and narrative disclosures needed.
