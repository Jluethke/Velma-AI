# Family Action Tracker

Assign and track action items from family meetings with owners, deadlines, and check-in points.

## Inputs

- `decisions_made`: Decisions from the meeting
- `family_members`: Available assignees

## Outputs

- `action_items`: Tasks with owners and deadlines
- `accountability_system`: How to check on progress
- `next_meeting_date`: When to meet again

## Process

1. Convert each decision to specific action items
2. Assign owners (volunteer first)
3. Set realistic deadlines
4. Choose accountability check-in method
5. Schedule next family meeting
