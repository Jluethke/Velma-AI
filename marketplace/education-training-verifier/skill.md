# Education Training Verifier

Verify education and training credentials.

## Inputs

- `application-package`: Provider's credentialing documents

## Outputs

- `education-verification`: Confirmed degrees, residency, and fellowship training
- `discrepancy-flags`: Any discrepancies between claimed and verified credentials

## Process

1. Contact medical schools to verify degree completion.
2. Verify residency and fellowship training with programs.
3. Confirm any additional certifications or training.
