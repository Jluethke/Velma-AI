# Right Fit Inversion

Invert the worst-fit profile into a sharpened right-fit definition and the criteria that identify it.

## Inputs

- `worst-fit-profile`: The named worst-fit composite
- `screening-questions`: The screening questions

## Outputs

- `right-fit-profile`: The sharpened profile of the buyer you want
- `targeting-criteria`: The three to five criteria that most cleanly separate right fit from worst fit

## Process

1. For each worst-fit trait, write its opposite: what the right-fit buyer expects, needs, and values.
2. Pick the three to five criteria that separate the two profiles most cleanly, since those are your targeting rules.
3. Write the right-fit profile in the same format as the worst-fit one, as a named composite.
4. Confirm a rep could sort a new lead into one profile or the other within five questions.
