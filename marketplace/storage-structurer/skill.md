# Storage Structurer

Store notes so they are found through search and backlinks, not folder hierarchies.

## Inputs

- `linked-notes`: Notes connected to their related notes
- `link-map`: A map of how the notes connect

## Outputs

- `storage-scheme`: A scheme for storing and naming notes
- `retrieval-rules`: How notes are found when needed

## Process

1. Give each note a title that reads as a claim or question.
2. Rely on search and backlinks rather than deep folders.
3. Add tags only where they aid retrieval across clusters.
4. Test retrieval by finding a note three ways.
