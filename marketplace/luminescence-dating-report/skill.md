# Luminescence Dating Report

Compile the luminescence dating results into a report with stratigraphic context, measurement data, and final ages.

## Inputs

- `luminescence-age`: Final age estimate with uncertainty
- `age-model-assessment`: De distribution analysis and age model choice
- `sar-diagnostics`: SAR protocol quality checks

## Outputs

- `luminescence-report`: Full dating report with methods, results, and geological interpretation
- `luminescence-data-archive`: All measurement data, dose rates, and age calculations for archival

## Process

1. Present the De distributions as radial plots and histograms
2. Tabulate all dose rate components and total dose rates
3. Report final ages in their stratigraphic context with figures
4. Discuss any complications (partial bleaching, dose rate changes) and their effect on the ages
