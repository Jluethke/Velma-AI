# Training Audience Analyzer

Analyze the workforce to segment the audience by role, access, and the security risks each group is most exposed to.

## Inputs

- `workforce-profile`: Roles, departments, access levels, and locations across the workforce.
- `risk-focus-areas`: Threats and incidents the program must address, such as phishing, data handling, and account security.

## Outputs

- `audience-segments`: Groups of staff that share a role, access level, and risk exposure.
- `role-risk-map`: Mapping of each segment to the security risks it is most likely to face.

## Process

1. Group the workforce by role, access level, and work setting.
2. Map each group to the threats and incidents it is most exposed to.
3. Flag high-access and high-exposure groups that need deeper coverage.
4. Record any language, location, or schedule constraints that affect delivery.
