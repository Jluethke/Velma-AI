# Board Vote Recorder

Present the resolution for vote and record the outcome.

## Inputs

- `revised-resolution`: The final resolution for vote
- `board-feedback`: Any remaining feedback or discussion points

## Outputs

- `vote-record`: Record of the vote: for, against, abstain, and whether the threshold was met
- `executed-resolution`: The resolution signed by the appropriate officers

## Process

1. Present the resolution at the board meeting or distribute for written consent.
2. Record each director's vote (for, against, abstain).
3. Determine whether the required threshold was met.
4. If approved, have the resolution signed by the secretary or chair.
