# Reldebt Energy Drain Identifier

Identify the unresolved open loops behind the relationships (an unanswered message, an unkept promise, an unspoken apology) because that specific open loop is what turns a dormant relationship into background energy drain, and naming it is the first step to closing it.

## Inputs

- `recovery-worth-scores`: Value and recoverability scores per relationship
- `recovery-candidates`: The relationships worth actively recovering

## Outputs

- `open-loop-list`: The specific unresolved thread behind each neglected relationship, where one exists
- `energy-drain-flags`: Relationships that carry real background energy cost, whether or not you choose to recover them

## Process

1. For each candidate, ask what is actually unresolved. Often there is a specific open loop: a message you never answered, a promise you did not keep, a thank-you you never sent, a conversation that ended badly.
2. Recognize that the open loop, not the mere passage of time, is usually what makes a dormant relationship weigh on you. You avoid reaching out because reaching out means facing the unanswered thing.
3. Flag the relationships that carry genuine energy cost. These are the ones that surface in your mind unprompted, the ones you keep meaning to deal with. That recurring background thought is the debt made visible.
4. Note that some drains attach to relationships you decided not to recover. Those still need closing, because an unresolved loop drains energy even when you do not want the relationship back, which the closure step handles.
