# Incapacitation And Security Section

Build the death or incapacitation section and the security section: will location, digital legacy, breach response, identity theft steps.

## Inputs

- `household-members`: Who lives in the household
- `trusted-persons-list`: People authorized to act on your behalf

## Outputs

- `incapacitation-section`: Location of: will, trust documents, life insurance policies, beneficiary designations. Who has power of attorney (financial and medical). Digital legacy: password manager master access, email recovery, social media memorialization instructions.
- `security-section`: Identity theft response steps (credit freeze, fraud alerts, FTC report). Data breach response steps (change passwords, enable MFA, monitor accounts). Physical security contacts (locksmith, alarm company).

## Process

1. Record the location of all legal documents: will, trust, power of attorney, life insurance policies, deeds, titles. Name the attorney who drafted them.
2. Define the digital legacy: how does a trusted person access your password manager, email, and financial accounts? Write the procedure without exposing the actual passwords in the playbook.
3. Write the identity theft response steps: freeze credit at all three bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion), file a fraud alert, file an FTC Identity Theft Report, notify affected financial institutions.
4. Write the data breach response steps: change passwords starting with email and financial accounts, enable multi-factor authentication everywhere, monitor accounts for unauthorized activity for 90 days.
