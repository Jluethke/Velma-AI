# Major Incident Declarer

Declare a major incident and activate the response.

## Inputs

- `incident-report`: The incident that triggered the major incident declaration
- `impact-scope`: Number of users, services, and business functions affected

## Outputs

- `major-incident-record`: Formal major incident record with severity and scope
- `response-activation`: Response team and communication bridge details

## Process

1. Assess whether the incident meets major incident criteria.
2. Declare the major incident and assign a severity level.
3. Activate the major incident response team.
4. Open the communication bridge (war room or call).
