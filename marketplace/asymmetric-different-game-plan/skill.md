# Asymmetric Different Game Plan

Turn the advantages and avoid zones into a plan to play a different game. The plan concentrates effort where you are asymmetrically strong and steers clear of the fights that favor the competition.

## Inputs

- `asymmetric-advantages`: The overlaps where you are strong and rivals are structurally weak
- `avoid-zones`: The areas where you are weak and rivals are strong

## Outputs

- `positioning-move`: The positioning that leans on your asymmetric advantage
- `game-plan-summary`: The concrete moves and the fights you will refuse

## Process

1. Choose the top-ranked asymmetric advantage as the base of the positioning.
2. Describe a position that makes your advantage central and the rival's strength beside the point.
3. Write the concrete moves that build on the advantage and the fights you will refuse.
4. Check the plan does not quietly drag you back into an avoid zone.
