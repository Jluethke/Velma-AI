# Environment Redesign For Eating

Redesign your food environment: what is visible, accessible, and convenient determines what you eat more than willpower.

## Inputs

- `default-meals`: Your 5 default meals
- `eating-patterns`: Current habits

## Outputs

- `environment-changes`: Kitchen and workspace changes: healthy defaults visible and accessible, unhealthy options hidden or removed
- `snack-defaults`: Default snacks pre-portioned and accessible: nuts, fruit, yogurt, cut vegetables. Replace, do not just remove.

## Process

1. Put healthy foods at eye level in the fridge and pantry. Move less healthy options to harder-to-reach locations. Visibility drives consumption more than preference.
2. Pre-portion snacks. A bag of nuts on the counter leads to eating a handful. A pre-portioned cup of nuts leads to eating a serving. Portion control is an environment problem, not a willpower problem.
3. Replace, do not remove. Removing snacks without replacing them creates a vacuum that willpower must fill. Replacing chips with nuts creates a new default that requires no willpower.
4. If you eat at your desk, stock desk snacks that are nutritious. Hungry people eat what is closest. If the closest option is a protein bar instead of a candy bar, you eat better without deciding to.
