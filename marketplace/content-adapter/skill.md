# Content Adapter

Adapt content for each target platform's format and requirements.

## Inputs

- `repurposing-candidates`: The top content assets to repurpose
- `format-matrix`: The content-to-format mapping

## Outputs

- `adapted-content`: Repurposed content pieces ready for each platform (text, images, video clips, carousels)
- `adaptation-notes`: Notes on what was changed for each platform (length, aspect ratio, tone, CTA)

## Process

1. For each content piece, adapt the text to the platform's tone and character limits.
2. Resize or reformat visuals for the platform's aspect ratio and specifications.
3. Add platform-specific elements (hashtags, tags, captions, alt text).
4. Quality check each adapted piece for accuracy and brand consistency.
