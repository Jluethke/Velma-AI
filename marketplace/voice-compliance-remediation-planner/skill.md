# Voice Compliance Remediation Planner

Build the remediation plan for all compliance findings.

## Inputs

- `accessibility-audit`: Accessibility audit results
- `accessibility-gaps`: Accessibility gaps

## Outputs

- `remediation-plan`: Prioritized remediation plan for all compliance gaps with owner, deadline, and cost estimate
- `voice-compliance-audit-report`: Complete voice compliance audit report covering recording, data handling, accessibility, and remediation

## Process

1. Consolidate all findings from recording, data handling, and accessibility audits.
2. Prioritize by legal risk (fines, lawsuits, regulatory action) and ease of fix.
3. Assign an owner and deadline for each remediation item.
4. Estimate the cost and effort for each fix.
5. Compile the full compliance audit report.
