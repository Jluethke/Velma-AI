# Flash Sale Marketing Prep

Prepare the marketing assets and landing pages for the sale.

## Inputs

- `sale-products`: Products in the sale
- `sale-goals`: Sale timing and messaging goals

## Outputs

- `marketing-assets`: Email, social, and ad creatives
- `landing-pages`: Sale landing page configuration

## Process

1. Design the email announcement and reminder sequence.
2. Create social media and ad creatives.
3. Build or configure the sale landing page with countdown timer.
4. Schedule the marketing sends aligned to the sale window.
