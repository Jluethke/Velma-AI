# Satisfaction Moment Mapping

Find the moments when customers feel the most value, since that is when a referral ask lands.

## Inputs

- `customer-journey`: The stages a customer moves through after buying
- `satisfaction-signals`: Signals of satisfaction, such as results reached, reviews, and renewals

## Outputs

- `peak-moments`: The points in the journey where satisfaction is highest
- `trigger-events`: The observable events that mark each peak moment

## Process

1. Map the customer journey and mark where customers report or show the most value.
2. Identify the events that reliably precede those peaks, such as hitting a first result or renewing.
3. Choose the peaks that are both high in satisfaction and easy to detect in your systems.
4. Avoid asking at neutral moments, since a mistimed ask teaches customers to ignore the ask.
