# Constraint Consistency Check

Check that product, pricing, and copy honor the stated constraints, since a broken promise costs more than no promise.

## Inputs

- `positioning-statement`: The subtractive positioning statement
- `messaging-pillars`: The pillars every message must support

## Outputs

- `consistency-report`: Where the product, pricing, or copy contradicts the stated constraints
- `alignment-fixes`: The changes needed to make the promise and the product match

## Process

1. Walk the product, pricing, and copy and flag anywhere they contradict a stated constraint.
2. Pay special attention to the sales floor, where the pressure to win a deal breaks the constraint first.
3. List the fixes needed so the promise and the product match, with an owner for each.
4. Set a recurring check, since positioning erodes quietly as teams make small exceptions.
