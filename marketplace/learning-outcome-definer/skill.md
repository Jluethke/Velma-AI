# Learning Outcome Definer

Define measurable learning outcomes aligned to standards and needs assessment findings.

## Inputs

- `needs-assessment-report`: Summary of learner needs and skill gaps
- `industry-standards`: Relevant industry or professional standards

## Outputs

- `learning-outcomes-list`: List of measurable learning outcomes
- `outcome-standard-map`: Mapping of outcomes to standards

## Process

1. Draft learning outcomes using action verbs (Bloom's taxonomy)
2. Align each outcome to one or more industry standards
3. Verify outcomes are measurable and assessable
4. Produce the outcomes list and alignment map
