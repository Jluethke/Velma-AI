# Market Intelligence Gatherer

Gather sales and marketing intelligence for the forecast.

## Inputs

- `cleaned-demand-history`: Cleaned historical demand data
- `product-hierarchy`: Product hierarchy and grouping definitions

## Outputs

- `market-intelligence`: Qualitative inputs from sales, marketing, and market research
- `event-calendar`: Planned promotions, launches, and market events

## Process

1. Collect input from sales on pipeline and customer expectations
2. Gather marketing plans for promotions and campaigns
3. Document planned product launches and discontinuations
4. Compile the event calendar with expected demand impact
