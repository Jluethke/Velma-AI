# Interest Uncoverer

Uncover the interests behind both parties' positions. Ask why, not what.

## Inputs

- `negotiation-positions`: What each party says they want
- `negotiation-context`: The relationship, history, and stakes

## Outputs

- `own-interests`: Your underlying interests ranked by importance: substantive (money, terms), procedural (how the process works), relational (the ongoing relationship)
- `counterpart-interests`: Their likely underlying interests, estimated from their behavior, statements, and situation

## Process

1. For your own position on each issue, ask: why do I want this? What underlying need does it serve? What would happen if I got a different position that served the same need?
2. For their position, ask: why might they want this? What need does it serve for them? If you are not sure, ask them directly: 'Help me understand why this is important to you.'
3. Rank your interests by importance. Some are essential (deal-breakers), some are important (strong preference), some are nice-to-have (willing to trade).
4. Look for differences in interest rankings: issues that are high-priority for you but low-priority for them (and vice versa) are where trades create mutual value.
