# Post Incident Review Publisher

Compile and share the post-incident review document and lessons learned.

## Inputs

- `remediation-actions`: Prioritized actions with owners
- `went-well-list`: Actions that worked effectively

## Outputs

- `post-incident-review-doc`: Complete post-incident review document
- `lessons-learned-summary`: Key lessons for broader distribution

## Process

1. Compile the post-incident review document with all sections
2. Write a lessons-learned summary for teams not directly involved
3. Schedule a follow-up review to verify remediation progress
4. Distribute the review to relevant stakeholders
