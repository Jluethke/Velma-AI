# Support Experience Test

Contact the competitor's support team with a real question and document the experience from first contact to resolution.

## Inputs

- `friction-inventory`: Friction points encountered
- `usage-log`: Usage experiences to date

## Outputs

- `support-evaluation`: Full support interaction documented: response time, quality of response, number of touches to resolution, channel used, overall satisfaction
- `support-comparison-notes`: How this support experience compares to your own product's support (where are you better, where are they better)

## Process

1. Submit a real support request about a genuine issue you encountered during the teardown. Use whatever channel they offer (chat, email, phone).
2. Time every step: time to first response, time to resolution, number of back-and-forth messages, and whether the issue was actually resolved.
3. Evaluate the quality: did the support agent understand the problem? Was the solution correct? Did they follow up? Was the experience personal or robotic?
4. Compare honestly to your own support. If they are better in any dimension, note it. Competitive analysis that ignores the competitor's strengths is useless.
