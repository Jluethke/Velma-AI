# Renewal Offer Generator

Generate and deliver renewal offers for expiring policies.

## Inputs

- `endorsed-policy`: The current policy approaching expiration
- `renewal-underwriting-notes`: Underwriting notes on loss experience and any changes for renewal

## Outputs

- `renewal-offer`: The renewal offer with proposed terms, premium, and any changes from the expiring policy
- `renewal-deadline`: The deadline for the insured to accept or decline the renewal

## Process

1. Review the expiring policy terms and loss history.
2. Apply any rate changes, coverage modifications, or underwriting conditions for renewal.
3. Generate the renewal offer with the new premium and terms.
4. Deliver the renewal offer to the insured and agent within the required notice period.
