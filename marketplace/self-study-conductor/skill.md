# Self Study Conductor

Conduct the self-study by evaluating institutional compliance against each standard.

## Inputs

- `standard-map`: Each standard mapped to responsible departments
- `evidence-checklist`: Checklist of evidence needed per standard

## Outputs

- `self-study-findings`: Compliance status and evidence for each standard
- `gap-list`: Standards where evidence is missing or compliance is partial

## Process

1. Collect evidence from each responsible unit
2. Evaluate compliance for each standard (met, partially met, not met)
3. Document findings with supporting evidence
4. Identify gaps requiring remediation
