# Homeschool Schedule Builder

Build a daily and weekly homeschool schedule balancing academics, breaks, activities, and family life.

## Inputs

- `curriculum_choices`: Selected curricula and subjects
- `children_ages`: Ages and grade levels
- `family_schedule`: Work, activities, and commitments

## Outputs

- `daily_schedule`: Typical daily schedule template
- `weekly_plan`: Subject rotation across the week
- `yearly_calendar`: School year calendar with breaks

## Process

1. Set daily start and end times
2. Allocate time per subject by priority
3. Build in breaks and physical activity
4. Plan subject rotation across the week
5. Map out yearly calendar with vacations
