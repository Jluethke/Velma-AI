# Winddown Sequence Design

Design the wind-down sequence that leads into the sleep window, dimming light early and ending screens well before bedtime.

## Inputs

- `target-bedtime`: The target bedtime
- `sleep-window`: The protected sleep window

## Outputs

- `winddown-sequence`: An ordered sequence of wind-down actions tied to times before bedtime
- `winddown-start-time`: The time the wind-down begins, roughly ninety minutes before bedtime

## Process

1. Set the wind-down to begin about ninety minutes before bedtime, and dim the lights at that point. Bright light late tells the body clock it is still day.
2. Stop screens about sixty minutes before bedtime, or at minimum cut their brightness and blue light, because screen light and stimulation both delay sleep onset.
3. Fill the wind-down with low-arousal actions in a consistent order, such as tidying, washing, and reading on paper, so the sequence itself becomes a sleep cue.
4. Keep the sequence short enough to hold on a normal night. A wind-down that demands a perfect evening will be abandoned on the first busy one.
