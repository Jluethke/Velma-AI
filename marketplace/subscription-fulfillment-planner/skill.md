# Subscription Fulfillment Planner

Plan the fulfillment logistics for recurring subscription shipments.

## Inputs

- `packaging-spec`: Packaging specifications
- `pricing-model`: Billing and shipping frequency

## Outputs

- `fulfillment-plan`: Subscription fulfillment workflow
- `shipping-schedule`: Monthly shipping timeline

## Process

1. Define the monthly fulfillment timeline (cutoff, assembly, ship).
2. Set up the assembly process (kitting, quality check, packing).
3. Configure the shipping method and carrier.
4. Plan for subscriber count scaling (warehouse space, labor).
