# Structured Interview Runner

Run structured interviews with consistent questions and scoring.

## Inputs

- `interview-candidates`: Candidates advancing to interviews
- `role-scorecard`: Key outcomes and qualifications

## Outputs

- `interview-scores`: Scores per candidate per competency
- `interview-notes`: Detailed notes from each interviewer

## Process

1. Brief the interview panel on the scorecard and questions
2. Conduct the interviews with consistent question sets
3. Score each candidate independently before debriefing
4. Debrief and calibrate scores across interviewers
