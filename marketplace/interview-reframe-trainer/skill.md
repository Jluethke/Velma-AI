# Interview Reframe Trainer

Train interviewers to evaluate for culture add instead of culture fit. The biggest obstacle is the interviewer's instinct: 'I liked this person' is a fit signal, not an add signal. Train interviewers to ask: 'What does this person bring that nobody on the team currently has?' and to treat discomfort with a candidate's different style as a positive signal, not a negative one.

## Inputs

- `culture-add-criteria`: The specific criteria for what the team needs
- `screening-questions`: The questions designed to surface missing perspectives

## Outputs

- `interviewer-guide`: A guide for interviewers explaining the culture add approach, with the specific criteria and questions for this role
- `bias-checklist`: A checklist interviewers use after each interview to check whether their reaction to the candidate was based on add criteria or on similarity to themselves

## Process

1. Explain the shift to interviewers: culture fit asks 'is this person like us?' Culture add asks 'does this person bring something we lack?' The first produces similarity. The second produces capability.
2. Give interviewers the specific criteria and questions. Their job is to assess the criteria, not to form a general impression.
3. Create the bias checklist: after each interview, the interviewer answers: (1) Did I form my impression in the first 5 minutes? (2) Is my positive or negative reaction based on similarity to me or difference from me? (3) Can I point to a specific criterion this candidate does or does not meet?
4. Practice: run a calibration exercise where interviewers evaluate the same candidate independently and compare their assessments against the criteria.
