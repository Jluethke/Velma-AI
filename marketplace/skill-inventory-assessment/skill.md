# Skill Inventory Assessment

Inventory every skill you use in your work and life, then estimate your percentile in each one relative to the general population.

## Inputs

- `work-description`: What you do for work and what you do outside work
- `skill-self-assessment`: Your honest sense of what you are good at and what you are not

## Outputs

- `skill-inventory`: Complete list of skills with estimated percentile for each
- `top-quartile-skills`: Skills where you are at or above the 75th percentile

## Process

1. List every skill you use regularly: technical skills, communication skills, domain knowledge, interpersonal skills, creative skills, analytical skills.
2. For each skill, estimate your percentile relative to the general working population. Use concrete evidence: formal training, years of practice, feedback received, output quality compared to peers.
3. Flag any skill where you are above the 75th percentile. These are your stack candidates.
4. Flag any skill where you are below the 25th percentile but wish you were better. These are potential growth targets.
