# Battle Card Creator

Create competitive battle cards for the sales team.

## Inputs

- `differentiation-opportunities`: Differentiation strategy
- `feature-comparison`: Feature and pricing data

## Outputs

- `battle-cards`: One-page battle card per competitor
- `positioning-analysis`: Complete competitive positioning analysis

## Process

1. Build a one-page battle card for each top competitor
2. Include strengths, weaknesses, objection handling, and win themes
3. Produce the complete positioning analysis document
