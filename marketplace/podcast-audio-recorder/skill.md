# Podcast Audio Recorder

Record the podcast audio in studio or remotely.

## Inputs

- `episode-outline`: Episode structure and talking points
- `guest-brief`: Guest information and questions

## Outputs

- `raw-audio-tracks`: Separate audio tracks for each speaker
- `recording-notes`: Notes on retakes, timestamps, and issues

## Process

1. Set up the recording environment and test levels
2. Record the episode following the outline
3. Note timestamps for retakes or edits needed
4. Export separate tracks for each participant
