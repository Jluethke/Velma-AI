# Ald Two Week Practice Plan

Build a two-week deliberate-practice plan with checkpoints. Two weeks is long enough to move a habit and short enough to stay focused. Practice needs a daily cue, a running count, and a midpoint check, or it quietly stops.

## Inputs

- `countermeasure`: The specific action you will practice
- `practice-cues`: The signals that trigger the countermeasure
- `evidence-summary`: The baseline counts to measure improvement against

## Outputs

- `practice-plan`: A two-week schedule naming where you practice daily and how you track each attempt
- `progress-checkpoints`: A midpoint and end check comparing new counts against the baseline, with a decision on what to do next

## Process

1. Name the daily setting where you will practice the countermeasure, so practice is scheduled rather than left to chance.
2. Track attempts with a simple count: times the cue appeared, times you ran the countermeasure. Running it is the win, even when the conversation is imperfect.
3. Set a day-seven checkpoint: compare the week's counts to the baseline, and adjust the cue if you keep missing it.
4. Set a day-fourteen check: if the failure dropped, lock the countermeasure in and pick your next failure. If it did not, keep the same failure and change the practice setting, not the target.
