# Relationship Deepening For Retention

While diversifying, deepen the relationship with large customers to reduce the chance of their departure.

## Inputs

- `diversification-plan`: The plan to reduce concentration
- `concentration-table`: Current large customers

## Outputs

- `retention-actions`: Specific actions to make each large customer more embedded: multi-contact relationships, expanded use cases, integration depth
- `early-warning-system`: Signals that a large customer might be at risk of leaving (usage decline, champion departure, competitor engagement)

## Process

1. For each concentrated customer, ensure you have relationships with at least 3 contacts (not just one champion). A single-contact relationship is fragile; that person leaving can end the contract.
2. Expand use cases: if the customer uses you for one thing, help them use you for two or three things. Each additional use case increases switching costs.
3. Set up early warning monitoring: track usage patterns, contact frequency, and satisfaction signals. A 20% drop in usage over 60 days is an early warning.
4. Diversification and retention are complementary, not contradictory. You want to keep large customers AND reduce their share by growing everything else.
