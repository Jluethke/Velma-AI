# Reldebt Recovery Worth Scorer

Decide which dormant relationships are actually worth recovering, scoring each on genuine mutual value and realistic recoverability, so effort goes to relationships that can and should come back rather than to every name on the list out of guilt.

## Inputs

- `last-contact-dates`: The last genuine contact date per person
- `silence-duration-ranking`: The list ranked by length of silence

## Outputs

- `recovery-worth-scores`: Each relationship scored on mutual value and on how realistically it can be recovered
- `recovery-candidates`: The subset worth actively recovering, separated from those to let rest

## Process

1. Score each relationship on value: what did it bring when it was active (meaning, growth, collaboration, support), and is that value still relevant to who you are now? Some relationships were tied to a job or a phase that has passed.
2. Score each on recoverability: how warm was the ending, how long the silence, and whether there is a natural reason to reconnect. A relationship that ended warmly and recently is highly recoverable, one that drifted after a falling-out is not.
3. Separate the genuinely worth-recovering from the guilt-driven. You are allowed to decide a relationship ran its course. Keeping a dead relationship on the active list is itself the debt the audit is trying to clear.
4. Produce the candidate list, deliberately short. Reconnecting well with five people beats sending ten hollow messages, because each real reconnection takes a specific, thoughtful approach.
