# Field Gps Mapper

Map the field with GPS boundaries and management zones.

## Inputs

- `field-location`: Farm and field identifiers
- `existing-maps`: Any prior maps, aerial photos, or soil surveys

## Outputs

- `field-boundary-map`: GPS boundaries for each field
- `management-zones`: Delineated zones based on soil, topography, and yield history

## Process

1. Drive or walk field boundaries with GPS to create the boundary file.
2. Overlay soil survey and topographic data.
3. Delineate management zones from yield maps, soil type, and elevation.
4. Export zone maps in a format compatible with field equipment.
