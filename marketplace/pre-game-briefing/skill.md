# Pre Game Briefing

Brief officials, staff, and team managers on the game day procedures.

## Inputs

- `venue-readiness-report`: Venue status and any issues to communicate
- `equipment-check-record`: Equipment status

## Outputs

- `briefing-record`: Attendance and items covered in the pre-game briefing
- `game-roster-sheets`: Verified rosters submitted by each team

## Process

1. Confirm official and staff attendance and assignments.
2. Review the ground rules, any venue-specific notes, and safety procedures.
3. Collect and verify game rosters from each team.
4. Record the briefing.
