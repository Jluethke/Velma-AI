# Filing Submitter

Submit the filing through the appropriate channel and confirm receipt.

## Inputs

- `approved-filing`: Approved filing document
- `exhibits-package`: Supporting exhibits

## Outputs

- `submission-confirmation`: Confirmation of filing receipt with tracking number
- `submission-record`: Internal record of the submission

## Process

1. Submit through the designated portal, mail, or electronic system
2. Obtain and record the confirmation or tracking number
3. Archive a copy of everything submitted
