# Insider Threat Referral Intake

Receive and assess the insider threat referral and its supporting indicators.

## Inputs

- `referral-details`: The insider threat referral with initial indicators
- `subject-role-info`: Subject's role, access level, and organizational context

## Outputs

- `investigation-scope`: Defined scope and objectives of the investigation
- `initial-risk-assessment`: Preliminary risk level based on the indicators

## Process

1. Review the referral and classify the type of insider threat (theft, sabotage, espionage, negligence)
2. Assess the subject's access level and potential for harm
3. Define the investigation scope, objectives, and legal boundaries
4. Coordinate with legal and HR before proceeding
