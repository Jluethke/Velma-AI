# Template Needs Inventory

Inventory the recurring document types that need templates.

## Inputs

- `department-list`: Departments or teams that produce recurring documents
- `brand-guidelines`: Branding standards (logo, colors, fonts, tone)

## Outputs

- `template-inventory`: List of recurring document types with frequency, audience, and current state
- `priority-list`: Templates ranked by frequency of use and impact of inconsistency

## Process

1. Interview each department to identify their recurring documents (proposals, reports, invoices, letters, presentations, SOWs).
2. For each document type, record how often it is created, who the audience is, and whether a template exists today.
3. Rank the templates by frequency and the cost of inconsistency (client-facing first, internal second).
4. Produce the template inventory and priority list.
