# Churn Rate Calculator

Calculate retention and churn rates by cohort and segment.

## Inputs

- `customer-data`: Customer records with start dates, end dates, and segments
- `analysis-period`: Time period to analyze

## Outputs

- `churn-metrics`: Churn and retention rates by cohort and segment
- `cohort-charts`: Cohort retention curves

## Process

1. Define cohorts by signup period.
2. Calculate the churn rate for each cohort at each time interval.
3. Segment by customer type, size, or product.
4. Produce the retention curves.
