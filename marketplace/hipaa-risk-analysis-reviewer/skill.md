# Hipaa Risk Analysis Reviewer

Review the HIPAA risk analysis covering all safeguard categories.

## Inputs

- `phi-systems`: Systems handling PHI
- `data-flow-map`: PHI data flows

## Outputs

- `risk-findings`: Identified risks to PHI by safeguard category
- `current-controls`: Existing controls mapped to each risk

## Process

1. Evaluate administrative safeguards (policies, training, access management).
2. Evaluate physical safeguards (facility access, workstation security, device controls).
3. Evaluate technical safeguards (access controls, audit logs, encryption, integrity).
