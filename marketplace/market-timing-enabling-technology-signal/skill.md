# Market Timing Enabling Technology Signal

Read the third signal: is the enabling technology in place and affordable enough to build and deliver the product now? Many good ideas fail only because the technology they need is not ready or not cheap yet.

## Inputs

- `opportunity-profile`: A tight profile of the opportunity and offering
- `timing-question-set`: The three timing questions framed for this opportunity

## Outputs

- `technology-readiness`: Whether the needed technology exists and is reliable
- `cost-trend`: Whether the cost to build and deliver is falling to a workable level

## Process

1. List the technologies the product depends on to work and to scale.
2. Check whether each one exists today and is reliable enough to build on.
3. Check whether the cost of those technologies is falling toward a level the market can bear.
4. Rate technology readiness and note the cost trend with evidence.
