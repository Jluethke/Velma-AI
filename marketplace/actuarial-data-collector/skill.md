# Actuarial Data Collector

Collect, validate, and organize the actuarial data.

## Inputs

- `analysis-scope`: Scope and evaluation parameters
- `data-requirements`: List of data sets needed

## Outputs

- `loss-triangles`: Organized loss development triangles (paid, incurred, reported counts) by accident year
- `exposure-and-rate-data`: Exposure data, premium data, and rate change history

## Process

1. Extract loss data from the claims system and organize into development triangles.
2. Collect premium and exposure data for the relevant periods.
3. Gather rate change history and industry benchmark data.
4. Validate the data for completeness and consistency.
