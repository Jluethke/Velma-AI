# Stakeholder Review Coordinator

Coordinate stakeholder review of the policy and collect feedback.

## Inputs

- `aligned-policy`: Framework-aligned policy draft
- `responsibility-matrix`: RACI matrix identifying reviewers

## Outputs

- `review-feedback`: Consolidated feedback from all stakeholders
- `revised-policy`: Policy updated with accepted feedback

## Process

1. Distribute the policy to stakeholders identified in the RACI matrix
2. Collect and consolidate feedback with resolution notes
3. Revise the policy based on accepted feedback
4. Document rejected feedback with justification
