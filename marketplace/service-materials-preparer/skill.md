# Service Materials Preparer

Prepare the materials for the service: slides, bulletins, handouts, and communion elements.

## Inputs

- `volunteer-assignments`: Confirmed volunteer roster for the service
- `tech-requirements`: Audio, visual, and streaming needs

## Outputs

- `presentation-file`: The slide deck or projection content for the service
- `printed-materials`: Bulletin, song sheets, handouts, and any special inserts

## Process

1. Build the slide deck: song lyrics, scripture text, sermon points, announcements, and transition slides.
2. Create the printed bulletin with the order of service, announcements, and response cards.
3. Prepare any physical materials: communion elements, offering containers, baptism supplies.
4. Send the presentation file to the tech team and the bulletin to the print coordinator by the deadline.
