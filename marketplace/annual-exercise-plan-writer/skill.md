# Annual Exercise Plan Writer

Produce the annual exercise plan document summarizing the series, schedule, and improvement goals.

## Inputs

- `annual-schedule`: Full year schedule
- `scenario-rationale`: Why each scenario was selected
- `capability-trend`: Expected improvement trajectory
- `improvement-tracker`: Prior action items and their status

## Outputs

- `annual-exercise-plan`: Complete plan document with executive summary, scenario descriptions, schedule, and success criteria
- `executive-briefing`: One-page summary for leadership approval

## Process

1. Write the executive summary: why these scenarios, what we will learn, what it costs
2. Include each scenario description with its learning objectives
3. Attach the schedule and participant requirements
4. Define success criteria for the program year
