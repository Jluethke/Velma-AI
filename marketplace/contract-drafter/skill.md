# Contract Drafter

Draft the contract from the template and business requirements.

## Inputs

- `request-record`: The logged request with business terms
- `template-recommendation`: The recommended template

## Outputs

- `draft-contract`: The initial draft contract
- `key-terms-summary`: Summary of key terms for review (pricing, term, liability, IP, termination)

## Process

1. Populate the template with the deal-specific terms (parties, pricing, scope, dates).
2. Adjust clauses to reflect the business requirements and risk tolerance.
3. Summarize the key terms for stakeholder review.
4. Send the draft for internal review before sharing with the counterparty.
