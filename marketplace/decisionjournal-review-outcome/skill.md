# Decisionjournal Review Outcome

At review time, record what actually happened and how it differs from what you expected.

## Inputs

- `review-date`: The specific date to review the decision.
- `review-prompts`: The prompts you will answer at review time.

## Outputs

- `actual-outcome`: What actually happened by the review date.
- `outcome-gap`: How the actual outcome differs from the expected one.

## Process

1. Write what actually happened by the review date.
2. Compare the actual outcome to the expected one you logged.
3. Note which assumptions held and which broke.
