# Remediation Executor

Execute the corrections and document the resolution.

## Inputs

- `violation-assessment`: Assessment of the violation
- `remediation-options`: Options with cost and timeline

## Outputs

- `correction-record`: Documentation of corrections made with before and after photos
- `remediation-cost`: Actual cost of the remediation

## Process

1. Select the remediation approach and schedule the work.
2. Execute the corrections and document with photos.
3. Record the actual cost and completion date.
