# Multi Champion Strategy

For large deals, build multiple champions at different levels of the organization to protect against single-point-of-failure risk.

## Inputs

- `stakeholder-map`: All stakeholders
- `champion-candidate`: Current champion

## Outputs

- `secondary-champion-candidates`: 1-2 additional people who could become champions at different levels (e.g., executive sponsor, end-user advocate)
- `multi-champion-plan`: How to develop relationships with secondary champions without undermining the primary one

## Process

1. For deals above a certain size, one champion is a single point of failure. If they leave, get promoted, or lose influence, the deal stalls.
2. Identify 1-2 potential secondary champions at different organizational levels. An executive sponsor (senior enough to override objections) and an end-user advocate (who can speak to daily value) create a three-level support structure.
3. Develop secondary champions carefully. The primary champion must not feel bypassed. Ask them: 'Would it be helpful if I also connected with [executive] to build support at that level?' Let the primary champion introduce you.
4. A deal with champions at three levels (executive, manager, end-user) is significantly more resilient than one with a single champion.
