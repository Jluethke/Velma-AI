# Harvest Marketing Planner

Set the grain or commodity marketing plan.

## Inputs

- `revenue-forecast`: Expected production and price outlook
- `break-even-prices`: Minimum prices needed

## Outputs

- `marketing-plan`: Sales strategy with timing, volumes, and price targets
- `risk-management-tools`: Hedging or insurance instruments selected

## Process

1. Set price targets above break-even for each crop.
2. Plan the sales calendar (pre-harvest contracts, harvest sales, storage sales).
3. Evaluate hedging tools (futures, options, basis contracts).
4. Select crop insurance coverage levels.
