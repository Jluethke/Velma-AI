# Emergency Supply Refresh

Refresh emergency supplies for the season: weather-specific supplies, food and water, first aid, and communication.

## Inputs

- `climate_zone`: Local weather risks
- `household_size`: Number of people and pets
- `current_supplies`: What emergency supplies exist

## Outputs

- `supply_checklist`: What to stock or replace
- `expiration_check`: Items nearing expiration
- `communication_plan`: Emergency contact and meetup plan

## Process

1. Check expiration dates on food and water
2. Restock season-specific supplies
3. Test flashlights, radios, and batteries
4. Update emergency contact list
5. Review family emergency plan
