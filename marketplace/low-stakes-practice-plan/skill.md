# Low Stakes Practice Plan

Plan to practice the replacement first in low-stakes settings.

## Inputs

- `replacement-behavior`: The new behavior or belief in concrete terms
- `difference-notes`: How the new pattern differs from the old

## Outputs

- `practice-settings`: Low-stakes settings where a slip costs little
- `cue-plan`: Cues that trigger the new pattern

## Process

1. Choose low-stakes settings where a slip costs little.
2. Set cues that trigger the new pattern in those settings.
3. Plan a gradual move toward higher-stakes settings.
4. Decide how you will notice when the old cue fires.
