# Interviewer Calibration Trainer

Train interviewers on how to use the rubric consistently.

## Inputs

- `scoring-rubric`: The scoring rubric
- `question-bank`: The interview questions

## Outputs

- `calibration-materials`: Training materials for interviewer calibration
- `calibration-exercise`: Practice scenarios for interviewers to score

## Process

1. Create sample candidate responses at different quality levels
2. Design the calibration exercise with independent scoring
3. Facilitate the calibration discussion to align ratings
4. Document the calibration outcomes and any rubric adjustments
