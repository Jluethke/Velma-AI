# Action Capture Protocol

End each meeting a few minutes early to write the decisions and actions, and send a summary within the hour. A decision that is not written down was not really made, and a summary sent fast is read while the context is fresh.

## Inputs

- `agenda-standard`: The agenda rule
- `attendee-rules`: Who attends and who reads

## Outputs

- `action-protocol`: The end-early routine for capturing decisions and actions
- `summary-standard`: The rule for sending a summary within the hour

## Process

1. Reserve the last few minutes of every meeting to record decisions and assign actions with owners.
2. Write each action as an owner, a task, and a date.
3. Send a short summary of decisions and actions within an hour, while context is fresh.
4. Route the summary to the people moved off the invite so they stay informed without attending.
