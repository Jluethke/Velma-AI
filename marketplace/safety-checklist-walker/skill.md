# Safety Checklist Walker

Walk the site against the safety checklist and document findings.

## Inputs

- `project-name`: Name of the construction project
- `inspection-date`: Date of the safety inspection

## Outputs

- `inspection-findings`: List of findings from the safety walk
- `violation-photos`: Photos documenting each violation

## Process

1. Walk the site systematically, checking fall protection, PPE compliance, housekeeping, electrical safety, and excavation safety.
2. Photograph each violation with its location.
3. Note positive observations and safe practices.
4. Classify each finding by severity (imminent danger, serious, other).
