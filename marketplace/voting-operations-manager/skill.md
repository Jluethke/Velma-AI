# Voting Operations Manager

Conduct early voting and election day voting operations.

## Inputs

- `certified-candidates`: Certified candidate list for the ballot
- `voter-rolls`: Final voter rolls

## Outputs

- `voting-records`: Records of all ballots cast (in-person, absentee, provisional)
- `incident-log`: Log of any voting irregularities or issues

## Process

1. Open and manage early voting locations per the election calendar.
2. Operate election day polling locations with trained poll workers.
3. Process absentee and provisional ballots per the applicable rules.
4. Document any irregularities or complaints in the incident log.
