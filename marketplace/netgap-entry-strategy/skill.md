# Netgap Entry Strategy

Design the entry strategy for the selected community: how you will show up, contribute, and build initial relationships so you enter as a genuine participant rather than an outsider collecting contacts, because the quality of entry determines whether the gap actually closes.

## Inputs

- `selected-gap`: The single gap and its target community
- `selection-rationale`: Why this gap and community were chosen first

## Outputs

- `entry-plan`: The concrete plan to enter the community: how to show up, how to contribute, and the cadence of presence
- `first-contact-targets`: The first few people to build real relationships with inside the community, chosen as footholds rather than trophies

## Process

1. Plan how you will show up and keep showing up. One appearance at an event does not enter a community. Decide the cadence (attend the monthly meetup, post regularly in the forum, volunteer for the association) that builds familiarity over time.
2. Lead with contribution. Decide what you can offer this community that is genuinely useful (knowledge, help, effort, a connection from your other clusters). Contribution is what converts you from visitor to member.
3. Choose a few first-contact targets as footholds, not trophies. The welcoming regular, the organizer, the fellow newcomer are better early relationships than the most senior person, because they integrate you into the community rather than giving you one isolated contact.
4. Set a realistic time horizon. Genuine entry typically takes months of repeated presence, so plan for that rather than expecting a few weeks to close a structural hole, and judge progress by relationships formed, not events attended.
