# Experimental Design Builder

Design an experiment with appropriate controls, treatment groups, sample size justification, and a randomization scheme.

## Inputs

- `hypothesis-statement`: The hypothesis to test
- `variable-definitions`: Variables and their measurement criteria
- `prediction-set`: Expected outcomes under the hypothesis

## Outputs

- `experiment-protocol`: Step-by-step procedure with controls, treatments, and timing
- `sample-size-justification`: Power analysis or precedent-based sample size rationale
- `randomization-plan`: Assignment method for subjects or samples to conditions

## Process

1. Choose the experimental design type (between, within, factorial, crossover)
2. Define treatment groups, controls, and replication count
3. Calculate or justify sample size from effect size and power requirements
4. Specify randomization and blinding procedures
5. Write the step-by-step protocol with timing and decision points
