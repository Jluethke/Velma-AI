# Budget Adoption Finalizer

Incorporate amendments and finalize the adopted budget.

## Inputs

- `budget-presentation`: The presented budget
- `amendments-list`: Amendments from the governing body

## Outputs

- `adopted-budget`: The final adopted budget document
- `budget-document`: Published budget document for the fiscal year

## Process

1. Incorporate all approved amendments into the budget.
2. Verify the budget balances (revenues equal or exceed expenditures).
3. Prepare the final budget document for adoption vote.
4. Publish the adopted budget and distribute to departments.
