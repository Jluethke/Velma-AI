# Business Bank Opener

Open the business bank account with proper documentation.

## Inputs

- `ein-number`: EIN
- `formation-documents`: Formation documents
- `initial-resolutions`: Initial resolutions

## Outputs

- `bank-account-details`: Account number and bank details
- `formation-package`: Complete corporate formation package

## Process

1. Select the bank and account type
2. Provide the EIN, formation documents, and resolutions
3. Complete account opening and record the details in the corporate book
