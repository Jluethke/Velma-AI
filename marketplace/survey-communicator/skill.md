# Survey Communicator

Communicate the survey purpose and timeline to employees.

## Inputs

- `survey-instrument`: The survey
- `survey-goals`: Goals of the survey

## Outputs

- `communication-plan`: Communication sent to all employees
- `faq-document`: FAQ addressing common employee concerns

## Process

1. Draft the launch communication explaining the purpose and anonymity protections
2. Create an FAQ addressing data usage, anonymity, and follow-up
3. Brief managers on how to encourage participation without pressuring
4. Schedule the communication timeline (launch, reminders, close)
