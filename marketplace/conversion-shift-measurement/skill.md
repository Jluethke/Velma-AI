# Conversion Shift Measurement

Measure how tier choice shifts after the decoy goes live, and adjust if the shift did not land.

## Inputs

- `page-layout`: The arrangement of the three tiers
- `anchor-rationale`: Why the decoy should move choice toward premium

## Outputs

- `conversion-shift-report`: How the mix of tier choices changed against the baseline
- `pricing-adjustments`: The next change to make if the shift fell short

## Process

1. Compare the new mix of tier choices against the baseline the audit captured.
2. Confirm the shift came from buyers moving up, not from buyers leaving, by watching total conversion.
3. If premium did not gain, test a different decoy price or differentiator rather than scrapping the idea.
4. Record the result so the pricing decision has evidence behind it, not just a theory.
