# Concession Inventory Builder

Inventory everything you can concede, ranked by cost to you and value to them.

## Inputs

- `negotiation-issues`: All issues on the table
- `own-interests`: Your interests and priorities

## Outputs

- `concession-inventory`: Every possible concession, with: cost to you (low/medium/high), estimated value to them (low/medium/high), and conditions (what you would want in return)
- `high-value-low-cost-concessions`: Concessions that cost you little but are valued highly by the other party: these are your trading chips

## Process

1. List everything you could give: price adjustments, timeline changes, scope additions, flexibility, guarantees, options, information, introductions, references.
2. For each, assess the cost to you: does this actually cost you something material, or is it low-cost to provide?
3. For each, estimate the value to them: is this something they care about deeply, or is it minor to them?
4. The best concessions are low-cost to you and high-value to them. These give you maximum leverage at minimum cost.
