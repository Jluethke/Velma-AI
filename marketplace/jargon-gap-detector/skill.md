# Jargon Gap Detector

Scan the explanation for jargon and hand-waving that hide a lack of understanding.

## Inputs

- `plain-explanation`: An explanation written in everyday words
- `analogy-list`: Analogies and examples that carry the idea

## Outputs

- `jargon-flags`: Places where a technical term stood in for understanding
- `knowledge-gaps`: Specific questions to research

## Process

1. Mark every place where you used a technical term without explaining it.
2. Mark every place where the explanation jumps or feels vague.
3. Turn each flagged spot into a specific question to research.
4. Rank the questions by how central they are to the concept.
