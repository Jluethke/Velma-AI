# Breach Individual Notification

Prepare and send notifications to affected individuals.

## Inputs

- `affected-individual-count`: Number and contact information for affected individuals
- `legal-guidance`: Legal requirements for individual notification content

## Outputs

- `individual-notifications`: Sent notifications to affected individuals
- `support-resources`: Credit monitoring or identity protection services offered

## Process

1. Draft individual notification letters following legal requirements
2. Include a description of the breach, data types exposed, and protective steps to take
3. Arrange credit monitoring or identity protection services if warranted
4. Send notifications and document delivery
