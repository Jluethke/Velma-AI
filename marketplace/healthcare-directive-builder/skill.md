# Healthcare Directive Builder

Create an advance healthcare directive covering end-of-life wishes, organ donation, and treatment preferences.

## Inputs

- `healthcare_poa`: Healthcare agent already selected
- `personal_values`: Quality of life priorities
- `medical_conditions`: Any existing conditions relevant to planning

## Outputs

- `directive_document`: Completed directive decisions
- `treatment_preferences`: Specific treatment wishes
- `organ_donation_decision`: Organ and tissue donation choice

## Process

1. Define quality of life thresholds
2. Specify treatment preferences for key scenarios
3. Document organ donation wishes
4. Note religious or cultural considerations
5. Identify who receives copies
