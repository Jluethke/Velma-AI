# Total Comp Negotiator

Negotiate the full compensation package, not just the base salary. Base salary is one component. Equity, bonus, benefits, flexibility, title, and development budget can add significant value.

## Inputs

- `market-range`: The salary range data
- `positioning-rationale`: Your positioning in the range

## Outputs

- `package-priorities`: All compensation components ranked by value to you: base, equity, bonus, signing bonus, PTO, remote flexibility, title, learning budget, severance
- `negotiation-script`: The specific language for the salary discussion: how to deflect early salary questions, how to present your ask, and how to counter their offer

## Process

1. List every compensation component: base salary, equity (options or RSUs), annual bonus, signing bonus, PTO/vacation, remote work flexibility, title, learning/development budget, relocation, severance terms.
2. Rank these by personal value: some people value equity higher, others value flexibility. Know your priorities before the conversation.
3. If base salary is capped (budget constraints), negotiate other components: more equity, a signing bonus, additional PTO, or a guaranteed review at 6 months.
4. Prepare responses for salary questions: if asked for salary expectations before receiving an offer, defer ('I would like to understand the full role before discussing compensation' or 'What is the range budgeted for this role?').
