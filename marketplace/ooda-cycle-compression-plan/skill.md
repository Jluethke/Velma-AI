# Ooda Cycle Compression Plan

Design changes to shrink the bottleneck and speed the whole loop. Faster cycling lets you act on newer information and forces the opponent to keep reacting to moves you have already changed.

## Inputs

- `loop-bottleneck`: The slowest stage and its share of total cycle time
- `cycle-time-total`: The full loop time from event to action

## Outputs

- `compression-plan`: Specific changes to cut the bottleneck stage
- `target-cycle-time`: The cycle time you aim for and why it beats the rival

## Process

1. Attack the bottleneck first. Speeding any other stage barely moves the total.
2. List specific changes: remove an approval, pre-decide common cases, automate a handoff, cut a data source.
3. Set a target cycle time that is faster than the competitor tempo, not just faster than today.
4. Plan to re-measure after the change, since the bottleneck usually moves to a new stage.
