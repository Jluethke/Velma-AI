# Bottleneck Stage Diagnosis

Diagnose why deals stall in the bottleneck stage: what is happening (or not happening) that causes delays.

## Inputs

- `stage-times`: Time in each stage
- `pipeline-stages`: The sales stages

## Outputs

- `bottleneck-stage`: The stage where deals spend the most time
- `stall-reasons`: The specific reasons deals stall in this stage (missing information, slow approvals, unclear next steps, champion disengagement, competitor evaluation)

## Process

1. Look at the bottleneck stage and review 10-15 deals that spent excessive time there. What was happening? Pull CRM notes, emails, and meeting records.
2. Common stall reasons by stage: Qualification stalls because the salesperson cannot reach the decision maker. Proposal stalls because the prospect is comparing options. Negotiation stalls because legal review is slow. Identify which applies to your bottleneck.
3. Interview your sales team: 'When a deal gets stuck in [stage], what is usually the reason?' Their answers, combined with the deal-level data, reveal the pattern.
4. Separate stalls caused by your process (you were slow to send the proposal, you did not follow up) from stalls caused by the prospect's process (their legal team takes 30 days, their budget cycle is quarterly).
