# Capture Habit Protocol

Establish the discipline of capturing without organizing. At capture time, add only the raw material and minimal context (source, date, one tag). Never file, categorize, summarize, or decide importance at capture time.

## Inputs

- `capture-channel-map`: Map of information sources to capture methods
- `unified-inbox-config`: The single inbox configuration

## Outputs

- `capture-protocol`: Written protocol for what to capture, what metadata to add, and what NOT to do at capture time
- `capture-friction-audit`: Audit of capture friction per channel with fixes for any path taking more than 10 seconds

## Process

1. Write the capture rule: if something is interesting, resonant, or potentially useful, capture it. Do not evaluate importance. That judgment is deferred to the organize step.
2. Define the minimum metadata to attach at capture time: the date (automatic), the source (where you found it), and one free-text tag (the project or topic it might relate to). Nothing else.
3. Write the anti-rules: do not file into folders at capture time. Do not rewrite or summarize. Do not decide if it is important. Do not spend more than 10 seconds per capture. These behaviors kill the capture habit.
4. Time each capture path end to end. Any path over 10 seconds gets redesigned or replaced.
