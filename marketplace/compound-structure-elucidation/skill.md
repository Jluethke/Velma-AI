# Compound Structure Elucidation

Identify the isolated compound by spectroscopic methods (NMR, MS, IR, UV).

## Inputs

- `purified-compound`: Isolated compound of sufficient purity and mass for spectroscopy
- `purification-log`: Chromatographic behavior hinting at compound class

## Outputs

- `structure-assignment`: Proposed molecular structure with supporting spectral data
- `spectral-data-package`: NMR, MS, IR, and UV spectra with peak assignments

## Process

1. Acquire high-resolution mass spectrum to determine the molecular formula
2. Record 1D and 2D NMR spectra (1H, 13C, COSY, HSQC, HMBC) and assign peaks
3. Use IR and UV data to confirm functional groups and chromophores
4. Compare against databases and literature to confirm or propose the structure
