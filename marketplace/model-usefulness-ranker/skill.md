# Model Usefulness Ranker

After one full cycle, rank the models by how much insight they produced in your specific domain and situation.

## Inputs

- `weekly-practice-schedule`: The 10-week rotation
- `application-log-template`: The log template (now filled with 10 weeks of data)

## Outputs

- `model-rankings`: The 10 models ranked by insight produced in your domain, with evidence from the application log
- `top-5-models`: Your personal top 5 models, the ones you should apply most frequently

## Process

1. Review your 10 weeks of application logs. For each model, count: how many times it changed your thinking, how many times the outcome validated its insight, how easy it was to apply.
2. Rank the models by impact. The models that changed your thinking AND were validated by outcomes rank highest.
3. Your top 5 become your default toolkit. When facing a decision, run through these 5 before choosing. The other 5 remain available for situations that match their trigger conditions.
4. If any model produced zero insight across the cycle, replace it with a model from outside the core 10 that might fit your domain better (e.g., Bayesian updating for data-heavy domains, theory of constraints for operations).
