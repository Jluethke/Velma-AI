# Warm Intro Connector Followup

Close the loop with every connector regardless of outcome, because the follow-up is what sustains the introduction engine: a connector who is thanked and told what happened will introduce you again, while one who hears nothing back quietly stops.

## Inputs

- `intro-tracking-log`: The log of introductions and their stages
- `intro-status-states`: The stages and the action due at each

## Outputs

- `connector-followup-plan`: A plan for closing the loop with each connector: thank them, report what happened, and offer reciprocation
- `connector-acknowledgment-notes`: Notes on how each connector prefers to be acknowledged and what you owe them in return

## Process

1. Thank the connector within a day of the introduction being forwarded, before any outcome is known. This acknowledges the act of introducing, which is the part they controlled, and it is the most important touch.
2. Close the loop at the outcome. If it converted, share the good result and credit them. If it did not, thank them anyway and say briefly that it was not a fit, without blaming their contact. Both outcomes earn a report.
3. Look for a way to reciprocate. The durable introduction engine runs on two-way value, so note what the connector cares about and make an introduction for them, or help in some other way, so the ledger stays balanced.
4. Record how each connector likes to be acknowledged (a quick note, a public thank-you with permission, a returned favor) so future follow-ups fit them, and so a reliable connector feels valued rather than used.
