# Candidate Filing Processor

Process candidate filings and voter registration updates.

## Inputs

- `logistics-plan`: Election logistics plan
- `election-calendar`: Election deadline calendar

## Outputs

- `certified-candidates`: List of candidates certified for the ballot
- `voter-rolls`: Updated voter registration rolls for the election

## Process

1. Receive and verify candidate filing documents and fees.
2. Certify candidates who meet all eligibility and filing requirements.
3. Process new voter registrations and address changes through the registration deadline.
4. Prepare the final voter rolls for each precinct.
