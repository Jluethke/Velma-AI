# Ticket Volume Forecaster

Forecast ticket volume by hour and day of week.

## Inputs

- `historical-volume`: Past ticket volumes with timestamps
- `business-calendar`: Holidays, product launches, and seasonal events

## Outputs

- `volume-forecast`: Forecasted ticket volume by hour and day
- `peak-periods`: Identified peak volume periods

## Process

1. Analyze historical volume patterns by hour, day, and month.
2. Adjust for known events (product launches, holidays, promotions).
3. Identify recurring peak and valley periods.
4. Produce the volume forecast.
