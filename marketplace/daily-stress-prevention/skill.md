# Daily Stress Prevention

Design your day to prevent chronic stress accumulation: scheduled recovery, transition rituals, and a hard stop.

## Inputs

- `intervention-menu`: 90-second interventions
- `stress-history`: Your stress patterns

## Outputs

- `prevention-schedule`: Daily recovery touchpoints: morning grounding (5 min), mid-day movement (10 min), end-of-day shutdown ritual (10 min)
- `hard-stop-protocol`: A defined end-of-work time with a shutdown ritual that signals to the brain that work is done

## Process

1. Morning grounding: 5 minutes of intentional calm before opening email or starting work. This sets the baseline for the day. Starting the day in a reactive state (email, news) starts the cortisol early.
2. Mid-day movement: a 10-minute walk between the morning and afternoon blocks. Movement metabolizes cortisol. A sedentary person accumulates cortisol; a moving person processes it.
3. End-of-day shutdown: at a defined time, write tomorrow's top 3 tasks, close all tabs, and say 'Shutdown complete' (or your own phrase). This ritual tells the brain to stop processing work.
4. The hard stop is not optional on most days. Working until you fall asleep means cortisol is still elevated during sleep, which degrades sleep quality, which increases stress the next day. The cycle must be broken.
