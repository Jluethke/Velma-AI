# Taphonomic Analysis

Analyze the preservation history of the fossil assemblage: transport, sorting, weathering, bioerosion, and burial conditions.

## Inputs

- `taxonomic-assignments`: Identified specimens with field context
- `strat-log-template`: Stratigraphic data from the excavation

## Outputs

- `taphonomic-report`: Assemblage-level taphonomic summary with preservation grades and transport indicators
- `depositional-interpretation`: Inferred depositional environment and burial history

## Process

1. Score each specimen for weathering stage, abrasion, bioerosion, and fragmentation
2. Map skeletal element representation and Voorhies Group ratios to assess transport sorting
3. Analyze spatial distribution within the grid for clustering, orientation, and dip patterns
4. Synthesize taphonomic signatures into a depositional and burial history narrative
