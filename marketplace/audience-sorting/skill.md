# Audience Sorting

Predict who each refusal repels and who it attracts, so you choose the tradeoffs on purpose.

## Inputs

- `refusal-statements`: The statements of what you will not do
- `rationale-notes`: The reason behind each refusal

## Outputs

- `repelled-segments`: The buyers each refusal will push away
- `attracted-segments`: The buyers each refusal will draw in and reassure

## Process

1. For each refusal, name the buyers who will walk away, and confirm they are buyers you are glad to lose.
2. Name the buyers who will lean in because the refusal signals focus and honesty.
3. Check that the attracted segments are large and reachable enough to build a business on.
4. If a refusal repels buyers you need and attracts few, send it back to the selection step.
