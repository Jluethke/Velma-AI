# Snm Request And Cost Assessor

Assess the request and the true cost of saying yes. Most reflexive yeses happen because the cost is not counted until later. Count it now: the hours, the trade-offs against other commitments, and the resentment a grudging yes tends to breed.

## Inputs

- `request-received`: What is being asked of you and by when
- `relationship-context`: Who is asking, your relationship, and what is at stake between you

## Outputs

- `request-summary`: The request stated plainly, including what a yes would actually commit you to
- `cost-of-yes`: The real cost of agreeing: time, trade-offs against other commitments, and likely resentment

## Process

1. Restate the request in full, including the parts left implicit. 'Can you help with the launch' often means weeks, not an afternoon.
2. Count the direct cost in time and the indirect cost in what you would give up to say yes.
3. Note the resentment cost. A yes you will resent damages the relationship more slowly but more deeply than a clean no.
4. Separate the request from the relationship. You can value the person and still decline the request.
