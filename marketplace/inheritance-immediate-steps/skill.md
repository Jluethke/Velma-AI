# Inheritance Immediate Steps

Handle the immediate logistics of receiving an inheritance: documentation, account access, and cooling-off period.

## Inputs

- `inheritance_type`: Cash, property, investments, or mixed
- `approximate_value`: Estimated total value
- `source_relationship`: Relationship to the deceased

## Outputs

- `immediate_checklist`: First steps to take
- `documentation_needed`: Legal and financial documents to gather
- `parking_strategy`: Where to hold funds during decision period

## Process

1. Secure all relevant legal documents
2. Open a separate account for inherited funds
3. Set a cooling-off period before major decisions
4. Identify any time-sensitive items (property taxes, etc.)
5. Gather team if needed (accountant, attorney)
