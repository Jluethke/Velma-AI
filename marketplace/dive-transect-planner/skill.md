# Dive Transect Planner

Design underwater survey transects with GPS coordinates, depth ranges, habitat types, and dive safety parameters.

## Inputs

- `study-site`: Geographic coordinates and general description of the survey area
- `survey-objectives`: Research questions and target organisms or habitats
- `dive-constraints`: Maximum depth, current conditions, visibility, and diver certification levels

## Outputs

- `transect-map`: Georeferenced transect layout with waypoints, depths, and habitat zones
- `dive-plan`: Dive profiles with bottom times, surface intervals, and emergency procedures
- `sampling-schedule`: Date, time, tide, and weather windows for each planned dive

## Process

1. Review bathymetric charts and satellite imagery to identify candidate transect locations
2. Select transect orientations that cross habitat gradients relevant to the research questions
3. Calculate dive profiles using no-decompression limits for the planned depths
4. Schedule dives around tidal cycles and weather windows for optimal visibility
5. Prepare transect markers, underwater slates, and GPS logging equipment
