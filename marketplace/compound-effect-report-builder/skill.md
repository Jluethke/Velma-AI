# Compound Effect Report Builder

Produce the compound effect report showing the baseline, projections, levers, and the recommended focus.

## Inputs

- `projection-table`: The scenario projections
- `lever-ranking`: The improvement levers ranked by impact
- `divergence-points`: When the scenarios diverge significantly

## Outputs

- `compound-effect-report`: The complete report: baseline, three scenario projections with concrete dollar or unit amounts, divergence timeline, ranked levers, and the single recommended focus action
- `tracking-protocol`: How to track whether the 1% improvement is actually happening: the specific metric to check, the frequency, and the threshold that indicates the improvement is working

## Process

1. Assemble the report sections: baseline (where you are), trajectory (where you're headed), scenarios (the range of outcomes from small changes), levers (what to change), and focus (the one action to take).
2. Include a concrete comparison to make the compounding intuitive. Example: 'The difference between a 7% and 8% annual return on $10,000 over 30 years is $76,123 versus $100,627. That 1% difference is worth $24,504.'
3. Define the tracking protocol: check the metric at the compounding frequency (monthly for finances, weekly for habits). If the growth rate after 3 periods is at or above the target, the improvement is working. If below, diagnose why and adjust the specific action.
4. Set the review schedule: revisit the full compound model quarterly. The growth rate will change as conditions change, and the optimal lever may shift. The model is a living tool, not a one-time calculation.
