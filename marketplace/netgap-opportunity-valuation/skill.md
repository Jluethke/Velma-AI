# Netgap Opportunity Valuation

Value each gap by what filling it would give you access to, and prioritize, since a network has more gaps than anyone can fill and the structural holes worth bridging are the ones connected to real opportunities or to resilience against a real risk.

## Inputs

- `zero-connection-regions`: The worlds your network does not touch
- `missing-cluster-list`: Clusters that should plausibly exist but do not

## Outputs

- `gap-value-scores`: Each gap scored by the value of what it would open up: information, opportunities, or resilience
- `prioritized-gaps`: The gaps ranked, so attention goes to the few highest-value holes rather than spreading thin

## Process

1. For each gap, name concretely what filling it would give you: information you currently cannot get, opportunities you cannot currently reach, or resilience against a risk your current clusters all share.
2. Apply Burt's core insight, that the value of a structural hole is the non-redundant information and opportunity on the other side. A gap into a world that thinks differently from all your clusters is worth more than one into a near-duplicate of what you have.
3. Weigh resilience, not just opportunity. If all your high-trust relationships sit in one industry or one employer, the gap into a different world is worth filling as insurance, because a single downturn could otherwise take out your whole network at once.
4. Rank the gaps and keep the priority list short. Filling one gap well, by genuinely entering a new community, takes months, so three prioritized gaps is plenty to work on at a time.
