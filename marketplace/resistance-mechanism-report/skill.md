# Resistance Mechanism Report

Synthesize phenotypic and genomic findings into a resistance mechanism report linking genotype to observed phenotype.

## Inputs

- `susceptibility-profile`: MIC values and resistance phenotype
- `resistance-genotype`: Resistance genes and their genomic context
- `virulence-inventory`: Virulence factor data for clinical context

## Outputs

- `resistance-report`: Integrated report mapping each resistance phenotype to its genetic determinant
- `clinical-implications`: Treatment recommendations and infection control notes based on resistance profile

## Process

1. Align each phenotypic resistance to its candidate gene(s) and assess concordance
2. Flag discordant results (phenotype without known gene, or gene without phenotype) for follow-up
3. Assess horizontal transfer risk based on mobile element context
4. Write the summary report with treatment implications and recommended infection control measures
