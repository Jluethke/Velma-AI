# Variance Source Investigator

Investigate the sources of food cost variance.

## Inputs

- `cost-variance`: Overall variance
- `variance-by-category`: Category-level variance

## Outputs

- `root-causes`: Identified causes of variance (waste, portioning, theft, pricing)
- `control-recommendations`: Recommended controls for each cause

## Process

1. Analyze waste logs for spoilage and over-production.
2. Audit portion sizes against recipe standards.
3. Review purchasing prices against contracted rates.
4. Assess shrinkage patterns for potential theft.
