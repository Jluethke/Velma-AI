# Financial Emergency Section

Build the financial emergency section: where the money is, who has access, and how to keep obligations covered if income stops.

## Inputs

- `household-members`: Who lives in the household
- `emergency-contact-sheet`: The master contact sheet

## Outputs

- `financial-section`: Every account (bank, investment, retirement) with the institution name, account number (last 4 digits), and who has access. Location of the emergency fund. Auto-pay setup for critical obligations.
- `financial-action-steps`: Step-by-step instructions for a financial emergency: which accounts to access first, which obligations are auto-paid, which require manual action, and who to call for each

## Process

1. List every financial account: checking, savings, emergency fund, investment, retirement, HSA, life insurance. For each, record the institution, the last 4 digits of the account number, and who has access.
2. Identify the emergency fund: where it is, how to access it, and how many months of obligations it covers.
3. List every recurring obligation: rent or mortgage, utilities, insurance premiums, loan payments, subscriptions. For each, note whether it is auto-paid and from which account.
4. Write the action steps: if the primary earner's income stops today, here is what is auto-paid, here is what needs manual attention, and here is how long the emergency fund lasts.
