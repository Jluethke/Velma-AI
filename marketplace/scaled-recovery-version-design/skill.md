# Scaled Recovery Version Design

Design the scaled-down recovery version of the habit, small enough that it is always possible on the day after a miss.

## Inputs

- `defined-streak-habit`: The habit carrying the streak
- `miss-definition`: What counts as a miss

## Outputs

- `scaled-down-version`: A tiny version of the habit, such as two pushups or one sentence, used after a miss
- `recovery-trigger`: The rule that the scaled-down version is performed the day after any miss, without exception

## Process

1. Shrink the habit to a version so small it cannot be refused, such as two pushups, one written sentence, or a five-minute walk.
2. The recovery version exists to preserve the pattern and the identity, not to produce results. Showing up tiny keeps the chain from breaking.
3. Write the recovery trigger: the day after any miss, you do at least the scaled-down version, whatever else happens that day.
4. Make the recovery version require no preparation, so it is available on exactly the kind of bad day that caused the first miss.
