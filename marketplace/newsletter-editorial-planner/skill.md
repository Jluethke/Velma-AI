# Newsletter Editorial Planner

Plan the editorial calendar for the newsletter including topics, cadence, and content sources.

## Inputs

- `newsletter-goals`: The purpose and audience for the newsletter
- `publishing-cadence`: How often the newsletter will be sent (weekly, biweekly, monthly)

## Outputs

- `editorial-calendar`: Calendar of upcoming newsletter issues with topics and deadlines
- `content-sources`: List of content sources to draw from for each issue

## Process

1. Define the newsletter sections and recurring features.
2. Plan topics for the next 4 to 8 issues based on audience interests and business goals.
3. Identify content sources for each section (internal updates, curated links, original writing, user contributions).
4. Set the production timeline (content deadline, design deadline, review deadline, send date).
