# Animatic Builder

Create the animatic with timed storyboard and scratch audio.

## Inputs

- `storyboard-panels`: Storyboard panels
- `script-or-narration`: Narration or dialogue for timing

## Outputs

- `animatic-video`: Timed animatic with scratch audio
- `timing-sheet`: Frame-level timing for each scene

## Process

1. Import storyboard panels into the timeline
2. Record or place scratch audio for dialogue and narration
3. Time each panel to match the audio and pacing
4. Review and adjust timing until the flow works
