# Disaster And Home Section

Build the natural disaster and home emergency sections: evacuation routes, go-bag, water shutoff, electrical panel, and insurance claims.

## Inputs

- `location-details`: Home address and local hazards (flood zone, earthquake zone, tornado alley, wildfire risk)
- `household-members`: Who lives in the household

## Outputs

- `disaster-section`: Evacuation routes (primary and alternate), meeting points (near home and out of area), go-bag location and contents checklist, shelter locations
- `home-emergency-section`: Location of: water main shutoff, gas shutoff, electrical panel, fire extinguishers. Insurance claim process and adjuster phone number. Contractor contacts for emergency repairs.

## Process

1. Identify the local hazards: flood, earthquake, tornado, wildfire, hurricane, winter storm. For each, define the evacuation route (primary and alternate) and the meeting point.
2. Define the go-bag: location, contents (documents, medications, cash, phone charger, water, food for 72 hours, first aid, flashlight). Check the expiration dates semi-annually.
3. Map the home emergency shutoffs: where is the water main shutoff valve, the gas shutoff, and the electrical panel? Can every adult in the household find and operate them?
4. Record the homeowners or renters insurance claim process: the phone number, the policy number, and the steps to file. Keep a home inventory (photos or video of possessions) stored in the cloud.
