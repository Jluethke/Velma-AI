# Collab Content Producer

Produce the collaborative content.

## Inputs

- `collab-agreement`: The agreed format and responsibilities
- `content-brief`: The creative brief

## Outputs

- `collab-content`: The finished collaborative content for both creators
- `promo-assets`: Cross-promotion assets for both creators to share

## Process

1. Coordinate the production schedule between both creators.
2. Produce the content according to the brief.
3. Create cross-promotion assets (clips, teasers, quotes) for both audiences.
4. Review and approve the final content with the partner.
