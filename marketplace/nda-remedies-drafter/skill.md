# Nda Remedies Drafter

Draft the remedies and enforcement provisions for breach.

## Inputs

- `nda-type`: Type of NDA
- `term-provisions`: Term and survival provisions

## Outputs

- `remedies-clause`: Remedies for breach including injunctive relief
- `dispute-provisions`: Dispute resolution and governing law

## Process

1. Draft the injunctive relief provision for breach
2. Specify the governing law and jurisdiction
3. Add the dispute resolution mechanism (litigation or arbitration)
