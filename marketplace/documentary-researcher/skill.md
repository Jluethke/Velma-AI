# Documentary Researcher

Research the subject and develop the documentary thesis.

## Inputs

- `subject-description`: Topic or subject of the documentary
- `distribution-target`: Intended platform or audience for the film

## Outputs

- `research-dossier`: Compiled research with sources and key facts
- `thesis-statement`: Central argument or perspective of the film

## Process

1. Conduct background research on the subject
2. Identify key characters, events, and story arcs
3. Develop the thesis or central question the film will explore
4. Compile the research dossier with source materials
