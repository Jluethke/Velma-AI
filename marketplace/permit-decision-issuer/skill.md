# Permit Decision Issuer

Issue the permit decision and collect fees.

## Inputs

- `review-findings`: Technical review findings
- `review-recommendation`: Approval, conditional approval, or denial recommendation

## Outputs

- `permit-decision`: The official permit decision with any conditions
- `permit-record`: Complete permit record including decision, fees, and conditions

## Process

1. Prepare the permit decision document based on the review recommendation.
2. Collect any remaining fees.
3. Issue the permit or denial notice to the applicant.
4. Record the decision in the permit tracking system.
