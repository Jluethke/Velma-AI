# Person Problem Separation

Reframe from 'you vs me' to 'us vs the problem.'

## Inputs

- `conflict-situation`: What the conflict is about
- `acknowledgment-statement`: How you acknowledged their emotions

## Outputs

- `reframed-problem`: The problem restated as a shared obstacle
- `shared-interest`: The interest both parties share that the problem threatens

## Process

1. Find the shared interest: what do both parties want? In most conflicts, both parties want something compatible.
2. Reframe: 'We both want this project to go well. The disagreement about the timeline is the obstacle. Let us figure it out together.'
3. The language matters: 'We' not 'you.' 'Us' not 'me.' 'Together' not 'you should.'
4. If the other person reverts to personal attacks, redirect: 'I hear your frustration. Let us focus on the problem.'
