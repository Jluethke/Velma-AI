# Case Elicitation Interview

Have the expert walk through 3-5 real cases where they made a non-obvious judgment call, using the critical decision method.

## Inputs

- `selected-expert`: The expert
- `interview-framing`: How the interview was set up

## Outputs

- `case-narratives`: 3-5 detailed case narratives, each including the situation, the cues the expert noticed, the interpretation, the decision, and the outcome
- `decision-points`: The specific moments in each case where the expert's choice diverged from what a novice would have done

## Process

1. Ask: 'Think of a time when you made a decision in [domain] that turned out well, and that most people in your position would not have made. Walk me through what happened.'
2. As they tell the story, probe at each decision point: 'What did you notice at that moment?' 'What did that cue mean to you?' 'What options did you consider?' 'Why did you rule out the other options?'
3. Repeat for 3-5 cases. Choose cases of different types: a routine case with a hidden twist, a case where the expert overruled conventional wisdom, a case where time pressure forced a fast decision.
4. Do not judge or evaluate during the interview. Your job is to extract, not to assess. Ask 'what happened next?' not 'was that the right call?'
