# Hazard Risk Assessor

Conduct the hazard identification and risk assessment.

## Inputs

- `jurisdiction-profile`: Geographic, demographic, and infrastructure profile of the jurisdiction
- `historical-incidents`: History of past emergencies and disasters in the area

## Outputs

- `hazard-inventory`: List of hazards with likelihood and impact ratings
- `risk-priorities`: Hazards ranked by risk level for planning priority

## Process

1. Identify all natural, technological, and human-caused hazards relevant to the jurisdiction.
2. Assess the likelihood and potential impact of each hazard.
3. Rank hazards by overall risk level.
4. Map vulnerable populations and critical infrastructure for each high-priority hazard.
