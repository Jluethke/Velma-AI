# Criteria Discussion Facilitator

Facilitate the discussion of the divergent cases. The facilitator does not overrule managers; they ask managers to justify ratings with evidence from the example until the group converges on what each level means.

## Inputs

- `divergence-cases`: The examples to discuss
- `systematic-lean-flags`: The managers who lean high or low

## Outputs

- `discussion-record`: The reasoning that settled each divergent case
- `resolved-ratings`: The agreed rating for each divergent case after discussion

## Process

1. Start with the widest-scatter example, asking managers at the high and low ends to point to the specific evidence behind their rating.
2. Compare examples across levels: if a high example has weaker evidence than a meets example, the ratings are inconsistent and the group adjusts.
3. Keep the discussion on the criteria, not on defending a number, asking what specifically separates one level from the next.
4. Record the resolved rating for each divergent case and the reasoning that settled it.
