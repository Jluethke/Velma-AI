# Planned Giving Materials Creator

Create marketing materials to promote planned giving.

## Inputs

- `vehicle-menu`: Planned giving options offered
- `organization-profile`: Mission and case for support

## Outputs

- `marketing-materials`: Brochures, web pages, and email content
- `advisor-toolkit`: Materials for donors' financial and legal advisors

## Process

1. Write plain-language descriptions of each giving vehicle
2. Create print and digital marketing materials
3. Develop sample bequest language for wills
4. Build a toolkit for donors to share with their advisors
