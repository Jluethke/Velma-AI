# Calibration Session Facilitator

Facilitate the calibration session. Managers present their ratings for each employee, with specific examples. The facilitator's job is to surface inconsistencies: if Manager A rates a behavior as 'exceeds expectations' and Manager B rates the same behavior as 'meets expectations,' one of them is wrong. The group resolves the inconsistency.

## Inputs

- `manager-distributions`: Rating distributions by manager
- `systematic-bias-flags`: Managers flagged as potential over-raters or under-raters

## Outputs

- `calibrated-ratings`: The final ratings after calibration adjustments, with notes on any changes from the original ratings
- `calibration-notes`: Key discussions and decisions from the calibration session, including where managers disagreed and how it was resolved

## Process

1. Start with the flagged managers. Ask them to present their highest and lowest rated employees with specific examples. The group assesses whether the examples justify the ratings.
2. For each rating level, compare examples across managers. If Manager A's 'exceeds' examples are weaker than Manager B's 'meets' examples, the ratings are inconsistent. Discuss and adjust.
3. The facilitator does not overrule managers. The facilitator asks questions: 'Manager A, you rated this as exceeds. Manager B rated a similar behavior as meets. Can you explain the difference?'
4. Record every adjustment: the original rating, the calibrated rating, and the reason for the change.
