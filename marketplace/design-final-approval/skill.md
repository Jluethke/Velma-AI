# Design Final Approval

Get final approval on the revised design and produce the review record.

## Inputs

- `revised-design`: The revised design files
- `revision-notes`: Summary of changes made

## Outputs

- `approved-design`: The approved final design
- `design-review-record`: Complete record of the review cycle (feedback, decisions, approvals)

## Process

1. Present the revised design to the approvers with the revision notes.
2. Collect final approval or identify remaining issues.
3. Archive the complete review record (original submission, feedback, revisions, approval).
4. Produce the approved design and review record.
