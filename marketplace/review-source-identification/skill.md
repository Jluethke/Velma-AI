# Review Source Identification

Identify where competitor reviews live: app stores, G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Amazon, Yelp, Google Maps, Reddit, niche forums.

## Inputs

- `competitor-list`: The competitors to mine (products, services, or brands that your target audience also considers)
- `market-category`: The product or service category you compete in

## Outputs

- `review-source-map`: For each competitor: the platforms where reviews exist, approximate volume, and the date range covered
- `collection-plan`: Which sources to prioritize and how many reviews to collect per competitor (target: 50-100 negative reviews per competitor)

## Process

1. For each competitor, search the major review platforms. Note which platforms have the most reviews and the most detailed negative reviews.
2. Prioritize platforms where reviewers write paragraphs, not just stars. Detailed complaints contain the language and expectations you need.
3. Plan the collection: aim for 50-100 negative reviews (1-3 stars) per competitor. If a competitor has fewer than 50 negative reviews, include the 3-star reviews that contain specific complaints.
