# Option Generator

Generate options that address both sets of interests. The best option is one that satisfies your top interest and their top interest simultaneously.

## Inputs

- `shared-interests`: The interests both parties share
- `different-but-compatible-interests`: The interests that allow trades
- `conflicting-interests`: The interests that require compromise

## Outputs

- `option-list`: 3-5 options that address both parties' interests, ranging from your ideal to a fair compromise
- `evaluation-criteria`: Objective criteria for choosing between options: fairness, precedent, market value, expert opinion, or mutual satisfaction

## Process

1. Brainstorm options without evaluating them. Generate at least 5 possibilities, including creative ones. 'What if we...' is the opening for each option.
2. For each option, check: does it address your top interest? Does it address their top interest? If an option addresses both, it is a strong candidate.
3. Where interests conflict and no option addresses both, propose objective criteria: 'Let us use [market rate / industry standard / past precedent / expert opinion] to decide this, rather than either of us imposing our preference.'
4. Present the options as a menu, not as a take-it-or-leave-it proposal. 'Here are three approaches I see. What are your thoughts? Do you see an option I am missing?'
