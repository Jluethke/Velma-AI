# Animal Condition Monitor

Monitor individual animal condition and behavior for early detection.

## Inputs

- `herd-profile`: Animal IDs and baseline condition scores
- `health-protocol`: Normal parameters and alert thresholds

## Outputs

- `condition-scores`: Body condition scores and behavior notes per animal
- `alert-list`: Animals flagged for follow-up

## Process

1. Score body condition on the standard scale for the species.
2. Observe feeding behavior, mobility, and social interaction.
3. Compare to baseline and flag animals below threshold.
4. Record all observations in the herd management system.
