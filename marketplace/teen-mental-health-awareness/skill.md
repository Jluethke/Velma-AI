# Teen Mental Health Awareness

Recognize mental health warning signs in teenagers and know when and how to get help.

## Inputs

- `baseline_behavior`: Normal behavior patterns
- `risk_factors`: Family history, social stressors, etc.

## Outputs

- `warning_signs`: Behavioral changes to watch for
- `conversation_guide`: How to talk about mental health
- `resource_list`: Crisis hotlines, therapists, school counselors

## Process

1. Learn normal teen development vs concerning changes
2. Identify specific warning signs by category
3. Prepare how to raise the topic without judgment
4. Research local teen mental health providers
5. Know crisis resources and when to use them
