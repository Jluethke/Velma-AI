# Building Systems Inventory

Inventory all building systems and equipment requiring maintenance.

## Inputs

- `property-details`: Property size, age, and facilities
- `equipment-records`: Existing equipment records and manuals

## Outputs

- `asset-registry`: Complete list of maintainable assets with location and age
- `criticality-ranking`: Assets ranked by impact of failure

## Process

1. Walk every area and list all maintainable equipment.
2. Record make, model, serial, age, and location for each.
3. Rank each asset by the impact of its failure on operations.
4. Organize the registry by system (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, elevator, FF&E).
