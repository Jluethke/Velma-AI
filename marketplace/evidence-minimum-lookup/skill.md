# Evidence Minimum Lookup

Look up the established minimum dose for each target so you aim at the floor that produces the result, not an arbitrary high bar.

## Inputs

- `selected-targets`: The outcomes being pursued
- `target-metrics`: The measure for each target

## Outputs

- `minimum-dose-table`: The minimum effective dose for each target, such as cardio minutes, strength sessions, or mobility minutes per week
- `dose-source-notes`: A note on where each minimum comes from and any condition that would change it

## Process

1. Record the general guidelines. Cardiovascular health responds to roughly one hundred fifty minutes of moderate activity a week. Strength responds to two resistance sessions a week covering the major muscle groups.
2. Add mobility and recovery minimums where they apply, such as about ten minutes of daily flexibility work and a baseline of sleep that training depends on.
3. Note that these are population floors. A beginner may see results below the floor, and a specific condition may raise or lower it, so treat the numbers as a starting dose.
4. Write down the condition under which each minimum would change, so you know when to consult a professional rather than guess.
