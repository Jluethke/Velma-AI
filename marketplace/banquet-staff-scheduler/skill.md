# Banquet Staff Scheduler

Schedule banquet staff by role and shift.

## Inputs

- `operational-requirements`: Staff requirements
- `event-timeline`: Event timing

## Outputs

- `banquet-staffing-plan`: Staff schedule with names, roles, and times
- `briefing-notes`: Pre-service briefing notes for the team

## Process

1. Calculate staff needed by role (servers, bartenders, runners).
2. Assign staff based on skill and availability.
3. Write the pre-service briefing notes.
4. Schedule the setup and breakdown crews.
