# Competitor Identifier

Identify the key competitors and their market segments.

## Inputs

- `market-context`: Market the company operates in
- `product-description`: Product or service being positioned

## Outputs

- `competitor-list`: Ranked list of competitors with market segment
- `competitor-profiles`: Brief profile of each competitor

## Process

1. Identify direct and indirect competitors
2. Profile each by size, market segment, and positioning
3. Rank by competitive threat level
