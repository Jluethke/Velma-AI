# Streak Tracking And Scaling

Track your streak (consecutive days of completing the stack) and scale the habit once the stack is automatic.

## Inputs

- `stack-formula`: The habit stack
- `environment-setup`: The prepared environment

## Outputs

- `streak-log`: Daily check: did you complete the stack today? Running streak count. The target is 21 consecutive days before scaling.
- `scaling-plan`: How to expand the habit once the stack is automatic: from 2 minutes to 5, then 10, then the target duration

## Process

1. Track with a simple yes/no daily check. The goal is an unbroken streak. If you miss a day, restart the streak counter. Never miss two days in a row.
2. The first 21 days are about consistency, not duration. Keep the habit tiny (2 minutes). The habit stack needs to become automatic before you scale.
3. After 21 consecutive days, increase by 2 minutes. Run another 7 days at the new duration. If the streak holds, increase again.
4. If scaling breaks the streak, drop back to the previous duration. The habit stack must remain effortless enough that you never skip it.
