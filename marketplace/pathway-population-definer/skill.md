# Pathway Population Definer

Define the patient population and inclusion criteria for the pathway.

## Inputs

- `target-condition`: Condition or procedure
- `evidence-summary`: Evidence-based recommendations

## Outputs

- `inclusion-criteria`: Criteria for patients who qualify for the pathway
- `exclusion-criteria`: Criteria for patients who should not follow the pathway

## Process

1. Define the diagnosis codes and clinical criteria for inclusion.
2. Identify patient populations that need a different approach.
3. Specify age, comorbidity, and severity boundaries.
