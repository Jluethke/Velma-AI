# Contract Full Read

Read the full agreement and identify all sections and defined terms.

## Inputs

- `contract-text`: Full text of the contract
- `party-name`: Name of the reviewing party

## Outputs

- `section-inventory`: List of all sections and defined terms
- `contract-summary`: Summary of the agreement structure

## Process

1. Read the entire agreement from cover to signature block
2. Catalog every section heading and article number
3. List all defined terms and their definitions
