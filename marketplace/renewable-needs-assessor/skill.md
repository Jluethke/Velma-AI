# Renewable Needs Assessor

Assess the organization's energy consumption and renewable energy goals.

## Inputs

- `energy-consumption-data`: Annual energy consumption by facility and fuel type
- `sustainability-goals`: The organization's renewable energy or carbon reduction targets

## Outputs

- `renewable-requirement`: The amount of renewable energy needed (MWh) and the timeline
- `site-assessment`: Assessment of onsite renewable potential (roof space, land, solar resource)

## Process

1. Total the organization's annual electricity consumption across all facilities.
2. Determine what percentage must come from renewables to meet the stated goal.
3. Assess each facility for onsite generation potential (solar, wind, geothermal).
4. Calculate the gap between onsite potential and total renewable requirement.
