# Agreement And Repair

Close with a specific agreement and repair the relationship.

## Inputs

- `creative-options`: Options generated
- `conflict-situation`: The original conflict

## Outputs

- `resolution-agreement`: Who does what differently, by when, and the check-in date
- `relationship-repair`: A statement affirming the relationship

## Process

1. Make the agreement specific and time-bound.
2. Set a check-in: 'Let us revisit this in two weeks.'
3. Repair the relationship: 'I appreciate that we worked through this.'
4. Follow through immediately on whatever you committed to.
