# Estimate Total Calculator

Calculate the total estimate including labor, parts, shop supplies, taxes, and fees.

## Inputs

- `labor-times`: Labor hours for each operation
- `parts-pricing`: Parts pricing for each operation

## Outputs

- `estimate-total`: Total estimate broken down by labor, parts, supplies, tax, and fees
- `estimate-document`: Formatted estimate document ready to present to the customer

## Process

1. Multiply the labor hours by the shop labor rate for each operation.
2. Add the parts retail prices for each operation.
3. Add the shop supplies percentage and any environmental or disposal fees.
4. Calculate the tax and total, and format the estimate document.
