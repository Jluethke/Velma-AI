# Podcast Interview Conductor

Conduct the podcast interview and capture the recording.

## Inputs

- `interview-outline`: The host's interview outline
- `booking-details`: Recording platform and technical setup

## Outputs

- `raw-recording`: The raw audio or video recording file
- `interview-notes`: Notes on key moments, timestamps, and highlights

## Process

1. Test the recording setup before the guest joins.
2. Conduct the interview following the outline while allowing natural conversation.
3. Note timestamps of key moments, quotable lines, and highlights.
4. Thank the guest and confirm next steps (review, publish date, promotion).
