# Netgap Single Gap Selector

Select one gap to work on first, against the discipline of filling gaps one at a time, because genuine entry into a new community demands sustained presence and spreading yourself across several at once produces shallow presence in all of them and real entry into none.

## Inputs

- `candidate-communities`: Communities where the target people gather, per gap
- `community-access-notes`: How open each community is and what entry takes

## Outputs

- `selected-gap`: The single gap to fill first, with its chosen target community
- `selection-rationale`: Why this gap and community first: value, accessibility, and fit with your current capacity

## Process

1. Choose one gap. The temptation is to start on several at once, but entering a community well takes repeated presence over months, and parallel attempts dilute into shallow dabbling that reaches no community.
2. Balance value against accessibility and your current capacity. The highest-value gap is not always the right first move if its community is hard to enter and you have little time. An accessible medium-value gap can be the better starting point to build momentum.
3. Pick the specific community within the gap, not just the gap. The target is show up at this group, not break into this industry. Concreteness is what makes the next step actionable.
4. Write down why this one first. When the entry gets slow, as it will, the rationale reminds you why you chose it and keeps you from abandoning it to chase a shinier gap before this one has paid off.
