# Meeting Conductor

Conduct the public meeting including public comment.

## Inputs

- `meeting-agenda`: The approved agenda
- `public-notice`: The published public notice

## Outputs

- `meeting-actions`: Record of all actions taken (votes, motions, decisions)
- `public-comments`: Summary of public comments received

## Process

1. Call the meeting to order and confirm a quorum.
2. Work through each agenda item, recording motions, seconds, and votes.
3. Conduct the public comment period and record all comments.
4. Adjourn the meeting.
