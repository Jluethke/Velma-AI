# Creative Intake Gathering

Gather the project context from the requestor including goals, audience, deliverables, timeline, and constraints.

## Inputs

- `project-request`: The initial project request or description from the requestor
- `requestor-context`: Who is requesting the work and their role

## Outputs

- `intake-summary`: Structured summary of goals, audience, deliverables, timeline, and constraints
- `clarification-log`: Questions asked and answers received during intake

## Process

1. Review the initial request and identify what information is missing.
2. Ask clarifying questions about goals (what success looks like), audience (who this is for), deliverables (what formats), timeline (key dates), and constraints (brand rules, platform limits).
3. Structure the answers into the intake summary.
4. Produce the intake summary and clarification log.
