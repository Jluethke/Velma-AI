# Offer Response Handler

Handle the offer: never accept immediately, counter with justification, and get the final terms in writing.

## Inputs

- `package-priorities`: Your component priorities
- `negotiation-script`: Your prepared language

## Outputs

- `response-plan`: Step-by-step: express enthusiasm, ask for time, evaluate against market data, prepare counter, deliver counter with justification
- `counter-offer-template`: The specific counter-offer with each component, the ask, and the justification for each adjustment

## Process

1. Step 1: Express genuine enthusiasm for the role. This is not fake. You want the offer to improve, not to disappear.
2. Step 2: Ask for time to evaluate ('I am excited about this opportunity. Could I have until [date] to review the full offer?'). Never counter on the spot.
3. Step 3: Evaluate the offer against your market research and priorities. Identify the gaps: where is the offer below market or below your target?
4. Step 4: Counter with justification: 'Based on [market data/my experience/the role scope], I was expecting [target] for [component]. Is there flexibility here?' Counter on the most important components first.
