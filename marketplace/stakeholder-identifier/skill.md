# Stakeholder Identifier

Identify every stakeholder: anyone who affects or is affected by the outcome.

## Inputs

- `initiative-description`: What is being communicated about (a project, an organization, a change, a product)
- `known-stakeholders`: Stakeholders you already know about

## Outputs

- `stakeholder-list`: Every stakeholder with their role, relationship to the initiative, and what they care about
- `hidden-stakeholders`: Stakeholders who are not obvious but will be affected (downstream users, regulators, competitors, support teams)

## Process

1. List the obvious stakeholders: the sponsor, the team, the end users, the manager, the customers.
2. Walk the impact chain: who is affected by the output? Who is affected by the process? Who is affected by the resource consumption? Who will be asked to change their behavior?
3. Identify the hidden stakeholders: the team whose roadmap shifts because of this initiative, the support team that will handle questions, the compliance team that needs to approve, the competitor who will respond.
4. For each stakeholder, note what they care about: outcomes, timeline, cost, quality, process, recognition, risk.
