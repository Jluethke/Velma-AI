# Sfi Struggle Builder

Build the struggle, the believable middle where the protagonist works toward a resolution and it is not easy. The struggle is where memory and emotion attach, because a problem solved instantly teaches nothing and moves no one. A false step before the turning point makes the resolution earned rather than given.

## Inputs

- `problem-statement`: The problem that drives the struggle
- `protagonist`: The figure working through the struggle

## Outputs

- `struggle-arc`: The sequence of attempts, setbacks, and effort between the problem and the resolution
- `turning-point`: The moment the struggle shifts toward resolution, which carries the core message

## Process

1. Lay out the attempts the protagonist makes, including one that does not work. Struggle without a setback is not struggle.
2. Place a turning point where something changes: a realization, a new approach, outside help. This moment is where your core message lives.
3. Keep the struggle believable for this protagonist and this problem. An unrealistic struggle breaks the identification you built.
4. Make the turning point grow out of the protagonist's effort, so the resolution feels earned and the lesson feels true.
