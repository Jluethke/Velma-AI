# Work Sample Validity Tracker

After hires are made, track the correlation between work sample scores and real performance at six and twelve months. A weak correlation means the sample is testing the wrong task. Fix the task, not the scoring.

## Inputs

- `signal-comparison-grid`: The grid of work sample against interview signals
- `divergence-cases`: The notes on divergent candidates

## Outputs

- `validity-report`: The correlation between work sample scores and actual performance at six and twelve months
- `work-sample-revision-plan`: The specific change to the task, materials, or rubric indicated by the validity data

## Process

1. At six months, collect the manager's performance rating for each hire and compare it against their work sample score.
2. At twelve months, repeat. The twelve-month figure is the one that matters.
3. If the correlation sits below 0.40, revisit the task audit and pick a different task to simulate.
4. Track the divergent cases separately: did the high-sample, low-interview hires succeed? If so, the sample saved you from a wrong rejection.
