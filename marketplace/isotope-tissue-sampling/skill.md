# Isotope Tissue Sampling

Collect and prepare tissue samples from organisms across trophic levels for isotope analysis.

## Inputs

- `study-design`: Target species, trophic levels, sample sizes, and tissue types to collect
- `site-description`: Habitat type, location, and baseline resource characterization

## Outputs

- `tissue-samples`: Dried, ground tissue samples with species, tissue type, and collection metadata
- `baseline-samples`: Primary producer and primary consumer samples for baseline isotope values

## Process

1. Collect tissue samples from organisms at each target trophic level (producers, herbivores, predators)
2. Select the tissue type appropriate for the time scale of interest (muscle, liver, blood, feathers)
3. Dry samples at 60 degrees C, grind to a fine powder, and weigh aliquots into tin capsules
4. Include baseline organisms (e.g., filter feeders, primary producers) for trophic position calculation
