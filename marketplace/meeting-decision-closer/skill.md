# Meeting Decision Closer

Close the meeting with the decision, the owners, and the deadlines. Read them aloud. If anyone is confused about what was decided, clarify now. Ambiguity at this stage multiplies into wasted work.

## Inputs

- `disagreement-log`: What disagreements were raised and resolved
- `new-information`: Anything that changed the analysis

## Outputs

- `decision-record`: What was decided, by whom, with what caveats
- `action-items`: Each action, its owner, and its deadline
- `next-meeting-criteria`: If no decision was made: what is needed to decide and when to reconvene

## Process

1. State the decision aloud: 'We decided to [option]. [Person] owns implementation. Deadline is [date].'
2. For each action item: name the owner and the deadline. Do not assign actions to groups. Assign to one person.
3. If no decision was made: state what information is needed, who will get it, and when the group reconvenes. Do not schedule another meeting to 'continue the discussion.' Schedule a meeting to make the decision with the missing information.
4. Send the decision record within 1 hour of the meeting ending. Delay allows memory to edit what was actually decided.
