# Internal Cadence Planner

Define the cadence and calendar for internal communications.

## Inputs

- `audience-segments`: Internal audience segments
- `communication-types`: Types of internal communications

## Outputs

- `communication-calendar`: Calendar showing what goes to whom and when
- `cadence-rules`: Rules for frequency (no segment receives more than N emails per week)

## Process

1. Set the send frequency for each communication type (weekly all-hands, monthly department, quarterly leadership).
2. Set the maximum email frequency per segment per week to avoid overload.
3. Build the annual communication calendar with recurring and one-time sends.
4. Align the calendar with business cycles (quarterly reviews, annual planning, holidays).
