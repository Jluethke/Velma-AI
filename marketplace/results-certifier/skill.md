# Results Certifier

Tabulate and certify the election results.

## Inputs

- `voting-records`: All ballots cast
- `incident-log`: Any reported irregularities

## Outputs

- `certified-results`: The official certified election results
- `election-administration-record`: Complete election administration file

## Process

1. Tabulate all ballots including absentee and provisional where eligible.
2. Conduct the canvass and verify the tabulation.
3. Certify the results and publish the official returns.
4. Compile the complete election administration record for retention.
