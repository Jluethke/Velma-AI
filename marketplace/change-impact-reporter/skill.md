# Change Impact Reporter

Produce the change impact report.

## Inputs

- `consequence-patterns`: Recurring unintended consequence patterns
- `prevention-recommendations`: Prevention strategies

## Outputs

- `change-impact-report`: Complete change impact report with patterns and recommendations
- `process-improvement-actions`: Specific actions to improve the change process

## Process

1. Summarize all changes, their effects, and any unintended consequences.
2. Highlight the top recurring patterns.
3. List prevention recommendations ranked by impact.
4. Produce the change impact report and process improvement actions.
