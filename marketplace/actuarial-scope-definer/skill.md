# Actuarial Scope Definer

Define the scope and objectives of the actuarial analysis.

## Inputs

- `analysis-request`: The request for actuarial analysis (pricing, reserving, capital modeling, or rate filing)
- `line-of-business`: The insurance line and segment to analyze

## Outputs

- `analysis-scope`: Defined scope including the purpose, lines of business, evaluation dates, and methods to consider
- `data-requirements`: List of data needed (loss triangles, exposure data, rate history, industry benchmarks)

## Process

1. Clarify the purpose: pricing, reserve estimation, capital adequacy, or regulatory filing.
2. Define the lines of business, accident years, and evaluation dates in scope.
3. Identify the actuarial methods to consider based on the analysis type.
4. List the specific data sets required and their sources.
