# Resolution Need Identifier

Identify the corporate action requiring board approval and determine the resolution type.

## Inputs

- `proposed-action`: The corporate action that needs board authorization
- `governance-requirements`: Bylaws or governance rules specifying what requires board action

## Outputs

- `resolution-type`: Type of resolution needed (ordinary, special, written consent, unanimous)
- `approval-requirements`: Quorum, voting threshold, and notice requirements

## Process

1. Determine whether the proposed action requires board approval per the bylaws or governing documents.
2. Identify the resolution type based on the action (officer appointment, contract approval, budget, merger).
3. Determine the quorum and voting threshold required.
4. Identify the notice requirements and lead time.
