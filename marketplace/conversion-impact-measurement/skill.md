# Conversion Impact Measurement

Measure the impact of anti-persona targeting on conversion rates, churn, and customer quality.

## Inputs

- `filtered-messaging`: Revised messaging after the filter
- `not-for-you-statement`: The exclusion statement placed in marketing materials

## Outputs

- `targeting-impact-report`: Before/after comparison of conversion rate, churn rate, support ticket volume, and customer satisfaction
- `refinement-recommendations`: Adjustments to the anti-persona or messaging based on measured results

## Process

1. Set the baseline: conversion rate, churn rate, support tickets per customer, and average customer satisfaction score before the change.
2. Deploy the filtered messaging and not-for-you statement. Run for at least 30 days or 100 conversions, whichever comes first.
3. Measure: did conversion volume drop? If yes, by how much? Did conversion quality improve (lower churn, fewer tickets, higher satisfaction)?
4. Calculate the net effect: fewer but better customers often produces higher revenue per customer and lower cost to serve. Show the math.
