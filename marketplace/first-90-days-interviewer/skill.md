# First 90 Days Interviewer

Interview each top performer about their first 90 days. The question is not 'what did you do?' but 'what did you do that, looking back, set you apart from people who started at the same time and did not perform as well?' Most will initially say 'I just worked hard.' Push past that. Ask for specific actions, decisions, or habits.

## Inputs

- `top-performers-list`: The 5 people to interview
- `selection-method`: How they were selected

## Outputs

- `first-90-days-data`: For each performer: the specific actions, decisions, or habits from their first 90 days that they believe set them apart
- `common-early-actions`: Actions that appeared in 3 or more of the 5 interviews

## Process

1. Interview each performer separately (30-45 minutes). Start with: Think back to your first 90 days. What did you do differently from someone who started the same week and did not perform as well?
2. When they say 'I worked hard' or 'I asked questions,' push: What specifically did you ask about? Who did you seek out? What did you do that was not in the onboarding plan?
3. Record the specific, concrete actions. 'Built a relationship with the warehouse team in the first week' is useful. 'Was proactive' is not.
4. After all 5 interviews, find the actions that appear in 3 or more. These are the signal.
