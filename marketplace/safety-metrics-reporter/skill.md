# Safety Metrics Reporter

Produce the safety inspection report with metrics.

## Inputs

- `resolution-status`: Status of corrective actions
- `inspection-findings`: Findings from the inspection

## Outputs

- `safety-inspection-report`: Complete safety inspection report
- `safety-metrics`: Metrics on incident rate, violations, and resolution time

## Process

1. Compile all findings and corrective actions into the report.
2. Calculate safety metrics (total violations, resolution rate, average resolution time).
3. Track trends in violation types over time.
4. Distribute the report to the project team and safety committee.
