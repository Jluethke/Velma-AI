# Care Quality Monitor

Set up ongoing monitoring of care quality: visit cadence, what to observe, and when to escalate.

## Inputs

- `specific_needs`: Care needs being addressed
- `facility_chosen`: Facility details

## Outputs

- `visit_schedule`: Regular visit plan with varied times
- `observation_checklist`: What to look for each visit
- `escalation_protocol`: When and how to raise concerns

## Process

1. Set unannounced visit schedule at varied times
2. Build observation checklist (grooming, mood, meals, activities)
3. Monitor weight and medication compliance
4. Know who to contact for concerns
5. Document observations for pattern tracking
