# Succession Plan Writer

Produce the succession planning document.

## Inputs

- `critical-roles`: Critical roles
- `bench-strength-map`: Bench strength
- `development-plans`: Development plans

## Outputs

- `succession-plan-document`: Complete succession plan
- `emergency-succession-list`: Interim successors for immediate vacancy scenarios

## Process

1. Compile the plan with critical roles, candidates, and development paths
2. Include the emergency succession list for immediate coverage
3. Document the review cadence and governance process
4. Distribute to authorized leadership on a need-to-know basis
