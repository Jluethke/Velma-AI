# Material Decomposer

Break the material into small, atomic facts and ideas worth remembering.

## Inputs

- `source-material`: The material you want to retain
- `retention-goal`: What you need to recall and why

## Outputs

- `atomic-items`: The smallest facts that stand on their own
- `priority-tags`: A priority and topic tag for each item

## Process

1. Split the material into the smallest facts that stand on their own.
2. Keep only items you genuinely need to recall from memory.
3. Tag each item by priority and topic.
4. Drop items that are better looked up than memorized.
