# Handyman Review Referral

Manage customer reviews and referral tracking to grow the business.

## Inputs

- `job-cost-log`: Completed job records
- `job-pipeline`: Client contact information

## Outputs

- `review-collection-results`: Reviews collected, average rating, and platform distribution
- `handyman-operations-plan`: Complete handyman service operations plan

## Process

1. Send a review request after each completed job with links to Google and Yelp.
2. Track the referral source for each new inquiry.
3. Respond to all reviews (positive and negative) within 48 hours.
4. Calculate the customer acquisition cost by referral channel.
