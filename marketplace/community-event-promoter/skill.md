# Community Event Promoter

Promote the event through internal and external channels.

## Inputs

- `volunteer-roster`: Confirmed volunteer team
- `volunteer-instructions`: Role details for volunteers

## Outputs

- `promotion-plan`: Channels, timeline, and materials for promoting the event
- `registration-count`: Number of registrations or RSVPs received

## Process

1. Create the promotional materials: flyer, social media graphics, email announcement, and bulletin insert.
2. Distribute through internal channels: email list, bulletin, announcements, and social media.
3. If community-open, distribute externally: community boards, local media, neighborhood flyers, partner organizations.
4. Track registrations or RSVPs and adjust logistics if attendance projections change.
