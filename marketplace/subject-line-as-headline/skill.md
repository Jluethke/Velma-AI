# Subject Line As Headline

Write the subject line as a headline that tells the reader whether to open and what to do.

## Inputs

- `email-purpose`: Why you are writing
- `recipient`: Who will read this

## Outputs

- `subject-line`: A subject line with action type and topic: 'Decision needed: Q1 launch date by Friday'
- `email-type`: action-required, decision-needed, FYI, or response-requested

## Process

1. Start the subject with the email type: 'Action needed:', 'Decision by [date]:', 'FYI:', or 'Response requested:'.
2. Include the topic and deadline in the subject.
3. Never use vague subjects: 'Quick question', 'Touching base', 'Update'.
4. If no action required, say so: 'FYI: No action needed.'
