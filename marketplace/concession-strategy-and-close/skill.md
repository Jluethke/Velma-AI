# Concession Strategy And Close

Plan your concessions in advance: each concession is smaller than the last, and each is traded for something in return.

## Inputs

- `anchor-offer`: The starting point
- `reservation-price`: The floor

## Outputs

- `concession-plan`: Pre-planned concessions: the first is the largest, each subsequent one is smaller, and each is conditional on receiving something in return
- `deal-structure`: The final deal terms including all trade-offs negotiated

## Process

1. Plan 3-4 concessions in advance, each smaller than the last. If the first concession is $10K, the second is $5K, the third is $2K. The decreasing size signals that you are approaching your limit.
2. Never concede without trading. Every concession you make should be paired with a request: 'I can do $100K if you can commit to a 2-year contract' or 'I can move on the price if we can accelerate the timeline.'
3. Avoid splitting the difference. It feels fair but leaves value on the table. Instead of 'let us meet in the middle,' trade on different dimensions: 'I will hold on price but give on payment terms.'
4. Close by summarizing the full deal, including all trade-offs. Both parties should feel they gave something and got something. If one party feels they lost, the deal is unstable.
