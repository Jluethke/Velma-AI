# Crystal Sample Preparation

Prepare the sample at the target concentration and purity for crystallization.

## Inputs

- `purified-sample`: Purified protein or compound with purity assessment
- `target-concentration`: Desired concentration range for crystallization

## Outputs

- `concentrated-sample`: Sample at target concentration with buffer conditions documented
- `sample-quality-report`: DLS, SDS-PAGE, or other quality metrics confirming monodispersity

## Process

1. Concentrate the sample by ultrafiltration to the target range
2. Measure concentration by UV absorbance or Bradford assay
3. Assess monodispersity by dynamic light scattering
4. Document buffer composition, pH, and any additives present
