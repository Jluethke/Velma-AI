# Literature Search Protocol

Conduct a structured literature search across databases, filter by relevance and recency, extract key findings, and identify gaps in current knowledge.

## Inputs

- `research-topic`: The subject area or question to investigate
- `scope-constraints`: Date range, language, and database preferences

## Outputs

- `source-list`: Ranked list of relevant papers with citation data
- `knowledge-gap-summary`: Identified gaps and open questions in existing work
- `key-findings-digest`: Condensed summary of established results

## Process

1. Define search terms and Boolean operators from the research topic
2. Query target databases and collect candidate papers
3. Screen titles and abstracts against inclusion criteria
4. Extract key findings, methods, and conclusions from included papers
5. Synthesize gaps between what is known and what remains unanswered
