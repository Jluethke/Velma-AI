# Grooming Supply Vehicle Tracker

Track supplies and vehicle maintenance for the mobile grooming unit.

## Inputs

- `daily-route`: Daily appointment load
- `grooming-menu`: Products and supplies used per service

## Outputs

- `supply-status`: Current supply levels and restock needs
- `vehicle-maintenance-log`: Vehicle and equipment maintenance schedule and history

## Process

1. Track product usage per appointment (shampoo, conditioner, blade wear).
2. Monitor water tank levels and waste tank capacity.
3. Schedule vehicle maintenance (oil changes, generator service, equipment calibration).
4. Restock supplies at the reorder point.
