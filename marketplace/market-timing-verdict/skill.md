# Market Timing Verdict

Reach a timing verdict: too early, too late, or on time, and state the action it implies. A timing verdict is only useful if it changes what you do next, not just a label.

## Inputs

- `timing-signal-summary`: The three signals summarized together
- `timing-tensions`: Where the signals disagree and what that implies

## Outputs

- `timing-verdict`: Too early, too late, or on time, with the reasoning
- `timing-action`: What the verdict implies you should do now

## Process

1. Weigh the combined signals into one verdict: too early, too late, or on time.
2. If too early, decide whether to wait, build a bridge product, or shape the market.
3. If too late, decide whether a differentiated entry is still possible or the window has closed.
4. If on time, state what moving now requires and what would make you move faster.
