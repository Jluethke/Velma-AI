# Solution Matching

Match each complaint theme to your product's specific solution for that problem.

## Inputs

- `complaint-themes`: The recurring complaints
- `competitor-specific-complaints`: Unique weaknesses

## Outputs

- `solution-matches`: For each complaint theme: how your product specifically solves this problem, with evidence (screenshots, data, customer quotes)
- `unaddressed-complaints`: Complaint themes where your product has the same problem (do NOT use these in sales conversations)

## Process

1. For each complaint theme, document exactly how your product addresses it. Be specific: 'Our onboarding takes 15 minutes, not 3 hours, because we auto-detect the integration settings.'
2. Provide evidence for each claim. A feature list is not evidence. A screenshot, a customer quote, or a measured metric is evidence.
3. Identify complaint themes where your product has the same problem. These are NOT selling points. Using them will backfire when the prospect discovers the truth.
4. The strongest solution matches are where the complaint is universal (every competitor has it) and your solution is unique (only you have fixed it). These are your differentiation.
