# Phone Screen Conductor

Conduct phone screens to assess basic fit, motivation, and salary expectations.

## Inputs

- `shortlist`: Candidates who passed resume screening
- `role-scorecard`: Key outcomes and qualifications

## Outputs

- `phone-screen-results`: Pass/fail decisions with notes per candidate
- `interview-candidates`: Candidates advancing to interviews

## Process

1. Schedule the phone screens
2. Ask about motivation, experience highlights, and salary expectations
3. Assess communication skills and cultural alignment
4. Decide advance or decline for each candidate
