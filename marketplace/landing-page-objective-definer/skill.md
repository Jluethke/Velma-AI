# Landing Page Objective Definer

Define the landing page objective, target audience, and success metrics.

## Inputs

- `campaign-context`: The campaign or product the landing page supports
- `target-audience`: Who the page is for

## Outputs

- `page-objective`: The single action the page should drive (signup, download, purchase)
- `audience-profile`: Summary of the target audience's needs and motivations

## Process

1. Identify the one action the page should drive.
2. Describe the target audience and what matters to them.
3. Define the success metric (conversion rate target, signups per week).
