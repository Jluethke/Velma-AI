# Mineral Identification Workflow

Identify minerals in hand samples and thin sections using optical properties, crystal form, hardness, and confirmatory tests.

## Inputs

- `sample-inventory`: Rock and mineral samples to identify
- `core-log`: Lithologic context for each sample

## Outputs

- `mineral-id-table`: Identified minerals per sample with diagnostic properties
- `thin-section-descriptions`: Petrographic descriptions of texture, mineralogy, and alteration
- `rock-classification`: Rock type and name per sample using IUGS or appropriate scheme

## Process

1. Examine hand samples for color, luster, streak, hardness, and crystal form
2. Prepare thin sections and examine under polarized light
3. Record mineral assemblage, texture, and modal proportions
4. Classify rocks using standard schemes (IUGS igneous, sedimentary grain-size, metamorphic grade)
