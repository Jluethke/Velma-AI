# Risk Appetite Definer

Define risk appetite and tolerance thresholds for the organization.

## Inputs

- `scored-risk-register`: Risk register with scores
- `risk-heat-map`: Heat map of risks

## Outputs

- `risk-appetite-statement`: Documented risk appetite and tolerance by category
- `threshold-criteria`: Criteria for acceptable, tolerable, and unacceptable risk levels

## Process

1. Review the scored risks with leadership.
2. Define the appetite (how much risk the organization is willing to accept) per category.
3. Set tolerance thresholds that trigger escalation or action.
4. Document the risk appetite statement.
