# Violation Notice Reviewer

Review the violation notice and assess the property for compliance.

## Inputs

- `violation-notice`: HOA violation notice with cited rule and deadline
- `property-details`: Property address and current condition

## Outputs

- `violation-assessment`: Assessment of whether the violation is valid and what correction is needed
- `remediation-options`: Options for resolving the violation with cost and timeline

## Process

1. Read the violation notice and identify the cited rule.
2. Inspect the property to confirm or dispute the violation.
3. List remediation options with estimated cost and timeline.
