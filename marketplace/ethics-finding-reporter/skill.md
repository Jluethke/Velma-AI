# Ethics Finding Reporter

Analyze findings, determine the outcome, and recommend corrective actions.

## Inputs

- `interview-summaries`: Interview summaries
- `evidence-collection`: Collected evidence

## Outputs

- `investigation-findings`: Findings with substantiated or unsubstantiated determination
- `investigation-report`: Complete investigation report with recommended actions

## Process

1. Analyze the evidence against the allegation.
2. Determine whether the allegation is substantiated, unsubstantiated, or inconclusive.
3. Recommend corrective or disciplinary actions.
4. Compile the investigation report and close the case.
