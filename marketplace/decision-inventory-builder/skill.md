# Decision Inventory Builder

List all the decisions currently pending or recently deferred. Most people have 5 to 20 pending decisions they have not made. Getting them out of your head and into a list is the first step. Include decisions you have been avoiding.

## Inputs

- `pending-decisions`: List of decisions you need to make or have been deferring
- `context-notes`: Any relevant constraints or background for the decisions

## Outputs

- `decision-inventory`: All pending decisions stated as clear choices
- `deferred-decisions`: Decisions that have been deferred more than once, flagged for attention

## Process

1. Write down every decision you are currently deferring, avoiding, or have not finalized.
2. State each as a clear choice: 'Should I X or Y?' not 'What should I do about Z?'
3. Flag any decision that has been deferred more than twice. Repeated deferral is itself a decision (to maintain the status quo) and should be evaluated as one.
4. Include small decisions alongside big ones. The volume of small undecided items drains more energy than most people realize.
