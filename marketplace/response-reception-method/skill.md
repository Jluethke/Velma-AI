# Response Reception Method

Receive the feedback without defensiveness, because how you receive feedback determines whether people give it to you in the future.

## Inputs

- `ask-scripts`: The asks you will make
- `feedback-sources`: The people giving feedback

## Outputs

- `reception-rules`: Rules for receiving feedback: no defending, no explaining, no minimizing, just listen and ask follow-up questions
- `follow-up-questions`: Questions to ask when you need more specificity: 'Can you give me an example?' and 'What would you have done differently?'

## Process

1. When receiving feedback, your only job is to understand it. Do not defend, explain, or justify. The moment you defend, the person stops being honest.
2. Say 'thank you' after every piece of feedback, positive or negative. This signals that you value honesty and will not punish it.
3. Ask clarifying questions: 'Can you give me a specific example of when that happened?' and 'If you were in my position, what would you have done instead?' These turn vague feedback into actionable insight.
4. If a piece of feedback stings, that is usually a sign it is accurate. The feedback that does not sting is the feedback you already knew.
