# Decisionjournal Log Assumptions

Record the key assumptions the decision rests on and what evidence would change your mind.

## Inputs

- `expected-outcome`: The result you expect from the decision.
- `confidence-level`: A confidence percentage on the expected result.

## Outputs

- `key-assumptions`: The assumptions that must hold for the outcome to happen.
- `change-my-mind`: The new fact that would change your mind.

## Process

1. List the assumptions that must hold for the expected outcome to happen.
2. For each assumption, write what you would see if it were wrong.
3. State plainly what new fact would change your mind.
