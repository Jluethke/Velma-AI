# Story Audience And Question Definer

Define the audience and the question being answered.

## Inputs

- `analysis-request`: What question or problem the analysis addresses
- `target-audience`: Who will receive the data story

## Outputs

- `audience-context`: What the audience knows, what they care about, what action they can take
- `core-question`: The single question the data story will answer

## Process

1. Identify who the audience is and what decisions they can make.
2. State the core question in one sentence.
3. Note what the audience already knows so the story does not repeat it.
