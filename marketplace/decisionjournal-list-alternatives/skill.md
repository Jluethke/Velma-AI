# Decisionjournal List Alternatives

Write down the options you considered and why you set each one aside.

## Inputs

- `logged-decision`: The decision written in one or two plain sentences.
- `decision-context`: The situation that prompted the decision and who it affects.

## Outputs

- `alternatives-considered`: The real options you weighed.
- `rejected-reasons`: Why you rejected each alternative.

## Process

1. List the real alternatives you weighed, not just the one you picked.
2. Write why you rejected each alternative.
3. Note any option you did not consider but should have.
