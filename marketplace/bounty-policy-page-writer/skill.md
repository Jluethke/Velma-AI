# Bounty Policy Page Writer

Write the public-facing program policy page and launch the program.

## Inputs

- `program-scope`: Program scope and rules of engagement
- `reward-schedule`: Reward schedule
- `triage-workflow`: Triage workflow for reference in policy

## Outputs

- `policy-page-content`: Complete policy page text ready to publish
- `launch-checklist`: Checklist of items to verify before going live

## Process

1. Write the policy page with scope, rules, rewards, safe harbor, and SLA commitments
2. Review for legal accuracy (safe harbor, exclusions, payment terms)
3. Build the launch checklist: internal team ready, platform configured, payment method set up, legal approved
4. Plan the launch announcement (private invite first, then public)
