# Awareness Training Report Compiler

Compile the effectiveness metrics, gaps, and delivery record into a program report with an executive summary and next-cycle recommendations.

## Inputs

- `effectiveness-metrics`: Completion, pass, and trend data by segment and objective.
- `knowledge-gap-report`: Where results fall short of the passing standard.
- `delivery-schedule`: The delivery record with dates and channels.

## Outputs

- `training-program-report`: Full report with objectives, delivery record, results, and remaining gaps.
- `executive-summary`: One-page summary of reach, results, and recommendations for the next cycle.

## Process

1. Assemble the objectives, delivery record, and results into one report.
2. Highlight the segments and objectives that met and missed their standard.
3. Write a one-page summary of reach and results for leadership.
4. Recommend changes to content, audience, or schedule for the next cycle.
