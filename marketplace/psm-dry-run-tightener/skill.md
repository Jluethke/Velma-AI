# Psm Dry Run Tightener

Run the presentation once out loud and tighten it. The dry run reveals the slides that confuse, the transitions that stall, and the places where you talk past your own point. A slide that you cannot explain in one or two spoken sentences is a slide that holds too much.

## Inputs

- `storyline-sequence`: The sequenced slides to run through
- `evidence-gaps`: The weak-evidence points flagged earlier, to confirm they were fixed or cut

## Outputs

- `tightened-deck`: The deck after the dry run: confusing slides fixed, weak slides cut, transitions smoothed
- `dry-run-notes`: Where the run lost clarity or stalled, and what changed as a result

## Process

1. Present the whole thing aloud to one listener or to a recording. Note every slide where you hesitate or over-explain.
2. For each slide, confirm you can state its one idea in one or two spoken sentences. If you cannot, the slide holds too much, so split or cut it.
3. Confirm the evidence gaps flagged earlier were closed. Any argument still thin should be strengthened or dropped now, not in the room.
4. Check the transitions. If moving between two slides needs a long bridge, their order is wrong or one of them is unnecessary.
