# Small Group Communication Facilitator

Facilitate communication within and across small groups.

## Inputs

- `group-roster`: Members and leaders of each group
- `waitlist`: Members waiting for placement

## Outputs

- `communication-channels`: The messaging platform or method for each group (group text, email list, app group)
- `leader-update-cadence`: How often leaders report to the organization and what they report

## Process

1. Set up the communication channel for each group: group text thread, email list, or messaging app group.
2. Provide leaders with a template for weekly or meeting-day communication: reminder, topic, and any prep.
3. Set the cadence for leader reports to the organization: attendance, concerns, and highlights.
4. Facilitate cross-group communication for organization-wide events or combined group activities.
