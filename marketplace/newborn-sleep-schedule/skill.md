# Newborn Sleep Schedule

Build an age-appropriate sleep schedule with wake windows, nap transitions, and bedtime routine.

## Inputs

- `child_age_weeks`: Current age in weeks
- `feeding_method`: Breast, bottle, or combo
- `sleep_environment`: Crib setup, room sharing, etc.

## Outputs

- `daily_schedule`: Hour-by-hour schedule template
- `wake_windows`: Age-appropriate wake windows
- `bedtime_routine`: Step-by-step bedtime routine

## Process

1. Determine age-appropriate wake windows
2. Map feeding schedule to sleep blocks
3. Design bedtime routine sequence
4. Plan nap transition timeline for coming months
