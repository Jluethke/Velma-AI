# Plane Polarized Light Exam

Examine the thin section under plane-polarized light to record color, relief, cleavage, and texture.

## Inputs

- `thin-section`: Polished thin section mounted on a glass slide
- `sample-context`: Field location, hand sample description, and geological setting

## Outputs

- `ppl-observations`: Mineral colors, relief, crystal habit, cleavage, and textural relationships under PPL
- `texture-description`: Rock texture: grain size, shape, sorting, fabric, and any deformation features

## Process

1. Scan the entire section at low magnification to note the overall texture and mineral distribution
2. Record each mineral phase: color, pleochroism, relief, crystal form, and cleavage
3. Document the texture: equigranular vs porphyritic, foliated vs massive, grain boundaries
4. Note any alteration, veining, inclusions, or accessory phases
