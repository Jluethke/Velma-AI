# Change Advisory Board Review

Review and approve the change through the advisory board.

## Inputs

- `change-request`: The change request to review
- `change-classification`: The change classification
- `approval-requirements`: Required approvers

## Outputs

- `approval-decision`: Approved, rejected, or deferred with rationale
- `conditions`: Any conditions attached to the approval

## Process

1. Present the change to the advisory board with the impact assessment.
2. Review the rollback plan and test evidence.
3. Vote on approval, rejection, or deferral.
4. Record the decision and any conditions.
