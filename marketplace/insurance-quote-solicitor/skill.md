# Insurance Quote Solicitor

Solicit competitive quotes and produce the insurance review report.

## Inputs

- `risk-assessment`: Risk exposure analysis
- `coverage-recommendations`: Recommended coverage changes

## Outputs

- `competitive-quotes`: Quotes from multiple carriers with comparison
- `insurance-review-report`: Complete insurance review with recommendations

## Process

1. Send the coverage specifications to at least three carriers.
2. Compare quotes on premium, deductibles, exclusions, and carrier financial strength.
3. Recommend the carrier and produce the insurance review report.
