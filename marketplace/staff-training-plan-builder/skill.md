# Staff Training Plan Builder

Build the training plan to embed the service standards.

## Inputs

- `service-standards`: Service standards
- `recovery-protocols`: Recovery protocols

## Outputs

- `training-plan`: Training program by role and topic
- `certification-criteria`: How staff demonstrate competency

## Process

1. Group standards and protocols by staff role.
2. Design training modules for each group.
3. Set the certification criteria for each role.
4. Schedule the training rollout.
