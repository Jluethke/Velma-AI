# Voiceprint Enrollment Designer

Design the voiceprint enrollment process for callers.

## Inputs

- `authentication-requirements`: Security level needed and regulatory constraints
- `caller-population`: Estimated caller volume and demographics

## Outputs

- `enrollment-flow`: Step-by-step enrollment process (passphrase, free-speech, or hybrid)
- `enrollment-scripts`: Prompts and instructions for callers during enrollment

## Process

1. Choose the enrollment method (text-dependent passphrase, text-independent free speech, or hybrid).
2. Design the enrollment flow with the minimum audio needed for a reliable voiceprint.
3. Write the caller prompts that guide enrollment naturally within an existing call.
