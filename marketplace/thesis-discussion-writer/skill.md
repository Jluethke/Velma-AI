# Thesis Discussion Writer

Write the discussion and conclusion chapters interpreting results in the context of literature, acknowledging limitations, and proposing future research.

## Inputs

- `results-chapter`: Completed results from the analysis step
- `literature-synthesis`: Literature review for contextualizing findings
- `gap-statement`: The original gap statement to evaluate whether it was addressed

## Outputs

- `discussion-chapter`: Discussion interpreting each finding relative to prior literature
- `limitations-section`: Honest assessment of study limitations and their impact on conclusions
- `conclusion-and-future`: Concluding summary and specific directions for future research

## Process

1. Restate each key finding and interpret it in the context of existing literature
2. Explain unexpected results and generate possible explanations
3. Discuss implications for theory, practice, or policy
4. Acknowledge limitations and assess how they affect the conclusions
5. Propose specific future research questions arising from the findings
