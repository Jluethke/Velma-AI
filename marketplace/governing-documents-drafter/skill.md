# Governing Documents Drafter

Draft the operating agreement, bylaws, or partnership agreement.

## Inputs

- `entity-type`: Entity type
- `business-plan`: Business plan and ownership structure

## Outputs

- `governing-document`: Operating agreement, bylaws, or partnership agreement
- `ownership-schedule`: Ownership percentages and capital contribution schedule

## Process

1. Draft the governing document appropriate for the entity type
2. Define ownership percentages, voting rights, and distribution rules
3. Include management structure, decision-making, and dispute resolution
