# Playbook Assembly And Storage

Assemble the playbook, store it in multiple locations, and brief the trusted persons.

## Inputs

- `medical-section`: The medical emergency section
- `financial-section`: The financial emergency section
- `disaster-section`: The disaster and home emergency section
- `incapacitation-section`: The incapacitation and security section

## Outputs

- `emergency-playbook`: The complete playbook document with all 6 sections, table of contents, and last-updated date
- `storage-and-briefing-record`: Where the playbook is stored (digital and physical locations) and who has been briefed on its existence and location

## Process

1. Compile all sections into a single document with a table of contents and a last-updated date on each section.
2. Store in at least three locations: one physical (printed, in a fireproof safe or a known drawer), one digital (cloud storage shared with trusted persons), and one with a trusted person outside the household.
3. Brief each trusted person: show them where the playbook is, walk them through the sections relevant to their role, and confirm they can access the digital copy.
4. Set a review cadence: update the playbook whenever a major life event occurs (move, marriage, birth, death, job change, new insurance) and conduct a full review annually.
