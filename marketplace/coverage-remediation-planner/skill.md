# Coverage Remediation Planner

Turn the decisions into a plan with owners, timelines, and a review cadence. Skills change as the work changes, so a matrix left to age becomes unreliable within a couple of quarters.

## Inputs

- `remediation-decisions`: The chosen paths
- `decision-rationale`: The reasons behind them

## Outputs

- `remediation-plan`: For each decision, the owner, the target, and what competent looks like, sequenced by impact
- `review-cadence`: A quarterly review to reassess levels, add new skills, and re-scan for single points and gaps

## Process

1. For each cross-training decision, name who learns, who teaches, what competent looks like, and a realistic target date.
2. For each hire or contract decision, name the owner and the trigger to start.
3. Sequence the plan by the impact rankings, addressing the highest-impact single points and gaps first.
4. Set a quarterly review to reassess levels, add new skills from recent work, and re-scan for new single points and gaps.
