# Small Group Leader Recruiter

Recruit, screen, and train small group leaders.

## Inputs

- `group-types`: The group categories and their requirements
- `meeting-guidelines`: How groups are run

## Outputs

- `leader-roster`: Approved leaders with their assigned group types and training status
- `training-curriculum`: Leader training materials covering facilitation, pastoral care basics, and reporting

## Process

1. Identify potential leaders from active, engaged members with relevant skills or experience.
2. Screen candidates: interview, background check if required, and reference check.
3. Train leaders on facilitation, handling sensitive situations, when to escalate to pastoral staff, and reporting attendance.
4. Match each leader to a group type based on their gifts and interests.
