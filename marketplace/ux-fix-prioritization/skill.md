# Ux Fix Prioritization

Prioritize the usability fixes by impact and effort.

## Inputs

- `issue-list`: All issues found during the audit
- `competitive-gaps`: Areas where competitors are ahead

## Outputs

- `prioritized-fixes`: Issues ranked by priority with estimated effort and expected impact
- `ux-audit-report`: Complete audit report (scope, findings, benchmarks, recommendations)

## Process

1. Score each issue on impact (how many users affected, how badly) and effort (design + dev time).
2. Rank by impact-to-effort ratio: high impact, low effort fixes first.
3. Group into quick wins, planned improvements, and long-term investments.
4. Compile the full audit report with executive summary, findings, benchmarks, and prioritized recommendations.
5. Produce the prioritized fixes and audit report.
