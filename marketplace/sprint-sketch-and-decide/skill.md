# Sprint Sketch And Decide

Sketch solutions individually and decide on the best one (days 2-3).

## Inputs

- `problem-map`: The mapped problem space
- `sprint-target`: The target area to solve

## Outputs

- `winning-sketch`: The selected solution sketch with annotations
- `storyboard`: Step-by-step storyboard of the selected solution

## Process

1. Have each team member sketch their solution individually (no groupthink).
2. Present all sketches anonymously and vote on the strongest ideas.
3. The decider picks the winning solution or combines elements from multiple sketches.
4. Create a step-by-step storyboard of the winning solution.
5. Produce the winning sketch and storyboard.
