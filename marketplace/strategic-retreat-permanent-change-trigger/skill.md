# Strategic Retreat Permanent Change Trigger

Define the permanent-change trigger: the point where the conditions that made the commitment sensible have changed for good. A temporary setback is worth pushing through; a permanent change is not.

## Inputs

- `commitment-profile`: The commitment and its goal stated clearly
- `retreat-triggers`: The named conditions that would signal retreat

## Outputs

- `permanent-change-trigger`: The condition signaling a permanent shift against the commitment
- `change-indicators`: The observable signs that the shift is permanent, not temporary

## Process

1. Name the conditions the commitment depends on: a market need, a technology, a rule, a cost.
2. For each, define what a permanent adverse change would look like, not just a dip.
3. List the observable signs that would tell a permanent change from a temporary one.
4. Set the trigger to fire on permanent change, and resist firing it on normal volatility.
