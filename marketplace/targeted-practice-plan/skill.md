# Targeted Practice Plan

Build a 90-day practice plan for the weakest skill, using deliberate practice principles rather than generic study.

## Inputs

- `weakest-skill`: The skill to improve
- `gap-analysis`: Specific sub-skills where you fall short

## Outputs

- `practice-plan`: 90-day plan with weekly targets, specific drills, and feedback mechanisms
- `progress-milestones`: Measurable checkpoints at 30, 60, and 90 days

## Process

1. For each deficient sub-skill, design a drill that isolates that sub-skill and provides immediate feedback on performance.
2. Schedule practice sessions: minimum 3 times per week, 30-60 minutes each, focused on one sub-skill per session.
3. Set measurable milestones at 30, 60, and 90 days. Milestones should be output-based (produced X quality of work) not input-based (studied for Y hours).
4. Identify a feedback source for each sub-skill: a mentor, a peer, a test, a portfolio review, or a measurable metric.
