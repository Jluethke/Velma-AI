# Curriculum Selector

Choose curriculum materials matching learning styles, educational philosophy, and budget.

## Inputs

- `learning_styles`: Each child's learning style
- `educational_philosophy`: Classical, Charlotte Mason, unschooling, etc.
- `budget`: Annual curriculum budget
- `required_subjects`: Subjects required by state

## Outputs

- `curriculum_choices`: Selected curriculum per subject
- `materials_list`: Books and materials to purchase
- `cost_total`: Total curriculum cost

## Process

1. Match philosophy to curriculum options
2. Review curricula for each required subject
3. Consider child's learning style fit
4. Compare costs and read reviews
5. Select and order materials
