# Documentary Field Shooter

Conduct interviews and capture field footage and b-roll.

## Inputs

- `interview-schedule`: Interview subjects and questions
- `field-shoot-plan`: Locations and shot list

## Outputs

- `interview-footage`: Recorded interviews
- `b-roll-footage`: Field footage and supplementary visuals

## Process

1. Set up and conduct each interview
2. Capture b-roll at each field location
3. Collect archival materials, photographs, and documents
4. Back up and organize all media daily
