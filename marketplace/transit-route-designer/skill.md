# Transit Route Designer

Design or adjust routes and stops based on demand analysis.

## Inputs

- `demand-patterns`: Ridership demand patterns
- `underserved-areas`: Areas with unmet demand

## Outputs

- `route-network`: Route map with stop locations and connections
- `service-standards`: Service frequency, span, and capacity targets per route

## Process

1. Design routes to connect high-demand origins and destinations.
2. Place stops at locations with the best balance of coverage and travel speed.
3. Set service frequency targets by time period (peak: 10-15 min, midday: 20-30 min, evening: 30-60 min).
4. Ensure connections between routes at transfer points.
