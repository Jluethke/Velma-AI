# Pico Question Builder

Formulate a clinical research question in PICO format: population, intervention, comparator, and outcome, with inclusion and exclusion criteria.

## Inputs

- `clinical-question`: The clinical problem or treatment to investigate
- `prior-evidence`: Existing trial results and guidelines

## Outputs

- `pico-statement`: Structured PICO question with defined terms
- `eligibility-criteria`: Inclusion and exclusion criteria for enrollment
- `study-type-recommendation`: Recommended design (RCT, cohort, case-control) with justification

## Process

1. Define the population with specific demographic and clinical characteristics
2. Specify the intervention (dose, route, duration, frequency)
3. Choose the comparator (placebo, active control, usual care)
4. Define the primary outcome with its measurement method and clinically meaningful difference
