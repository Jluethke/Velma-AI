# Risk Category Identifier

Identify risks across strategic, operational, financial, compliance, and reputational categories.

## Inputs

- `organization-context`: Description of the organization, industry, and scope
- `existing-risk-data`: Prior risk assessments or incident logs

## Outputs

- `risk-register-draft`: Initial list of identified risks by category
- `risk-sources`: Sources and evidence for each risk

## Process

1. Review the organization structure, strategy, and operating environment.
2. Identify risks in each category through interviews, workshops, and data review.
3. Document each risk with a description, category, and source.
4. Compile the draft risk register.
