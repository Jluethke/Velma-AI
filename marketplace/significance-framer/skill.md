# Significance Framer

Write the significance and innovation sections showing why the work matters and what is novel about the approach.

## Inputs

- `specific-aims-draft`: Aims page from the drafting step
- `literature-review`: Key citations establishing the state of the field
- `competing-approaches`: Existing approaches and their limitations that the proposal addresses

## Outputs

- `significance-section`: Written significance section grounded in the literature and health/societal impact
- `innovation-section`: Innovation section highlighting novel concepts, approaches, or technologies
- `citation-list`: Organized reference list for the significance and innovation sections

## Process

1. Establish the burden or scale of the problem being addressed
2. Review the current state of knowledge with key supporting citations
3. Identify the critical barrier or gap that the proposed work fills
4. Articulate what is innovative about the proposed approach compared to existing methods
5. Connect the expected outcomes to tangible impact on the field, health, or society
