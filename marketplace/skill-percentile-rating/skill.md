# Skill Percentile Rating

Rate your honest percentile standing in each skill against the people who use it professionally.

## Inputs

- `skill-inventory`: A grouped list of skills you currently use
- `candidate-skill-list`: Skills you could realistically develop within a year or two

## Outputs

- `percentile-ratings`: An honest percentile estimate for each skill
- `top-quartile-skills`: Skills where you sit in or could reach the top quartile

## Process

1. For each skill, estimate your percentile against people who use it professionally.
2. Base the estimate on evidence such as results, feedback, or comparison.
3. Flag any skill where you sit in the top quartile or could reach it.
4. Separate skills that are already strong from skills that still need work.
