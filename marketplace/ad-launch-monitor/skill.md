# Ad Launch Monitor

Launch the campaign and monitor performance against KPIs.

## Inputs

- `ad-creatives`: Approved ad creatives
- `tracking-config`: Tracking setup

## Outputs

- `launch-checklist`: Pre-launch verification checklist
- `campaign-setup-document`: Complete campaign setup and initial performance

## Process

1. Run the pre-launch checklist (targeting, budget, tracking, creatives)
2. Launch the campaign
3. Monitor the first 48 hours and flag any issues
