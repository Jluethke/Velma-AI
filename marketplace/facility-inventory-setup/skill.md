# Facility Inventory Setup

Inventory the facility spaces and equipment available for use.

## Inputs

- `facility-description`: The building or campus and its spaces (sanctuary, hall, classrooms, kitchen, grounds)
- `equipment-list`: Available equipment (tables, chairs, AV, kitchen, sports)

## Outputs

- `space-catalog`: Each space with capacity, features, available hours, and restrictions
- `equipment-catalog`: Available equipment with quantities and condition

## Process

1. List each usable space: name, capacity (seated, standing, classroom), features (kitchen access, AV, outdoor), and parking.
2. Note the restrictions: noise limits, hours of use, alcohol policy, decorating rules.
3. Inventory the equipment: tables, chairs, AV gear, kitchen equipment, sports equipment, and their condition.
4. Set the available hours for each space, accounting for regular programming.
