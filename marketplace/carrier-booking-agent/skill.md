# Carrier Booking Agent

Book the selected carriers and confirm pickup schedules.

## Inputs

- `consolidated-plan`: Consolidated shipment groupings
- `carrier-comparison`: Rate and service comparison across carriers

## Outputs

- `booking-confirmations`: Carrier booking confirmations with tracking numbers
- `pickup-schedule`: Scheduled pickup dates and locations

## Process

1. Submit booking requests to selected carriers
2. Confirm rates and service terms
3. Schedule pickups at origin locations
4. Record tracking numbers and confirmation details
