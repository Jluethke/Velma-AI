# Sprint Challenge Definition

Define the sprint challenge, recruit the team, and prepare the materials.

## Inputs

- `business-problem`: The business problem or opportunity to address
- `available-team`: The people available to participate in the sprint

## Outputs

- `sprint-challenge`: The specific challenge statement the sprint will address
- `sprint-team`: The selected team members and their roles (decider, facilitator, experts)

## Process

1. Frame the business problem as a specific challenge question ('How might we...').
2. Select the sprint team: a decider (has authority), a facilitator, and domain experts.
3. Prepare the room, materials, and schedule for the five days.
4. Produce the challenge statement and team roster.
