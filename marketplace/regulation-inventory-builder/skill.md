# Regulation Inventory Builder

Inventory all applicable regulations by jurisdiction and business area.

## Inputs

- `business-profile`: Description of the business activities, jurisdictions, and regulated areas
- `existing-inventory`: Any existing list of regulations being tracked

## Outputs

- `regulation-inventory`: Complete inventory of applicable regulations with citation, jurisdiction, and business area
- `gap-list`: Regulations not previously tracked that should be

## Process

1. Map the business activities to the applicable regulatory bodies and frameworks.
2. Inventory each applicable regulation with its citation, jurisdiction, and enforcement agency.
3. Compare against the existing inventory to identify gaps.
4. Produce the complete regulation inventory and gap list.
