# Allergy Adverse Reaction Documenter

Document allergies and adverse drug reactions.

## Inputs

- `patient-responses`: Patient-reported allergy information
- `medication-list`: Current medications with doses and frequency

## Outputs

- `allergy-list`: Documented allergies with severity and reaction type
- `interaction-flags`: Potential medication-allergy interactions

## Process

1. Record each allergy with the specific reaction.
2. Classify severity (mild, moderate, severe, life-threatening).
3. Cross-check against current medications for interactions.
