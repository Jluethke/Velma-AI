# Orientation Conductor

Conduct the first-day orientation covering company overview, policies, and culture.

## Inputs

- `hire-details`: New hire information
- `welcome-package`: Welcome materials and schedule

## Outputs

- `orientation-completion`: Record of completed orientation items
- `policy-acknowledgments`: Signed policy acknowledgments

## Process

1. Walk through the company history, mission, and values
2. Review key policies (code of conduct, security, expenses)
3. Tour the office or virtual workspace
4. Collect signed acknowledgments
