# Stated Vs Real Reason Capture

Capture the reason the buyer gave, then the reason the evidence suggests, and rate how sure you are of each.

## Inputs

- `stage-tagged-losses`: Losses tagged by stage
- `decision-map`: Who decided versus who you worked

## Outputs

- `reason-pairs`: For each loss, the stated reason paired with the likely real reason
- `reason-confidence`: Confidence rating on each real-reason judgment

## Process

1. Record the stated reason exactly as the buyer phrased it, including vague ones like budget or timing.
2. From notes, pricing history, and who decided, write the real reason you believe sits behind the stated one.
3. Rate confidence in each real reason as high, medium, or low based on how much evidence supports it.
4. Where confidence is low, note what you would need to ask the buyer to raise it.
