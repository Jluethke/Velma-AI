# Specific Question Crafter

Craft one specific, well-researched question that is easy to answer.

## Inputs

- `work-study-notes`: Notes on what the candidate did and why
- `open-questions`: Genuine questions their work leaves open

## Outputs

- `single-question`: The one question worth asking
- `question-context`: The work you already did on it

## Process

1. Pick the one question whose answer would help you most.
2. Show the work you already did so the question is specific.
3. Make it answerable in a few sentences, not an essay.
4. Remove anything they could find by a quick search.
