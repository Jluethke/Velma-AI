# Engagement Results Analyzer

Analyze the survey results for themes and insights.

## Inputs

- `raw-responses`: Survey responses
- `benchmarks`: External benchmarks

## Outputs

- `results-analysis`: Analysis of scores, trends, and themes
- `key-drivers`: Top drivers of engagement and disengagement

## Process

1. Calculate overall and dimension-level scores
2. Compare against prior results and external benchmarks
3. Segment by department, tenure, and level
4. Identify the top drivers of engagement and disengagement through correlation analysis
