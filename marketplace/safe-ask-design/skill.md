# Safe Ask Design

Design the ask so that giving honest negative feedback is safe and easy for the respondent.

## Inputs

- `feedback-targets`: Specific questions to ask
- `feedback-sources`: The 3 people you will ask

## Outputs

- `ask-scripts`: The exact words you will use to ask each person, including the safety framing
- `timing-plan`: When to ask each person (ideally within 24-48 hours of the event)

## Process

1. Open with a frame that makes honesty easy: 'I am actively working on improving X, and I need honest feedback to do that. Sugarcoating it will not help me. Can you tell me [specific question]?'
2. Ask one question at a time. Multiple questions in one ask get averaged into one vague answer.
3. Time the ask within 24-48 hours of the event. After that, memory fades and the feedback becomes generic ('it was fine').
4. Ask in a private setting (one-on-one, not in a group). People will not give honest feedback in front of others. Written (text, email) can work if the person is uncomfortable giving negative feedback face-to-face.
