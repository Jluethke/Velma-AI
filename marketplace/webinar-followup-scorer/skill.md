# Webinar Followup Scorer

Send the follow-up sequence and score attendees for sales handoff.

## Inputs

- `event-recording`: Recording and transcript
- `attendee-data`: Attendance and engagement data

## Outputs

- `followup-sequence`: Post-event emails with recording and resources
- `webinar-report`: Event report with attendance, engagement, and qualified leads

## Process

1. Send the recording and resources to attendees and no-shows
2. Score attendees by engagement level
3. Produce the webinar report with qualified leads for sales
