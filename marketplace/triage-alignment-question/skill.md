# Triage Alignment Question

Ask the third triage question: does this relationship align with who you are becoming? A life change often shifts your direction, and some relationships fit the person you were while others fit the person you are growing into. Alignment is the hardest question because a relationship can be energy-giving and mutual yet still belong to a chapter that is closing.

## Inputs

- `investment-assessments`: Mutual-investment reading per relationship
- `one-sided-flags`: Relationships you maintain entirely alone

## Outputs

- `alignment-assessments`: Each relationship marked by how well it fits the direction you are growing toward after the change
- `alignment-conflicts`: Relationships that are positive on energy and investment but pull against who you are becoming

## Process

1. For each relationship, ask whether it supports the direction you are heading or holds you in a version of yourself you are leaving behind. Some relationships are anchored to habits, places, or identities that the change is moving you past.
2. Be honest about relationships that keep you in old patterns. A relationship can be comfortable and even positive and still quietly anchor you to a self you are trying to grow out of, which is the subtlest finding in the triage.
3. Do not confuse alignment with usefulness. This is about whether the relationship fits your life and values going forward, not whether the person is advantageous. A deep friendship that spans many chapters aligns even if it serves no purpose.
4. Flag the alignment conflicts: relationships that score well on energy and mutual investment yet pull against your direction. These are the genuinely hard calls, and the disposition step addresses them with options beyond a simple keep or cut.
