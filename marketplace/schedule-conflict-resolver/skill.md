# Schedule Conflict Resolver

Manage schedule conflicts and site access between subcontractors.

## Inputs

- `meeting-minutes`: Minutes from coordination meeting
- `action-items`: Action items from the meeting

## Outputs

- `conflict-resolutions`: Resolved schedule conflicts with agreed plan
- `access-schedule`: Updated site access schedule by area and sub

## Process

1. Identify overlapping work in the same area or requiring the same access.
2. Negotiate the sequence with the affected subcontractors.
3. Update the site access schedule.
4. Communicate the resolution to all affected parties.
