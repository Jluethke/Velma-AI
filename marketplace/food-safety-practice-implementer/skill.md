# Food Safety Practice Implementer

Implement required practices to close the gaps.

## Inputs

- `gap-assessment`: Identified gaps and priorities
- `remediation-priorities`: Ranked list of items to address

## Outputs

- `implemented-practices`: New or modified procedures with effective dates
- `training-log`: Staff training completed on new procedures

## Process

1. Implement water testing at required frequency.
2. Establish worker hygiene training and facilities.
3. Set up traceability (lot tracking from field to buyer).
4. Train all staff on new procedures and document the training.
