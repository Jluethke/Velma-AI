# Budget Review Presenter

Review the consolidated budget and present it to the governing body.

## Inputs

- `consolidated-budget`: The consolidated budget
- `variance-analysis`: Variance analysis

## Outputs

- `budget-presentation`: Presentation-ready budget summary with recommendations
- `amendments-list`: List of amendments from the governing body's review

## Process

1. Prepare the budget summary with revenue projections and expenditure totals.
2. Highlight key variances, new requests, and recommended adjustments.
3. Present to the governing body and record their questions and amendments.
4. Compile the amendments list for incorporation.
