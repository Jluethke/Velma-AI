# Wallet Share Assessor

Assess current revenue and identify expansion opportunities.

## Inputs

- `account-name`: Account name
- `current-contracts`: Active contracts and revenue

## Outputs

- `wallet-share-analysis`: Current share of the account's spend in this category
- `expansion-opportunities`: Specific growth opportunities identified

## Process

1. Calculate current revenue from this account.
2. Estimate the account's total spend in this category.
3. Compute the wallet share percentage.
4. List specific products or departments not yet served.
