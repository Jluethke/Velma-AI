# Sharing Quality And Discovery

Maintain the quality of shared knowledge and make it discoverable. Shared knowledge that nobody reads is a write-only database. The system must connect producers to consumers.

## Inputs

- `pilot-workflow-results`: Results from the pilot workflow
- `rollout-plan`: Plan for expanding to remaining workflows

## Outputs

- `quality-feedback-loop`: Feedback mechanism: consumers rate artifacts, producers see what was useful, quality improves over time
- `knowledge-sharing-protocol-doc`: Complete protocol documentation: opportunity audit, mechanism design, adoption playbook, quality loop, and discovery system

## Process

1. Build the discovery layer: a weekly digest of the most useful artifacts produced that week. This is a curated summary, not a dump. Someone selects the 3 to 5 most useful items and writes a one-line description of each.
2. Add a feedback mechanism: when someone reads an artifact and finds it useful (or useless), they can mark it. Over time, this feedback identifies the highest-quality producers and the most useful formats.
3. Review artifact quality quarterly: sample 10 artifacts from each workflow. Are they specific enough to be useful? Are they too brief to convey the lesson? Adjust the templates based on what is working.
4. The ultimate metric: time-to-competence for new hires. If the shared knowledge base is working, new people ramp up faster because they have access to the judgment, decisions, and lessons that used to live only in long-tenured people's heads.
