# Methodological Critic

Critically evaluate the original study's methodology for threats to internal and external validity, statistical issues, and potential confounds.

## Inputs

- `extracted-protocol`: Complete protocol extracted from the original paper
- `target-paper`: Full text including results and discussion
- `related-replications`: Any prior replication attempts or commentaries on this study

## Outputs

- `validity-assessment`: Identified threats to internal and external validity
- `statistical-critique`: Assessment of sample size, effect size, analytical choices, and p-value patterns
- `replication-risk-factors`: Factors that may make replication succeed or fail (hidden moderators, context dependence)

## Process

1. Assess internal validity: randomization, blinding, confound control, attrition
2. Evaluate statistical practices: sample size justification, multiple comparisons, reporting completeness
3. Check for signs of flexibility in analysis (many dependent variables, subgroup analyses)
4. Review prior replications or failures for this finding or similar effects
5. Identify context-dependent factors that might not transfer to the replication setting
