# 90 Day Outcome Plan Builder

Build the 90-day plan for the new hire, structured around the outcomes. The first 30 days are for understanding the current state. Days 30-60 are for designing the changes. Days 60-90 are for implementing the first changes and demonstrating early movement on at least one outcome metric.

## Inputs

- `outcome-based-job-description`: The outcome-based JD
- `evaluation-criteria`: How success is measured

## Outputs

- `ninety-day-plan`: A structured 90-day plan with specific milestones at 30, 60, and 90 days, each tied to an outcome
- `early-win-target`: The single outcome metric most likely to show movement in 90 days, which the new hire should focus on first

## Process

1. Days 1-30: For each outcome, the new hire learns the current state. What is the current metric value? What has been tried before? Who are the key people involved?
2. Days 30-60: For the highest-priority outcome, the new hire designs the intervention. What will they change, and what result do they expect?
3. Days 60-90: Implement the intervention. Track the metric weekly. At day 90, report: the metric moved from X to Y, or it did not move because [specific reason].
4. The early win target should be the outcome that is most controllable (the new hire can influence it without depending on many other teams) and most visible (stakeholders will notice the improvement).
