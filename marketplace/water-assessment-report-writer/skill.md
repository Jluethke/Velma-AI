# Water Assessment Report Writer

Write the water quality assessment report: site descriptions, results summary, index scores, compliance status, trends, and recommendations.

## Inputs

- `wqi-scores`: Water quality index results
- `compliance-table`: Regulatory compliance results
- `compiled-results`: Full dataset for trend analysis

## Outputs

- `assessment-report`: Structured report suitable for regulators or stakeholders
- `management-recommendations`: Prioritized actions to address identified water quality issues

## Process

1. Describe each monitoring site and its upstream influences.
2. Present results in summary tables and parameter-specific figures.
3. Report the WQI scores and their qualitative interpretation.
4. Summarize compliance status and recommend actions for exceedance parameters.
