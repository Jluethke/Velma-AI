# Essential Energy Protection

Schedule the essential decisions for the hours when judgment is sharpest and batch like decisions together. Essential decisions deserve the energy freed by automating and eliminating the rest.

## Inputs

- `categorized-decisions`: Decisions tagged by category
- `elimination-actions`: Changes freeing up attention

## Outputs

- `energy-schedule`: When essential decisions are placed against the energy curve
- `batching-plan`: Groups of similar decisions handled together

## Process

1. Map the day's energy curve from the timestamps in the log.
2. Place the essential decisions in the high-energy windows and keep those windows clear.
3. Group similar essential decisions so context does not have to be rebuilt each time.
4. Guard the protected windows against the automatable and eliminable traffic now being removed.
