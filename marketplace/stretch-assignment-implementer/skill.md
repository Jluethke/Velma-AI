# Stretch Assignment Implementer

Implement stretch assignments and development experiences.

## Inputs

- `development-plans`: Individual development plans
- `resource-requirements`: Resources needed

## Outputs

- `assignment-log`: Log of stretch assignments and rotations completed
- `progress-notes`: Manager and mentor observations on progress

## Process

1. Coordinate stretch assignments with receiving managers
2. Brief the candidate on learning objectives for each assignment
3. Collect feedback from assignment supervisors
4. Track progress against the development plan milestones
