# Vital Few Identifier

Name the vital few: the inputs above the knee of the curve. These are the inputs where additional investment produces disproportionate returns. They are your highest-leverage activities, customers, or products.

## Inputs

- `sorted-inputs`: The ranked input list with cumulative percentages
- `concentration-ratio`: The Pareto ratio

## Outputs

- `vital-few`: The inputs above the Pareto knee, with the output each produces and the percentage of total
- `trivial-many`: The inputs below the knee, with the same data

## Process

1. Draw the line at the Pareto knee (where cumulative output crosses 80%). Everything above is the vital few.
2. For the vital few, ask: is there a common characteristic? Are they all from the same channel, the same customer type, the same time of day? The pattern in the vital few is actionable intelligence.
3. For the trivial many, count the total resources consumed. This is your opportunity cost: the resources spent on the 80% of inputs that produce only 20% of output.
