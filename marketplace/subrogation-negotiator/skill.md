# Subrogation Negotiator

Negotiate the subrogation settlement and collect the recovery.

## Inputs

- `demand-letters`: Sent demands and responses received
- `response-deadlines`: Tracked deadlines

## Outputs

- `settled-recoveries`: Settled subrogation amounts with payment confirmation
- `subrogation-record`: Complete subrogation record for each pursued claim

## Process

1. Review responses from liable parties and counter-offers.
2. Negotiate the settlement amount considering litigation costs and probability.
3. Collect the agreed recovery and apply to the claim file.
4. Close the subrogation file and document the outcome.
