# Temperature And Timing Optimization

Set bedroom temperature and sleep timing for optimal sleep quality.

## Inputs

- `light-protocol`: Light exposure plan
- `sleep-baseline`: Current sleep data

## Outputs

- `temperature-protocol`: Bedroom temperature 65-68F (18-20C). A warm shower 90 minutes before bed triggers a core temperature drop that signals sleep onset.
- `sleep-timing`: Fixed wake time 7 days per week (not bedtime). The body's circadian clock anchors to wake time, not sleep time.

## Process

1. Set the bedroom to 65-68F (18-20C). Core body temperature must drop for sleep onset. A room that is too warm prevents this drop. Most people keep their bedrooms 5-10 degrees too warm.
2. A warm shower or bath 90 minutes before bed accelerates the core temperature drop. The warm water brings blood to the surface; when you step out, the rapid cooling triggers sleepiness.
3. Fix your wake time, not your bedtime. Wake up at the same time 7 days per week, including weekends. The circadian clock anchors to wake time. A variable bedtime is fine; a variable wake time disrupts the clock.
4. Caffeine has a 6-hour half-life. Coffee at 2pm means 50% of the caffeine is still active at 8pm. Set a caffeine cutoff time 8-10 hours before your target sleep time.
