# First Order Consequence Mapper

For each option, list the first-order consequences: what happens immediately and directly as a result of taking this action. These are the obvious effects that everyone sees.

## Inputs

- `defined-options`: The options stated as specific actions
- `status-quo-option`: What happens with no change

## Outputs

- `first-order-map`: For each option: the immediate, direct consequences (positive and negative)
- `first-order-tradeoffs`: The obvious tradeoffs visible at the first-order level

## Process

1. For each option, list what happens in the first week or month after the decision.
2. Include both positive and negative first-order effects. Do not filter for the ones you want to see.
3. Note who is affected by each first-order consequence (teams, customers, partners, yourself).
4. Do the same for the status quo option. The consequences of inaction are real consequences.
