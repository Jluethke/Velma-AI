# Learner Needs Assessor

Conduct a needs assessment covering learner profiles, prerequisite skills, and industry requirements.

## Inputs

- `program-scope`: Subject area and target learner population
- `industry-standards`: Relevant industry or professional standards

## Outputs

- `needs-assessment-report`: Summary of learner needs and skill gaps
- `learner-profile`: Composite profile of the target learner

## Process

1. Survey the target learner population for existing skills and goals
2. Analyze industry and professional standards for required competencies
3. Identify the gap between current skills and required outcomes
4. Produce the needs assessment report with prioritized gaps
