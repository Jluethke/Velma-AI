# Training Success Metric Definer

Establish metrics to measure the effectiveness of the awareness program.

## Inputs

- `delivery-schedule`: Training plan to measure against
- `assessment-bank`: Assessments providing measurement data

## Outputs

- `success-metrics`: KPIs for program effectiveness
- `curriculum-package`: Complete curriculum with all components

## Process

1. Define completion rate targets by department
2. Set phishing simulation click rate improvement targets
3. Establish incident reporting rate benchmarks
4. Compile all components into the final curriculum package
