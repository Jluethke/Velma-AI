# Facility Compliance Auditor

Audit the facility against licensing standards for health, safety, and environment.

## Inputs

- `requirements-checklist`: The facility requirements from licensing regulations
- `staff-compliance-status`: Current staff compliance status

## Outputs

- `facility-audit-results`: Pass/fail status for each facility requirement with notes on any deficiencies
- `corrective-actions`: List of items needing correction before the next inspection

## Process

1. Walk through each classroom checking square footage, maximum capacity postings, and emergency exits.
2. Verify health and safety items (fire extinguishers, first aid kits, medication storage, cleaning supplies locked).
3. Check outdoor play areas (fencing, surfacing, equipment condition).
4. Verify required postings are displayed (license, ratios, emergency plans, menus, allergy lists).
5. Document any deficiencies and assign corrective action owners and deadlines.
