# Notification Audit And Purge

Audit every notification source and eliminate all except those that require immediate action.

## Inputs

- `usage-data`: Your digital usage data
- `concerns`: What you want to improve

## Outputs

- `notification-audit`: Every app that sends notifications, categorized: essential (calls, messages from key people), useful (calendar, reminders), and unnecessary (social media, news, marketing)
- `notification-policy`: New policy: only essential notifications are allowed. Everything else is disabled.

## Process

1. List every app that sends notifications. For each, ask: does this notification require me to act in the next hour? If no, disable it.
2. Essential notifications: calls and texts from family and close colleagues. Calendar reminders. Truly urgent work alerts.
3. Disable everything else: social media notifications, email notifications, news alerts, app marketing, game notifications. You will check these on your own schedule, not when the app decides.
4. Notifications are not information; they are interruptions. Every notification breaks your focus and takes 23 minutes to recover from (research on context switching). 60 notifications per day = potentially 23 hours of recovery time.
