# Targeted Growth Planner

Design a targeted relationship-building plan that fills structural gaps and increases network resilience, instead of adding contacts randomly.

## Inputs

- `density-score`: Network interconnectedness measure
- `diversity-score`: Network diversity across axes

## Outputs

- `growth-targets`: Specific types of contacts to add (by industry, role, seniority, geography) to address the biggest gaps
- `growth-strategy`: How to meet these target contacts (which events, communities, introductions, and platforms are most likely to connect you with the right people)

## Process

1. From the diversity gaps, define 3-5 target profiles. Example: if you lack senior-level contacts outside your industry, the target is 'VP or above in an adjacent industry.' If you lack early-career contacts, the target is 'people 3-5 years into their career in your field.' Be specific enough that you could recognize the person.
2. For each target profile, identify the highest-probability channels. Where do these people gather? Industry conferences, specific online communities, alumni networks, professional associations, local meetups. Rank channels by the ratio of target contacts to effort required.
3. Set a growth pace: adding 2-3 meaningful new contacts per month is sustainable. Adding 20 is collecting business cards. Each new contact should reach 'active' stage within 90 days or the connection was not meaningful enough to maintain.
4. Build the parallel bridge strategy: when you identify a structural hole worth filling, do not introduce just one pair of people. Introduce 2-3 pairs. This creates redundancy so the bridge survives if any single relationship cools. It also creates a small cross-cluster community that sustains itself through shared context.
