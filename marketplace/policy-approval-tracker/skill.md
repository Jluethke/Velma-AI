# Policy Approval Tracker

Obtain management approval and formal sign-off on the policy.

## Inputs

- `revised-policy`: Policy incorporating stakeholder feedback
- `review-feedback`: Documentation of the review process

## Outputs

- `approved-policy`: Policy with formal management approval
- `approval-record`: Sign-off documentation with dates and signatories

## Process

1. Present the policy to the approval authority with review documentation
2. Record the approval decision, conditions, and sign-off
3. Assign the policy version number and effective date
4. Archive the approval record
