# Status Quo Cost Calculation

Calculate the total annual cost of the prospect's current approach (the status quo) using numbers from the discovery conversation.

## Inputs

- `discovery-notes`: Notes from the discovery conversation showing the prospect's current process, pain points, and the numbers they shared
- `product-description`: What your product does

## Outputs

- `status-quo-cost`: The total annual cost of the current approach: direct costs (time, errors, tools) plus indirect costs (missed opportunities, risk)
- `cost-breakdown`: The status quo cost broken down by category with the formula for each

## Process

1. Sum all direct costs of the current approach: labor time spent on the task, cost of errors and rework, cost of current tools or vendors, cost of manual processes.
2. Add indirect costs: opportunities lost because the current process is slow or inaccurate, risk exposure from errors or non-compliance, customer churn caused by slow service.
3. Use the prospect's own numbers wherever possible. When they said 'about 20 hours per week,' use 20. When they said 'we lose maybe 2 deals a month to slow response time,' use 2.
4. The total status quo cost is the number your proposal will be compared against. It is the implicit price tag of doing nothing.
