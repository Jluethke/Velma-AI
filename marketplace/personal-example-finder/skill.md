# Personal Example Finder

For each mental model, find 3 examples from your own past decisions where that model would have been useful (whether or not you used it at the time).

## Inputs

- `model-definitions`: The 10 model definitions and trigger conditions
- `current-decision-domain`: Your primary decision domain

## Outputs

- `example-library`: 3 examples per model (30 total) drawn from your own experience, each showing what the model reveals about that decision
- `missed-insight-notes`: For each example where you did NOT use the model at the time, what you would have done differently

## Process

1. For each model, review your recent decisions (past 6-12 months) and find 3 where the model applies. At least 1 should be a decision where you did not use the model and it would have changed your choice.
2. For each example, write: (1) the decision, (2) what the model reveals when applied, (3) whether you used the model at the time, (4) what you would have done differently.
3. If you cannot find 3 examples for a model, the model may not be relevant to your domain. Replace it with one that is. The 10 core models are starting points, not sacred.
4. The purpose of personal examples is to anchor abstract models to concrete memories. Abstract definitions fade; personal stories stick.
