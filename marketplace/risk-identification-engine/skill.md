# Risk Identification Engine

Identify security risks through assessment, threat modeling, and stakeholder interviews.

## Inputs

- `system-inventory`: Inventory of systems and assets to assess
- `threat-landscape`: Current threat intelligence and known threat actors

## Outputs

- `risk-list`: List of identified risks with descriptions and categories
- `risk-context`: Supporting context for each risk including threat sources

## Process

1. Review the asset inventory and identify what could go wrong
2. Conduct threat modeling sessions for critical systems
3. Interview stakeholders for operational and business risks
4. Categorize each risk by type (technical, operational, compliance)
