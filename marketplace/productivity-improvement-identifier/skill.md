# Productivity Improvement Identifier

Identify the factors behind low productivity and recommend workflow or training improvements.

## Inputs

- `efficiency-rates`: Efficiency and proficiency rates for each technician
- `benchmark-comparison`: How each technician compares to benchmarks

## Outputs

- `improvement-recommendations`: Specific recommendations for technicians below benchmark
- `productivity-report`: Complete productivity report for the period

## Process

1. For each technician below benchmark, analyze the job mix to see if they are assigned low-flag work.
2. Check for patterns (slow on diagnostics, fast on maintenance, waiting for parts).
3. Recommend specific actions (training, tooling, workflow changes, job assignment adjustments).
4. Produce the complete productivity report with rates, trends, and recommendations.
