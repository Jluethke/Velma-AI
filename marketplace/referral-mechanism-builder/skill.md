# Referral Mechanism Builder

Build the referral mechanism: how the referrer shares, what the recipient sees, and how the loop closes.

## Inputs

- `peak-satisfaction-moments`: When to ask
- `ask-placement-plan`: Where to place the ask
- `referral-value-design`: What value the referrer gets

## Outputs

- `referral-mechanism-spec`: The complete referral flow: trigger, ask copy, sharing method, recipient landing experience, and reward delivery
- `friction-audit`: Every step in the referral flow rated by friction level, with recommendations to reduce each

## Process

1. Design the sharing method: unique link (lowest friction), email invitation (higher trust), or in-product share button (contextual). Match the method to where your customers communicate.
2. Write the ask copy. It should: acknowledge the peak moment, give a reason to share that benefits the referrer (not just the recipient), and make sharing require one click or less.
3. Design the recipient landing: when someone clicks a referral link, they should see who referred them, why, and what they get. Personal context converts better than generic landing pages.
4. Reduce friction at every step. Count the clicks from 'decide to refer' to 'referral received.' If it is more than 3 clicks, simplify.
