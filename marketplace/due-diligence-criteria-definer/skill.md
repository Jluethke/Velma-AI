# Due Diligence Criteria Definer

Define the risk criteria and information requirements for due diligence.

## Inputs

- `third-party-name`: Name and description of the third party
- `relationship-type`: Type of relationship (vendor, partner, agent, customer)

## Outputs

- `risk-criteria`: Risk criteria tailored to the relationship type
- `information-request`: List of information and documents to collect

## Process

1. Determine the risk tier based on relationship type and access to data or systems.
2. Define the due diligence criteria (financial, legal, operational, security, reputational).
3. Build the information request list.
4. Set the timeline for collection and review.
