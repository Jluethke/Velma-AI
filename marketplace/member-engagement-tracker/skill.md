# Member Engagement Tracker

Track member engagement through attendance, participation, and giving to identify active, at-risk, and disengaged members.

## Inputs

- `communication-plan`: The communication channels and cadence
- `directory-policy`: Member information sharing rules

## Outputs

- `engagement-metrics`: What is tracked (attendance, volunteering, giving, event participation) and how it is scored
- `at-risk-criteria`: Thresholds that flag a member as disengaged or at risk of leaving

## Process

1. Define the engagement signals: worship attendance, group participation, volunteer service, event attendance, giving frequency.
2. Set up the tracking method for each signal: check-in system, sign-up sheets, giving records.
3. Create an engagement score that combines the signals into a single indicator per member.
4. Define the at-risk threshold and the follow-up action (personal outreach, invitation, pastoral visit).
