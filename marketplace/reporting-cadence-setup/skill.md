# Reporting Cadence Setup

Establish the reporting cadence and templates for the engagement.

## Inputs

- `tool-inventory`: Tools and channels available
- `access-roster`: Team access details

## Outputs

- `reporting-schedule`: Calendar of status reports, reviews, and steering committee meetings
- `report-templates`: Templates for status reports, milestone reviews, and risk logs

## Process

1. Define the reporting frequency (weekly status, biweekly review, monthly steering).
2. Create templates for each report type with the required sections.
3. Schedule the recurring meetings and send calendar invites.
4. Assign report ownership (who writes, who reviews, who receives).
