# Dpo Requirements Assessor

Assess whether a Data Protection Officer is required and review their role.

## Inputs

- `processing-register`: Processing activities for DPO assessment
- `organization-profile`: Organization type and sector

## Outputs

- `dpo-assessment`: Determination of DPO requirement and current status
- `dpo-recommendations`: Recommendations for DPO appointment or role changes

## Process

1. Evaluate whether the organization meets DPO appointment criteria
2. Review the current DPO role for independence and resources
3. Check that the DPO has appropriate access and reporting lines
