# Call Debrief

Debrief the call into notes the pipeline can act on, so the next touch continues the conversation.

## Inputs

- `next-step-proposal`: The agreed next step
- `situation-notes`: What the buyer said about their current state

## Outputs

- `call-debrief`: A structured record of situation, problems, costs, and the agreed next step
- `follow-up-actions`: The actions you owe the buyer and the dates they are due

## Process

1. Write the debrief while it is fresh, capturing the situation, problems, costs, and the next step.
2. Record the buyer's own phrasing, since the next touch should sound like it heard the last call.
3. List what you promised and when it is due, so no commitment to the buyer is dropped.
4. Update the pipeline so the deal's stage reflects what the call actually established.
