# Supplier Negotiator

Negotiate terms and pricing with the selected supplier.

## Inputs

- `quote-comparison`: Side-by-side comparison of supplier quotes
- `negotiation-targets`: Target prices and terms for negotiation

## Outputs

- `agreed-terms`: Negotiated terms including price, payment, and delivery
- `purchase-order-draft`: Draft purchase order ready for approval

## Process

1. Present the counter-offer based on the negotiation targets
2. Negotiate payment terms, delivery schedule, and warranty
3. Agree on quality assurance and inspection rights
4. Produce the agreed terms and draft purchase order
