# Social Ad Budget Bidder

Set the budget allocation and bidding strategy.

## Inputs

- `social-ad-brief`: Budget and objective
- `platform-shortlist`: Platforms in use

## Outputs

- `budget-allocation`: Budget per platform and campaign
- `bidding-strategy`: Bid type and caps per platform

## Process

1. Allocate budget across platforms proportional to audience size
2. Select the bidding strategy (auto, target CPA, target ROAS)
3. Set daily and lifetime spend caps
