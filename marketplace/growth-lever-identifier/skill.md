# Growth Lever Identifier

Identify the available growth levers and prioritize them.

## Inputs

- `audience-profile`: Who the current audience is
- `growth-baseline`: Current growth and engagement metrics

## Outputs

- `growth-levers`: Prioritized list of growth tactics (SEO, collabs, paid, community, cross-platform)
- `experiment-plan`: The plan for testing each growth lever

## Process

1. List the available growth levers: SEO, collaborations, paid promotion, community engagement, cross-platform posting, email list building.
2. For each lever, estimate the potential impact based on the audience profile and baseline.
3. Prioritize by expected impact relative to effort and cost.
4. Design an experiment for the top 3 levers with clear success metrics.
