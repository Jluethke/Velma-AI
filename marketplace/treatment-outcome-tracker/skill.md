# Treatment Outcome Tracker

Track treatments administered and their outcomes.

## Inputs

- `alert-list`: Animals needing treatment
- `health-protocol`: Treatment guidelines

## Outputs

- `treatment-log`: Treatments given with dates, products, doses, and withdrawal periods
- `outcome-summary`: Recovery rates and treatment effectiveness

## Process

1. Record the diagnosis and treatment for each animal.
2. Track withdrawal periods for meat and milk.
3. Follow up at specified intervals to assess recovery.
4. Summarize outcomes to identify recurring health issues.
