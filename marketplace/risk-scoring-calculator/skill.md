# Risk Scoring Calculator

Score each risk by likelihood and impact using a consistent methodology.

## Inputs

- `risk-list`: Identified risks to score
- `risk-context`: Context supporting likelihood and impact assessment

## Outputs

- `scored-risks`: Risks with likelihood, impact, and overall risk scores
- `risk-matrix`: Visual risk matrix showing distribution

## Process

1. Assess likelihood on a defined scale (rare to almost certain)
2. Assess impact on a defined scale (insignificant to catastrophic)
3. Calculate the overall risk score (likelihood times impact)
4. Plot the risks on the risk matrix
