# Snm Alternative Offer

Offer an alternative only if a real one exists. A genuine alternative softens the no and keeps the relationship productive. A hollow alternative offered to feel helpful creates a new obligation you did not intend and a new thing to decline later.

## Inputs

- `request-summary`: The request, to find an alternative that addresses the underlying need
- `cost-of-yes`: The cost that ruled out the full yes, to keep the alternative within what you can give

## Outputs

- `alternative-offer`: A real alternative you can deliver: a smaller slice, a different time, another person, a useful pointer
- `no-alternative-note`: If no honest alternative exists, a note to that effect so you do not invent a hollow one

## Process

1. Ask what the person actually needs under the request. An alternative that meets the need, even partly, is worth more than the full yes you declined.
2. Offer only what you can deliver: a smaller piece, a later date, a referral to someone better placed, a resource that helps.
3. Keep the alternative within the cost you can bear, or you have just said yes to a different thing you will resent.
4. If there is no real alternative, say nothing more. A hollow offer is worse than none, because it reopens the ask.
