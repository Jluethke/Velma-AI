# Proposal Presentation Close

Present the proposal to the client and handle negotiation.

## Inputs

- `proposal-document`: The proposal document
- `executive-summary`: Executive summary

## Outputs

- `client-decision`: The client's decision (accept, negotiate, decline) with any requested changes
- `proposal-record`: Complete record of the proposal process including all versions, client feedback, and final outcome

## Process

1. Present the proposal in a meeting (not just emailed), walking through the executive summary first.
2. Address questions and objections, documenting each one.
3. If the client requests changes, revise and resubmit within the agreed turnaround.
4. Once accepted, transition to contract and engagement setup.
5. If declined, ask for feedback and log the reason for future learning.
