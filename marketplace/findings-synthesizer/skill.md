# Findings Synthesizer

Synthesize all study findings into a structured report with implications for theory, practice, and future research.

## Inputs

- `meta-inferences`: Integrated findings from mixed-methods analysis
- `conceptual-model`: Original theoretical framework for comparison with results
- `limitations`: Known threats to validity and generalizability

## Outputs

- `findings-report`: Complete findings section with tables, figures, and narrative interpretation
- `implications-section`: Practical and theoretical implications of the results
- `future-research-agenda`: Specific questions and designs for follow-up studies

## Process

1. Summarize key findings organized by research question
2. Compare results to the original conceptual model and note supported and unsupported hypotheses
3. Discuss findings in the context of prior literature, noting agreements and departures
4. Articulate practical implications for policy, programs, or professional practice
5. Identify study limitations and propose specific future research directions
