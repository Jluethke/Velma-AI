# Soil Chemical Properties

Measure soil chemical properties: pH, cation exchange capacity, organic matter, and nutrient levels.

## Inputs

- `prepared-samples`: Sieved soil samples ready for chemical testing

## Outputs

- `chemical-results`: pH, CEC, organic matter percentage, and nutrient concentrations
- `nutrient-status`: Nutrient levels rated as deficient, adequate, or excessive

## Process

1. Measure pH in water and CaCl2 solution.
2. Determine organic matter by loss-on-ignition or Walkley-Black method.
3. Measure cation exchange capacity by ammonium acetate extraction.
4. Extract and quantify available nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium.
