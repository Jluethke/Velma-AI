# Editorial Calendar Builder

Build the editorial calendar with publication dates, owners, and status tracking.

## Inputs

- `journey-content-map`: Content mapped to stages
- `content-gaps-found`: Priority gaps to fill

## Outputs

- `editorial-calendar`: Calendar with titles, dates, owners, and status
- `production-requirements`: Resources needed per content piece

## Process

1. Prioritize content pieces by gap severity and business impact
2. Assign publication dates and owners
3. Estimate production effort for each piece
