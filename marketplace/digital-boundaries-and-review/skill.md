# Digital Boundaries And Review

Set digital boundaries: phone-free times, device-free spaces, and a weekly review of usage trends.

## Inputs

- `device-redesign`: New device setup
- `concerns`: What you want to improve

## Outputs

- `digital-boundaries`: Specific rules: no phone in bedroom, no phone during meals, no phone for the first 30 minutes after waking, no phone after 9pm
- `weekly-review`: A weekly 5-minute check: screen time this week vs last week, pickups this week vs last week, and subjective focus/mood assessment

## Process

1. Set phone-free zones: bedroom (improves sleep), dining table (improves relationships), and first 30 minutes after waking (reduces reactive state).
2. Set phone-free times: after 9pm (or 2 hours before bed), during focused work blocks, and during face-to-face conversations.
3. Weekly review: check screen time vs previous week. Is the trend down? Check pickups. Check which apps consumed the most time. Adjust access friction for any app that is creeping up.
4. The goal is not zero screen time. Digital tools are useful. The goal is intentional use: you choose when to engage instead of the app choosing for you.
