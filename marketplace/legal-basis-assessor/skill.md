# Legal Basis Assessor

Assess the legal basis for the data processing under applicable privacy law.

## Inputs

- `processing-record`: Description of the processing activity
- `regulatory-landscape`: Applicable privacy regulations

## Outputs

- `legal-basis-determination`: The identified legal basis with justification
- `consent-requirements`: Consent requirements if consent is the basis

## Process

1. Evaluate each candidate legal basis (consent, contract, legitimate interest, legal obligation)
2. Select the appropriate basis with documented justification
3. Identify any consent requirements and their implementation
4. Document the balancing test if legitimate interest is selected
