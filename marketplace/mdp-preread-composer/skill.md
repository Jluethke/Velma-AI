# Mdp Preread Composer

Compose the pre-read and schedule it to land at least 24 hours before the meeting. The pre-read holds the decision, the options, the trade-offs, and your recommendation. The meeting is for disagreement, not for reading aloud what everyone could have read alone.

## Inputs

- `decision-statement`: The decision to be made
- `options-set`: The options on the table
- `option-tradeoffs`: The gain and cost for each option

## Outputs

- `preread-document`: A pre-read under two pages holding decision, options, trade-offs, and a recommendation
- `distribution-timing`: When the pre-read goes out and to whom, set at least 24 hours before the meeting

## Process

1. Write the pre-read to under two pages: decision and options first, trade-offs and recommendation second. Longer material goes in an appendix.
2. State a recommendation. A pre-read that lists options without a recommendation makes the author look neutral and leaves the room with nowhere to start.
3. Schedule distribution at least 24 hours ahead and label it as required reading for the meeting by name.
4. List who receives it. Anyone who will be asked to weigh in but does not get the pre-read will slow the room to their speed.
