# Digital Product Creator

Create the digital product.

## Inputs

- `product-spec`: The validated product specification
- `validation-results`: What the audience told you they want

## Outputs

- `finished-product`: The completed digital product ready for delivery
- `product-assets`: Supporting assets (preview, sample, demo)

## Process

1. Outline the product structure (modules, chapters, sections, templates).
2. Create the content for each section.
3. Design the packaging and format for delivery (PDF, video, platform, download).
4. Create preview or sample assets for use in marketing.
