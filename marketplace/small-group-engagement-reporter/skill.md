# Small Group Engagement Reporter

Track small group attendance and engagement, and produce the coordination report.

## Inputs

- `communication-channels`: How groups communicate
- `leader-update-cadence`: Leader reporting schedule

## Outputs

- `engagement-report`: Attendance trends, participation rates, and group health indicators
- `coordination-plan`: Summary of all groups, their status, and recommendations for the next period

## Process

1. Collect attendance data from leader reports for each meeting.
2. Calculate participation rate (attended vs. enrolled) by group and across all groups.
3. Flag groups with declining attendance or leader concerns for follow-up.
4. Produce the coordination plan: groups continuing, new groups to launch, groups to merge or retire, and leader needs.
