# Post Visit Referral Closeout

Complete the referral by sending records and a consult report back to the referring provider.

## Inputs

- `scheduled-appointment`: The appointment record
- `verified-referral`: The original referral for context

## Outputs

- `consult-report`: Report sent to the referring provider with findings and recommendations
- `referral-tracking-record`: Complete referral record from intake through closeout

## Process

1. After the visit, prepare the consult report with findings, diagnoses, and recommendations.
2. Send the consult report to the referring provider within the agreed timeframe.
3. Update the referral tracking record with the visit outcome and report sent date.
4. Close the referral in the tracking system.
