# Moat Scorecard Synthesis

Combine the four moat scores into a single honest scorecard. The point is to see which moats are real, which are wishful, and whether any one of them is strong enough to rely on.

## Inputs

- `cost-advantage-score`: A 0 to 5 score for durable cost advantage
- `switching-cost-score`: A 0 to 5 score for customer switching cost
- `network-effect-score`: A 0 to 5 score for network effect strength
- `intangible-asset-score`: A 0 to 5 score for intangible asset strength

## Outputs

- `moat-scorecard`: The four scores side by side with their evidence
- `dominant-moat`: The strongest moat, or a note that none is strong

## Process

1. Place the four scores side by side with their evidence lines.
2. Flag any score above 3 as a candidate real moat and any at or below 1 as absent.
3. Identify the dominant moat, the single strongest and most durable one.
4. If no score clears 3, state plainly that there is no strong moat yet.
