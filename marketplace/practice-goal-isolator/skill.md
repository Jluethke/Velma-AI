# Practice Goal Isolator

Turn a broad goal into one specific sub-skill to practice at the edge of your ability.

## Inputs

- `broad-goal`: The general ability you want to improve
- `current-level`: A description of your current performance

## Outputs

- `target-sub-skill`: One specific sub-skill stated as an observable behavior
- `ability-edge-note`: A note on where this sits at the edge of your ability

## Process

1. State the broad goal, then break it into component sub-skills.
2. Pick one sub-skill that is just beyond your current reliable performance.
3. Write the target as an observable, specific behavior rather than a vague aim.
4. Confirm the target is narrow enough to practice in isolation.
