# Randomization And Blinding Plan

Design the randomization and blinding scheme: allocation method, stratification factors, concealment mechanism, and unblinding procedures.

## Inputs

- `pico-statement`: Arms and comparators
- `sample-size-report`: Number of participants per arm

## Outputs

- `randomization-scheme`: Method (simple, block, stratified, adaptive), block sizes, and stratification factors
- `blinding-protocol`: Who is blinded (participant, investigator, assessor), matching method, and supply logistics
- `unblinding-procedure`: When and how to unblind (emergency, interim, final)

## Process

1. Choose the randomization method based on study size and stratification needs
2. Set block sizes and stratification factors to balance arms
3. Design the blinding mechanism (matching placebo, coded supplies, central dispensing)
4. Define emergency unblinding procedures and the code custodian role
