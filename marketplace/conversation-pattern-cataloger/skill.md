# Conversation Pattern Cataloger

Catalog the reusable conversation patterns for common interactions.

## Inputs

- `design-principles`: The conversational design principles
- `channel-guidelines`: Channel-specific guidelines

## Outputs

- `pattern-catalog`: Catalog of conversation patterns: greeting, identification, information gathering, confirmation, error recovery, handoff, closing
- `anti-patterns`: Documented anti-patterns to avoid (dead ends, loops, over-prompting, wall of text)

## Process

1. Document the greeting pattern (what to say, how to identify the user's need).
2. Document the information gathering pattern (ask one thing at a time, confirm as you go).
3. Document the confirmation pattern (repeat back, ask for correction).
4. Document the error recovery pattern (acknowledge the error, offer alternatives, escalate if needed).
5. Document the anti-patterns and why each is harmful.
