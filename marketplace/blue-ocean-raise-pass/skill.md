# Blue Ocean Raise Pass

Decide which factors should be raised well above the industry standard. These are the points where customers are underserved and would value much more than the industry currently offers.

## Inputs

- `competition-factors`: The factors the industry competes on
- `reduce-list`: Factors already marked to reduce

## Outputs

- `raise-list`: Factors to push well above industry norm
- `raise-rationale`: The underserved need each one answers

## Process

1. Find the factors where customers settle for less than they want because the whole industry underdelivers.
2. Mark those to raise well above the standard, and say how far.
3. Tie each raise to a specific unmet need or recurring customer complaint.
4. Check that the raises are funded by the cost freed from the eliminate and reduce passes.
