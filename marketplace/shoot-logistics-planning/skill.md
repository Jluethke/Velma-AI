# Shoot Logistics Planning

Scout locations, hire crew, and plan the shoot logistics.

## Inputs

- `shot-list`: The shots to plan for
- `creative-direction-doc`: The creative direction to match locations and crew to

## Outputs

- `shoot-schedule`: The shoot day schedule (call times, locations, shot order, wrap)
- `crew-and-talent`: Hired crew and talent with roles, rates, and contact info

## Process

1. Scout and book locations that match the creative direction.
2. Hire the crew (photographer, assistant, stylist, hair/makeup) and talent (models).
3. Build the shoot day schedule with setup, shot order, and buffer time.
4. Produce the shoot schedule and crew/talent list.
