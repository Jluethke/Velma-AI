# Lost Deal Identification

Identify 10-15 recent lost deals where you reached the proposal or negotiation stage (early-stage drops are less informative).

## Inputs

- `deal-history`: Recent deals that were lost, with the stage at which they were lost and the stated reason
- `time-period`: Period to review (last 6-12 months)

## Outputs

- `interview-candidates`: 10-15 lost deals where you reached late stage, with contact info for the decision maker
- `stated-reasons`: The reason each deal was lost according to your CRM or sales team

## Process

1. Pull all deals lost in the past 6-12 months. Filter to those that reached the proposal, negotiation, or decision stage. Early-stage drops are usually about fit, not about your weaknesses.
2. Record the stated loss reason from your CRM. These are almost always wrong or incomplete because they are recorded by the salesperson, not the buyer. Common fake reasons: 'price too high' (often means value was unclear), 'went with incumbent' (often means switching cost was not addressed).
3. Select 10-15 deals to interview. Prioritize deals where: the stated reason is vague, the deal was large, or the loss was surprising.
4. Prepare a short email requesting a 15-minute conversation: 'We are trying to improve and your honest feedback would help us. This is not a sales call.'
