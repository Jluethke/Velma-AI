# Frc Runway Calculator

Divide cash by burn to get the months of runway.

## Inputs

- `available-cash`: The cash on hand
- `monthly-burn`: The net monthly burn

## Outputs

- `runway-months`: The months of runway at the current rate
- `runway-end-date`: The date the cash runs out at the current rate

## Process

1. Divide the available cash by the net monthly burn.
2. Convert the result to months of runway.
3. Mark the date the cash runs out at the current rate.
