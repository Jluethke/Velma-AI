# Crisis Scenario Definer

Define the crisis scenarios your organization or household could face and categorize them by severity and audience.

## Inputs

- `context`: The organization or household context
- `stakeholder-groups`: The audiences that would need communication during a crisis (customers, employees, investors, media, family, neighbors)

## Outputs

- `crisis-scenarios`: The defined crisis scenarios with severity levels (minor, major, severe) and the primary affected audiences for each
- `response-time-targets`: For each scenario and severity: the target time for the first communication (minor: 24 hours, major: 4 hours, severe: 1 hour)

## Process

1. List the plausible crisis scenarios: service outage, product defect, key employee departure, security breach, data loss, legal action, negative media coverage, financial difficulty, natural disaster affecting operations.
2. For each scenario, define severity levels: minor (limited impact, quickly resolved), major (significant impact, extended resolution), severe (existential impact, fundamental response needed).
3. Map each scenario to its audiences: who needs to know, in what order, and how quickly?
4. Set the response time targets: the first communication must go out within the target window. Speed matters more than completeness. 'We are aware and investigating' within 1 hour beats a detailed report in 24 hours.
