# Spc Characteristic Selector

Select the critical quality characteristics to monitor with SPC.

## Inputs

- `process-description`: Description of the manufacturing process
- `quality-requirements`: Product quality specifications and tolerances

## Outputs

- `critical-characteristics`: List of CTQ characteristics to monitor
- `measurement-methods`: How each characteristic will be measured

## Process

1. Review product specifications and tolerances
2. Identify characteristics that affect fitness for use
3. Rank by impact on customer satisfaction and defect cost
4. Select measurement methods for each characteristic
