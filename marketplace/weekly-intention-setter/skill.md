# Weekly Intention Setter

Set the week's intention and compile the weekly plan.

## Inputs

- `weekly-calendar`: Time-blocked calendar
- `weekly-priorities`: Week's top priorities

## Outputs

- `weekly-intention`: One sentence that captures the week's focus
- `weekly-plan`: Complete weekly plan document

## Process

1. Write one sentence that captures what a successful week looks like.
2. Compile priorities, calendar, and preparation list into the weekly plan.
3. Share the plan with anyone who needs to know.
