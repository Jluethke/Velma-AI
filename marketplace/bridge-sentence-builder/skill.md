# Bridge Sentence Builder

Write the bridge sentence: the single sentence that connects the audience's current frame to your message. The bridge uses their vocabulary, acknowledges their concern, and introduces your point as a natural extension of what they already want.

## Inputs

- `audience-desired-outcome`: What the audience wants
- `overlap-zone`: Where your outcomes align
- `vocabulary-list`: Their words, not yours

## Outputs

- `bridge-sentence`: The single sentence that connects their frame to your message
- `supporting-points`: 2-3 points that reinforce the bridge, each using the audience's vocabulary

## Process

1. Draft the bridge sentence using this structure: '[What they want] + [your mechanism for getting there].' Example: 'You want predictable costs (their want), and this contract structure caps your exposure at $X (your mechanism).'
2. Test the bridge: does it use their words? Does it start from their concern? Does it feel like agreement rather than argument?
3. Write 2-3 supporting points, each reinforcing the bridge. Each point should use their vocabulary and reference their desired outcome.
4. Cut any point that requires the audience to adopt your frame before they can understand it. Those go later, not here.
