# Environment Trait Matcher

Identify the personality traits or working styles that make someone effective in this specific environment, not in general. A trait that predicts success at a 50-person startup may predict failure at a 5,000-person bank. Ask: 'What about you, as a person, makes you good at this job in THIS company?' Not in any company. In this one.

## Inputs

- `hidden-skills-inventory`: The unlisted skills top performers use daily
- `job-description-gaps`: Where the job description is wrong

## Outputs

- `environment-trait-profile`: The personality traits and working styles that predict success in this specific environment, with concrete examples from each performer
- `anti-patterns`: Traits or styles that the performers say would fail in this environment, even if they would succeed elsewhere

## Process

1. Ask each performer: What about you, as a person, makes you good at this job here? Not at any company. At this one.
2. Push for specifics. 'I am adaptable' is not useful. 'I can switch between deep focus work and constant interruptions within the same hour, and this role requires that because the office is open-plan and the work is detail-oriented' is useful.
3. Ask the inverse: What kind of person would fail here, even if they were technically skilled?
4. Compile the traits and anti-patterns across all 5. The traits that appear in 3+ interviews are the environment-specific predictors.
