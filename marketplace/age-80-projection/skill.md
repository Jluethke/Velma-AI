# Age 80 Projection

Project yourself to age 80. You are looking back on your life. For each option (action and inaction), write the story of how that choice played out over decades. The projection must include both the best and worst realistic outcomes of each choice, not just the fantasy version.

## Inputs

- `action-option`: The action choice with specifics
- `inaction-option`: The inaction choice with specifics

## Outputs

- `action-best-case`: The realistic best-case story of taking the action, as told from age 80
- `action-worst-case`: The realistic worst-case story of taking the action, as told from age 80
- `inaction-story`: The story of not taking the action, as told from age 80

## Process

1. Write the action best-case story from the perspective of your 80-year-old self. What happened because you took this chance?
2. Write the action worst-case story. You tried and it did not work out. What happened? How did you recover?
3. Write the inaction story. You did not try. What did your life look like instead? Was it enough?
4. Be realistic in all three stories. No fantasies, no catastrophizing. What would actually happen?
