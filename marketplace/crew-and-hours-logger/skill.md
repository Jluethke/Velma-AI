# Crew And Hours Logger

Document crew counts and hours worked by trade for the day.

## Inputs

- `project-name`: Name of the construction project
- `report-date`: Date of the field report

## Outputs

- `crew-log`: Crew counts and hours by trade
- `labor-summary`: Total labor hours and headcount for the day

## Process

1. List each trade present on site (carpentry, electrical, plumbing, ironwork, etc.).
2. Record headcount and hours worked for each trade.
3. Note any overtime, short staffing, or no-shows.
4. Calculate the total labor hours for the day.
