# Decision Journal Setup

Set up the decision journal structure. Each entry captures the decision, the alternatives, the reasoning, the expected outcome, the confidence level, and the information gap. The structure must be fast to fill in (under 5 minutes per entry) or you will not use it.

## Inputs

- `journal-scope`: What types of decisions to journal (all decisions above a certain threshold, or specific categories)
- `review-cadence`: How often to review the journal (quarterly recommended)

## Outputs

- `journal-template`: The template for each journal entry with all required fields
- `threshold-criteria`: The criteria for which decisions are worth journaling (to avoid journaling every lunch choice)

## Process

1. Define the entry template: date, decision, alternatives considered (top 2 to 3), reasoning (why this option), expected outcome (specific and measurable), confidence level (50% to 99%), information wished for (what you did not know but would have changed the decision).
2. Set the threshold: journal decisions that are either irreversible, involve more than X dollars or Y hours, or affect other people.
3. Choose the medium: a spreadsheet works, a text file works, an app works. The medium that you will actually use is the right one.
4. Commit to filling in the entry BEFORE the outcome is known. A journal filled in after the fact is a narrative, not a prediction.
