# Tenant Negotiation Tracker

Track negotiations with tenants on renewal terms.

## Inputs

- `renewal-offers`: Draft renewal offer letters
- `offer-terms-summary`: Summary of proposed terms

## Outputs

- `negotiation-log`: Record of all communications and counteroffers
- `agreed-terms`: Final agreed renewal terms per tenant

## Process

1. Send the renewal offer and log the delivery date.
2. Record each counteroffer and response with dates.
3. Document the final agreed terms or the decision to vacate.
