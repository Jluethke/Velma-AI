# Interview Guide Assembler

Assemble all components into the final interview guide.

## Inputs

- `question-bank`: Interview questions
- `scoring-rubric`: Scoring rubric
- `panel-schedule`: Panel assignments
- `feedback-process`: Feedback collection process

## Outputs

- `interview-guide`: Complete interview guide for the hiring team
- `candidate-experience-plan`: Plan for candidate communication and experience throughout the process

## Process

1. Compile all materials into a single document
2. Add the candidate experience timeline
3. Review the complete guide with the hiring manager
4. Distribute to the interview panel
