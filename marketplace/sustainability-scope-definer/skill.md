# Sustainability Scope Definer

Define the audit scope, criteria, and methodology.

## Inputs

- `organization-context`: Description of the organization, its operations, and its sustainability commitments
- `audit-objectives`: What the audit should assess and what standards or criteria to use

## Outputs

- `audit-plan`: Audit plan including scope, criteria, schedule, and evidence requirements
- `criteria-checklist`: Checklist of criteria organized by environmental, social, and governance categories

## Process

1. Define the organizational scope (which sites, operations, and time period).
2. Select the audit criteria (internal policy, ISO 14001, GRI, industry standards).
3. Build the criteria checklist organized by E, S, and G categories.
4. Schedule the audit activities and identify the evidence needed for each criterion.
