# Alignment Maintenance Check

Maintain alignment throughout the project by checking for drift at each major milestone.

## Inputs

- `resolved-expectations`: The agreed expectations
- `initiative-description`: The project context and milestones

## Outputs

- `alignment-check-protocol`: The protocol for checking alignment at each milestone: what to ask, who to ask, and how to surface new divergences
- `alignment-drift-log`: A running log of alignment checks: when checked, what divergences surfaced, and how they were resolved

## Process

1. At each major milestone, re-check alignment by asking each key stakeholder: 'Is the project still delivering what you expect? Has your definition of success changed?'
2. If expectations have shifted (which is normal as more is learned), surface the shift and resolve it using the same process as the initial divergence resolution.
3. Log the alignment checks and any shifts. The log reveals patterns: is one stakeholder's expectations constantly shifting? That may indicate unclear requirements or scope creep originating from that stakeholder.
4. Alignment maintenance takes 30 minutes per milestone. Alignment failure at delivery takes weeks to resolve. The investment is asymmetric.
