# Active Commitment Inventory

List all active commitments that consume ongoing resources. Include projects, subscriptions, relationships, memberships, roles, investments, and any recurring obligation. Most people have 20 to 50 active commitments they have never consciously evaluated.

## Inputs

- `commitment-categories`: Categories to audit: professional, financial, social, health, educational, subscriptions, volunteer, other
- `time-window`: How far back to look for commitments you started but never re-evaluated

## Outputs

- `commitment-inventory`: All active commitments with their ongoing cost (time, money, energy per month)
- `oldest-commitments`: Commitments that have been running the longest without re-evaluation

## Process

1. Walk through each category and list every active commitment. Include subscriptions, memberships, recurring meetings, ongoing projects, and roles.
2. For each commitment, estimate the monthly cost in time, money, and energy.
3. Flag the oldest commitments: those running for more than 12 months without a conscious re-evaluation.
4. Include commitments you are emotionally attached to. Those are the ones most likely to harbor sunk cost bias.
