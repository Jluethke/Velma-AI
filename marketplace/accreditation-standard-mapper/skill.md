# Accreditation Standard Mapper

Map the accreditation standards to institutional programs and identify responsible parties.

## Inputs

- `accrediting-body`: Name and standards of the accrediting organization
- `institution-profile`: Overview of the institution and its programs

## Outputs

- `standard-map`: Each standard mapped to responsible departments and evidence sources
- `evidence-checklist`: Checklist of evidence needed per standard

## Process

1. List all accreditation standards and sub-standards
2. Map each standard to the institutional unit responsible
3. Identify the evidence types required for each standard
4. Produce the standard map and evidence checklist
