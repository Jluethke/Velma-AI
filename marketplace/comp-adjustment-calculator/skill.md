# Comp Adjustment Calculator

Adjust comparables for differences and calculate market rent.

## Inputs

- `comparable-listings`: Raw comparable data
- `market-snapshot`: Current market conditions

## Outputs

- `adjusted-comps`: Comparables adjusted for differences in size, amenities, condition
- `market-rent-range`: Calculated market rent range per unit

## Process

1. Adjust each comparable for differences in size, condition, amenities, and location.
2. Calculate the adjusted rent per square foot for each comparable.
3. Determine the market rent range from the adjusted comparables.
