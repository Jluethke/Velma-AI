# Old Pattern Analyzer

Understand why the old pattern exists and what it was once good for.

## Inputs

- `unlearning-target`: The specific pattern to unlearn, stated narrowly
- `evidence-of-staleness`: The evidence that it is outdated

## Outputs

- `pattern-origin`: When and why the pattern was learned
- `maintaining-conditions`: What keeps the pattern in place now

## Process

1. Trace when and why the old pattern was learned.
2. Identify the need it once met or still seems to meet.
3. List the conditions that keep the pattern in place now.
4. Note the cues that currently trigger it.
