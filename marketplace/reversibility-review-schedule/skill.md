# Reversibility Review Schedule

Set the ongoing review cadence. Two-way door decisions get a quick check at the reversal date. One-way door decisions get a longer review to capture lessons. Build the habit of classifying new decisions by door type before engaging with them.

## Inputs

- `two-way-decisions-made`: The two-way door decisions made in this session
- `one-way-analysis`: The one-way door analyses completed
- `sliding-door-schedule`: The sliding door schedule

## Outputs

- `review-calendar`: Calendar of all check-in dates: reversal checks for two-way doors, lesson reviews for one-way doors, decide-by dates for sliding doors
- `classification-habit`: The one-question habit to apply to every new decision: is this a one-way or two-way door?

## Process

1. Compile all check-in dates into a single calendar.
2. For two-way doors: at the check-in, ask only 'is this working or should I reverse it?' Do not re-analyze.
3. For one-way doors: 3 months after the decision, review what you predicted versus what happened. Update your calibration.
4. Build the classification habit: before spending any time on a new decision, ask 'is this a one-way or two-way door?' and match your process to the answer.
