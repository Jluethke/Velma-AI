# Creator Revenue Auditor

Audit the current revenue streams and their performance.

## Inputs

- `creator-profile`: The creator's platforms, audience size, and content type
- `current-revenue-data`: Revenue figures by stream for the past 6 to 12 months

## Outputs

- `revenue-audit`: Breakdown of revenue by stream with trends and margins
- `audience-monetization-rate`: Revenue per subscriber or follower across streams

## Process

1. List every active revenue stream (ad revenue, sponsorships, merch, subscriptions, tips, courses, affiliates).
2. Pull revenue data for each stream over the analysis period.
3. Calculate the revenue share, growth trend, and margin for each stream.
4. Calculate the overall revenue per subscriber or follower.
