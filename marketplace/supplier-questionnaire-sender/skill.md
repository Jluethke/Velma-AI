# Supplier Questionnaire Sender

Send the supplier self-assessment questionnaire.

## Inputs

- `qualification-criteria`: Criteria to assess
- `candidate-suppliers`: List of candidate suppliers to evaluate

## Outputs

- `questionnaire-responses`: Completed questionnaires from candidate suppliers
- `document-submissions`: Certifications, quality manuals, and references submitted

## Process

1. Prepare the supplier questionnaire covering all criteria areas
2. Send to candidate suppliers with submission deadline
3. Collect responses and supporting documentation
4. Screen responses for minimum requirements (disqualify if not met)
