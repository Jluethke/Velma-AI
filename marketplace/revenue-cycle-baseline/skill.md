# Revenue Cycle Baseline

Establish baseline metrics for the practice's revenue cycle performance.

## Inputs

- `practice-financial-data`: Billing data including charges, payments, adjustments, AR aging, and denial rates
- `reporting-period`: The time period to analyze (month, quarter, year)

## Outputs

- `baseline-metrics`: Key revenue cycle metrics: days in AR, clean claim rate, denial rate, collection rate, cost to collect
- `benchmark-comparison`: How the practice compares to industry benchmarks by specialty

## Process

1. Calculate days in accounts receivable (total AR divided by average daily charges).
2. Calculate the clean claim rate (claims accepted on first submission divided by total claims).
3. Calculate the denial rate (denied claims divided by total claims) and net collection rate.
4. Compare each metric against MGMA or specialty-specific benchmarks.
