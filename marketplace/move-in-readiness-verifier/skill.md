# Move In Readiness Verifier

Verify all prerequisites are met before the tenant moves in.

## Inputs

- `lease-agreement`: Executed lease document
- `tenant-info`: Tenant contact and identification details

## Outputs

- `readiness-checklist`: Verified checklist of all move-in prerequisites
- `outstanding-items`: Any items still needed before move-in

## Process

1. Confirm the lease is fully executed and all deposits collected.
2. Verify renter's insurance is on file.
3. Confirm the unit is move-in ready: cleaned, repaired, and inspected.
