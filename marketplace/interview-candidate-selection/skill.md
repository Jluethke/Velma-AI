# Interview Candidate Selection

Select 8-12 people who recently purchased (or recently switched from a competitor) and schedule 45-minute interviews.

## Inputs

- `product-or-service`: What was purchased
- `recent-customers`: Customers who purchased in the past 30-90 days

## Outputs

- `interview-list`: 8-12 customers scheduled for interviews, selected to represent different segments and use cases
- `interview-guide`: The JTBD interview guide with the four forces questions

## Process

1. Select customers who purchased in the past 30-90 days. Recent enough to remember the decision clearly, not so recent that they have not used the product yet.
2. Include variety: different company sizes, different use cases, different industries. You want to see whether the 'job' is consistent across segments or varies.
3. Schedule 45 minutes per interview. JTBD interviews require time to probe the decision timeline deeply.
4. Prepare the interview guide focused on the timeline: what was the trigger, what did they try first, what pushed them to search, and what pulled them to your product.
