# Ux Participant Recruiter

Recruit participants matching the target persona.

## Inputs

- `screener-criteria`: Participant criteria and quotas
- `research-plan`: Methodology and session details

## Outputs

- `participant-roster`: Confirmed participants with schedule
- `consent-forms`: Signed consent and NDA forms

## Process

1. Distribute the screener survey to the recruitment pool
2. Screen and select participants matching the criteria
3. Schedule sessions and send calendar invites
4. Collect signed consent forms from all participants
