# Brief Stakeholder Review

Route the draft brief for stakeholder review, collect feedback, and consolidate it.

## Inputs

- `draft-brief`: The draft creative brief
- `brief-assumptions`: Assumptions to validate with stakeholders

## Outputs

- `consolidated-feedback`: All stakeholder feedback organized by section
- `review-decisions`: Decisions made during review (approved assumptions, resolved conflicts)

## Process

1. Send the draft to each stakeholder with the assumptions list.
2. Collect feedback within the review window.
3. Consolidate and resolve conflicting feedback.
4. Produce the consolidated feedback and review decisions.
