# Influencer Vetter

Identify and vet potential influencers against the criteria.

## Inputs

- `influencer-criteria`: The selection criteria
- `campaign-brief`: The campaign brief for context

## Outputs

- `influencer-shortlist`: Vetted list of influencers with reach, engagement rate, audience demographics, and fit score
- `vetting-notes`: Notes on each candidate including past brand work, content quality, and any red flags

## Process

1. Search for influencers matching the criteria on the target platforms.
2. Score each candidate on reach, engagement rate, audience demographics, and content alignment.
3. Check past brand collaborations and any controversial content.
4. Rank the candidates and produce the shortlist with vetting notes.
