# Estimate Presentation Handler

Present the estimate to the customer, answer questions, and record the outcome.

## Inputs

- `estimate-document`: Formatted estimate to present
- `estimate-total`: Total and breakdown for discussion

## Outputs

- `customer-decision`: Approved, declined, or approved in part with notes
- `estimate-record`: Final estimate record with customer decision and any modifications

## Process

1. Present the estimate to the customer in clear language, grouping by priority.
2. Explain the difference between needed repairs and recommended maintenance.
3. Record the customer decision (full approval, partial approval, or decline).
4. If partially approved, note which line items were authorized and adjust the work order.
