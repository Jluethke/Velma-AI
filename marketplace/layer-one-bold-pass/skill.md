# Layer One Bold Pass

Layer 1: bold the key passages. Read through the material and bold every passage that stands out as important, surprising, or useful. No judgment about relative importance yet. Just mark what resonates.

## Inputs

- `prepared-source`: Source material with metadata
- `initial-read-notes`: Notes from first reading

## Outputs

- `layer-one-result`: Source text with key passages bolded (typically 10 to 30 percent of the original)
- `bold-passage-count`: Number of bolded passages and the percentage of the original they represent

## Process

1. Read through the material. Bold every passage that is important, surprising, useful, or resonant. Err on the side of bolding too much rather than too little.
2. A bolded passage is typically 1 to 3 sentences. If you are bolding entire paragraphs, you are not compressing enough.
3. Target: 10 to 30 percent of the original text should be bolded. Under 10 percent means you are being too selective on the first pass. Over 30 percent means you are not compressing.
4. This pass should take 5 to 10 minutes for a 2000-word article. If it takes longer, you are over-thinking it.
