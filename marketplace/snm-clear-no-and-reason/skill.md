# Snm Clear No And Reason

State the no clearly and give one brief real reason, not an excuse. A vague no invites a second ask and a negotiation. A clear no with a genuine reason closes the matter and keeps your credibility. An excuse, by contrast, is a reason you do not believe, and people can tell.

## Inputs

- `no-decision`: The decision to decline
- `decision-rationale`: The real reason behind the decision

## Outputs

- `no-statement`: A clear, unambiguous no that does not leave the door half open
- `brief-reason`: One short, true reason, offered without over-explaining or inventing an excuse

## Process

1. State the no plainly: 'I am not able to take this on.' Avoid phrasing that reads as a maybe unless you mean maybe.
2. Give one real reason drawn from your rationale, in a sentence. The truth, briefly stated, closes the matter.
3. Do not stack reasons. Multiple reasons invite the person to solve each one and reopen the ask.
4. Do not invent an excuse. An excuse is a reason you would drop if challenged, and offering one teaches the person to challenge it.
