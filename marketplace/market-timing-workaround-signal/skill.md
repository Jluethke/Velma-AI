# Market Timing Workaround Signal

Read the first signal: are people already spending time or money on workarounds to this problem? Existing spending on clumsy workarounds is the strongest sign that real demand already exists.

## Inputs

- `opportunity-profile`: A tight profile of the opportunity and offering
- `timing-question-set`: The three timing questions framed for this opportunity

## Outputs

- `workaround-evidence`: Evidence of money or effort spent on workarounds today
- `demand-signal-strength`: How strong the existing demand looks, low, medium, or high

## Process

1. Find how people solve this problem today, including manual, duct-tape, or multi-tool workarounds.
2. Estimate the time or money they already spend on those workarounds.
3. Treat spending on a bad workaround as proof of demand. No workaround spending is a warning of too early.
4. Rate the demand signal low, medium, or high with the evidence behind it.
