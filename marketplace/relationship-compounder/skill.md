# Relationship Compounder

Follow up with results and repeat the cycle so the relationship grows naturally.

## Inputs

- `action-record`: A record of what you did with the answer
- `result-notes`: What resulted from acting on it

## Outputs

- `follow-up-message`: A message reporting what you did and what happened
- `mentoring-cadence`: A light rhythm of study, question, and report

## Process

1. Report back what you did with their answer and what happened.
2. Ask the next specific question only once you have shown follow-through.
3. Set a light cadence of study, question, and report.
4. Offer help back where you are able to give it.
