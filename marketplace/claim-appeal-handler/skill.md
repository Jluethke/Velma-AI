# Claim Appeal Handler

Appeal the determination if the payment is insufficient.

## Inputs

- `discrepancy-list`: Identified disagreements with the determination
- `loss-documentation`: Original evidence and records

## Outputs

- `appeal-filing`: Formal appeal with supporting evidence
- `claim-record`: Complete record of the claim from notice through resolution

## Process

1. Decide whether to accept the determination or appeal.
2. If appealing, prepare additional evidence supporting a higher loss.
3. File the appeal within the required deadline.
4. Maintain the complete claim record regardless of outcome.
