# Channel Touchpoint Inventory

Inventory all marketing channels and touchpoints in the conversion path.

## Inputs

- `marketing-channels`: List of active marketing channels
- `conversion-goals`: Conversion events to attribute

## Outputs

- `touchpoint-inventory`: All touchpoints with channel, type, and tracking status
- `data-gaps`: Touchpoints with missing or incomplete tracking

## Process

1. List every marketing channel and its touchpoints
2. Check tracking coverage for each touchpoint
3. Document any data gaps
