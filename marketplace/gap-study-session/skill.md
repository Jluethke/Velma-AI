# Gap Study Session

Study the source material to repair the points where the explanation broke.

## Inputs

- `explanation-draft`: A full draft of the explanation
- `breakdown-points`: Specific points where the explanation broke

## Outputs

- `gap-fill-notes`: Notes that repair each breakdown
- `repaired-explanation`: The explanation with the gaps filled

## Process

1. Research each breakdown point until you can explain it cleanly.
2. Rewrite those parts of the explanation with the new understanding.
3. Check that the repaired parts connect to the rest.
4. Confirm no new jargon crept in during the repair.
