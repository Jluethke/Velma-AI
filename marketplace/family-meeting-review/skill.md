# Family Meeting Review

Review family meeting effectiveness and improve the process over time.

## Inputs

- `meeting_notes`: Notes from the meeting
- `action_items`: Items assigned
- `participant_feedback`: How members felt about the meeting

## Outputs

- `completion_rate`: How many prior action items got done
- `process_improvements`: What to change for next time
- `celebration_items`: Accomplishments to acknowledge

## Process

1. Check completion of prior action items
2. Gather quick feedback from each member
3. Note what worked and what dragged
4. Adjust format for next meeting
5. Acknowledge family wins and progress
