# Family Notification Plan

Create a notification plan: who to tell, in what order, and how, plus practical tasks they can take on.

## Inputs

- `family_and_friends`: Key people to notify
- `professional_contacts`: Employer, lawyer, financial advisor, etc.
- `executor`: Estate executor

## Outputs

- `notification_list`: Ordered list of who to notify and how
- `task_assignments`: Practical tasks assigned to specific people
- `information_packet`: Key information the executor needs

## Process

1. Prioritize notification order
2. Note preferred contact method per person
3. Assign practical tasks to capable contacts
4. Prepare executor information packet
5. Include professional contacts to notify
