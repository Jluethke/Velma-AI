# Warm Intro Target Definition

Define precisely who you want to reach and why, because a specific target (a named person or a tightly described role) makes mutual connections findable and makes the eventual ask easy for a connector to act on, while a vague target stalls the whole chain.

## Inputs

- `outreach-goal`: What you are trying to accomplish with the introduction (a sale, a hire, advice, a partnership, a job)
- `target-criteria`: The attributes of the right person to reach: role, company type, seniority, location, or a specific named individual

## Outputs

- `target-profile`: A precise description of the person or role to reach, specific enough that a connector could recognize who fits
- `target-shortlist`: Named individuals who fit the profile, where you can name them, in priority order

## Process

1. State the goal of the introduction in one sentence, because the goal determines who the right target is. Advice, a sale, and a job each point to different people even in the same company.
2. Turn the criteria into a profile a stranger could match against. Not someone in operations but a VP of operations at a mid-size logistics company dealing with route planning. Specificity is what lets a connector think of a name.
3. Where you can, name actual individuals who fit, and rank them. A named target makes the mutual-connection search concrete instead of hypothetical.
4. Sanity check that the target can actually give you what you want. The most senior person is often not the right target, because the person with the problem you solve may be two levels down.
