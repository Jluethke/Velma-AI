# Photo Sharing Privacy Setup

Set up photo and video sharing with privacy controls and consent management.

## Inputs

- `message-templates`: Communication templates including photo-based daily reports

## Outputs

- `privacy-controls`: Photo sharing rules, consent status for each family, and distribution restrictions
- `sharing-guidelines`: Staff guidelines for taking and sharing photos and videos of children

## Process

1. Review each family's photo and media consent form status.
2. Configure the platform to restrict photo visibility (only parents of children in the photo, or classroom-only sharing).
3. Write the staff guidelines for photography (when, where, what is appropriate, no social media).
4. Set up the review process for any photos before distribution.
