# Panel Criteria Definer

Define the panel membership criteria based on research needs.

## Inputs

- `research-agenda`: The upcoming research topics and questions for the next 6 to 12 months
- `user-segments`: The product's user segments and their attributes

## Outputs

- `panel-criteria`: The demographic, behavioral, and segment criteria for panel membership
- `panel-size-target`: Target panel size by segment with recruitment quotas

## Process

1. Review the research agenda to identify the user segments needed most frequently.
2. Define the panel criteria (demographics, usage level, tenure, geography, role).
3. Set the target panel size per segment, overrecruiting by 30 percent to account for attrition.
4. Document the inclusion and exclusion criteria.
