# App Defaults Redesign

Redesign your phone and computer defaults: home screen, app placement, and access patterns to reduce mindless consumption.

## Inputs

- `notification-policy`: New notification settings
- `usage-data`: Top time-consuming apps

## Outputs

- `device-redesign`: Home screen with only productivity and communication apps. Social media and entertainment apps moved to a secondary screen or removed entirely.
- `access-friction`: Friction added to time-sink apps: logged out, app limits set, or browser-only access

## Process

1. Home screen: only apps you use for productive purposes (calendar, notes, maps, phone, messages). Remove all social media and entertainment apps from the home screen.
2. Add friction to time-sink apps: log out after each use (so you must log in each time), set app time limits, or delete the app and access only through the browser (browser versions are deliberately less engaging).
3. Remove infinite-scroll apps from easy access. The design of these apps is specifically engineered to keep you scrolling. Accessing them should require deliberate effort, not an absent-minded thumb tap.
4. Set your phone to grayscale in the evenings. Color is a significant driver of engagement. A grayscale phone is functionally useful but visually boring, which reduces mindless pickup.
