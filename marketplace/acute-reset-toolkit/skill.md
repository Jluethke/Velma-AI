# Acute Reset Toolkit

Assemble physiological reset tools for acute stress, the kind that calm the body quickly in the moment.

## Inputs

- `classified-stressors`: The tagged stressors
- `type-distribution`: The share of each stress type

## Outputs

- `acute-reset-tools`: A short set of fast physiological resets such as box breathing, cold water, and a brisk walk
- `acute-use-cues`: The body signals that tell you to reach for a reset tool

## Process

1. Choose two or three resets that act on the body directly. Box breathing slows the heart, cold water on the face triggers a calming reflex, and a brisk walk discharges the stress response.
2. Acute stress is physiological first, so the fastest path down is through the body, not through reasoning about the situation.
3. Define the cues that should trigger a reset, such as a racing heart, shallow breathing, or clenched muscles. The cue is what tells you to use the tool before the spike peaks.
4. Practice the resets when calm so they are available under stress. A tool you have never rehearsed will not come to mind in the moment you need it.
