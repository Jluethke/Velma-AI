# Sfi Delivery Packager

Package the full arc with delivery notes and a memorability check. The same story told flat and told well land differently, so the delivery choices matter as much as the structure. The final check is whether a listener could retell the story a day later and carry the message with it.

## Inputs

- `protagonist`: The central figure
- `problem-statement`: The problem that opens the story
- `struggle-arc`: The believable middle
- `resolution`: How it ends and what it means

## Outputs

- `story-script`: The full story from protagonist to resolution, sequenced and ready to tell
- `memorability-check`: The result of testing whether a listener can retell the story and recover the core message

## Process

1. Assemble the full arc in order: protagonist, problem and stakes, struggle and turning point, resolution and message.
2. Add delivery notes: where to slow down, where the detail is vivid, where to let a pause carry weight.
3. Cut anything that does not serve the core message. A tight story is remembered. A sprawling one is lost.
4. Run the memorability check: tell it to one person, wait, and ask them to retell it. If the message survives their retelling, the story works.
