# In Market Review

Have in-market reviewers check the translations for accuracy and naturalness.

## Inputs

- `translated-content`: The translations to review
- `adaptation-notes`: Notes on cultural adaptations for reviewer context

## Outputs

- `reviewed-content`: Translations with reviewer corrections incorporated
- `review-feedback`: Reviewer comments and corrections per locale

## Process

1. Send translated content to native-speaking reviewers in each target market.
2. Collect feedback on accuracy, naturalness, tone, and cultural fit.
3. Incorporate corrections and flag any remaining disputes.
4. Produce the reviewed content and review feedback.
