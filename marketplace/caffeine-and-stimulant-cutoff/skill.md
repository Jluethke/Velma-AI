# Caffeine And Stimulant Cutoff

Set cutoff times for caffeine and other stimulants so they do not undermine the sleep window hours later.

## Inputs

- `fixed-wake-time`: The fixed wake time
- `target-bedtime`: The target bedtime

## Outputs

- `caffeine-cutoff-time`: The time after which no caffeine is consumed, usually early afternoon
- `stimulant-rules`: Rules for other sleep-disrupting inputs such as alcohol, heavy meals, and late intense exercise

## Process

1. Set a caffeine cutoff in the early afternoon, around eight hours before bedtime, because caffeine has a long half-life and lingers well into the evening.
2. Add rules for alcohol. It may bring on drowsiness but it fragments the second half of the night, so keep it away from the hours before bed.
3. Set a cutoff for heavy meals and for intense exercise, both of which raise arousal and body temperature and push sleep onset later when done too close to bed.
4. Keep the rules simple enough to remember, anchored to clock times, so they are easy to follow without tracking every input.
