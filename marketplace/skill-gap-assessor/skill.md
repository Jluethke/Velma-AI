# Skill Gap Assessor

Assess skill gaps through a needs analysis.

## Inputs

- `role-requirements`: Skills required by role and level
- `performance-data`: Performance review data and manager feedback

## Outputs

- `skill-gap-map`: Map of skill gaps by role, team, and level
- `priority-skills`: Top skills to develop ranked by business impact

## Process

1. Survey employees and managers on perceived skill gaps
2. Cross-reference with performance data and business priorities
3. Map the gaps by role and level
4. Prioritize by frequency and business impact
