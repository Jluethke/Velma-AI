# Proposal Discovery Session

Conduct the discovery session to understand the client's needs for the proposal.

## Inputs

- `prospect-context`: Background on the prospect (company, industry, known pain points)
- `engagement-type`: Type of engagement being considered (advisory, implementation, assessment, training)

## Outputs

- `discovery-findings`: Structured findings from the discovery session
- `success-criteria`: What the client will consider a successful engagement

## Process

1. Prepare the discovery questions tailored to the engagement type.
2. Conduct the session, focusing on: current state, desired state, obstacles, prior attempts, decision criteria, and timeline.
3. Document the findings in a structured format.
4. Confirm the success criteria with the client before leaving the session.
