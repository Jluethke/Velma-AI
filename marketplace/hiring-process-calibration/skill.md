# Hiring Process Calibration

After hires are made, track the correlation between work sample scores and actual job performance at 6 and 12 months. This closes the loop. If the correlation is low, the work sample task is not simulating the right thing. Adjust the task, not the scoring.

## Inputs

- `signal-comparison-matrix`: The 2x2 matrix from the comparison step
- `divergence-analysis`: Notes on divergent candidates

## Outputs

- `calibration-report`: Correlation between work sample scores and actual performance at 6 and 12 months, with recommendations for adjusting the work sample task
- `process-improvement-plan`: Specific changes to the work sample, rubric, or process based on calibration data

## Process

1. At 6 months post-hire, collect the manager's performance rating and compare against the work sample score. Calculate the correlation.
2. At 12 months, repeat. The 12-month correlation is the one that matters.
3. If the correlation is below 0.40, the work sample is not testing the right thing. Revisit the task decomposition and pick a different task.
4. If the correlation is above 0.40 but below 0.54 (the research benchmark), look at whether the rubric dimensions are weighted correctly.
5. Track separately: what happened to the divergent candidates? Did the high-work-sample / low-interview candidates succeed? If yes, the work sample saved you from a bad rejection.
