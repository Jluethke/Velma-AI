# Exit Interviewer

Conduct the exit interview to understand the reasons for departure.

## Inputs

- `employee-details`: Name, role, department, and last day
- `departure-reason`: Stated reason for leaving

## Outputs

- `exit-interview-notes`: Detailed notes from the exit interview
- `retention-insights`: Themes and actionable insights for retention

## Process

1. Schedule the exit interview before the last week
2. Ask about the reasons for leaving, manager effectiveness, and culture
3. Ask what would have changed their decision
4. Identify patterns by comparing with prior exit interviews
