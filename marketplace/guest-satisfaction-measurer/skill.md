# Guest Satisfaction Measurer

Measure guest satisfaction and produce the experience report.

## Inputs

- `service-standards`: Service standards
- `measurement-criteria`: How to measure each standard

## Outputs

- `satisfaction-scorecard`: Guest satisfaction scores by touchpoint
- `guest-experience-plan`: Final guest experience plan with improvement priorities

## Process

1. Select the survey instruments (post-stay, in-stay, review sites).
2. Collect and aggregate satisfaction data.
3. Score each touchpoint against the standard.
4. Identify the top improvement priorities.
