# Wq Report Writer

Produce the water quality assessment report with all results, standard comparisons, and recommendations.

## Inputs

- `compliance-table`: Parameter-by-parameter standard comparison
- `exceedance-summary`: Summary of exceedances

## Outputs

- `wq-report`: The full water quality assessment report
- `data-tables`: Complete results tables with units, methods, and detection limits

## Process

1. Summarize sampling effort and conditions.
2. Present results by parameter group with exceedances highlighted.
3. Interpret likely sources of any exceedances (point source, nonpoint, natural background).
4. Recommend monitoring frequency adjustments or remediation actions based on findings.
