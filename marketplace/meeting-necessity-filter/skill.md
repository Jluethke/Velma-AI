# Meeting Necessity Filter

Before scheduling: determine if a meeting is the right tool. Most meetings should be emails, documents, or async threads.

## Inputs

- `meeting-purpose`: What needs to be accomplished
- `participants`: Who needs to be involved

## Outputs

- `meeting-or-not`: Whether a meeting is the right tool (meeting) or not (email, document, async thread), with the reasoning
- `meeting-agenda`: If a meeting: the agenda with timeboxed items, decision owners, and pre-read materials. If not: the alternative action.

## Process

1. Apply the meeting filter: a meeting is justified when you need real-time interaction to reach a decision that cannot be reached asynchronously. This includes: resolving disagreements, brainstorming (not updates), making decisions where body language or tone matters, and handling sensitive topics.
2. A meeting is NOT justified for: sharing information (use a document), collecting status (use a form), approving routine decisions (use email), or working sessions where people work in parallel (use async with a sync check-in).
3. If the meeting is justified, write the agenda: each item has a time box (in minutes), a decision owner (the person who will make the call), and the pre-read (the information attendees need before the meeting, sent at least 24 hours in advance).
4. Invite only the people who must contribute to the decisions. Everyone else gets the meeting notes after.
