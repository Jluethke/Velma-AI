# Stakeholder Intro Scheduler

Schedule introductory meetings with key stakeholders the new hire will work with.

## Inputs

- `hire-details`: Role and team context
- `milestone-goals`: Goals that identify key collaborators

## Outputs

- `intro-schedule`: Calendar of intro meetings
- `stakeholder-map`: Map of key stakeholders and their relationship to the role

## Process

1. Identify the key stakeholders from the role's cross-functional interactions
2. Schedule 30-minute intro meetings across the first two weeks
3. Provide context to each stakeholder on the new hire's role
4. Share the stakeholder map with the new hire
