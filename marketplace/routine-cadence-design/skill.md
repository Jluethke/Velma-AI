# Routine Cadence Design

Design the daily, weekly, monthly, and annual routines that maintain health between checkups, and place them on the calendar.

## Inputs

- `risk-profile`: The combined risk profile
- `screening-schedule`: The screening and checkup schedule

## Outputs

- `routine-cadence-plan`: Health routines sorted into daily, weekly, monthly, and annual cadences
- `calendar-entries`: The routines placed as recurring calendar entries

## Process

1. Sort maintenance into cadences. Daily routines cover movement, sleep, and hydration. Weekly routines cover planning and heavier training. Monthly routines cover self-checks and reviews. Annual routines cover the scheduled screenings.
2. Match each routine to the cadence that fits its purpose, so nothing that needs daily attention is left to a monthly review.
3. Place the recurring routines on the calendar as entries, so maintenance runs on a schedule rather than waiting for a symptom to force it.
4. Keep each cadence light enough to sustain. A maintenance system overloaded with daily tasks collapses, and the point is steady routine, not maximum effort.
