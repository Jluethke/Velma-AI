# Warm Intro Connector Selection

Choose the best connector for each target by balancing relationship strength on both sides against how much social capital the ask will cost the connector, since the ideal connector knows the target well, thinks well of you, and will not be overspending their own credibility by introducing you.

## Inputs

- `mutual-connection-map`: Potential connectors and paths per target
- `connection-strength-notes`: Relationship strength on both sides

## Outputs

- `selected-connectors`: The chosen connector for each target, with a backup where one exists
- `connector-fit-rationale`: Why each connector was chosen, and the cost the introduction imposes on them

## Process

1. Score each potential connector on three things: strength of their tie to the target, how favorably they view you, and how low the cost of the ask is to them. The best connector is strong on all three, not just the first.
2. Prefer a connector for whom the introduction is a small deposit, not a large withdrawal. Someone who introduces people routinely and whose standing with the target is secure can make the introduction without risking their own credibility.
3. Avoid overusing one super-connector for every target. Spreading asks protects the relationship you most rely on and keeps any single person from feeling mined.
4. Pick a backup connector where the map allows, so that if the first connector declines or goes quiet, you are not restarting the search from zero.
