# Npm Tradeables Inventory

Inventory three things you can offer that cost you little but are worth a lot to them, and anticipate the same in reverse. These asymmetric trades are where value is created rather than just divided. The best agreements trade on differences in what each side values.

## Inputs

- `negotiation-context`: What is being negotiated and the full set of terms in play
- `their-batna-estimate`: Their situation and pressures, which reveal what they value most

## Outputs

- `low-cost-high-value-offers`: Three things you can give that cost you little and are worth a lot to the other side
- `their-likely-asks`: The concessions they are likely to want, and which of them cost you little to grant

## Process

1. List every term beyond price: timing, volume, exclusivity, payment schedule, scope, support. Value lives in these, not only in the headline number.
2. Find three you can give cheaply that they would value highly. Faster payment, a reference, a longer term, flexibility on a date they care about.
3. Anticipate their asks and sort them by what each would cost you. Cheap-to-you asks are concessions you can grant freely to win something back.
4. Pair your low-cost offers with the asks you most want, so each thing you give is traded, not donated.
