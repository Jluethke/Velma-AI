# Combination Market Fit

Test each candidate combination against a real market need to find the strongest fit.

## Inputs

- `candidate-combinations`: Candidate pairings and triples of your stronger skills
- `rarity-notes`: An estimate of how rare each combination is

## Outputs

- `market-matched-combinations`: Combinations matched to the needs they could serve
- `fit-rationale`: The reasoning behind the chosen combination

## Process

1. List the market needs or problems you could serve with each combination.
2. Score each combination on demand, your access to it, and competition.
3. Select the combination with the strongest market fit.
4. Write the rationale so you can revisit the choice later.
