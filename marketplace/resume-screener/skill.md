# Resume Screener

Screen resumes and applications against the role scorecard.

## Inputs

- `candidate-list`: Sourced candidates
- `role-scorecard`: Key outcomes and qualifications

## Outputs

- `shortlist`: Candidates who meet the minimum criteria
- `screening-notes`: Notes on each candidate with pass/fail reasoning

## Process

1. Score each resume against the required qualifications
2. Flag candidates who meet all required criteria
3. Rank by strength of match to preferred qualifications
4. Produce the shortlist with reasoning
