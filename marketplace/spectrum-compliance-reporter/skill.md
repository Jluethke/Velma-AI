# Spectrum Compliance Reporter

Track and report regulatory compliance for all spectrum holdings.

## Inputs

- `spectrum-inventory`: Holdings inventory
- `interference-report`: Interference management report

## Outputs

- `compliance-status`: Compliance status for each license (compliant, at risk, non-compliant)
- `spectrum-management-report`: Complete spectrum management report

## Process

1. Verify buildout milestones are met for each license.
2. Confirm emission levels are within authorized limits.
3. Prepare regulatory filings and renewal applications on schedule.
4. Compile the spectrum management report with recommendations.
