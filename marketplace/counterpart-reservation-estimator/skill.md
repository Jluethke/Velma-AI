# Counterpart Reservation Estimator

Estimate the other party's reservation price. You will not know it exactly, but you can estimate it by understanding their alternatives.

## Inputs

- `counterpart-context`: What you know about the other party's situation, needs, and alternatives
- `negotiation-issues`: The issues on the table

## Outputs

- `estimated-counterpart-reservation`: Your best estimate of their worst acceptable terms on each issue, with the reasoning
- `counterpart-batna-estimate`: What you believe their best alternative is, and therefore their walk-away point

## Process

1. Research their alternatives: who else can they negotiate with? What happens if they make no deal? What are their costs of delay or switching?
2. Estimate their reservation price: given their alternatives, what is the worst deal they would still accept?
3. Look for signals: urgency (they need a deal quickly = lower reservation price), competition (they have many alternatives = higher reservation price), anchoring (their first offer signals where they think the zone is).
4. This estimate is uncertain. Update it as the negotiation reveals new information.
