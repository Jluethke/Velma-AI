# Ux Competitor Benchmarking

Benchmark the product against competitors on the same user flows.

## Inputs

- `competitor-list`: The competitors to benchmark against
- `audit-plan`: The user flows and criteria to compare on

## Outputs

- `benchmark-comparison`: Side-by-side comparison of the product vs competitors on each flow
- `competitive-gaps`: Areas where competitors do better and what can be learned

## Process

1. Walk through the same user flows on each competitor product.
2. Score each product on the evaluation criteria.
3. Identify where the audited product falls behind and where it leads.
4. Produce the benchmark comparison and competitive gaps.
