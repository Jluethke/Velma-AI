# Renewal Offer Preparer

Prepare the renewal offer letter with proposed terms.

## Inputs

- `market-rent-estimates`: Market rent for each unit
- `renewal-pricing-recommendations`: Recommended rent and terms

## Outputs

- `renewal-offers`: Draft renewal offer letters for each tenant
- `offer-terms-summary`: Summary of proposed terms per unit

## Process

1. Draft the renewal letter with the new rent, lease term, and any changed conditions.
2. Include a response deadline and instructions for acceptance.
3. Attach a comparison showing the current vs. proposed terms.
