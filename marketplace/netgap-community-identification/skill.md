# Netgap Community Identification

For the prioritized gaps, identify the actual communities, venues, and groups where the people on the other side gather, turning an abstract gap into a concrete place you could show up, since you fill a gap by entering a community, not by collecting individual contacts.

## Inputs

- `gap-value-scores`: Each gap scored by the value it would open up
- `prioritized-gaps`: The gaps ranked by priority

## Outputs

- `candidate-communities`: For each prioritized gap, the specific communities, events, groups, or venues where those people actually gather
- `community-access-notes`: How open each community is to newcomers and what it takes to enter credibly

## Process

1. For each prioritized gap, find where those people actually congregate: professional associations, conferences, online communities, local meetups, alumni groups, or recurring events specific to that world.
2. Rank candidate communities by the ratio of target people present to the effort of entering. A focused community full of the people you want to reach beats a large general venue where they are a small fraction.
3. Note each community's openness. Some welcome newcomers immediately, some require a credential or an invitation, some expect you to contribute before you belong. The access path shapes the entry strategy.
4. Prefer communities where you can genuinely contribute, not just extract. You enter a community credibly by being useful in it, and a community you join only to mine for contacts will sense it and keep you at the edge.
