# Staff Ppe Trainer

Train staff on PPE use and hand hygiene procedures.

## Inputs

- `ppe-requirements`: Required PPE by interaction type
- `environmental-plan`: Environmental controls in place

## Outputs

- `training-completion-log`: Record of staff trained
- `competency-results`: Demonstration results for donning and doffing

## Process

1. Conduct training on correct donning and doffing procedures.
2. Demonstrate proper hand hygiene technique.
3. Assess competency through direct observation.
