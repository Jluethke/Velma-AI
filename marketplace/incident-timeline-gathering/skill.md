# Incident Timeline Gathering

Gather the complete incident timeline from all responders and systems.

## Inputs

- `incident-id`: Identifier for the incident being reviewed
- `responder-list`: List of all personnel who responded to the incident

## Outputs

- `raw-timeline`: Collected timeline entries from all sources
- `evidence-references`: References to logs, tickets, and communications from the incident

## Process

1. Collect timeline entries from each responder (what they did and when)
2. Gather timestamps from monitoring systems, tickets, and communication channels
3. Collect any screenshots, log excerpts, or other evidence from the response
4. Merge all entries into a single chronological timeline
