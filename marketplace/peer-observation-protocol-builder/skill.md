# Peer Observation Protocol Builder

Create peer observation and feedback protocols for ongoing teaching improvement.

## Inputs

- `institutional-priorities`: Teaching priorities of the institution
- `needs-survey-results`: Faculty development needs

## Outputs

- `observation-protocol`: Structured peer observation form and process
- `feedback-guidelines`: Guidelines for providing constructive peer feedback

## Process

1. Define the observation criteria aligned to teaching standards
2. Create the observation form with structured and open-ended sections
3. Write guidelines for pre-observation meeting, observation, and debrief
4. Pilot the protocol with volunteer pairs
