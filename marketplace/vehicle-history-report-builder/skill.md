# Vehicle History Report Builder

Compile the full vehicle service history into a formatted report.

## Inputs

- `updated-history`: Complete vehicle service history
- `schedule-status`: Current maintenance schedule status

## Outputs

- `history-report`: Formatted vehicle history report with all service visits and schedule compliance
- `vehicle-health-summary`: Summary of the vehicle condition based on maintenance history

## Process

1. Organize the service records chronologically with mileage at each visit.
2. Highlight the manufacturer schedule compliance (on time, late, or missed).
3. Note any recurring issues or patterns in the service history.
4. Produce the formatted report suitable for the customer or for resale documentation.
