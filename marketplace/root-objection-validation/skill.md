# Root Objection Validation

Test the dominant objection against a fresh sample of losses and, where possible, against the buyers themselves.

## Inputs

- `loss-patterns`: Named loss clusters
- `dominant-objection`: The leading objection to validate

## Outputs

- `validated-objection`: The dominant objection after testing, kept, revised, or replaced
- `counter-evidence`: Cases that did not fit the objection and what they imply

## Process

1. Pull a sample of recent losses that were not part of the original synthesis and check whether the objection holds.
2. Where you can, reach three to five lost buyers and ask one direct question about why they chose otherwise.
3. Keep, revise, or replace the objection based on what the sample and the buyers say.
4. Record the losses that did not fit as counter-evidence, since they often point to a second pattern worth its own study.
