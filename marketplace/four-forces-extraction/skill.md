# Four Forces Extraction

Extract the four forces acting on the customer during the decision: push (dissatisfaction with current), pull (attraction to new), anxiety (fear of switching), and habit (comfort with current).

## Inputs

- `timeline-narratives`: Full decision stories
- `trigger-events`: What triggered the search

## Outputs

- `four-forces-map`: For each customer: the specific push, pull, anxiety, and habit forces, with quotes
- `force-patterns`: The forces that appear across multiple customers (the universal job)

## Process

1. For each interview, identify the push: what was bad enough about the old solution that they could not tolerate it anymore? This is not a feature request; it is a life or work situation that became intolerable.
2. Identify the pull: what about your product specifically attracted them? Not features, but the progress they imagined making. 'I could see myself finally getting my reports done in minutes instead of hours.'
3. Identify the anxiety: what almost stopped them from switching? Common anxieties: 'What if it does not work?' 'What if I lose my data?' 'What if my team hates it?' These are barriers you must address in marketing and onboarding.
4. Identify the habit: what kept them using the old solution for as long as they did? 'I was used to it' or 'Everyone on my team knows how to use it.' Habits are the inertia your product must overcome.
