# Weekly Application Practice

Set up a weekly practice routine: apply one model deliberately to a real decision each week and record what it revealed.

## Inputs

- `model-definitions`: The 10 models with trigger conditions
- `example-library`: Personal examples for each model

## Outputs

- `weekly-practice-schedule`: A 10-week rotation assigning one model per week, with instructions for applying it to a real decision that week
- `application-log-template`: A template for recording each week's application: the decision, the model, what it revealed, whether it changed your choice

## Process

1. Assign one model per week for a 10-week cycle. Start with the model you are least familiar with (it needs the most practice).
2. Each week, identify at least one real decision (not hypothetical) to apply the model to. Apply the model by asking its key question and writing down the answer before making the decision.
3. After the decision is made (or the week ends), record: what the model revealed, whether it changed your thinking, and whether the outcome validated or contradicted the model's insight.
4. After 10 weeks, you will have applied each model at least once. The ones that produced the most insight for your domain become your core toolkit.
