# Trust Ledger Deposit Plan Builder

Build specific deposit plans for the low accounts, matched to cause, because a neglect-low account recovers through steady small deposits while a withdrawal-low account recovers only through a sincere apology and changed behavior, not through unrelated niceness.

## Inputs

- `low-account-list`: Thin and overdrawn relationships ranked and diagnosed
- `overdrawn-warnings`: Accounts too low for any near-term withdrawal

## Outputs

- `deposit-plans`: Per-relationship deposit plans matched to cause, with specific actions drawn from the deposit catalog
- `deposit-schedule`: When each deposit happens, paced so the deposits read as genuine rather than as a sudden transactional campaign

## Process

1. For neglect-low accounts, plan a series of small genuine deposits over time: attend to the small things, follow through on a minor commitment, make contact with no ask attached. Consistency rebuilds these, not a grand gesture.
2. For withdrawal-low accounts, the first deposit is usually a sincere apology that names the specific withdrawal, without excuses, followed by visibly changed behavior. Unrelated kindness before the apology reads as avoidance and can deepen the deficit.
3. Draw each action from the deposit catalog so it fits the context. A client deposit (proactively flagging a risk) differs from a personal deposit (remembering what matters to them), and using the wrong register can feel hollow.
4. Pace the schedule so deposits do not arrive all at once. A sudden flood of attention right before you need something is itself read as a withdrawal, because the motive is obvious. Spread deposits so they look like how you treat the relationship, not like a down payment on a request.
