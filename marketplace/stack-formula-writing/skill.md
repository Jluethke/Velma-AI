# Stack Formula Writing

Write the explicit stacking formula that binds the tiny habit to the chosen anchor so the trigger is unambiguous.

## Inputs

- `reliable-anchor-habits`: The reliable existing habits
- `tiny-habit-version`: The under-two-minute new habit

## Outputs

- `stack-formula`: A single sentence of the form after I do the anchor I will do the tiny habit
- `anchor-match-rationale`: Why this anchor fits this habit in time, place, and theme

## Process

1. Write the formula in one sentence: after I do the anchor, I will do the tiny habit. Name both parts exactly, with no vagueness about when.
2. Match the anchor to the habit by location and theme. A kitchen anchor fits a water or vitamin habit. A desk anchor fits a planning habit.
3. Confirm the anchor ends in the same place the new habit begins, so there is no travel or transition that gives resistance a gap to enter.
4. Write why the pairing fits. A mismatched pairing, such as attaching stretching to an anchor that happens while rushing out the door, will not hold.
