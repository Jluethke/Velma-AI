# Buying Org Mapping

Map the buying organization: the roles that touch the decision, their influence, and who owns the problem.

## Inputs

- `account-intel`: What you know about the account: structure, goals, and recent changes
- `stakeholder-notes`: Notes from calls on who said what and who reports to whom

## Outputs

- `org-map`: A map of the people in the deal and their roles
- `influence-lines`: Who influences whom, including the quiet influencers off the org chart

## Process

1. Lay out the known people in the account with their titles and apparent roles in the decision.
2. Draw the reporting lines and, more important, the influence lines that do not follow the chart.
3. Mark the economic buyer, the technical evaluators, and the people who can block.
4. Flag the gaps, since the people you have not met often decide the outcome.
