# Panel Recruiter

Recruit panel members who meet the defined criteria.

## Inputs

- `panel-criteria`: Criteria for panel membership
- `panel-size-target`: Target panel size per segment

## Outputs

- `recruited-members`: List of recruited panel members with segment tags and contact info
- `recruitment-metrics`: Recruitment conversion rate and segment fill status

## Process

1. Select recruitment channels (in-app prompt, email outreach, social media, customer community).
2. Send the recruitment invitation with a screening questionnaire to verify criteria.
3. Screen applicants against the panel criteria and segment quotas.
4. Onboard accepted members (consent form, incentive terms, communication preferences).
