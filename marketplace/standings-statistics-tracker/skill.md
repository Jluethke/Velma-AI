# Standings Statistics Tracker

Track game results, standings, and player or team statistics throughout the season.

## Inputs

- `official-assignments`: Officials responsible for reporting results
- `official-schedule`: Game schedule with officials

## Outputs

- `league-standings`: Current standings for each division (wins, losses, ties, points)
- `season-statistics`: Player and team statistics for the season

## Process

1. Collect game results from officials or team managers after each game.
2. Update standings tables for each division.
3. Compile individual and team statistics.
4. Publish updated standings and statistics on the regular cadence.
