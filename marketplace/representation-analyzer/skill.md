# Representation Analyzer

Analyze workforce representation across levels and functions.

## Inputs

- `demographic-dataset`: Demographic data
- `data-scope`: Dimensions to analyze

## Outputs

- `representation-report`: Representation percentages by group, level, and function
- `representation-trends`: Year-over-year trends in representation

## Process

1. Calculate representation percentages at each level
2. Compare against the overall workforce composition
3. Compare against industry and labor market benchmarks
4. Identify levels and functions with the largest gaps
