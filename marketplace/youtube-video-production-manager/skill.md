# Youtube Video Production Manager

Manage the production of a single YouTube video from script to final edit.

## Inputs

- `content-calendar`: The planned video topic and format
- `content-pillars`: The content category this video falls under

## Outputs

- `video-file`: The finished video file ready for upload
- `thumbnail-file`: The thumbnail image designed for the video

## Process

1. Write the video script or outline based on the scheduled topic.
2. Shoot or record the footage according to the script.
3. Edit the video (cuts, transitions, graphics, audio mixing).
4. Design the thumbnail with a clear focal point and readable text.
