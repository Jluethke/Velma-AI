# Copy Brand Reviewer

Review all copy for tone consistency and brand alignment.

## Inputs

- `channel-copy-set`: All channel copy drafts
- `positioning-brief`: Brand voice and positioning reference

## Outputs

- `reviewed-copy`: Copy with brand and tone corrections applied
- `review-notes`: Flagged issues and changes made

## Process

1. Check each piece against the brand voice guidelines
2. Verify consistent messaging across channels
3. Flag any claims that need legal or compliance review
4. Apply final edits for clarity and concision
