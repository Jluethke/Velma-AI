# Music Audio Master

Master the track for distribution across formats and platforms.

## Inputs

- `stereo-mix`: The final stereo mix
- `reference-tracks`: Reference tracks for loudness and tonal balance

## Outputs

- `mastered-audio`: Mastered audio files in distribution formats
- `master-report`: Loudness measurements and technical specifications

## Process

1. Apply corrective EQ and stereo imaging adjustments
2. Apply multiband compression and limiting for loudness
3. Verify loudness targets for each distribution platform
4. Export in all required formats (WAV, FLAC, MP3)
