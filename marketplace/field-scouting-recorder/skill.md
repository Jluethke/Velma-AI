# Field Scouting Recorder

Scout the field and document pest or disease symptoms.

## Inputs

- `field-map`: Map of the field with zones or sections
- `scouting-observations`: Visual observations of symptoms or damage

## Outputs

- `symptom-report`: Documented symptoms with location, severity, and photos
- `affected-area-map`: Map of affected zones and spread pattern

## Process

1. Walk the field on a systematic pattern (W or zigzag).
2. Record symptoms: leaf damage, discoloration, wilting, insect presence.
3. Photograph representative samples at each location.
4. Map the affected area and note the spread direction.
