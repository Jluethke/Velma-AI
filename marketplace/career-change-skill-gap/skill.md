# Career Change Skill Gap

Map current skills against target career requirements to identify gaps and transferable strengths.

## Inputs

- `current_role`: Current job and industry
- `target_career`: Desired career or field
- `current_skills`: Existing skills and experience

## Outputs

- `skill_gaps`: Skills needed but not yet held
- `transferable_skills`: Existing skills that carry over
- `gap_priority`: Gaps ranked by importance to close first

## Process

1. Research target career requirements
2. List all current skills and certifications
3. Identify direct transferable skills
4. Map remaining gaps
5. Prioritize gaps by hiring impact
