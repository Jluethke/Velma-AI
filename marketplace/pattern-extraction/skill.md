# Pattern Extraction

Compare feedback from all 3 sources and look for the pattern, not the outlier.

## Inputs

- `reception-rules`: How feedback was received
- `feedback-sources`: The 3 sources and their responses

## Outputs

- `feedback-patterns`: Themes that appeared in 2 or more responses (these are the real signals)
- `action-items`: 1-2 specific things to change based on the patterns, with a plan for when and how to practice the change

## Process

1. Lay out all three responses side by side. Look for themes that appear in 2 or more. A theme in 2 of 3 is a pattern. A theme in 1 of 3 is an opinion.
2. Ignore outlier feedback that contradicts the other two. One person's pet peeve is not a universal problem.
3. Convert each pattern into one specific action item. 'I talked too fast' becomes 'In the next presentation, pause for 2 seconds after each key point.' The action must be behavioral and observable.
4. Limit to 1-2 action items. Trying to change 5 things at once changes nothing. Pick the pattern with the highest impact and practice it until it is natural, then address the next one.
