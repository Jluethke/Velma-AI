# Decision Log Capture

Log every decision made during one representative day, however small. The count itself is the first finding, because most people make far more decisions than they believe and most carry no real stakes.

## Inputs

- `observation-day`: The representative day being logged
- `role-context`: The role and setting shaping the day's decisions

## Outputs

- `decision-log`: Every decision made during the day
- `decision-count`: The total number of decisions logged

## Process

1. Record each decision as it happens, from what to eat to what to approve, without filtering for importance.
2. Note roughly when each decision happened so energy patterns show up later.
3. Capture small repeated decisions as separate entries, since their volume is the point.
4. Total the count at the end of the day to establish the raw decision load.
