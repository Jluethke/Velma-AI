# Pathway Outcome Measurer

Measure outcomes against benchmarks and produce the pathway document.

## Inputs

- `pilot-data`: Pilot outcomes and variances
- `outcome-measures`: Target outcome values

## Outputs

- `outcome-report`: Comparison of pilot outcomes to targets
- `clinical-pathway-document`: Finalized clinical pathway for implementation

## Process

1. Compare pilot outcomes against the defined targets.
2. Revise the pathway based on pilot data and feedback.
3. Finalize the pathway document for broad implementation.
