# Petrogenesis Interpretation

Interpret the rock's origin and history from the mineral assemblage, textures, and geological context.

## Inputs

- `rock-name`: Classified rock name
- `confirmed-minerals`: Mineral assemblage
- `texture-description`: Textural evidence
- `sample-context`: Field relationships and regional geology

## Outputs

- `petrogenetic-interpretation`: Interpreted formation conditions, source, and subsequent history
- `petrographic-report`: Complete petrographic report with descriptions, photomicrographs, classification, and interpretation

## Process

1. Infer formation conditions from the mineral assemblage (P-T range, melt composition, metamorphic grade)
2. Interpret the textural sequence: crystallization order, deformation events, alteration stages
3. Relate the petrography to the regional geological context and tectonic setting
4. Write the final report with annotated photomicrographs, mineral table, classification, and interpretation
