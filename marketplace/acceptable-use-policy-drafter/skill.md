# Acceptable Use Policy Drafter

Draft the acceptable use policy and prohibited conduct rules.

## Inputs

- `service-scope`: Service scope
- `product-description`: Product description

## Outputs

- `aup-text`: Acceptable use policy text
- `prohibited-conduct`: List of prohibited user activities

## Process

1. Define permitted uses of the service
2. List prohibited activities (abuse, fraud, illegal use, reverse engineering)
3. Specify consequences for violations
