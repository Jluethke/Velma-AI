# Incident Stakeholder Mapping

Identify all stakeholders who need to be notified about the incident.

## Inputs

- `incident-summary`: Summary of the incident including type, severity, and impact
- `organizational-context`: Organizational structure and communication requirements

## Outputs

- `stakeholder-list`: Categorized list of stakeholders requiring notification
- `notification-priorities`: Priority order for stakeholder notifications

## Process

1. Identify internal stakeholders (executives, IT, legal, HR, affected business units)
2. Identify external stakeholders (customers, regulators, partners, media)
3. Categorize by urgency and notification method
4. Set the notification priority order
