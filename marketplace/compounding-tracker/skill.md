# Compounding Tracker

Track how the system compounds and surface clusters ready to become outputs.

## Inputs

- `weekly-review-checklist`: A checklist for the weekly review
- `review-cadence`: The fixed time and rhythm for the review

## Outputs

- `knowledge-system-map`: A map of how the system has grown
- `output-candidates`: Clusters ready to become an article, project, or decision

## Process

1. Watch which clusters of linked notes keep growing.
2. Flag clusters dense enough to turn into an article, project, or decision.
3. Record the system map and the candidate outputs.
4. Schedule a next step for the strongest candidate.
