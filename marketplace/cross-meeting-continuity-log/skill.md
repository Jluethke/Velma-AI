# Cross Meeting Continuity Log

Keep a running log across meetings so one-on-ones build on each other instead of restarting weekly. The log is also where patterns show up: a blocker that never clears, a growth goal that never advances.

## Inputs

- `commitment-pair`: The commitments made
- `commitment-log`: Where they are recorded

## Outputs

- `continuity-record`: One running document per report holding agendas, obstacles, and commitments, newest on top
- `pattern-signals`: Recurring blockers and stalled growth goals surfaced by reading across meetings

## Process

1. Maintain one running document per report, newest entry on top, holding agendas, obstacles, and commitments.
2. Start each meeting by checking the previous commitments and open obstacles.
3. Watch for patterns across weeks: a recurring blocker points to a system problem, a stalled growth goal points to crowding-out.
4. Review the log before development and career conversations, since it is the record of what actually happened week to week.
