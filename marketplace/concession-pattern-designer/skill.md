# Concession Pattern Designer

Design the concession pattern: how to sequence and size concessions to signal reasonableness while protecting your position.

## Inputs

- `concession-inventory`: The available concessions
- `high-value-low-cost-concessions`: The priority trading chips

## Outputs

- `concession-sequence`: The planned order and size of concessions: start large, then smaller, then smaller, signaling approach to your limit
- `reciprocity-requirements`: What you require in return for each concession: never concede without receiving

## Process

1. First concession: the largest. It signals goodwill and willingness to negotiate. But it must come with a request: 'I can move to X on this issue. In return, I need Y on that issue.'
2. Subsequent concessions: each one smaller than the previous. The decreasing size signals that you are approaching your limit. If your first move was 20%, the second should be 10%, the third 5%.
3. Never make a concession without getting one in return. If they ask for something, respond with: 'I can consider that. What can you offer on [issue]?'
4. Label your concessions: 'This is a significant move for me because [reason].' Unlabeled concessions are taken for granted. Labeled concessions build reciprocity pressure.
