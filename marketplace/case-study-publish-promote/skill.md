# Case Study Publish Promote

Publish the case study and promote it across channels.

## Inputs

- `case-study-final`: The designed case study
- `case-study-assets`: Promotional assets

## Outputs

- `published-url`: URL of the published case study
- `promotion-record`: Record of where the case study was shared and the engagement it received

## Process

1. Publish the case study on your website or portfolio platform.
2. Share on LinkedIn, relevant communities, and in your email newsletter.
3. Tag the client (with permission) for additional reach.
4. Add the case study link to relevant proposal templates and pitch decks.
