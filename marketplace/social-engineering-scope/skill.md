# Social Engineering Scope

Define the social engineering assessment scope, approved scenarios, and obtain management authorization.

## Inputs

- `organization-profile`: Company name, industry, and employee count
- `management-authorization`: Written approval for social engineering testing

## Outputs

- `approved-scenarios`: List of approved attack scenarios with constraints
- `target-groups`: Employee groups selected for testing

## Process

1. Review management authorization for scope boundaries and prohibited techniques.
2. Select employee groups for testing across departments and seniority levels.
3. Define success criteria and metrics for each scenario.
4. Establish a safe word and immediate stop procedures.
