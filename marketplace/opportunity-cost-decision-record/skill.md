# Opportunity Cost Decision Record

Record the opportunity cost analysis as a decision record. Future you will want to know not just what you chose, but what you gave up and why. This record also feeds into the decision journal for calibration tracking.

## Inputs

- `opportunity-cost-verdict`: The verdict and reasoning
- `value-gap`: The value difference between the commitment and the best alternative
- `alternative-options`: All alternatives considered

## Outputs

- `decision-record`: The complete record: what was chosen, what was given up, the estimated opportunity cost, and why the choice was made
- `review-date`: When to check whether the predicted value gap materialized

## Process

1. Document: the commitment chosen (or rejected), the alternatives considered, the expected values, the opportunity cost, and the final decision.
2. Set a review date to check whether the predicted values materialized.
3. If you chose the commitment despite a negative value gap, document why (information the analysis did not capture, strategic reasons, gut feeling). This is useful calibration data.
