# Volunteer Recognition Planner

Recognize and retain volunteers through appreciation programs.

## Inputs

- `hours-log`: Volunteer hours and contributions
- `attendance-report`: Attendance and engagement data

## Outputs

- `recognition-plan`: Recognition activities and schedule
- `volunteer-management-report`: Summary of the volunteer program

## Process

1. Define recognition tiers based on hours and impact
2. Plan recognition events and communications (thank-you letters, awards, social media features)
3. Survey volunteers for satisfaction and feedback
4. Compile the volunteer management report with metrics and recommendations
