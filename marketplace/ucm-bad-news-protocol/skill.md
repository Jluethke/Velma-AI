# Ucm Bad News Protocol

When the message is bad news, structure it to go early, lead with what you are already doing about it, and state what you need. Managers can handle bad news, but not late bad news, and not bad news with no plan attached. Early plus a plan turns a problem into a managed situation.

## Inputs

- `topic`: The situation, including whether it is bad news
- `specific-ask`: What you need, which bad news usually includes

## Outputs

- `bad-news-structure`: The bad news framed as what happened, what you are doing, and what you need, in that order
- `timing-plan`: When to deliver it, chosen to be as early as the facts allow

## Process

1. Lead with the news and the action you have already taken. 'The build failed, and I have rolled back and started the fix' is a managed problem.
2. State what you need from the manager next, if anything, so the message ends on a path forward.
3. Go early. Bad news delivered late costs trust twice: once for the problem and once for the delay in surfacing it.
4. Do not pad bad news with reassurance it cannot support. State the facts, the plan, and the ask, and let the plan do the reassuring.
