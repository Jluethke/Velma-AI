# Compliance Verifier

Verify correction or escalate the violation.

## Inputs

- `violation-notice`: The issued violation notice
- `compliance-deadline`: The correction deadline

## Outputs

- `compliance-status`: Whether the violation was corrected, partially corrected, or unresolved
- `enforcement-record`: Complete enforcement case record

## Process

1. Conduct a follow-up inspection after the compliance deadline.
2. Determine whether the violation has been corrected.
3. If unresolved, escalate to fines, liens, or legal action per the enforcement policy.
4. Close the case if corrected, or document the escalation and next steps.
