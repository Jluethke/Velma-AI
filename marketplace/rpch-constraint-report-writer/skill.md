# Rpch Constraint Report Writer

Write the decision record for the mix change.

## Inputs

- `projected-revenue`: The revenue of the target mix
- `target-mix`: The mix to adopt

## Outputs

- `constraint-revenue-report`: Record of the constraint, the ranking, and the gain
- `mix-change-plan`: The steps to move to the target mix

## Process

1. Document the binding constraint and the ranking.
2. State the target mix and the displaced work.
3. Produce the change plan with the expected gain.
