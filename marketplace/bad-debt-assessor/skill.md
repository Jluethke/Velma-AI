# Bad Debt Assessor

Assess bad debt exposure and record allowance or write-offs.

## Inputs

- `aging-report`: Current receivables aging report
- `collection-actions`: Collection history and outcomes

## Outputs

- `bad-debt-estimate`: Estimated allowance for doubtful accounts
- `write-off-recommendations`: Invoices recommended for write-off

## Process

1. Review the aging report and identify accounts with high risk of non-payment.
2. Calculate the allowance for doubtful accounts using historical loss rates.
3. Identify specific invoices that meet write-off criteria.
4. Prepare the journal entries for allowance adjustment and write-offs.
