# Video Footage Capture

Execute the shoot and capture all required footage.

## Inputs

- `production-schedule`: Shoot schedule with call times
- `shot-list`: Required shots to capture

## Outputs

- `raw-footage`: All captured video files organized by scene
- `continuity-notes`: Notes on take quality, continuity, and selects

## Process

1. Set up camera, lighting, and audio for each scene
2. Capture all shots on the shot list with multiple takes
3. Log each take with quality notes and continuity observations
4. Offload and back up media at end of each shoot day
