# Quality System Establishment

Establish the quality management system: document structure, process definition, resource requirements, and management responsibility per ISO 13485.

## Inputs

- `device-master-record`: Complete device specifications, drawings, BOMs, and manufacturing procedures from design transfer
- `regulatory-requirements`: Applicable quality system regulations (21 CFR 820, ISO 13485, EU MDR Annex IX)

## Outputs

- `quality-manual`: Quality policy, organizational structure, process map, and document control system
- `process-validation-plan`: List of manufacturing processes requiring validation with IQ/OQ/PQ protocol outlines

## Process

1. Define the quality policy and quality objectives. The policy is a statement of commitment to regulatory compliance and continual improvement. Quality objectives must be measurable: defect rate targets, CAPA closure time, audit findings trend.
2. Map the manufacturing processes: incoming inspection, assembly/fabrication steps, in-process inspection, final inspection, packaging, labeling, sterilization, storage, and distribution. For each process, assign an owner, define inputs and outputs, and identify process controls.
3. Establish the document control system: procedures, work instructions, forms, and records. Every document must have a unique identifier, revision level, approval signature, and effective date. Changes require documented change orders with impact assessment.
4. Identify processes that require validation (where the output cannot be fully verified by subsequent inspection or test). Welding, bonding, sealing, sterilization, and software are typically special processes requiring IQ (equipment qualified), OQ (process operates within parameters), and PQ (process consistently produces conforming product).
5. Define infrastructure and work environment requirements: cleanroom classification (ISO 14644, typically ISO 7 or 8 for medical device assembly), environmental monitoring (particle counts, temperature, humidity), personnel training and gowning, and equipment calibration program.
