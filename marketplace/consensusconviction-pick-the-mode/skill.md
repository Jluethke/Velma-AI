# Consensusconviction Pick The Mode

Pick consensus when information is distributed, buy-in matters, and downside is limited; pick conviction when expertise is concentrated and speed matters.

## Inputs

- `combined-score`: The three scores brought together into one view.
- `score-pattern`: Whether the scores agree or conflict.

## Outputs

- `chosen-mode`: Consensus or conviction for this decision.
- `mode-rationale`: Why the mode fits the three scores.

## Process

1. Choose consensus when all three tests point toward shared decision making.
2. Choose conviction when expertise is concentrated and speed matters more than buy-in.
3. Write why the mode fits the three scores.
