# Sales Integration

Wire the content into the sales process so reps send the right piece at the right moment.

## Inputs

- `content-plan`: Answers mapped to formats and channels
- `publishing-schedule`: The publishing order

## Outputs

- `sales-assignment-guide`: Which piece a rep sends at which stage and objection
- `close-rate-tracking`: How to tell whether buyers who read the content close faster

## Process

1. For each stage and common objection, name the content a rep should send to move the deal.
2. Train reps to use content as an answer, not as homework, so it speeds the deal rather than stalling it.
3. Track whether deals where the buyer read the content close faster or at a higher rate.
4. Feed what reps learn back into the question list, so the content keeps matching real objections.
