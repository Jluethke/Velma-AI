# App Review Submitter

Submit the app for review and respond to any reviewer feedback.

## Inputs

- `uploaded-build`: Build uploaded and ready
- `distribution-settings`: Distribution configured

## Outputs

- `review-status`: Current review status and any reviewer notes
- `submission-checklist`: Complete record of what was submitted and when

## Process

1. Submit the app for review with any reviewer notes.
2. Monitor the review status for approval or rejection.
3. If rejected, address the specific issues cited and resubmit.
4. Document the submission timeline and outcome.
