# Red Team Scope Definer

Define the objectives, rules of engagement, and scope for the red team exercise.

## Inputs

- `target-description`: Description of the target organization or system
- `stakeholder-goals`: What the exercise should test or prove
- `legal-authorization`: Signed authorization and rules of engagement

## Outputs

- `scope-document`: Formal scope with in-scope and out-of-scope targets
- `rules-of-engagement`: Constraints, communication protocols, and emergency stop procedures

## Process

1. Interview stakeholders to define success criteria
2. Enumerate in-scope systems, networks, and personnel
3. Define out-of-scope items and prohibited techniques
4. Establish communication channels and emergency stop procedures
