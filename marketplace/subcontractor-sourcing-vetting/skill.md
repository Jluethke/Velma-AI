# Subcontractor Sourcing Vetting

Source and vet potential subcontractors.

## Inputs

- `sub-role-definition`: Role and skill requirements
- `budget-allocation`: Budget range

## Outputs

- `candidate-shortlist`: Vetted candidates with portfolio samples, rate, and availability
- `vetting-notes`: Notes from interviews, portfolio review, and reference checks

## Process

1. Source candidates from your network, freelance platforms, or referrals.
2. Review portfolios and work samples for quality and relevance.
3. Conduct a brief interview or trial task to assess fit.
4. Check references from prior clients or collaborators.
5. Select the top candidate based on skill, rate, availability, and reliability.
