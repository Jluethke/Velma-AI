# Patient Medication Communicator

Communicate medication changes to the patient.

## Inputs

- `reconciled-med-list`: Final medication list
- `change-summary`: Summary of changes

## Outputs

- `patient-med-card`: Patient-friendly medication card with instructions
- `communication-record`: Documentation that the patient was informed

## Process

1. Explain each change in plain language.
2. Provide a printed or digital medication card.
3. Confirm patient understanding with teach-back.
