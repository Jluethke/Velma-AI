# Cost Identifier

Identify and estimate all costs: direct, indirect, and opportunity costs.

## Inputs

- `decision-framework`: Alternatives and evaluation criteria
- `evaluation-scope`: Time horizon and boundary conditions

## Outputs

- `cost-estimates`: Itemized cost estimates for each alternative by period
- `cost-assumptions`: Assumptions behind each cost estimate

## Process

1. Identify direct costs (purchase price, implementation, labor, materials).
2. Identify indirect costs (training, downtime, maintenance, overhead).
3. Estimate opportunity costs for each alternative.
4. Assign costs to time periods across the evaluation horizon.
