# Vegetation Climate Interpretation

Interpret the pollen zones in terms of vegetation change and inferred climate conditions.

## Inputs

- `pollen-diagram`: Completed pollen diagram with zones
- `pollen-zones`: Zone boundaries and their defining taxa
- `sample-context`: Regional vegetation, climate, and archaeological or geological context

## Outputs

- `vegetation-reconstruction`: Reconstructed vegetation for each zone with dominant communities described
- `climate-inference`: Inferred climate conditions (temperature, moisture) for each zone based on indicator taxa

## Process

1. Describe the vegetation assemblage in each zone using modern analogue comparisons
2. Identify key indicator taxa for climate inference (e.g., thermophilous trees, steppe herbs)
3. Correlate zone boundaries with known climate events, archaeological phases, or disturbance history
4. Assess the role of human impact vs climate as drivers of vegetation change
