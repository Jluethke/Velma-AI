# Project Delivery Closeout

Deliver the final work, collect payment, and close out the project.

## Inputs

- `milestone-status`: Final milestone status
- `deliverables-log`: Record of all deliverables and feedback

## Outputs

- `final-deliverable-package`: All final deliverables, files, and documentation handed to the client
- `project-closeout-record`: Record of the completed project including final invoice, payment confirmation, and client feedback

## Process

1. Submit the final deliverables and get client sign-off.
2. Transfer all files, credentials, and assets to the client per the contract.
3. Send the final invoice and confirm payment.
4. Request a testimonial or referral if the engagement went well.
5. Archive the project files and update your portfolio.
