# Design Revision Execution

Revise the design based on the resolved feedback.

## Inputs

- `design-files`: The original design files
- `resolved-feedback`: The prioritized list of changes

## Outputs

- `revised-design`: The updated design files incorporating the feedback
- `revision-notes`: Summary of what changed and why

## Process

1. Work through the resolved feedback list in priority order.
2. Make each change and note what was done and why.
3. Verify each change against the original review criteria.
4. Produce the revised design and revision notes.
