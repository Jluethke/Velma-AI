# Missing Protections Checker

Check for standard protections that are absent from the contract.

## Inputs

- `section-inventory`: List of sections
- `risk-flags`: Flagged risk clauses

## Outputs

- `missing-protections`: List of missing standard clauses
- `gap-priority`: Priority ranking of gaps to address

## Process

1. Check for force majeure, governing law, and dispute resolution
2. Verify insurance, audit rights, and data protection clauses exist
3. Compare against a standard clause checklist for the contract type
