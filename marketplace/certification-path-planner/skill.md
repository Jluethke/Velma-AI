# Certification Path Planner

Choose and plan certification or education path: programs, costs, duration, and scheduling around work.

## Inputs

- `skill_gaps`: Skills to acquire
- `budget`: Training budget available
- `time_availability`: Hours per week available for study

## Outputs

- `certification_plan`: Chosen programs with timeline
- `cost_breakdown`: Total education costs
- `study_schedule`: Weekly study plan

## Process

1. Research certification options per skill gap
2. Compare program quality, cost, and duration
3. Check employer tuition assistance
4. Build study schedule around current commitments
5. Set exam or completion target dates
