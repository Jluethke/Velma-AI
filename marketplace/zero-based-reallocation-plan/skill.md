# Zero Based Reallocation Plan

Produce the reallocation plan: what to cut, what to keep, and where the freed money goes.

## Inputs

- `bloat-diagnoses`: The cause and specific transactions behind each bloated category
- `reversibility-assessment`: How easy each reduction is
- `justified-allocations`: The target allocations

## Outputs

- `reallocation-actions`: Specific actions to bring each bloated category to its justified level: cancellations, downgrades, renegotiations, and habit changes
- `freed-capital-destination`: Where the recovered money goes: debt paydown, savings, investment, or reallocation to underfunded categories

## Process

1. For each bloated category, write the specific actions: which subscriptions to cancel, which services to downgrade, which purchases to reduce in frequency. Every action should be a concrete step, not a resolution.
2. Sequence the actions by reversibility: easy cuts first (can be done this week), medium cuts next (take a billing cycle), hard cuts last (require behavioral change over 30 to 60 days).
3. Determine where the freed money goes. Priority order: first fill any underfunded categories (where actual was below justified and causing problems), then debt with the highest interest rate, then savings until the emergency buffer is full, then investment.
4. Calculate the total monthly improvement: sum of all bloat reductions minus any upward adjustments to categories that were legitimately underfunded.
