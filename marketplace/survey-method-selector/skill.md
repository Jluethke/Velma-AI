# Survey Method Selector

Select the survey methodology and design the questions.

## Inputs

- `survey-goals`: What the survey should measure (satisfaction, effort, loyalty)
- `customer-segments`: Customer groups to survey

## Outputs

- `survey-design`: Survey instrument with questions and scale
- `sampling-plan`: Which customers to survey and when

## Process

1. Choose the method (CSAT, NPS, CES) based on the goals.
2. Write questions that are specific, unbiased, and actionable.
3. Define the sampling plan by customer segment.
4. Set the trigger points (post-ticket, post-purchase, periodic).
