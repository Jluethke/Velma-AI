# Internal Controls Tester

Test the design and operating effectiveness of internal controls.

## Inputs

- `audit-plan`: Audit plan identifying key controls to test
- `control-documentation`: Client documentation of internal controls

## Outputs

- `controls-test-results`: Results of controls testing with exceptions noted
- `controls-reliance-decision`: Decision on whether to rely on controls

## Process

1. Identify the key controls relevant to financial reporting assertions.
2. Perform walkthroughs to confirm control design is appropriate.
3. Select samples and test operating effectiveness.
4. Document exceptions and assess their impact on the audit approach.
