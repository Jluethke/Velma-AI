# Review Cadence Planner

Design the weekly and monthly review cadence for habit progress.

## Inputs

- `checkin-protocol`: Daily check-in routine
- `tracking-schema`: Tracking methods in use

## Outputs

- `review-schedule`: Weekly and monthly review dates and agenda
- `adjustment-criteria`: Rules for when to adjust a habit target

## Process

1. Set the weekly review day and questions to answer.
2. Set the monthly review day for trend analysis.
3. Define criteria for adjusting targets (e.g., 90% adherence for two weeks means raise the bar).
