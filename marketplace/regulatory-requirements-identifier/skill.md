# Regulatory Requirements Identifier

Identify the applicable regulations and specific filing requirements.

## Inputs

- `filing-purpose`: Purpose and type of the filing
- `jurisdiction`: Applicable jurisdiction or regulatory body

## Outputs

- `requirements-checklist`: Complete list of filing requirements
- `deadline-schedule`: Filing deadlines and milestones

## Process

1. Identify the specific regulation and filing type
2. List all required forms, documents, and data
3. Note the filing deadline and any interim milestones
