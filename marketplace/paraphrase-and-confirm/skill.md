# Paraphrase And Confirm

After they finish, paraphrase what you heard and confirm accuracy.

## Inputs

- `attention-plan`: Your listening setup
- `listening-focus`: What you need to understand

## Outputs

- `paraphrase-practice`: The habit of saying 'What I hear you saying is [their point]. Is that right?'
- `confirmation-record`: What they said, confirmed by them as accurate

## Process

1. After each major point, paraphrase: 'What I hear you saying is [restate in your words]. Is that accurate?'
2. If your paraphrase is wrong, their correction reveals the gap between said and understood.
3. Do not add interpretation to the paraphrase.
4. Paraphrasing slows the conversation, which is the point.
