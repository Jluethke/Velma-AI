# Voc Report Writer

Produce the voice of customer report.

## Inputs

- `root-cause-map`: Root causes by theme
- `improvement-opportunities`: Prioritized improvements

## Outputs

- `voc-report`: Voice of customer report with themes, causes, and recommendations
- `executive-summary`: One-page summary for leadership

## Process

1. Write the findings section with data from each theme.
2. Map root causes to recommended actions.
3. Write the executive summary.
4. Produce the full report.
