# Move Out Damage Assessor

Conduct the walk-through and assess damage beyond normal wear.

## Inputs

- `inspection-appointment`: Scheduled inspection
- `comparison-checklist`: Room-by-room checklist

## Outputs

- `damage-report`: Itemized damage report with photos and repair cost estimates
- `deposit-deduction-worksheet`: Deductions itemized with amounts and justification

## Process

1. Walk each room and compare against the move-in condition report.
2. Document damage beyond normal wear with photos.
3. Estimate repair costs for each item.
4. Prepare the deposit deduction worksheet.
