# Ideal Customer Sharpener

Use the anti-persona as a contrast to sharpen the definition of who you ARE for. The exclusion reveals the inclusion.

## Inputs

- `anti-persona-profile`: Who you are not for
- `not-for-you-statement`: The exclusion statement

## Outputs

- `sharpened-ideal-profile`: Refined ideal customer profile that is specific enough to recognize on sight
- `differentiating-traits`: The 3-5 traits that most clearly separate the ideal from the anti-persona

## Process

1. For each anti-persona trait, write the opposite: what the ideal customer expects, needs, and values instead.
2. Identify the 3-5 differentiating traits that most cleanly separate the ideal from the anti-persona. These are your targeting criteria.
3. Write the ideal profile in the same format as the anti-persona: a named composite with demographics, behaviors, expectations, and desired outcome.
4. Test: if you read both profiles to a salesperson, could they sort a new lead into one or the other within five questions?
