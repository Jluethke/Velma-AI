# Repurposed Content Scheduler

Schedule the repurposed content and track performance.

## Inputs

- `adapted-content`: The adapted content pieces
- `target-platforms`: The platforms to publish on

## Outputs

- `publishing-schedule`: Schedule of repurposed content across platforms with dates and times
- `repurposing-plan`: The complete repurposing plan with cadence, metrics, and review schedule

## Process

1. Schedule each piece on the appropriate platform at the optimal posting time.
2. Space out repurposed content to avoid flooding any single channel.
3. Define the metrics to compare repurposed content against original (engagement, reach, cost per piece).
4. Compile the repurposing plan with the review cadence.
