# Safety Resolution Tracker

Track the resolution of safety corrective actions.

## Inputs

- `corrective-actions`: Corrective actions with deadlines

## Outputs

- `resolution-status`: Status of each corrective action (open, resolved, overdue)
- `re-inspection-results`: Results of re-inspections verifying corrections

## Process

1. Monitor each corrective action against its deadline.
2. Re-inspect to verify the correction was made.
3. Escalate overdue items to the project manager and safety director.
4. Close the action when verified as resolved.
