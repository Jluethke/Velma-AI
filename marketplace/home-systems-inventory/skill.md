# Home Systems Inventory

Inventory all home systems with ages, warranties, and expected replacement timelines.

## Inputs

- `home_age`: Year home was built
- `home_type`: House, condo, townhome, etc.
- `known_systems`: HVAC, water heater, roof age, etc.

## Outputs

- `systems_inventory`: All systems with age and condition
- `replacement_timeline`: Expected replacement dates
- `warranty_status`: Active warranties

## Process

1. List all major home systems
2. Document age and condition of each
3. Check warranty status and expiration
4. Estimate remaining useful life
5. Flag systems approaching end of life
