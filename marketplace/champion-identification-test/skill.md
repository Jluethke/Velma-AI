# Champion Identification Test

Identify the champion by testing who takes action between meetings, not who talks the most during them.

## Inputs

- `stakeholder-map`: All people in the buying decision
- `deal-context`: The deal status

## Outputs

- `champion-candidate`: The person who takes unsolicited action to advance the deal between meetings
- `champion-evidence`: Specific actions they have taken: scheduled follow-up meetings, shared materials internally, introduced you to other stakeholders, answered your questions proactively

## Process

1. After each meeting, note who follows up without being asked. The champion is the person who emails you a question that evening, schedules the next meeting before you ask, or shares your materials with their team without prompting.
2. Test by asking for a small action at the end of each meeting: 'Could you share this summary with [person]?' The champion does it. The non-champion forgets.
3. Do not confuse the champion with the most enthusiastic person in the room. Enthusiasm without action is not championship. The quiet person who forwards your proposal to the CFO with a note saying 'I think we should do this' is your champion.
4. If nobody takes action between meetings, you may not have a champion yet. Without one, the deal is at high risk.
