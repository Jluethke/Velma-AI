# Change Impact Assessment

Assess the impact of the scope change on timeline, budget, and deliverables.

## Inputs

- `change-request-record`: The documented change request
- `initial-assessment`: Preliminary severity classification

## Outputs

- `impact-analysis`: Detailed analysis of how the change affects timeline, budget, and other deliverables
- `options-list`: Options for accommodating the change (with tradeoffs for each)

## Process

1. Estimate the additional hours or effort required.
2. Identify any deliverables that will be delayed or affected.
3. Calculate the cost impact at the current billing rate.
4. Identify at least two options: absorb the change with tradeoffs, or add time and budget.
5. Document the risks of each option.
