# Campaign Goal Setter

Set the fundraising campaign goal, timeline, and success metrics.

## Inputs

- `campaign-purpose`: What the campaign will fund
- `historical-results`: Past campaign performance data

## Outputs

- `campaign-goal`: Dollar target with timeline
- `success-metrics`: KPIs for tracking progress

## Process

1. Review past campaign results and donor capacity
2. Set a dollar goal grounded in historical performance and growth targets
3. Define the campaign timeline with key milestones
4. Select KPIs (total raised, donor count, average gift, retention rate)
