# Freight Optimization Reporter

Produce the freight optimization report with cost and performance analysis.

## Inputs

- `delivery-status-report`: Current status of all shipments
- `savings-estimate`: Estimated cost savings from consolidation

## Outputs

- `freight-report`: Freight optimization report with cost, transit time, and carrier performance
- `improvement-recommendations`: Recommendations for future shipment optimization

## Process

1. Summarize total freight cost against budget
2. Analyze carrier on-time performance
3. Compare actual savings to the consolidation estimate
4. List recommendations for the next shipping cycle
