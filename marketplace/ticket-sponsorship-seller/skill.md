# Ticket Sponsorship Seller

Sell tickets and sponsorships to meet the revenue target.

## Inputs

- `event-concept`: Event details
- `event-budget`: Revenue targets by category

## Outputs

- `ticket-sales-report`: Tickets sold and revenue
- `sponsorship-commitments`: Confirmed sponsors with levels and benefits

## Process

1. Design sponsorship levels with benefits at each tier
2. Solicit sponsors starting with past supporters and major donors
3. Open ticket sales and promote through all channels
4. Track sales against targets and adjust promotion as needed
