# Blameless Timeline Builder

Build a factual timeline of events leading to the outcome. No blame, no interpretation, just what happened and when.

## Inputs

- `incident-or-project`: The project, incident, or outcome being analyzed
- `participants`: The people involved who can contribute to the timeline

## Outputs

- `event-timeline`: A chronological list of events: what happened, when, and who was involved. No interpretation, no blame.
- `decision-points`: The key decision points in the timeline: where a different decision could have changed the outcome

## Process

1. Collect inputs from every participant: what did you do, what did you see, what did you decide, and when? Collect individually to avoid contamination.
2. Build the timeline: event, timestamp, person involved. Use the 'newspaper test': if a reporter could verify this event from logs, emails, or records, it belongs in the timeline.
3. Identify the decision points: moments where a different decision could have led to a different outcome. These are not blame points; they are learning points.
4. Set the ground rule: the timeline describes what happened, not who is at fault. Systems produce outcomes; people operate within systems. Fix the system, not the person.
