# Survey Administrator

Administer the survey with anonymity protections.

## Inputs

- `survey-instrument`: The survey
- `communication-plan`: Communications sent

## Outputs

- `raw-responses`: Collected survey responses
- `response-rate`: Response rate overall and by department

## Process

1. Launch the survey on the platform
2. Send reminders at midpoint and near close
3. Monitor response rates by department and send targeted reminders
4. Close the survey and export the raw data
