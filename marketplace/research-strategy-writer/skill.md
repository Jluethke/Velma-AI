# Research Strategy Writer

Write the research strategy section with detailed methods for each aim, including experimental design, data analysis plans, and contingencies.

## Inputs

- `specific-aims-draft`: Aims with approach summaries
- `preliminary-data`: Figures and descriptions of preliminary results
- `methods-expertise`: Available methods, equipment, and collaborator expertise

## Outputs

- `research-strategy`: Complete research strategy section with sub-sections per aim
- `preliminary-data-figures`: Formatted figures with legends showing key preliminary results
- `rigor-and-reproducibility`: Section addressing scientific rigor, biological variables, and authentication

## Process

1. Write the approach for each aim with rationale, methods, expected results, and interpretation
2. Incorporate preliminary data figures at points where they demonstrate feasibility
3. Include power analyses and sample size justifications for each experiment
4. Address potential pitfalls and alternative approaches for each aim
5. Write the rigor and reproducibility section per funder requirements
