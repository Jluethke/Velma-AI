# Pay Mix Designer

Set on-target earnings and the base-to-variable pay mix.

## Inputs

- `role-profiles`: Role definitions and selling motions
- `market-compensation-data`: Benchmark data for similar roles

## Outputs

- `pay-mix-structure`: OTE, base, and variable split per role
- `market-comparison`: How the proposed pay compares to market

## Process

1. Set the on-target earnings for each role.
2. Determine the base-to-variable split (e.g., 50/50 for hunters, 70/30 for farmers).
3. Compare against market data.
4. Adjust for geographic and experience differentials.
