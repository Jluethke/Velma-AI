# Ald Primary Failure Identifier

Reconcile your self-observation with the outside feedback to name your one primary failure. When the two disagree, the outside view usually wins, because the failure you cannot see is the one doing the most damage.

## Inputs

- `failure-frequency`: Your self-logged counts over the week
- `external-feedback`: What others reported
- `feedback-themes`: The failures named by more than one person

## Outputs

- `primary-failure`: The single failure you will work on first, chosen from the combined evidence
- `evidence-summary`: The log counts and feedback that point to this failure, kept as a baseline to measure against later

## Process

1. Lay your self-counts beside the feedback themes. Where they agree, you have your answer.
2. Where they disagree, weight the outside view more heavily, especially for filtering and judging, which are nearly invisible from the inside.
3. Choose exactly one primary failure. Working two at once splits attention and neither improves.
4. Write the evidence summary as a baseline: the counts and the examples, so that in two weeks you can tell whether anything changed.
