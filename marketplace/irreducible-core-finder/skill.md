# Irreducible Core Finder

From the essential claims, find the one or two that are truly irreducible: the core of what you are.

## Inputs

- `essential-claims`: Claims that survived the subtraction test

## Outputs

- `irreducible-core`: The 1-2 claims that define what you are at the most fundamental level. Everything else is a consequence of these.
- `derivative-claims`: Essential claims that are true but are consequences of the core, not the core itself

## Process

1. Look at the essential claims. Ask: which of these is a cause, and which is an effect? 'Fast delivery' might be an effect of 'local production.' 'Personal service' might be an effect of 'small team by design.'
2. The core is the cause. The effects are derivative. You keep the derivatives in your messaging, but they orbit the core rather than competing with it.
3. The irreducible core is typically 1-2 statements. If you have more than 3, you have not subtracted enough. Each additional claim halves the impact of the others.
4. Test the core: can you explain it in one sentence to someone who knows nothing about your category? If you need two sentences, it is not yet irreducible.
