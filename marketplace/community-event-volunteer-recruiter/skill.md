# Community Event Volunteer Recruiter

Recruit and schedule volunteers for the event.

## Inputs

- `logistics-plan`: The event logistics and activity schedule
- `vendor-list`: External vendors and their needs

## Outputs

- `volunteer-roster`: Volunteers assigned to roles with shift times
- `volunteer-instructions`: Role-specific instructions and contact information

## Process

1. List every volunteer role: setup, registration, food service, activity stations, parking, cleanup, first aid.
2. Recruit from the membership and community, matching skills to roles.
3. Schedule volunteers in shifts to avoid burnout and ensure coverage for the full event.
4. Send each volunteer their role, shift time, arrival instructions, and the event-day contact number.
