# Market Potential Analyzer

Analyze total addressable market by geography or segment.

## Inputs

- `market-data`: Industry, geographic, and firmographic data
- `segmentation-criteria`: How to divide the market (geography, vertical, size)

## Outputs

- `market-potential-map`: Potential revenue by territory unit
- `segment-profiles`: Description of each segment and its characteristics

## Process

1. Define the territory units (zip code, state, industry, named accounts).
2. Estimate the addressable revenue in each unit.
3. Rank units by potential.
4. Group units into candidate territories.
