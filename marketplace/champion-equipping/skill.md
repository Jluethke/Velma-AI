# Champion Equipping

Equip the champion with the specific materials and arguments they need for internal conversations you are not part of.

## Inputs

- `champion-candidate`: The identified champion
- `stakeholder-map`: The other people the champion must convince

## Outputs

- `champion-toolkit`: Materials tailored for the champion to use internally: a one-page executive summary, an ROI calculation, answers to likely objections from each stakeholder
- `objection-prep`: The specific objections each stakeholder is likely to raise and the champion's counter-arguments

## Process

1. Ask the champion directly: 'When you present this internally, what questions will you get? What objections will [CFO/CTO/VP] raise?' The champion knows the internal dynamics better than you do.
2. Create materials the champion can use without modification: a one-page summary they can forward, an ROI calculation they can present, a competitive comparison they can reference.
3. For each likely objection from each stakeholder, give the champion a one-sentence counter-argument. The counter-argument must use the stakeholder's language and priorities, not yours.
4. The champion is doing your job inside the building. Make that job as easy as possible. The easier it is for them, the more likely they are to do it.
