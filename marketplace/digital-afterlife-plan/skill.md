# Digital Afterlife Plan

Plan for digital accounts and presence after death: social media, email, cloud storage, and subscriptions.

## Inputs

- `digital_accounts`: List of online accounts
- `digital_executor`: Person to manage digital assets
- `wishes`: Memorialize, delete, or transfer preferences

## Outputs

- `account_disposition`: What to do with each account
- `access_instructions`: How to access each account
- `legacy_content`: Messages or content to preserve or share

## Process

1. Inventory all digital accounts
2. Categorize by disposition wish
3. Document access methods securely
4. Set up legacy contacts where platforms allow
5. Prepare any posthumous messages or content
