# Social Trend Analyzer

Analyze trends from social listening data and produce the report.

## Inputs

- `mention-categories`: The categorized mentions
- `sentiment-baseline`: The baseline sentiment distribution
- `alert-rules`: The configured alerts

## Outputs

- `trend-analysis`: Weekly trend analysis showing volume changes, emerging themes, and sentiment shifts
- `social-listening-report`: The complete social listening report with findings and recommendations

## Process

1. Compare current week mention volume against the prior period and baseline.
2. Identify emerging themes or topics gaining traction.
3. Flag any sentiment shifts that cross the alert thresholds.
4. Summarize findings and recommend actions in the social listening report.
