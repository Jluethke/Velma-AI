# Fermentation Target Definition

Define the fermentation target product, organism, and baseline conditions to optimize from.

## Inputs

- `target-product`: What the fermentation should produce (metabolite, protein, biomass)
- `organism-strain`: The production organism and its known requirements

## Outputs

- `baseline-conditions`: Current best-known media, temperature, pH, aeration for this organism and product
- `optimization-objectives`: Which responses to maximize or minimize (titer, yield, productivity, cost)

## Process

1. Review the literature for the organism's growth requirements and known production conditions.
2. Establish baseline conditions from published protocols or prior runs.
3. Define the optimization objective: maximize titer, yield (product per substrate), or volumetric productivity.
4. Identify constraints: pH range, temperature limits, dissolved oxygen requirements, and cost limits.
