# Account Applicant Collector

Collect the applicant information and account preferences.

## Inputs

- `applicant-details`: Name, address, date of birth, SSN, employment, and contact information
- `account-type-request`: Type of account requested (checking, savings, money market, CD)

## Outputs

- `application-data`: Structured applicant record
- `account-preferences`: Selected account type and features (overdraft, online, debit card)

## Process

1. Record the applicant details into the onboarding system.
2. Confirm the requested account type and optional features.
3. Generate the application record with a unique reference.
