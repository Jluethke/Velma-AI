# Feedback Closure Reporter

Produce the feedback closure report with volume, resolution, and satisfaction metrics.

## Inputs

- `sla-compliance-rate`: SLA performance by team and category
- `closure-satisfaction`: Respondent satisfaction with feedback handling

## Outputs

- `feedback-closure-report`: Report with feedback volume, resolution rates, SLA compliance, and satisfaction scores

## Process

1. Aggregate feedback volume by category, urgency, and source over the reporting period.
2. Calculate resolution rate and average time to resolution by team.
3. Report SLA compliance rate and identify teams or categories with chronic overdue items.
4. Include respondent satisfaction scores and produce the feedback closure report.
