# Service Inventory Collector

Inventory all IT services currently provided.

## Inputs

- `it-organization`: IT teams and their responsibilities
- `existing-documentation`: Any existing service lists or documentation

## Outputs

- `service-inventory`: List of all IT services with owners
- `service-gaps`: Services that are provided but undocumented

## Process

1. Interview each IT team to identify the services they provide.
2. Cross-reference with ticket categories and request types.
3. Identify services that are provided but not formally documented.
4. Compile the complete service inventory.
