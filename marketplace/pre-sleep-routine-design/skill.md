# Pre Sleep Routine Design

Design a 30-minute wind-down routine that transitions your brain from active to sleep-ready.

## Inputs

- `temperature-protocol`: Temperature and timing plan
- `sleep-complaints`: Current sleep problems

## Outputs

- `wind-down-routine`: A specific 30-minute routine: what you do, in what order, starting at the same time each night
- `removal-list`: Activities to remove from the pre-sleep period: screens, work email, news, intense exercise, heavy meals

## Process

1. Start the routine at the same time each night, 30 minutes before target sleep time. Consistency trains the brain to associate the routine with sleep onset.
2. The routine should be low-stimulation: reading (paper, not screen), stretching, journaling, breathing exercises, or quiet conversation. Not TV, not social media, not work email.
3. If racing thoughts prevent sleep, use a 'worry journal': spend 5 minutes writing everything on your mind. The act of writing externalizes the thoughts and reduces their activation.
4. Do not exercise within 3 hours of sleep. Exercise raises core temperature and cortisol, both of which delay sleep onset. Morning or early afternoon exercise improves sleep quality; evening exercise disrupts it.
