# Mentoring Evaluation Reporter

Evaluate the mentoring program outcomes and produce the final report.

## Inputs

- `milestone-status`: Final status of each pair's progress
- `mid-program-feedback`: Feedback collected during the program

## Outputs

- `mentoring-evaluation`: Evaluation of program outcomes and satisfaction
- `mentoring-program-document`: Final program document with recommendations for next cycle

## Process

1. Survey mentors and mentees on satisfaction and goal achievement
2. Assess research outputs (publications, presentations, grants submitted)
3. Identify program strengths and areas for improvement
4. Produce the evaluation report and recommendations
