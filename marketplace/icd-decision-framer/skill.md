# Icd Decision Framer

Frame the choice and its visible costs.

## Inputs

- `decision-context`: The choice being made and its purpose
- `options-to-compare`: The options under comparison
- `your-hourly-value`: The dollar value of an hour of your time

## Outputs

- `option-set`: The options with their key attributes
- `visible-cost-table`: The sticker price of each option

## Process

1. List the options being compared.
2. Record the sticker price of each option as its visible cost.
3. Record the hourly value of your time and the period the costs cover.
