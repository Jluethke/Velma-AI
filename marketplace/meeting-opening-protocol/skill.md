# Meeting Opening Protocol

Open the meeting by checking whether everyone read the pre-read. If they did not, cancel the meeting and reschedule. Meetings where half the room has not read the pre-read produce discussions, not decisions.

## Inputs

- `pre-read-document`: What was sent in advance
- `attendee-list`: Who is in the room

## Outputs

- `readiness-check-result`: Whether the meeting proceeds or is rescheduled
- `meeting-rules`: The rules for the meeting: discuss disagreements only, time-boxed, decision at the end

## Process

1. Spend the first 2 minutes asking: 'Has everyone read the pre-read?' If more than one person has not, reschedule.
2. If everyone has read it, state the rules: 'We are here to decide [decision]. The recommendation is [X]. We will discuss only where you disagree with the recommendation.'
3. Set the time box: 'We have [N] minutes. We will decide by the end or define what we need to decide next time.'
4. Do not summarize the pre-read. That rewards the people who did not read it.
