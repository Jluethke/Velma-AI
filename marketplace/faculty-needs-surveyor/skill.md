# Faculty Needs Surveyor

Assess faculty development needs across teaching, technology, and research.

## Inputs

- `faculty-roster`: List of faculty with roles, disciplines, and experience levels
- `institutional-priorities`: Teaching and research priorities of the institution

## Outputs

- `needs-survey-results`: Aggregated faculty development needs by category
- `priority-topics`: Ranked list of development topics

## Process

1. Survey faculty on self-assessed development needs
2. Analyze student evaluation data for common areas of concern
3. Cross-reference with institutional priorities
4. Rank topics by frequency and institutional alignment
