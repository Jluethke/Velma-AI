# Age And Risk Profile

Build a profile of your age and risk factors, because the right checkups and routines depend on both.

## Inputs

- `age-and-sex`: Your age and sex, which set the baseline screening calendar
- `personal-and-family-history`: Your own conditions and the conditions that run in your family

## Outputs

- `risk-profile`: A profile combining age-based baseline risk with your personal and family history
- `risk-flags`: The specific elevated risks that call for earlier or more frequent screening

## Process

1. Start from age and sex, which set the standard screening calendar most guidelines are organized around.
2. Layer in personal history: existing conditions, past results, and current measures that raise or lower risk.
3. Layer in family history, flagging conditions that appeared in close relatives, since these often move a screening earlier or make it more frequent.
4. Mark the risk flags clearly. These are the points where your calendar will differ from the standard one, and they drive the schedule in the next step.
