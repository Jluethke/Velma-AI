# Difficulty Progression Rule

Define when and how to increase drill difficulty as you improve, so practice stays at the edge of ability.

## Inputs

- `session-protocol`: The practice session structure
- `tracking-method`: How you track success rate

## Outputs

- `progression-rules`: When to increase difficulty (success rate threshold), how to increase it (specific levers), and when to decrease it (frustration threshold)
- `difficulty-levers`: The specific dimensions you can adjust: speed, complexity, constraints, time pressure

## Process

1. Increase difficulty when your success rate exceeds 80% for three consecutive sessions. You have learned this level; staying here is repetition, not practice.
2. Decrease difficulty if your success rate drops below 50% for two consecutive sessions. You jumped too far; back off one increment.
3. Identify the specific levers for your drill. For writing: increase word count constraints, add a new audience, reduce revision time. For a physical skill: increase speed, reduce rest between reps, add an obstacle.
4. When you increase difficulty, expect your success rate to drop to 60-70%. This is correct. If it does not drop, you did not increase enough.
