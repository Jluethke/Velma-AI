# Engagement Action Planner

Create the action plan to address survey findings.

## Inputs

- `results-analysis`: Analysis results
- `key-drivers`: Top drivers

## Outputs

- `action-plan`: Prioritized action plan with owners and timelines
- `communication-back`: Communication to employees about what the organization heard and will do

## Process

1. Prioritize the improvement areas by impact and feasibility
2. Assign owners and timelines for each action item
3. Draft the communication back to employees
4. Set checkpoints to track progress before the next survey
