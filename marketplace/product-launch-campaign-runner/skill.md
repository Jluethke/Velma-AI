# Product Launch Campaign Runner

Run the launch campaign across email, social, and affiliates.

## Inputs

- `sales-page`: The live sales page
- `product-assets`: Marketing assets for the launch

## Outputs

- `launch-metrics`: Sales, revenue, conversion rate, traffic by source
- `launch-record`: Complete record of the launch with what worked and what to improve

## Process

1. Execute the launch sequence (pre-launch tease, launch day push, mid-launch update, final call).
2. Promote through email list, social media, and affiliate or referral partners.
3. Monitor sales and conversion rate in real time during the launch window.
4. Compile the launch results and document lessons for the next product.
