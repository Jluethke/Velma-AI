# Key Terms Identifier

Identify and extract key commercial and legal terms from the contract.

## Inputs

- `contract-text`: Full text of the contract
- `section-inventory`: List of sections and defined terms

## Outputs

- `key-terms-summary`: Extracted key terms with clause references
- `commercial-terms`: Payment, scope, term, and termination details

## Process

1. Extract scope of work or services description
2. Identify payment terms, amounts, and schedules
3. Note the contract term, renewal, and termination provisions
