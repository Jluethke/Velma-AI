# Progressive Expansion

After 30 days of consistency, expand gradually: longer duration, higher intensity, or additional days. Never more than one variable at a time.

## Inputs

- `movement-schedule`: The current plan
- `movement-prescription`: Starting parameters

## Outputs

- `expansion-plan`: Month 2: increase duration to 30 minutes. Month 3: add a 4th day. Month 4: increase intensity on one day. Never more than one change per month.
- `plateau-response`: If progress stalls or motivation drops, return to the minimum effective dose rather than quitting entirely

## Process

1. Change one variable per month: duration OR frequency OR intensity. Never two at once. Two simultaneous changes double the failure risk.
2. Month 2: increase from 20 to 30 minutes. Month 3: add a 4th day. Month 4: increase intensity on one session (hills, intervals, or faster pace). This slow progression builds a stable habit.
3. If you miss a week, do not 'make up for it' by doubling the next week. Return to the minimum dose and rebuild the streak.
4. The long-term target is 150 minutes of moderate activity per week (the WHO recommendation). At the progression rate above, you reach it in month 4. Most people who start at 150 minutes quit in month 1.
