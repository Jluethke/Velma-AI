# Training Needs Assessor

Assess the threat landscape and organizational risk profile to determine training needs.

## Inputs

- `threat-landscape`: Current threats targeting the organization
- `organization-profile`: Roles, departments, and risk areas

## Outputs

- `training-needs`: Identified training needs by role and risk area
- `priority-topics`: Ranked list of topics by risk reduction potential

## Process

1. Analyze the top threats facing the organization
2. Map threats to the roles most likely to encounter them
3. Identify knowledge gaps from prior incident data and phishing results
4. Rank training topics by risk reduction potential
