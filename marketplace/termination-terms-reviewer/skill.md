# Termination Terms Reviewer

Review termination provisions, severance, and change of control terms.

## Inputs

- `agreement-text`: Employment agreement text
- `comp-analysis`: Compensation analysis

## Outputs

- `termination-analysis`: Analysis of termination triggers and severance
- `coc-provisions`: Change of control and acceleration provisions

## Process

1. Review at-will status and cause/good-reason definitions
2. Analyze severance package terms and conditions
3. Check for change of control acceleration and double-trigger provisions
