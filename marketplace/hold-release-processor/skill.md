# Hold Release Processor

Release the litigation hold when authorized and document the release.

## Inputs

- `hold-compliance-status`: Current compliance status
- `reminder-log`: Complete reminder history

## Outputs

- `release-notice`: The hold release notice sent to all custodians
- `hold-management-record`: Complete record of the hold from trigger through release

## Process

1. Obtain authorization to release the hold from the responsible attorney.
2. Draft and send the release notice to all custodians and IT.
3. Compile the complete hold management record (trigger, notices, acknowledgments, reminders, release).
4. Archive the record in the matter file.
