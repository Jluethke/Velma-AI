# Owner Distribution Preparer

Prepare the owner distribution and produce the monthly financial report.

## Inputs

- `financial-statements`: P&L, NOI, and cash flow
- `budget-variance-analysis`: Variance analysis

## Outputs

- `owner-distribution`: Distribution amount and supporting calculation
- `monthly-financial-report`: Complete monthly financial report package

## Process

1. Calculate the owner distribution based on cash flow and reserve requirements.
2. Prepare the distribution check or wire.
3. Package the financial statements, variance analysis, and distribution into the monthly report.
