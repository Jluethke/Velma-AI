# Findings Presenter

Present findings to leadership with recommendations.

## Inputs

- `results-analysis`: Analysis results
- `key-drivers`: Top engagement drivers

## Outputs

- `leadership-presentation`: Presentation for leadership
- `department-reports`: Reports for each department head

## Process

1. Create the executive summary with top-level findings
2. Build department-level reports with team-specific data
3. Highlight areas of strength and areas needing attention
4. Present to leadership and facilitate discussion
