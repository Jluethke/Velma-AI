# Final Formulation Report

Compile all development data into the final formulation report with process specification, scale-up notes, and commercialization readiness assessment.

## Inputs

- `sensory-scores`: Sensory evaluation results
- `shelf-life-estimate`: Shelf life determination
- `compliance-checklist`: Regulatory compliance status

## Outputs

- `formulation-report`: Complete product development report with formulation, process, stability, sensory, nutrition, and regulatory sections
- `scale-up-protocol`: Pilot plant process parameters and critical control points for scale-up

## Process

1. Compile the formulation with exact ingredient percentages, water activity, and pH targets
2. Document the process flow from batching through filling with time, temperature, and shear specifications
3. Summarize shelf life, sensory, and regulatory findings with pass/fail against acceptance criteria
4. Outline scale-up considerations: equipment, batch size, mixing time adjustments, and HACCP plan
