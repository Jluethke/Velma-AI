# High Importance Protection

Protect cognitive energy for the high-importance decisions by scheduling them during peak mental hours and batching them.

## Inputs

- `classified-decisions`: The decision quadrants
- `elimination-rules`: The rules removing low-importance decisions

## Outputs

- `decision-schedule`: When high-importance decisions will be made (ideally in the first 2-4 hours of the day, before fatigue accumulates)
- `decision-batching-plan`: Similar decisions grouped together so they are made in one session instead of scattered throughout the day

## Process

1. Identify your peak cognitive hours. For most people, this is the first 2-4 hours after waking (after any morning routine). Schedule high-importance decisions during this window.
2. Batch similar decisions together. All hiring decisions in one block. All budget decisions in one block. All strategic decisions in one block. Context-switching between decision types increases fatigue.
3. Never make high-importance/irreversible decisions at the end of a long day. If forced to, defer to the next morning.
4. Create a decision-making ritual for high-importance items: define the decision in writing, list the options, score each option against 2-3 criteria, choose, and record the rationale. The ritual prevents impulsive choices during low-energy moments.
