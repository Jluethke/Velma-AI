# Followup Capture Protocol

Design the capture protocol that gets a follow-up out of your head and into the system within 24 hours of an interaction, because the window in which you remember what was said and what you intended to do closes fast, and a follow-up not captured in that window is usually lost.

## Inputs

- `interaction-types`: The kinds of interactions that generate follow-ups: meetings, calls, chance conversations, messages, events
- `capture-tools`: The tools available to capture into: a task app, a notes app, voice memo, a CRM, a notebook

## Outputs

- `capture-protocol`: The protocol for capturing a follow-up right after an interaction, tuned to each interaction type
- `capture-window-rules`: The rule that follow-ups are captured within 24 hours, with the practical trigger that enforces it

## Process

1. Set the core rule: capture within 24 hours, ideally within minutes. The memory of what mattered and what you meant to do decays quickly, and a vague recollection a week later is not enough to act on.
2. Design capture per interaction type. A scheduled meeting can end with two minutes of capture built in, a chance conversation needs an immediate voice memo or note before the next thing crowds it out, a message thread can be captured as you close it.
3. Choose the lowest-friction tool you will actually use in the moment. The best capture tool is the one already in your hand, so a quick note or voice memo beats a polished system you have to sit down to open.
4. Attach capture to an existing trigger so it does not depend on remembering to remember: the act of leaving a meeting, hanging up a call, or closing a conversation becomes the cue to capture before moving on.
