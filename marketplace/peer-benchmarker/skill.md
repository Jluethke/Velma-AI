# Peer Benchmarker

Benchmark the company's ratios against industry peers.

## Inputs

- `liquidity-ratios`: Company liquidity ratios
- `profitability-ratios`: Company profitability ratios
- `leverage-ratios`: Company leverage ratios
- `efficiency-ratios`: Company efficiency ratios

## Outputs

- `benchmark-comparison`: Company vs peer comparison by ratio category
- `strengths-weaknesses`: Identified financial strengths and weaknesses

## Process

1. Select appropriate peer companies or industry averages for comparison.
2. Compare each ratio category against the peer set.
3. Identify ratios where the company outperforms or underperforms peers.
4. Summarize financial strengths and areas needing improvement.
