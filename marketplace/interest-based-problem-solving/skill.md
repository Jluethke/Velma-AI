# Interest Based Problem Solving

Focus on interests (what each party needs) rather than positions (what each party demands).

## Inputs

- `reframed-problem`: The shared obstacle
- `shared-interest`: What both parties want

## Outputs

- `interests-map`: Each party's underlying interests behind their stated positions
- `creative-options`: 3-5 options that serve both parties' interests

## Process

1. Distinguish positions from interests. A position is 'I want a raise.' The interest might be financial security, recognition, or career progression.
2. Ask: 'What is important to you about [their position]?' The answer reveals the interest.
3. Generate options that serve both interests.
4. Present 3-5 options and let the other party participate in choosing.
