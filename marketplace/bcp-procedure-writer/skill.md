# Bcp Procedure Writer

Document the detailed recovery procedures in the BCP document.

## Inputs

- `recovery-strategies`: Selected strategies to document
- `recovery-priority-list`: Order of recovery

## Outputs

- `bcp-document`: Complete business continuity plan with procedures
- `contact-list`: Emergency contact list for plan activation

## Process

1. Write step-by-step procedures for each recovery scenario
2. Include decision trees for plan activation
3. Document roles, responsibilities, and the communication chain
4. Compile the emergency contact list
