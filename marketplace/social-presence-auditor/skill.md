# Social Presence Auditor

Audit the current social media presence and performance.

## Inputs

- `social-accounts`: List of social media accounts to audit
- `business-goals`: Business objectives for social media

## Outputs

- `social-audit-report`: Current follower counts, engagement rates, and content analysis
- `improvement-opportunities`: Gaps and opportunities identified

## Process

1. Document current follower counts and growth trends
2. Analyze engagement rates by content type and platform
3. Identify top-performing and underperforming content
4. Benchmark against competitors in the same space
