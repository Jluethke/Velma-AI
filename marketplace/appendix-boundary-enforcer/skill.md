# Appendix Boundary Enforcer

Everything that does not serve the Situation-Complication-Resolution goes in the appendix. The appendix is for people who want to check your work, not for people who need to make the decision.

## Inputs

- `dataset-description`: The full dataset
- `data-relevance-filter`: What matters vs. what is noise

## Outputs

- `appendix-contents`: List of supporting data, methodology, and additional charts for the appendix
- `main-presentation-contents`: Only: situation, complication chart, resolution. Nothing else in the main body.

## Process

1. Move every chart except the complication chart to the appendix.
2. Move methodology, sample sizes, caveats, and alternative analyses to the appendix.
3. The main presentation should fit on 3 slides or one page: situation (1), complication with chart (1), resolution (1).
4. Test: if the audience reads only the main body, do they have enough to make the decision? If yes, the appendix is correctly separated.
