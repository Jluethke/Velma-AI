# Nas Anchor Builder

Set the anchor twenty to forty percent beyond the target.

## Inputs

- `target-outcome`: The target the anchor is set beyond
- `market-range`: The range that keeps the anchor defensible

## Outputs

- `anchor-value`: The opening anchor number
- `anchor-justification`: The reasoning that makes the anchor credible

## Process

1. Place the anchor twenty to forty percent past the target in your favor.
2. Keep the anchor within the bounds of a defensible number.
3. Write the reasoning that makes the anchor credible.
