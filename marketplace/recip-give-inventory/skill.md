# Recip Give Inventory

Inventory what you give to each relationship across the full value lens, honestly and concretely, since most people either undercount their giving (and feel resentful) or overcount it (and feel entitled), and the rebalance depends on an accurate count.

## Inputs

- `focus-relationships`: The relationships being mapped
- `lens-definition`: What counts as value in these relationships

## Outputs

- `give-inventory`: For each relationship, the concrete things you give across the value lens
- `give-frequency-notes`: How often and how substantially you give, so a one-time large gift is not confused with steady small support

## Process

1. For each relationship, list what you actually give across every category in the lens: time spent, help offered, support provided, information shared, introductions made, opportunities passed along.
2. Be concrete, not aspirational. Count what you have genuinely given recently, not what you intend to give or what you gave once years ago. The map needs current reality.
3. Record frequency and weight, because they change the picture. Steady small support reads very differently from a single large favor, and both differ from availability (being reliably there) which is a real but easily overlooked form of giving.
4. Watch your own bias. If you tend toward resentment, you likely undercount. If you tend toward entitlement, you likely overcount. Name your lean and correct for it deliberately.
