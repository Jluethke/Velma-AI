# Reader Action Definer

Define what the reader should do after reading. If you do not know, you are not ready to write. Every sentence that does not move the reader toward that action is a sentence to cut.

## Inputs

- `draft-or-topic`: The message, email, memo, or proposal you are writing or revising
- `reader-description`: Who will read this and what role they play

## Outputs

- `reader-action`: The specific action the reader should take after reading, in one sentence
- `reader-questions`: The questions the reader needs answered before they can take that action

## Process

1. Write the action in one sentence: 'Approve the budget by Friday.' 'Schedule a meeting with the vendor.' 'Stop doing X and start doing Y.'
2. If the piece is informational (no action required), state the takeaway: 'Understand that X changed and what it means for their work.'
3. List the questions the reader will have before acting: How much does it cost? What is the timeline? What happens if they say no?
4. Your message must answer these questions. Anything else is decoration.
