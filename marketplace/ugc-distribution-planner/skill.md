# Ugc Distribution Planner

Distribute UGC across marketing channels and track performance.

## Inputs

- `curated-content-library`: The approved UGC library
- `marketing-goals`: The program's marketing goals

## Outputs

- `distribution-plan`: Schedule of UGC placements across channels (social, website, email, ads)
- `ugc-program-plan`: The complete UGC program plan with metrics and review cadence

## Process

1. Map each UGC piece to the channels where it will be used (social posts, website galleries, email campaigns, ad creative).
2. Schedule the placements and set the attribution (creator credit).
3. Define the metrics to track (engagement on UGC vs. brand content, submission volume, conversion impact).
4. Compile the full UGC program plan.
