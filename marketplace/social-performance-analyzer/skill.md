# Social Performance Analyzer

Analyze content performance and produce the monthly report.

## Inputs

- `published-posts`: Published content to measure
- `business-goals`: Objectives to measure against

## Outputs

- `performance-report`: Monthly metrics with analysis and recommendations
- `next-month-adjustments`: Changes to apply in the next month's calendar

## Process

1. Pull engagement metrics for each post (likes, comments, shares, clicks)
2. Calculate aggregate metrics by pillar and platform
3. Identify top and bottom performers with reasons
4. Recommend adjustments for the next month's strategy
