# Diversification Strategy

Build a diversification strategy that reduces concentration over 6-12 months without alienating existing large customers.

## Inputs

- `departure-scenarios`: Impact of losing concentrated customers
- `concentration-table`: Current revenue distribution

## Outputs

- `diversification-plan`: Specific actions to reduce concentration: acquire more small/medium customers, develop new revenue streams, reduce dependency on the largest customer
- `target-concentration`: The target distribution after 12 months (no customer above 15%, top 3 below 40%)

## Process

1. The goal is not to lose the large customer. The goal is to grow the rest of the business so that no single customer represents a dangerous share.
2. Identify the customer profile of your next 10 ideal customers (similar to your best non-concentrated customers). Focus acquisition efforts on this profile.
3. Develop at least one revenue stream that does not depend on any single customer: a product, a service tier, or a market segment that distributes across many buyers.
4. Set 12-month targets: no customer above 15%, top 3 customers below 40% of total revenue. Track monthly.
