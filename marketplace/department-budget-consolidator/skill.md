# Department Budget Consolidator

Collect and consolidate department budget requests.

## Inputs

- `budget-call-package`: The issued budget call
- `submission-deadline`: Submission deadline

## Outputs

- `consolidated-budget`: All department requests consolidated into a single budget document
- `variance-analysis`: Analysis of changes from the prior year and from the baseline

## Process

1. Collect budget submissions from each department.
2. Verify submissions are complete and follow the required format.
3. Consolidate all requests into the unified budget document.
4. Calculate variances from the prior year and from the baseline for each line item.
