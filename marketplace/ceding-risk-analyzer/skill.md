# Ceding Risk Analyzer

Analyze the ceding company's risk profile and reinsurance needs.

## Inputs

- `portfolio-data`: The ceding company's portfolio data (premium, loss history, exposure by line and geography)
- `reinsurance-objectives`: The objectives for the reinsurance program (volatility reduction, capacity, catastrophe protection, capital relief)

## Outputs

- `risk-analysis`: Analysis of the portfolio's risk profile, peak exposures, and retention capacity
- `reinsurance-needs`: Identified reinsurance needs by type (proportional, excess, catastrophe)

## Process

1. Analyze the portfolio by line, geography, and loss experience.
2. Model the peak exposures and probable maximum loss scenarios.
3. Assess the company's retention capacity and risk tolerance.
4. Identify the types and amounts of reinsurance needed.
