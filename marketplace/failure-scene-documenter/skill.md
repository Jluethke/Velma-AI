# Failure Scene Documenter

Document the failed component and its surroundings before any destructive analysis.

## Inputs

- `failed-component`: The component that failed, with service history and operating conditions
- `failure-circumstances`: When, where, and how the failure was discovered

## Outputs

- `visual-documentation`: Photographs, measurements, and sketches of the failure in context
- `service-history-summary`: Operating conditions, maintenance records, and any prior issues

## Process

1. Photograph the failed component from all angles before handling
2. Record dimensions, orientation, and relationship to adjacent parts
3. Collect the service history including loads, temperatures, environment, and maintenance
4. Note any obvious damage patterns, corrosion products, or deformation
