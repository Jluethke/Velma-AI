# Sfi Core Message Extractor

Extract the one message the story has to carry and the change you want it to produce. A story with two messages lands neither. Decide the single idea first, because every later choice of protagonist, problem, and resolution is made in service of it.

## Inputs

- `influence-goal`: What you want the audience to believe, feel, or do after the story
- `audience`: Who the audience is and what they currently think about the topic

## Outputs

- `core-message`: The single idea the story must deliver, in one sentence
- `change-sought`: The specific shift in belief or action the story is meant to produce

## Process

1. Write the one idea the story must leave behind. If you list two, choose the one the other depends on.
2. Write the change you want: what the audience thinks or does differently once the story lands.
3. Check that the idea is a message, not a topic. 'Customer trust is fragile' is a message. 'Our support process' is a topic.
4. Keep this core message in view through every later step. Anything in the story that does not serve it is a candidate to cut.
