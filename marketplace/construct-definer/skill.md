# Construct Definer

Define the theoretical constructs under study with operational definitions, measurement indicators, and literature grounding.

## Inputs

- `research-question`: The specific question or hypothesis the study addresses
- `theoretical-framework`: The theory or conceptual model guiding the study
- `prior-literature`: Key studies and existing measures of the constructs

## Outputs

- `construct-definitions`: Operational definitions for each construct with measurable indicators
- `conceptual-model`: Diagram of relationships between constructs and expected directions
- `measurement-plan`: Mapping of each construct to specific survey items or interview prompts

## Process

1. Extract construct definitions from the theoretical framework and prior literature
2. Write operational definitions that specify observable indicators for each construct
3. Map relationships between constructs into a conceptual model with directional hypotheses
4. Identify existing validated scales or measures for each construct
5. Document gaps where new measurement items need development
