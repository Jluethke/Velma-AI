# Ninety Second Intervention

Design a 90-second intervention that interrupts the stress response: physiological sigh, box breathing, or cold water on the face.

## Inputs

- `physical-signals`: Your stress signals
- `current-stress-level`: Current state

## Outputs

- `intervention-menu`: 3 interventions that work in 90 seconds: physiological sigh (double inhale, long exhale), box breathing (4-4-4-4), and cold exposure (cold water on wrists or face)
- `context-matching`: Which intervention to use in which context: sigh at desk, box breathing in a meeting, cold water in a bathroom

## Process

1. The physiological sigh is the fastest intervention: two quick inhales through the nose, then one long exhale through the mouth. This activates the parasympathetic nervous system within seconds.
2. Box breathing for sustained calm: inhale 4 seconds, hold 4 seconds, exhale 4 seconds, hold 4 seconds. Repeat 3-4 cycles. This takes 60-90 seconds and is invisible in a meeting.
3. Cold exposure: run cold water over your wrists for 30 seconds, or splash cold water on your face. This triggers the dive reflex, which slows heart rate and calms the nervous system.
4. Practice the interventions when you are NOT stressed. The habit must be automatic so that when a stress signal fires, the intervention launches without deliberation.
