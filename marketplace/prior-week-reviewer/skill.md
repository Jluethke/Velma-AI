# Prior Week Reviewer

Review the prior week for wins, misses, and lessons.

## Inputs

- `last-week-plan`: The plan from last week
- `week-notes`: Any notes or reflections from the week

## Outputs

- `week-review`: Summary of wins, misses, and lessons
- `carryover-items`: Tasks that carry forward to the new week

## Process

1. List what was accomplished versus what was planned.
2. Note the top 3 wins and what made them work.
3. Note the top 3 misses and what blocked them.
4. Extract lessons and items to carry forward.
