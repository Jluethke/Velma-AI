# Insurance Coverage Assessment

Assess current coverage adequacy and identify gaps across auto, home, health, life, and umbrella.

## Inputs

- `current_policies`: Existing insurance policies
- `assets_to_protect`: Assets, income, and liabilities
- `life_situation`: Family, dependents, mortgage, etc.

## Outputs

- `coverage_gaps`: Where coverage is insufficient
- `over_insurance`: Where paying for unneeded coverage
- `priority_list`: Insurance actions ranked by urgency

## Process

1. Inventory all current policies and coverage
2. Assess coverage against actual risk exposure
3. Identify gaps in coverage
4. Flag areas of over-insurance
5. Prioritize actions by risk
