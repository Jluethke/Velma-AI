# Missing Perspective Definer

Define what is missing from the team's perspectives and capabilities. This is not about what the team cannot do technically. It is about how the team thinks and what blind spots that creates. A team of all engineers misses the customer perspective. A team of all generalists misses deep expertise. A team of all optimists misses risk awareness.

## Inputs

- `composition-profile`: The team's current makeup
- `homogeneity-flags`: Where the team is too uniform

## Outputs

- `missing-perspectives`: The specific perspectives, experiences, and thinking styles that the team lacks, with an explanation of why each gap matters
- `blind-spot-examples`: Concrete examples of decisions or outcomes where the team's homogeneity caused a problem or missed an opportunity

## Process

1. For each homogeneity flag, ask: what has this uniformity caused us to miss? Interview team members and stakeholders for examples.
2. For each gap identified in the composition audit, describe the perspective that is missing and why it matters for the team's mission.
3. Write the blind-spot examples as stories: 'We launched feature X without considering use case Y because nobody on the team had experience Z.'
4. Prioritize the missing perspectives by impact: which gap, if filled, would most improve the team's decisions and output?
