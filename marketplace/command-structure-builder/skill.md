# Command Structure Builder

Define who is in charge during each scenario and what authority they have.

## Inputs

- `trigger-scenarios`: The disruption scenarios
- `recovery-priority-order`: The recovery priorities

## Outputs

- `command-structure`: For each severity level: the incident commander, the team leads, and their specific authorities (spend limits, contract authority, communication authority)
- `succession-plan`: If the primary commander is unavailable, the succession order with at least 3 deep

## Process

1. Designate the incident commander for each severity level. For partial: the function owner. For major: the operations leader (or, for personal, the primary adult). For total: the senior leader or designated trusted person.
2. Define the commander's authority: spending authority (up to $X without further approval), contract authority (can engage emergency vendors), communication authority (speaks for the organization).
3. Build the succession plan: at least 3 people deep for each role. If person 1 is unavailable, person 2 takes over. Document how person 2 is notified.
4. For personal plans: the succession is your trusted person network. Define who takes which role.
