# Volunteer Recruitment Plan

Build a recruitment plan targeting the right volunteers through the right channels with clear asks.

## Inputs

- `role_descriptions`: Roles to fill
- `headcount_needed`: How many per role
- `community_channels`: Available recruitment channels

## Outputs

- `recruitment_message`: Tailored recruitment messaging
- `channel_plan`: Which channels to use for which roles
- `signup_system`: How volunteers sign up

## Process

1. Write role descriptions highlighting impact
2. Match roles to recruitment channels
3. Create simple signup process
4. Set recruitment timeline with milestones
5. Plan follow-up for sign-ups
