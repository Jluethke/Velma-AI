# Current Eating Audit

Audit what you actually eat for 7 days, without changing anything. Most people do not know what they eat.

## Inputs

- `current-diet`: What you think you eat
- `nutrition-goals`: What you want to change: energy, weight, health markers, performance

## Outputs

- `food-log`: 7-day log: every meal, snack, and drink with approximate portions and times
- `eating-patterns`: Patterns: meal timing, snacking frequency, default choices when tired, weekend vs weekday differences

## Process

1. Log everything for 7 days without trying to eat differently. The point is to see reality, not to perform good behavior. Include drinks (especially alcohol and sugary drinks).
2. Note the context of each meal: where were you, what time was it, how hungry were you, and was it planned or improvised? Improvised meals are usually lower quality.
3. Identify the default choices: what do you eat when you are tired and do not want to cook? What do you reach for as a snack? These defaults determine 80% of your nutrition over a year.
4. Calculate rough proportions: how much of your food is whole/minimally processed vs ultra-processed? A simple test: if you cannot identify all the ingredients by looking at it, it is processed.
