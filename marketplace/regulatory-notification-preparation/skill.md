# Regulatory Notification Preparation

Prepare and submit the notification to the relevant regulatory authority.

## Inputs

- `breach-assessment`: The breach scope and severity assessment
- `notification-determination`: Notification requirements and deadlines

## Outputs

- `regulatory-notification`: The notification submitted to the regulator
- `submission-record`: Record of submission (date, method, confirmation)

## Process

1. Draft the regulatory notification with the required information (nature of breach, data categories, number of individuals, consequences, measures taken).
2. Have the notification reviewed by legal counsel.
3. Submit the notification to the supervisory authority within the legal deadline (72 hours for GDPR).
4. Record the submission details (date, method, reference number, confirmation receipt).
