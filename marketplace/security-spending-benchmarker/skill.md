# Security Spending Benchmarker

Benchmark the proposed security budget against industry peers by revenue, employee count, and sector.

## Inputs

- `cost-estimates`: Proposed investment costs
- `current-security-stack`: Current spend baseline

## Outputs

- `benchmark-comparison`: Current and proposed spend compared to industry median, 25th, and 75th percentile
- `peer-context`: Context on what comparable organizations spend and how they allocate

## Process

1. Calculate current security spend as a percentage of IT budget and revenue
2. Compare against published benchmarks (Gartner, IANS, Hitch Partners)
3. Note where the organization falls relative to peers (below, at, above median)
4. Contextualize: higher spend is not always better; tie to risk posture
