# Consensusconviction Test Buyin Need

Test whether buy-in from the group matters more than the speed of deciding.

## Inputs

- `info-distribution-score`: How distributed the needed information is.
- `info-holders`: Who holds the information the decision depends on.

## Outputs

- `buyin-score`: How much buy-in matters for this decision.
- `buyin-reasons`: Why buy-in does or does not matter here.

## Process

1. Judge how much the decision depends on people willingly carrying it out.
2. Weigh the value of buy-in against the value of deciding quickly.
3. Score how much buy-in matters on a shared scale.
