# Materials Sample Prep

Prepare samples for characterization: cutting, mounting, polishing, coating, and sectioning matched to each downstream technique.

## Inputs

- `material-description`: Composition, form (bulk, film, powder), and handling precautions
- `techniques-planned`: Which characterization methods will be used

## Outputs

- `sample-inventory`: Labeled samples with dimensions, preparation method, and target technique
- `preparation-log`: Step-by-step prep record (grinding grits, polishing compounds, etchants, coatings)
- `storage-conditions`: Temperature, atmosphere, and shelf-life constraints per sample

## Process

1. Section the material into specimens sized for each technique
2. Mount, grind, and polish metallographic samples to the required finish
3. Apply conductive coatings for electron microscopy samples
4. Prepare powder specimens for XRD (grinding, sieving, mounting)
5. Label and store all samples under appropriate conditions
