# Survey Action Planner

Turn survey findings into an improvement action plan.

## Inputs

- `score-analysis`: Quantitative survey results
- `theme-analysis`: Qualitative themes from comments

## Outputs

- `survey-report`: Full survey report with scores, themes, and trends
- `action-plan`: Prioritized improvements with owners and deadlines

## Process

1. Rank improvement areas by impact and feasibility.
2. Assign owners and deadlines for each action.
3. Define the success metric for each action.
4. Produce the survey report and action plan.
