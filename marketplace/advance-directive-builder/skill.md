# Advance Directive Builder

Create detailed advance directives covering medical treatments, organ donation, and comfort care preferences.

## Inputs

- `personal_values`: Quality of life priorities
- `medical_history`: Relevant health conditions
- `healthcare_agent`: Person chosen to make decisions

## Outputs

- `directive_decisions`: All medical preference decisions
- `scenario_preferences`: Treatment choices for specific scenarios
- `agent_instructions`: Detailed guidance for healthcare agent

## Process

1. Define quality of life thresholds
2. Address specific medical scenarios
3. Document organ and tissue donation wishes
4. Write detailed instructions for healthcare agent
5. Specify comfort care preferences
