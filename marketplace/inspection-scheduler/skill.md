# Inspection Scheduler

Schedule the loss control inspection and prepare the inspector.

## Inputs

- `inspection-request`: The request from underwriting with the insured's information and coverage details
- `prior-inspection-report`: Previous inspection report if available

## Outputs

- `inspection-appointment`: Confirmed appointment with the insured including date, time, and inspector contact
- `inspection-checklist`: Customized checklist for the inspection based on the industry and coverage type

## Process

1. Review the underwriting request and prior inspection findings.
2. Customize the inspection checklist for the industry and hazard exposures.
3. Contact the insured to schedule the visit.
4. Prepare the inspector with background information and the checklist.
