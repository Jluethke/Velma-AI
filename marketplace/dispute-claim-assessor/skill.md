# Dispute Claim Assessor

Assess the incoming claim or complaint for merits and exposure.

## Inputs

- `claim-details`: The claim, complaint, or demand received
- `internal-facts`: Internal facts and documentation relevant to the dispute

## Outputs

- `merits-assessment`: Assessment of the strength of the claim and the defense
- `exposure-estimate`: Estimated financial and non-financial exposure

## Process

1. Analyze the claim: what is alleged, what relief is sought, and what is the legal basis.
2. Review the internal facts and documentation that support or undermine the claim.
3. Assess the strength of the claim and the available defenses.
4. Estimate the financial exposure (damages, costs, fees) and non-financial risks (reputation, precedent).
