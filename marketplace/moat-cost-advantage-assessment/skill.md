# Moat Cost Advantage Assessment

Assess whether the business has a durable cost advantage: the ability to produce or deliver at lower cost than rivals in a way they cannot easily copy. Scale, process, location, and privileged access are the usual sources.

## Inputs

- `business-profile`: A tight profile of the business model and customers
- `competitor-set`: The named set of real competitors

## Outputs

- `cost-advantage-score`: A 0 to 5 score for durable cost advantage
- `cost-advantage-evidence`: The concrete reasons behind the score

## Process

1. Ask whether the business can deliver the same thing at lower cost than the competitor set, and why.
2. Trace the cost edge to a source: scale, a better process, a location, or privileged access to inputs.
3. Test durability. If a well-funded rival could match the cost in a year, the advantage is weak.
4. Score 0 to 5 and write the evidence. A score with no evidence is a guess.
