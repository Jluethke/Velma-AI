# Survey Objective Definer

Define the survey research question, target audience, and success criteria.

## Inputs

- `research-topic`: The subject area or question the survey will investigate
- `audience-description`: Who should respond to the survey (role, segment, demographics)

## Outputs

- `research-question`: The specific question the survey will answer
- `target-audience-profile`: Detailed profile of the intended respondent group
- `success-criteria`: Response rate target and minimum sample size

## Process

1. Clarify the decision the survey results will inform and write a single research question.
2. Define the target audience by relevant attributes (role, tenure, geography, usage level).
3. Set the target response rate, minimum sample size, and margin of error.
4. Identify any known biases or constraints that could affect the results.
