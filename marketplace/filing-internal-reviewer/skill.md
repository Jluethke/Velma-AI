# Filing Internal Reviewer

Conduct internal review of the draft filing for accuracy and completeness.

## Inputs

- `draft-filing`: Draft filing document
- `requirements-checklist`: Requirements checklist

## Outputs

- `review-comments`: Internal review comments and corrections
- `approved-filing`: Filing approved for submission

## Process

1. Review all facts and figures for accuracy
2. Check completeness against the requirements checklist
3. Obtain sign-off from subject matter experts and management
