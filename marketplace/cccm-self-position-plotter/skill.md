# Cccm Self Position Plotter

Plot your own position on each dimension. You cannot see a gap until you have located yourself, and most people mistake their own cultural defaults for simply the normal way to communicate. Naming your own position turns an invisible default into one point on a spectrum.

## Inputs

- `dimension-definitions`: The eight dimensions as spectrums

## Outputs

- `your-positions`: Your own position on each of the eight dimensions, with a note on how strongly you hold it
- `your-defaults-note`: The habits you treat as simply normal, which are in fact one cultural position among several

## Process

1. Place yourself on each dimension based on how you actually behave, not how you would like to.
2. Note where you hold a position strongly. A strong default is the one most likely to cause friction when the counterpart sits at the other pole.
3. Name the behaviors you consider simply normal: how blunt your feedback is, how you show respect to seniority, how you treat a start time.
4. Recognize these as a position, not a standard. The counterpart has their own normal, equally invisible to them.
