# System Adjustment Cycle

Adjust the system for the coming year based on the review, updating screenings, routines, and alert rules as risk changes.

## Inputs

- `annual-review-checklist`: The completed annual review
- `trend-tracker`: The year over year view of measures

## Outputs

- `system-adjustment-plan`: The changes to screenings, routines, and alert rules for the coming year
- `next-year-targets`: The specific measures or routines to focus on in the year ahead

## Process

1. Update the screening schedule for any new risk flags the year revealed, moving screenings earlier or adding them where trends warrant, confirmed with a clinician.
2. Adjust the routines to address the measures trending the wrong way, so the daily and weekly cadence targets the actual risk rather than a generic one.
3. Revise the alert rules where a measure has moved, so next year's thresholds reflect your current starting point rather than last year's.
4. Name a small number of focus targets for the year, since a system that tries to improve everything at once tends to hold nothing, and a few targets keep the effort real.
