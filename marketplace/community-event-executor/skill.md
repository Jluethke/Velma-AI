# Community Event Executor

Execute the event day and collect feedback afterward.

## Inputs

- `promotion-plan`: How the event was promoted
- `registration-count`: Expected attendance

## Outputs

- `event-record`: Actual attendance, budget spent, issues encountered, and photos or documentation
- `feedback-summary`: Attendee and volunteer feedback with scores and suggestions

## Process

1. Execute setup according to the logistics plan, briefing volunteers on their roles.
2. Run the event: manage the schedule, handle issues as they arise, welcome attendees.
3. Record actual attendance and capture photos or documentation.
4. After the event, send a feedback survey to attendees and volunteers.
5. Produce the event record: actual vs. budget, attendance, feedback summary, and lessons learned.
