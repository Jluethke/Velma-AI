# Divergence Resolver

Resolve the divergences: bring misaligned stakeholders to a shared understanding before work begins or continues.

## Inputs

- `alignment-matrix`: The side-by-side comparison with divergences highlighted
- `key-stakeholders`: The stakeholders involved

## Outputs

- `resolved-expectations`: The unified expectations: what the project will deliver, when, and how success is measured, agreed by all key stakeholders
- `unresolved-divergences`: Any divergences that could not be resolved, with the escalation plan (who makes the final call)

## Process

1. For each divergence, bring the disagreeing stakeholders together. Present both versions neutrally: 'Stakeholder A expects [X], Stakeholder B expects [Y]. Can we align on one?'
2. If the divergence is a misunderstanding (they meant the same thing in different words), clarify and document the shared understanding.
3. If the divergence is a genuine disagreement (they want different things), facilitate the trade-off conversation: 'We can do X or Y within the constraints. Which is more important?'
4. For divergences that cannot be resolved at this level, escalate to the sponsor or decision authority. Do not proceed with unresolved divergences; they will become delivery disputes.
