# Tiny Version Definition

Shrink the new habit to a version that takes under two minutes, because a tiny habit fires before resistance can build.

## Inputs

- `new-habit-goal`: The new habit you want to establish
- `reliable-anchor-habits`: The reliable existing habits

## Outputs

- `tiny-habit-version`: The new habit reduced to an action that takes under two minutes
- `two-minute-check`: Confirmation that the tiny version can be completed in under two minutes on any day

## Process

1. Take the full habit and find the smallest first action. Do thirty minutes of yoga becomes unroll the mat. Read every night becomes open the book and read one page.
2. Strip the tiny version down until it takes under two minutes. The point is to establish the firing of the habit, not the full effort.
3. Confirm the tiny version requires no setup, decision, or willpower on a bad day. If it still feels like work, shrink it again.
4. Resist adding the full effort yet. The tiny version is the entire habit for now, and trying to do more early is what breaks the chain.
