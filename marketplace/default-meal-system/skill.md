# Default Meal System

Design 5 default meals that you can prepare in under 20 minutes, that you enjoy, and that meet basic nutritional needs. These are your defaults, not your only meals.

## Inputs

- `food-log`: What you actually eat
- `eating-patterns`: Your patterns

## Outputs

- `default-meals`: 5 go-to meals: 2 breakfasts, 2 dinners, and 1 lunch that you enjoy, can prepare quickly, and meet protein/vegetable targets
- `grocery-template`: A standard grocery list for these 5 meals that you can reorder weekly with minimal thought

## Process

1. Choose 5 meals you already like (or close variations). Do not introduce entirely new foods. Willpower is limited; new foods require willpower. Familiar foods that are slightly healthier require almost none.
2. Each meal should have protein (palm-sized portion), vegetables (fill half the plate), and a starch or grain. This is not strict macronutrient counting; it is a simple visual template.
3. Each meal must be preparable in under 20 minutes. Meals that take 45 minutes get skipped for takeout on busy nights. Speed is a feature, not a compromise.
4. Build a standard grocery list for these 5 meals. Automate the grocery order if possible. Decision fatigue around 'what to eat' is the primary failure mode of nutrition systems.
