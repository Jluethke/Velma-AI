# Home Repair Budget

Build an annual home repair and maintenance budget based on home value, age, and systems condition.

## Inputs

- `home_value`: Estimated home value
- `systems_inventory`: Systems and condition
- `replacement_timeline`: Upcoming major replacements

## Outputs

- `annual_budget`: Recommended annual maintenance budget
- `reserve_fund_target`: Target emergency repair fund
- `major_expense_timeline`: Big-ticket items coming in next 5 years

## Process

1. Calculate 1-2% of home value baseline
2. Add known upcoming major replacements
3. Build reserve fund target for emergencies
4. Create monthly savings plan
5. Prioritize deferred maintenance
