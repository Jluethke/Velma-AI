# Treatment Goal Identifier

Identify short-term and long-term treatment goals.

## Inputs

- `clinical-summary`: Summary of clinical findings
- `severity-assessment`: Severity classification

## Outputs

- `short-term-goals`: Goals achievable within weeks to months
- `long-term-goals`: Goals for sustained health improvement

## Process

1. Define measurable short-term goals (symptom relief, stabilization).
2. Define long-term goals (disease management, functional recovery).
3. Align goals with patient preferences and values.
