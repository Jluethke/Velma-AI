# Procurement Tracker

Track purchase order delivery and quality.

## Inputs

- `purchase-order-draft`: Draft purchase order ready for approval
- `agreed-terms`: Negotiated terms including price, payment, and delivery

## Outputs

- `delivery-tracking-log`: Delivery status and inspection results per order
- `procurement-record`: Complete procurement record from requirements through delivery

## Process

1. Issue the purchase order and confirm with the supplier
2. Track the production and shipping status
3. Inspect deliveries against the quality specifications
4. Produce the procurement record with cost and quality summary
