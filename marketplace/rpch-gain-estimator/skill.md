# Rpch Gain Estimator

Estimate the revenue gain from the new mix.

## Inputs

- `target-mix`: The proposed activity mix
- `revenue-per-hour-table`: The ratios used to total the mix

## Outputs

- `projected-revenue`: Total revenue of the target mix
- `revenue-delta`: The gain over the current mix and its assumptions

## Process

1. Total the revenue of the target mix.
2. Compare it against the current mix revenue.
3. Report the gain and the assumptions behind it.
