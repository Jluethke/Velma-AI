# Discharge Readiness Assessor

Assess the patient's readiness for discharge.

## Inputs

- `patient-record`: Current clinical record
- `discharge-criteria`: Clinical criteria for safe discharge

## Outputs

- `readiness-status`: Ready, not ready, or conditional
- `barriers-list`: Any barriers to discharge that need resolution

## Process

1. Evaluate clinical stability against discharge criteria.
2. Assess functional status and self-care ability.
3. Identify social or logistical barriers to discharge.
