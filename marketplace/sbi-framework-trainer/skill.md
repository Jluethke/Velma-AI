# Sbi Framework Trainer

Train the feedback givers on the SBI framework: Situation (when and where), Behavior (what specifically happened), Impact (the measurable effect).

## Inputs

- `feedback-purpose`: The purpose and scope of the feedback system
- `context`: The operating context

## Outputs

- `sbi-guide`: The SBI framework guide: how to structure feedback as Situation-Behavior-Impact with examples for the specific context
- `common-mistakes`: The mistakes to avoid: vague feedback, personality feedback, feedback without impact, delayed feedback, public negative feedback

## Process

1. Teach the Situation: describe when and where the behavior occurred. 'In yesterday's team meeting' or 'On the customer call last Tuesday.' Specificity prevents the recipient from dismissing the feedback as general.
2. Teach the Behavior: describe what the person did, not what you think they intended. 'You interrupted the client twice during their explanation' not 'You were rude to the client.' Observable behavior, not inferred motivation.
3. Teach the Impact: describe the measurable effect. 'The client stopped sharing details and the meeting ended 15 minutes early without a decision' not 'It was bad.' Impact makes feedback actionable because it ties behavior to consequence.
4. Provide examples for the specific context. For a product feedback system, SBI becomes: Situation (the use case), Behavior (what the product did), Impact (the effect on the user's workflow).
