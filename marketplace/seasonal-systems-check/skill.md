# Seasonal Systems Check

Service and check home systems for the coming season: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and fire safety.

## Inputs

- `home_systems`: Major systems and their ages
- `upcoming_season`: Season to prepare for
- `last_service_dates`: When systems were last serviced

## Outputs

- `systems_checklist`: Systems to inspect or service
- `service_appointments`: Professional services to schedule
- `diy_maintenance`: Maintenance the homeowner can do

## Process

1. Schedule HVAC service for upcoming season
2. Check and replace HVAC filters
3. Test smoke and CO detectors
4. Inspect plumbing for freeze risk or leaks
5. Check sump pump and drainage systems
