# Deal Sample Selector

Select a representative sample of won and lost deals for analysis.

## Inputs

- `deal-history`: Historical deal records with outcomes
- `analysis-scope`: Time period and segments to analyze

## Outputs

- `deal-sample`: Selected deals for review
- `sample-criteria`: How the sample was chosen and what it represents

## Process

1. Define the time window and segments to include.
2. Select deals stratified by size, segment, and outcome.
3. Verify the sample is large enough for pattern detection.
4. Document the selection criteria.
