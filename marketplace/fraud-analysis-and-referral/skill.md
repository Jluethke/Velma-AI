# Fraud Analysis And Referral

Analyze the evidence and refer confirmed fraud for action.

## Inputs

- `evidence-package`: All collected evidence
- `witness-statements`: Recorded statements and testimony

## Outputs

- `fraud-determination`: Determination of whether fraud occurred with supporting analysis
- `fraud-investigation-record`: Complete investigation record including referral to SIU or law enforcement if applicable

## Process

1. Analyze the evidence for indicators of material misrepresentation or fabrication.
2. Compare the claimant's statements against the physical evidence and records.
3. Make the fraud determination with documented rationale.
4. Refer confirmed fraud to the special investigations unit or law enforcement and deny the claim.
