# Incident Effectiveness Assessment

Assess what went well and what did not during the incident response.

## Inputs

- `incident-narrative`: Documented narrative of the incident
- `key-decisions-log`: Key decisions and their context

## Outputs

- `went-well-list`: Actions and processes that worked effectively
- `improvement-areas`: Areas where the response could have been better

## Process

1. Identify actions that reduced impact or accelerated recovery
2. Identify delays, miscommunications, or ineffective actions
3. Assess the effectiveness of tools, processes, and communication channels used
4. Frame findings as systemic issues, not individual blame
