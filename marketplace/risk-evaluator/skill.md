# Risk Evaluator

Evaluate the risk against underwriting guidelines and appetite.

## Inputs

- `risk-profile`: Complete risk profile with loss history and exposures
- `third-party-reports`: External reports (credit, inspection, benchmarks)

## Outputs

- `risk-assessment`: Assessment of the risk with a recommended risk grade and any concerns
- `underwriting-decision`: Preliminary decision: quote, decline, or refer to senior underwriter

## Process

1. Compare the risk against the underwriting guidelines and appetite for the line of business.
2. Evaluate loss trends, industry hazards, and control adequacy.
3. Assign a risk grade based on the evaluation criteria.
4. Make a preliminary decision and document the rationale.
