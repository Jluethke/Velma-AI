# Farm Financing Evaluator

Evaluate financing options for operating and capital needs.

## Inputs

- `financing-needs`: Loan amounts and timing
- `farm-financials`: Balance sheet and income history

## Outputs

- `financing-options`: Available loan products and terms
- `financing-recommendation`: Recommended financing structure

## Process

1. Identify operating loan options (FSA, commercial bank, Farm Credit).
2. Compare interest rates, terms, and flexibility.
3. Assess equipment financing for capital needs.
4. Recommend the financing structure that minimizes cost.
