# Change Request Submitter

Submit a change request with impact and risk assessment.

## Inputs

- `change-description`: What is being changed and why
- `affected-systems`: Systems and services affected by the change

## Outputs

- `change-request`: Completed change request form
- `impact-assessment`: Impact and risk rating for the change

## Process

1. Describe the change, its purpose, and the expected outcome.
2. Identify all affected systems, services, and users.
3. Assess the risk (likelihood of failure times severity of impact).
4. Document the rollback procedure.
