# Blue Team Debrief Facilitator

Conduct a collaborative debrief between the red and blue teams.

## Inputs

- `findings-list`: Vulnerabilities and weaknesses exploited
- `detection-timeline`: When blue team detected activity
- `operation-log`: Timestamped action log

## Outputs

- `debrief-notes`: Notes from the joint debrief session
- `improvement-recommendations`: Agreed-upon improvements for detection and response

## Process

1. Walk through the attack timeline with the blue team
2. Compare red team actions with blue team alerts and responses
3. Identify detection gaps and response delays
4. Agree on prioritized improvements for detection, prevention, and response
