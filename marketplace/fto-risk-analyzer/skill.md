# Fto Risk Analyzer

Identify freedom-to-operate risks from third-party IP.

## Inputs

- `ip-inventory`: IP inventory
- `strength-assessment`: IP strength assessment

## Outputs

- `fto-risks`: Third-party patents or IP that may be infringed
- `fto-mitigation`: Possible mitigation strategies for each risk

## Process

1. Search for third-party patents in the relevant technology areas
2. Analyze claim coverage against the target's products and services
3. Propose design-around, licensing, or invalidity strategies
