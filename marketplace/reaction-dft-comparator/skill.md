# Reaction Dft Comparator

Compare experimental mechanistic findings with DFT computational results for transition states and intermediates.

## Inputs

- `energy-profile`: Experimental free energy diagram
- `dft-calculations`: Computed structures, energies, and vibrational frequencies for intermediates and transition states

## Outputs

- `experiment-theory-comparison`: Side-by-side comparison of experimental and computed barriers and geometries
- `agreement-assessment`: Quantitative agreement metrics and discrepancies noted

## Process

1. Align computational and experimental species by structure correspondence
2. Compare computed barriers with experimental activation parameters
3. Assess whether computed transition state geometries are consistent with observed selectivity
4. Note discrepancies and suggest whether the computational level of theory is adequate
