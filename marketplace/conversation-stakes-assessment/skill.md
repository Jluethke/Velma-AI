# Conversation Stakes Assessment

Assess what is at stake for both sides before the conversation. If you do not know what the other person stands to lose, you cannot deliver the message in a way they can receive.

## Inputs

- `situation-description`: What happened and what needs to be addressed
- `relationship-context`: Your relationship to the other person (boss, partner, client, friend, direct report)

## Outputs

- `your-stakes`: What you stand to lose or gain from this conversation
- `their-stakes`: What the other person stands to lose or gain
- `conversation-goal`: The specific outcome you need from this conversation, stated in one sentence

## Process

1. Write down what you stand to lose if this conversation goes badly (the relationship, a project, credibility, peace of mind).
2. Write down what the other person stands to lose (their job, their self-image, a project they own, the relationship with you).
3. State the conversation goal in one sentence. 'I need X to change by Y date' or 'I need to communicate Z and preserve the working relationship.'
4. If you cannot state the goal in one sentence, you are not ready for the conversation.
