# Data Owner Trainer

Train data owners on classification criteria and their responsibilities.

## Inputs

- `classified-inventory`: Inventory showing what each owner is responsible for
- `handling-rules`: Rules each owner must follow

## Outputs

- `training-records`: Records of completed training per owner
- `trained-owner-list`: List of owners who completed training

## Process

1. Identify all data owners from the inventory
2. Deliver training on the classification scheme and their responsibilities
3. Assess understanding through knowledge checks
4. Record completion and schedule refresher training
