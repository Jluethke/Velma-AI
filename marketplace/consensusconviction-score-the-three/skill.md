# Consensusconviction Score The Three

Combine the three tests into one reading of whether the decision leans consensus or conviction.

## Inputs

- `downside-score`: How limited the downside is.
- `downside-notes`: The worst realistic outcome of the decision.

## Outputs

- `combined-score`: The three scores brought together into one view.
- `score-pattern`: Whether the scores agree or conflict.

## Process

1. Bring the three scores together into one view.
2. Mark whether all three point the same way or conflict.
3. Name the pattern the scores make.
