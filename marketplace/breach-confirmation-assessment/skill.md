# Breach Confirmation Assessment

Confirm the data breach and assess the types and volume of data exposed.

## Inputs

- `breach-indicators`: Initial indicators that a data breach occurred
- `system-context`: Systems involved and their data classifications

## Outputs

- `breach-confirmation`: Confirmed breach with data types and exposure method
- `data-inventory`: Inventory of exposed data types, volumes, and sensitivity levels

## Process

1. Investigate the indicators to confirm unauthorized data access or exfiltration
2. Identify all data types exposed (PII, PHI, financial, credentials, trade secrets)
3. Estimate the volume of records and number of affected individuals
4. Determine the method of exposure (exfiltration, misconfiguration, lost device)
