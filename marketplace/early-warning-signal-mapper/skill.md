# Early Warning Signal Mapper

For each scenario, identify the early warning signals that would tell you it is materializing.

## Inputs

- `scenario-matrix`: The four named scenarios
- `scenario-narratives`: The narrative descriptions

## Outputs

- `signal-map`: For each scenario: 3-5 observable, measurable signals that would indicate this scenario is becoming reality
- `signal-monitoring-plan`: How to track each signal: the data source, the measurement, and the threshold that triggers action

## Process

1. For each scenario, ask: what would you see in the next 6 months if this future were unfolding? Look for leading indicators, not lagging ones.
2. Make each signal observable and measurable: not 'the market shifts' but 'quarterly revenue from the enterprise segment declines by more than 15% for two consecutive quarters.'
3. Identify which signals differentiate between scenarios. A signal that appears in all four scenarios is not useful for planning.
4. Define the monitoring plan: where do you get the data, how often do you check, and what threshold triggers a reassessment?
