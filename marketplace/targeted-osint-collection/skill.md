# Targeted Osint Collection

Perform targeted open-source intelligence gathering on employees and infrastructure for the authorized engagement.

## Inputs

- `red-team-scope`: Authorized scope, objectives, and rules of engagement
- `target-organization`: Organization name and known domains

## Outputs

- `employee-profiles`: Key personnel identified with roles, email formats, and public information
- `infrastructure-intel`: External infrastructure, cloud services, and technology stack identified
- `social-media-findings`: Relevant information from social media profiles and posts

## Process

1. Map the organization's email format and enumerate employee names from LinkedIn and public directories
2. Identify technology stack from job postings, GitHub repositories, and DNS records
3. Collect publicly available information about key personnel and their roles
4. Identify potential pretexting scenarios based on gathered intelligence
