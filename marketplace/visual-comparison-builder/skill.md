# Visual Comparison Builder

Create a visual that makes the cost comparison impossible to miss: the status quo bar towering over the solution bar.

## Inputs

- `inverted-proposal`: The structured proposal
- `status-quo-cost`: The current cost

## Outputs

- `comparison-visual`: A bar chart or side-by-side table showing the annual status quo cost, your annual price, and the net savings - designed to make the gap immediately obvious
- `highlight-annotations`: Annotations on the visual that call out the key numbers

## Process

1. Create a bar chart with two bars: status quo annual cost (tall) and your annual price (short). The gap between them is the annual savings.
2. Use actual dollar amounts, not percentages. '$87,000 vs $12,000' is more impactful than '86% savings.'
3. Include a time dimension: 'Over 3 years, the status quo costs $261,000 while our solution costs $36,000.' Multi-year framing amplifies the gap.
4. This visual should be the most memorable element of the proposal. Decisions are made visually, and this comparison image is what the champion will remember and describe to the CFO.
