# Regret Minimized Decision

Make the decision that minimizes lifetime regret. State the decision clearly, along with the specific regret you are choosing to accept (because every choice involves accepting some regret) and the regret you are avoiding.

## Inputs

- `regret-verdict`: Which regret is worse
- `adjusted-regret-comparison`: The adjusted comparison

## Outputs

- `decision`: The regret-minimized decision: what to do and why
- `accepted-regret`: The specific regret you are choosing to accept, stated explicitly so you can make peace with it

## Process

1. Choose the option that minimizes the larger regret.
2. State the regret you are accepting. Every decision involves accepting some regret; naming it reduces its power.
3. If you chose action: define the first step and the timeline. Do not let the decision sit unexecuted.
4. If you chose inaction: write down why, specifically. If you revisit this decision later, you want to know what you were thinking.
