# Blind Spot Integrator

Turn the audience's questions into your next round of study.

## Inputs

- `teaching-record`: A record of how the teaching went
- `audience-questions`: The questions the audience asked

## Outputs

- `blind-spot-list`: The blind spots the questions revealed
- `next-study-plan`: The plan for the next study and teaching cycle

## Process

1. Group the questions into the blind spots they reveal.
2. Prioritize the blind spots by how central they are to the topic.
3. Plan the next study and teaching cycle around them.
4. Decide what to teach again once the blind spots close.
