# Awareness Campaign Executor

Execute the campaign across channels per the media timeline.

## Inputs

- `campaign-assets`: Approved assets
- `media-timeline`: Activation schedule

## Outputs

- `execution-log`: What ran, when, and where
- `mid-campaign-metrics`: Reach, impressions, and engagement during the campaign

## Process

1. Launch each channel per the timeline
2. Monitor delivery and adjust pacing
3. Collect mid-campaign metrics
