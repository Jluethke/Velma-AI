# Campaign Goal Definer

Define the campaign goal, success metrics, and target audience segment.

## Inputs

- `campaign-objective`: What the campaign should accomplish
- `audience-data`: Available audience segments and their attributes

## Outputs

- `campaign-brief`: Goal, KPIs, and audience definition
- `segment-criteria`: Targeting rules for the audience segment

## Process

1. State the campaign goal in measurable terms
2. Define the primary and secondary KPIs
3. Select the audience segment and document the targeting criteria
