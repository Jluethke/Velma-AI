# Review Conversation Conductor

Guide managers through delivering the performance review conversation.

## Inputs

- `calibrated-ratings`: Final ratings
- `peer-feedback`: Aggregated feedback
- `self-assessments`: Self-assessments

## Outputs

- `delivered-reviews`: Record of completed review conversations
- `employee-reactions`: Employee responses and discussion notes

## Process

1. Prepare the review document with ratings, evidence, and development areas
2. Coach managers on delivering constructive feedback
3. Conduct the review conversations
4. Document the discussion outcomes
