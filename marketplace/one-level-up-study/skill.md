# One Level Up Study

Study someone who is one level above you (not a master, just slightly better) and identify the specific thing they do differently.

## Inputs

- `skill-description`: The skill
- `avoided-sub-skill`: The sub-skill most likely holding you back

## Outputs

- `comparison-target`: A specific person who is one level above you in this skill, with evidence of their performance
- `specific-difference`: The 1-2 specific, observable things they do differently from you that account for the performance gap

## Process

1. Find someone who is one level above you, not a world expert. The gap between you and someone one level up is small enough to analyze. The gap between you and a master is too large to be useful.
2. Observe them performing the skill. Watch recordings if available. Focus on the specific moments where their performance diverges from yours.
3. Identify 1-2 specific, observable differences. Not vague ('they are more confident') but specific ('they pause for 2 seconds before answering tough questions' or 'they check edge cases before writing the main logic').
4. The specific difference is your next practice target. You now know exactly what to change, not just 'get better.'
