# Breach Scope Assessment

Assess the scope and severity of the data breach.

## Inputs

- `incident-report`: The initial incident report describing the breach
- `affected-systems`: Systems involved in the breach

## Outputs

- `breach-assessment`: Assessment of the breach (data types, volume, number of individuals, risk of harm)
- `notification-determination`: Whether regulatory and individual notification is required under applicable law

## Process

1. Determine what personal data was compromised (categories, sensitivity, volume).
2. Estimate the number of affected individuals and their jurisdictions.
3. Assess the risk of harm to individuals (identity theft, financial loss, discrimination, reputational damage).
4. Determine whether regulatory notification is required (risk threshold, mandatory reporting) and the applicable deadlines.
