# Matter Resolution Recorder

Record the resolution and outcome of the matter.

## Inputs

- `matter-record`: The matter file
- `document-index`: Complete document index

## Outputs

- `resolution-record`: Record of the outcome including terms, amounts, and dates
- `lessons-learned`: Notes on what went well and what to improve for future matters

## Process

1. Document the resolution: outcome type (settled, won, lost, withdrawn, expired), terms, and key dates.
2. Record any financial outcome (settlement amount, judgment, costs, fees recovered).
3. Note lessons learned and any follow-up obligations.
4. Update the matter status to resolved.
