# Change Agreement Negotiator

Agree on one specific change, not a vague promise to do better. The change should be observable, so that in a week both of you can tell whether it happened.

## Inputs

- `elicitation-questions`: The questions used to surface their view
- `listening-plan`: The listening plan

## Outputs

- `agreed-change`: A single observable change the person will make, stated in terms of what they will do differently
- `change-owner`: Who owns the change and what support they need from you to make it

## Process

1. Narrow to a single specific change rather than a list, so it is achievable.
2. State the change in observable terms: what the person will do differently next time, in what situation.
3. Confirm the person agrees it is reasonable and owns it, rather than accepting it only to end the conversation.
4. Name any support they need from you to make the change, and commit to providing it.
