# Privacy Policy Compiler

Compile and review the final privacy policy against applicable regulations.

## Inputs

- `purpose-register`: Purpose register
- `third-party-disclosures`: Third-party disclosures
- `rights-section`: User rights section

## Outputs

- `privacy-policy-draft`: Complete privacy policy document
- `regulatory-checklist`: Compliance checklist against GDPR, CCPA, and other regulations

## Process

1. Assemble all sections into the complete privacy policy
2. Review against GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable regulations
3. Verify readability and accessibility of the language
