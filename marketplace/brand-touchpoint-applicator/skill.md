# Brand Touchpoint Applicator

Apply the brand identity to key touchpoints.

## Inputs

- `brand-guidelines`: Brand rules and specifications
- `asset-library`: Logo files and design assets

## Outputs

- `branded-templates`: Templates for business cards, letterhead, social, and web
- `brand-identity-package`: Complete deliverable with all assets and guidelines

## Process

1. Design business card and stationery templates
2. Create social media profile and post templates
3. Design the website header, footer, and key page layouts
4. Package all assets, templates, and guidelines for handoff
