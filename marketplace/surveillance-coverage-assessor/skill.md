# Surveillance Coverage Assessor

Assess surveillance camera coverage, monitoring, and recording integrity.

## Inputs

- `facility-description`: Facility layout
- `entry-point-inventory`: Entry point locations to verify camera coverage

## Outputs

- `camera-coverage-map`: Map showing camera locations, angles, and blind spots
- `monitoring-effectiveness`: Assessment of whether cameras are actively monitored and recordings are protected

## Process

1. Map all camera locations and their fields of view
2. Identify blind spots: areas without camera coverage, especially around entry points and sensitive areas
3. Verify that recordings are stored with integrity protections and adequate retention
4. Test whether the security operations center actively monitors feeds or only reviews after incidents
