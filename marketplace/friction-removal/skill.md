# Friction Removal

Remove the friction at each transition so the next yes costs the buyer as little effort as possible.

## Inputs

- `sequenced-offers`: The ordered offers
- `transition-triggers`: Signals a buyer is ready to advance

## Outputs

- `friction-fixes`: The specific friction removed at each transition
- `transition-copy`: The words that carry the buyer from one offer to the next

## Process

1. At each transition, list what the buyer must do, decide, or wait for, and cut whatever is not essential.
2. Pre-fill, pre-schedule, or pre-decide whatever you can on the buyer's behalf.
3. Write the transition copy so the next step sounds like the obvious continuation of the last yes.
4. Remove any new decision that forces the buyer to reconsider a yes they already gave.
