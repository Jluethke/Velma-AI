# Crisis Communication Drill

Run a crisis communication drill: pick a scenario, execute the communication plan, and identify the gaps.

## Inputs

- `crisis-templates`: The pre-written templates
- `crisis-scenarios`: The scenarios to drill

## Outputs

- `drill-results`: What worked (templates were found quickly, communication went out on time), what failed (wrong contact list, template did not fit the scenario, approval took too long), and the fixes
- `updated-playbook`: The revised crisis communication playbook incorporating the drill findings

## Process

1. Pick a scenario at random. Announce it to the team. Start the clock.
2. Execute the communication plan: find the template, fill in the blanks, get approval from the designated approver, and send to the designated audience. Measure the time from scenario announcement to first communication sent.
3. After the drill, review: was the template found quickly? Did it fit the scenario? Was the contact list current? Did the approval process cause delay? Was the communication clear and accurate?
4. Update the playbook with the findings. Schedule the next drill (semi-annually). Store the playbook where the team can find it during an actual crisis.
