# Critical Role Identifier

Identify the critical roles that need succession plans.

## Inputs

- `org-structure`: Organization structure and role catalog
- `business-strategy`: Strategic priorities and growth plans

## Outputs

- `critical-roles`: Roles identified as critical for succession planning
- `risk-assessment`: Vacancy risk assessment for each critical role

## Process

1. Identify roles where vacancy would significantly impact operations
2. Assess the difficulty of replacing each role externally
3. Consider the retirement timeline of current incumbents
4. Prioritize by combined risk and impact
