# Referral Ask Design

Write a specific ask tied to a specific problem and a specific kind of person, not a vague request to spread the word.

## Inputs

- `peak-moments`: The high-satisfaction points
- `trigger-events`: The events that mark them

## Outputs

- `referral-ask`: The exact wording of the ask, naming a specific problem and person
- `ask-timing`: When and how to deliver the ask relative to the trigger event

## Process

1. Write the ask as a single, concrete question: who is one person you know dealing with this specific problem?
2. Avoid the vague ask to tell your friends, since a vague ask returns nothing.
3. Tie the ask to the value the customer just felt, so it reads as a natural extension of their win.
4. Decide the moment and the medium, such as right after a success milestone in a short message.
