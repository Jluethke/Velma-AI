# Snm Acknowledgment Writer

Write the acknowledgment that opens the message. Acknowledging the request shows you took it seriously and respects the person before you decline. A no that opens with the refusal, skipping acknowledgment, reads as dismissive even when it is not.

## Inputs

- `request-summary`: The request to acknowledge accurately

## Outputs

- `acknowledgment-line`: An opening that shows you understood the request and took it seriously
- `tone-note`: The tone to carry through, matched to the relationship and the ask

## Process

1. Open by naming the request accurately, so the person sees you understood what they asked.
2. Show it mattered without implying a yes. 'Thank you for thinking of me for this' acknowledges without committing.
3. Keep the acknowledgment short. A long preamble reads as softening the ground for a no the person can already feel coming.
4. Set the tone to fit the relationship: warmer for a friend, plainer for a transactional ask. The tone carries through the whole message.
