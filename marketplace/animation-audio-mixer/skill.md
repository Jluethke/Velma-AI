# Animation Audio Mixer

Record and mix the final audio for the animation.

## Inputs

- `composited-scenes`: Final animation video
- `script-or-narration`: Script for final voice recording

## Outputs

- `finished-animation`: Complete animation with final audio
- `audio-mix-report`: Audio specifications and deliverable formats

## Process

1. Record the final voiceover and dialogue
2. Design and place sound effects for each scene
3. Mix music, dialogue, and effects to proper levels
4. Export the finished animation in all required formats
