# Freelance Proposal Writer

Write a project proposal based on discovery findings.

## Inputs

- `discovery-notes`: Notes from the discovery call
- `project-scope-draft`: Draft scope from discovery

## Outputs

- `project-proposal`: The complete proposal including scope, approach, timeline, deliverables, pricing, and terms
- `pricing-breakdown`: Itemized pricing by deliverable or phase

## Process

1. Write the problem statement and objectives in the client's language, reflecting what they said in discovery.
2. Define the approach, methodology, and phases of work.
3. List the deliverables with acceptance criteria for each.
4. Set the timeline with milestones and the pricing structure (fixed, hourly, milestone-based).
5. Include the terms: payment schedule, revision policy, cancellation clause, IP ownership.
