# Feedback Action Converter

Convert feedback into action. This is where most feedback systems fail. Feedback that is collected but not acted on trains people to ignore the system.

## Inputs

- `collection-design`: The feedback collection mechanism
- `feedback-purpose`: The primary purpose of the system

## Outputs

- `action-conversion-process`: How feedback is turned into specific actions: behavior change commitments with deadlines, skill development plans, product changes, or process improvements
- `feedback-effectiveness-metrics`: How to measure whether the feedback system is working: behavior change rate, action completion rate, and recipient satisfaction with feedback quality

## Process

1. For each piece of feedback, the recipient (or the responsible party) must respond with an action: 'I will [specific behavior change] starting [date]' or 'We will [product change] by [date]' or 'Noted, no action needed because [reason].'
2. Track the actions: did the behavior change? Did the product change ship? If actions are not tracked, the system degrades to a suggestion box.
3. Measure the system's effectiveness: what percentage of feedback resulted in a measurable action? What percentage of actions were completed? What is the recipient's rating of feedback quality (specific and actionable vs. vague and unhelpful)?
4. Review the system quarterly: is the feedback getting more specific over time (SBI training is working)? Are actions being completed (accountability is working)? Are the desired outcomes changing (the system is producing results)?
