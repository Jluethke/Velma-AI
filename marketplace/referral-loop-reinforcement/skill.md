# Referral Loop Reinforcement

Close the loop so the customers who refer well keep referring, and the program compounds.

## Inputs

- `source-ledger`: Record of referrals and their sources
- `close-rate-by-source`: Close rate and value by source

## Outputs

- `reinforcement-plan`: How to keep strong referrers engaged and asking again
- `referrer-recognition`: How referrers are recognized in a way that fits your relationship with them

## Process

1. Identify the customers who refer often and whose referrals close, and treat them as a distinct group.
2. Design a way to recognize them that fits the relationship, since the wrong reward can cheapen a genuine referral.
3. Set a cadence to re-engage strong referrers at their next peak moment, not at random.
4. Review the ledger regularly and retire the parts of the ask that are not producing referrals.
