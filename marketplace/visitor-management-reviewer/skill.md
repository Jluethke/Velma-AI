# Visitor Management Reviewer

Review visitor management procedures for security gaps.

## Inputs

- `access-control-policies`: Visitor management policies
- `tailgating-results`: Tailgating results to correlate with visitor controls

## Outputs

- `visitor-management-findings`: Findings from visitor management review
- `physical-security-report`: Complete physical security assessment report

## Process

1. Review the visitor registration and badge issuance process
2. Test whether visitor badges grant access beyond intended areas
3. Check whether visitors are escorted as required by policy
4. Compile all findings into the physical security assessment report with risk ratings and remediation
