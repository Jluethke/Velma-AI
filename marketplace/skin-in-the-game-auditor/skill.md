# Skin In The Game Auditor

Audit whether the decision-makers bear the consequences of their decisions. Systems where decision-makers are insulated from consequences become fragile because bad decisions are not corrected.

## Inputs

- `system-description`: The system context
- `fragility-assessment`: The fragility assessment

## Outputs

- `accountability-map`: For each major decision area: who decides, who bears the consequences, and whether there is alignment between the two
- `accountability-fixes`: Where decision-maker and consequence-bearer are different: the structural change to align them

## Process

1. For each major decision area (spending, hiring, product direction, risk acceptance, health choices, investment), identify who makes the decision and who bears the consequence if it goes wrong.
2. Where the decision-maker and consequence-bearer are the same person, the system self-corrects: bad decisions cause pain, which corrects future decisions.
3. Where they are different, the system is fragile: the decision-maker has no incentive to avoid bad decisions. Examples: a manager who hires poorly but does not work with the hire. An advisor who recommends risky investments but does not invest their own money.
4. Design the structural fix: put the decision-maker's own resources at stake, or give the consequence-bearer veto power over the decision.
