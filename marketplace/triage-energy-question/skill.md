# Triage Energy Question

Ask the first triage question of each relationship: on balance, does this person give energy or drain it? This is the most immediate signal and the easiest to read honestly after a life change, when your energy is often limited and you can feel clearly which interactions restore you and which deplete you.

## Inputs

- `triage-inventory`: The relationships to triage
- `change-impact-notes`: How the change has shifted each relationship

## Outputs

- `energy-assessments`: Each relationship marked as net energy-giving, neutral, or net energy-draining, over a fair sample of interactions
- `energy-flagged-relationships`: The clearly draining relationships flagged for closer examination in the later questions

## Process

1. For each relationship, recall how you feel after your typical interactions, not your best or worst single memory. Energized and lighter, neutral, or depleted and heavier is the reading you want.
2. Judge over a fair sample. Everyone has an off day, and a good relationship going through a hard stretch is not a draining one. Look at the pattern across many interactions, not a recent rough patch.
3. Separate draining from demanding. A relationship that asks a lot but that you choose to pour into (a child, a friend in crisis) can be demanding without being draining, because the giving is meaningful. Draining is when you leave consistently depleted with nothing gained.
4. Flag the consistently draining relationships. A drain is not an automatic cut, since the later questions may reveal real mutual investment or alignment, but it is the signal that this relationship needs a conscious decision rather than default continuation.
