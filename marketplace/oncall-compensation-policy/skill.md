# Oncall Compensation Policy

Establish compensation or time-off policies for on-call duty including pay differentials and recovery time.

## Inputs

- `rotation-schedule`: The on-call rotation calendar
- `handoff-checklist`: Handoff procedures for context

## Outputs

- `compensation-policy`: The on-call compensation and time-off policy
- `oncall-program-document`: Complete on-call program documentation

## Process

1. Research industry compensation standards for on-call work
2. Define pay differentials or time-off-in-lieu policies
3. Document the policy and obtain management approval
4. Compile the full on-call program document
