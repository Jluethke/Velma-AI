# Bess Horizon Comparer

Compare the payback period against the planning horizon.

## Inputs

- `payback-months`: The months to recover the cost
- `planning-horizon`: The period the decision must fit inside

## Outputs

- `horizon-verdict`: Whether the payback falls inside or outside the horizon
- `margin-of-safety`: The months of margin or overrun

## Process

1. Compare the payback months against the horizon.
2. Mark the investment as inside or outside the horizon.
3. Compute the months of margin or overrun.
