# Problem Owner Identification

Identify who personally owns the pain your product removes, since a champion without the pain will not fight for you.

## Inputs

- `org-map`: The map of people in the deal
- `influence-lines`: Who influences whom

## Outputs

- `problem-owners`: The people who feel the problem directly
- `pain-intensity`: How sharply each one feels it and why

## Process

1. For each person, ask whether the problem shows up in their own goals, metrics, or daily work.
2. Separate the people who merely agree the problem exists from the people it actually hurts.
3. Rate how intensely each problem owner feels the pain and what event made it real for them.
4. Note who gains the most visible win if the problem is solved, since that person has a reason to push.
