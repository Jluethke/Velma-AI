# Influencer Negotiator

Negotiate terms and deliverables with selected influencers.

## Inputs

- `influencer-shortlist`: The vetted influencer shortlist
- `campaign-budget`: The available budget

## Outputs

- `partnership-agreements`: Signed or agreed terms for each influencer (deliverables, timeline, compensation, usage rights)
- `content-briefs`: Individual content briefs for each influencer

## Process

1. Contact each influencer on the shortlist with the campaign brief.
2. Negotiate deliverables (number of posts, formats, platforms), compensation, and timeline.
3. Agree on content usage rights and exclusivity terms.
4. Finalize the partnership agreement and send individual content briefs.
