# Incident Legal Disclosure Coordination

Coordinate with legal counsel on disclosure obligations and timing.

## Inputs

- `customer-notification`: Draft customer communications
- `stakeholder-list`: All identified stakeholders

## Outputs

- `legal-approved-comms`: Communications approved by legal for release
- `disclosure-timeline`: Approved timeline for each disclosure

## Process

1. Review all draft communications with legal counsel
2. Confirm regulatory disclosure obligations and deadlines
3. Get legal approval on communication content and timing
4. Document the approved disclosure timeline
