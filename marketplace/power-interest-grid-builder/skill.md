# Power Interest Grid Builder

Place each stakeholder on the power-interest grid: high power/high interest (manage closely), high power/low interest (keep satisfied), low power/high interest (keep informed), low power/low interest (monitor).

## Inputs

- `stakeholder-list`: The full stakeholder list with roles and concerns
- `initiative-description`: The initiative context

## Outputs

- `power-interest-grid`: Each stakeholder placed in one of four quadrants with their power score (1-5) and interest score (1-5)
- `quadrant-strategies`: The communication strategy for each quadrant: frequency, channel, message depth, and escalation protocol

## Process

1. Rate each stakeholder's power (1-5): their ability to help or block the initiative. A senior executive with budget authority is a 5. A downstream user with no decision-making role is a 1.
2. Rate each stakeholder's interest (1-5): how much they care about the initiative's outcome. Someone whose daily work changes is a 5. Someone who is tangentially aware is a 1.
3. Place each stakeholder in the grid. Manage closely (high/high): frequent two-way communication, involve in decisions. Keep satisfied (high/low): periodic updates, no surprises. Keep informed (low/high): regular updates, listen to concerns. Monitor (low/low): periodic summaries.
4. Check for misalignment: a stakeholder you are treating as low-interest who is actually high-interest will feel ignored. A low-power stakeholder you are treating as high-power will consume disproportionate time.
