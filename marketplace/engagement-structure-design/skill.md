# Engagement Structure Design

Define the engagement structure and billing model.

## Inputs

- `client-objectives`: What the client wants to achieve
- `engagement-constraints`: Budget, timeline, and resource constraints

## Outputs

- `engagement-model`: The engagement structure (fixed-fee, T&M, retainer, milestone-based) with rationale
- `resource-plan`: Who will work on the engagement, their roles, and allocation

## Process

1. Assess the client's objectives, risk tolerance, and budget certainty to select the billing model.
2. For fixed-fee: define the deliverables and price. For T&M: set the rate card and estimate. For retainer: set the monthly allocation. For milestone: define the phases and payments.
3. Identify the team members, their roles, and their time allocation.
4. Document the rationale for the chosen structure.
