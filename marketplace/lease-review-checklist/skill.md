# Lease Review Checklist

Review a lease for tenant-unfriendly clauses, illegal provisions, and important deadlines.

## Inputs

- `lease_document`: Lease text or key terms
- `state_of_residence`: State for tenant law reference

## Outputs

- `clause_analysis`: Review of each major clause
- `illegal_provisions`: Clauses that may be unenforceable
- `key_dates`: Important deadlines and notice periods

## Process

1. Review rent, deposit, and fee terms
2. Check lease duration and renewal terms
3. Identify maintenance responsibility clauses
4. Flag potentially illegal provisions
5. Note all notice period requirements
