# Elder Care Needs Assessment

Assess care needs across daily living, medical, cognitive, social, and safety dimensions.

## Inputs

- `current_abilities`: What the person can do independently
- `medical_conditions`: Chronic conditions and medications
- `cognitive_status`: Memory and decision-making ability
- `social_situation`: Current living and social situation

## Outputs

- `care_level_needed`: Independent, assisted, memory care, or skilled nursing
- `specific_needs`: Detailed list of care requirements
- `urgency_assessment`: How quickly a move is needed

## Process

1. Assess activities of daily living (ADLs)
2. Evaluate instrumental ADLs
3. Review medical and medication management needs
4. Assess cognitive function level
5. Determine safety risks in current situation
