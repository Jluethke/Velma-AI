# Cover Letter Writer Science

Draft the cover letter to the journal editor explaining the significance of the work and why it fits the journal's scope.

## Inputs

- `manuscript-draft`: The near-final manuscript
- `target-journal-scope`: The journal's aims and scope statement

## Outputs

- `cover-letter`: Cover letter addressed to the editor
- `suggested-reviewers`: List of 3-5 suggested peer reviewers with justification

## Process

1. State the main finding in one sentence.
2. Explain why this finding fits the journal's scope and audience.
3. Note any conflicts of interest or prior preprint posting.
4. Suggest qualified reviewers who are not collaborators.
