# Neuro Research Question Refiner

Refine a neuroscience research question into a testable hypothesis with specified brain regions, cognitive processes, and expected contrasts.

## Inputs

- `research-question`: The broad neuroscience question to investigate
- `prior-findings`: Relevant imaging and behavioral results from the literature

## Outputs

- `refined-hypothesis`: Testable hypothesis with specific brain regions, direction of effect, and contrast of interest
- `roi-list`: A priori regions of interest with anatomical definitions
- `cognitive-model`: Diagram of the cognitive process and where the manipulation intervenes

## Process

1. Narrow the question to a specific cognitive process and its neural substrate
2. State the hypothesis in terms of expected activation or connectivity changes
3. Define regions of interest from anatomical atlases or prior coordinates
4. Diagram the cognitive model showing where the experimental manipulation acts
