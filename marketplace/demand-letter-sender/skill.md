# Demand Letter Sender

Prepare and send the subrogation demand letter to the liable party.

## Inputs

- `subrogation-candidates`: Selected claims for subrogation pursuit
- `recovery-estimates`: Estimated recovery amounts

## Outputs

- `demand-letters`: Sent demand letters with claimed amounts and supporting documentation
- `response-deadlines`: Deadlines for each demand response

## Process

1. Draft the demand letter with the claim facts, liability basis, and demanded amount.
2. Attach supporting documentation (paid amounts, investigation findings, evidence of liability).
3. Send the demand to the liable party or their insurer.
4. Set the response deadline and calendar the follow-up date.
