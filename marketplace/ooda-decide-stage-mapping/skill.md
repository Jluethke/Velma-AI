# Ooda Decide Stage Mapping

Map the Decide stage: how an understanding turns into a chosen action, including every approval the decision must pass through. Decision rights and sign-offs are common hidden delays here.

## Inputs

- `loop-context`: The situation framed as a repeating decision loop
- `orient-map`: How information is turned into meaning and options

## Outputs

- `decide-map`: How decisions get made and who must approve them
- `decide-latency`: The time from understanding to a committed choice

## Process

1. Trace how a choice gets made: who decides, who must approve, and what evidence they require.
2. Measure the delay from having understanding to a committed decision.
3. Flag every approval step and ask whether it adds judgment or only adds delay.
4. Record the decide latency as a number.
