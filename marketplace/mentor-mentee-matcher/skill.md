# Mentor Mentee Matcher

Match mentors with mentees based on research interests, methodology, and compatibility.

## Inputs

- `participant-pool`: Available mentors and mentees with research profiles
- `mentoring-goals`: Defined goals for the mentoring program

## Outputs

- `matching-results`: Proposed mentor-mentee pairs with rationale
- `backup-matches`: Alternative pairings if primary matches decline

## Process

1. Collect research interest and methodology profiles from all participants
2. Score compatibility on research overlap, methodology, and availability
3. Propose primary and backup matches
4. Allow participants to review and confirm their matches
