# Sales Ammunition Creation

Turn the solution matches into usable sales materials: talk tracks, objection handlers, and competitive battle cards.

## Inputs

- `solution-matches`: Each complaint matched to your solution
- `review-corpus`: The raw reviews for quoting

## Outputs

- `talk-tracks`: For each top complaint theme: a 30-second talk track that names the problem (in the reviewer's words) and your solution (with evidence)
- `battle-cards`: One-page competitive battle cards for each competitor showing their top complaints and your corresponding strengths

## Process

1. Write a talk track for each top theme: 'One thing we hear from people switching from [competitor] is [exact reviewer quote]. We built [specific feature] to address exactly that. Here is how it works: [30-second demo or explanation].'
2. The talk track uses the reviewer's words, not your marketing language. When a prospect hears their own frustration described accurately, they trust you.
3. Create a one-page battle card for each competitor: their top 3 complaint themes, your solution for each, and the evidence. Sales reps can reference this before competitive calls.
4. Update the review corpus and materials quarterly. Competitors change, new reviews appear, and some complaints get fixed.
