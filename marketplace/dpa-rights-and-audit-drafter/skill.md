# Dpa Rights And Audit Drafter

Draft data subject rights assistance and audit rights provisions.

## Inputs

- `breach-clause`: Breach notification provisions
- `subprocessor-clause`: Sub-processor terms
- `security-requirements`: Security requirements

## Outputs

- `rights-assistance-clause`: Data subject rights assistance obligations
- `dpa-final-draft`: Complete DPA ready for execution

## Process

1. Define processor obligations to assist with data subject rights requests
2. Specify audit rights including scope, frequency, and notice requirements
3. Compile all provisions into the final DPA document
