# Burnout Energy Audit

Map where energy goes and where it leaks: work tasks, relationships, obligations, and self-care deficits.

## Inputs

- `typical_week`: How a typical week looks hour by hour
- `energy_drains`: Activities that feel draining
- `energy_sources`: Activities that feel restorative

## Outputs

- `energy_map`: Time allocation vs energy impact
- `drain_ranking`: Biggest drains ranked by fixability
- `restoration_gaps`: Missing restorative activities

## Process

1. Map weekly time blocks to energy impact
2. Identify top 5 energy drains
3. Rank drains by fixability (can change vs stuck)
4. Identify restorative activities that disappeared
5. Calculate drain-to-restore ratio
