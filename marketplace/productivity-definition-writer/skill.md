# Productivity Definition Writer

Define full productivity in measurable, observable terms for this specific role. 'Up to speed' is not a definition. 'Handles 20 tickets per day with a 95% resolution rate and no escalations for routine issues' is a definition. The definition must be specific enough that two managers would agree on whether a new hire has reached it.

## Inputs

- `role-title`: The role being onboarded
- `current-performance-benchmarks`: Performance data from current team members at full productivity

## Outputs

- `productivity-definition`: The specific, measurable criteria that define full productivity for this role
- `milestone-ladder`: Intermediate milestones between day one and full productivity (e.g., at 2 weeks can handle X, at 4 weeks can handle Y)

## Process

1. Look at the performance data for current team members who are considered fully productive. What are their output metrics?
2. Set the full productivity threshold at 80% of the team median. Not the top performer. Not the average. The median, at 80%. A new hire who reaches this level is productive.
3. Define 3-4 intermediate milestones between day one and full productivity. Each milestone should be independently verifiable.
4. Test the definition: describe it to two managers separately and ask if they would agree that a new hire meeting these criteria is fully productive. If they disagree, the definition is too vague.
