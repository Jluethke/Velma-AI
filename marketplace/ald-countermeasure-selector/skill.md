# Ald Countermeasure Selector

Select the specific countermeasure for your primary failure. Each failure has a different fix, and a generic 'listen harder' fixes none of them. The countermeasure must be a small physical or mental action you can run in the moment.

## Inputs

- `primary-failure`: The single failure you will work on

## Outputs

- `countermeasure`: The specific in-the-moment action that counters your failure
- `practice-cues`: The signals that tell you the failure is about to happen, so you can trigger the countermeasure in time

## Process

1. Match the failure to its countermeasure. Interrupting: wait two full seconds of silence before you speak. Rehearsing: repeat the speaker's last phrase silently to keep your attention on them. Filtering: ask one question that could prove your expectation wrong. Judging: summarize their view to their satisfaction before you evaluate it.
2. Write the countermeasure as a single concrete action, not a principle. You will run it under pressure, so it has to be simple.
3. Identify the cues that precede your failure: a rising feeling, a pause you want to fill, a word that triggers your verdict. The cue is your trigger to run the countermeasure.
4. Choose one setting from your log to practice in first, ideally the one where the failure spiked.
