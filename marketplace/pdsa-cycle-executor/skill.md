# Pdsa Cycle Executor

Run the Plan-Do-Study-Act cycle.

## Inputs

- `change-concept`: Proposed change
- `quality-measures`: Measures to track

## Outputs

- `cycle-results`: Data collected during the cycle
- `lessons-learned`: What worked, what did not, and why

## Process

1. Plan: define the test, predictions, and data collection.
2. Do: carry out the test on a small scale.
3. Study: analyze the results and compare to predictions.
