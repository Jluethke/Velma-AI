# Availability Publication

Publish the finalized availability to all downstream booking and scheduling systems.

## Inputs

- `updated-calendar`: The master calendar with all updates applied
- `scheduling-rules`: Rules governing the availability

## Outputs

- `published-availability`: The availability as published to booking systems and team members
- `availability-management-plan`: Complete availability management plan documenting the process and rules

## Process

1. Push the updated availability to the appointment booking system so customers see accurate open slots.
2. Notify team members of their finalized schedules.
3. Set up alerts for last-minute changes (callouts, emergencies) that require immediate re-publication.
4. Compile the availability management plan documenting the full process, rules, and escalation paths.
