# One Session Reset Checklist

Make every environment change in a single session, because a gradual rollout lets the old cues persist and the design never takes hold.

## Inputs

- `friction-removal-plan`: The increase-behavior changes
- `friction-addition-stack`: The decrease-behavior obstacles

## Outputs

- `environment-reset-checklist`: A single checklist of every physical and digital change to make in one sitting
- `reset-completion-record`: A record of which changes were actually completed during the session

## Process

1. Combine both plans into one checklist split into physical changes and digital changes, so you can move through the home and the phone in order.
2. Block thirty to sixty minutes and make every change at once. A design rolled out over a week leaves old cues in place long enough to win.
3. Check off each item as you complete it, and note any you could not finish, such as a change that needs a household member to agree.
4. Take a photo or written snapshot of the finished environment so you can restore it later when things drift back.
