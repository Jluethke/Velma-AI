# Vital Signs Chief Complaint Collector

Collect vital signs and the chief complaint.

## Inputs

- `demographic-record`: Structured demographic data
- `medical-history`: Past medical, surgical, and family history

## Outputs

- `vital-signs`: BP, HR, temp, respiratory rate, SpO2, weight, height
- `chief-complaint`: Reason for the visit in the patient's words

## Process

1. Measure and record all vital signs.
2. Document the chief complaint and history of present illness.
3. Note the onset, duration, and severity of symptoms.
