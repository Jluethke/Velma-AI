# Time Cost Calculator

Calculate the time cost: how many hours does this subject consume, multiplied by your effective hourly rate?

## Inputs

- `analysis-subject`: The subject being analyzed
- `direct-costs`: The direct costs already calculated

## Outputs

- `time-cost`: Hours consumed per unit times the effective hourly rate, broken down by: usage time, maintenance time, and decision time
- `effective-hourly-rate`: The rate used: total annual income divided by total annual working hours

## Process

1. Calculate the effective hourly rate: total annual income (or revenue, for a business) divided by total annual hours worked. For a salaried person working 2,000 hours at $80,000, the rate is $40/hour.
2. Measure three types of time: usage time (hours spent actively using the subject), maintenance time (hours spent maintaining, configuring, troubleshooting, or managing it), and decision time (hours spent deciding whether to keep it, comparing alternatives, or dealing with issues it creates).
3. Multiply total hours per unit by the effective hourly rate. A subscription that takes 2 hours per month to manage costs $80/month in time at a $40/hour rate, even if the subscription itself is only $20/month.
4. The time cost is often the largest hidden cost for individuals and small businesses. If the time cost exceeds the direct cost, the subject is primarily costing you time, not money.
