# Budget Consolidator

Consolidate the budget, review with leadership, and finalize.

## Inputs

- `scenario-models`: Three-scenario budget models
- `revenue-assumptions`: Key assumptions to present

## Outputs

- `approved-budget`: Final approved budget document
- `budget-presentation`: Budget presentation for board or leadership

## Process

1. Consolidate all inputs into the master budget (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow).
2. Prepare the budget presentation with key metrics and scenario comparison.
3. Present to leadership and incorporate feedback.
4. Finalize and distribute the approved budget to departments.
