# Deadline And Pressure Handler

Handle deadline pressure and last-minute demands. Deadlines create urgency that leads to bad concessions. Separate real deadlines from manufactured pressure.

## Inputs

- `concession-sequence`: Your planned concession pattern
- `negotiation-context`: The negotiation dynamics

## Outputs

- `deadline-assessment`: For each deadline: is it real (external constraint) or manufactured (pressure tactic)? What happens if the deadline passes?
- `pressure-response-plan`: How to respond to last-minute demands, take-it-or-leave-it offers, and artificial urgency without making panic concessions

## Process

1. When a deadline is imposed, ask: what happens if we miss it? If the answer is 'nothing material,' the deadline is manufactured. If there are real consequences (regulatory filing, budget cycle, project start), it is real.
2. Real deadlines: work backward from the deadline. Know your minimum acceptable deal and your walk-away point before the deadline pressure intensifies.
3. Manufactured deadlines: name the tactic. 'I understand the urgency, but I want to make sure we reach the right agreement, not just a fast one. Can we extend to [date]?'
4. Last-minute demands (adding a new issue at the final stage): never accept without trading. 'I was not expecting this issue. I need to reassess the entire package if we are adding new terms.'
