# Dietary Needs Assessment

Assess dietary requirements, preferences, allergies, and nutritional goals for the household.

## Inputs

- `household_members`: Number of people and ages
- `dietary_restrictions`: Allergies, intolerances, preferences
- `nutritional_goals`: Weight loss, muscle gain, maintenance, etc.

## Outputs

- `dietary_profile`: Household dietary requirements
- `calorie_targets`: Daily calorie and macro targets
- `ingredient_blacklist`: Ingredients to avoid

## Process

1. Catalog all dietary restrictions and allergies
2. Set calorie and macronutrient targets per person
3. Identify preferred cuisines and flavors
4. Note meal timing preferences
5. Build ingredient blacklist
