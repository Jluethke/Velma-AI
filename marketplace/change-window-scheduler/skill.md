# Change Window Scheduler

Schedule the change implementation window.

## Inputs

- `change-request`: The approved change request
- `approval-decision`: The approval decision
- `conditions`: Conditions on the approval

## Outputs

- `change-schedule`: Scheduled date, time, and duration
- `notification-plan`: Who to notify before, during, and after

## Process

1. Check the change calendar for conflicts.
2. Select a window that minimizes user impact.
3. Notify all stakeholders of the scheduled window.
4. Confirm resource availability for the window.
