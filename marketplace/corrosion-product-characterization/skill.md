# Corrosion Product Characterization

Characterize the corrosion products and attack morphology using microscopy and spectroscopy.

## Inputs

- `electrochemical-data`: Corrosion conditions for context
- `prepared-corrosion-specimens`: Corroded specimens for analysis

## Outputs

- `corrosion-morphology`: Optical and SEM images showing pit depth, intergranular attack, or uniform thinning
- `product-composition`: EDS or XRD identification of corrosion product phases

## Process

1. Examine the corroded surface under optical microscopy at low and high magnification.
2. Image representative areas under SEM for higher resolution morphology.
3. Run EDS on corrosion products to identify elemental composition.
4. Classify the corrosion form: uniform, pitting, crevice, intergranular, galvanic, or stress corrosion cracking.
