# Risk Owner Assignment

Assign an owner to each risk in the critical and high tiers. A risk without an owner is not managed.

## Inputs

- `scored-risk-list`: The ranked list of scored risks
- `risk-tiers`: The tier groupings

## Outputs

- `owned-risk-list`: Each critical and high risk with a named owner and their authority to act
- `unowned-risks`: Any risks that could not be assigned because no one has the authority or knowledge, flagged as organizational gaps

## Process

1. For each critical and high risk, identify the person (or role, for a household: the adult responsible) closest to the risk source, with authority to spend money or change process to mitigate it.
2. If no one has both proximity and authority, the risk is an organizational gap. Escalate it: someone must be given the authority or the risk is effectively unmanaged.
3. Document the owner's name, what they are authorized to do (spend up to $X, change process Y, hire Z), and how they will report on the risk.
4. For personal risks, the owner is you. The assignment step forces you to acknowledge that you are the one who must act.
