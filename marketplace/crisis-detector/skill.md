# Crisis Detector

Detect and assess a social media crisis.

## Inputs

- `alert-trigger`: The alert or observation that flagged the potential crisis
- `brand-context`: The brand and its current social media presence

## Outputs

- `crisis-assessment`: Assessment of severity (low, medium, high, critical), scope, and velocity of spread
- `affected-channels`: List of channels where the crisis is active

## Process

1. Review the alert trigger and verify it is a real crisis, not normal noise.
2. Measure the current volume and velocity (how fast mentions are growing).
3. Assess the sentiment and identify the key complaint or issue.
4. Classify the severity level and list the affected channels.
