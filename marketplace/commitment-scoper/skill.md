# Commitment Scoper

Define what the commitment actually costs in terms of the resources it consumes. Not just money but time, attention, relationships, and optionality. A commitment that takes 10 hours per week for 6 months is not just a time cost; it forecloses every other 10-hour-per-week activity for 6 months.

## Inputs

- `proposed-commitment`: The thing you are considering saying yes to
- `resource-inventory`: Your current resources: available time per week, budget, team capacity, other constraints

## Outputs

- `true-cost-breakdown`: The full resource cost of the commitment: time, money, attention, optionality consumed
- `foreclosed-capacity`: What capacity is no longer available for other things if you say yes

## Process

1. State the commitment in specific terms: what exactly will it require, for how long?
2. Break the cost into resource categories: hours per week, dollars, team members allocated, mental bandwidth consumed.
3. For each resource category, state what capacity remains after the commitment.
4. Name any optionality that is foreclosed: doors that close because of this commitment (non-compete, exclusive relationship, geographic lock-in).
