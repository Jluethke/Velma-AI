# Motivation Scoring

Score your motivation honestly, separating reflective motivation from the automatic pulls that override it in the moment.

## Inputs

- `framed-behavior`: The behavior stated as an observable action
- `past-attempt-notes`: The record of past attempts

## Outputs

- `motivation-score`: A score for how much you actually want the behavior, in reflection and in the moment
- `motivation-evidence`: Where reflective intention and in-the-moment impulse diverge

## Process

1. Separate reflective motivation, what you want when you think it over, from automatic motivation, the habits and impulses that act in the moment.
2. Score reflective motivation from one to ten. Most people rate this high, which is exactly why they assume motivation is the problem.
3. Score automatic motivation separately. A person can genuinely want to change yet be pulled by a strong habit loop that fires before reflection arrives.
4. Record where the two diverge. A large gap between wanting it on reflection and doing the opposite in the moment points to a habit and cue problem, not a desire problem.
