# Positive History Builder

Build positive credit history through strategic account management and authorized user strategies.

## Inputs

- `current_accounts`: Open credit accounts
- `account_ages`: Age of each account
- `available_strategies`: Secured card, authorized user, etc.

## Outputs

- `account_strategy`: Accounts to keep, close, or open
- `payment_automation`: Autopay setup for on-time payments
- `timeline_projection`: Expected score improvement timeline

## Process

1. Never close oldest accounts
2. Set up autopay on every account
3. Consider secured card if limited history
4. Explore authorized user on old account
5. Project score improvement over 6-12 months
