# Brand Strategy Definer

Define the brand strategy including mission, values, personality, and voice.

## Inputs

- `market-analysis`: Competitive landscape and positioning
- `audience-profiles`: Target audience segments

## Outputs

- `brand-strategy`: Brand mission, values, personality, and positioning statement
- `brand-voice-guide`: Voice attributes, do/don't examples, and tone spectrum

## Process

1. Draft the brand mission and vision statements
2. Define the core values (3 to 5) with behavioral descriptions
3. Select the brand personality archetype and attributes
4. Write the brand voice guidelines with examples
