# Inspector Trainer

Train inspectors on procedures and measurement techniques.

## Inputs

- `inspection-procedures`: Inspection instructions
- `equipment-list`: Tools and gages

## Outputs

- `trained-inspectors`: Qualified inspector roster
- `training-log`: Training completion records

## Process

1. Train inspectors on each procedure with hands-on practice
2. Conduct proficiency testing with known samples
3. Verify inter-inspector agreement (attribute agreement analysis)
4. Document qualifications and requalification schedule
