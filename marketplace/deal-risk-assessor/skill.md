# Deal Risk Assessor

Identify risks and blockers that could prevent the deal from closing.

## Inputs

- `bant-assessment`: BANT dimension scores
- `competitive-assessment`: Competitive position analysis

## Outputs

- `risk-register`: Ranked list of deal risks
- `mitigation-actions`: Actions to address each risk

## Process

1. Score each risk dimension (budget risk, champion risk, timing risk, competitive risk).
2. Rank risks by likelihood and impact.
3. Define a mitigation action for each high-priority risk.
4. Identify any showstopper risks that cannot be mitigated.
