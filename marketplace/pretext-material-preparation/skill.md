# Pretext Material Preparation

Prepare pretexting materials including phishing emails, landing pages, phone scripts, and physical props.

## Inputs

- `approved-scenarios`: Approved attack scenarios
- `organization-profile`: Organization details for realistic pretexts

## Outputs

- `phishing-materials`: Email templates and landing pages for phishing campaigns
- `pretext-scripts`: Phone and in-person pretext scripts

## Process

1. Research the organization's communication style, email format, and common vendors.
2. Create phishing email templates matching internal communication patterns.
3. Build credential harvesting landing pages that mimic legitimate login portals.
4. Write phone pretext scripts for common scenarios (IT support, vendor, executive).
