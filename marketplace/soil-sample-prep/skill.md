# Soil Sample Prep

Prepare soil samples for laboratory analysis following standard protocols.

## Inputs

- `field-samples`: Raw soil samples with collection metadata (location, depth, date)
- `analysis-plan`: Which tests will be run and their preparation requirements

## Outputs

- `prepared-samples`: Air-dried, sieved, and split samples ready for each test
- `prep-log`: Record of preparation steps, weights, and any anomalies

## Process

1. Air-dry samples at room temperature, noting initial moisture content.
2. Remove roots, stones, and debris, then sieve through 2mm mesh.
3. Split into aliquots for physical, chemical, and biological analyses.
4. Record sample weights and store biological aliquots at appropriate temperature.
