# Account Compromise Confirmation

Confirm that the account is compromised by analyzing the indicators.

## Inputs

- `compromise-indicators`: Alerts or reports suggesting account compromise
- `account-details`: Account username, role, and access level

## Outputs

- `compromise-confirmation`: Confirmed compromise with evidence and method of access
- `indicator-summary`: Summary of all compromise indicators identified

## Process

1. Review the indicators (impossible travel, unusual login location, anomalous activity)
2. Check authentication logs for unauthorized access patterns
3. Verify with the account owner whether the activity was legitimate
4. Confirm the compromise and document the evidence
