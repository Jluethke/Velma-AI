# Current Belief Mapping

Map what the other person currently believes and why.

## Inputs

- `persuasion-goal`: What you want the other person to believe, decide, or do
- `their-current-position`: What they currently believe and why

## Outputs

- `belief-map`: Their current belief, the evidence supporting it, and the emotional attachment to it
- `belief-gap`: The gap between their current belief and where you want them to go

## Process

1. Understand their belief fully before trying to change it.
2. Identify the type: factual, experiential, social, or identity-based. Each type requires a different approach.
3. Identity-based beliefs are the hardest to change through argument. Questions are the only effective approach.
4. Map the gap: what their evidence misses, not what yours adds.
