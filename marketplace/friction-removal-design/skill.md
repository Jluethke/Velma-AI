# Friction Removal Design

Cut the friction on the increase behavior down to one step or zero, so starting it no longer requires a decision.

## Inputs

- `increase-friction-points`: The steps currently blocking the increase behavior
- `defined-increase-target`: The increase behavior

## Outputs

- `friction-removal-plan`: Specific changes that reduce the increase behavior to a one-step or zero-step start
- `default-path-change`: One change that makes the increase behavior the automatic path rather than a choice

## Process

1. For each friction point, decide how to remove it in advance. Lay out clothes the night before, keep the book on the pillow, prefill the water bottle.
2. Aim for a one-step start. The goal is that beginning the behavior takes a single visible action rather than a chain of small decisions.
3. Change one default so the behavior happens unless you stop it. A fruit bowl at eye level and the snacks out of sight makes the good choice the lazy choice.
4. Pick a trigger you already encounter daily and attach the behavior to it, so the environment reminds you instead of your memory.
