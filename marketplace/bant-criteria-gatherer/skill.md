# Bant Criteria Gatherer

Gather budget, authority, need, and timeline information for a deal.

## Inputs

- `deal-description`: Overview of the deal and prospect
- `discovery-notes`: Notes from discovery calls and meetings

## Outputs

- `bant-assessment`: Scored assessment of each BANT dimension
- `information-gaps`: BANT questions still unanswered

## Process

1. Extract budget signals from discovery notes.
2. Identify the decision maker and buying process.
3. Assess the urgency and specificity of the need.
4. Determine the timeline and any deadline pressures.
