# Subcontractor Work Management

Manage the subcontractor's deliverables, quality, and payment.

## Inputs

- `sub-workspace-access`: Subcontractor access details
- `deliverable-schedule`: Agreed deliverable schedule

## Outputs

- `quality-review-log`: Record of deliverable reviews, feedback given, and revisions made
- `sub-payment-record`: Record of invoices received, payments made, and total cost

## Process

1. Review each deliverable against the acceptance criteria at each milestone.
2. Provide clear, actionable feedback for any revisions needed.
3. Process the subcontractor's invoices on the agreed schedule.
4. Track the total cost against the budget allocation.
5. At project end, archive the subcontractor's work and close out the agreement.
