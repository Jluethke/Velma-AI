# Fraud Evidence Gatherer

Gather evidence for the fraud investigation.

## Inputs

- `flagged-claims`: Claims selected for fraud investigation
- `investigation-priority`: Priority ranking for investigation

## Outputs

- `evidence-package`: Collected evidence including records, surveillance results, statements, and expert opinions
- `witness-statements`: Statements from witnesses, claimants, and involved parties

## Process

1. Obtain and review all claim documentation and correspondence.
2. Conduct recorded statements with the claimant and witnesses.
3. Order surveillance if warranted by the claim type and indicators.
4. Engage forensic experts (fire investigators, accident reconstructionists, medical reviewers) as needed.
