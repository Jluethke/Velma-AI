# Asymmetric Avoid Zone Inversion

Invert the analysis: where your weaknesses meet competitors' strengths are the zones to avoid. Fighting there is a head-to-head battle on their terms, the exact fight an asymmetric strategy exists to skip.

## Inputs

- `self-weakness-list`: The weaknesses you cannot quickly fix
- `competitor-strength-map`: Each competitor's real strengths

## Outputs

- `avoid-zones`: The areas where you are weak and rivals are strong
- `head-to-head-risks`: The specific fights to refuse

## Process

1. Lay your self-weakness list against the competitor strength map.
2. Mark every point where your weakness meets a rival strength. These are avoid zones.
3. Name the specific head-to-head fights that would drag you into those zones.
4. Treat avoid zones as discipline: winning elsewhere means not fighting here.
