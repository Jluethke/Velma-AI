# Small Claims Evidence Organizer

Organize evidence for small claims court: documents, photos, communications, and witness preparation.

## Inputs

- `evidence_available`: All available evidence
- `dispute_description`: Timeline of events

## Outputs

- `evidence_binder`: Organized evidence with labels
- `timeline_of_events`: Chronological narrative with evidence links
- `witness_list`: Witnesses and what they can testify to

## Process

1. Gather all relevant documents
2. Organize chronologically
3. Label each piece of evidence
4. Create copies for court and opposing party
5. Prepare witnesses on what to expect
