# Pet Health Schedule

Build a veterinary care schedule: vaccinations, checkups, dental, and preventive treatments by age.

## Inputs

- `pet_type`: Dog, cat, etc.
- `breed`: Breed for breed-specific needs
- `age`: Current age
- `health_history`: Known health issues

## Outputs

- `vet_schedule`: Annual veterinary care calendar
- `vaccination_tracker`: Vaccination status and due dates
- `preventive_plan`: Flea, tick, heartworm schedule

## Process

1. Map age-appropriate vaccinations
2. Schedule annual or biannual checkups
3. Plan dental care schedule
4. Set up parasite prevention calendar
5. Note breed-specific health screenings
