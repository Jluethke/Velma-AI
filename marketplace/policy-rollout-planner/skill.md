# Policy Rollout Planner

Plan the communication strategy and training rollout for the approved policy.

## Inputs

- `approved-policy`: The approved policy document
- `responsibility-matrix`: RACI matrix identifying affected personnel

## Outputs

- `rollout-plan`: Communication and training schedule
- `policy-package`: Final policy document with rollout materials

## Process

1. Identify all audiences who need to know the policy
2. Create communication materials tailored to each audience
3. Schedule training sessions and awareness activities
4. Define metrics to measure policy adoption
