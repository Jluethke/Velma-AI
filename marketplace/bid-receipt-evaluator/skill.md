# Bid Receipt Evaluator

Receive bids and evaluate them against the criteria.

## Inputs

- `solicitation-document`: The published solicitation
- `evaluation-criteria`: Scoring criteria and weights

## Outputs

- `bid-scores`: Each bid scored against the evaluation criteria
- `evaluation-summary`: Summary of the evaluation with ranking and recommendation

## Process

1. Receive and log all bids by the submission deadline.
2. Verify each bid meets the minimum requirements and is responsive.
3. Score each bid against the evaluation criteria.
4. Rank the bids and prepare the evaluation summary with a recommendation.
