# Renewable Contract Negotiator

Negotiate and execute the renewable energy contract.

## Inputs

- `option-comparison`: Comparison of procurement options
- `recommended-approach`: Recommended approach

## Outputs

- `contract-terms`: Negotiated contract terms (price, duration, settlement, credit)
- `procurement-record`: Complete record of the procurement decision and contract

## Process

1. Issue the RFP to qualified developers or REC suppliers.
2. Evaluate bids on price, project quality, counterparty credit, and contract terms.
3. Negotiate the key terms: price escalator, curtailment risk, settlement mechanism, and termination.
4. Execute the contract and document the procurement decision for the sustainability report.
