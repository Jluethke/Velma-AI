# Netgap Progress Tracker

Track progress on closing the selected gap and decide when it is filled enough to move to the next one, so gap-filling becomes an ongoing practice that keeps your network structurally healthy rather than a one-time project that quietly lapses.

## Inputs

- `entry-plan`: The plan to enter and contribute to the community
- `first-contact-targets`: The first foothold relationships inside the community

## Outputs

- `gap-fill-tracker`: A simple tracker of entry progress: presence maintained, relationships formed, and whether the gap is beginning to deliver its value
- `progress-review-cadence`: When to review progress, when to judge the gap filled enough, and when to begin the next gap

## Process

1. Track the inputs you control: whether you are maintaining the planned presence and contribution. Showing up as planned is the leading indicator, because relationships and value follow from consistent presence, not from intention.
2. Track the outputs that signal the gap is closing: real relationships forming (people who now recognize and respond to you), and the first non-redundant information or opportunity reaching you through the new community.
3. Define filled enough rather than finished. A gap is filled when you have a few genuine relationships in the community and value has started to flow, at which point the community largely sustains itself with lighter upkeep.
4. Set a review cadence to decide when to start the next prioritized gap, and fold the filled gap into ordinary relationship maintenance so it does not decay back into a hole while your attention moves on.
