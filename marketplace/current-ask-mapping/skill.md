# Current Ask Mapping

Map every ask you currently make of a prospect, in order, with the size of each commitment.

## Inputs

- `current-funnel`: The current sequence of steps from first touch to purchase
- `offer-description`: What you ultimately want the buyer to say yes to

## Outputs

- `ask-inventory`: Every ask in the current journey, in sequence
- `commitment-sizes`: The size of each ask in time, money, and risk to the buyer

## Process

1. List every ask the buyer faces, from giving an email to signing a contract, in the order they hit it.
2. For each ask, rate the commitment it demands in the buyer's time, money, and perceived risk.
3. Mark which asks are reversible and which feel permanent, since permanent asks carry more weight.
4. Note where the buyer must say yes to something before they have seen any value.
