# Visualization Audit

Audit current visualizations across the organization for consistency.

## Inputs

- `dashboard-inventory`: List of existing dashboards and reports with visualizations
- `brand-guidelines`: Existing brand guidelines (colors, fonts) if available

## Outputs

- `audit-findings`: Inconsistencies found across current visualizations
- `pattern-catalog`: Common patterns and anti-patterns observed

## Process

1. Collect screenshots or links from all existing dashboards and reports.
2. Catalog the chart types, colors, fonts, and labeling patterns in use.
3. Identify inconsistencies and common mistakes.
