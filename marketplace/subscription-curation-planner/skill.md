# Subscription Curation Planner

Select the product curation strategy and pricing model.

## Inputs

- `audience-profile`: Target audience profile
- `value-proposition`: Value proposition

## Outputs

- `curation-strategy`: Product curation approach and sourcing plan
- `pricing-model`: Pricing tiers and billing frequency

## Process

1. Choose the curation model (curated, personalized, surprise, or fixed).
2. Source products and negotiate supplier terms.
3. Set pricing tiers (monthly, quarterly, annual with discount).
4. Calculate unit economics (COGS, shipping, packaging, margin).
