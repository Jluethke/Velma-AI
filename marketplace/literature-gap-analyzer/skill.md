# Literature Gap Analyzer

Systematically review the literature to identify what is known, where gaps exist, and how the proposed thesis fills a specific gap.

## Inputs

- `thesis-topic`: Specific topic and research question from the narrowing step
- `search-databases`: Databases to search (PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, Google Scholar, etc.)
- `inclusion-criteria`: Date range, language, study type, and relevance filters

## Outputs

- `literature-synthesis`: Organized synthesis of what is known, grouped by theme or chronology
- `gap-statement`: Specific statement of the gap the thesis addresses with supporting evidence
- `reference-library`: Organized citation database with annotations for each source

## Process

1. Develop search strings from the research question keywords and synonyms
2. Run searches across selected databases and deduplicate results
3. Screen titles and abstracts against inclusion criteria
4. Read and annotate included papers, extracting methods, findings, and limitations
5. Synthesize findings into themes and identify the specific gap the thesis will address
