# Adjuster Inspection Coordinator

Coordinate the adjuster inspection and preserve evidence.

## Inputs

- `adjuster-assignment`: Adjuster contact and scheduled date
- `loss-documentation`: All supporting evidence

## Outputs

- `inspection-record`: Notes from the adjuster visit and measurements taken
- `preserved-evidence`: Evidence maintained per policy requirements

## Process

1. Do not destroy evidence or harvest the affected area before inspection.
2. Prepare all records for the adjuster to review.
3. Walk the field with the adjuster and answer questions.
4. Record the adjuster's measurements and observations.
