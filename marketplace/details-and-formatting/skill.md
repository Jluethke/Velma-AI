# Details And Formatting

Format details for scanning: bullets, bold, under 200 words total.

## Inputs

- `context-block`: The context
- `email-purpose`: Full details

## Outputs

- `formatted-details`: Supporting details as bullet points with bold on key items
- `total-word-count`: Total email word count (target: under 200)

## Process

1. Use bullet points for any list of 3+ items.
2. Bold key items: deadlines, numbers, names of people who need to act.
3. Target 200 words total. Move details to attachment if longer.
4. End with a single clear next step.
