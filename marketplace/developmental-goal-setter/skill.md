# Developmental Goal Setter

Define the developmental goals for the age group across all domains.

## Inputs

- `age-group`: The age group for the curriculum (infant, toddler, preschool, school-age)
- `assessment-data`: Current developmental observations and assessments for children in the group

## Outputs

- `developmental-goals`: Goals organized by domain (cognitive, language, social-emotional, physical, creative)
- `focus-areas`: Priority areas based on the current group's needs and interests

## Process

1. Review the age-appropriate developmental milestones for each domain.
2. Assess where the current group of children is relative to those milestones.
3. Identify the focus areas where the group would benefit from targeted activities.
4. Set measurable goals for the planning period (month or quarter).
