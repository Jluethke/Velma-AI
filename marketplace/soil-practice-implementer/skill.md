# Soil Practice Implementer

Implement the selected soil health practices.

## Inputs

- `practice-plan`: Assigned practices and timeline
- `field-map`: Field locations and boundaries

## Outputs

- `implementation-record`: What was done, when, and where
- `observation-notes`: Field observations during implementation

## Process

1. Execute each practice according to the timeline.
2. Record application rates, dates, and methods.
3. Document any deviations from the plan and the reasons.
4. Note initial field observations after implementation.
