# Secondorder Ask And Then What Second

Ask 'and then what?' again: trace the second-order effects out to their third-order results.

## Inputs

- `second-order-effects`: What each first-order effect sets off in turn.
- `second-order-notes`: Where a good first-order effect starts to turn bad.

## Outputs

- `third-order-effects`: The effects at the end of each chain.
- `order-chain`: The full chain from decision to third-order result.

## Process

1. For each second-order effect, ask what it leads to over a longer span.
2. Write the third-order effects at the end of each chain.
3. Assemble the full chain from decision to third-order result.
