# Bess Decision Writer

Write the proceed or skip recommendation.

## Inputs

- `horizon-verdict`: Whether the payback fits the horizon
- `sensitivity-table`: The stressed payback results

## Outputs

- `payback-report`: The payback period, margin, and stress results
- `invest-decision`: The proceed or skip recommendation with reasoning

## Process

1. State the payback period and the margin against the horizon.
2. Summarize the stress results.
3. Recommend proceed or skip with the reasoning.
