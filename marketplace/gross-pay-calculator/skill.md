# Gross Pay Calculator

Calculate gross pay including regular hours, overtime, bonuses, and commissions.

## Inputs

- `validated-timesheets`: Approved timesheets
- `employee-roster`: Pay rates and compensation details

## Outputs

- `gross-pay-register`: Gross pay calculations for each employee
- `overtime-report`: Overtime hours and costs by department

## Process

1. Calculate regular pay (hourly rate times regular hours or salary proration).
2. Calculate overtime pay at the applicable rate (1.5x or 2x).
3. Add bonuses, commissions, and other supplemental pay.
4. Generate the gross pay register and overtime report.
