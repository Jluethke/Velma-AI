# Sleep Quality Tracking

Track sleep quality and not only hours, because time in bed does not equal restorative sleep.

## Inputs

- `winddown-sequence`: The wind-down sequence
- `environment-settings`: The target bedroom settings

## Outputs

- `quality-tracker`: A daily log of sleep quality markers, not just duration
- `quality-vs-duration-metrics`: A view of how rested you feel relative to hours spent in bed

## Process

1. Log duration, but add quality markers: how long it took to fall asleep, how many times you woke, and how rested you felt on waking.
2. Record a simple morning rating of alertness. Feeling unrested after eight hours is a quality problem that duration alone would hide.
3. Watch for the mismatch between long time in bed and poor rest. That gap points to fragmented sleep from environment, stimulants, or stress rather than too little time.
4. Keep the log light enough to sustain, a few marks each morning, so it survives past the first enthusiastic week.
