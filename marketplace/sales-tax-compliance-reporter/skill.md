# Sales Tax Compliance Reporter

Produce the sales tax compliance report with filing status and rate change tracking.

## Inputs

- `filed-returns`: Filing status for all jurisdictions
- `reconciliation-report`: Tax reconciliation results

## Outputs

- `compliance-report`: Sales tax compliance status report
- `rate-change-alerts`: Upcoming rate changes and exemption updates

## Process

1. Summarize filing status (on time, late, pending) by jurisdiction.
2. Report on reconciliation results and outstanding variances.
3. Monitor for upcoming rate changes and new legislation.
4. Update the tax rate table for the next period.
