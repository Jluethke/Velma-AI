# Question Delivery

Deliver the questions in conversation, adapting in real time.

## Inputs

- `question-sequence`: The planned questions
- `anticipated-answers`: Expected responses

## Outputs

- `conversation-record`: Your questions, their answers, and where they ended up
- `conclusion-reached`: The conclusion they arrived at in their own words

## Process

1. Ask questions with genuine curiosity, not as cross-examination.
2. Listen to each answer fully before asking the next question.
3. If they arrive at a different conclusion than expected, consider whether theirs is better.
4. If they arrive at your conclusion, let them own it.
