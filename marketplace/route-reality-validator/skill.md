# Route Reality Validator

Validate optimized routes against real-world conditions.

## Inputs

- `candidate-routes`: Optimized routes
- `optimization-metrics`: Route metrics

## Outputs

- `validated-routes`: Routes adjusted for real-world conditions (traffic, construction, weather)
- `adjustment-notes`: Notes on adjustments made and reasons

## Process

1. Check routes against current traffic patterns and known congestion zones.
2. Incorporate construction closures and detours.
3. Adjust estimated times for weather conditions if applicable.
4. Flag any routes that became infeasible after real-world adjustments.
