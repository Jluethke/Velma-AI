# Cart Recovery Performance Tracker

Track recovery rate, revenue, and optimize the recovery program.

## Inputs

- `email-sequence`: Active recovery sequences
- `exit-intent-config`: Exit-intent configuration

## Outputs

- `recovery-metrics`: Recovery rate, revenue recovered, and cost per recovery
- `cart-recovery-plan`: Cart recovery program plan and optimization roadmap

## Process

1. Track open rate, click rate, and conversion rate per email in the sequence.
2. Calculate revenue recovered and cost per recovery (discounts given).
3. Compare recovery rates across segments and channels.
4. Identify the highest-performing tactics and produce the optimization plan.
