# Creative Concept Development

Develop the initial creative concepts based on the brief.

## Inputs

- `approved-brief`: The creative brief to work from
- `team-assignments`: Who is developing the concepts

## Outputs

- `concept-options`: Two to three creative concept options with rationale
- `concept-recommendation`: The recommended concept with supporting reasoning

## Process

1. Research references and inspiration aligned with the brief's direction.
2. Develop two to three distinct concept options.
3. For each concept, explain how it meets the brief's objectives.
4. Recommend one concept and explain why.
5. Produce the concept options and recommendation.
