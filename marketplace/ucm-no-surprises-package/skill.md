# Ucm No Surprises Package

Assemble the message and make sure your manager is never surprised in front of others. A manager caught off guard in a meeting by something you knew will remember it. A short pre-brief before anything goes public protects them and protects your standing with them.

## Inputs

- `headline`: The leading point
- `specific-ask`: What you need and whether it is a decision
- `bad-news-structure`: The bad-news framing, if the message carries bad news
- `timing-plan`: When the message goes out

## Outputs

- `upward-message`: The assembled message: headline, framed detail, specific ask, delivered on the timing plan
- `pre-brief-plan`: A plan to brief the manager before anything that could surprise them in front of others

## Process

1. Assemble in order: headline, the detail framed in their terms, the specific ask with its decision-or-awareness flag.
2. Identify anything in the message that could reach a group setting: a status meeting, a leadership review, a board update.
3. Pre-brief the manager on those items before the public moment, even in a sentence. The goal is that nothing you knew surprises them in a room.
4. Confirm the timing. Early and privately first, then public, so the manager is never learning it at the same time as their peers.
