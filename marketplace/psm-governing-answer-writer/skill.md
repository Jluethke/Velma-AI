# Psm Governing Answer Writer

Write the governing answer: the single sentence the whole presentation exists to deliver. In the Minto Pyramid, this answer sits at the top, and everything below supports it. Start here, because if the answer is not clear to you, no structure will make it clear to the audience.

## Inputs

- `presentation-goal`: What you want the audience to decide, do, or believe afterward
- `audience`: Who they are, what they already know, and what question they walked in with

## Outputs

- `governing-answer`: The one-sentence answer the presentation delivers, stated before any supporting detail
- `audience-question`: The question in the audience's mind that this answer resolves

## Process

1. Write the question the audience actually has: 'Should we enter this market?' or 'Why did the project slip?'
2. Write the answer to that question in one sentence. This is the governing answer, and it goes on the first content slide, not the last.
3. Check that the answer resolves the audience's question, not a different question you would rather answer.
4. Strip hedging from the answer. 'We should enter the market in the second half' is an answer. 'There are many factors to consider' is a stall.
