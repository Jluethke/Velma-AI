# Rca Evidence Collector

Collect data and physical evidence related to the problem.

## Inputs

- `problem-definition`: Problem statement
- `containment-actions`: Containment status

## Outputs

- `evidence-package`: Collected data, samples, photos, and process records
- `timeline-of-events`: Chronological sequence of events around the failure

## Process

1. Gather physical evidence (failed parts, samples, photos)
2. Collect process data (machine parameters, material lot numbers, operator logs)
3. Interview operators and witnesses
4. Build the timeline of events leading to and following the failure
