# Qms Process Mapper

Map the organization's processes and their interactions.

## Inputs

- `qms-scope`: QMS scope
- `quality-policy`: Quality policy

## Outputs

- `process-map`: Process interaction diagram (turtle diagrams or SIPOC)
- `process-owners`: Assigned process owners for each process

## Process

1. Identify all processes within the QMS scope
2. Classify as management, core (realization), or support processes
3. Define inputs, outputs, resources, and controls for each process
4. Map the interactions between processes
