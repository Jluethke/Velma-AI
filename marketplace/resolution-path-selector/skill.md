# Resolution Path Selector

Select the resolution path based on the merits assessment and business objectives.

## Inputs

- `merits-assessment`: Assessment of claim strength and defenses
- `exposure-estimate`: Estimated exposure

## Outputs

- `selected-path`: The selected resolution path (direct negotiation, mediation, arbitration, litigation)
- `resolution-strategy`: Strategy for the selected path including goals and settlement authority

## Process

1. Evaluate the resolution options: direct negotiation, mediation, arbitration, or litigation.
2. Consider cost, time, confidentiality, precedent, and business relationship factors.
3. Obtain settlement authority from the business if negotiation or mediation is selected.
4. Document the selected path and strategy.
