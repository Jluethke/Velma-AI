# Service Rehearsal Coordinator

Coordinate the rehearsal for the worship team and tech crew.

## Inputs

- `presentation-file`: The slide deck for the service
- `printed-materials`: Bulletin and other printed items

## Outputs

- `rehearsal-notes`: Adjustments made during rehearsal (timing, transitions, audio levels)
- `service-ready-checklist`: Confirmation that all elements are prepared and tested

## Process

1. Schedule the rehearsal: worship team sound check, tech walk-through, and run-through of transitions.
2. Test all audio, video, and streaming equipment during the sound check.
3. Walk through the full order of service noting transitions, cues, and timing.
4. Log any adjustments and confirm the service is ready to execute.
