# Acoustic Report Writer

Produce the acoustic analysis report with methods, detections, indices, and ecological interpretation.

## Inputs

- `acoustic-indices`: Computed acoustic indices and occupancy estimates
- `temporal-patterns`: Diel and seasonal activity patterns

## Outputs

- `acoustic-report`: The full acoustic analysis report
- `detection-database`: Complete database of all detections with features and classifications

## Process

1. Summarize survey effort (recorder-hours, spatial coverage).
2. Present species detection results with occupancy estimates and confidence intervals.
3. Interpret acoustic indices in the context of habitat quality and disturbance.
4. Document detector performance, limitations, and recommendations for future surveys.
