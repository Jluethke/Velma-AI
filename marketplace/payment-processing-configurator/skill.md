# Payment Processing Configurator

Configure payment processing for registration fees including early bird, family discounts, and scholarships.

## Inputs

- `form-design`: Registration form to integrate payment into
- `waiver-templates`: Documents that must be signed before payment

## Outputs

- `payment-configuration`: Fee structure, discount rules, scholarship application, and refund policy
- `payment-processor-setup`: Payment processor integration details

## Process

1. Define the fee schedule with early bird, standard, and late registration pricing.
2. Configure family and multi-sport discount rules.
3. Set up the scholarship application and approval workflow.
4. Document the refund and cancellation policy.
