# Energy Audit Report Compiler

Compile the energy audit findings into the final report.

## Inputs

- `ecm-list`: Energy conservation measures with savings and costs
- `prioritized-measures`: Ranked list of measures

## Outputs

- `energy-audit-report`: The complete energy audit report with baseline, findings, and recommendations
- `implementation-plan`: Suggested implementation sequence and timeline

## Process

1. Compile the executive summary with total savings potential and top recommendations.
2. Include the baseline analysis, facility description, and equipment inventory.
3. Present each measure with its savings, cost, payback, and implementation notes.
4. Outline the recommended implementation sequence starting with no-cost and low-cost measures.
