# Subcontractor Agreement Setup

Negotiate terms and sign the subcontractor agreement.

## Inputs

- `candidate-shortlist`: Selected subcontractor
- `vetting-notes`: Interview and reference notes

## Outputs

- `sub-agreement`: Signed subcontractor agreement covering scope, rate, IP, confidentiality, and payment terms
- `onboarding-plan`: Steps to onboard the subcontractor to the project

## Process

1. Draft the subcontractor agreement with scope, deliverables, rate, payment schedule, and timeline.
2. Include IP assignment, confidentiality, and non-solicitation clauses as needed.
3. Negotiate and finalize the terms.
4. Execute the agreement.
