# Ald Four Failures Primer

Learn the four listening failures precisely enough to spot them in yourself. Interrupting cuts the speaker off. Rehearsing plans your reply while they talk. Filtering hears only what confirms your expectation. Judging decides they are wrong before they finish. Each feels like listening from the inside.

## Inputs

- `listening-context`: The settings where your listening matters most: one-on-ones, meetings, home, customer calls
- `recent-conversations`: Two or three recent conversations that did not go the way you wanted

## Outputs

- `failure-definitions`: The four failures written in your own words with a concrete example of each from your life
- `self-suspected-failures`: Your honest first guess at which one or two you commit most, before any feedback

## Process

1. Write each of the four failures in your own words. Generic definitions do not stick. Yours will.
2. For each failure, write one example from your recent conversations where you suspect you did it. If you cannot find an example, that failure is probably not your main one.
3. Rank the four by how often you suspect you commit each. This is a hypothesis, not a conclusion. Feedback comes next.
4. Note that the failure you least want to admit is often the one others see most. Flag that one for honest attention.
