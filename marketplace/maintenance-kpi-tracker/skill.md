# Maintenance Kpi Tracker

Track maintenance completion and equipment performance.

## Inputs

- `scheduling-system`: CMMS with active work orders
- `training-records`: Trained technician roster

## Outputs

- `maintenance-metrics`: PM completion rate, MTBF, MTTR, OEE
- `equipment-uptime-report`: Uptime and availability by equipment

## Process

1. Track PM completion rate (target above 90%)
2. Calculate MTBF and MTTR for critical equipment
3. Compute OEE (availability x performance x quality)
4. Generate weekly and monthly maintenance dashboards
