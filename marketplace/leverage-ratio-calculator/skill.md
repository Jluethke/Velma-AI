# Leverage Ratio Calculator

Calculate leverage ratios: debt-to-equity, debt-to-assets, and interest coverage.

## Inputs

- `balance-sheet`: Balance sheet with debt and equity details
- `income-statement`: Income statement with interest expense

## Outputs

- `leverage-ratios`: Debt-to-equity, debt-to-assets, and interest coverage
- `debt-capacity-assessment`: Assessment of remaining borrowing capacity

## Process

1. Calculate debt-to-equity ratio (total debt divided by total equity).
2. Calculate debt-to-assets ratio.
3. Calculate interest coverage ratio (EBIT divided by interest expense).
4. Assess debt capacity relative to covenants and industry norms.
