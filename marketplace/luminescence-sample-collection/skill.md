# Luminescence Sample Collection

Collect sediment samples for luminescence dating under light-safe conditions and record the stratigraphic context.

## Inputs

- `sampling-site`: Exposed section or core with target dating horizons identified
- `sampling-protocol`: Opaque tube insertion method and field documentation requirements

## Outputs

- `collected-samples`: Sediment samples in opaque tubes with stratigraphic positions recorded
- `field-dosimetry-data`: In-situ gamma spectrometry or dosimeter placement records

## Process

1. Drive opaque metal or PVC tubes horizontally into the cleaned section face
2. Seal both ends immediately to prevent light exposure
3. Record the stratigraphic position, depth below surface, and water table level
4. Measure the field gamma dose rate with a portable spectrometer or install dosimeters
