# Annotator Trainer

Train the annotation team on the schema and tools.

## Inputs

- `annotation-guidelines`: Labeling instructions
- `tool-documentation`: Tool usage guide

## Outputs

- `training-record`: Who was trained and their qualification scores
- `calibration-set`: Gold standard examples for ongoing calibration

## Process

1. Walk annotators through the guidelines and examples.
2. Run a qualification test on the calibration set.
3. Review errors and clarify edge cases.
4. Certify annotators who meet the quality threshold.
