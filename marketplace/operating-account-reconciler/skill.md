# Operating Account Reconciler

Reconcile the property operating account.

## Inputs

- `income-data`: Income by category
- `expense-data`: Expenses by category

## Outputs

- `reconciled-balance`: Reconciled bank balance with book balance
- `reconciliation-adjustments`: Any adjustments identified during reconciliation

## Process

1. Compare the bank statement against the book records.
2. Identify and resolve discrepancies.
3. Record any adjustments needed.
