# Training Content Developer

Develop training content covering phishing, social engineering, data handling, and incident reporting.

## Inputs

- `learning-objectives`: Objectives to teach
- `role-curriculum-map`: Structure of the curriculum

## Outputs

- `training-modules`: Content modules organized by topic
- `exercise-bank`: Practical exercises and scenarios

## Process

1. Create content modules with real-world examples
2. Build interactive exercises and scenario-based learning
3. Develop role-specific content for specialists
4. Include current threat examples from the organization's sector
