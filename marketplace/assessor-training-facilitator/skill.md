# Assessor Training Facilitator

Train assessors on applying the competency rubrics consistently.

## Inputs

- `task-rubrics`: Scoring rubrics for each task
- `collection-procedures`: Evidence collection procedures

## Outputs

- `trained-assessors`: Record of assessors who completed calibration training
- `competency-assessment-framework`: Complete competency assessment framework document

## Process

1. Conduct calibration sessions using sample evidence
2. Practice scoring and discuss disagreements
3. Verify inter-rater reliability meets the target threshold
4. Certify trained assessors and compile the framework document
