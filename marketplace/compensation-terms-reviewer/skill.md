# Compensation Terms Reviewer

Review compensation, benefits, and equity terms in the agreement.

## Inputs

- `agreement-text`: Full text of the employment agreement
- `position-details`: Role, level, and market context

## Outputs

- `comp-analysis`: Analysis of compensation and benefits terms
- `equity-terms`: Stock option or equity grant terms and vesting schedule

## Process

1. Extract base salary, bonus, and commission structures
2. Review equity terms including vesting, acceleration, and exercise windows
3. Compare against market benchmarks for the role and level
