# Stress Type Classification

Classify each logged episode as acute, chronic, or anticipatory, because each type responds to a different tool.

## Inputs

- `stress-log`: The record of stress episodes
- `trigger-list`: The recurring triggers

## Outputs

- `classified-stressors`: Each episode and trigger tagged as acute, chronic, or anticipatory
- `type-distribution`: The share of your stress that falls into each type

## Process

1. Tag acute stress: a sharp spike tied to a specific event that passes, such as a near miss in traffic or a tense exchange.
2. Tag chronic stress: a steady background load from an ongoing source, such as an overloaded schedule, a strained relationship, or money pressure.
3. Tag anticipatory stress: worry about something that has not happened yet, such as a looming deadline, conversation, or appointment.
4. Tally the distribution. A person mostly carrying chronic stress needs structural change, while one spiking on acute events needs reset tools, and the mix tells you where to invest.
