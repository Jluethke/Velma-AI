# High Potential Identifier

Identify high-potential employees for accelerated development.

## Inputs

- `bench-strength-map`: Bench strength
- `critical-roles`: Critical roles needing successors

## Outputs

- `high-potential-list`: Identified high-potential employees
- `development-needs`: Development gaps for each high-potential

## Process

1. Evaluate candidates on performance, potential, and aspiration
2. Use consistent criteria across the organization
3. Identify development gaps relative to target roles
4. Validate the list with senior leadership
