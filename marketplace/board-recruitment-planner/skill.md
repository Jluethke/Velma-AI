# Board Recruitment Planner

Recruit board members to fill skill and perspective gaps.

## Inputs

- `role-descriptions`: Board position descriptions
- `current-board-roster`: Existing board members and their skills

## Outputs

- `skills-gap-matrix`: Skills needed vs. skills on the current board
- `prospect-list`: Candidates to recruit with approach strategy

## Process

1. Map current board skills (finance, legal, marketing, fundraising, program expertise)
2. Identify gaps in skills, demographics, and community connections
3. Build a prospect list with cultivation approach for each
4. Prioritize recruitment based on the most pressing gaps
