# Player Registration Collector

Collect player registrations with personal information, waivers, and supporting documents.

## Inputs

- `team-name`: The team being managed
- `roster-requirements`: League rules for roster size, age, and documentation

## Outputs

- `registration-submissions`: All submitted player registrations with documents
- `missing-items-list`: Players with incomplete submissions

## Process

1. Distribute the registration form (personal info, emergency contact, medical, waivers).
2. Collect completed forms and supporting documents (birth certificate, proof of residency).
3. Flag incomplete submissions and follow up for missing items.
4. Compile all submissions into the registration file.
