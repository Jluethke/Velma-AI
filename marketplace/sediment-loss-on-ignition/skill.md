# Sediment Loss On Ignition

Measure organic and carbonate content by loss-on-ignition at two temperatures.

## Inputs

- `subsamples`: Dried and weighed subsamples at each depth

## Outputs

- `loi-results`: Organic matter (550C) and carbonate (950C) percentages per depth
- `loi-profile`: Downcore plot of organic and carbonate content

## Process

1. Dry subsamples at 105C overnight and record dry weight.
2. Combust at 550C for 4 hours and record weight loss (organic matter).
3. Combust at 950C for 2 hours and record further weight loss (carbonate).
4. Calculate percentages and plot the downcore profiles.
