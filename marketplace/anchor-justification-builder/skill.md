# Anchor Justification Builder

Build the justification for the anchor. The anchor's power comes from the story that accompanies it, not the number alone.

## Inputs

- `anchor-position`: The first offer
- `market-range`: The supporting data

## Outputs

- `anchor-justification`: The data and reasoning that support the anchor position: comparable transactions, unique value, market conditions, timing factors
- `anchor-delivery-script`: How to present the anchor: the opening statement that frames the number with its justification

## Process

1. Select the 2-3 strongest data points that support the anchor. Quality of evidence matters more than quantity.
2. Build the narrative: explain why the anchor is the right starting point given the specific circumstances. Reference comparables that favor your position.
3. Prepare the delivery: state the anchor confidently and immediately provide the justification. 'Based on [data], [comparable], and [factor], I am proposing [anchor].'
4. Anticipate the counter-anchor: what will they say? Prepare responses to their likely objections.
