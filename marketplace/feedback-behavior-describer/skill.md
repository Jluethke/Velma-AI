# Feedback Behavior Describer

Describe the behavior using only what was observable. No interpretation of intent, no adjectives, no generalizations. Behavior is what a camera would record.

## Inputs

- `situation-anchor`: When and where the behavior happened
- `behavior-to-address`: What you want to address

## Outputs

- `behavior-description`: The observable behavior, free of interpretation
- `intent-assumptions-removed`: Interpretations you removed from the description (saved so you can check yourself)

## Process

1. Write what the person did using verbs, not adjectives. 'Interrupted the presenter twice during slides 3 and 7' not 'was rude.'
2. Remove every attribution of intent. You do not know their intent. You know their behavior.
3. Remove every generalization. 'You interrupted twice' not 'you always interrupt.'
4. Test: could the person agree that this is what happened, even if they disagree about whether it was a problem? If yes, the description is clean.
