# Investment Account Setup

Choose the right account types (401k, IRA, taxable) and platform based on goals and tax situation.

## Inputs

- `employer_benefits`: 401k match and plan details
- `income_level`: Annual income for tax bracket
- `investment_goals`: Retirement, house, education, etc.

## Outputs

- `account_plan`: Which accounts to open and priority order
- `platform_recommendation`: Brokerage platform recommendation
- `tax_optimization`: Roth vs traditional analysis

## Process

1. Capture full employer match first
2. Analyze Roth vs traditional based on current tax bracket
3. Choose additional account types by goal
4. Compare brokerage platforms on fees and features
5. Document account opening steps
