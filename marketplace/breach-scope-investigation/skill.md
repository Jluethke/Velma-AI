# Breach Scope Investigation

Investigate the full scope of the breach including timeline and access patterns.

## Inputs

- `breach-confirmation`: Confirmed breach details
- `data-inventory`: Types and volumes of exposed data

## Outputs

- `scope-findings`: Full scope including timeline, access patterns, and attacker methods
- `affected-individual-count`: Number and categories of affected data subjects

## Process

1. Analyze logs to determine the earliest point of unauthorized access
2. Map all systems and data stores the attacker accessed
3. Identify the exfiltration method and volume of data taken
4. Determine the number and categories of affected individuals
