# Incentive Mechanics Builder

Choose the incentive mechanics, accelerators, and SPIFs.

## Inputs

- `pay-mix-structure`: OTE and pay mix by role
- `revenue-model`: Revenue types and priorities

## Outputs

- `incentive-plan`: Mechanics including rates, accelerators, and decelerators
- `spif-calendar`: Planned sales performance incentive funds

## Process

1. Set the commission rate or bonus formula for each role.
2. Design accelerators for above-quota performance.
3. Define decelerators or floors for below-quota performance.
4. Plan any time-bound SPIFs for strategic priorities.
