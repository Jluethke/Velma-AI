# Expense Audit Summarizer

Produce the expense audit summary with findings and recommendations.

## Inputs

- `policy-exceptions`: Policy exceptions found
- `duplicate-flags`: Duplicate expenses detected

## Outputs

- `expense-audit-summary`: Summary of audit findings and statistics
- `policy-recommendations`: Recommendations for policy updates

## Process

1. Summarize the total expenses reviewed, approved, and denied.
2. List the top policy violations by category and frequency.
3. Calculate the savings from denied or reduced claims.
4. Recommend policy updates to address recurring issues.
