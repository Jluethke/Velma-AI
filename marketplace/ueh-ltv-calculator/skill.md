# Ueh Ltv Calculator

Compute the lifetime value of a unit.

## Inputs

- `contribution-margin`: The margin each unit contributes
- `unit-model`: The unit and its retention behavior

## Outputs

- `ltv-value`: The lifetime value of one unit
- `retention-assumptions`: The retention or repeat assumptions behind the estimate

## Process

1. Estimate the average lifespan of a unit from retention or repeat rate.
2. Multiply the contribution margin by the expected lifespan.
3. State the retention assumptions behind the estimate.
