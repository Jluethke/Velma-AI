# Purple Team Report Writer

Compile the purple team exercise report with coverage metrics, gap analysis, and improvement outcomes.

## Inputs

- `detection-scorecard`: Initial detection scorecard
- `validation-results`: Post-tuning validation results
- `remaining-gaps`: Gaps still remaining after tuning

## Outputs

- `purple-team-report`: Full purple team exercise report
- `detection-improvement-delta`: Before and after detection coverage comparison

## Process

1. Summarize the exercise scope, techniques tested, and methodology
2. Present the before and after detection coverage percentages
3. Document each gap found, its root cause, and the fix applied
4. List remaining gaps with a remediation timeline and owner
