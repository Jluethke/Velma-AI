# Procurement Scaling Planner

Plan procurement or auto-scaling to meet projected capacity needs within budget constraints.

## Inputs

- `scenario-models`: Capacity models for each scenario
- `scaling-recommendations`: Recommended scaling actions

## Outputs

- `procurement-plan`: Plan for acquiring or scaling infrastructure
- `cost-estimate`: Estimated cost of the capacity plan

## Process

1. Determine which resources need vertical vs horizontal scaling
2. Price the scaling options (reserved instances, spot, on-demand)
3. Build the procurement timeline aligned with growth milestones
4. Calculate the total cost estimate for the capacity plan
