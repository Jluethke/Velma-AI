# Feedback Follow Up Tracker

Track whether feedback produces change. Set a follow-up date, observe the behavior, and close the loop. If the same feedback is delivered three times with no change, escalate from behavioral feedback to structural change (process, role, environment).

## Inputs

- `change-request`: What you asked for
- `success-measure`: How you will know it changed

## Outputs

- `follow-up-plan`: When to check, what to observe, and what to do if change happened or did not happen
- `structural-escalation-criteria`: The threshold (3 instances of same feedback) at which you stop giving behavioral feedback and change the structure

## Process

1. Set a follow-up observation window: 2 weeks for behavioral changes, 1 month for habit changes.
2. During the window, observe the behavior against the success measure. Do not give more feedback during the window. Give the person time.
3. At follow-up: if the behavior changed, acknowledge it explicitly. Recognition of change reinforces change.
4. If the behavior did not change after 3 feedback cycles, the problem is not behavioral. Change the process, the role, the environment, or the team composition.
