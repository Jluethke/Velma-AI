# Dcf Opening And Nextsteps Builder

Assemble the conversation: a curiosity-led opening, the acknowledgment and impact in the middle, and a concrete proposal for next steps. The opening sets the frame, and a frame of joint problem-solving beats a frame of verdict-delivery every time.

## Inputs

- `curiosity-questions`: The open questions that invite their story
- `acknowledgment-statements`: The sentences that show you understood their feelings
- `impact-statements`: The effect on you, free of intent claims
- `grounding-reframes`: The reframes that keep you steady under identity threat

## Outputs

- `conversation-opening`: An opening line that names the shared problem and invites their view before you deliver yours
- `proposed-next-steps`: A concrete, jointly framed proposal for what changes and how you both check it worked

## Process

1. Write an opening that states the purpose as a shared problem and signals you want to understand their view, not just state yours. 'I want to talk about the launch and understand how you saw it' opens, 'we need to talk about your mistakes' closes.
2. Sequence the middle: lead with a curiosity question, listen and acknowledge, then offer your impact. Delivering impact before listening tells them the outcome was decided before they spoke.
3. Draft next steps as a proposal, not a decree, and leave room for them to shape it. Ownership of the fix predicts whether the fix holds.
4. End by naming how you will both know it worked and when you will check. A next step with no check is a hope.
