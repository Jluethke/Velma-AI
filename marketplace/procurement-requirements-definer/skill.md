# Procurement Requirements Definer

Define procurement requirements and specifications.

## Inputs

- `material-needs`: Materials and quantities needed
- `quality-specifications`: Quality and performance specifications

## Outputs

- `procurement-brief`: Procurement brief with specifications, quantities, and timeline
- `evaluation-criteria`: Criteria for evaluating supplier quotes

## Process

1. Document the material specifications and acceptance criteria
2. Define the quantity, delivery schedule, and lead time requirements
3. Set the budget and payment terms
4. Establish the evaluation criteria (price, quality, lead time, reliability)
