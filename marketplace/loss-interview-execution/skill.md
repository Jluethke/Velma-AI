# Loss Interview Execution

Interview each lost prospect using a structured protocol that makes honesty easy and avoids defensiveness.

## Inputs

- `interview-candidates`: Lost deals to interview
- `stated-reasons`: CRM loss reasons for comparison

## Outputs

- `interview-notes`: Verbatim notes from each interview, capturing the real reason, the decision process, and what would have changed the outcome
- `real-reasons`: The actual reason each deal was lost, as stated by the buyer (not your interpretation)

## Process

1. Open the conversation by making honesty safe: 'I appreciate you taking this call. We are genuinely trying to improve. The more honest you are, the more it helps us. Nothing you say will be taken personally.'
2. Ask the core questions in order: (1) Walk me through how you made this decision. (2) What were the top 3 factors? (3) Where did we fall short compared to the option you chose? (4) Was there anything we could have done differently that would have changed the outcome?
3. Listen without defending. When the prospect says something that stings, write it down verbatim instead of explaining why they are wrong. The instinct to defend destroys the honesty.
4. After each interview, record the real reason (in their words) and compare it to the stated CRM reason. The gap between the two is your data quality problem.
