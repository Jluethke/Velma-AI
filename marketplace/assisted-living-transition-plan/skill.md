# Assisted Living Transition Plan

Plan the physical and emotional transition to assisted living: downsizing, moving logistics, and adjustment support.

## Inputs

- `move_date`: Target move date
- `current_home`: Current living situation
- `emotional_readiness`: Resident's feelings about the move

## Outputs

- `downsizing_plan`: What to bring, store, donate, or sell
- `move_logistics`: Moving timeline and task list
- `adjustment_support`: Plan for emotional adjustment period

## Process

1. Measure new space and plan what fits
2. Create keep, donate, sell, and store categories
3. Schedule the move with movers or family help
4. Set up new space with familiar items
5. Plan first-week visit schedule for family
