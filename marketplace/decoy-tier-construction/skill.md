# Decoy Tier Construction

Construct the middle decoy tier and set its price to anchor the premium tier as the smart buy.

## Inputs

- `tier-map`: The map of current tiers
- `value-metric`: The metric buyers compare on

## Outputs

- `decoy-tier`: The middle tier defined by name, price, and intended role
- `anchor-rationale`: Why this price and position make the premium tier look like the better value

## Process

1. Set the decoy price near two and a half times the basic tier, inside the range the audit showed buyers accept.
2. Position the decoy so that the step from decoy to premium looks small next to the value it adds.
3. Write the anchor rationale: the decoy exists to reframe the premium price, not mainly to be sold.
4. Sanity check that the decoy is still a fair offer on its own, since an obviously bad tier erodes trust.
