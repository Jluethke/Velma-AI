# Npm Batna Definer

Define your BATNA, the best alternative to a negotiated agreement, and estimate theirs. Your BATNA is what you do if this deal falls through, and it sets the floor on what you should accept. Knowing both BATNAs tells you who holds the leverage before a word is spoken.

## Inputs

- `negotiation-context`: What is being negotiated, with whom, and what each side wants from it
- `counterparty`: Who the other side is and what you know about their situation and constraints

## Outputs

- `your-batna`: Your best alternative if no agreement is reached, stated concretely
- `their-batna-estimate`: Your best estimate of the other side's alternative, with the evidence behind the estimate

## Process

1. Write your BATNA as a concrete action, not a feeling: 'If we do not agree, we sign with the other vendor at their quoted rate.'
2. Estimate their BATNA from what you know of their options, their timeline, and their pressures. Guessing is fine as long as you mark it as a guess.
3. Compare the two. The side with the stronger BATNA walks in with more leverage, and knowing which side that is changes how you play.
4. If you cannot name your own BATNA, you are negotiating blind and will accept a bad deal out of fear of no deal.
