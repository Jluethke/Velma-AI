# Switching Cost Architect

At each micro-commitment, something accumulates that makes leaving cost more than staying. This is not about trapping people. It is about building genuine value that happens to compound.

## Inputs

- `revised-journey`: Journey with micro-commitments inserted
- `micro-commitment-designs`: What happens at each step

## Outputs

- `accumulated-value-map`: At each step in the journey, what the person has built up that they would lose by leaving (knowledge, customization, relationships, progress, data)
- `natural-lock-in-points`: The steps where accumulated value is highest and the person would naturally choose to continue rather than start over elsewhere

## Process

1. At each step, identify what accumulates: data they have entered, preferences they have set, progress they have made, relationships they have formed, knowledge they have gained, or reputation they have built.
2. The accumulation must be genuine value, not artificial barriers. 'I have 6 months of data here' is genuine. 'They make it hard to export my data' is artificial and breeds resentment.
3. Identify the natural lock-in points: the stages where so much value has accumulated that leaving would mean starting over. These are where the big ask can be placed, because the person is already invested.
4. Design the big ask (the final desired outcome) to land just after a natural lock-in point. The person has already committed time, attention, and identity. The big ask feels like a natural next step, not a jump.
