# Messaging Filter Application

Run every piece of current messaging through the anti-persona filter. If the anti-persona would find it appealing, it is too generic.

## Inputs

- `sharpened-ideal-profile`: Refined ideal customer profile
- `differentiating-traits`: Traits that separate ideal from anti-persona
- `anti-persona-profile`: Who you are not for

## Outputs

- `filtered-messaging`: Revised messaging that speaks directly to the ideal profile and would not attract the anti-persona
- `removed-generic-claims`: Claims, features, and benefits that were removed because they appealed equally to both profiles

## Process

1. Collect every piece of current messaging: website copy, ads, sales decks, email sequences, social bios.
2. For each claim or benefit, ask: would the anti-persona find this appealing? If yes, it is too generic. Flag it.
3. Rewrite flagged claims to speak specifically to the differentiating traits. Replace 'for everyone' language with 'for people who' language.
4. List what was removed. These removed items are your competitors' messaging territory, and you are choosing not to fight there.
