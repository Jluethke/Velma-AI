# Bid Decision Maker

Make a bid/no-bid decision based on fit and probability of winning.

## Inputs

- `requirements-matrix`: Capability mapping
- `gap-analysis`: Unmet requirements

## Outputs

- `bid-decision`: Go or no-go with rationale
- `win-probability`: Estimated probability of winning

## Process

1. Score the fit based on requirements coverage.
2. Assess the competitive landscape for this bid.
3. Estimate the cost of responding versus the expected value.
4. Make and document the bid/no-bid decision.
