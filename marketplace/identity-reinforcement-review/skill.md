# Identity Reinforcement Review

Review the vote tally to judge how firmly the identity has formed and choose the next identity to build.

## Inputs

- `vote-tracker`: The accumulated tally of votes
- `conflict-resolution-plan`: How conflicting identities were handled

## Outputs

- `identity-strength-assessment`: A judgment of how settled the identity is, based on vote count and whether it now feels true
- `next-identity-target`: The next identity to begin building, or a decision to deepen the current one

## Process

1. Read the vote count. A long unbroken tally means the identity has evidence behind it even on days it does not feel true.
2. Ask whether the behavior has started to feel like who you are rather than something you make yourself do. That shift is the real marker of a formed identity.
3. If the identity is settled, you can scale the action upward or begin a second identity. If it is still fragile, keep the vote small and protect the streak.
4. Choose the next identity so it reinforces the first rather than competing with it, so the identities stack rather than split your attention.
