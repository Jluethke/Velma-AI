# Content Pillar Definer

Define content pillars and posting frequency.

## Inputs

- `social-audit-report`: Current performance and audience insights
- `business-goals`: Objectives the content should support

## Outputs

- `content-pillars`: 3 to 5 content themes with rationale
- `posting-schedule`: Frequency and best times by platform

## Process

1. Define 3 to 5 content pillars aligned with audience interests and business goals
2. Assign a percentage mix across pillars
3. Research optimal posting times for each platform
4. Set the weekly posting frequency by platform
