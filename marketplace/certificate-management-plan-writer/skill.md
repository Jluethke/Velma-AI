# Certificate Management Plan Writer

Produce the certificate management plan documenting the CA hierarchy, renewal automation, monitoring, and revocation procedures.

## Inputs

- `certificate-inventory`: Complete inventory
- `automation-configuration`: Renewal setup
- `revocation-procedures`: Revocation runbooks
- `rotation-test-results`: Test outcomes

## Outputs

- `certificate-management-plan`: Complete plan covering inventory, renewal, monitoring, revocation, and rotation
- `ca-hierarchy-diagram`: Visual diagram of the certificate authority trust chain

## Process

1. Document the CA hierarchy and trust relationships
2. Summarize the renewal automation coverage and manual procedures
3. Include the monitoring and alerting configuration
4. Attach the revocation runbooks and rotation test results
