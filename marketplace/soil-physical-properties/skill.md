# Soil Physical Properties

Measure soil physical properties: texture, bulk density, and water holding capacity.

## Inputs

- `prepared-samples`: Sieved soil samples ready for physical testing
- `field-bulk-density-cores`: Intact cores for bulk density measurement

## Outputs

- `physical-results`: Texture class, bulk density, porosity, and water holding capacity
- `texture-triangle-position`: Sand/silt/clay percentages plotted on the texture triangle

## Process

1. Determine particle size distribution by hydrometer or pipette method.
2. Classify texture using the USDA texture triangle.
3. Measure bulk density from intact core weight and volume.
4. Determine water holding capacity at field capacity and permanent wilting point.
