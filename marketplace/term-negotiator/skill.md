# Term Negotiator

Negotiate the high-impact terms. For each term, know the market standard, your preferred position, and your walk-away.

## Inputs

- `negotiation-focus-list`: The terms to negotiate
- `deal-priorities`: Your priorities

## Outputs

- `term-positions`: For each high-impact term: the market standard, your preferred position, your reservation position, and the justification for your preferred position
- `negotiation-sequence`: The order to negotiate terms: start with terms where agreement is easiest to build momentum

## Process

1. For each high-impact term, research the market standard: what do comparable contracts include? This is your baseline.
2. Set your preferred position: what would make this term most valuable to you? Set your reservation position: the worst acceptable version of this term.
3. Prepare the justification: why is your preferred position reasonable? Reference market data, comparable deals, or risk analysis.
4. Negotiate easier terms first. Agreement builds momentum and relationship. Save the hardest terms for after you have established a collaborative dynamic.
