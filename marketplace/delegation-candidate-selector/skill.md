# Delegation Candidate Selector

Narrow the candidate pool to the tasks worth delegating first. Not every low-value task is ready to hand off; some need documentation, and some are too tangled with judgment to transfer cleanly yet.

## Inputs

- `scored-task-grid`: The scored two-by-two grid
- `delegation-candidates`: The pool of candidate tasks

## Outputs

- `delegation-shortlist`: The tasks to delegate now, ranked by the time each frees
- `retained-tasks`: The tasks kept, confirmed as genuinely high-expertise or high-value

## Process

1. From the candidate pool, flag tasks that recur often enough that delegating them frees real time.
2. Set aside tasks that cannot be delegated until they are documented, and note what each needs.
3. Confirm the retained tasks are genuinely high-expertise or high-value, not just tasks you enjoy.
4. Produce a shortlist of tasks to delegate now, ranked by the time each frees.
