# Dei Gap Prioritizer

Prioritize DEI gaps by impact and tractability.

## Inputs

- `representation-report`: Representation data
- `policy-review-findings`: Policy review findings
- `pay-equity-results`: Pay equity results

## Outputs

- `prioritized-gaps`: Ranked list of gaps to address
- `dei-roadmap`: Roadmap with timelines and owners for each priority

## Process

1. Rank gaps by magnitude of disparity and business impact
2. Assess the tractability of each gap (quick wins vs systemic changes)
3. Assign owners and timelines for each priority
4. Set measurable targets for progress
