# Article 30 Record Builder

Inventory all processing activities as required by GDPR Article 30.

## Inputs

- `organization-profile`: Organization structure and business units
- `system-inventory`: Systems processing personal data

## Outputs

- `processing-register`: Complete Article 30 record of processing activities
- `processing-gaps`: Activities not yet documented in the register

## Process

1. Survey each business unit for processing activities
2. Document purpose, categories of data, recipients, and retention for each
3. Identify activities missing from the current register
4. Compile into the Article 30 required format
