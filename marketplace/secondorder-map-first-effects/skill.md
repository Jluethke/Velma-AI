# Secondorder Map First Effects

List the direct, near-term effects of the decision, the ones that follow right away.

## Inputs

- `decision-defined`: The decision stated as a clear choice.
- `obvious-effect`: The immediate result most people would expect.

## Outputs

- `first-order-effects`: The direct, near-term effects of the decision.
- `first-order-notes`: Which first-order effects are good and which are bad.

## Process

1. List every effect that follows directly from the action.
2. Keep these to near-term results that need no further chain of events.
3. Note which first-order effects are good and which are bad.
