# Status Report Structure Designer

Design the status report structure: what is reported, in what order, and at what frequency.

## Inputs

- `audience`: Who reads the status report and what decisions they make from it
- `initiative-description`: What is being reported on (a project, a team, an operation, a personal goal)

## Outputs

- `report-structure`: The report template: headline status (green/yellow/red), exceptions (what changed, what is blocked, what needs a decision), metrics (3-5 key measures), and upcoming milestones
- `reporting-frequency`: How often the report is sent, based on the rate of change and the audience's decision cadence

## Process

1. Define the headline status: green (on track, no action needed), yellow (at risk, action may be needed), red (off track, action needed now). The reader should know the overall state in one glance.
2. Define the exception section: what changed since the last report? What is blocked and needs help? What decisions need to be made by the reader? This is the section that matters. If there are no exceptions, the report is: 'Green. No exceptions. Next milestone: [date].'
3. Define the metrics: 3-5 numbers that the reader can track over time. Choose metrics that are leading (predict future state) not lagging (report past state).
4. Set the frequency: match it to the audience's decision cadence. If they make weekly decisions, report weekly. If they review monthly, report monthly. More frequent than the decision cadence is noise.
