# Netgap Zero Connection Finder

Find the regions where you have zero connections: the industries, roles, communities, and worlds your network does not touch at all. These blind spots are invisible from inside your network precisely because no one in it can point them out to you.

## Inputs

- `cluster-map`: Your network grouped into clusters
- `cluster-labels`: Labels, sizes, and internal density of each cluster

## Outputs

- `zero-connection-regions`: The worlds you have no path into: industries, functions, seniority levels, geographies, or communities absent from your clusters
- `missing-cluster-list`: The specific clusters that should plausibly exist in your network given your goals but do not

## Process

1. Step back from the clusters you have and ask what is not there. List the industries, professional functions, seniority levels, geographies, and communities that are absent entirely from your map.
2. Reason from your goals to the clusters you would expect to need. If a goal depends on reaching a world (a new industry, a type of role, a funding source) and no cluster touches it, that is a structural gap, not a minor omission.
3. Accept that this step is inherently hard, because you are trying to see what you cannot see. Use external references (industry maps, org structures, who normally surrounds a goal like yours) to surface worlds you would not think of unprompted.
4. Distinguish gaps that matter from gaps that do not. You do not need to know everyone in every field. A missing cluster is only a real gap if it sits between you and something you want or need to hear about.
