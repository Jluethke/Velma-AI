# Lending Product Definer

Define the loan products, terms, and eligibility criteria.

## Inputs

- `target-market`: Borrower segment and geography
- `capital-constraints`: Available capital, cost of funds, and risk appetite

## Outputs

- `product-catalog`: Defined loan products with rates, terms, fees, and eligibility criteria
- `pricing-model`: Rate setting methodology and risk-based pricing tiers

## Process

1. Define each loan product (type, amount range, term range, rate range).
2. Set the eligibility criteria (credit score, income, DTI, geographic).
3. Build the pricing model with risk-based tiers.
