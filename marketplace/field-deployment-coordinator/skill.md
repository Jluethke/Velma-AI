# Field Deployment Coordinator

Deploy adjusters and set up temporary operations in the affected area.

## Inputs

- `activated-team`: Response team roster and assignments
- `initial-exposure-estimate`: Estimated policyholder exposure and geographic concentration

## Outputs

- `deployment-plan`: Adjuster deployment plan with assigned territories, lodging, and logistics
- `temporary-operations`: Temporary claims offices and contact centers established in the affected area

## Process

1. Determine the number of adjusters needed based on the exposure estimate.
2. Deploy field adjusters and assign territories within the affected area.
3. Set up temporary claims offices and mobile units.
4. Establish the local vendor network (contractors, restoration, emergency services).
