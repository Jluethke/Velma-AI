# Volunteer Screener Onboarder

Screen and onboard new volunteers with orientation and training.

## Inputs

- `applicant-pool`: Volunteer applicants
- `volunteer-needs`: Roles and requirements

## Outputs

- `approved-volunteers`: Screened and onboarded volunteers
- `onboarding-records`: Completed orientation and training records

## Process

1. Screen applicants (background checks where required, reference checks)
2. Conduct orientation covering mission, policies, and expectations
3. Provide role-specific training
4. Collect signed agreements and emergency contact information
