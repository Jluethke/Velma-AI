# Proposal Pricing Builder

Build the pricing for the proposal based on the approach and effort estimate.

## Inputs

- `proposed-approach`: Engagement approach and phases
- `effort-estimate`: Effort by phase and role

## Outputs

- `pricing-model`: The pricing structure with total, phase breakdown, and payment schedule
- `value-justification`: How the pricing relates to the expected ROI or value delivered

## Process

1. Apply the rate card to the effort estimate to calculate the base price.
2. Decide whether to present as fixed-fee, T&M with a cap, or blended rate.
3. Structure the payment schedule aligned with milestones or phases.
4. Prepare the value justification linking the investment to the expected outcomes.
5. Set the discount or negotiation range if needed.
