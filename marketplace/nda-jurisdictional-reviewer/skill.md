# Nda Jurisdictional Reviewer

Review the NDA for enforceability in the relevant jurisdictions.

## Inputs

- `remedies-clause`: Remedies provisions
- `ci-definition`: Confidential information definition
- `term-provisions`: Term provisions

## Outputs

- `enforceability-notes`: Jurisdictional enforceability assessment
- `nda-final-draft`: Final NDA draft ready for execution

## Process

1. Check non-compete elements for enforceability in the target state
2. Verify the term length is reasonable for the jurisdiction
3. Compile the final draft with all provisions assembled
