# Medical History Gatherer

Gather the patient's medical history and current medications.

## Inputs

- `demographic-record`: Structured demographic data
- `patient-responses`: Patient-reported history

## Outputs

- `medical-history`: Past medical, surgical, and family history
- `medication-list`: Current medications with doses and frequency

## Process

1. Record past medical and surgical history.
2. Document family medical history.
3. List all current medications including OTC and supplements.
