# End Of Life Financial Prep

Prepare finances for end of life: account access, bill payment continuity, and estate settlement readiness.

## Inputs

- `financial_accounts`: All financial accounts
- `recurring_bills`: Ongoing financial obligations
- `executor`: Person handling the estate

## Outputs

- `account_access_guide`: How executor accesses each account
- `bill_continuity_plan`: How bills get paid during settlement
- `estate_checklist`: Settlement steps and timeline

## Process

1. List all accounts with access methods
2. Identify automatic payments and subscriptions
3. Ensure executor is listed appropriately
4. Plan bill payment continuity
5. Create estate settlement task list
