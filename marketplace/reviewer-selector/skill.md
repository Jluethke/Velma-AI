# Reviewer Selector

Select the reviewers for each participant based on their working relationships.

## Inputs

- `competency-framework`: The competencies and behaviors being evaluated
- `role-levels`: Participants and their roles

## Outputs

- `reviewer-assignments`: For each participant: self, manager, peers, and direct reports selected as reviewers
- `reviewer-guidelines`: Instructions for reviewers on how to provide useful feedback

## Process

1. For each participant, identify their manager, 3 to 5 peers, and all direct reports as reviewers.
2. Include self-assessment as a required component.
3. Verify each reviewer has sufficient working interaction to provide meaningful ratings.
4. Prepare reviewer guidelines explaining the purpose, scale, and confidentiality of the process.
