# Enhanced Due Diligence Reviewer

Conduct enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers and produce the KYC record.

## Inputs

- `risk-rating`: Customer risk rating
- `risk-factors`: Contributing risk factors

## Outputs

- `edd-report`: Enhanced due diligence findings and recommendation
- `kyc-record`: Complete KYC record with all documentation and decisions

## Process

1. For high-risk customers, collect additional information (source of wealth, source of funds, business purpose).
2. Conduct deeper background research and reference checks.
3. Document the due diligence findings and make the accept or decline recommendation.
4. Compile the complete KYC record for the compliance file.
