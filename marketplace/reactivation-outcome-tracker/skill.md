# Reactivation Outcome Tracker

Track reactivation outcomes to improve future efforts: which strategies work, which contacts come back, and what the overall reactivation rate tells you about your relationship maintenance habits.

## Inputs

- `reactivation-plans`: Strategies assigned to each segment
- `release-criteria`: When to stop reactivation attempts

## Outputs

- `reactivation-metrics`: Response rate by segment, reactivation rate (returned to active), time-to-reactivation, and cost per reactivated contact
- `root-cause-analysis`: Why contacts went dormant in the first place (unfulfilled promises, life changes, relationship ran its course, you stopped reaching out) to prevent future dormancy

## Process

1. Track 4 metrics: response rate (how many responded at all), reactivation rate (how many returned to active engagement), time-to-reactivation (days from first outreach to re-engagement), and persistence (how many attempts it took). Break these down by RFV segment to see which tier has the best return on effort.
2. Analyze the non-responders: look for patterns. Did they all go dormant around the same time (suggesting an external event, not relationship decay)? Were they all in the same lifecycle stage (suggesting your maintenance at that stage is insufficient)? Were they all on the same channel (suggesting channel decay, not relationship decay)?
3. Conduct the root cause analysis on successful reactivations. Ask (or infer): why did the relationship go dormant? Common causes: you stopped initiating (a maintenance failure), they had a life or job change (natural transition), an unresolved issue created friction (a repair opportunity), the relationship simply ran its course (acceptable loss). Each cause implies a different prevention strategy.
4. Feed results back into the lifecycle engine: if high-frequency contacts go dormant at a predictable rate, your decay windows may be too generous. If contacts in a particular cluster all go dormant together, there may be a structural issue in that part of your network. The reactivation data is a diagnostic tool for the entire relationship system.
