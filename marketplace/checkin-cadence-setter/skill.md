# Checkin Cadence Setter

Set the check-in cadence by the person's readiness, not by your anxiety. A person new to a task checks in often at first, then less as they prove reliable; a proven person checks in on results only.

## Inputs

- `authority-grants`: The authority given
- `escalation-rules`: The escalation rules set

## Outputs

- `checkin-schedule`: The check-in frequency and focus for each task, matched to readiness
- `cadence-rationale`: The plan to loosen the cadence as the person proves reliable, so they can see the path to full ownership

## Process

1. For a person new to a task, schedule frequent short check-ins early, focused on approach, then widen the interval as they show competence.
2. For a proven person, check in on outcomes at natural milestones, not on method.
3. Make check-ins about removing obstacles and confirming direction, not inspecting every step.
4. Record the cadence and the plan to loosen it, so the person can see the path to full ownership.
