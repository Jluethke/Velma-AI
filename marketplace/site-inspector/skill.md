# Site Inspector

Conduct the on-site inspection and document findings.

## Inputs

- `inspection-appointment`: Confirmed appointment details
- `inspection-checklist`: Customized inspection checklist

## Outputs

- `inspection-findings`: Documented findings from the site inspection with photos and notes
- `hazard-list`: Identified hazards with severity ratings and recommended corrective actions

## Process

1. Tour the premises following the inspection checklist.
2. Document conditions with photos and field notes.
3. Interview the site contact about operations, maintenance, and safety practices.
4. Identify hazards and rate their severity.
