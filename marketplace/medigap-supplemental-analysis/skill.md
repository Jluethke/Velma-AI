# Medigap Supplemental Analysis

Evaluate Medigap supplemental insurance plans to cover Medicare gaps.

## Inputs

- `plan_choice`: Original Medicare or Advantage
- `health_status`: Current health conditions
- `budget`: Monthly premium budget

## Outputs

- `medigap_options`: Available Medigap plans with premiums
- `coverage_comparison`: What each plan covers vs gaps
- `recommendation`: Best plan for the situation

## Process

1. Identify if Medigap is needed or available
2. Compare Plan F, G, and N coverage
3. Get premium quotes from multiple insurers
4. Factor in guaranteed issue rights
5. Recommend based on health and budget
