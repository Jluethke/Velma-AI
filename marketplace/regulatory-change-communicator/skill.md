# Regulatory Change Communicator

Communicate regulatory changes to affected teams and update the compliance record.

## Inputs

- `implementation-plan`: Implementation plan
- `change-evidence`: Evidence of changes

## Outputs

- `communication-record`: Record of communications to affected teams
- `regulatory-change-record`: Complete record of the regulatory change lifecycle

## Process

1. Notify affected teams of the changes and new requirements.
2. Update training materials and procedures.
3. File the regulatory change record with all evidence.
4. Schedule follow-up monitoring.
