# Revenue Forecast Builder

Build the revenue forecast using bottom-up or top-down methods.

## Inputs

- `trend-analysis`: Historical revenue trends
- `sales-pipeline`: Current sales pipeline and booking projections

## Outputs

- `revenue-forecast`: Projected revenue by product line and period
- `revenue-assumptions`: Key assumptions underlying the revenue forecast

## Process

1. Build a bottom-up forecast from the sales pipeline and customer contracts.
2. Build a top-down forecast from market size and growth rate assumptions.
3. Reconcile the two approaches and select the final forecast basis.
4. Document all key assumptions (pricing, volume, churn, new business).
