# Seasonal Maintenance Checklist

Generate seasonal maintenance checklists for spring, summer, fall, and winter.

## Inputs

- `systems_inventory`: Home systems list
- `climate_zone`: Geographic climate type
- `home_type`: Property type

## Outputs

- `spring_checklist`: Spring maintenance tasks
- `summer_checklist`: Summer maintenance tasks
- `fall_checklist`: Fall maintenance tasks
- `winter_checklist`: Winter maintenance tasks

## Process

1. Map maintenance tasks to appropriate season
2. Adjust for climate zone
3. Prioritize by consequence of skipping
4. Estimate time per task
5. Flag professional-only items
