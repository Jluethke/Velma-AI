# Podcast Episode Planner

Plan the episode topic, outline, and guest logistics.

## Inputs

- `show-concept`: Podcast show description and target audience
- `episode-topic`: Topic or theme for this episode

## Outputs

- `episode-outline`: Structured outline with segments and talking points
- `guest-brief`: Guest information and interview questions if applicable

## Process

1. Research the topic and identify key angles
2. Structure the episode into segments with time targets
3. Prepare interview questions or talking points for each segment
4. Confirm guest availability and send the brief
