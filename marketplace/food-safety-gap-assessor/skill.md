# Food Safety Gap Assessor

Conduct a gap assessment against the selected standard.

## Inputs

- `applicable-standard`: Standard to comply with
- `current-practices`: Current farm operations and procedures

## Outputs

- `gap-assessment`: List of gaps between current practices and the standard
- `remediation-priorities`: Gaps ranked by risk and effort to close

## Process

1. Walk through each section of the standard against current practices.
2. Document gaps where current practices fall short.
3. Assess each gap for food safety risk.
4. Rank remediation priorities by risk and implementation difficulty.
