# Product System Builder

Build the approved product in the policy administration and rating systems.

## Inputs

- `filing-package`: Approved forms and rates
- `rating-algorithm`: The rating algorithm to implement

## Outputs

- `system-configuration`: The configured product in the administration and rating systems, tested and ready
- `product-design-document`: Complete product design document with coverage, rates, system configuration, and launch plan

## Process

1. Configure the policy forms and endorsements in the administration system.
2. Implement the rating algorithm in the rating engine.
3. Test the end-to-end quote, bind, and issue workflow with sample scenarios.
4. Document the product configuration and deliver the launch-ready product.
