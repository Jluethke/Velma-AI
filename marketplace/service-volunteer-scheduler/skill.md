# Service Volunteer Scheduler

Schedule the volunteers needed for this specific service (musicians, readers, ushers, tech).

## Inputs

- `order-of-service`: The sequence of service elements
- `content-assignments`: Who handles each element

## Outputs

- `volunteer-assignments`: Each volunteer role filled with a name and confirmation status
- `tech-requirements`: Audio, visual, and streaming needs for this service

## Process

1. List every volunteer role needed: sound operator, projector operator, live stream operator, greeters, ushers, nursery, communion servers.
2. Check the master volunteer schedule and fill each slot, confirming with assigned volunteers.
3. Identify the tech requirements: microphones, slides, video playback, live stream setup.
4. Send the volunteer assignments with arrival time and any special instructions.
