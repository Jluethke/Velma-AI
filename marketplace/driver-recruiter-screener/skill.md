# Driver Recruiter Screener

Recruit and screen commercial driver candidates.

## Inputs

- `driver-requirements`: Required qualifications (CDL class, endorsements, experience, clean record)
- `open-positions`: Number and type of driver positions to fill

## Outputs

- `screened-candidates`: Candidates who passed screening (MVR, background, drug test, road test)
- `screening-records`: Screening results and documentation for each candidate

## Process

1. Post the driver positions and source candidates.
2. Verify CDL validity and endorsements.
3. Run motor vehicle record (MVR) check, background check, and pre-employment drug screen.
4. Conduct a road test and evaluate driving skills.
