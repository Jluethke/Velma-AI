# Data Collection Before Opinions

Collect data before opinions. What actually happened (metrics, events, deliverables) vs. what people feel happened.

## Inputs

- `period-under-review`: The time period or project being reviewed
- `team-members`: The participants in the retrospective

## Outputs

- `objective-data`: Metrics, events, and deliverables from the period: what was delivered, what was late, what failed, what was blocked, what surprised
- `team-sentiment-data`: Each team member's responses to: 'What went well?', 'What was frustrating?', 'What would you change?' Collected individually before the group discussion.

## Process

1. Before the retrospective meeting, collect the objective data: velocity metrics, defect counts, deployment frequency, incident count, customer feedback, anything measurable.
2. Before the meeting, have each team member independently answer three questions: What went well? What was frustrating? What one thing would you change? Individual responses prevent groupthink.
3. Compile the data. Look for patterns: are multiple people frustrated by the same thing? Does the metric data confirm or contradict the sentiment?
4. Present the data at the start of the retrospective. Data first, then discussion. This anchors the conversation in reality instead of the loudest opinion.
