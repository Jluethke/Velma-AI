# Water Treatment Compliance And Operations

Establish regulatory compliance monitoring, residuals handling, and operational procedures.

## Inputs

- `process-sizing`: Designed treatment processes and their capabilities
- `chemical-requirements`: Chemical systems and expected dose ranges

## Outputs

- `compliance-monitoring-plan`: Monitoring schedule, parameters, locations, and reporting requirements per drinking water regulations
- `residuals-management`: Sludge quantities, treatment, and disposal method

## Process

1. Map regulatory requirements. US: Safe Drinking Water Act, EPA primary and secondary standards. Surface Water Treatment Rule: minimum 3-log Giardia, 4-log virus removal/inactivation. Disinfectants/DBP Rule: TTHM < 80 ug/L, HAA5 < 60 ug/L (Stage 2 DBPR). Lead and Copper Rule: action levels. State-specific requirements may be more stringent.
2. Design continuous monitoring. Turbidity: individual filter effluent turbidimeters (< 0.3 NTU in 95% of readings, never > 1 NTU for conventional). Combined filter effluent for compliance reporting. Chlorine residual: continuous analyzer at entry to distribution (free or total depending on disinfectant). pH, temperature at key process points. Flow measurement for CT calculation.
3. Design sampling program. Daily: turbidity, chlorine residual, pH. Monthly: coliform bacteria (total coliform rule: < 5% positive in distribution). Quarterly or annual (depending on system size): DBPs, inorganic chemicals, SOCs, VOCs, radionuclides. Establish sampling locations: raw water, settled water, filter effluent, clearwell, distribution system entry point, and distribution system sampling sites.
4. Estimate residuals. Sludge production from coagulation: 0.5 to 1.5 kg dry solids per m3 of water treated (depends on raw water turbidity and coagulant dose). Filter backwash water: 2 to 5% of plant production. Dewatering options: gravity thickening to 2 to 4% solids, then mechanical dewatering (belt filter press to 15 to 25%, centrifuge to 20 to 30%, or plate-and-frame press to 30 to 40%). Disposal: landfill (if passes TCLP for metals), land application, or sanitary sewer (check local limits).
5. Develop operating procedures. Startup sequence for each process unit. Chemical feed calibration procedure. Filter backwash initiation criteria (time, head loss, turbidity breakthrough). Emergency procedures: raw water quality event (increase coagulant, switch to backup source), filter breakthrough (take filter offline, investigate), loss of disinfection (boil water advisory protocol). Operator certification requirements per state.
