# Packaging Proof And Press Check

Coordinate the proof approval and press check with the printer.

## Inputs

- `print-ready-files`: The production-ready artwork
- `packaging-specs`: The physical specs for the printer

## Outputs

- `approved-proof`: The approved proof with sign-off
- `packaging-design-record`: Complete record of the packaging design (specs, concepts, artwork, proof)

## Process

1. Send the print-ready files to the printer with the specs.
2. Review the proof for color accuracy, registration, and text correctness.
3. Approve the proof or request corrections.
4. Attend the press check if color accuracy is critical.
5. Produce the approved proof and packaging design record.
