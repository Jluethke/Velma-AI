# Malpractice Sanctions Checker

Check malpractice history and disciplinary sanctions.

## Inputs

- `application-package`: Provider's documents
- `license-status`: Licensure verification results

## Outputs

- `malpractice-history`: Claims, settlements, and judgments
- `sanction-records`: Disciplinary actions from any state or federal body

## Process

1. Review the provider's self-reported malpractice history.
2. Query malpractice insurance carriers for claims history.
3. Check OIG exclusion list and state disciplinary databases.
