# Regulatory Impact Assessor

Assess the impact of regulatory changes on current operations.

## Inputs

- `change-summaries`: Summaries of regulatory changes
- `current-obligations`: Existing obligations

## Outputs

- `impact-assessment`: Assessment of operational, process, and system impacts
- `gap-analysis`: Gaps between current state and new requirements

## Process

1. Map each regulatory change to affected processes and controls.
2. Assess the magnitude of required changes (minor update vs major overhaul).
3. Identify gaps between current practices and new requirements.
4. Prioritize changes by effective date and risk.
