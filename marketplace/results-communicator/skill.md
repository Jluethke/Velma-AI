# Results Communicator

Communicate citizen science results back to volunteers and the public with accessible summaries, interactive maps, and acknowledgments.

## Inputs

- `aggregated-data`: Analysis results from the aggregation step
- `volunteer-list`: Participating volunteers with contribution counts
- `audience-targets`: Volunteers, general public, policymakers, or scientific community

## Outputs

- `public-summary`: Plain-language summary of key findings with visualizations
- `interactive-map`: Web map showing observations and results that volunteers can explore
- `acknowledgment-list`: Contributor acknowledgments formatted for publication and website display

## Process

1. Write a plain-language summary aimed at the volunteer reading level
2. Create visualizations (maps, charts, infographics) showing major findings
3. Build or update the project website and interactive map with current results
4. Send personalized thank-you messages to volunteers with their individual contribution stats
5. Prepare contributor acknowledgment lists for publications and reports
