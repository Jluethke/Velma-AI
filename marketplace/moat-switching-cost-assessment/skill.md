# Moat Switching Cost Assessment

Assess switching costs: how much time, money, or risk a customer takes on to leave for a competitor. High switching costs keep customers even when a rival is slightly better or cheaper.

## Inputs

- `business-profile`: A tight profile of the business model and customers
- `competitor-set`: The named set of real competitors

## Outputs

- `switching-cost-score`: A 0 to 5 score for customer switching cost
- `switching-cost-evidence`: What specifically makes leaving costly

## Process

1. Walk through what a customer must do to move to a competitor: data, retraining, contracts, integration, risk.
2. Estimate the real cost of each in time, money, and risk to the customer.
3. Ask whether the switching cost is rising or falling as the market matures.
4. Score 0 to 5 and record the specific sources of the cost.
