# Podcast Music Inserter

Add intro, outro, transitions, and background music.

## Inputs

- `edited-audio`: Cleaned episode audio
- `show-concept`: Show brand and music style

## Outputs

- `mixed-episode`: Episode with all music and branding elements
- `episode-duration`: Final episode length

## Process

1. Insert the show intro with theme music
2. Add transition stingers between segments
3. Mix background music at appropriate levels
4. Append the outro with calls to action
