# Digital Product Validator

Validate the digital product idea with the audience before building.

## Inputs

- `product-concept`: The product idea (what it is, who it is for, what problem it solves)
- `audience-data`: The creator's audience size, engagement, and expressed interests

## Outputs

- `validation-results`: Evidence of demand (survey responses, waitlist signups, pre-orders)
- `product-spec`: The validated product specification (scope, format, pricing)

## Process

1. Test the product concept with the audience (poll, survey, landing page with waitlist).
2. Analyze the response to gauge demand (signup rate, survey interest level).
3. Refine the product scope and format based on audience feedback.
4. Set the price based on the value proposition and audience willingness to pay.
