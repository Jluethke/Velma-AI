# Manual Report Inventory

Inventory all current manual reports and their characteristics.

## Inputs

- `report-owners`: People who currently produce manual reports
- `report-scope`: Which department or function to audit

## Outputs

- `report-catalog`: List of reports with frequency, audience, data sources, and time to produce
- `automation-candidates`: Reports ranked by automation potential (frequency times effort)

## Process

1. Interview each report owner about what they produce, how often, and how long it takes.
2. Catalog each report: name, frequency, audience, data sources, format, production time.
3. Rank by automation potential: high-frequency and high-effort reports first.
