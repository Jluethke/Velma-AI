# Feature Pricing Comparator

Compare features and pricing across competitors.

## Inputs

- `competitor-list`: Competitors to compare
- `product-description`: Own product for comparison

## Outputs

- `feature-comparison`: Feature matrix across competitors
- `pricing-comparison`: Pricing models and tiers across competitors

## Process

1. Build the feature comparison matrix
2. Document pricing models and tiers
3. Identify feature gaps and advantages
