# Skill Stack Inventory

List your current skills and the candidate skills you could realistically develop.

## Inputs

- `background-summary`: A short summary of your work history and projects
- `interest-areas`: Areas you are drawn to or want to grow into

## Outputs

- `skill-inventory`: A grouped list of skills you currently use
- `candidate-skill-list`: Skills you could realistically develop within a year or two

## Process

1. List every skill you currently use in work or projects, grouped by type.
2. Add candidate skills you could realistically reach within a year or two.
3. Record each skill with a short note on how you use or would use it.
4. Separate skills tied to your interest areas from the rest.
