# Quarterly Diet Review

Review the information diet quarterly. Sources that were high-signal six months ago may be noise now. New sources may deserve a trial. The diet is a living system, not a one-time setup.

## Inputs

- `daily-consumption-budget`: Current time budget
- `consumption-schedule`: Current consumption schedule and cutoff rules

## Outputs

- `diet-review-results`: Quarterly review: which sources are still high-signal, which have degraded, and what new sources to trial
- `information-diet-doc`: Complete information diet documentation: audit method, signal scoring, elimination criteria, budget, schedule, and review cycle

## Process

1. Every quarter, re-score each retained source: review the last 10 items, count the actionable or educational ones, calculate the signal ratio. Drop any source that has fallen below 30 percent.
2. Check for new sources: has a colleague, mentor, or industry peer recommended something? Give it a 30-day trial. Score it after the trial.
3. Review the budget: is the time allocation still right? If you are consistently stopping before the budget is used, reduce the budget. If you are consistently going over, either the budget is too tight or you have too many sources.
4. The actionability test: from last quarter's consumption, list three specific actions you took because of something you read. If you cannot name three, the diet is too heavy on interesting and too light on useful.
