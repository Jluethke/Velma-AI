# Paleo Dating Selection

Select and justify the appropriate geochronological methods for dating the fossil-bearing horizon.

## Inputs

- `strat-log-template`: Stratigraphic column and lithology data
- `depositional-interpretation`: Depositional environment and mineralogy of the horizon

## Outputs

- `dating-plan`: Selected dating methods with sample requirements and expected precision
- `sample-collection-guide`: Instructions for collecting datable material from the section

## Process

1. Evaluate available datable materials: volcanic ash, detrital zircons, organic carbon, carbonate cement
2. Match each material to the best method (U-Pb, Ar-Ar, radiocarbon, magnetostratigraphy, biostratigraphy)
3. Estimate achievable precision and compare against the research question's temporal resolution needs
4. Write sampling protocol specifying collection method, contamination avoidance, and chain of custody
