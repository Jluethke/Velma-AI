# Retrospective Decision Capture

Capture past decisions retroactively. Interview the people who made them and record the reasoning before it fades. Prioritize decisions that are frequently questioned or relitigated.

## Inputs

- `decision-trigger-criteria`: Criteria for loggable decisions
- `decision-capture-workflow`: The capture workflow

## Outputs

- `retroactive-decision-records`: Decision records for the most important past decisions, captured through interviews
- `relitigation-reduction-baseline`: Count of how often key decisions are currently questioned, to measure improvement after the log is in place

## Process

1. List the decisions that are most frequently questioned or relitigated. These are the highest-value retroactive captures because every relitigation wastes time that the log would save.
2. Interview the decision makers. Ask: what was the question, what options did you consider, why did you choose this one, and what do you expect to happen? If they cannot remember the reasoning, that itself is the evidence for why the log is needed.
3. Write the decision records and have the decision maker review them for accuracy. Store them in the log alongside new decisions.
4. Measure the current relitigation rate: how often per month do people question or revisit these decisions? This is the baseline. After 3 months with the log, measure again.
