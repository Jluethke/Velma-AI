# Process Risk Identifier

Identify key financial processes and the risks of material misstatement.

## Inputs

- `process-inventory`: List of financial processes to assess
- `risk-criteria`: Risk assessment criteria (likelihood, impact, volume)

## Outputs

- `risk-assessment`: Risk assessment for each financial process
- `high-risk-processes`: Processes requiring priority control assessment

## Process

1. List the key financial processes (revenue, purchasing, payroll, treasury, reporting).
2. Identify what could go wrong in each process (errors, fraud, omissions).
3. Assess the likelihood and financial impact of each risk.
4. Prioritize processes for control assessment based on risk score.
