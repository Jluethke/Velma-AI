# Move Out Inspection Scheduler

Schedule and prepare for the move-out inspection.

## Inputs

- `move-out-notice`: Tenant's notice to vacate with move-out date
- `original-condition-report`: Move-in condition report for comparison

## Outputs

- `inspection-appointment`: Scheduled inspection date and time
- `comparison-checklist`: Room-by-room checklist referencing the move-in report

## Process

1. Schedule the walk-through inspection with the tenant.
2. Prepare the comparison checklist from the original move-in condition report.
3. Confirm the appointment with the tenant.
