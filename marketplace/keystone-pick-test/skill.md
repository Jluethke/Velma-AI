# Keystone Pick Test

Choose the one keystone habit by weighing cascade reach against how readily you could actually adopt it.

## Inputs

- `cascade-link-map`: The directed map of which changes drive which
- `downstream-count-per-change`: Upstream reach for each change

## Outputs

- `chosen-keystone`: The single habit you will start with, chosen for high cascade reach and realistic adoptability
- `keystone-pick-rationale`: A short written reason tying the choice to its downstream effects and your ability to start it

## Process

1. Score each change on cascade reach using its downstream count, then score it on adoptability from one to ten, where a change you have failed at repeatedly scores low.
2. Score each change on feedback speed. A habit that produces a felt reward within an hour, such as a walk, builds itself faster than one whose payoff takes weeks.
3. Multiply reach by adoptability by feedback speed. The highest product is your keystone. If two tie, take the one you are more confident you can actually start.
4. Write one paragraph naming the keystone, the changes you expect it to pull along, and why you can begin it this week.
