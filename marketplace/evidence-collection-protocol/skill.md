# Evidence Collection Protocol

Document and collect physical evidence at a scene following contamination prevention, packaging standards, and photographic documentation.

## Inputs

- `scene-description`: Type of scene, size, access conditions, and preliminary observations
- `evidence-types`: Expected evidence categories (trace, biological, digital, chemical)
- `collection-team`: Available personnel, equipment, and time constraints

## Outputs

- `evidence-inventory`: Cataloged list of all collected items with unique identifiers, locations, and descriptions
- `scene-documentation`: Photographs, sketches, and measurements documenting the scene before and during collection
- `collection-notes`: Detailed notes on collection method, packaging, and any deviations from protocol

## Process

1. Secure the scene perimeter and establish a single entry/exit path
2. Photograph the scene in overview, mid-range, and close-up before touching anything
3. Sketch the scene layout with measurements and evidence positions
4. Collect evidence using appropriate techniques (swabs, tweezers, envelopes) with glove changes between items
5. Package and label each item with a unique identifier, collector initials, date, and time
