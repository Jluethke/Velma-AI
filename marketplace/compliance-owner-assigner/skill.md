# Compliance Owner Assigner

Assign an owner and deadline to each compliance requirement.

## Inputs

- `requirement-map`: Mapped compliance requirements
- `deadline-schedule`: Compliance deadlines

## Outputs

- `assigned-obligations`: Each obligation assigned to an owner with a due date
- `accountability-matrix`: RACI matrix for compliance obligations

## Process

1. Identify the natural owner for each obligation based on business function.
2. Assign primary and backup owners.
3. Set internal deadlines with enough lead time before the regulatory deadline.
4. Build the accountability matrix and distribute to all owners.
