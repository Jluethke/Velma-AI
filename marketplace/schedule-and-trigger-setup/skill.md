# Schedule And Trigger Setup

Schedule movement at specific times with specific triggers so it becomes automatic.

## Inputs

- `movement-prescription`: The activity plan
- `current-activity`: Your current schedule

## Outputs

- `movement-schedule`: Specific days and times: 'Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 7am: 20-minute walk from my front door'
- `trigger-setup`: The trigger that initiates movement: alarm, habit stack, calendar event, or environmental cue (e.g., shoes by the door)

## Process

1. Schedule specific days and times. 'I will exercise this week' fails. 'Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 7am' succeeds. Specificity turns intention into behavior.
2. Use a habit stack if possible: 'After I finish my morning coffee, I walk for 20 minutes.' The coffee habit triggers the movement habit.
3. Prepare the environment: shoes and clothes laid out the night before. If you have to search for shoes at 7am, you will skip it.
4. Protect the time: treat movement like a meeting. It goes on the calendar. It is not the first thing cancelled when the day gets busy.
