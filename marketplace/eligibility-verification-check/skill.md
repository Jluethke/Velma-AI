# Eligibility Verification Check

Verify each player's eligibility against league rules.

## Inputs

- `registration-submissions`: Player registrations to verify
- `missing-items-list`: Outstanding items to resolve before verification

## Outputs

- `verified-players`: Players confirmed eligible with documentation on file
- `ineligible-players`: Players who do not meet eligibility requirements with reasons

## Process

1. Check each player's age against the division age cutoff.
2. Verify residency or membership requirements.
3. Confirm medical clearance and signed waivers are on file.
4. Produce the eligibility determination for each player.
