# Vote Frequency Design

Design how often you cast the vote, choosing consistency over intensity because the tally is what shapes the identity.

## Inputs

- `proving-actions`: The set of identity-proving actions
- `smallest-vote-action`: The smallest action that still counts

## Outputs

- `vote-schedule`: How often and when you will perform the smallest action, favoring daily over occasional
- `consistency-over-intensity-rule`: A written rule that a small action done daily beats a large action done rarely

## Process

1. Choose a frequency you can hit on your worst week, not your best. Daily small beats weekly large because it casts more votes and never lets the identity go quiet.
2. Attach the action to a fixed time or an existing routine so the vote has a reliable slot rather than depending on remembering.
3. Write the consistency rule plainly: showing up small is a vote, and a missed perfect day is worth less than a completed tiny one.
4. Decide what the minimum vote looks like on a terrible day, so even then the identity gets a mark in its favor.
