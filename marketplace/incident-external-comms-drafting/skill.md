# Incident External Comms Drafting

Draft external communications for customers, regulators, and media.

## Inputs

- `incident-summary`: Summary of the incident
- `notification-priorities`: External stakeholder priority list

## Outputs

- `customer-notification`: Draft notification for affected customers
- `media-statement`: Prepared statement for media inquiries

## Process

1. Draft customer notification with incident details and protective steps
2. Draft regulatory notification if required
3. Prepare a holding statement for media inquiries
4. Review all external communications with legal counsel
