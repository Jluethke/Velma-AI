# Methodology Chapter Writer

Write the methodology chapter specifying research design, data sources, measurement instruments, analytical methods, and ethical considerations.

## Inputs

- `research-question`: The specific research question and any sub-questions
- `gap-statement`: The literature gap being addressed
- `available-resources`: Lab equipment, datasets, software, and budget available

## Outputs

- `methodology-chapter`: Complete methods chapter draft with all sub-sections
- `analysis-plan`: Pre-specified statistical or analytical plan with decision rules
- `ethics-application`: IRB or ethics committee application if human or animal subjects are involved

## Process

1. Select and justify the research design (experimental, observational, mixed, etc.)
2. Describe data sources, sampling strategy, and measurement instruments
3. Specify the analytical methods with software, parameters, and decision criteria
4. Address validity threats and how the design mitigates them
5. Draft the ethics application if required by the institutional review board
