# Community Content Planner

Plan the content and engagement activities for the community.

## Inputs

- `community-purpose`: The community's purpose and audience
- `onboarding-flow`: The onboarding flow for context on member journey

## Outputs

- `content-calendar`: Weekly schedule of community content and activities
- `engagement-tactics`: List of recurring engagement activities (AMAs, polls, challenges, discussions)

## Process

1. Identify the content themes that serve the community's purpose.
2. Schedule recurring activities (weekly discussion threads, monthly AMAs, challenges).
3. Plan spontaneous engagement tactics (polls, questions of the day).
4. Assign content creation responsibilities and deadlines.
