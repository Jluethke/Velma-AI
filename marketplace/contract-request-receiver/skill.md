# Contract Request Receiver

Receive and log the contract request with the business requirements.

## Inputs

- `contract-request`: The request for a new contract or amendment
- `business-requirements`: The business terms and objectives for the contract

## Outputs

- `request-record`: Logged request with type, parties, timeline, and business terms
- `template-recommendation`: Recommended contract template based on the request type

## Process

1. Log the request with the requesting party, contract type, and timeline.
2. Identify the counterparty and any existing relationship or agreements.
3. Select the appropriate template based on the contract type.
4. Flag any non-standard terms requested by the business.
