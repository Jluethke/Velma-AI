# Witness Interviewer

Interview the complainant, respondent, and witnesses.

## Inputs

- `investigation-plan`: Investigation plan
- `confidentiality-notice`: Confidentiality expectations

## Outputs

- `interview-records`: Detailed notes from each interview
- `evidence-collected`: Evidence gathered during interviews (documents, communications)

## Process

1. Interview the complainant first to understand the full allegation
2. Interview witnesses identified by the complainant
3. Interview the respondent with specific questions about the allegations
4. Interview any additional witnesses identified during the process
