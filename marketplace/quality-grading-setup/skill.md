# Quality Grading Setup

Set up quality grading and inspection at the receiving point.

## Inputs

- `crop-data`: Crop type and quality standards
- `delivery-coordination`: Expected delivery schedule and volumes

## Outputs

- `grading-protocol`: Sampling plan and quality standards by load
- `rejection-criteria`: Thresholds for rejecting or downgrading loads

## Process

1. Define quality parameters (moisture, damage, foreign material, test weight).
2. Set the sampling protocol for incoming loads.
3. Establish acceptance, downgrade, and rejection thresholds.
4. Train receiving staff on the grading procedure.
