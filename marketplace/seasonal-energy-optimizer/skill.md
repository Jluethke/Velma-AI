# Seasonal Energy Optimizer

Optimize energy usage for the coming season: thermostat strategy, appliance efficiency, and utility rate plans.

## Inputs

- `current_energy_costs`: Recent utility bills
- `home_systems`: Heating and cooling systems
- `upcoming_season`: Season to optimize for

## Outputs

- `energy_plan`: Season-specific energy saving actions
- `thermostat_schedule`: Optimal thermostat settings
- `utility_rate_check`: Whether current rate plan is best

## Process

1. Review current energy usage patterns
2. Set optimal thermostat schedule
3. Identify energy waste sources
4. Compare utility rate plans
5. Calculate expected savings from changes
