# Strain Isolation Protocol

Isolate a pure microbial strain from a mixed sample using selective media, colony morphology, and confirmatory identification.

## Inputs

- `sample-source`: Origin of the sample (clinical, environmental, food, etc.)
- `target-organism`: Suspected organism or group to isolate
- `biosafety-level`: Required BSL and containment procedures

## Outputs

- `pure-isolate`: Confirmed pure culture with strain ID and cryostock location
- `isolation-record`: Media used, colony morphology, Gram stain, and confirmatory test results

## Process

1. Plate sample on selective and differential media appropriate for the target group
2. Pick isolated colonies and streak for purity through three successive passages
3. Confirm identity by Gram stain, catalase/oxidase, and 16S rRNA sequencing or MALDI-TOF
4. Prepare glycerol cryostocks at -80C and record strain provenance in the lab database
