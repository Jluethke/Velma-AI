# Lab Training Curriculum

Build a training program for new lab members covering safety, equipment operation, core methods, data management, and lab culture.

## Inputs

- `safety-manual`: Lab safety protocols from the safety design step
- `sop-collection`: Standard operating procedures for core methods
- `team-roles`: Expected roles (graduate student, postdoc, technician, undergraduate) and their responsibilities

## Outputs

- `training-plan`: Sequenced training modules with estimated hours per module
- `competency-assessments`: Practical assessments demonstrating proficiency in each core skill
- `onboarding-checklist`: Day-by-day onboarding checklist for the first two weeks

## Process

1. Sequence training modules from safety orientation through independent method execution
2. Create demonstrations and hands-on exercises for each core method
3. Design competency assessments where trainees perform procedures under observation
4. Write the two-week onboarding checklist covering accounts, access, safety, and first methods
5. Set expectations for ongoing training and periodic competency re-verification
