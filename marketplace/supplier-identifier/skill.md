# Supplier Identifier

Identify potential suppliers for the procurement.

## Inputs

- `procurement-brief`: Procurement brief with specifications, quantities, and timeline
- `evaluation-criteria`: Criteria for evaluating supplier quotes

## Outputs

- `supplier-shortlist`: Shortlisted suppliers with capability summaries
- `rfq-package`: Request for quotation package ready to send

## Process

1. Search supplier databases and industry directories
2. Pre-screen suppliers against capability and certification requirements
3. Prepare the request for quotation with specifications and terms
4. Produce the supplier shortlist and RFQ package
