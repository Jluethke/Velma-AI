# Onboarding Buddy Assigner

Assign an onboarding buddy and set expectations for the relationship.

## Inputs

- `hire-details`: New hire information
- `orientation-completion`: Orientation status

## Outputs

- `buddy-assignment`: Assigned buddy with contact details
- `buddy-guide`: Guide for the buddy on their responsibilities

## Process

1. Select a buddy from the team or adjacent team
2. Brief the buddy on their role (answering questions, social introductions, culture guidance)
3. Introduce the new hire and buddy
4. Set a check-in cadence for the first month
