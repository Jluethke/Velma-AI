# Recovery Timeline Builder

Build a phased recovery timeline with milestones for sleep, exercise, social connection, and joy.

## Inputs

- `restoration_gaps`: Missing restorative activities
- `boundary_list`: New boundaries being implemented
- `constraints`: Financial, family, and schedule constraints

## Outputs

- `recovery_phases`: 4-phase recovery plan with milestones
- `daily_minimums`: Non-negotiable daily recovery activities
- `progress_indicators`: Signs recovery is working

## Process

1. Phase 1 (weeks 1-2): Sleep and basic self-care
2. Phase 2 (weeks 3-4): Add exercise and social connection
3. Phase 3 (weeks 5-8): Rebuild interests and joy
4. Phase 4 (ongoing): Maintenance and prevention
5. Define daily minimums that cannot be skipped
