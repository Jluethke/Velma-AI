# Final Pay Processor

Process final pay, benefits, and any outstanding expenses.

## Inputs

- `employee-details`: Employee and payroll information
- `access-revocation-log`: Confirmation of completed offboarding steps

## Outputs

- `final-pay-record`: Record of final pay calculation and disbursement
- `benefits-summary`: Summary of benefits continuation options (COBRA, 401k rollover)

## Process

1. Calculate final pay including unused PTO
2. Process any outstanding expense reimbursements
3. Prepare benefits continuation information
4. Issue the final paycheck per state law requirements
