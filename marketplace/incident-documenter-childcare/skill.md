# Incident Documenter Childcare

Document the incident with all required details immediately after it occurs.

## Inputs

- `incident-description`: What happened (injury, illness, behavioral event, allegation, facility issue)
- `involved-parties`: Names and roles of children, staff, and witnesses involved

## Outputs

- `incident-report-draft`: Written incident report with date, time, location, description, parties, and immediate actions taken
- `severity-classification`: Severity level (minor, moderate, serious, reportable to licensing)

## Process

1. Record the date, time, and exact location of the incident.
2. Write a factual description of what happened (avoid interpretation or blame).
3. List all involved parties and witnesses with their roles.
4. Document the immediate response (first aid administered, child comforted, area secured).
5. Classify the severity based on the center's incident classification policy.
