# Cloud Forensics Report Writer

Produce the cloud forensics report with the attack reconstruction and remediation steps.

## Inputs

- `attack-reconstruction`: The step-by-step attack reconstruction
- `impact-assessment`: Assessment of the incident impact
- `preservation-log`: Record of evidence preservation actions

## Outputs

- `cloud-forensics-report`: The complete cloud forensics investigation report
- `remediation-actions`: Specific actions to contain and remediate the incident

## Process

1. Write the attack narrative with evidence citations
2. Detail the impact in terms of data, access, and business operations
3. List remediation actions: credential rotation, policy fixes, patches
4. Document the evidence chain and preservation methods
