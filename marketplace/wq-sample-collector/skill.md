# Wq Sample Collector

Collect water samples following standard protocols appropriate to the parameters being measured.

## Inputs

- `sampling-plan`: Locations, depths, frequencies, and parameters to measure
- `site-access`: Access logistics and safety considerations

## Outputs

- `collected-samples`: Water samples in appropriate containers with preservatives applied
- `field-log`: Collection times, conditions, in-situ measurements, and chain of custody

## Process

1. Prepare bottles with appropriate preservatives for each analyte group.
2. Collect samples at specified depth, avoiding surface film and bottom sediment disturbance.
3. Record in-situ measurements (temperature, dissolved oxygen, pH, conductivity) with calibrated probes.
4. Label, cool to 4C, and transport to the lab within holding time.
