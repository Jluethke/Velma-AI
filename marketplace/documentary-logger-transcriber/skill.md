# Documentary Logger Transcriber

Log and transcribe all footage for the paper edit.

## Inputs

- `interview-footage`: All interview recordings
- `b-roll-footage`: All field footage

## Outputs

- `transcripts`: Text transcripts of all interviews
- `footage-log`: Timecoded log of all footage with descriptions

## Process

1. Transcribe every interview with timecodes
2. Log all b-roll with descriptions and quality ratings
3. Tag the strongest quotes and most visual footage
4. Organize by theme and story arc
