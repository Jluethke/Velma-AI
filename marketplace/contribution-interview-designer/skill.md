# Contribution Interview Designer

Design the interview so interviewers assess the specific contribution a candidate brings, not a general impression. The guide directs each interviewer to one or two criteria so the panel covers the whole profile.

## Inputs

- `add-criteria`: The observable criteria
- `add-screening-questions`: The questions that surface them

## Outputs

- `interview-guide`: An assignment of one or two criteria to each interviewer, with the question and the evidence to listen for
- `contribution-evidence-form`: A form where interviewers record specific examples rather than adjectives

## Process

1. Assign each interviewer one or two criteria so no criterion is left unassessed and none is assessed five times.
2. For each criterion, give the interviewer the question and the evidence to listen for.
3. Build an evidence form where interviewers record specific examples, not adjectives.
4. Instruct interviewers to treat an unfamiliar style as a possible sign of the missing perspective, not an automatic negative.
