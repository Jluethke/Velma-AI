# Monetization Opportunity Finder

Identify new monetization opportunities based on the creator's audience and content.

## Inputs

- `revenue-audit`: Current revenue stream performance
- `audience-monetization-rate`: How well the audience is currently monetized

## Outputs

- `opportunity-list`: Ranked list of new monetization opportunities with estimated revenue potential
- `gap-analysis`: Which monetization methods peers use that this creator does not

## Process

1. Research what monetization methods similar creators in the niche use.
2. Identify gaps where the creator is undermonetized compared to peers.
3. Estimate the revenue potential for each opportunity based on audience size and engagement.
4. Rank opportunities by potential revenue, effort to implement, and brand fit.
