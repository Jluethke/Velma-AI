# Homeschool Legal Requirements

Understand and comply with state homeschool legal requirements: notification, subjects, testing, and records.

## Inputs

- `state_of_residence`: State for legal requirements
- `children_ages`: Ages of children being homeschooled
- `previous_schooling`: Previous school type (public, private, none)

## Outputs

- `legal_requirements`: State-specific legal obligations
- `filing_checklist`: Required notifications and filings
- `record_keeping_plan`: What records to maintain

## Process

1. Research state homeschool statutes
2. Identify required subjects by grade
3. Determine notification or filing requirements
4. Understand testing or portfolio requirements
5. Set up record-keeping system
