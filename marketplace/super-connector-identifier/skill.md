# Super Connector Identifier

Track referral chains to identify your super-connectors: the small number of contacts who generate a disproportionate share of your referrals.

## Inputs

- `ask-touchpoints`: When referral asks are appropriate
- `ask-scripts`: How to make the ask

## Outputs

- `referral-attribution-log`: Every referral tracked back to its source, with the chain of introductions if multi-hop
- `super-connector-list`: Contacts who have made 3 or more successful referrals, ranked by referral volume and quality

## Process

1. Build the attribution log: for every new contact or opportunity that came through a referral, record who made the introduction, when, and what happened (converted, did not convert, still in progress). If the referral was multi-hop (A introduced B who introduced C), track the full chain.
2. Identify the power law: in most networks, 10-15% of contacts generate 80%+ of referrals. These are your super-connectors. They refer frequently because they are (a) well-connected, (b) motivated by social capital, and (c) trust you enough to stake their reputation repeatedly.
3. Profile your super-connectors. What do they have in common? What motivates them? How do they prefer to refer (email introduction, verbal mention, social media tag)? Understanding their pattern lets you make it easier for them and identify similar people who could become super-connectors.
4. Invest disproportionately in super-connectors. They should be at the top of your relationship maintenance list. Send them thank-you notes, give them early access to your work, update them on the outcomes of their referrals. A super-connector who feels valued will continue referring. One who feels taken for granted will stop.
