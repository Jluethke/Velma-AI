# Velocity Improvement Intervention

Design an intervention that reduces time in the bottleneck stage.

## Inputs

- `bottleneck-stage`: The slowest stage
- `stall-reasons`: Why deals stall there

## Outputs

- `intervention`: The specific change to reduce time in the bottleneck stage
- `expected-time-reduction`: The estimated reduction in days at the bottleneck stage

## Process

1. Match the intervention to the stall reason. If the stall is 'waiting for legal review,' the intervention might be: provide a pre-approved contract template that reduces legal review from 30 days to 5 days.
2. If the stall is 'prospect is comparing options,' the intervention might be: deliver the competitive comparison proactively at the start of the stage instead of waiting for the prospect to ask.
3. If the stall is internal (your team is slow), the intervention is a process change with a deadline commitment: 'Proposals will be delivered within 48 hours of the qualification call.'
4. Choose one intervention for the bottleneck stage. Do not try to fix multiple stages simultaneously. Reducing time in the bottleneck by 30% has a larger impact on total cycle time than reducing time in a non-bottleneck by 50%.
