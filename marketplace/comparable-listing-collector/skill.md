# Comparable Listing Collector

Collect comparable rental listings from the market.

## Inputs

- `subject-units`: Units to price with specifications
- `search-criteria`: Geographic radius, unit type, and size range

## Outputs

- `comparable-listings`: Comparable rentals with rent, size, amenities, and age
- `market-snapshot`: Summary of current market conditions: vacancy rate, average rent, trend

## Process

1. Search rental platforms for listings matching the criteria.
2. Record rent, size, amenities, age, and days on market for each comparable.
3. Calculate the market vacancy rate and average rent for the area.
