# Endpoint Selector

Select primary and secondary endpoints: clinical relevance, measurement validity, expected effect size, and regulatory acceptability.

## Inputs

- `pico-statement`: The research question with defined outcome
- `prior-evidence`: Effect sizes observed in previous trials

## Outputs

- `endpoint-definitions`: Primary and secondary endpoints with measurement method, timing, and minimally important difference
- `endpoint-justification`: Clinical relevance, regulatory precedent, and psychometric properties per endpoint
- `assessment-schedule`: When each endpoint is measured during the study

## Process

1. Select the primary endpoint based on clinical relevance and regulatory guidance
2. Choose secondary endpoints that capture additional dimensions of efficacy and safety
3. Define the measurement method, instrument, and scoring for each
4. Set the minimally clinically important difference for the primary endpoint
