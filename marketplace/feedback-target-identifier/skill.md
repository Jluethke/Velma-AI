# Feedback Target Identifier

Identify the specific behavior or output you want feedback on, not a general request for opinions.

## Inputs

- `recent-performance`: A recent event, presentation, project, or interaction where you want to know how you did
- `self-assessment`: Your own sense of how it went and where you might be weak

## Outputs

- `feedback-targets`: 2-3 specific behaviors or outputs you want feedback on, each stated as a concrete observable thing
- `blind-spot-hypotheses`: Areas where you suspect you have blind spots (you think you are fine but might not be)

## Process

1. Convert 'how did I do' into 2-3 specific questions. 'How was my presentation' becomes 'Did I explain the pricing model clearly enough?' and 'Was the Q&A section too long?'
2. Include at least one question about a suspected blind spot. These are areas where your self-assessment might be wrong because you lack the external perspective to evaluate yourself.
3. Avoid questions that invite flattery ('Did you like it?') or are too broad ('Any feedback?'). Specific questions produce specific answers.
4. Frame each question so that an honest negative answer is as easy to give as a positive one. 'Was the timing right, or should I have cut 5 minutes?' makes it easy to say 'cut 5 minutes.'
