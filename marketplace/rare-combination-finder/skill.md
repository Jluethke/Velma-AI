# Rare Combination Finder

Find unusual combinations of two or three skills that few people hold together.

## Inputs

- `percentile-ratings`: An honest percentile estimate for each skill
- `top-quartile-skills`: Skills where you sit in or could reach the top quartile

## Outputs

- `candidate-combinations`: Candidate pairings and triples of your stronger skills
- `rarity-notes`: An estimate of how rare each combination is

## Process

1. Pair and triple your stronger skills to form candidate combinations.
2. Estimate how rare each combination is among people in your field.
3. Keep combinations that are both rare and plausible for you to own.
4. Note the one skill in each combination that makes it distinctive.
