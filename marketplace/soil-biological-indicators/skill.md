# Soil Biological Indicators

Measure soil biological activity: microbial biomass and soil respiration.

## Inputs

- `prepared-samples`: Fresh (not dried) soil aliquots for biological testing

## Outputs

- `biological-results`: Microbial biomass carbon, soil respiration rate, and metabolic quotient
- `biological-health-rating`: Biological activity rated relative to reference values for the soil type

## Process

1. Measure microbial biomass carbon by chloroform fumigation-extraction.
2. Measure basal soil respiration by CO2 evolution over 24 hours.
3. Calculate the metabolic quotient (qCO2) as respiration per unit biomass.
4. Compare results against reference values for the climate zone and soil type.
