# Trademark Clearance Searcher

Conduct a trademark clearance search across federal, state, and common law sources.

## Inputs

- `proposed-mark`: The proposed trademark (word, logo, or both)
- `goods-services`: Goods and services the mark will cover

## Outputs

- `search-results`: Clearance search results with conflicting marks
- `risk-assessment`: Risk of confusion with existing marks

## Process

1. Search the USPTO TESS database for identical and similar marks
2. Search state trademark databases and common law sources
3. Assess the likelihood of confusion with each identified mark
