# Dispute Resolution Drafter

Draft dispute resolution, governing law, and arbitration provisions.

## Inputs

- `liability-section`: Liability provisions
- `target-audience`: Target audience

## Outputs

- `dispute-section`: Dispute resolution and arbitration clauses
- `governing-law`: Governing law and venue selection

## Process

1. Select governing law and venue
2. Draft binding arbitration clause with class action waiver if applicable
3. Include small claims court carve-out
