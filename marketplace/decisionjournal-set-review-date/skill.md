# Decisionjournal Set Review Date

Schedule the review and write the prompts you will answer when the outcome is in.

## Inputs

- `key-assumptions`: The assumptions that must hold for the outcome to happen.
- `change-my-mind`: The new fact that would change your mind.

## Outputs

- `review-date`: The specific date to review the decision.
- `review-prompts`: The prompts you will answer at review time.

## Process

1. Set a specific date to review the decision against its outcome.
2. Write the prompts you will answer at review time.
3. Store the entry where you will actually see it on that date.
