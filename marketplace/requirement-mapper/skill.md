# Requirement Mapper

Map each regulation to its specific compliance requirements.

## Inputs

- `regulation-inventory`: Inventory of applicable regulations
- `gap-list`: Newly identified regulations to map

## Outputs

- `requirement-map`: Each regulation mapped to its specific compliance requirements (filings, reports, controls, training)
- `deadline-schedule`: Schedule of all compliance deadlines

## Process

1. For each regulation, identify the specific compliance obligations (filings, reporting, controls, training, audits).
2. Note the frequency and deadlines for each obligation.
3. Identify overlapping requirements across regulations to avoid duplication.
4. Produce the requirement map and deadline schedule.
