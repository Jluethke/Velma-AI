# Community Health Tracker

Track community health metrics and produce the management report.

## Inputs

- `content-calendar`: The content and activity schedule
- `moderation-protocol`: The moderation protocol in use

## Outputs

- `health-metrics`: Metrics on growth, engagement rate, retention, and moderation actions
- `community-management-plan`: The complete community management plan document

## Process

1. Define the metrics to track (member growth, active members, posts per day, engagement rate, churn).
2. Set up the data collection method for each metric.
3. Establish the review cadence (weekly health check, monthly deep dive).
4. Compile the full community management plan from all prior outputs.
