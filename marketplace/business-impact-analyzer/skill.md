# Business Impact Analyzer

Conduct a business impact analysis to identify critical processes and their dependencies.

## Inputs

- `organization-profile`: Organization structure and business units
- `system-inventory`: Systems supporting business processes

## Outputs

- `impact-analysis`: BIA results with process criticality rankings
- `dependency-map`: Map of process dependencies on systems, people, and vendors

## Process

1. Identify all business processes and their owners
2. Assess the financial, operational, and reputational impact of each process being unavailable
3. Map dependencies: systems, personnel, vendors, and facilities
4. Rank processes by criticality and maximum tolerable downtime
