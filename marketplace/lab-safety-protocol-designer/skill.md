# Lab Safety Protocol Designer

Design lab safety protocols covering chemical hygiene, biological safety, radiation (if applicable), emergency procedures, and PPE requirements.

## Inputs

- `hazard-inventory`: List of chemicals, biological agents, radiation sources, and physical hazards
- `regulatory-requirements`: OSHA, EPA, institutional EHS, and biosafety committee requirements
- `lab-layout`: Floor plan showing benches, fume hoods, exits, safety equipment locations

## Outputs

- `safety-manual`: Complete lab safety manual covering all identified hazard categories
- `emergency-procedures`: Step-by-step emergency response for fire, spill, exposure, and medical events
- `ppe-matrix`: PPE requirements mapped to each procedure and hazard class

## Process

1. Inventory all hazards by category (chemical, biological, physical, radiological)
2. Write handling, storage, and disposal procedures for each hazard class
3. Design emergency response procedures for each credible accident scenario
4. Create the PPE matrix mapping protective equipment to specific procedures
5. Submit protocols for institutional EHS review and approval
