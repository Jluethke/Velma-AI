# Faculty Impact Evaluator

Measure the impact of the faculty development program on teaching quality.

## Inputs

- `workshop-calendar`: Schedule of completed workshops
- `needs-survey-results`: Baseline faculty development needs

## Outputs

- `impact-report`: Analysis of program impact on teaching quality
- `faculty-development-plan`: Updated plan for next cycle based on findings

## Process

1. Compare pre- and post-program student evaluation scores
2. Analyze participation rates and faculty satisfaction surveys
3. Review teaching portfolio growth over the program period
4. Produce the impact report and updated development plan
