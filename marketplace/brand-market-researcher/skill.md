# Brand Market Researcher

Research the competitive landscape and target audience for brand positioning.

## Inputs

- `business-description`: What the business does and its value proposition
- `competitor-list`: Known competitors to analyze

## Outputs

- `market-analysis`: Competitive landscape and positioning opportunities
- `audience-profiles`: Target audience segments with demographics and psychographics

## Process

1. Audit competitor brand positioning, visual identity, and messaging
2. Identify white space in the market for differentiation
3. Define target audience segments with demographics and behaviors
4. Map audience needs to brand positioning opportunities
