# Ugc Strategy Definer

Define the UGC types to collect and the collection channels.

## Inputs

- `brand-context`: The brand, product, or service the UGC is for
- `marketing-goals`: What the UGC program should achieve (social proof, engagement, content volume)

## Outputs

- `ugc-types`: Defined types of UGC to collect (reviews, photos, videos, testimonials, unboxing)
- `collection-channels`: Channels and mechanisms for collecting UGC (hashtags, submission forms, contests, review prompts)

## Process

1. Identify which UGC types best serve the marketing goals.
2. Select the collection mechanisms for each type (branded hashtag, landing page form, email prompt, in-app prompt).
3. Design any incentive structure (contests, features, discounts).
4. Document the collection strategy.
