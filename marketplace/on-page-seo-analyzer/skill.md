# On Page Seo Analyzer

Analyze on-page optimization: titles, meta descriptions, headings, and content quality.

## Inputs

- `crawl-summary`: Pages and their metadata from the crawl
- `site-url`: Site being audited

## Outputs

- `on-page-issues`: On-page optimization problems per page
- `content-scores`: Content quality scores and readability metrics

## Process

1. Check title tags and meta descriptions for length and keyword usage
2. Analyze heading structure and internal linking
3. Score content quality and identify thin or duplicate content
