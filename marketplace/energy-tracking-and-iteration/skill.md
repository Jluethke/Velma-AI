# Energy Tracking And Iteration

Track your energy for 30 days under the new system and iterate on the schedule.

## Inputs

- `renewal-rituals`: New rituals
- `optimized-schedule`: The new schedule

## Outputs

- `energy-improvement`: Comparison of average daily energy before and after the system: should see higher peak energy and shorter/shallower troughs
- `schedule-refinements`: Adjustments based on what worked and what did not

## Process

1. Continue the hourly energy rating for 30 days. Compare week 4 to the original baseline.
2. Expected improvements: peak energy hours feel sharper (because they are now protected), trough hours feel less drained (because they have renewal rituals), and total productive output increases.
3. If peak hours are still being invaded by meetings, the protection is not working. Communicate boundaries explicitly: 'I am available for meetings after 11am. Before that, I am heads-down on project work.'
4. Iterate quarterly: energy cycles shift with seasons, life changes, and work demands. What worked in Q1 may not work in Q3.
