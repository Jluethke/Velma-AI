# Coverage Inventory Builder

Inventory all covered properties and their current coverage.

## Inputs

- `property-portfolio`: All properties with addresses and values
- `current-policies`: Active insurance policies with coverage details

## Outputs

- `coverage-inventory`: Each property with its coverage type, limits, and premium
- `coverage-gaps`: Properties or risks without adequate coverage

## Process

1. List each property with its insured value, coverage type, and limits.
2. Compare coverage against replacement cost estimates.
3. Identify gaps: insufficient limits, missing coverages, or excluded perils.
