# Survey Design Vw

Design the Van Westendorp survey with the four standard questions and a clear product description.

## Inputs

- `product-description`: Clear description of the product or service being priced
- `target-audience`: Who will be surveyed (must be actual potential buyers, not random people)

## Outputs

- `survey-instrument`: The four-question survey with product description, ready to distribute
- `sample-plan`: How many responses needed (minimum 30, ideally 100+) and how to reach the target audience

## Process

1. Write a clear, neutral product description. The respondent must understand what they are pricing. No selling language, no superlatives.
2. Use the four standard Van Westendorp questions: (1) At what price would this be so cheap you would question its quality? (2) At what price is this a bargain - a great deal? (3) At what price is this getting expensive but you would still consider it? (4) At what price is this too expensive to consider?
3. Plan to survey actual potential buyers, not friends or colleagues. The responses must represent real purchase intent. Minimum 30 responses for a rough estimate, 100+ for reliable curves.
4. Include a screening question to verify the respondent is in the target audience. Responses from non-buyers distort the curves.
