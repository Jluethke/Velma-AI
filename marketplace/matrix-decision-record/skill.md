# Matrix Decision Record

Record the decision matrix analysis for future reference and calibration. Include the criteria, weights, scores, totals, gut-check result, and final decision. This feeds into the decision journal.

## Inputs

- `final-decision`: The decision made
- `gut-check-result`: Whether the matrix and gut agreed
- `weighted-totals`: The matrix results

## Outputs

- `matrix-record`: The complete decision matrix record: criteria, weights, scores, totals, gut check, decision, and outcome check date
- `outcome-check-date`: When to compare the outcome to the prediction

## Process

1. Save the complete matrix: criteria, weights, scores, totals, ranking, gut-check result, and final decision.
2. Note whether the decision followed the matrix or overrode it, and why.
3. Set an outcome check date to evaluate whether the decision was right.
4. At the check date, note whether following or overriding the matrix produced the better outcome.
