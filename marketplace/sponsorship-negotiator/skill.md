# Sponsorship Negotiator

Negotiate the sponsorship terms and finalize the agreement.

## Inputs

- `fit-assessment`: Whether the brand is a good fit
- `deal-parameters`: The proposed deliverables and budget

## Outputs

- `signed-agreement`: The finalized sponsorship agreement with all terms
- `deliverable-schedule`: Timeline for content creation, approval, and publication

## Process

1. Set the rate based on the creator's audience size, engagement, and deliverable type.
2. Negotiate the deliverables (format, placement, frequency, exclusivity, usage rights).
3. Agree on the payment terms (upfront, on publish, net 30).
4. Finalize and sign the agreement.
