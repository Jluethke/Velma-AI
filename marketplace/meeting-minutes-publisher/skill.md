# Meeting Minutes Publisher

Record, approve, and publish the meeting minutes.

## Inputs

- `meeting-actions`: Actions taken during the meeting
- `public-comments`: Public comments received

## Outputs

- `meeting-minutes`: The official meeting minutes
- `meeting-record`: Complete meeting record (agenda, minutes, notices, attachments)

## Process

1. Draft the minutes from the meeting notes and action record.
2. Include all motions, votes, and public comments.
3. Submit the minutes for approval at the next meeting.
4. Publish the approved minutes and file the complete meeting record.
