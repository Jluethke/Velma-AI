# Ucm Specific Ask Builder

State specifically what you need from your manager, and whether this is a decision or an awareness item. A vague ask leaves the manager guessing at their role and often produces nothing. Marking a message as decision-needed or awareness-only lets them triage it correctly.

## Inputs

- `headline`: The point the ask follows from
- `manager-priorities`: What the manager cares about, to frame the ask in their interest

## Outputs

- `specific-ask`: Exactly what you need: a decision, an approval, an introduction, or nothing but awareness
- `decision-or-fyi-flag`: A clear marker of whether this needs a decision or is for awareness only

## Process

1. State the ask as a specific action: 'I need your approval on the revised budget by Friday' or 'I need you to raise this with the vendor's executive.'
2. If no action is needed, say so plainly, so the manager can note it and move on instead of hunting for a hidden ask.
3. Flag the message as decision-needed or awareness-only at the top. This single marker is what lets a busy manager triage correctly.
4. Make the ask proportionate. Asking the manager to do what you could do yourself spends credibility you will want later.
