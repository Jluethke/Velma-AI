# Social Engineering Report

Produce the social engineering assessment report with metrics, findings, and training recommendations.

## Inputs

- `detection-rates`: Detection and reporting rates
- `response-time-metrics`: Response time measurements

## Outputs

- `social-engineering-report`: Complete social engineering assessment report
- `training-recommendations`: Targeted security awareness training recommendations

## Process

1. Summarize metrics across all channels with comparisons to industry benchmarks.
2. Identify the most effective attack vectors and the most vulnerable groups.
3. Write targeted training recommendations for the highest-risk departments.
4. Propose process improvements (reporting button, verification procedures) based on findings.
