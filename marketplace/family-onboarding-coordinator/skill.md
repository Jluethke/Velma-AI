# Family Onboarding Coordinator

Onboard the family with orientation, communication setup, and first-day preparation.

## Inputs

- `classroom-assignment`: The child's classroom and teacher assignment
- `start-date`: The confirmed first day

## Outputs

- `onboarding-checklist`: Completed orientation items (tour, handbook review, communication app setup, supply list)
- `enrollment-record`: Final enrollment record with all documentation confirmed

## Process

1. Schedule and conduct the family orientation (facility walkthrough, introduce the teacher, review policies).
2. Set up the family on the communication app (daily reports, messaging, billing).
3. Provide the supply list and label instructions for the child's items.
4. Confirm the first-day drop-off and pickup logistics.
5. File the completed enrollment record.
