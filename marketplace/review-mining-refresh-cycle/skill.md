# Review Mining Refresh Cycle

Set up a quarterly refresh to keep the competitive intelligence current.

## Inputs

- `talk-tracks`: Current sales materials
- `battle-cards`: Current battle cards

## Outputs

- `refresh-schedule`: Quarterly review mining calendar with specific dates and responsibilities
- `change-log`: Template for tracking what changed each quarter: new complaints, fixed complaints, new competitors

## Process

1. Collect new reviews quarterly. Focus on reviews from the past 3 months.
2. Compare new themes to the previous quarter. Are any complaint themes fading (competitor fixed it)? Are new themes emerging?
3. Update talk tracks and battle cards with new data. Remove talking points about problems the competitor has fixed.
4. Share updates with the sales team. The first 5 minutes of a quarterly sales meeting can cover: 'Here is what changed in the competitive landscape based on real customer reviews.'
