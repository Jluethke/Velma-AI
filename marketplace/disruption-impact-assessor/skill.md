# Disruption Impact Assessor

Assess the impact of disruption for each critical process.

## Inputs

- `critical-processes`: List of critical processes
- `dependency-map`: Dependency map

## Outputs

- `impact-profiles`: Financial, operational, reputational, and regulatory impact over time for each process
- `maximum-tolerable-downtime`: Maximum tolerable downtime for each critical process

## Process

1. For each critical process, estimate the financial impact of disruption over time (1 hour, 4 hours, 1 day, 1 week).
2. Assess operational, reputational, and regulatory impacts.
3. Determine the maximum tolerable downtime.
4. Identify the recovery time objective and recovery point objective.
