# Maintenance Completion Tracker

Track work completion and follow up with the tenant.

## Inputs

- `assignment-record`: Assigned tech and scheduled date
- `tenant-notification`: Notification record

## Outputs

- `completion-record`: Work completed with tech notes and photos
- `maintenance-log`: Full maintenance log entry for the property

## Process

1. Confirm the tech completed the visit and collect completion notes.
2. Record parts used, labor hours, and before/after photos.
3. Follow up with the tenant within 48 hours to confirm satisfaction.
4. Close the work order and update the property maintenance log.
