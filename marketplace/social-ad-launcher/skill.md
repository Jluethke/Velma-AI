# Social Ad Launcher

Launch the ads and run the initial A/B test.

## Inputs

- `social-ad-creatives`: Approved creatives
- `budget-allocation`: Budget and bidding config

## Outputs

- `launch-status`: Launch confirmation and initial delivery data
- `ab-test-results`: Early A/B test results

## Process

1. Submit ads for platform review
2. Launch and monitor delivery for the first 48 hours
3. Collect initial A/B test data
