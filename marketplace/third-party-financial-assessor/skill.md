# Third Party Financial Assessor

Assess the financial stability and data security practices of the third party.

## Inputs

- `screening-results`: Screening results
- `information-request`: Collected information and documents

## Outputs

- `financial-assessment`: Assessment of financial health and stability
- `security-assessment`: Assessment of data security and privacy practices

## Process

1. Review financial statements and credit reports.
2. Assess data security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001) and practices.
3. Evaluate business continuity and insurance coverage.
4. Rate the overall financial and security posture.
