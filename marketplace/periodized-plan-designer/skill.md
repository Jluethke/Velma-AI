# Periodized Plan Designer

Design the periodized training plan covering off-season, pre-season, in-season, and recovery phases.

## Inputs

- `assessment-results`: Baseline fitness data for the team
- `athlete-goals`: Individual athlete targets

## Outputs

- `training-plan`: Periodized plan with phases, volume, intensity, and focus areas
- `phase-calendar`: Calendar mapping each training phase to dates

## Process

1. Define the macrocycle (annual plan) with phase dates aligned to the competition schedule.
2. Design each mesocycle (3-6 week block) with volume, intensity, and training focus.
3. Build in deload weeks and transition periods between phases.
4. Produce the periodized training plan.
