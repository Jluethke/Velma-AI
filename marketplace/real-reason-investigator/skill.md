# Real Reason Investigator

For each loss, dig beneath the stated reason to find the likely real reason. The stated reason is a social convenience. The real reason is the useful signal.

## Inputs

- `loss-inventory`: Structured list of losses
- `loss-volume-summary`: Loss patterns by stage and reason

## Outputs

- `real-reason-analysis`: For each loss: the stated reason, the likely real reason, the evidence for the real reason, and the moment the loss became inevitable
- `objection-map`: The actual objections ranked by frequency, with the language prospects used

## Process

1. For each loss, review the communication trail: emails, call notes, proposal feedback. Look for the moment energy dropped.
2. Common translations: 'budget' often means 'not convinced of the value'; 'timing' often means 'not urgent enough'; 'went silent' often means 'found something easier'.
3. For competitor losses, identify what the competitor offered that you did not (or what they communicated that you did not). The gap is the real reason.
4. For the losses that went silent: reconstruct the last touchpoint. What was asked of the prospect? If it was too much too soon, the ask was the real reason.
