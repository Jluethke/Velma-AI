# Soar Time Savings Measurer

Measure the time and effort savings from automation compared to the manual process.

## Inputs

- `simulation-results`: Test results from simulated alerts
- `edge-case-notes`: Edge cases and handling decisions

## Outputs

- `time-savings-analysis`: Comparison of manual vs automated response time and analyst effort
- `roi-calculation`: Return on investment in analyst hours saved per month

## Process

1. Compare automated response time against the historical manual average
2. Calculate analyst hours saved per alert and project monthly savings based on volume
3. Identify remaining manual steps and their time cost
4. Compute the ROI: analyst hours saved minus the time spent building and maintaining the playbook
