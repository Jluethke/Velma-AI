# Family Communication Onboarder

Onboard families onto the communication system and establish expectations.

## Inputs

- `privacy-controls`: Configured privacy settings
- `sharing-guidelines`: Photo sharing guidelines

## Outputs

- `family-activation-status`: List of families with their activation status on the communication platform
- `communication-setup-document`: Complete communication system documentation for staff and families

## Process

1. Send the platform invitation to all enrolled families with setup instructions.
2. Track which families have activated their accounts and follow up on any who have not.
3. Share the communication guidelines with families (how to message staff, expected response times, emergency contacts).
4. Compile the full communication system documentation for the staff handbook.
