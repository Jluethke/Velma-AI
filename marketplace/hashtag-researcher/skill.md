# Hashtag Researcher

Research relevant hashtags by category.

## Inputs

- `brand-name`: The brand or business name
- `industry-niche`: The industry or niche the brand operates in
- `target-platforms`: The platforms where hashtags will be used

## Outputs

- `hashtag-candidates`: List of candidate hashtags organized by category (branded, campaign, community, trending, niche)
- `volume-data`: Post volume and competition level for each candidate hashtag

## Process

1. Generate branded hashtag candidates (brand name, tagline, campaign names).
2. Research community and niche hashtags used by the target audience.
3. Identify trending and seasonal hashtags relevant to the industry.
4. Pull volume and competition data for each candidate.
