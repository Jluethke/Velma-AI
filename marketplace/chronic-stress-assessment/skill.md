# Chronic Stress Assessment

Monthly assessment: is your baseline stress level rising or falling? If rising, identify the structural cause and change it.

## Inputs

- `prevention-schedule`: Daily prevention
- `current-stress-level`: Starting state

## Outputs

- `monthly-stress-score`: Monthly average stress rating (1-10) compared to previous months
- `structural-changes`: If the trend is rising: the specific structural change needed (reduce workload, set boundaries, address relationship conflict, or change environment)

## Process

1. Rate your stress 1-10 at the end of each week. Average the 4 weekly scores for a monthly score.
2. Compare to previous months. A rising trend over 2+ months indicates a structural problem that daily interventions cannot solve. Something needs to change.
3. Structural changes: if the cause is workload, reduce it. If the cause is a relationship, address it. If the cause is financial, plan for it. If the cause is health, see a professional.
4. Daily stress management treats symptoms. Monthly assessment treats causes. Both are necessary.
