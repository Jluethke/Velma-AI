# Field Condition Assessor

Assess field conditions including soil test results, drainage, and rotation history.

## Inputs

- `soil-test-results`: Lab results for soil nutrient and pH levels
- `field-history`: Rotation history and prior crop performance

## Outputs

- `field-assessment`: Summary of field readiness and constraints
- `soil-amendment-needs`: Recommended soil amendments before planting

## Process

1. Review soil test results for nutrient levels, pH, and organic matter.
2. Evaluate drainage and compaction from field observation.
3. Summarize rotation history and any disease or pest carry-over risks.
4. List soil amendments needed before planting.
