# Anxiety Trigger Mapping

Identify and categorize anxiety triggers by context, frequency, and intensity.

## Inputs

- `recent_episodes`: Recent anxiety episodes and circumstances
- `life_domains`: Work, relationships, health, finances, etc.
- `physical_symptoms`: Physical manifestations experienced

## Outputs

- `trigger_inventory`: Categorized list of triggers
- `pattern_analysis`: Recurring patterns and timing
- `severity_baseline`: Current severity and frequency baseline

## Process

1. Log recent episodes with context
2. Categorize triggers by domain
3. Rate frequency and intensity per trigger
4. Identify time-of-day and situational patterns
5. Establish baseline severity for tracking progress
