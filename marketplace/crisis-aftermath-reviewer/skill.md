# Crisis Aftermath Reviewer

Monitor the aftermath and produce the crisis response report.

## Inputs

- `publication-log`: Log of all responses published
- `crisis-assessment`: The initial crisis assessment for comparison

## Outputs

- `aftermath-analysis`: Analysis of sentiment recovery, mention volume decline, and audience reaction to the response
- `crisis-response-report`: The complete crisis response report with timeline, actions, outcomes, and lessons learned

## Process

1. Monitor mention volume and sentiment for 48 to 72 hours post-response.
2. Track whether the response resolved the issue or escalated it.
3. Compile the full timeline from detection through resolution.
4. Document lessons learned and updates needed for the crisis playbook.
