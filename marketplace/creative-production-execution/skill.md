# Creative Production Execution

Execute the approved concept through draft, review, and revision cycles.

## Inputs

- `concept-options`: The concept options presented
- `project-plan`: The project timeline and milestones

## Outputs

- `production-deliverables`: The drafted deliverables ready for review
- `revision-log`: Record of revisions made during production

## Process

1. Produce the first draft of each deliverable based on the approved concept.
2. Run internal review with the creative lead.
3. Revise based on internal feedback.
4. Produce the deliverables and revision log.
