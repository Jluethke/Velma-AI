# Ueh Health Report Writer

Write the unit economics health check.

## Inputs

- `health-diagnosis`: The diagnosis of the unit
- `ltv-cac-ratio`: The headline ratio to report

## Outputs

- `unit-economics-report`: The margin, cost, value, ratio, and payback in one record
- `improvement-levers`: The levers ranked by expected effect

## Process

1. State the contribution margin, acquisition cost, lifetime value, ratio, and payback.
2. Summarize the diagnosis in plain terms.
3. List the levers ranked by expected effect.
