# Champion Scoring

Score each candidate on pain, authority, and willingness to advocate, and pick the primary champion.

## Inputs

- `problem-owners`: People who feel the problem
- `pain-intensity`: How sharply each feels it

## Outputs

- `champion-scores`: Each candidate scored on pain, authority, and willingness
- `primary-champion`: The candidate best placed to carry the deal internally

## Process

1. Score each candidate on three axes: how much they feel the pain, how much authority they hold, and how willing they are to advocate.
2. Weight willingness heavily, since a motivated mid-level champion often beats a senior bystander.
3. Pick the primary champion and name a backup, because champions change roles or leave.
4. Record what would have to be true for the backup to step in.
