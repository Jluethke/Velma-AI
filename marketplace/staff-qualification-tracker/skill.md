# Staff Qualification Tracker

Track staff qualifications, certifications, and background check status.

## Inputs

- `requirements-checklist`: The licensing requirements for staff qualifications

## Outputs

- `staff-compliance-status`: Each staff member's current compliance status (background checks, CPR/First Aid, training hours, credentials)
- `upcoming-expirations`: Certifications or clearances expiring in the next 90 days

## Process

1. Inventory each staff member's current credentials (degree, CDA, state credentials).
2. Verify background check completion and renewal dates.
3. Track CPR and First Aid certification expiration dates.
4. Calculate remaining training hours needed for the licensing period.
5. Flag any staff members approaching expiration on required items.
