# Incident Timeline Builder

Build a precise timeline of the incident from the earliest contributing event to the final outcome. Use facts and timestamps, not narratives.

## Inputs

- `incident-description`: What happened, in factual terms
- `incident-data`: Logs, timestamps, records, witness accounts, any evidence

## Outputs

- `incident-timeline`: Chronological sequence of events with timestamps, actions taken, and outcomes at each point
- `proximate-cause`: The immediate action or failure that directly produced the bad outcome

## Process

1. Collect all available evidence: system logs, timestamps, communications, physical evidence, witness statements.
2. Arrange events chronologically. Note the time between events; long gaps may indicate missed information.
3. Identify the proximate cause: the last action (or inaction) before the bad outcome. This is what traditional analysis stops at.
4. Do not assign blame. The goal is to understand what happened, not who to punish. Blame stops the analysis too early.
