# Office Action Responder

Respond to any USPTO office actions or refusals.

## Inputs

- `serial-number`: Application serial number
- `search-results`: Original clearance search results

## Outputs

- `response-filed`: Office action response with arguments and evidence
- `application-status`: Updated application status after response

## Process

1. Analyze the examining attorney's refusal or requirements
2. Draft arguments and provide evidence to overcome the refusal
3. File the response within the six-month deadline
