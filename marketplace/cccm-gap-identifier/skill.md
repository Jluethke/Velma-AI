# Cccm Gap Identifier

Identify the dimensions where you and your counterpart sit far apart and where that gap will do the most damage. Not every difference matters for every interaction. The gaps worth preparing for are the ones that intersect with what this specific conversation depends on.

## Inputs

- `your-positions`: Your positions on the eight dimensions
- `counterpart-positions`: The counterpart's estimated positions

## Outputs

- `key-gaps`: The dimensions where you and the counterpart sit far apart and the gap matters for this interaction
- `gap-risk-ranking`: The gaps ranked by how much damage a mismatch would do in this specific setting

## Process

1. Lay your positions beside the counterpart's and measure the distance on each dimension.
2. Keep the gaps that intersect with what this interaction depends on. A trust-dimension gap matters more for a new partnership than for a one-time transaction.
3. Rank the surviving gaps by the damage a mismatch would cause: a blown relationship, a misread yes, a deadline understood differently.
4. Focus on the top two or three. Trying to adjust for all eight at once produces stilted, unnatural communication that helps no one.
