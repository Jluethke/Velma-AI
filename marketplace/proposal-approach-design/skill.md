# Proposal Approach Design

Design the consulting approach and methodology for the proposal.

## Inputs

- `discovery-findings`: Findings from the discovery session
- `success-criteria`: Client's success criteria

## Outputs

- `proposed-approach`: The methodology, phases, and activities for the engagement
- `effort-estimate`: Estimated effort by phase, role, and total

## Process

1. Select the methodology or framework that fits the client's situation.
2. Break the engagement into phases with clear activities and outputs for each.
3. Estimate the effort for each phase by role (partner, senior, analyst).
4. Identify the key assumptions and risks that could change the estimate.
5. Validate that the approach addresses each of the client's success criteria.
