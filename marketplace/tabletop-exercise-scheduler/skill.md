# Tabletop Exercise Scheduler

Schedule the exercises across the year, coordinating with participant availability and business calendar.

## Inputs

- `complexity-schedule`: Ordered exercise sequence
- `scenario-packages`: Ready-to-run exercises

## Outputs

- `annual-schedule`: Dates, participants, locations, and logistics for each exercise
- `participant-invitations`: Invitations with pre-reading and role assignments

## Process

1. Identify required participants for each exercise (incident commander, SOC, legal, communications, executives)
2. Schedule quarterly, avoiding change freezes and major business events
3. Book rooms or video conferencing and prepare materials
4. Send invitations with pre-reading at least two weeks in advance
