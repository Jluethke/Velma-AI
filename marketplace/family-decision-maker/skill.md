# Family Decision Maker

Help families make decisions together: voting methods, consensus building, and compromise strategies.

## Inputs

- `decisions_needed`: Decisions on the agenda
- `stakeholders`: Who is affected by each decision
- `constraints`: Budget, time, or other constraints

## Outputs

- `decision_method`: Voting or consensus method per decision
- `options_laid_out`: Clear options with pros and cons
- `decisions_made`: Final decisions with reasoning

## Process

1. List decisions and who they affect
2. Present options with pros and cons
3. Choose decision method (vote, consensus, parent decides)
4. Document the decision and reasoning
5. Identify any follow-up needed
