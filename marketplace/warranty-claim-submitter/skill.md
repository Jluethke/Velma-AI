# Warranty Claim Submitter

Submit the warranty claim to the manufacturer with all required documentation.

## Inputs

- `repair-documentation`: Photos, measurements, and notes from the repair
- `warranty-labor-record`: Labor operations and parts used

## Outputs

- `claim-submission`: Submitted claim with the manufacturer claim number
- `expected-payment`: Expected payment amount and timeline based on the claim

## Process

1. Enter the claim in the manufacturer warranty system with the correct operation codes.
2. Attach the required documentation (photos, measurements, causal part number).
3. Submit the claim within the manufacturer filing deadline.
4. Record the claim number and expected payment amount.
