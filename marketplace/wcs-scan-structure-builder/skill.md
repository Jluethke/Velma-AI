# Wcs Scan Structure Builder

Structure the message so a reader can scan it in seconds. Headers, bullets, and bold let the eye find the action and the key facts without reading every word. A wall of text hides the ask inside it, and a hidden ask does not get done.

## Inputs

- `action-sentence`: The action to keep visible at the top
- `context-block`: The minimum context to lay out for scanning

## Outputs

- `scannable-layout`: The message arranged with headers, short bullets, and bold on the facts that drive the decision
- `formatting-rules`: The rules you applied, so your messages stay consistent and quick to scan over time

## Process

1. Keep the action sentence at the very top, before any header, so it is the first thing seen.
2. Break the context into short bullets. A bullet longer than two lines is a paragraph wearing a dot and should be split.
3. Bold only the few facts that drive the decision: the number, the date, the name. Bold everything and you have bolded nothing.
4. Add headers only if the message has distinct sections. A short message needs the action and a few bullets, not a table of contents.
