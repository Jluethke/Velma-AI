# Knowledge Transfer Planner

Plan and execute knowledge transfer from the departing employee.

## Inputs

- `employee-details`: Role and responsibilities
- `departure-reason`: Timeline context

## Outputs

- `knowledge-transfer-plan`: Plan for transferring responsibilities and institutional knowledge
- `transfer-completion-status`: Status of each transfer item

## Process

1. Identify the key responsibilities and ongoing projects
2. Assign each responsibility to a successor or interim owner
3. Schedule knowledge transfer sessions
4. Document critical processes and contacts
