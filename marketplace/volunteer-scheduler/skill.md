# Volunteer Scheduler

Assign roles and schedule shifts based on skills and availability.

## Inputs

- `approved-volunteers`: Onboarded volunteers
- `volunteer-needs`: Roles and time slots to fill

## Outputs

- `volunteer-schedule`: Shift assignments by date and role
- `coverage-gaps`: Unfilled slots needing attention

## Process

1. Match volunteers to roles based on skills and preferences
2. Collect availability and scheduling constraints
3. Build the schedule and confirm assignments
4. Identify and flag coverage gaps for additional recruitment
