# Survey Instrument Selector

Select the survey instrument and questions.

## Inputs

- `survey-goals`: What the organization wants to learn from the survey
- `prior-results`: Results from the previous survey cycle if available

## Outputs

- `survey-instrument`: Selected survey with question set
- `benchmarks`: External benchmarks to compare against

## Process

1. Review the survey goals and prior results
2. Select or design the question set covering key engagement drivers
3. Include demographic questions for segmentation
4. Identify external benchmarks for comparison
