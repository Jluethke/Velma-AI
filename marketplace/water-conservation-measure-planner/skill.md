# Water Conservation Measure Planner

Identify water conservation measures and calculate savings.

## Inputs

- `water-baseline`: Baseline water consumption by end use
- `waste-points`: Identified waste points

## Outputs

- `conservation-measures`: List of measures with estimated water savings (gallons), cost, and payback
- `water-conservation-plan`: The complete water conservation plan with targets and implementation schedule

## Process

1. For each waste point, define the corrective measure (low-flow fixtures, smart irrigation, leak repair, recycling).
2. Estimate the annual water savings in gallons and the associated cost savings.
3. Estimate the implementation cost and calculate the simple payback.
4. Compile the plan with targets, prioritized measures, and an implementation schedule.
