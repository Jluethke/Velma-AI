# Food Safety Audit Preparer

Prepare for the food safety audit.

## Inputs

- `record-system`: Complete records and procedures
- `applicable-standard`: Audit checklist items

## Outputs

- `audit-readiness-report`: Self-assessment score and items needing attention
- `audit-binder`: Organized documentation package for the auditor

## Process

1. Conduct a self-assessment using the audit checklist.
2. Organize all documentation in the audit binder.
3. Walk the facility and fields to verify practices match documentation.
4. Brief staff on audit procedures and their roles during the visit.
