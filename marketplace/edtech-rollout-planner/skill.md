# Edtech Rollout Planner

Plan and execute the full institutional rollout of the selected technology.

## Inputs

- `training-materials`: Faculty training resources
- `support-plan`: Ongoing support plan

## Outputs

- `rollout-timeline`: Phased rollout schedule by department or program
- `adoption-plan`: Complete technology adoption plan document

## Process

1. Design a phased rollout schedule (early adopters, then all departments)
2. Set adoption targets and monitoring checkpoints
3. Plan the communication campaign for faculty and students
4. Produce the complete adoption plan with success metrics
