# Financial Statement Preparer

Prepare the monthly financial statements: income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement.

## Inputs

- `reviewed-trial-balance`: Final reviewed trial balance
- `prior-period-financials`: Previous period financial statements for comparison

## Outputs

- `income-statement`: Profit and loss statement for the period
- `balance-sheet`: Balance sheet as of period end
- `cash-flow-statement`: Statement of cash flows for the period

## Process

1. Map trial balance accounts to income statement line items and compute net income.
2. Map trial balance accounts to balance sheet line items and verify assets equal liabilities plus equity.
3. Prepare the cash flow statement using the indirect method.
4. Add comparative period columns and variance analysis.
