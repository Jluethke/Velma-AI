# Competitive Frame Redefiner

Redefine your competitive frame based on the job, not the product category. Your real competitors are whatever the customer would hire instead, even if it is in a completely different category.

## Inputs

- `job-statements`: The jobs your product is hired for
- `forces-diagram`: The forces driving each job

## Outputs

- `real-competitor-map`: For each job: the set of solutions the customer considers (including non-obvious ones from other categories, including doing nothing)
- `competitive-advantage-by-job`: For each job: where you win against the real competitors and where you lose

## Process

1. For each job, list every solution the interviewees mentioned considering. This includes: direct competitors, products from other categories, manual workarounds, hiring a person, and doing nothing.
2. 'Doing nothing' is often the strongest competitor. The force of habit keeps people in the status quo even when better options exist.
3. For each real competitor, map where you are stronger and weaker on the four forces. You win when you reduce anxiety better, or when you have a stronger pull, or when you address the push more directly.
4. If a real competitor is from a completely different category, that is valuable information. It means your marketing should address the job, not the category.
