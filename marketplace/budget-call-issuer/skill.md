# Budget Call Issuer

Issue the budget call with instructions and templates to departments.

## Inputs

- `fiscal-year`: The fiscal year being budgeted
- `budget-guidelines`: Policy guidelines, constraints, and priorities for the budget cycle

## Outputs

- `budget-call-package`: The budget call document with instructions and submission templates
- `submission-deadline`: Deadline and schedule for department budget submissions

## Process

1. Prepare the budget call letter with fiscal year priorities and constraints.
2. Include submission templates, baseline figures, and instructions.
3. Set the submission deadline and review schedule.
4. Distribute to all department heads.
