# Moat Business Model Capture

Capture the business model, customers, and competitors so the moat assessment has concrete facts to work from. Vague inputs produce flattering, useless moat scores.

## Inputs

- `business-description`: What the business sells and how it makes money
- `customer-base`: Who the customers are and why they buy
- `main-competitors`: The rivals who could take this business

## Outputs

- `business-profile`: A tight profile of the business model and customers
- `competitor-set`: The named set of real competitors

## Process

1. Write how the business makes money in two or three sentences.
2. Describe who buys, how often, and what they would use instead.
3. Name the real competitors, including the next-best alternative a customer could switch to.
4. Keep the profile factual. Save judgments about strength for the scoring steps.
