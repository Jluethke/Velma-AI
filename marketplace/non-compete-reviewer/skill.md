# Non Compete Reviewer

Review non-compete and non-solicitation provisions for scope and enforceability.

## Inputs

- `agreement-text`: Employment agreement text
- `position-details`: Role and industry context

## Outputs

- `restrictive-covenant-analysis`: Analysis of non-compete and non-solicitation terms
- `enforceability-risk`: Enforceability assessment by jurisdiction

## Process

1. Identify the geographic scope, duration, and activity restrictions
2. Assess reasonableness of the restrictions for the role
3. Check enforceability in the employee's state of residence
