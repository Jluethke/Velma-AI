# Environment Review Cycle

Review the redesigned environment after 2 weeks and again after 6 weeks to check whether the behavior change is holding.

## Inputs

- `friction-changes`: Physical and digital changes made
- `social-changes`: Social environment changes made

## Outputs

- `behavior-tracking-results`: Actual frequency of each target behavior over the review period compared to the target
- `environment-adjustments`: Changes that worked (keep), changes that did not work (modify), and new friction points discovered (address)

## Process

1. Track each target behavior daily for the review period. Use a simple tally: did the behavior happen today (yes/no/how many times)?
2. At the 2-week mark, compare actual frequency to target. Behaviors where the environment change worked (within 20% of target) need no adjustment. Behaviors that did not change need a different environmental intervention.
3. For behaviors that did not change, look for cues you missed in the original audit. There is usually a trigger you did not remove or a friction point you did not add.
4. At the 6-week mark, the behaviors that held through 6 weeks are becoming habits. Shift attention to the ones that are still struggling. The environment may need a more aggressive redesign.
