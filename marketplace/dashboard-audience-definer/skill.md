# Dashboard Audience Definer

Define who reads the dashboard, what decisions they make, and how often they look.

## Inputs

- `audience`: Who reads the dashboard and their role
- `decisions`: The decisions the dashboard must inform

## Outputs

- `dashboard-purpose`: The audience, decisions, cadence, and ranked information requirements
- `information-requirements`: The specific questions the dashboard must answer, ranked by importance

## Process

1. Identify the primary audience: executives (headline), managers (diagnosis), operators (detail).
2. List the decisions and the questions each decision needs answered.
3. Rank questions by importance. The most important gets the most prominent position.
4. Set the cadence: match it to the decision frequency.
