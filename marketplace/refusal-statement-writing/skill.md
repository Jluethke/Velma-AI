# Refusal Statement Writing

Write the things you will not do as clear statements a buyer can understand in one read.

## Inputs

- `owned-constraints`: The constraints you will state publicly

## Outputs

- `refusal-statements`: Plain statements of what you will not do and will not be
- `rationale-notes`: The reason behind each refusal, in the buyer's interest

## Process

1. Turn each owned constraint into a plain statement of what you will not do.
2. Write the reason in terms of the buyer's benefit, since a refusal without a reason sounds like a weakness.
3. Keep each statement short enough to repeat, since a positioning line that cannot be repeated does not travel.
4. Avoid hedging words that soften the refusal, since the clarity is the point.
