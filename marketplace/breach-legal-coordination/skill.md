# Breach Legal Coordination

Coordinate with legal counsel and compliance on notification obligations.

## Inputs

- `data-inventory`: Types of data exposed and jurisdictions affected
- `affected-individual-count`: Number and categories of affected individuals

## Outputs

- `notification-requirements`: List of regulatory and contractual notification obligations with deadlines
- `legal-guidance`: Legal counsel guidance on disclosure and liability

## Process

1. Identify all applicable breach notification laws by jurisdiction (GDPR, state laws, HIPAA)
2. Determine contractual notification obligations to partners and customers
3. Calculate notification deadlines for each jurisdiction
4. Obtain legal guidance on disclosure scope and liability exposure
