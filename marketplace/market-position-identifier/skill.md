# Market Position Identifier

Identify roles that are above or below market and assess the business impact.

## Inputs

- `comp-analysis`: Compensation analysis
- `pay-ratios`: Compa-ratios

## Outputs

- `market-position-summary`: Summary of market position by role and level
- `risk-assessment`: Attrition and recruiting risk from pay gaps

## Process

1. Flag roles below the 25th percentile as high risk
2. Flag roles above the 75th percentile for cost review
3. Cross-reference with attrition data and time-to-fill metrics
4. Prioritize adjustments by business impact
