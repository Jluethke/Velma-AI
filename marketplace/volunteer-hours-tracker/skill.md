# Volunteer Hours Tracker

Track volunteer hours, contributions, and attendance.

## Inputs

- `volunteer-schedule`: Shift assignments
- `approved-volunteers`: Active volunteer roster

## Outputs

- `hours-log`: Tracked hours by volunteer and role
- `attendance-report`: Attendance rates and no-show patterns

## Process

1. Set up the time tracking system (sign-in sheets, app, or CRM)
2. Record hours for each shift
3. Flag no-shows and chronic absences
4. Generate monthly summaries for reporting and recognition
