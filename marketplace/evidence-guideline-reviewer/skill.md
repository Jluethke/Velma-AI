# Evidence Guideline Reviewer

Review the evidence base and clinical guidelines for the condition.

## Inputs

- `target-condition`: The condition or procedure for the pathway
- `current-practice`: Description of current care processes

## Outputs

- `evidence-summary`: Summary of key evidence and guideline recommendations
- `best-practice-list`: Evidence-based practices to incorporate

## Process

1. Search clinical guidelines from major organizations.
2. Review systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
3. Identify the highest-quality evidence for each care decision.
