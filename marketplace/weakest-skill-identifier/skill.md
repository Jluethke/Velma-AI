# Weakest Skill Identifier

Identify which of the three skills in your stack is currently weakest by measuring output quality, not self-perception.

## Inputs

- `chosen-stack`: The 3 skills in your target stack
- `skill-inventory`: Complete skill list with percentiles

## Outputs

- `weakest-skill`: The skill that needs the most improvement to balance the stack
- `gap-analysis`: Specific sub-skills or areas within the weakest skill where you fall short

## Process

1. For each of the three skills, gather evidence of your current output quality: recent work samples, peer feedback, test results, or competition benchmarks.
2. Compare your evidence against people who are solidly in the 75th percentile for that skill. Where do you fall short?
3. The weakest skill is the one with the largest gap between your current level and the 75th percentile. If all three are above 75th, the weakest is the one closest to dropping below.
4. Within the weakest skill, list the specific sub-skills or knowledge areas where you are deficient. These become your practice targets.
