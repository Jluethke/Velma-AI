# Crop Selection Advisor

Select crops based on market demand, climate zone, and field constraints.

## Inputs

- `field-assessment`: Field readiness and constraints
- `market-conditions`: Current commodity prices and demand forecasts

## Outputs

- `crop-selections`: Selected crops with acreage allocation
- `expected-yields`: Projected yields per crop per acre

## Process

1. Match field conditions to crop suitability by climate zone.
2. Evaluate market demand and price forecasts for candidate crops.
3. Allocate acreage across selected crops based on profitability and risk.
4. Estimate expected yields using historical data and soil quality.
