# Settlement Documenter

Document the settlement or outcome of the dispute.

## Inputs

- `process-log`: Log of actions taken
- `preliminary-outcome`: The outcome to document

## Outputs

- `settlement-agreement`: The settlement agreement or judgment documenting the outcome
- `dispute-resolution-record`: Complete record of the dispute from claim through resolution

## Process

1. Draft the settlement agreement with all terms (payment, release, confidentiality, non-admission).
2. Obtain all required signatures.
3. If litigated, record the judgment or verdict.
4. Compile the complete dispute resolution record.
