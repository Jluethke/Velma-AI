# Transfer Verification

Verify the transfer is complete. The knowledge holder should be able to step away without the operation degrading.

## Inputs

- `milestone-criteria`: The criteria for each apprenticeship stage
- `captured-knowledge-documents`: The documented knowledge

## Outputs

- `transfer-verification-results`: The test results: did the receiver handle real situations correctly without help? Were there knowledge gaps? What additional transfer is needed?
- `transfer-completion-record`: The complete record: what was transferred, how it was verified, what gaps remain, and the plan to address them

## Process

1. Test the transfer with real situations: the receiver handles a full cycle of work without the knowledge holder present. The knowledge holder reviews afterward.
2. Identify the gaps: which decisions did the receiver make differently than the knowledge holder would have? Were the differences improvements, neutral, or errors?
3. For errors, conduct a targeted transfer session on the specific knowledge gap. For improvements, update the documented knowledge.
4. The transfer is complete when the receiver can handle 90% of situations without assistance and knows when to escalate the remaining 10%.
