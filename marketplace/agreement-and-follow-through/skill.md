# Agreement And Follow Through

Close the conversation with a specific agreement and follow-through plan. A conversation without an agreement is a discussion. An agreement without follow-through is fiction.

## Inputs

- `option-list`: The options generated
- `evaluation-criteria`: The criteria for choosing

## Outputs

- `agreement-record`: The specific agreement: what each party will do, by when, and how success will be measured
- `follow-up-plan`: The follow-up: when will you check in on the agreement, and what happens if it is not being followed

## Process

1. Select the option or combine elements from multiple options. Confirm agreement by restating it: 'So we are agreeing that [person A will do X by date Y] and [person B will do Z by date W]. Is that right?'
2. Write down the agreement. Verbal agreements are forgotten or remembered differently. Even an email summary sent within 24 hours is better than memory.
3. Set the follow-up: 'Let us check in on [date] to see how this is going.' The follow-up is not optional. Without it, the agreement degrades.
4. If the conversation did not produce an agreement, acknowledge it honestly: 'We did not reach agreement today. Here is what I heard. Let us schedule another conversation for [date] after we have both had time to think.' An honest non-agreement is better than a false agreement.
