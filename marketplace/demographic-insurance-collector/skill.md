# Demographic Insurance Collector

Collect demographic and insurance information from the patient.

## Inputs

- `patient-name`: Full legal name of the patient
- `contact-info`: Phone, email, and address

## Outputs

- `demographic-record`: Structured demographic data
- `insurance-details`: Payer, policy number, group number

## Process

1. Collect name, date of birth, gender, and address.
2. Record primary and secondary insurance information.
3. Capture emergency contact details.
