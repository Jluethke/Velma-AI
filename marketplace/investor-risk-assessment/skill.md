# Investor Risk Assessment

Determine risk tolerance based on age, goals, timeline, income stability, and emotional comfort with volatility.

## Inputs

- `age`: Current age
- `investment_timeline`: Years until funds needed
- `income_stability`: Stable salary, variable, freelance, etc.
- `existing_savings`: Emergency fund and savings status

## Outputs

- `risk_profile`: Conservative, moderate, or aggressive
- `asset_allocation_target`: Suggested stock/bond/cash split
- `risk_capacity_notes`: Factors that constrain or expand risk

## Process

1. Assess time horizon and age-based risk capacity
2. Evaluate income stability and emergency reserves
3. Gauge emotional tolerance for drawdowns
4. Determine risk profile
5. Map profile to target allocation
