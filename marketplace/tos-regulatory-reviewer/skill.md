# Tos Regulatory Reviewer

Review the ToS for compliance with applicable consumer protection regulations.

## Inputs

- `aup-text`: AUP text
- `dispute-section`: Dispute resolution
- `liability-section`: Liability provisions

## Outputs

- `compliance-notes`: Regulatory compliance findings and required changes
- `tos-final-draft`: Final terms of service document

## Process

1. Check COPPA compliance if the service may be used by minors
2. Review accessibility and ADA considerations
3. Verify consumer protection compliance in target jurisdictions
