# Kb Article Writer

Write knowledge base articles for the top support issues.

## Inputs

- `top-issues-list`: Issues to write articles for
- `resolution-patterns`: Common resolution steps from ticket history

## Outputs

- `draft-articles`: Written knowledge base articles
- `article-metadata`: Tags, categories, and keywords for each article

## Process

1. For each issue, extract the resolution steps from ticket history.
2. Write the article with a problem statement, steps to resolve, and expected outcome.
3. Add screenshots or diagrams where helpful.
4. Tag each article with category, keywords, and difficulty level.
