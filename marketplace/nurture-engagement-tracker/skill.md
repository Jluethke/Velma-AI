# Nurture Engagement Tracker

Track engagement metrics across the nurture sequence.

## Inputs

- `touchpoint-plan`: Nurture touchpoints
- `scoring-model`: Lead scoring rules

## Outputs

- `engagement-report`: Engagement rates per touchpoint and segment
- `nurture-sequence-document`: Complete nurture sequence with performance data

## Process

1. Collect engagement data per touchpoint
2. Calculate conversion rates between funnel stages
3. Produce the nurture sequence document with recommendations
