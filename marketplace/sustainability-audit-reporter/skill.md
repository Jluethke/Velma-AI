# Sustainability Audit Reporter

Compile the sustainability audit findings into the final report.

## Inputs

- `assessment-findings`: Findings by criterion
- `improvement-opportunities`: Improvement opportunities identified

## Outputs

- `sustainability-audit-report`: The complete audit report with findings, non-conformances, and recommendations
- `corrective-action-plan`: Plan for addressing non-conformances with owners and deadlines

## Process

1. Summarize the overall audit results: number of conformances, non-conformances, and observations.
2. Detail each non-conformance with the evidence, the criterion violated, and the recommended corrective action.
3. Present the improvement opportunities with their potential impact.
4. Produce the corrective action plan with owners, deadlines, and verification methods.
