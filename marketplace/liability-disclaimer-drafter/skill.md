# Liability Disclaimer Drafter

Draft liability limitations, disclaimers, and warranty exclusions.

## Inputs

- `service-scope`: Service scope
- `ip-ownership-clause`: IP provisions

## Outputs

- `liability-section`: Limitation of liability provisions
- `warranty-disclaimers`: Warranty disclaimers and as-is language

## Process

1. Draft the limitation of liability with a cap
2. Include warranty disclaimers (as-is, no merchantability)
3. Add force majeure and service availability disclaimers
