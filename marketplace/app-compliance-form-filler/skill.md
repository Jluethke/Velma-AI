# App Compliance Form Filler

Complete the required developer forms: privacy policy, content rating, and data safety.

## Inputs

- `listing-package`: The prepared listing
- `app-name`: The app name

## Outputs

- `compliance-forms`: All required forms completed (privacy policy, content rating, data safety declarations)
- `privacy-policy-url`: URL of the published privacy policy

## Process

1. Write or update the privacy policy covering all data the app collects.
2. Complete the content rating questionnaire.
3. Fill out the data safety section (what data is collected, how it is used, whether it is shared).
4. Publish the privacy policy at a public URL.
