# Resource Strategy Builder

Build a just-in-time resource strategy: identify what you will need to learn at each milestone and where you will find it, but do not study it all upfront.

## Inputs

- `milestone-plan`: The milestones with hard parts
- `target-skill`: The skill being learned

## Outputs

- `resource-map`: For each milestone, the 1-3 resources (tutorials, documentation, examples, people) you will consult when you hit the hard part
- `learning-triggers`: The specific moment in each milestone when you should stop building and start learning (the point where you are stuck, not the point where you are bored)

## Process

1. Do NOT study all the material before starting the project. Upfront study produces the illusion of preparation without the reality of skill. Study when you are stuck, not before.
2. For each milestone, identify the hard part and the 1-3 resources most likely to help when you encounter it. A good resource is specific (covers this exact topic), practical (shows examples), and concise (does not require 40 hours of reading).
3. Define the learning trigger: the moment when you have tried for 30 minutes and are genuinely stuck. That is when you consult the resource. Not before. The struggle is the learning.
4. If a resource does not help within 20 minutes, switch to a different resource or ask a person. Spending 3 hours on a bad tutorial is wasted practice time.
