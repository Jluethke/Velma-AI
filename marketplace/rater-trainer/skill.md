# Rater Trainer

Train raters on the codebook using worked examples, practice rounds, and group discussion of disagreements.

## Inputs

- `codebook-draft`: The codebook to train on
- `training-dataset`: A small dataset with known correct codes for practice

## Outputs

- `training-log`: Record of training sessions, questions raised, and clarifications made
- `rater-readiness-assessment`: Whether each rater demonstrated sufficient understanding to proceed

## Process

1. Present the codebook and walk through each code with examples.
2. Have raters independently code the training dataset.
3. Compare results and discuss every disagreement as a group.
4. Assess readiness: raters proceed only if training agreement exceeds the threshold.
