# Legal Basis Documenter

Document the legal basis and purpose for each data processing activity.

## Inputs

- `data-inventory`: Personal data inventory
- `processing-map`: Data processing map

## Outputs

- `purpose-register`: Register of processing purposes and legal bases
- `consent-requirements`: Activities requiring explicit consent

## Process

1. Assign a legal basis to each processing activity (consent, contract, legitimate interest)
2. Document the specific purpose for each processing activity
3. Identify activities requiring explicit opt-in consent
