# Copy Audience Researcher

Research the audience and product positioning for the campaign.

## Inputs

- `product-info`: Product or service description and features
- `campaign-objectives`: Goals, budget, and target audience

## Outputs

- `audience-insight`: Audience pain points, desires, and language patterns
- `positioning-brief`: How the product solves the audience's problem

## Process

1. Review existing customer research and testimonials
2. Identify the primary pain points the product addresses
3. Analyze competitor messaging for gaps and opportunities
4. Define the product positioning relative to alternatives
