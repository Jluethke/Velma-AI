# Daily Cash Reconciler

Reconcile the daily cash position and produce the treasury report.

## Inputs

- `investment-positions`: Current investment portfolio
- `facility-utilization`: Current borrowing levels

## Outputs

- `reconciled-position`: Actual versus forecast cash position reconciliation
- `treasury-management-report`: Daily treasury report with positions, actions, and variances

## Process

1. Compare actual balances against the forecast.
2. Identify and explain variances.
3. Compile the daily treasury report with all positions, actions, and yield metrics.
