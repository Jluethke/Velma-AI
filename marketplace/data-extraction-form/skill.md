# Data Extraction Form

Extract structured data from each included study using a standardized form.

## Inputs

- `screened-set`: full-text studies that passed screening
- `structured-question`: PICO question guiding which data to extract

## Outputs

- `extraction-table`: structured table of extracted data per study
- `extraction-discrepancies`: items where extractors disagreed

## Process

1. Design the extraction form covering study characteristics, methods, participants, interventions, outcomes, and results
2. Pilot the form on three studies and revise unclear fields
3. Extract data from each study independently (dual extraction where feasible)
4. Reconcile discrepancies and finalize the extraction table
