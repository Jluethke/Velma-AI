# Feedback Collection Mechanism

Design the collection mechanism: how feedback is gathered, when, and from whom.

## Inputs

- `sbi-guide`: The SBI framework guide
- `feedback-scope`: What is in and out of scope

## Outputs

- `collection-design`: The feedback collection process: trigger events (after a meeting, after a sprint, after a customer interaction), the collection method (form, conversation, retrospective), and the storage
- `frequency-and-timing`: When feedback is collected: event-triggered (within 48 hours of the behavior) vs. periodic (weekly, monthly). Event-triggered is more accurate.

## Process

1. Define the trigger events: what prompts feedback collection? After a client meeting, after a sprint, after a product release, after a significant interaction. Event-triggered feedback is more specific than calendar-triggered.
2. Design the collection form: three fields matching SBI: 'Describe the situation (when and where),' 'Describe the specific behavior (what happened),' 'Describe the impact (what was the effect).' Reject entries that skip any field.
3. Set the timing: feedback collected within 48 hours of the behavior is accurate. Feedback collected at the end of the quarter is a popularity contest based on recency.
4. Define who provides feedback to whom: direct reports to managers, peers to peers, customers to the team, self-assessment. 360-degree feedback uses all four.
