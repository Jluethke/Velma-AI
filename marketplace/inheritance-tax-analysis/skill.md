# Inheritance Tax Analysis

Analyze tax implications of the inheritance: estate tax, income tax on distributions, and step-up basis.

## Inputs

- `inheritance_type`: Types of assets inherited
- `approximate_value`: Value of inheritance
- `state_of_residence`: State for state tax analysis
- `current_income`: Annual income for bracket analysis

## Outputs

- `tax_obligations`: Taxes owed and when
- `basis_analysis`: Stepped-up basis for inherited assets
- `tax_planning_opportunities`: Ways to minimize tax impact

## Process

1. Determine if estate tax applies
2. Analyze income tax on IRA or retirement distributions
3. Calculate stepped-up cost basis on investments and property
4. Check state inheritance or estate tax
5. Identify tax planning strategies
