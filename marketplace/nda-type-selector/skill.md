# Nda Type Selector

Determine the NDA type based on the business relationship and information flow.

## Inputs

- `business-context`: Description of the business relationship
- `information-flow`: Which parties disclose confidential information

## Outputs

- `nda-type`: Mutual or one-way NDA recommendation
- `party-roles`: Disclosing and receiving party designations

## Process

1. Assess whether information flows one way or both ways
2. Determine if mutual or unilateral NDA is appropriate
3. Assign disclosing and receiving party roles
