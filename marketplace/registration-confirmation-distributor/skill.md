# Registration Confirmation Distributor

Confirm registrations and distribute program information to participants.

## Inputs

- `capacity-status`: Confirmed registrations
- `waitlist-queue`: Waitlist status for pending registrants

## Outputs

- `confirmation-record`: All confirmations sent with participant details and payment receipts
- `program-info-package`: Information package distributed to confirmed participants (schedule, rules, contacts)

## Process

1. Send registration confirmation with payment receipt.
2. Distribute the information package (schedule, rules, equipment list, contacts, venue directions).
3. Send waitlist status notifications with expected timeline.
4. Produce the confirmation and distribution record.
