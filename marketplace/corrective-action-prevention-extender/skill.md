# Corrective Action Prevention Extender

Prevent recurrence and extend to similar processes.

## Inputs

- `permanent-actions`: Corrective actions
- `verified-cause`: Root cause

## Outputs

- `prevention-actions`: Systemic prevention actions (D7)
- `horizontal-deployment`: Similar products or processes where the same fix was applied

## Process

1. Identify systemic changes to prevent recurrence (update FMEA, control plan, procedures)
2. Identify similar products, processes, or plants where the same risk exists
3. Deploy the corrective action horizontally to those areas
4. Update training materials and error-proofing devices
