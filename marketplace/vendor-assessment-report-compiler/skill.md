# Vendor Assessment Report Compiler

Compile the control evaluation, risk rating, and contract requirements into a vendor assessment report with an executive summary and a go or no-go recommendation.

## Inputs

- `control-evaluation`: Per-control ratings and findings.
- `risk-rating`: Overall vendor risk tier and per-finding severity.
- `contract-security-requirements`: Clauses and obligations to add to the agreement.

## Outputs

- `vendor-assessment-report`: Full report with scope, control results, findings, risk rating, and contract requirements.
- `executive-summary`: One-page summary with the risk tier, the top findings, and a go or no-go recommendation.

## Process

1. Assemble the scope, control results, findings, and risk rating into one report.
2. Attach the contract requirements and the remediation conditions.
3. Write a one-page summary with the risk tier and the top findings.
4. State a go or no-go recommendation and the conditions that would change it.
