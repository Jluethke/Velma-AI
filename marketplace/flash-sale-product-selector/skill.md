# Flash Sale Product Selector

Select products, set discount levels, and reserve inventory.

## Inputs

- `product-catalog`: Available products for the sale
- `sale-goals`: Revenue and inventory clearance goals

## Outputs

- `sale-products`: Selected products with discount levels
- `inventory-reserves`: Reserved inventory for the sale

## Process

1. Select products based on excess inventory, margin room, or traffic goals.
2. Set discount levels that meet margin floors.
3. Reserve inventory to prevent overselling.
4. Calculate the expected revenue and margin impact.
