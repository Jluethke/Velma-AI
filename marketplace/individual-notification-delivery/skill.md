# Individual Notification Delivery

Notify the affected individuals about the breach.

## Inputs

- `breach-assessment`: The breach assessment
- `regulatory-notification`: What was reported to the regulator

## Outputs

- `individual-notification`: The notification sent to affected individuals
- `delivery-record`: Record of notification delivery (method, date, count)

## Process

1. Draft the individual notification in plain language (what happened, what data was affected, what we are doing, what they should do).
2. Select the delivery method (email, letter, website notice) based on the number of individuals and contact information available.
3. Send the notification to all affected individuals.
4. Set up a support channel (phone, email, FAQ page) for questions from affected individuals.
