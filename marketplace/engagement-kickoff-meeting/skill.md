# Engagement Kickoff Meeting

Conduct the engagement kickoff meeting with the client.

## Inputs

- `reporting-schedule`: Agreed reporting cadence
- `report-templates`: Report templates

## Outputs

- `kickoff-minutes`: Minutes from the kickoff meeting including decisions and action items
- `engagement-setup-record`: Complete record of the engagement setup (SOW, tools, reporting, kickoff)

## Process

1. Present the engagement overview: objectives, scope, team, timeline, and governance.
2. Walk through the communication plan and reporting cadence.
3. Confirm roles, responsibilities, and escalation paths.
4. Capture action items and assign owners with due dates.
5. Distribute the kickoff minutes and confirm the engagement is officially underway.
