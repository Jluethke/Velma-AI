# Meeting Hygiene Rollout

Roll the cancellations and the survivor standards out together and track the time reclaimed. The reform holds only if the reclaimed hours are visible and the standards are enforced automatically rather than by willpower.

## Inputs

- `cancelled-meetings`: The meetings removed
- `reclaimed-hours`: Hours returned by cancelling
- `action-protocol`: The capture routine for survivors

## Outputs

- `rollout-plan`: The plan to put cancellations and standards in place
- `time-reclaimed-estimate`: Total annual time returned by the full reform

## Process

1. Sequence the cancellations and the survivor standards into a single rollout.
2. Make the agenda, attendee, and summary rules the default so they do not rely on willpower.
3. Add the hours reclaimed by cancelling to the hours saved by trimming invites.
4. Set a review date to confirm meetings did not quietly creep back onto the calendar.
