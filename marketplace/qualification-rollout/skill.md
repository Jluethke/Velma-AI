# Qualification Rollout

Fold the questions, copy, and criteria into the live qualification process and set the metric that proves pipeline quality improved.

## Inputs

- `screening-questions`: The screening questions
- `self-select-copy`: The self-select copy
- `targeting-criteria`: The right-fit targeting criteria

## Outputs

- `qualification-playbook`: The combined playbook reps follow to screen, exit, or advance a lead
- `pipeline-quality-metrics`: Metrics and a review date to confirm fewer bad-fit deals enter the pipeline

## Process

1. Assemble the questions, scoring rubric, self-select copy, and targeting criteria into one short playbook.
2. Give reps a scripted, respectful way to exit a call when the disqualifiers trigger.
3. Choose the metric that should improve, such as win rate, average deal size, or early churn.
4. Set a review date to compare the metric against the baseline before the changes went live.
