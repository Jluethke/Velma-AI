# Landscaping Job Costing

Track actual costs per job against the estimate for profitability analysis.

## Inputs

- `crew-assignments`: Completed job records with actual hours
- `estimate-worksheet`: Original estimates for comparison

## Outputs

- `job-cost-report`: Actual vs estimated costs per job with variance analysis
- `landscaping-operations-plan`: Complete landscaping business operations plan

## Process

1. Record actual labor hours per job from crew timesheets.
2. Record actual material quantities used versus estimated.
3. Calculate the gross margin per job and compare to the estimate.
4. Identify patterns in cost overruns and adjust future estimates.
