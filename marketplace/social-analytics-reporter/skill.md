# Social Analytics Reporter

Produce the analytics report with recommendations.

## Inputs

- `performance-analysis`: The performance analysis
- `audience-insights`: The audience behavior insights
- `content-rankings`: Content performance rankings

## Outputs

- `analytics-report`: The complete analytics report with metrics, trends, and recommendations
- `action-items`: Specific adjustments to make in the next period (content mix, posting times, platform focus)

## Process

1. Summarize the key metrics and period-over-period changes.
2. Highlight the top findings (what worked, what did not, what changed).
3. Recommend specific adjustments to content strategy, posting schedule, and platform allocation.
4. List the action items with owners and deadlines.
