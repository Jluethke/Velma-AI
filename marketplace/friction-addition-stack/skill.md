# Friction Addition Stack

Stack three or four friction points against the decrease behavior, because a single obstacle is usually not enough to interrupt an automatic habit.

## Inputs

- `decrease-friction-points`: The cues and steps that make the decrease behavior easy
- `defined-decrease-target`: The decrease behavior

## Outputs

- `friction-addition-stack`: A stack of three to four obstacles placed between you and the decrease behavior
- `friction-point-count`: The count of obstacles in the stack, confirmed to be at least three

## Process

1. Remove the cue first. Delete the app from the home screen, move the snack to a closed high cabinet, take the television remote out of the room.
2. Add steps to starting the behavior. Logging out after each use, unplugging a device, or storing a trigger item in another room each add a decision point.
3. Insert a delay rule, such as waiting ten minutes before acting on the urge. The urge often passes inside that window.
4. Count the obstacles and confirm there are at least three. One obstacle is cleared without thought. Three or four force a conscious choice each time.
