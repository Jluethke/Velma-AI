# Commitment And Follow Through

Help them commit to action and follow through on their self-generated conclusion.

## Inputs

- `conclusion-reached`: Their self-generated conclusion
- `persuasion-goal`: The original goal

## Outputs

- `action-commitment`: The specific action they commit to, stated in their own words
- `follow-up-plan`: When you will check in on progress

## Process

1. Ask: 'What is the first step you would take?' Self-generated first steps have higher follow-through.
2. Ask: 'When would you do that by?' A deadline makes it concrete.
3. Offer support, not direction: 'How can I help?' positions you as an ally.
4. Set a follow-up for accountability.
