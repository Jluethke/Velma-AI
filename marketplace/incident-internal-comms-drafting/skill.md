# Incident Internal Comms Drafting

Draft internal communications for different audience levels.

## Inputs

- `incident-summary`: Summary of the incident
- `stakeholder-list`: Internal stakeholders and their information needs

## Outputs

- `executive-briefing`: Briefing for executive leadership
- `staff-notification`: Notification for affected staff and IT teams

## Process

1. Draft an executive briefing with impact, response status, and decisions needed
2. Draft a technical briefing for IT and security staff
3. Draft a general notification for affected business units
4. Review all drafts with legal before distribution
