# Podcast Guest Prep Sender

Send pre-interview preparation materials to the guest.

## Inputs

- `confirmed-guest`: The booked guest details
- `booking-details`: Recording logistics

## Outputs

- `prep-package`: The preparation materials sent to the guest (topics, questions, format)
- `interview-outline`: The interview outline for the host

## Process

1. Research the guest's background, recent work, and talking points.
2. Draft the interview questions and topic flow.
3. Send the guest a prep document with the topics, format, and any requests (bio, headshot, links).
4. Finalize the host's interview outline with follow-up questions.
