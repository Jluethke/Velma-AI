# Proposal Surface Scan

List every element of your proposal that touches someone's budget, authority, status, workload, or comfort. These are the surfaces where objections grow.

## Inputs

- `proposal-description`: The proposal, plan, or ask you need to defend
- `stakeholder-list`: Who will be affected by or must approve this proposal

## Outputs

- `objection-surfaces`: List of proposal elements that touch budget, authority, status, workload, or comfort for each stakeholder
- `stakeholder-exposure-map`: For each stakeholder, what this proposal costs them (money, time, power, convenience)

## Process

1. For each element of the proposal, ask: who pays for this, who loses authority, who has more work, who looks bad, who is uncomfortable?
2. Map each stakeholder to the specific cost they bear. A proposal with zero cost to a stakeholder gets zero objection from that stakeholder.
3. Rank stakeholders by how much the proposal costs them. The person who loses the most will object the loudest.
4. Note asymmetries: if the proposal benefits person A at the expense of person B, person B's objection is predictable and rational.
