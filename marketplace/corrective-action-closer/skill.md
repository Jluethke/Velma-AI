# Corrective Action Closer

Verify effectiveness and close the corrective action.

## Inputs

- `prevention-actions`: Prevention actions
- `verification-results`: Verification data

## Outputs

- `effectiveness-verification`: Confirmation of sustained improvement over monitoring period
- `corrective-action-report`: Complete 8D report (D8)

## Process

1. Monitor the process for the defined effectiveness period (typically 60 to 90 days)
2. Verify that the problem has not recurred
3. Recognize the team (D8)
4. Close the corrective action record and archive the 8D report
