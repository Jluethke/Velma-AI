# Coordination Meeting Runner

Hold the weekly subcontractor coordination meeting.

## Inputs

- `scope-matrix`: Subcontractor scope matrix
- `contact-directory`: Subcontractor contact directory

## Outputs

- `meeting-minutes`: Minutes from the coordination meeting
- `action-items`: Action items assigned during the meeting

## Process

1. Prepare the agenda covering schedule, access, deliveries, and conflicts.
2. Run the meeting with all subcontractor representatives.
3. Document decisions, commitments, and action items.
4. Distribute the minutes within 24 hours.
