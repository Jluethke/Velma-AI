# Professional Help Navigator

Guide the decision to seek professional help: therapist types, insurance coverage, and first appointment prep.

## Inputs

- `escalation_criteria`: Criteria that triggered this step
- `insurance_plan`: Mental health coverage details
- `preferences`: In-person vs telehealth, gender, modality preferences

## Outputs

- `provider_search_plan`: How to find and vet therapists
- `insurance_guidance`: Coverage details and out-of-pocket estimates
- `first_session_prep`: What to bring and expect at first appointment

## Process

1. Determine appropriate provider type (therapist, psychiatrist, both)
2. Check insurance mental health benefits
3. Search provider directories with filters
4. Prepare first appointment talking points
5. Set expectations for therapy timeline
