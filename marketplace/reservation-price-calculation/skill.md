# Reservation Price Calculation

Calculate your reservation price: the worst deal you would accept, because anything worse means you are better off taking your BATNA.

## Inputs

- `alternatives-list`: All alternatives
- `alternative-ranking`: BATNA identified

## Outputs

- `reservation-price`: The specific number, term, or condition below which you walk away. This is not a target; it is a floor.
- `walk-away-commitment`: The written commitment: if the deal goes below this point, you leave. Written before the negotiation to prevent emotional concession in the room.

## Process

1. Your reservation price is the value of your BATNA plus switching costs. If your BATNA is a competitor offering $90K, and switching to them costs $5K in time, your reservation price is $85K (net value).
2. Write the reservation price on paper before the negotiation. The purpose is to prevent yourself from making emotional concessions when the conversation gets difficult.
3. Never reveal your reservation price. It is internal. Revealing it gives the counterparty a target for their offer.
4. If your reservation price is very close to the counterparty's expected offer, the Zone of Possible Agreement (ZOPA) is narrow. Narrow ZOPAs require more creative deal structures to find value both parties can share.
