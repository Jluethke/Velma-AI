# Procurement Requirements Writer

Define the procurement requirements and prepare the solicitation document.

## Inputs

- `procurement-need`: What is being procured and why
- `funding-source`: Appropriation, grant, or fund paying for the procurement

## Outputs

- `requirements-document`: Statement of work or specifications with evaluation criteria
- `solicitation-package`: Complete solicitation (RFP, RFQ, or IFB) with terms, timeline, and submission instructions

## Process

1. Write the statement of work or performance specifications.
2. Define the evaluation criteria and their relative weights.
3. Set the procurement timeline (posting, questions, proposals, evaluation, award).
4. Assemble the solicitation package with all terms and conditions.
