# Clean Desk Policy Audit

Test clean desk policy compliance and document information left exposed in work areas.

## Inputs

- `sensitive-area-findings`: Areas accessed for policy checking
- `authorization-scope`: Areas approved for policy audit

## Outputs

- `policy-compliance-findings`: Clean desk and security policy violations found
- `physical-pentest-report`: Final physical penetration test report

## Process

1. Survey workspaces for sensitive documents left on desks
2. Check for unlocked screens, posted passwords, and visible credentials
3. Photograph evidence of policy violations (with authorization)
4. Compile all findings into the physical penetration test report with risk ratings
