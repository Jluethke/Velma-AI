# Outcome Definer

Define the 3-5 measurable outcomes the role must deliver in its first year. An outcome is not an activity. 'Manage the support queue' is an activity. 'Reduce average ticket resolution time from 4 hours to 2 hours' is an outcome. Outcomes have a number, a direction, and a timeframe.

## Inputs

- `business-need`: Why this role is being created or redesigned
- `stakeholder-expectations`: What the hiring manager and key stakeholders expect this role to accomplish

## Outputs

- `role-outcomes`: The 3-5 measurable outcomes the role must deliver in year one, each with a metric, a target, and a deadline
- `outcome-priorities`: The rank order of the outcomes: if the person can only accomplish 3 of the 5, which 3 matter most?

## Process

1. Interview the hiring manager and 2-3 stakeholders. For each, ask: If this role is filled perfectly, what will be true in 12 months that is not true today? Push for specifics and numbers.
2. Convert each answer to an outcome format: [metric] moves from [current level] to [target level] by [date].
3. If a stakeholder gives an activity ('manage the support queue'), ask: What result does managing the queue produce? That result is the outcome.
4. Limit to 3-5 outcomes. More than 5 means the role is trying to do too many things. Force the stakeholders to prioritize.
