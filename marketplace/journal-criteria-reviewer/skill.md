# Journal Criteria Reviewer

Self-review the manuscript against the target journal's criteria: scope fit, novelty, statistical rigor, figure quality, and word count.

## Inputs

- `manuscript-draft`: Complete manuscript with all sections
- `target-journal-scope`: Journal aims, scope, and formatting requirements
- `compliance-gaps`: Outstanding checklist issues

## Outputs

- `self-review-report`: Assessment of manuscript readiness with specific issues to fix
- `submission-package`: Final list of files to submit: manuscript, figures, supplementary, cover letter, checklist

## Process

1. Check word count, figure count, and reference count against journal limits.
2. Verify figure resolution and format meet the journal's requirements.
3. Assess whether the abstract, title, and keywords match the journal's conventions.
4. Compile the final submission package file list.
