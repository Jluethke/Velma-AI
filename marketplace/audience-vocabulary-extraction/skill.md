# Audience Vocabulary Extraction

Identify the exact words and phrases the audience uses to describe the problem, the domain, and what success looks like. Your message must use their words, not yours.

## Inputs

- `belief-map`: The audience's existing beliefs and assumptions
- `audience-description`: Who the audience is

## Outputs

- `vocabulary-list`: The specific words and phrases the audience uses for this topic, with notes on connotation
- `words-to-avoid`: Words that trigger resistance, confusion, or the wrong frame in this audience

## Process

1. List the words the audience actually uses when they talk about this problem. Pull from emails, meetings, Slack messages, or past conversations.
2. Note connotations: 'investment' vs. 'cost' vs. 'spend' are the same dollar amount with different emotional weight.
3. Identify jargon that signals in-group membership. Using the right jargon builds trust. Using the wrong jargon signals outsider.
4. List words to avoid: terms that trigger automatic resistance, carry baggage from past failures, or map to a frame you do not want.
