# Investigation Planner

Plan the investigation scope, timeline, and method.

## Inputs

- `assessment-decision`: Investigation warranted
- `preliminary-scope`: Initial scope

## Outputs

- `investigation-plan`: Plan with scope, timeline, witness list, and document list
- `confidentiality-notice`: Confidentiality expectations for all participants

## Process

1. Define the specific allegations to investigate
2. Identify witnesses to interview and documents to review
3. Set the timeline accounting for witness availability
4. Prepare the confidentiality notice for participants
