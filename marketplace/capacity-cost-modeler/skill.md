# Capacity Cost Modeler

Model the cost of the capacity expansion plan.

## Inputs

- `expansion-plan`: Planned upgrades
- `implementation-timeline`: Phased timeline

## Outputs

- `cost-model`: Capital and operating cost estimates by phase and segment
- `capacity-planning-report`: Complete capacity planning report with recommendations

## Process

1. Estimate capital costs for each upgrade (equipment, installation, licensing).
2. Estimate recurring operating costs (power, maintenance, circuit fees).
3. Calculate the cost per unit of additional capacity.
4. Compile the full report with utilization data, forecast, bottlenecks, plan, and costs.
