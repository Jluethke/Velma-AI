# Next Step Close

Propose one specific, scheduled next step rather than leaving it at let me know.

## Inputs

- `mapped-solution`: The solution tied to the stated problems
- `urgency-signals`: Which problems the buyer feels most

## Outputs

- `next-step-proposal`: One concrete next step with a date and an owner
- `commitment-check`: Confirmation that the buyer actually agreed, not just nodded

## Process

1. Propose one specific next step with a date, such as a scoped pilot or a meeting with the decision maker.
2. Avoid the open-ended let me know, since it hands control of the deal to the buyer's busy calendar.
3. Tie the next step to the urgency the buyer expressed, so it feels like their idea.
4. Confirm the commitment explicitly, since a vague yes is a no that has not happened yet.
