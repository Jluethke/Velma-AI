# Quadrant Communication Designer

Design a communication strategy for each quadrant. The strategy for a high-influence, high-interest stakeholder (1-on-1 meetings, early previews, input on decisions) is different from a low-influence, high-interest one (regular updates, feedback opportunities).

## Inputs

- `quadrant-assignment`: Each stakeholder in their quadrant
- `initiative-description`: The initiative context

## Outputs

- `communication-plan-per-quadrant`: For each quadrant: frequency, format, content, and owner of the communication
- `individual-plans`: For Manage Closely stakeholders, an individual communication plan for each person

## Process

1. Manage Closely (high influence, high interest): 1-on-1 meetings, early previews before group announcements, input on key decisions, direct access to the project lead.
2. Keep Satisfied (high influence, low interest): brief periodic updates on outcomes (not details), early warning on anything that might concern them, minimal time asks.
3. Keep Informed (low influence, high interest): regular group updates, feedback opportunities, recognition of their input even when it cannot change the direction.
4. Monitor (low influence, low interest): general announcements only. Do not spend time on custom communication for this group.
