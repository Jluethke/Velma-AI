# Document Reviewer

Review relevant documents and electronic communications.

## Inputs

- `investigation-plan`: Documents to review
- `evidence-collected`: Evidence from interviews

## Outputs

- `document-findings`: Relevant findings from document review
- `evidence-inventory`: Complete inventory of all evidence reviewed

## Process

1. Collect relevant emails, messages, and documents
2. Review for corroboration or contradiction of interview statements
3. Note any additional leads for follow-up
4. Catalog all evidence reviewed
