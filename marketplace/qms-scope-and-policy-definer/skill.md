# Qms Scope And Policy Definer

Define the QMS scope, quality policy, and quality objectives.

## Inputs

- `organization-context`: Products, markets, interested parties, regulatory requirements
- `strategic-direction`: Organization strategy and goals

## Outputs

- `qms-scope`: Defined scope of the quality management system
- `quality-policy`: Quality policy statement and measurable objectives

## Process

1. Identify the organization's context and interested parties (clause 4)
2. Define the scope: which products, processes, and locations are included
3. Draft the quality policy aligned with strategic direction
4. Set measurable quality objectives with targets and timelines
