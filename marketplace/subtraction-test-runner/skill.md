# Subtraction Test Runner

For each claim, ask: if I removed this, would the right customer still choose me? If yes, remove it. If no, keep it.

## Inputs

- `claim-inventory`: Every distinct claim in current messaging
- `offering-description`: What you sell or do

## Outputs

- `essential-claims`: The claims that survived the subtraction test: removing any one of them would cause the right customer to choose differently
- `removable-claims`: The claims that can be removed without changing the right customer's decision

## Process

1. Take each claim one at a time. Imagine your messaging without it. Ask: would the right customer (the one you defined in your ideal profile) still choose you?
2. If yes: the claim is not load-bearing. It is filler, table stakes, or a me-too statement. Mark it removable.
3. If no: the claim is essential. It is a reason the right customer chooses you over the alternative. Keep it.
4. Be honest about table stakes. 'Fast support' is table stakes in most categories. If every competitor also claims fast support, removing it does not change the decision.
5. If you cannot tell whether a claim is essential, ask 5 customers: 'If we stopped saying X, would it change your decision?' Their answer is the test.
