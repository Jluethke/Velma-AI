# Parent Notification Incident

Notify the parents of involved children according to the severity and notification policy.

## Inputs

- `incident-report-draft`: The draft incident report
- `severity-classification`: The severity level of the incident

## Outputs

- `parent-notification-record`: Record of when and how each parent was notified, including their response
- `signed-incident-acknowledgment`: Parent signature acknowledging the incident report at pickup

## Process

1. For serious incidents, call the parents immediately.
2. For moderate incidents, notify by the end of the day via the communication app and in person at pickup.
3. For minor incidents, include in the daily report and discuss at pickup.
4. Have the parent sign the incident report acknowledgment at pickup.
5. Document the notification timeline and parent response.
