# Proof Integration

Back each answer with evidence, examples, and real numbers so the honesty is believable, not just claimed.

## Inputs

- `draft-answers`: The honest draft answers
- `honesty-checks`: The skeptical-reader pass

## Outputs

- `proof-backed-answers`: Answers with evidence attached to each claim
- `evidence-gaps`: Claims you cannot yet prove, flagged for follow-up

## Process

1. For each claim in an answer, attach a number, an example, or a reference the buyer can check.
2. Prefer specific proof over general assurance, since a single concrete example outweighs a paragraph of claims.
3. Where you lack proof, mark the gap rather than papering over it with confident language.
4. Route the evidence gaps to whoever can close them, such as product, finance, or customer success.
