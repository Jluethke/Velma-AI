# Telehealth Consent Collector

Confirm informed consent for the telehealth visit.

## Inputs

- `identity-confirmed`: Identity verification status
- `patient-location`: Patient's jurisdiction

## Outputs

- `consent-status`: Consent obtained, declined, or previously on file
- `consent-record`: Signed or verbal consent documentation

## Process

1. Explain the nature of the telehealth visit and its limitations.
2. Inform the patient about privacy, recording, and data handling.
3. Obtain and document verbal or electronic consent.
