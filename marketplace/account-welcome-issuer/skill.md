# Account Welcome Issuer

Issue credentials and welcome materials to the new account holder.

## Inputs

- `account-number`: New account number
- `account-setup-record`: Account configuration details

## Outputs

- `welcome-package`: Login credentials, debit card request, and welcome guide
- `account-opening-record`: Complete record of the account opening process

## Process

1. Generate the online banking credentials and send securely.
2. Initiate the debit card request if selected.
3. Send the welcome guide with account details and next steps.
4. Finalize the account opening record.
