# Eisenhower Assign Actions

Assign each task an action: do it, schedule it, delegate it, or drop it.

## Inputs

- `protected-time`: Time set aside for the not-urgent-important tasks.
- `q2-plan`: The plan that keeps the quadrant from being crowded out.

## Outputs

- `action-assignments`: The do, schedule, delegate, or drop call for each task.
- `task-schedule`: The scheduled and protected tasks in time order.

## Process

1. Do or schedule the important tasks by quadrant.
2. Delegate the urgent tasks that are not important to you.
3. Drop the tasks that are neither urgent nor important.
