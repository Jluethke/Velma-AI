# Election Logistics Planner

Plan the election logistics including polling locations, equipment, and staffing.

## Inputs

- `election-type`: The type of election (general, primary, special, municipal)
- `jurisdiction-data`: Registered voter counts, precinct boundaries, and historical turnout data

## Outputs

- `logistics-plan`: Plan covering polling locations, equipment allocation, and poll worker assignments
- `election-calendar`: Calendar of election deadlines (filing, registration, early voting, election day)

## Process

1. Determine polling locations based on precinct populations and accessibility requirements.
2. Allocate voting equipment based on projected turnout.
3. Recruit and assign poll workers to each location.
4. Set the election calendar with all statutory deadlines.
