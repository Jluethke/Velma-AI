# Alternative Enumerator

List the top 3 to 5 alternatives you could pursue with the same resources. These are the things you are implicitly saying no to. Include alternatives from different categories: the obvious competitor option, the contrarian option, the do-nothing-and-invest-the-resources option.

## Inputs

- `foreclosed-capacity`: What capacity becomes unavailable
- `true-cost-breakdown`: The resource cost of the proposed commitment

## Outputs

- `alternative-options`: The top 3 to 5 things you could do instead with the same resources
- `do-nothing-option`: What happens if you invest the resources in your current best use instead of the new commitment

## Process

1. List the single best thing you could do with the time this commitment would consume.
2. List the single best thing you could do with the money this commitment would consume.
3. List the contrarian option: the thing that sounds wrong but might be right.
4. List the do-nothing option: what if you just kept doing what you are doing now with those resources?
5. For each alternative, state it in specific terms (not 'something else' but 'use those 10 hours per week to build X').
