# Product Roadmap Feeder

Feed the unfilled gaps into product planning. These are features or improvements that real customers are asking for with their wallets when they leave competitors.

## Inputs

- `unmet-needs-list`: Needs that no competitor currently satisfies
- `gap-pattern-report`: All gaps ranked by frequency and severity

## Outputs

- `feature-opportunity-list`: Product or service features that would fill a proven market gap, ranked by the number of customers who expressed the need
- `competitive-moat-candidates`: Gaps so large and so frequently mentioned that filling them would be a lasting differentiator

## Process

1. List every gap you cannot currently fill. For each, estimate the effort to fill it (small, medium, large).
2. Rank by frequency times feasibility. A gap mentioned by 100 reviewers that takes medium effort to fill is a better bet than a gap mentioned by 200 that takes large effort.
3. Identify moat candidates: gaps that are hard to fill (requiring deep expertise, infrastructure, or a fundamentally different approach). If you fill a hard-to-fill gap, competitors cannot easily copy it.
4. Add the top 3-5 items to the product roadmap with the review evidence attached. The evidence makes the case for building it.
