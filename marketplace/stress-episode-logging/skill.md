# Stress Episode Logging

Log recent stress episodes and their triggers, because the right response depends on knowing what kind of stress each one is.

## Inputs

- `recent-stress-episodes`: Specific moments of stress over the past week or two
- `stress-triggers`: What tended to set each episode off

## Outputs

- `stress-log`: A record of each episode with its trigger, intensity, and how it resolved
- `trigger-list`: The recurring triggers that appear across episodes

## Process

1. Record each stress episode with what triggered it, how intense it was, and how long it lasted. Do this close to the event while the detail is fresh.
2. Note how each episode resolved. Did it pass on its own, did something you did help, or did it linger and color the whole day.
3. Pull out the triggers that recur. A trigger that appears repeatedly is a candidate for a structural fix rather than repeated in-the-moment coping.
4. Keep the log plain and quick. The aim is enough detail to classify the stress, not a full journal that becomes its own burden.
