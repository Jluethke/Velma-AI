# Full Attention Setup

Set up conditions for full attention: eliminate distractions, signal you are listening.

## Inputs

- `conversation-context`: Who you are listening to and what the conversation is about
- `your-goal`: What you need to understand from this conversation

## Outputs

- `attention-plan`: Physical and mental setup for deep listening
- `listening-focus`: The specific information to extract

## Process

1. Remove distractions: phone face-down, laptop closed, notifications off.
2. Physical signals: face the speaker, maintain eye contact, nod occasionally.
3. Mental commitment: do not formulate your response while they are talking.
4. Set your listening focus: what do you need to learn?
