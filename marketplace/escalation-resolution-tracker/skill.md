# Escalation Resolution Tracker

Track escalation progress and communicate updates to the customer.

## Inputs

- `escalated-tickets`: Tickets in escalation
- `escalation-briefs`: Briefs provided to handling agents

## Outputs

- `resolution-log`: Record of actions taken and resolution details
- `customer-updates`: Updates sent to the customer during escalation

## Process

1. Monitor each escalated ticket for progress updates.
2. Send periodic status updates to the customer.
3. Record all actions taken by the handling agent.
4. Close the escalation when the issue is resolved.
