# Competitor Review Collection

Collect 2-3 star reviews of your top 3-5 competitors from every review platform.

## Inputs

- `competitor-list`: Your top 3-5 competitors
- `review-platforms`: Where reviews exist (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Google Reviews, app stores, industry forums)

## Outputs

- `review-corpus`: 100-300 reviews rated 2-3 stars across all competitors, with the full text, rating, and date
- `review-count-by-competitor`: How many reviews collected per competitor

## Process

1. Focus on 2-3 star reviews. 1-star reviews are often rage-driven and unspecific. 4-5 star reviews tell you what works. 2-3 star reviews contain the most actionable complaints: specific enough to be useful, measured enough to be credible.
2. Collect at least 20 reviews per competitor. Fewer than 20 does not give enough data to detect patterns.
3. Copy the full text, not just the star rating. The value is in the specific language the reviewer uses to describe their frustration.
4. Note the date. Reviews older than 2 years may describe problems the competitor has since fixed.
