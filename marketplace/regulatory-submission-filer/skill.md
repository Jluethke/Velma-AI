# Regulatory Submission Filer

Submit the reports and archive for examination.

## Inputs

- `generated-reports`: Clean reports ready for filing
- `validation-results`: Passing validation results

## Outputs

- `submission-confirmations`: Filing confirmations from each regulator
- `regulatory-reporting-record`: Complete archive of data, reports, validations, and submissions

## Process

1. Submit each report through the regulator's filing system.
2. Confirm acceptance and log the confirmation numbers.
3. Archive the complete package (data, report, validations, confirmation) for examination.
