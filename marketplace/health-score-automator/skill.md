# Health Score Automator

Automate score calculation and set up alerts.

## Inputs

- `scoring-model`: Weighted formula
- `threshold-definitions`: Score thresholds

## Outputs

- `automated-scoring`: Automated health score calculation pipeline
- `alert-rules`: Alert configurations for at-risk and critical accounts

## Process

1. Build the automated pipeline to calculate scores on a daily cadence.
2. Configure alerts for accounts crossing into at-risk or critical.
3. Route alerts to the account owner or CSM.
4. Set up the health score dashboard.
