# Postpartum Support Plan

Build a postpartum support network with meal trains, visitor schedules, and mental health check-ins.

## Inputs

- `support_network`: Available family and friends
- `budget_for_help`: Budget for hired help if any
- `mental_health_history`: Any prior depression or anxiety history

## Outputs

- `support_schedule`: Who helps when for the first 8 weeks
- `meal_train`: Meal train signup plan
- `mental_health_checkpoints`: Scheduled check-in dates and warning signs

## Process

1. Map available helpers by week
2. Set up meal train with dietary notes
3. Schedule mental health screening dates
4. Identify professional resources (lactation, therapist, doula)
5. Create visitor policy and communication plan
