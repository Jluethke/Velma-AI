# College School Research

Build a balanced college list with reach, match, and safety schools based on academic profile and preferences.

## Inputs

- `gpa`: Current GPA
- `test_scores`: SAT/ACT scores if available
- `preferences`: Location, size, major, campus culture preferences
- `budget_constraints`: Family contribution ability

## Outputs

- `school_list`: Categorized reach/match/safety list
- `comparison_matrix`: Side-by-side comparison of key factors

## Process

1. Assess academic profile against admission data
2. Filter by preferences and budget
3. Categorize into reach, match, and safety tiers
4. Build comparison matrix with acceptance rates, costs, outcomes
