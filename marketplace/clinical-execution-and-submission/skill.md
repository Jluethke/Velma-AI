# Clinical Execution And Submission

Execute the clinical trial, manage data, analyze results, and compile the clinical evidence package for regulatory submission.

## Inputs

- `clinical-study-protocol`: Approved study protocol with endpoints and analysis plan
- `ide-application`: IDE approval (if required) and IRB approvals

## Outputs

- `clinical-study-report`: Final clinical study report with safety and effectiveness results
- `regulatory-submission-clinical-section`: Clinical evidence section formatted for 510(k), De Novo, or PMA submission

## Process

1. Select and qualify clinical sites: sites must have the patient population, investigator expertise, and infrastructure (IRB, regulatory experience, data management). Conduct site initiation visits to train investigators on the protocol, device, and data collection.
2. Manage enrollment: track screening, enrollment, and follow-up rates at each site. If enrollment is slower than projected, identify barriers (restrictive inclusion criteria, competing studies, site capacity) and address them. Do not change the protocol to ease enrollment without FDA notification.
3. Monitor data quality: use a clinical data management system with edit checks. Conduct source data verification at sites (compare CRFs to medical records). Identify and resolve data queries promptly. An independent data monitoring committee (IDMC) reviews safety data at pre-specified intervals for serious adverse events.
4. Manage adverse event reporting: report unanticipated adverse device effects (UADE) to FDA, all IRBs, and investigators within 10 working days. Report device-related deaths within 10 working days per 21 CFR 812.150. Maintain the adverse event log throughout the study.
5. Analyze per the pre-specified statistical analysis plan. Report the primary endpoint result with point estimate and confidence interval. Present all adverse events in tabular form with rates and relationship to device. Compile the clinical study report per MEDDEV 2.7/1 or FDA format, and prepare the clinical section of the regulatory submission.
