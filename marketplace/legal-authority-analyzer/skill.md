# Legal Authority Analyzer

Analyze the collected authorities and develop the legal analysis.

## Inputs

- `source-collection`: Collected legal sources
- `research-question`: The question being answered

## Outputs

- `legal-analysis`: Analysis of the applicable authorities and how they apply to the facts
- `conclusion`: The answer to the research question with confidence level

## Process

1. Identify the controlling authorities (binding statutes, regulations, and case law).
2. Analyze how the authorities apply to the specific facts of the matter.
3. Address any conflicting authorities or open questions.
4. Formulate the conclusion and assess the confidence level.
