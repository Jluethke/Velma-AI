# Contract Negotiator

Send the quote, negotiate terms, and finalize the contract.

## Inputs

- `approved-quote`: Internally approved quote
- `approval-conditions`: Conditions from internal approvers

## Outputs

- `final-contract`: Executed contract with agreed terms
- `negotiation-log`: Record of changes made during negotiation

## Process

1. Send the quote to the customer.
2. Track requested changes and counter-proposals.
3. Negotiate within approved boundaries.
4. Finalize and execute the contract.
