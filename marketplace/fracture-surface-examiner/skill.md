# Fracture Surface Examiner

Examine fracture surfaces at macro and micro scales to identify the fracture origin, propagation direction, and mode.

## Inputs

- `failed-component`: The failed part or section containing the fracture surface
- `visual-documentation`: Initial documentation photographs and notes

## Outputs

- `fractographic-record`: Macro and SEM images of the fracture surface with annotations
- `fracture-features`: Identified features: beach marks, striations, chevrons, dimples, cleavage, intergranular facets

## Process

1. Examine the fracture surface under the stereomicroscope to find the origin
2. Identify macroscopic features (beach marks, ratchet marks, shear lips)
3. Examine under SEM at higher magnification for striations, dimples, or cleavage
4. Map the crack path relative to the microstructure
