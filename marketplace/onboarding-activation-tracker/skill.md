# Onboarding Activation Tracker

Track onboarding progress and identify stuck users.

## Inputs

- `welcome-sequence`: Active onboarding sequence
- `milestone-map`: Expected milestones and timeline

## Outputs

- `onboarding-support-plan`: Complete onboarding support plan
- `activation-metrics`: Activation rate and time-to-activate by segment

## Process

1. Track milestone completion per customer.
2. Identify users who stall at a milestone beyond the expected timeline.
3. Trigger proactive outreach to stuck users.
4. Calculate activation rate and time-to-activate by segment.
