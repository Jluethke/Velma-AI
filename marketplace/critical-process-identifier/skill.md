# Critical Process Identifier

Identify critical business processes and their dependencies.

## Inputs

- `organization-structure`: Organizational units and their functions
- `process-inventory`: Inventory of business processes

## Outputs

- `critical-processes`: List of critical business processes ranked by importance
- `dependency-map`: Dependencies between processes, systems, and third parties

## Process

1. Survey business units to identify their key processes.
2. Rank processes by revenue impact, regulatory obligation, and customer impact.
3. Map dependencies (IT systems, third parties, personnel, facilities).
4. Identify single points of failure.
