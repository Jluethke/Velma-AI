# Membership Tier Designer

Define the membership tiers, pricing, and benefits.

## Inputs

- `creator-audience-data`: Audience size, engagement, and willingness-to-pay signals
- `content-assets`: What exclusive content or access the creator can offer

## Outputs

- `tier-structure`: The membership tiers with pricing and benefits for each
- `platform-selection`: The chosen membership platform (Patreon, Discord, Substack, custom)

## Process

1. Research what membership tiers and pricing work for similar creators in the niche.
2. Design 2 to 3 tiers with escalating benefits (content, access, community, perks).
3. Set pricing based on the audience's demonstrated willingness to pay.
4. Select the membership platform based on features, fees, and audience preferences.
