# Peer Feedback Collector

Collect peer and upward feedback for each employee.

## Inputs

- `review-criteria`: The review criteria
- `self-assessments`: Self-assessments to identify peer reviewers

## Outputs

- `peer-feedback`: Aggregated peer feedback per employee
- `feedback-themes`: Common themes across feedback

## Process

1. Identify peer reviewers (direct collaborators, cross-functional partners)
2. Distribute the feedback forms with the criteria
3. Aggregate and anonymize the feedback
4. Identify recurring themes per employee
