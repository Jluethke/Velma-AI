# Learning Outcome Documenter

Document learning outcomes and share curriculum highlights with parents.

## Inputs

- `weekly-plan`: The weekly plan that was executed
- `materials-list`: Materials used

## Outputs

- `outcome-observations`: Documented observations of children's learning and development during activities
- `parent-curriculum-update`: Summary sent to parents about what their children are learning and how to extend it at home

## Process

1. Observe and document children's engagement, progress, and challenges during each activity.
2. Note individual milestones or breakthroughs worth sharing with families.
3. Compile the weekly curriculum summary for parents (what was learned, upcoming themes).
4. Suggest home extension activities parents can do to reinforce the learning.
