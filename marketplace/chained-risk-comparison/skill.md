# Chained Risk Comparison

Compare the chained risk against individual severity ratings to show the true exposure.

## Inputs

- `impact-demonstrations`: Demonstrated business impact
- `vuln-inventory`: Original individual ratings

## Outputs

- `risk-comparison`: Side-by-side comparison of individual vs chained risk
- `adjusted-ratings`: Recommended severity adjustments based on chain context

## Process

1. List each vulnerability's individual severity rating
2. Calculate the effective severity when chained
3. Highlight cases where low-rated items combine to critical impact
