# Weekly Recipe Selector

Select recipes for the week balancing nutrition, variety, prep efficiency, and ingredient overlap.

## Inputs

- `dietary_profile`: Dietary requirements
- `calorie_targets`: Nutritional targets
- `prep_time_budget`: Hours available for cooking per week

## Outputs

- `weekly_menu`: Meals for each day
- `recipe_list`: All recipes with ingredients and instructions
- `ingredient_overlap`: Shared ingredients across recipes

## Process

1. Select recipes matching dietary profile
2. Balance protein sources across the week
3. Maximize ingredient overlap to reduce waste
4. Vary cuisines and cooking methods
5. Fit prep time within weekly budget
