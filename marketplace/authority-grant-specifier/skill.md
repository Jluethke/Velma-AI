# Authority Grant Specifier

Give real authority with each task. Delegating responsibility without authority sets the person up to fail and pulls the work back to you. State what they can decide, spend, and commit to on their own.

## Inputs

- `outcome-statements`: The outcomes defined
- `boundary-conditions`: The boundaries set

## Outputs

- `authority-grants`: The decisions, spending, and commitments each person now owns outright
- `escalation-rules`: The specific situations where the person must come to you before acting

## Process

1. For each task, state the decisions the person now owns outright.
2. State the spending and commitments they can make without checking first.
3. Define the escalation rule: the specific situations where they must come to you before acting.
4. Communicate the authority to anyone else who needs to honor it, so the person is not second-guessed by peers.
