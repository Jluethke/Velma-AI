# Pss Context Adapter

Adapt the reconciled range to the specific pricing context.

## Inputs

- `reconciled-price-range`: The range supported by both methods
- `product-description`: The context, such as a freelance rate, subscription, product, or salary

## Outputs

- `context-adjusted-prices`: Prices adjusted for the context and its norms
- `pricing-scenarios`: Low, middle, and high scenarios with their tradeoffs

## Process

1. Apply the norms of the context, such as rounding, tiers, or hourly conversion.
2. Adjust the range for positioning and competition.
3. Lay out low, middle, and high scenarios with their tradeoffs.
