# Identity Conflict Resolution

Surface competing identities that pull against the new one, because a habit fails fastest when it fights an identity you still hold.

## Inputs

- `vote-tracker`: The running tally of votes
- `identity-statement`: The identity being built

## Outputs

- `conflicting-identity-list`: The existing identities that contradict the new one and the situations where they take over
- `conflict-resolution-plan`: How to reframe or contain each conflicting identity so it stops casting opposing votes

## Process

1. Name the identities that contradict the new one. A person building I eat well may still hold I am someone who treats myself when stressed.
2. Find the situations where the old identity takes over. Conflicts cluster around specific triggers such as stress, social settings, or fatigue.
3. Reframe the old identity rather than fighting it head on. I am someone who handles stress well can replace I treat myself when stressed, keeping the need but changing the action.
4. For situations you cannot reframe yet, plan to avoid or change the context so the old identity is not handed the vote by default.
