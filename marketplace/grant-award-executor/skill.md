# Grant Award Executor

Issue the notice of award and execute the grant agreement.

## Inputs

- `grant-selection`: The selected grantee and award amount
- `grant-terms`: Terms, conditions, and reporting requirements for the grant

## Outputs

- `grant-agreement`: The executed grant agreement
- `monitoring-plan`: Plan for monitoring grantee compliance and progress

## Process

1. Prepare and issue the notice of award to the selected grantee.
2. Negotiate and execute the grant agreement with terms and conditions.
3. Set up the monitoring plan with reporting dates and compliance checkpoints.
4. Establish the drawdown and reimbursement procedures.
