# Executive Audience Profiler

Define the executive reporting audience and their information needs.

## Inputs

- `executive-team`: List of executives who receive reports
- `reporting-context`: Current state of executive reporting (what exists, what is missing)

## Outputs

- `audience-needs`: What each executive needs to see and how often
- `report-inventory`: Existing reports mapped to audience needs, with gaps identified

## Process

1. List each executive and their area of responsibility.
2. Identify what decisions they make and what information they need.
3. Map existing reports to these needs and identify gaps.
