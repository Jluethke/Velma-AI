# Email Sequence Mapper

Map the email sequence with timing, triggers, and content themes for each step.

## Inputs

- `campaign-brief`: Goal and audience from the prior step
- `segment-criteria`: Audience targeting rules

## Outputs

- `sequence-map`: Ordered list of emails with timing and triggers
- `content-themes`: Content theme for each email in the sequence

## Process

1. Determine the number of emails and the cadence
2. Define the trigger for each email (time-based, behavior-based)
3. Assign a content theme and CTA to each email
