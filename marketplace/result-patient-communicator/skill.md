# Result Patient Communicator

Communicate results and the plan to the patient.

## Inputs

- `action-plan`: Clinical actions
- `clinical-correlation`: Contextualized findings

## Outputs

- `patient-notification`: Patient-friendly explanation of results
- `lab-interpretation-note`: Provider's interpretation note for the chart

## Process

1. Translate results into plain language for the patient.
2. Explain what the results mean and what happens next.
3. Document the communication in the medical record.
