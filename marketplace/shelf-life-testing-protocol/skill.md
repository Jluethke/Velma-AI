# Shelf Life Testing Protocol

Design and execute accelerated and real-time shelf life studies tracking microbial, chemical, and sensory stability.

## Inputs

- `bench-samples`: Lead formulation prototypes to test
- `target-market`: Required shelf life duration and storage conditions

## Outputs

- `shelf-life-data`: Microbial counts, pH, water activity, color, and sensory scores over time at each condition
- `shelf-life-estimate`: Predicted shelf life with safety margin and failure mode

## Process

1. Set up real-time storage (target conditions) and accelerated storage (elevated temperature/humidity) for each formulation
2. Pull samples at defined intervals and test: total plate count, yeast/mold, pH, Aw, color, and sensory
3. Plot degradation curves and identify the shelf-life-limiting parameter
4. Apply Q10 or Arrhenius modeling to extrapolate from accelerated to real-time shelf life
