# Infection Rate Tracker

Track infection rates and produce the infection control report.

## Inputs

- `audit-results`: Compliance audit data
- `risk-profile`: Baseline risk information

## Outputs

- `infection-rate-data`: Infection counts and rates over time
- `infection-control-plan`: Complete infection control plan and report

## Process

1. Track new infections by unit, pathogen, and time period.
2. Compare rates against baseline and national benchmarks.
3. Produce the infection control summary with trends and recommendations.
