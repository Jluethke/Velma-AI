# Grant Closeout Manager

Close out the grant and produce the final report.

## Inputs

- `monitoring-findings`: Monitoring findings over the grant period
- `drawdown-approvals`: All processed drawdowns

## Outputs

- `closeout-package`: Final financial reconciliation and performance report
- `grant-administration-record`: Complete grant administration file

## Process

1. Verify all grant funds are expended or returned.
2. Reconcile the financial records against the drawdown approvals.
3. Collect the grantee's final performance report.
4. Close the grant in the tracking system and archive the file.
