# Rent Pricing Recommender

Compare current rents against market and recommend adjustments.

## Inputs

- `adjusted-comps`: Adjusted comparables
- `market-rent-range`: Market rent range per unit

## Outputs

- `pricing-recommendations`: Recommended rent for each unit with justification
- `market-analysis-report`: Complete rental market analysis report

## Process

1. Compare each unit's current rent against the market range.
2. Identify units priced above or below market.
3. Recommend specific rent adjustments with timing.
4. Produce the market analysis report with supporting data.
