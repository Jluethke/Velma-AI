# Pathway Pilot Runner

Pilot the pathway with a defined patient group.

## Inputs

- `pathway-map`: Complete pathway to test
- `variance-criteria`: Variance tracking setup

## Outputs

- `pilot-data`: Outcomes and variances from the pilot period
- `staff-feedback`: Clinician feedback on usability and completeness

## Process

1. Implement the pathway for a pilot group of patients.
2. Track outcomes and variances during the pilot.
3. Collect staff feedback on the pathway's clinical fit and usability.
