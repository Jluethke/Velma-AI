# Tutoring Billing Management

Handle billing for sessions, packages, and subscriptions.

## Inputs

- `session-calendar`: Completed and upcoming sessions
- `service-offerings`: Pricing per format and package

## Outputs

- `billing-record`: Invoice history with payments, outstanding balances, and package usage
- `practice-management-plan`: Complete tutoring or coaching practice management plan

## Process

1. Generate invoices based on completed sessions or package billing cycle.
2. Track package session balances and expiration dates.
3. Send payment reminders for outstanding invoices.
4. Reconcile payments received and follow up on overdue accounts.
