# Win Loss Comparison Validator

Compare the loss patterns against your wins to confirm the diagnosis. If your wins share traits that your losses lack, the contrast validates the pattern.

## Inputs

- `counterfactual-actions`: Proposed changes based on loss patterns
- `loss-patterns`: The recurring loss patterns

## Outputs

- `validated-patterns`: Loss patterns confirmed by contrast with wins
- `false-pattern-flags`: Patterns that appeared in losses but also appear in wins, meaning they are not the real cause

## Process

1. Pull your recent wins. For each, note the same attributes: stage progression, objections raised (and overcome), proof provided, and decision timeline.
2. Compare: what did the wins have that the losses lacked? Common differences: earlier proof of value, a champion inside the buyer, a triggering event that created urgency, or a simpler ask.
3. Flag any loss pattern that also appears in wins. If 'budget concern' appears in both wins and losses, budget is not the deciding factor. Remove it from the roadmap.
4. The validated patterns are the ones that appear in losses but not in wins. These are your real failure modes.
