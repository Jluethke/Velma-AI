# Stated Objection Collector

Collect every stated objection you have heard or can predict. Stated objections are what people say out loud. They are almost never the real concern.

## Inputs

- `objection-surfaces`: Where objections will come from
- `stakeholder-exposure-map`: What each stakeholder loses

## Outputs

- `stated-objections`: List of every stated objection, attributed to the stakeholder likely to raise it
- `frequency-ranking`: Objections ranked by how often they are raised (past data or prediction)

## Process

1. List every objection you have heard in previous conversations, meetings, or emails about this or similar proposals.
2. For each stakeholder, predict what they will say based on their exposure map. Use their actual vocabulary.
3. Rank by frequency: the objection raised by the most stakeholders, or raised most often, goes first.
4. Include the objections that are whispered but never said in meetings. Those are often the real blockers.
