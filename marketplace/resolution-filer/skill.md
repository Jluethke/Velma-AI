# Resolution Filer

File the executed resolution in the corporate records and distribute as needed.

## Inputs

- `executed-resolution`: The signed resolution
- `vote-record`: Record of the vote

## Outputs

- `filed-resolution`: The resolution filed in the corporate minute book
- `board-resolution-record`: Complete record including the action, resolution, vote, and filing

## Process

1. File the executed resolution in the corporate minute book or records system.
2. Distribute copies to the parties who need to act on the resolution.
3. File any required external filings (state filings, SEC, bank) referencing the resolution.
4. Produce the complete board resolution record.
