# Motivation Discovery

Discover what the champion personally gains from a win and what risk they fear, so the case speaks to both.

## Inputs

- `primary-champion`: The chosen champion
- `champion-scores`: The scoring across candidates

## Outputs

- `champion-motivation`: What the champion stands to gain personally and professionally
- `risk-concerns`: What the champion is afraid will go wrong if they back you

## Process

1. Learn what a win does for the champion's standing, goals, and career, not just for the company.
2. Surface the risk they carry by advocating, such as looking wrong in front of a boss.
3. Ask what would make them confident enough to put their name behind the decision.
4. Record both the upside they want and the downside they fear, since the case must address each.
