# Collab Pitch Negotiator

Pitch the collaboration concept and agree on the format and timeline.

## Inputs

- `partner-shortlist`: The ranked list of potential partners
- `collab-goals`: What the collaboration should achieve

## Outputs

- `collab-agreement`: The agreed collaboration format, timeline, and responsibilities
- `content-brief`: The creative brief for the collaborative content

## Process

1. Reach out to the top candidates with a specific collaboration concept.
2. Propose the format (guest appearance, joint video, challenge, swap, co-creation).
3. Agree on the timeline, responsibilities, and promotion commitments.
4. Draft the creative brief so both parties are aligned on the content.
