# Van Westendorp Survey Builder

Build and administer the Van Westendorp survey: four questions about price perception.

## Inputs

- `product-description`: What is being priced, described clearly enough for the respondent to assess value
- `target-audience`: Who will answer the survey (current customers, prospects, market segment)

## Outputs

- `survey-instrument`: The four Van Westendorp questions, customized for the product, with the response format (open-ended price entry)
- `survey-data`: The raw responses: for each respondent, four price points (too cheap, cheap, expensive, too expensive)

## Process

1. Ask four questions about the same product: 1) At what price would you consider this product so inexpensive that you would question its quality? (too cheap) 2) At what price would you consider this product a bargain, a great buy for the money? (cheap) 3) At what price would this product begin to seem expensive, although you might still consider buying it? (expensive) 4) At what price would this product be too expensive to consider? (too expensive)
2. Each question requires an open-ended price response. Do not use ranges or multiple choice.
3. Administer to at least 100 respondents from the target audience. More respondents produce smoother curves.
4. Collect demographic data alongside the price data to segment the analysis later.
