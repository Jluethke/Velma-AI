# Ingredient Sourcing Spec

Source ingredients, confirm supplier specifications, and verify regulatory status (GRAS, novel food) for the target market.

## Inputs

- `product-concept`: Target product type, flavor profile, and nutritional goals
- `target-market`: Regulatory jurisdiction and consumer segment

## Outputs

- `ingredient-list`: Approved ingredients with supplier, spec sheet, cost, and regulatory status
- `allergen-matrix`: Allergen declarations per ingredient and cross-contact risk assessment

## Process

1. Define the ingredient functional roles: structure, sweetness, preservation, color, flavor
2. Source candidates from approved suppliers and collect certificates of analysis
3. Verify GRAS status or novel food authorization for each ingredient in the target market
4. Build the allergen matrix and identify cross-contact risks in the supply chain
