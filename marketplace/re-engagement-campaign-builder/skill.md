# Re Engagement Campaign Builder

Design and trigger re-engagement campaigns for dormant subscribers.

## Inputs

- `engagement-segments`: Engagement segment definitions and thresholds
- `segment-movement-report`: Data on subscribers entering the dormant segment

## Outputs

- `re-engagement-campaigns`: The re-engagement email sequences with triggers, content, and incentives
- `re-engagement-results`: Results of re-engagement campaigns (reactivated, still dormant, unsubscribed)

## Process

1. Design the re-engagement trigger (subscriber enters the dormant segment).
2. Write the re-engagement email sequence (we miss you, last chance, preference update, final notice).
3. Set the incentive strategy (exclusive content, discount, survey).
4. Measure the reactivation rate and cost per reactivation.
