# Collab Performance Measurer

Publish, cross-promote, and measure the collaboration results.

## Inputs

- `collab-content`: The finished content
- `promo-assets`: Cross-promotion assets

## Outputs

- `collab-metrics`: Performance metrics (new followers, views, engagement from partner audience)
- `collaboration-record`: Complete record of the collaboration with results and relationship notes

## Process

1. Coordinate the publish timing so both creators go live in sync.
2. Cross-promote on all platforms using the prepared assets.
3. Track the metrics: new followers from partner audience, views, engagement, subscriber overlap change.
4. Document the results and relationship notes for future collaborations.
