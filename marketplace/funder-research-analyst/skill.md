# Funder Research Analyst

Research the funder's priorities, requirements, and past grants.

## Inputs

- `funder-name`: Name of the funding organization
- `project-summary`: Brief description of the project to be funded

## Outputs

- `funder-profile`: Funder priorities, eligibility, and past awards
- `alignment-assessment`: How the project aligns with funder priorities

## Process

1. Review the funder's mission, strategic priorities, and funding guidelines
2. Analyze past grants (amounts, recipients, geographic focus)
3. Assess alignment between the project and funder priorities
4. Note application requirements, deadlines, and restrictions
