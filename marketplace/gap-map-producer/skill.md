# Gap Map Producer

Produce the final gap map: a structured document showing what is known, what is not, and where the most valuable opportunities lie.

## Inputs

- `concept-matrix`: Papers-by-concepts matrix
- `ranked-gaps`: Gaps ordered by impact and tractability
- `contradiction-list`: Conflicting findings to highlight

## Outputs

- `gap-map`: Structured gap map document
- `research-agenda`: Suggested studies to fill the top-ranked gaps

## Process

1. Organize the concept matrix into a visual or tabular overview of coverage.
2. Highlight the top-ranked gaps and contradictions.
3. For each high-priority gap, sketch the study design that would fill it.
4. Compile into a single document with executive summary, map, and agenda.
