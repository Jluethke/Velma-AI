# Teardown Synthesis And Action

Synthesize the 30-day teardown into actionable insights for your own product strategy.

## Inputs

- `product-decisions`: Their inferred product strategy
- `vulnerability-map`: Their gaps and weaknesses

## Outputs

- `competitive-insights`: The 5 most important insights from the teardown, each tied to a specific opportunity for your product
- `action-items`: Specific product, marketing, or sales changes to make based on the teardown findings

## Process

1. Select the 5 most important insights. An insight is important if it reveals an opportunity that: your product can address, the competitor is unlikely to fix quickly, and customers care about.
2. For each insight, write one action item: a product feature to build, a marketing message to create, or a sales talking point to add.
3. Document the teardown in a format others can use: a one-page summary of the competitor's strengths, weaknesses, and your opportunities.
4. Schedule the next teardown (different competitor) in 6 months. The competitive landscape changes, and a single teardown has a shelf life.
