# Hybrid Meeting Tech Updater

Update meeting room technology for hybrid meetings.

## Inputs

- `layout-plan`: Office layout
- `hybrid-policy`: Hybrid policy

## Outputs

- `tech-upgrade-plan`: Plan for upgrading meeting room technology
- `meeting-norms`: Norms for running effective hybrid meetings

## Process

1. Assess current meeting rooms for hybrid readiness (cameras, displays, audio)
2. Specify the technology upgrades needed per room
3. Define meeting norms (cameras on, shared documents, equal participation)
4. Budget and schedule the upgrades
