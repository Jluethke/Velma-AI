# Seasonal Buying Guide

Buy produce and proteins at peak season for best price and quality, with preservation strategies.

## Inputs

- `climate_zone`: Geographic region
- `dietary_profile`: Foods regularly consumed
- `freezer_space`: Available freezer capacity

## Outputs

- `seasonal_calendar`: What is cheapest each month
- `bulk_buy_plan`: When to stock up on what
- `preservation_methods`: Freezing, canning, and dehydrating guides

## Process

1. Map seasonal price drops for produce by month
2. Identify proteins with seasonal price cycles
3. Plan bulk purchases at price lows
4. Match preservation method to each food type
5. Calculate storage requirements
