# Claim Investigator

Investigate the claim through interviews, documentation review, and inspection.

## Inputs

- `adjuster-assignment`: Assigned adjuster and investigation plan
- `claim-file`: Claim file with loss details and coverage

## Outputs

- `investigation-findings`: Findings from the investigation including statements, photos, and expert reports
- `liability-assessment`: Assessment of liability, causation, and damages

## Process

1. Interview the insured, claimant, and witnesses.
2. Inspect the loss site or damaged property and document with photos.
3. Collect and review supporting documents (police reports, medical records, repair estimates).
4. Engage experts (engineers, appraisers, medical reviewers) as needed.
