# Probing For Depth

Ask probing questions to go deeper into what they mean, feel, and need.

## Inputs

- `confirmation-record`: What they said
- `listening-focus`: What you need to understand

## Outputs

- `deeper-understanding`: Not just what they said, but what they meant and what they need
- `probe-questions-used`: The probing questions that produced deepest understanding

## Process

1. Ask 'What do you mean by [specific word]?' People use the same words for different things.
2. Ask 'How did that make you feel?' when emotions are involved.
3. Ask 'What would a good outcome look like for you?' to reveal their underlying interest.
4. Do not ask leading questions disguised as probes.
