# Small Claims Filing Guide

Walk through the filing process: forms, fees, service of process, and scheduling.

## Inputs

- `court_location`: Which small claims court
- `defendant_info`: Name and address of person being sued

## Outputs

- `filing_checklist`: Forms and fees needed
- `service_plan`: How to serve the defendant
- `court_date_prep`: What to expect after filing

## Process

1. Obtain correct forms for the court
2. Complete claim form with clear description
3. Pay filing fee and file
4. Arrange service of process on defendant
5. Note court date and preparation deadlines
