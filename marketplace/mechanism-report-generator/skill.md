# Mechanism Report Generator

Compile the mechanism elucidation study into a final report with energy profiles and evidence tables.

## Inputs

- `proposed-mechanism`: The proposed reaction mechanism
- `experiment-theory-comparison`: Agreement between experimental and computational results
- `evidence-summary`: Supporting evidence for each mechanistic step

## Outputs

- `mechanism-report`: Final report with mechanism scheme, energy profile, and evidence table
- `supporting-data`: All kinetic data, spectra, and computational results

## Process

1. Draw the complete mechanism scheme with arrow-pushing notation
2. Present the energy profile with experimental and computed values overlaid
3. Build the evidence table mapping each mechanistic claim to its supporting data
4. Package all raw kinetic data, spectra, and DFT output files
