# Grant Financial Reporter

Prepare interim and final financial reports per the grant schedule.

## Inputs

- `budget-vs-actual`: Current budget to actual data
- `compliance-requirements`: Reporting format and schedule requirements

## Outputs

- `financial-report`: Grant financial report in the required format
- `narrative-supplement`: Narrative explaining variances and progress

## Process

1. Compile expenditures by category in the grantor's required format.
2. Calculate the percentage of budget expended.
3. Write the narrative explaining material variances from the budget.
4. Submit the report by the required deadline.
