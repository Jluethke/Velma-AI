# Existing Habit Inventory

List every automatic habit you already do daily: things you do without thinking, at the same time, in the same sequence.

## Inputs

- `target-habit`: The new habit you want to build
- `daily-routine`: Your current daily routine from waking to sleeping

## Outputs

- `habit-inventory`: Every automatic daily habit: brush teeth, make coffee, sit at desk, check phone, eat lunch, etc.
- `habit-timing`: When each automatic habit occurs and how long it takes

## Process

1. Walk through your day from waking to sleeping and write down every action that happens automatically: things you do without deciding to do them.
2. Focus on the transitions: waking up, arriving at work, finishing lunch, getting home. Transitions are natural stack points because they already have a trigger (the preceding event).
3. Note which habits are deeply ingrained (never skipped) versus loosely held (sometimes skipped). Only stack onto deeply ingrained habits.
4. The best stack points are habits that: happen daily, happen at a consistent time, require minimal effort, and are followed by a natural pause.
