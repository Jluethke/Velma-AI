# Symptom Inventory

List every symptom (downstream problem) you are currently fighting, without trying to fix any of them yet.

## Inputs

- `current-problems`: The recurring problems, complaints, failures, or firefighting that consumes your time
- `frequency-data`: How often each problem occurs (daily, weekly, monthly)

## Outputs

- `symptom-list`: Every recurring problem listed with its frequency, time cost per occurrence, and who handles it
- `total-firefighting-cost`: The total time and money spent fighting these symptoms per month

## Process

1. List every recurring problem you deal with. Include customer complaints, system outages, quality defects, team conflicts, missed deadlines, budget overruns.
2. For each symptom, record: how often it occurs, how long it takes to fix each time, who fixes it, and what the downstream impact is if it is not fixed.
3. Calculate the total firefighting cost: the sum of time and money spent reacting to these symptoms per month. This number is usually shockingly high because each individual symptom feels small.
4. Do not try to fix any symptom yet. The goal of this step is to see the full picture of where your reactive time goes.
