# Seasonal Yard Prep

Prepare yard and exterior for seasonal changes: lawn, garden, drainage, and outdoor systems.

## Inputs

- `property_features`: Yard, garden, pool, sprinklers, etc.
- `upcoming_season`: Season to prepare for
- `climate_zone`: Geographic climate

## Outputs

- `yard_checklist`: Outdoor tasks for the season
- `planting_guide`: What to plant or remove this season
- `equipment_prep`: Equipment to service or store

## Process

1. Prepare lawn for seasonal transition
2. Clean gutters and check drainage
3. Service or winterize irrigation system
4. Prepare garden beds for season
5. Store or service outdoor equipment
