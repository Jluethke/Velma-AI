# Interview Preparation Coach

Prepare staff for auditor interviews by briefing them on their control areas.

## Inputs

- `control-matrix`: Controls each person is responsible for
- `evidence-repository`: Evidence they may be asked about

## Outputs

- `interview-prep-materials`: Briefing documents per interviewee
- `practice-questions`: Sample questions auditors may ask

## Process

1. Identify staff who will be interviewed and their control areas
2. Brief each person on what the auditor will ask and what evidence exists
3. Provide sample questions and coached responses
4. Conduct a practice session
