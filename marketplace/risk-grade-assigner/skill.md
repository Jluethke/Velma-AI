# Risk Grade Assigner

Assign the risk grade based on the inspection findings and deliver the report.

## Inputs

- `inspection-findings`: Site inspection documentation
- `hazard-list`: Identified hazards with severity ratings

## Outputs

- `risk-grade`: Assigned risk grade with supporting rationale
- `loss-control-report`: Complete loss control report with findings, recommendations, and risk grade

## Process

1. Score the inspection findings against the grading criteria.
2. Assign the risk grade (e.g., highly protected, good, average, below average).
3. Compile the report with findings, recommendations, and the risk grade.
4. Deliver the report to underwriting and the insured.
