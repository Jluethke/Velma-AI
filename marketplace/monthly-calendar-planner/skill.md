# Monthly Calendar Planner

Plan the monthly content calendar with themes and individual posts.

## Inputs

- `content-pillars`: Content themes and mix
- `posting-schedule`: Frequency and timing by platform

## Outputs

- `content-calendar`: Monthly calendar with post topics, dates, and platforms
- `creative-briefs`: Brief for each post's copy and visual

## Process

1. Map the month's key dates, events, and campaigns
2. Assign content pillars to each posting slot
3. Write the topic and angle for each post
4. Create the creative brief for each post's visual and copy
