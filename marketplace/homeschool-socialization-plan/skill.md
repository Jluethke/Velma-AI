# Homeschool Socialization Plan

Plan socialization opportunities: co-ops, sports, clubs, community activities, and field trips.

## Inputs

- `children_ages`: Ages and interests
- `location`: Home location for finding groups
- `interests`: Each child's social and activity interests

## Outputs

- `social_activities`: Regular social activities enrolled in
- `co_op_options`: Homeschool co-ops nearby
- `field_trip_calendar`: Planned educational outings

## Process

1. Research local homeschool co-ops and groups
2. Identify sports leagues and clubs
3. Plan monthly field trips
4. Schedule regular playdates or group activities
5. Balance structured and unstructured social time
