# Risk Owner Assigner

Assign risk owners and define accountability for each identified risk.

## Inputs

- `scored-risks`: Scored risks requiring ownership
- `organization-profile`: Organizational structure for owner assignment

## Outputs

- `owned-risks`: Risks with assigned owners and accountability
- `owner-notifications`: Notification records for each assigned owner

## Process

1. Match each risk to the appropriate owner based on system and process ownership
2. Notify each owner of their assigned risks and scoring
3. Document the assignment with acceptance from each owner
