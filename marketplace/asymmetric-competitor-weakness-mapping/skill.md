# Asymmetric Competitor Weakness Mapping

Map the structural weaknesses of each competitor, the limits built into their size, model, or commitments that they cannot easily escape. Structural weaknesses are more reliable to attack than temporary stumbles.

## Inputs

- `competitor-set`: The named competitors
- `business-context`: A factual profile of your business

## Outputs

- `competitor-weakness-map`: Each competitor's structural weaknesses
- `competitor-strength-map`: Each competitor's real strengths

## Process

1. For each competitor, find weaknesses built into their size, business model, or past commitments.
2. Separate structural weaknesses, which are hard for them to fix, from temporary ones.
3. Also record each competitor's genuine strengths, needed for the avoid-zone step.
4. Favor weaknesses a large rival cannot address without hurting their core business.
