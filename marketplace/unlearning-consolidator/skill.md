# Unlearning Consolidator

Use the relapse data to adjust cues and consolidate the new pattern.

## Inputs

- `relapse-log`: A judgment-free log of relapses
- `relapse-triggers`: The triggers around each relapse

## Outputs

- `adjusted-cues`: Cues and settings revised to handle the triggers
- `unlearning-plan`: The consolidated plan and its review points

## Process

1. Find the triggers that cause the most relapses.
2. Adjust cues and settings to handle those triggers.
3. Move to higher-stakes settings as relapses drop.
4. Compile the consolidated unlearning plan and its review points.
