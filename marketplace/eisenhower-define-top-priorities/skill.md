# Eisenhower Define Top Priorities

Name your top three priorities so importance can be judged against them rather than who asked.

## Inputs

- `task-list`: Every task and commitment, each with what it is for.
- `task-notes`: Where each task came from.

## Outputs

- `top-priorities`: The three priorities that matter most for the period.
- `priority-rationale`: Why each one earns a top spot.

## Process

1. Write the three priorities that matter most for the period.
2. State why each one earns a top spot.
3. Keep the list to three so importance stays meaningful.
