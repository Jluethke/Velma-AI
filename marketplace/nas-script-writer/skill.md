# Nas Script Writer

Write the opening and the responses.

## Inputs

- `anchor-justification`: The reasoning behind the anchor
- `concession-ladder`: The planned concession path

## Outputs

- `opening-script`: The line that states the anchor with its reasoning
- `response-playbook`: Responses to a low counter-anchor and common objections

## Process

1. Write the opening line that states the anchor with its reasoning.
2. Prepare responses to a low counter-anchor and to common objections.
3. Set the rule to let silence pull the midpoint toward the target.
