# Proposed Rule Drafter

Draft the proposed rule with regulatory analysis.

## Inputs

- `policy-objective`: The problem being addressed and the desired outcome
- `authorizing-statute`: The statutory authority for the rulemaking

## Outputs

- `proposed-rule-text`: Draft regulatory text with preamble explaining the basis, purpose, and expected impact
- `regulatory-impact-analysis`: Cost-benefit analysis, small business impact, and paperwork burden estimate

## Process

1. Draft the regulatory text with clear definitions, requirements, and compliance dates.
2. Write the preamble explaining the statutory authority, problem, and proposed solution.
3. Prepare the regulatory impact analysis (costs, benefits, alternatives considered).
4. Include the small business flexibility analysis and paperwork reduction act estimate.
