# Policy Scope Identifier

Identify the policy scope, applicable regulations, and organizational context.

## Inputs

- `organization-profile`: Description of the organization, industry, and size
- `regulatory-landscape`: List of applicable regulations and standards

## Outputs

- `policy-scope-document`: Defined scope including systems, data, and personnel covered
- `regulatory-requirements-map`: Mapping of regulations to policy requirements

## Process

1. Catalog the systems, data types, and personnel within scope
2. Map each regulation to its specific requirements for the policy area
3. Identify overlapping requirements across regulations
4. Produce the scope document with clear boundaries
