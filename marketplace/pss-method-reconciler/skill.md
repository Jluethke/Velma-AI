# Pss Method Reconciler

Reconcile the Van Westendorp range with the Gabor-Granger revenue point.

## Inputs

- `intersection-points`: The Van Westendorp acceptable range and optimal point
- `revenue-maximizing-price`: The Gabor-Granger revenue peak

## Outputs

- `reconciled-price-range`: A single price range supported by both methods
- `method-comparison`: Where the two methods agree and where they diverge

## Process

1. Place the revenue maximizing price against the acceptable range.
2. Narrow to the band supported by both methods.
3. Note where the methods agree and where they diverge.
