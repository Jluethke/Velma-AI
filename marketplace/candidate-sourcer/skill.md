# Candidate Sourcer

Source candidates through job boards, referrals, and direct outreach.

## Inputs

- `job-description`: The finalized job description
- `role-scorecard`: Key outcomes the hire must deliver

## Outputs

- `candidate-list`: List of sourced candidates with contact info and source channel
- `sourcing-metrics`: Count by channel and response rates

## Process

1. Post the role on relevant job boards
2. Activate the employee referral program
3. Identify and contact passive candidates through direct outreach
4. Track response rates by channel
