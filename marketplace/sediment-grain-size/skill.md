# Sediment Grain Size

Measure grain size distribution at each depth to infer depositional energy.

## Inputs

- `subsamples`: Subsamples treated to remove organics

## Outputs

- `grain-size-results`: Percentage sand, silt, clay and statistical parameters (mean, sorting, skewness) per depth
- `grain-size-profile`: Downcore plot of grain size fractions

## Process

1. Remove organic matter with hydrogen peroxide and disperse with sodium hexametaphosphate.
2. Measure particle size distribution by laser diffraction or sieve-pipette method.
3. Calculate statistical parameters: mean grain size, sorting, skewness.
4. Plot downcore profiles and interpret in terms of depositional energy changes.
