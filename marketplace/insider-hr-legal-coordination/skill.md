# Insider Hr Legal Coordination

Coordinate findings with HR and legal for appropriate response.

## Inputs

- `exfiltration-findings`: Evidence of exfiltration or other insider activity
- `investigation-scope`: Original scope and objectives

## Outputs

- `response-decision`: Decision on employment action, legal referral, or monitoring
- `coordination-log`: Record of all HR and legal consultations

## Process

1. Present findings to HR and legal with supporting evidence
2. Assess whether the evidence supports employment action, legal referral, or continued monitoring
3. Determine whether law enforcement notification is appropriate
4. Document all decisions and their rationale
