# Supervised Practice Cycle

Have the learner practice on real or simulated cases with the expert observing and correcting in real time.

## Inputs

- `expertise-document`: The documented cues and heuristics
- `practice-scenarios`: Scenarios for practice

## Outputs

- `practice-results`: For each practice case: what the learner decided, what the expert would have decided, and where they diverged
- `calibration-notes`: Where the learner's application of the heuristics needs adjustment (too aggressive, too conservative, missing a cue, misinterpreting a cue)

## Process

1. Present the learner with each practice scenario. Have them talk through their thinking aloud: what cues they notice, what those cues mean, what they would do.
2. Compare to the expert's expected decision. Where they diverge, identify whether the learner missed a cue, misinterpreted a cue, or applied the wrong heuristic.
3. Have the expert observe 1-2 real cases where the learner makes the decision and the expert provides feedback immediately after. Real cases reveal gaps that practice scenarios miss.
4. Repeat until the learner's decisions match the expert's on 80% or more of the decision points. The remaining 20% represents judgment that requires more experience to develop.
