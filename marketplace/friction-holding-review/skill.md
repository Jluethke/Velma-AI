# Friction Holding Review

After two and six weeks, check whether the behaviors actually shifted and adjust the friction where the design did not hold.

## Inputs

- `environment-reset-checklist`: The changes that were made
- `reset-completion-record`: Which changes were completed

## Outputs

- `behavior-shift-results`: Actual frequency of each behavior over the review period against its target
- `friction-adjustment-list`: Friction to keep, friction to strengthen, and new cues discovered that need removing

## Process

1. Tally each behavior daily through the review period. Keep the tally simple enough that you never skip it.
2. At two weeks, compare each behavior to its target. A behavior within reach of target means the design worked. A behavior unchanged means a cue was missed or the friction stack was too shallow.
3. For any unchanged decrease behavior, hunt for the cue you did not remove. There is almost always a path you left open.
4. At six weeks, behaviors that held are becoming automatic. Redirect attention to the ones still struggling and deepen their friction design.
