# Risk Identifier And Escalator

Identify at-risk items and escalate blockers.

## Inputs

- `tracking-dimensions`: What gets tracked
- `update-cadence`: Update frequency and method

## Outputs

- `risk-criteria`: Rules for flagging items as at-risk
- `escalation-protocol`: How and when blockers get escalated

## Process

1. Define at-risk criteria (missed milestones, stale updates, unresolved blockers for N days).
2. Set the escalation path (owner, manager, steering committee).
3. Define the escalation timing and format.
4. Produce the risk criteria and escalation protocol.
