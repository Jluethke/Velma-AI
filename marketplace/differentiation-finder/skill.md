# Differentiation Finder

Identify differentiation opportunities from the competitive analysis.

## Inputs

- `messaging-map`: Competitor messaging
- `feature-comparison`: Feature gaps and advantages

## Outputs

- `differentiation-opportunities`: Ranked opportunities to differentiate
- `positioning-statement`: Draft positioning statement

## Process

1. Identify areas where competitors cluster and whitespace exists
2. Draft the positioning statement
3. Rank differentiation opportunities by defensibility and audience value
