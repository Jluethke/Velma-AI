# Telehealth Order Placer

Place medication orders or lab/imaging orders as needed.

## Inputs

- `encounter-note`: Encounter documentation
- `assessment-findings`: Clinical findings

## Outputs

- `prescription-orders`: Medications prescribed during the visit
- `diagnostic-orders`: Labs, imaging, or referrals ordered

## Process

1. Prescribe medications through e-prescribing.
2. Order labs or imaging at a facility near the patient.
3. Generate referrals as needed.
