# Reading Block Scheduler

Schedule dedicated reading blocks in the calendar.

## Inputs

- `next-reads`: Next items to read
- `prioritized-list`: Full prioritized reading list

## Outputs

- `reading-schedule`: Calendar blocks for reading
- `reading-targets`: Page or article count targets per week

## Process

1. Identify available time slots for reading.
2. Block recurring reading time (daily or weekly).
3. Set targets: pages per session or articles per week.
