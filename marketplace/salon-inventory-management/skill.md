# Salon Inventory Management

Manage inventory for products, tools, and supplies used in service delivery and retail sales.

## Inputs

- `service-menu`: Services offered and products used in each
- `staff-schedule`: Staff schedule to estimate consumption rates

## Outputs

- `inventory-list`: Complete inventory with current stock, reorder points, and supplier information
- `reorder-schedule`: Automated reorder triggers and supplier order schedule

## Process

1. List all products and supplies consumed during service delivery.
2. Set par levels and reorder points based on usage rates.
3. Track retail product inventory separately from back-bar supplies.
4. Schedule regular inventory counts and reconciliation.
