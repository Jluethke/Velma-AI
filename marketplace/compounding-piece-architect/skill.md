# Compounding Piece Architect

Architect a compounding content piece. Compounding pieces are built differently from decaying pieces: they are thorough, structured for scanning, and designed to earn links.

## Inputs

- `compounding-topic-list`: Topics worth investing in
- `content-classification`: Patterns from existing compounding content

## Outputs

- `content-architecture-template`: The structural template for a compounding piece: title formula, section structure, link-earning elements, and update triggers
- `production-standards`: Minimum standards for a compounding piece: depth, citations, visuals, and update commitment

## Process

1. Title formula: include the exact phrase people search for. 'How to [do X]' or '[X] vs [Y]: [difference that matters]'. The title is for search, not for cleverness.
2. Structure for scanning: use H2 sections that each answer a sub-question. A reader should be able to get value from any single section without reading the whole piece.
3. Include link-earning elements: original data, a unique framework, a comparison table, or a tool. Other sites link to things they cannot create themselves.
4. Plan the update cycle: compounding content must be refreshed when the information changes. Set a reminder to review and update every 6-12 months.
