# Legal Assignment Router

Assign the request to the appropriate attorney or team based on classification and workload.

## Inputs

- `matter-classification`: The matter type
- `urgency-assessment`: Urgency and complexity assessment

## Outputs

- `assignment-record`: Record of the assignment: attorney, team, and expected response date
- `response-timeline`: Expected response and resolution timeline communicated to the requester

## Process

1. Match the matter type and complexity to the appropriate practice group or attorney.
2. Check current workload to balance assignments.
3. Set the expected response time based on urgency level.
4. Notify the assigned attorney and communicate the timeline to the requester.
