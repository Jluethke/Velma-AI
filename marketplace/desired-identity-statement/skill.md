# Desired Identity Statement

Write the identity you want to hold rather than the outcome you want to reach, because behaviors that match an identity last and behaviors chasing an outcome fade.

## Inputs

- `goal-area`: The area of life you want to change, such as fitness, reading, or diet
- `who-you-want-to-become`: The kind of person you would like to be in that area

## Outputs

- `identity-statement`: A present-tense identity statement such as I am the kind of person who moves every day
- `identity-evidence-gap`: The gap between that identity and what your current actions would prove to an observer

## Process

1. Rewrite the goal as an identity. I want to run becomes I am a runner. I want to read more becomes I am a reader. The identity is the target, the behavior is the proof.
2. State the identity in the present tense and in plain words. A future-tense identity keeps the person you want to be permanently out of reach.
3. Ask what an observer would conclude you are from your actions this past month. The distance between that and your statement is the evidence gap.
4. Confirm the identity is about process, not achievement. I am a runner survives a slow week. I am a marathon finisher ends the day the race is over.
