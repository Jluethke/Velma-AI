# Privacy Review Documentation

Document the privacy-by-design review and its outcomes.

## Inputs

- `recommended-measures`: The recommended privacy measures
- `design-changes`: Required design changes

## Outputs

- `review-record`: The documented privacy-by-design review
- `implementation-requirements`: Privacy requirements that must be met before launch

## Process

1. Compile the review record (processing description, risks, measures, design changes, residual risk).
2. List the privacy requirements that must be implemented before launch.
3. Obtain sign-off from the privacy team and the product owner.
4. Schedule the post-launch review to verify that the measures are working as intended.
