# Stakeholder Map Build

Map every person involved in the buying decision and their role: decision maker, influencer, gatekeeper, end user, budget holder.

## Inputs

- `deal-context`: The deal, the prospect organization, and what you know about the people involved
- `known-contacts`: People you have met or communicated with so far

## Outputs

- `stakeholder-map`: Every person in the decision with their name, title, role in the decision (decision maker, influencer, gatekeeper, end user, budget holder), and your relationship strength with them
- `unknown-stakeholders`: Roles you know exist but have not identified the person (e.g., you know there is a budget holder but do not know who)

## Process

1. List every person you have interacted with. Assign each a role: decision maker (has final authority), influencer (shapes the decision but does not make it), gatekeeper (controls access), end user (will use the product daily), budget holder (controls the money).
2. Identify gaps: roles that must exist but where you do not know who holds them. The most dangerous gap is an unknown decision maker (you think you are selling to the decision maker but they actually report to someone else).
3. Rate your relationship strength with each person: strong (they take your calls, share internal info), medium (they are engaged but cautious), weak (they attend meetings but do not respond between them), hostile (they have objections or prefer a competitor).
4. The stakeholder map is the foundation. Without it, you are selling blind.
