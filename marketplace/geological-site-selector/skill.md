# Geological Site Selector

Select field sites based on geological maps, remote sensing data, research objectives, and access logistics.

## Inputs

- `research-objectives`: What geological questions to answer
- `regional-geology`: Existing maps, reports, and remote sensing imagery

## Outputs

- `site-list`: Ranked candidate sites with coordinates, access notes, and expected formations
- `logistics-plan`: Access routes, permits, equipment transport, and field schedule
- `sampling-targets`: Which formations, contacts, or features to sample at each site

## Process

1. Review existing geological maps and satellite imagery
2. Identify exposures, contacts, and features relevant to the research question
3. Rank sites by scientific value, accessibility, and safety
4. Plan access logistics and permitting
