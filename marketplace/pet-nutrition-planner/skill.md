# Pet Nutrition Planner

Plan pet nutrition: food selection, portion control, treats, and dietary adjustments by life stage.

## Inputs

- `pet_type`: Species and breed
- `age`: Current age and life stage
- `weight`: Current and ideal weight
- `health_conditions`: Conditions affecting diet

## Outputs

- `feeding_plan`: Daily feeding schedule and portions
- `food_recommendations`: Recommended food brands and types
- `treat_guidelines`: Safe treats and daily limits

## Process

1. Calculate caloric needs by size and activity
2. Select appropriate food for life stage
3. Set portion sizes and feeding schedule
4. Identify safe and unsafe treat options
5. Plan for diet transitions
