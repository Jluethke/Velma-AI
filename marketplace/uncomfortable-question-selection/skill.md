# Uncomfortable Question Selection

Select the five questions that most content avoids: cost, problems, comparisons, reviews, and who is best.

## Inputs

- `question-list`: The real buyer questions
- `question-frequency`: How often each is asked

## Outputs

- `priority-questions`: The five questions to answer first, weighted to the uncomfortable ones
- `avoidance-reasons`: Why the company has avoided answering each, so the fear is named

## Process

1. From the list, pull the questions that touch cost, problems, comparisons, reviews, and who is best for whom.
2. Rank them by frequency and by how directly they block a purchase.
3. Pick the top five, making sure at least three are ones the company has been reluctant to answer.
4. For each, write why it has gone unanswered, since naming the fear makes it easier to overcome.
