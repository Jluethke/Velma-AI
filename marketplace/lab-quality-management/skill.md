# Lab Quality Management

Establish a quality management system with control charts, proficiency testing, equipment logs, and regular audit procedures.

## Inputs

- `sop-collection`: SOPs with embedded quality criteria
- `quality-criteria`: Acceptance limits for each method
- `regulatory-requirements`: Any accreditation or funding agency quality requirements

## Outputs

- `qms-document`: Quality management system manual with policies and procedures
- `control-charts`: Initial control chart templates for each monitored parameter
- `audit-schedule`: Internal audit calendar with assigned responsibilities

## Process

1. Define quality policies covering data integrity, method validation, and corrective actions
2. Set up control chart templates for each analytical method with warning and action limits
3. Schedule proficiency testing or interlaboratory comparisons at regular intervals
4. Create equipment maintenance and calibration log templates
5. Design the internal audit process with quarterly self-assessment and annual review
