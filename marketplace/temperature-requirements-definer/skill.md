# Temperature Requirements Definer

Define temperature requirements by product type.

## Inputs

- `product-list`: Products requiring temperature-controlled shipping
- `regulatory-standards`: Applicable regulatory temperature standards

## Outputs

- `temperature-profiles`: Required temperature range and tolerance per product
- `regulatory-checklist`: Regulatory requirements checklist per product

## Process

1. Document the required temperature range for each product
2. Set acceptable excursion limits (time and degrees)
3. Map products to applicable regulatory standards
4. Produce the temperature profiles and regulatory checklist
