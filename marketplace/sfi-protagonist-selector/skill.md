# Sfi Protagonist Selector

Choose a protagonist the audience can identify with. The audience experiences the story through the protagonist, so the protagonist has to be someone they recognize or can stand in for. A hero too distant from the audience turns the story into someone else's problem.

## Inputs

- `core-message`: The idea the story carries
- `change-sought`: The shift the story aims to produce

## Outputs

- `protagonist`: The central figure of the story, chosen so the audience identifies with them
- `audience-identification-note`: Why this protagonist connects to the audience and where the connection might be weak

## Process

1. Pick a protagonist close to the audience: a customer like them, a team like theirs, a person in their role.
2. Make the protagonist specific, not a category. 'A warehouse manager named in the story' lands harder than 'operations teams.'
3. Check the identification. If the audience would see the protagonist as other, the story's lesson will feel like it applies to someone else.
4. Confirm the protagonist can plausibly face the problem that carries your core message. The right hero for one message is the wrong hero for another.
