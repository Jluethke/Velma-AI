# Time Boxed Discussion

Run the meeting in 30 minutes or less: 5 minutes to confirm, 15 to discuss, 5 to decide, 5 for next steps.

## Inputs

- `pre-read-document`: The pre-read
- `required-attendees`: Attendees

## Outputs

- `decision-made`: The specific decision, stated clearly, with the rationale
- `next-steps`: Who does what by when as a result of the decision
- `meeting-record`: A 3-line record: the decision, the rationale, and the next steps with owners and deadlines

## Process

1. Open with 2 minutes confirming the decision and options. Do not re-explain the pre-read.
2. Run 15 minutes of focused discussion. Use a timer. When someone goes off-topic, redirect: 'That is interesting but not part of this decision. Can we take it offline?'
3. At the 20-minute mark, push for a decision. 'We have heard the discussion. What is the call?' If the decision maker needs more information, the meeting fails; schedule a follow-up.
4. Close with next steps in the last 5 minutes: who does what, by when. Read them aloud. Get verbal confirmation. Send the 3-line record within the hour.
