# Pediatrician Selection

Evaluate and select a pediatrician based on location, insurance, philosophy, and availability.

## Inputs

- `insurance_plan`: Current health insurance
- `location`: Home address or zip
- `parenting_priorities`: Vaccination stance, breastfeeding support, etc.

## Outputs

- `shortlist`: Ranked pediatrician candidates
- `interview_questions`: Questions for meet-and-greet visits

## Process

1. Filter by insurance acceptance and proximity
2. Research online reviews and board certifications
3. Assess office hours and after-hours availability
4. Prepare interview question list
5. Score and rank candidates
