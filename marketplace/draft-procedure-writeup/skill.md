# Draft Procedure Writeup

Write the first-draft procedure from the ordered steps and decision points, and surface the knowledge the draft silently assumes. Assumed knowledge is the hidden reason newcomers get stuck.

## Inputs

- `raw-step-sequence`: Ordered actions
- `decision-points`: Branch conditions

## Outputs

- `draft-procedure`: The first written procedure with steps and branches
- `assumed-knowledge`: Terms, access, and context the draft takes for granted

## Process

1. Turn the ordered actions into numbered steps a reader can follow in sequence.
2. Write each decision point as a clear condition with the path to take for each case.
3. List every term, tool, or access the draft assumes the reader already has.
4. Keep steps at the level of actions a newcomer can perform, not summaries an expert would.
