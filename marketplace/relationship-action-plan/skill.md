# Relationship Action Plan

Turn relationship insights into concrete weekly actions, date plans, and check-in schedules.

## Inputs

- `pain_points`: Areas needing work
- `shared_goals`: Agreed goals
- `de_escalation_tools`: Agreed conflict tools

## Outputs

- `weekly_actions`: Specific actions for each partner this week
- `date_plan`: Next 4 date ideas with logistics
- `check_in_schedule`: Regular check-in calendar

## Process

1. Convert each pain point to one specific action
2. Schedule regular check-in cadence
3. Plan intentional quality time
4. Set 30-day review date
