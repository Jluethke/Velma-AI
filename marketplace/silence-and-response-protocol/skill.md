# Silence And Response Protocol

After Observation, Impact, and Request: stop talking. The silence is where the other person processes and responds. Most people fill the silence with softening, backtracking, or over-explaining, and the message is lost.

## Inputs

- `request-statement`: What you just asked for
- `their-stakes`: What the other person stands to lose, which predicts their likely response

## Outputs

- `silence-plan`: What you will do during the silence (count to 10, breathe, wait) and what you will NOT do (soften, restate, apologize for asking)
- `response-paths`: The 3-4 likely responses and how to handle each: agreement, deflection, anger, counter-attack

## Process

1. Plan the silence: after delivering the request, count to 10 internally. Do not speak. The discomfort is the conversation working.
2. Map the likely responses: agreement (accept and schedule follow-up), deflection (redirect to the observation), anger (acknowledge the emotion without withdrawing the request), counter-attack (stay on the observation, do not argue about intent).
3. For each response path, write one sentence you will say. Keep it short. Long responses after silence undo the silence.
4. Plan the close: restate the request, confirm the measurable check, and set the follow-up date.
