# Energy Cycle Mapping

Map your personal energy cycle: when are you sharpest, when do you fade, and what causes energy crashes.

## Inputs

- `typical-day`: Your typical daily schedule
- `energy-complaints`: When you feel most drained or unfocused

## Outputs

- `energy-map`: Hourly energy ratings for a typical day: morning, mid-morning, post-lunch, afternoon, evening. Rated 1-10.
- `crash-triggers`: Activities or times that reliably cause energy drops: meetings, meals, poor sleep, specific types of work

## Process

1. For 5 workdays, rate your energy 1-10 at each hour from waking to sleeping. Use a simple scale: 10 = sharp and focused, 5 = functional but not sharp, 1 = barely functioning.
2. Identify the pattern: most people have a peak in the morning (9-11am), a dip after lunch (1-3pm), and a secondary peak in late afternoon (4-6pm). Your pattern may differ.
3. Identify crash triggers: what reliably drops your energy? Long meetings, heavy lunches, poor sleep the night before, specific types of administrative work.
4. The energy map is your scheduling template. High-energy hours get the hardest work. Low-energy hours get routine tasks.
