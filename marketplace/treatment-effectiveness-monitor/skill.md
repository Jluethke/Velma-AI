# Treatment Effectiveness Monitor

Apply treatment, monitor effectiveness, and produce the pest management record.

## Inputs

- `management-plan`: Selected treatment approach
- `affected-area-map`: Zones to treat

## Outputs

- `treatment-record`: What was applied, when, where, and by whom
- `effectiveness-assessment`: Post-treatment evaluation of pest or disease reduction

## Process

1. Apply the treatment per the management plan.
2. Scout treated areas 5 to 7 days after application.
3. Compare post-treatment pest levels to pre-treatment levels.
4. Document effectiveness and decide on follow-up action.
