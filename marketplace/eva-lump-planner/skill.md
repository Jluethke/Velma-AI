# Eva Lump Planner

Plan sinking funds for the lumpy spends.

## Inputs

- `lumpy-spend-set`: The large infrequent charges

## Outputs

- `sinking-fund-plan`: Each large expense divided across the months until due
- `monthly-set-aside`: The monthly amount needed to meet the lumps

## Process

1. List each large periodic expense with its due date and amount.
2. Divide each amount across the months until it is due.
3. Total the monthly set-aside needed to meet them without a shock.
