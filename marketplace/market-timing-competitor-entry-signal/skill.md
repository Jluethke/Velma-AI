# Market Timing Competitor Entry Signal

Read the second signal: have three or more competitors entered this space in the last two years? A wave of recent entrants shows the market is waking up. A crowd of long-established players can show you are late.

## Inputs

- `opportunity-profile`: A tight profile of the opportunity and offering
- `timing-question-set`: The three timing questions framed for this opportunity

## Outputs

- `competitor-entry-count`: How many competitors entered in the last two years
- `entry-trend`: Whether entry is accelerating, steady, or long settled

## Process

1. Count the competitors who entered this space in the last two years.
2. Note whether entry is speeding up, steady, or whether the field settled long ago.
3. Read three or more recent entrants as a sign the timing window is opening.
4. Read a long-settled field of entrenched players as a sign you may be late.
