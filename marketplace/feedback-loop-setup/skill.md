# Feedback Loop Setup

Set up an immediate feedback mechanism so you know after every rep whether you succeeded or failed and why.

## Inputs

- `isolation-drill`: The drill to practice
- `difficulty-calibration`: How to set the challenge level

## Outputs

- `feedback-mechanism`: How you will get immediate feedback on each rep (self-scoring, recording review, coach observation, automated test)
- `tracking-method`: How you will track success rate over time to detect improvement or stagnation

## Process

1. Identify the fastest feedback mechanism available. In order of speed: real-time automated feedback (a test, a score), self-review of a recording immediately after, coach or peer observation, delayed review of output.
2. If no immediate feedback is available, create a rubric with 3-5 criteria you can score yourself on within 30 seconds of completing a rep.
3. Set up a tracking system: a simple spreadsheet or notebook where you log the date, number of reps, success rate, and any notes on what went wrong.
4. Plan to review your tracking data weekly to spot trends: improving, plateauing, or declining.
