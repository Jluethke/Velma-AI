# Post Repair Quality Check

Verify the repair resolved the original complaint and no new issues were introduced.

## Inputs

- `repair-log`: Record of work performed
- `diagnostic-findings`: Original diagnostic findings for comparison

## Outputs

- `quality-check-result`: Pass or fail with notes on what was verified
- `road-test-notes`: Observations from the post-repair road test if applicable

## Process

1. Verify the original complaint is resolved (retest, clear codes, road test).
2. Check that no new warning lights, noises, or leaks are present.
3. Confirm all fasteners, covers, and components are properly reinstalled.
4. Sign off on the quality check and update the work order status.
