# Breach Evidence Preservation

Preserve all evidence related to the breach for investigation and potential legal proceedings.

## Inputs

- `scope-findings`: Full scope of the breach
- `system-context`: Systems involved in the breach

## Outputs

- `evidence-package`: Preserved and cataloged evidence with chain of custody
- `forensic-timeline`: Reconstructed timeline of attacker activity

## Process

1. Preserve all relevant logs, disk images, and memory captures
2. Hash and catalog each evidence item with chain-of-custody documentation
3. Build the forensic timeline from all available evidence sources
4. Store evidence in a secure location with access controls and audit logging
