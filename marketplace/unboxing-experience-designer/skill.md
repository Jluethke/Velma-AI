# Unboxing Experience Designer

Design the unboxing experience and packaging.

## Inputs

- `curation-strategy`: Product curation approach
- `audience-profile`: Target audience preferences

## Outputs

- `packaging-spec`: Packaging design and materials specification
- `insert-plan`: Box insert content (card, booklet, samples)

## Process

1. Design the outer packaging (branded box, tissue, stickers).
2. Plan the product arrangement for visual impact on opening.
3. Create the box insert content (product guide, personalized note, bonus item).
4. Spec the packaging materials and costs.
