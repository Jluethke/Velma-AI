# Substantive Procedures Executor

Perform substantive audit procedures: confirmations, analytical procedures, and detail testing.

## Inputs

- `audit-plan`: Audit plan with substantive procedures defined
- `controls-reliance-decision`: Level of controls reliance achieved

## Outputs

- `substantive-results`: Results of all substantive procedures performed
- `misstatements-log`: Log of identified misstatements

## Process

1. Send confirmations (bank, receivable, payable, legal) and evaluate responses.
2. Perform analytical procedures comparing actuals to expectations.
3. Test details of account balances (vouching, tracing, recalculation).
4. Document all findings and accumulate identified misstatements.
