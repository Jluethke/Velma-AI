# Current Sleep Baseline

Measure your current sleep baseline for 7 days: time to bed, time to sleep, wake-ups, wake time, and subjective quality rating.

## Inputs

- `current-sleep-habits`: Your current sleep and wake times, bedroom environment, and pre-sleep routine
- `sleep-complaints`: What is wrong with your sleep: trouble falling asleep, waking up, not feeling rested, inconsistent schedule

## Outputs

- `sleep-baseline`: 7-day log: bedtime, estimated time to fall asleep, number of wake-ups, final wake time, total estimated sleep, and quality rating 1-10
- `sleep-patterns`: Patterns identified: weekend vs weekday differences, time-to-sleep trends, quality correlations

## Process

1. Track for 7 consecutive days using a simple log. Record: what time you got in bed, what time you estimate you fell asleep, how many times you woke up, what time you finally woke up, and rate the quality 1-10.
2. Calculate averages: average time to fall asleep (sleep latency), average total sleep time, average quality rating. Sleep latency over 20 minutes indicates a problem with your pre-sleep routine or timing.
3. Note the weekend-weekday difference. If you sleep 2+ hours more on weekends, you are carrying sleep debt. The weekend is not 'catching up'; it is evidence that weeknight sleep is insufficient.
4. Identify the biggest lever: is the problem falling asleep (latency), staying asleep (wake-ups), or not sleeping long enough (early alarm)? Each has a different solution.
