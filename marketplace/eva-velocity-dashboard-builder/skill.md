# Eva Velocity Dashboard Builder

Build the view of how fast money leaves.

## Inputs

- `leak-findings`: The audited slow leaks
- `sinking-fund-plan`: The plan for the lumpy spends

## Outputs

- `velocity-dashboard`: The steady drain and the smoothed lumps in one view
- `monthly-outflow-shape`: The month-by-month shape of outflows

## Process

1. Combine the leak findings and the sinking-fund plan into one view.
2. Show the steady drain and the smoothed lumps side by side.
3. Highlight the fastest-draining categories.
