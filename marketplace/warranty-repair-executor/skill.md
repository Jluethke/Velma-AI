# Warranty Repair Executor

Perform the warranty repair per the manufacturer procedure and document the work.

## Inputs

- `coverage-determination`: Confirmed warranty coverage details
- `claim-requirements`: Documentation and procedure requirements

## Outputs

- `repair-documentation`: Photos, measurements, failed parts, and technician notes per manufacturer requirements
- `warranty-labor-record`: Labor operations performed at the warranty labor rate and time

## Process

1. Follow the manufacturer diagnostic and repair procedure exactly as specified.
2. Take the required photos before, during, and after the repair.
3. Record any measurements the manufacturer requires (wear, clearance, voltage).
4. Retain the failed parts for the required period in case of manufacturer audit.
