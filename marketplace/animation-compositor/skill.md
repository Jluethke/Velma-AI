# Animation Compositor

Composite the animation layers and add visual effects.

## Inputs

- `animated-scenes`: Rendered animation frames
- `environment-designs`: Background artwork

## Outputs

- `composited-scenes`: Final composited scenes with effects
- `render-settings`: Export settings and format specifications

## Process

1. Layer characters over backgrounds with proper depth
2. Add lighting effects, shadows, and atmospheric elements
3. Apply camera moves (pans, zooms, rack focus)
4. Render the final composited frames
