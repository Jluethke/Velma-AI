# Ugc Curation Workflow

Curate and approve submitted content for use.

## Inputs

- `rights-framework`: The rights and permissions framework
- `ugc-types`: The types of UGC being collected

## Outputs

- `curated-content-library`: Approved UGC organized by type, theme, and quality tier
- `rejection-log`: Log of rejected content with reasons

## Process

1. Review incoming submissions against brand standards and quality criteria.
2. Verify that rights and permissions are in place for each piece.
3. Tag and categorize approved content by type, theme, and quality tier.
4. Log rejected content with the reason for rejection.
