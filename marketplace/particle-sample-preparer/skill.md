# Particle Sample Preparer

Prepare the sample for measurement ensuring it is representative and properly dispersed.

## Inputs

- `bulk-material`: The bulk material from which the sample is drawn
- `measurement-protocol`: Preparation requirements for the selected technique

## Outputs

- `prepared-sample`: Sample ready for measurement, properly dispersed and at correct concentration
- `preparation-notes`: Dispersant used, sonication time, dilution factor, and any observations

## Process

1. Take a representative sub-sample using appropriate splitting (riffling, spinning, cone and quartering)
2. Disperse the sample in the measurement medium (wet: surfactant + sonication; dry: compressed air)
3. Adjust concentration to the optimal range for the instrument
4. Note any aggregation, settling, or dissolution observed during preparation
