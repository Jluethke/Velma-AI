# Policy Archive Manager

Archive policy documents and maintain the policy record for retention requirements.

## Inputs

- `cancellation-notice`: Cancellation or expiration confirmation
- `policy-status-update`: Final policy status

## Outputs

- `archived-policy-record`: The complete archived policy record with all documents, endorsements, and correspondence
- `policy-administration-record`: Summary record of the full policy lifecycle for reporting

## Process

1. Compile all policy documents, endorsements, billing records, and correspondence.
2. Store the archive per the document retention policy.
3. Set the retention period based on the line of business and regulatory requirements.
4. Generate the policy lifecycle summary for reporting.
