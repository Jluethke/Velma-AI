# Differentiating Behavior Interview

Interview each top performer to surface what sets them apart. Most will first say they just work hard. Push past that to specific actions and to the skills they use daily that no one told them to learn.

## Inputs

- `daily-activity-log`: What each performer actually does
- `time-allocation-map`: Where their hours go

## Outputs

- `differentiating-behaviors`: The concrete actions and habits that separate top performers from their peers
- `hidden-skill-list`: Skills the performers use daily that were never named when they were hired

## Process

1. Ask each performer what they do that colleagues who perform less well do not do, pushing for concrete actions rather than traits.
2. When they say they work hard or ask questions, ask what specifically they ask about and who they seek out.
3. Ask what skill they use every day that was not mentioned when they were hired.
4. Ask what they would spend the most time on if they had one week to train their replacement; the answer is usually a hidden skill.
5. Record concrete behaviors and skills, discarding vague self-descriptions.
