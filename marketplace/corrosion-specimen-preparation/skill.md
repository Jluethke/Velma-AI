# Corrosion Specimen Preparation

Prepare corrosion test specimens to a defined surface condition and document initial mass and dimensions.

## Inputs

- `materials-to-evaluate`: Materials and their forms
- `test-parameters`: Specimen geometry requirements for the test method

## Outputs

- `prepared-corrosion-specimens`: Specimens with measured initial mass, area, and surface finish
- `pre-exposure-documentation`: Photographs and surface condition records

## Process

1. Cut specimens to the required dimensions and grind to a uniform surface finish.
2. Clean with solvent, rinse, and dry to a constant weight.
3. Measure and record initial mass (to 0.1 mg), exposed surface area, and dimensions.
4. Photograph the surface and apply masking to non-test areas if needed.
